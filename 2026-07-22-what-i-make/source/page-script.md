# Page Script — What I Make

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions are deliberately clear, nonredundant, and long enough to explain every major change without making the reader infer the plot. Jet and every recurring design must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 — Cover

One lyrical layered composition. Jet stands in the three-walled floating living room, smiling as he strums the living vine guitar wrapped around his arms. He has dark-brown skin, full beard, long dreadlocks beneath his black hoodie, visible Dream Anime shirt, striped black pants, white sneakers, and belt bag. The purple felt notebook lies open beside him to the star-topped plant painting. The friendly wood-paneled flying jacuzzi hovers nearby with its rainbow RGB face. Below the open floor, green and pink aurora tendrils curve across the Arctic; far inside them, the steampunk cowboy chases. The sky above becomes bright blue with fluffy pink clouds. Visible cover text only: “WHAT I MAKE” and “JULY 22, 2026”. No issue number.

## Page 2 — Peace Takes Flight

Five panels. Jet lies conscious but immobilized during sleep paralysis in a dark bedroom. Sparkling purple Lucid Light releases him into the continuing Storyverse. He is then sitting in the steaming sentient wood-paneled jacuzzi in the Arctic, with bitter wind and ice outside but perfectly warm water around him. Close view of the friendly rainbow RGB eyes and smiling mouth listening while Jet wonders whether quiet relaxation can compare with his superhero adventures. Hover pads activate beneath the tub. Final large panel: the jacuzzi lifts into the sky and carries a delighted Jet directly toward the Aurora Borealis.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and entered the same Storyverse continuity I had explored since June 11, 2025.”
- “I found myself inside a friendly, sentient, wood-paneled jacuzzi in the middle of the Arctic.”
- “The air was bitterly cold, but the water around me was perfectly warm, and the jacuzzi seemed devoted to helping me relax.”
- “I wondered whether simply sitting still might feel dull beside all my superhero adventures.”
- “The jacuzzi understood. Hover pads awakened beneath us, and together we rose toward the Aurora Borealis.”

## Page 3 — Through the Northern Lights

Five cinematic panels. The flying jacuzzi enters huge green and pink aurora tendrils shaped like luminous blood vessels. The light breaks around Jet and the wooden tub like cotton candy, constantly reforming. Close-up of Jet's wet skin and dreadlocks surrounded by tiny electromagnetic tingles. The jacuzzi's RGB face looks blissfully proud. Orchestral music is represented only through sweeping instrumental imagery rising from below, with no written sound effects. Jet looks down. Final wide panel: an armored steampunk war train races across elevated tracks over the ice and fires cannons upward at the jacuzzi.

Caption text:

- “We flew directly into the northern lights, through long green and pink tendrils that curved across the sky like glowing blood vessels.”
- “The aurora separated around us like luminous cotton candy, then shifted and formed itself again.”
- “Because the light was electromagnetic, a gentle tingling sensation moved across my skin whenever we passed through it.”
- “Orchestral music rose from below, where an armed steampunk war train raced across the frozen landscape.”
- “I did not know who was fighting or why, but the train suddenly began firing at us.”

## Page 4 — The War I Refused

Six dynamic panels. Jet instructs the jacuzzi upward and the tub climbs beyond the train's range. The steampunk cowboy appears behind them on the flying iron-and-brass horse and fires his musket. One amber projectile tears across Jet's shoulder and splinters a small section of the jacuzzi's wooden rim; both look hurt. Jet draws the rainbow crystal Sword of Light and forms a radiant multicolor barrier around them; the wounds and wood repair inside the glow. The cowboy keeps chasing. Jet throws the golden glittering tether, which connects to the horse's brass chest. Gold light spreads through its gears like a computer interface. Final panel: Jet and the healed jacuzzi fly safely away while the unmanned mechanical horse erupts into a contained distant burst of steam, gears, and light; the cowboy has been thrown clear with a parachute-like billowing duster silhouette, defeated but without graphic injury.

Caption text:

- “I wanted no part in their war, so I asked the jacuzzi to climb beyond the train's firing range.”
- “A cowboy on a flying steampunk horse pursued us and fired a musket that wounded both me and the jacuzzi.”
- “I drew my rainbow Sword of Light and created a barrier that blocked more shots while healing the damage already done.”
- “When he refused to leave us alone, I connected my golden energy tether to his gear-driven horse.”
- “The magical bond let me interface with the analog machine as if I were hacking a computer.”
- “I activated the horse's self-destruct system, ended the pursuit, and continued toward peace instead of joining the battle.”

## Page 5 — The Room With No Horizon

Five panels. The flying jacuzzi approaches a living room floating freely among clouds. The room has one solid back wall and three missing walls; a couch sits against the wall and a television faces it near the open sky. Jet climbs out, clothing damp but unchanged, and sits on the couch. He considers the television, then turns away from it. He removes the handmade purple patchwork felt notebook. Final warm panel: Jet opens the notebook on his lap while a fireplace-like amber glow surrounds him even though no actual fireplace is present; he holds the glittering pink magical pencil and looks safe, nostalgic, and curious.

Caption text:

- “Eventually, the jacuzzi carried me to a living room floating freely in the sky.”
- “Three walls were missing, so its couch and television stood almost completely open to the clouds.”
- “I climbed out, sat on the couch, and considered different ways I could relax.”
- “Instead of watching television, I opened a notebook stitched from soft pieces of purple felt and summoned a pencil glowing with pink magic.”
- “The blank pages filled me with nostalgic warmth, as though I were sitting beside a fireplace somewhere safe and familiar.”

## Page 6 — Something Only for Me

Five intimate panels. Close-up of Jet putting the pink magical pencil to the cream page. The green stalk develops leaf by leaf. A radiant star appears at its top instead of a flower. Bands of coral, orange, gold, pink, and violet sunset colors spread outward in a circle. The apparently pencil-made image becomes a delicate watercolor painting with fine ink and sketch details. Jet studies it peacefully, holding the notebook close. Final panel shows the small personal picture reflected in his warm brown eyes while the empty television remains dark behind him.

Caption text:

- “I was not especially skilled at drawing while awake, but a dream did not have the same limitations.”
- “I drew a leafy green stalk with a radiant star where its flower should have been.”
- “Circular bands of sunset color spread from the star, and the pencil drawing became a delicate watercolor layered with ink and sketch details.”
- “Beginning with a blank page and placing a small part of myself onto it felt peaceful and deeply satisfying.”
- “The drawing never needed to be shown to anyone else. It could exist entirely for me, and that alone gave it meaning.”

## Page 7 — The Living Song

Five panels. Real green vines sprout gently around Jet's hands and forearms while he holds the open notebook. Leaves, flowers, and stems intertwine into the curved body and neck of a living guitar wrapped around his arms. The Arctic aurora beyond the room transforms into a bright blue sky full of soft fluffy pink clouds. Jet stands near the open edge and tests the vine strings. The friendly jacuzzi hovers beside him with an encouraging rainbow smile. Final large panel: Jet strums confidently as flowers open across the guitar and the cloudscape seems to move with the rhythm.

Caption text:

- “As I considered the value of creating purely for myself, a real plant began growing around my arms.”
- “Its vines, leaves, flowers, and living stems intertwined until they formed a guitar.”
- “The aurora disappeared, and the floating room opened onto a bright blue sky filled with soft pink clouds.”
- “I began playing a song about creation, personal freedom, and the right to decide what belongs inside my own imagination.”
- “Other people might become angry at what someone chooses to make, but their anger does not give them ownership over another person's art.”

## Page 8 — What I Make

Five celebratory panels. Jet sings and plays the living vine guitar in the open floating room. Smaller panels show the open notebook's star-plant painting, the silent television, and the friendly jacuzzi listening against the pink clouds. Jet's expression is joyful and defiant rather than angry. Sunset-colored light from the drawing begins to echo through the blue sky, tying the private page to the enormous dream world. Final large panel: Jet stands centered above the clouds, vines and flowers around his arms, while the jacuzzi smiles nearby and the notebook remains open in the foreground. The final hook occupies two clearly separated rectangular caption boxes, not bubbles.

Caption text:

- “I sang that private creation was rewarding in itself, because art did not need an audience before it could matter.”
- “Something could remain intimate, personal, and meaningful even when it was created for only one person.”
- “The small picture in my notebook had already changed my experience of the entire sky.”
- “Cry me a river, bleed me a lake.”
- “You can’t take or control what I make.”
