Dream journal entry dated 07/08/2026.

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in an arctic Storyverse world. He discovers a warm-hearted city of igloos whose people barter with auralite crystals, drum together under the aurora, and treat each other like one big family. The city's dangerous nearby caves contain a rare dinosaur species with natural armor that looks like auralite, allowing the dinosaurs to lure prey. The miners usually avoid them, because the dinosaurs stay deep underground where geothermal heat keeps them alive.

When a sinkhole opens in the middle of the city, the geothermal vent below first feels like a miracle. The people gather around it for warmth, but the heat also makes the city safe for the armored dinosaurs, which emerge and attack. Jet intervenes with Lucid Light by creating a wandering hero named Insulation Annie. Annie can expand her back into a huge shield of insulation putty. To save the city, she lies over the sinkhole and plugs it with her back, sealing herself permanently in place. The dinosaurs retreat once the heat is blocked. The people honor Annie and feed her offerings, but generations later she withers away and is forgotten. Only the insulation putty remains, protecting the city forever.

The story should be clear that Annie saves the city, but her victory is tragic because she loses the freedom to choose her own home.
