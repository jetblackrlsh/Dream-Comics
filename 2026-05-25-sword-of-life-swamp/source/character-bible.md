# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries the Sword of Life: a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword that glows with living prismatic light. He also carries a rope-like golden glittering energy tether, though the tether is secondary in this issue.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow sword, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. When the plant spirit bonds with him, add a thick bark mask over his face and bark knuckles over his fists without erasing the black hoodie silhouette.

## Plant Hero Spirit

Ancient heroic spirit with a humanoid body made of twisting tree roots, wooden limbs, glowing green sap lines, and a cape made of moss. The spirit has gentle but fierce guardian energy, with leaf fragments and soft green light trailing from its body.

Continuity notes: The plant hero spirit should read as a living tree-root guardian, not a monster. When bonded with Jet, show root energy wrapping around Jet, forming a bark face mask, bark knuckles, and moss-like protective accents.

## Cyborg Sword-Shop Keeper

Sharp-featured cyborg merchant with one human-like side and one chrome mechanical side, glowing scanner eyes, a dark metallic apron, and tool belts for magical sword appraisal. One arm can transform into a laser cannon with blue-white energy rings.

Continuity notes: Start him as a calm shopkeeper behind the counter, then make the cannon arm obvious after the betrayal. Keep his sword-shop apron and scanner eyes visible across pages.

## Robot Knights

Tall armored robots shaped like medieval knights, with polished steel armor, angular helmets, glowing eye slits, and laser beams fired from their eyes. Their movements are precise and law-enforcement-like. They carry no visible mouths and no handheld guns.

Continuity notes: Repeat the glowing eye slits and steel knight silhouettes whenever they appear. Their eye lasers should be red-orange and should visibly deflect from the Sword of Life.

## Whittmore

Mysterious nature-aligned woman in a swamp. She wears flowing white robes and a white hat shaped like a trapezoid. She has extremely long brown hair, an angular elven face, pale skin, and radiant blue eyes. She appears calm, clever, and quietly protective.

Continuity notes: The extremely long brown hair, trapezoid white hat, white robes, angular elven face, and radiant blue eyes are her key identifiers.

## Sword Shop, Swamp, and Alien Rulers

The magical sword shop is a luminous armory full of floating crystal blades, neon appraisal glyphs, polished counters, and cyber-mystic tools. The swamp is misty, green, reflective, and alive with roots, reeds, blue fireflies, and half-hidden alien technology. The alien rulers are mostly seen as distant holographic silhouettes, surveillance eyes, and hovering drones rather than as fully introduced characters.
