Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone on the moon, where a pizzeria sells slices made from flattened moons. The moon pizza tastes chalky and secretly contains burning moon mites. When Jet stops resisting and gives the mites permission to stay, they form insectoid exoskeleton armor that makes him stronger and tougher. After another customer resists the mites and burns to ash, Jet demands accountability from the pizzeria manager, a mammoth chef who answers criticism with violence. Jet uses the moon-mite armor to defeat him across the moon and forces him to add a clear menu disclaimer.
