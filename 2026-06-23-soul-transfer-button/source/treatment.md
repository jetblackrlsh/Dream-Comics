# Treatment

## Title

06/23/2026: Soul Transfer Button

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet argues that universes can interact, presses a glowing green rotate button, and is hurled into a soul-transference research world where a brilliant, frightening scientist studies swapped souls with unsettling delight.

## Central Conflict

Jet begins in a lucid debate with unfamiliar people who insist that other universes cannot be touched. When the green rotate button appears, Jet chooses contact over doubt, and the act throws his soul beyond ordinary reality. He awakens into another world as a soul-transference researcher, surrounded by people whose strange behavior snaps into normalcy with blank, unfamiliar expressions. The conflict is not a physical fight. It is Jet's growing realization that the phenomenon works and that one researcher, a long-bearded man with round blue glasses, has turned other people's disorientation into a controlled experimental playground.

## Theme

Discovery can prove a door exists without proving that every person who opens it is safe.

## Emotional Arc

Jet starts curious and confident, defending the possibility of interaction between universes. Pressing the rotate button turns that intellectual argument into a bodily shock: his soul is flung out of reality and into a new life. As the laboratory world stabilizes around him, wonder becomes unease. The successful soul transfers are undeniable, but the lead researcher's enthusiasm feels too close to control for its own sake. Jet ends awake but uncertain, carrying the uncomfortable thought that he may have crossed worlds and may not have returned to the exact one he left.

## Ending

The final page shows Jet waking in his room while a faint green oval glow and branching bubble universes linger in the dark. He has no proof that he returned to the same reality, only the memory of a lab where souls learned to puppet unfamiliar bodies.

## Cast

- Jet: the lucid dreamer and protagonist. He is a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow futuristic crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether, though this issue focuses more on his lucid awareness than on combat.
- Unfamiliar Debate Group: ordinary dream people in modern casual clothes, gathered in a dim, abstract discussion space. They look skeptical and dismissive when discussing other universes.
- Green Rotate Button: a glowing green oval button floating at eye level. It contains a rotating arrow symbol and shines like a lucid interface from outside reality.
- Soul-Transfer Researcher Jet: Jet after the transfer, wearing his familiar face and hair but also a white lab coat over his black hoodie and Dream Anime tee, carrying a clipboard or tablet in a soul-transfer research facility.
- Lead Researcher: an adult man with a long brown beard, round blue glasses, sharp eyes magnified by the lenses, a white lab coat, ink-stained gloves, and an unnervingly delighted smile. He is brilliant, precise, and scary because he treats people's lives as experimental arrangements.
- Transferred Subjects: ordinary people in a research facility who shift from strange, puppetlike behavior into sudden normal posture, but their eyes and expressions show they do not know the lives they have entered.
- Soul Transference Lab: a polished but uncanny research facility with glass observation rooms, teal-green soul projectors, branching multiverse diagrams, rotating transfer chairs, body-scan rings, and green-purple energy streams.
