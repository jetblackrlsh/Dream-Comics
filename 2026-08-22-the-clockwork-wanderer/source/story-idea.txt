Jet explores a planet made from ancient analog spacecraft that drifted together under gravity, creating a bronze, gold, and black world of gigantic gears, pirate treasure, old credits, and forgotten antiques. He encounters a mysterious woman in a slim white hard-polymer astronaut suit with an opaque mirrored helmet, glowing green orbs along her limbs, and cobalt-blue ringed gauntlets. She is battling powerful pink-skinned, red-eyed, white-haired barbarian women whose footsteps crack the clockwork ground. Her gauntlets reflect their laser fire, forcing them to escape in enormous superhuman leaps, but she remains vulnerable to a direct punch. She reveals that the barbarians stole her ship and refuses Jet's offer of portal rescue because she wants no handout. Respecting her autonomy, Jet uses sparkling purple Lucid Light to create a winged jetpack that expands her own options. She accepts it, thanks him, and flies over the horizon to continue her independent adventure.
