# Treatment

## Logline

After sleep paralysis sends Jet into a superhero Storyverse world where The Liquifier has vaporized nearly every hero, Jet survives as living vapor, turns Lucid Light into Lucid Lightning, restores the gaseous heroes from the clouds, and helps Sucker and Void Man trap the villain for good.

## Central Conflict

Jet arrives alone in a ruined superhero city with only phasing heroes left alive. Everyone thinks The Liquifier is only a villain who can become liquid, but the truth is worse: The Liquifier can touch people and shift them into any phase state. When the villain hides as gas and infiltrates the heroes' improvised HQ, Jet cannot phase like Ghost Cat, Leon, and Skelebot, so he is vaporized. The conflict becomes whether Jet can stay conscious as vapor long enough to use lucidity itself as a rescue strategy.

## Theme

A lucid dreamer wins by understanding the hidden rule of the world and turning that rule into a strategy.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alert and alone, feeling the pressure of a superhero world that has already lost most of its protectors. The surviving phasing heroes give him a narrow sense of hope, but the gas infiltration proves that the villain has been inside their safe place the whole time. Jet's vaporization becomes the low point, then turns into a breakthrough when he realizes the missing heroes are still present in the clouds as gaseous consciousness. His fear becomes focused command as Lucid Lightning restores the city's heroes. The ending feels like a classic comic-book team finish: one hero pulls the villain into position, another contains the threat, and Jet sees the world saved by clear strategy.

## Ending

Jet stimulates the cloud of vaporized heroes with purple Lucid Lightning. Each lightning strike restores heroic essence and returns a hero to the city. Sucker, a humanoid lollipop hero with super breath, inhales The Liquifier as the villain tries to escape in gas form. At the last second Void Man, a pitch-black energy hero with a pocket dimension in his chest, jumps between them and absorbs The Liquifier into the void. The final page shows the restored heroes gathered in the city while Jet stands beneath clearing storm clouds, knowing the dream played out like the most direct kind of superhero comic.
