# Character Bible - The Vengeful Swarm

## Jet

- Black man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hood.
- Wears an open black hoodie over a clearly visible colorful “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic but natural build. No crown, cape, mask, helmet, conventional superhero costume, armor, or wings.
- Expression range: alert curiosity, controlled alarm, physical strain, frustrated concentration, sudden realization, solemn accountability, gentle compassion, and relieved peace.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy filled with tiny stars, like a living night sky.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hooded outfit, shirt, pants, sneakers, and belt bag on every page.

## Psilones - Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life

- One consistent living futuristic rainbow crystal sword.
- Broad translucent faceted blade containing saturated red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet bands; dark silver geometric technological hilt; radiant rainbow-white aura; tiny flowing sparks inside the blade.
- Possesses its own life, will, and autonomous movement. It can hover and act without Jet touching it.
- Its life-giving strike is non-gory: warm rainbow-white energy with gold and green currents flows into the target and animates it.
- Never depict Psilones as a metal broadsword, katana, ordinary fantasy sword, double-bladed weapon, or another character's weapon.

## Individual ghost bugs

- Countless recognizable spectral insects of many species: roaches, beetles, ants, moths, mantises, dragonflies, bees, grasshoppers, and others.
- Each is translucent and luminous in ghostly cyan, mint green, violet, and white, with soft trailing wisps and tiny glowing eyes.
- Their swarm moves like a supernatural liquid wave while individual insect silhouettes remain visible.
- They are frightening through scale and motion, not gore. No blood, exposed anatomy, rot, realistic infestation filth, or body horror.

## The ghost-bug humanoid

- A single six-to-seven-foot athletic humanoid body composed entirely of thousands of interlocking spectral insects; never gigantic and never taller than Jet by more than about one foot.
- Sleek but visibly swarm-built silhouette with translucent teal, cyan, violet, and green exoskeletal plates formed from overlapping insect bodies; long agile limbs; claw-like hands; bright compound-eye face; streams of small ghost bugs constantly orbiting and rejoining it.
- Moves with explosive insect speed and strength. Its posture is coordinated, intelligent, and purposeful rather than mindlessly monstrous.
- Creates technology by directing insect spirits into intricate engineering patterns. It never carries weapons and never changes into a separate creature.
- Continuity rule: keep the same height, luminous color palette, compound-eye face, insect-built anatomy, and athletic proportions on every appearance.

## The inhibitor chip

- A small black hexagonal computer chip with bright acid-green circuitry and a pulsing violet center.
- Initially sits embedded cleanly at the back of Jet's neck with no blood or gore.
- When Psilones gives it life, it pulls free, unfolds six tiny mechanical legs, and scuttles away like a harmless robotic beetle.
- Never depict it as a large device, collar, helmet, needle, bomb, or organic parasite.

## The blessing

- A warm spiritual radiance distinct from Lucid Light: concentric gold-white rings, gentle amber rays, and soft prismatic sparks flowing from Jet's open hand through the humanoid.
- Every insect spirit inside the collective receives the blessing. Their aggressive eyes soften, their ghostly colors brighten, and their forms rise upward like peaceful stars.
- The afterlife appears as a radiant opening in the dark dream sky, not a religious figure or specific denominational symbol.

## Dream setting

- A vast indigo-black dreamspace with a glossy mist-covered ground, distant violet clouds, and no ordinary buildings or landscape.
- During the repeating struggle, fractured panels and pale bedroom glimpses may show Jet briefly waking before dropping back into the same dreamspace.
- The final blessing transforms the threatening darkness into a calm starry dawn with a gold-white opening above.

## Continuity exclusions

- Jet's established crew—Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper—does not physically appear in this focused dream.
- Tetheron is mentioned only as an available but unsuitable tool and does not appear in the artwork.
- Do not add other heroes, ministers, angels, deities, demons, soldiers, bystanders, or named characters.
