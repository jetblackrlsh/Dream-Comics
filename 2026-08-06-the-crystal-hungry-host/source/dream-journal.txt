Dream Journal Reconstruction

The dream began at a nightclub where visitors had to choose a host who would hang out with them and act like a friend while they were there. All of the hosts were humanoid dragons. Each dragon was a different color, had a unique power and personality, and had a tome displayed in front of them. The tome explained who that host was and provided information that guests could read before deciding which one they wanted.

As I looked through the different options, I noticed that one of the humanoid dragon hosts was actually a robot. Its tome explained that it could be customized and that it was effectively an AI robot companion. I immediately thought that this was clearly the best option.

The other dragon hosts became annoyed by my reaction. They complained that they constantly had to compete against the robot and that everyone considered it the best because it was so customizable. They argued that they had real lived experience that the robot did not have, along with great personal stories that they could tell.

I responded that the robot’s entire experience came from working at the nightclub. Since it effectively worked there twenty-four hours a day, it probably had countless stories about the club and had likely seen everything that could possibly happen there. Even when the other hosts were asleep or off work, the robot remained on the clock. I also thought its customizable personality was a major advantage because I could shape it into my perfect host. Altogether, that made it very difficult to beat.

The other dragon hosts then told me that if I chose the robot, they would effectively go hungry. They said it was already extremely difficult for them to get work while competing against it, and that choosing it would contribute to screwing them over. I felt bad about that.

I remembered that a couple of nights earlier, I had performed a heist in another dream and stolen a large amount of molten gold from a dragon. During that dream, I had learned that dragons actually ate gold and did not value it only as money. Since I still had access to that molten gold, I used my Lucid Light to call out to Leon, my ghostly green UFO. Leon dropped several cups filled with molten gold, and I gave one cup to each of the dragon hosts I had not selected so that none of them would go hungry. After feeding them, I entered the nightclub with the robot host.

Inside the club, I was able to enter a VIP lounge where I could relax and talk privately with my host. As I sat down with the robot, I noticed three magical stones embedded in its chest: one red, one green, and one blue. The robot announced that it needed to recharge and explained that it was powered by magical stones.

It then suddenly lunged toward Silonis, the rainbow crystal sword I carry in my dreams and one of the most powerful artifacts in the Storyverse. I realized that because the robot needed to recharge, and because Silonis was made from magical crystal, it was trying to eat the sword.

I used my Lucid Light to cloak Silonis and make the sword appear to be made from ordinary metal rather than magical stone. However, the robot’s internal sensors could see through the illusion. It still recognized that the sword was made from the type of crystal it could consume for power.

Once I realized I could not trick the robot into abandoning its pursuit of Silonis, I used geomancy, one of the first powers I ever learned during my early Storyverse adventures. Through geomancy, I removed the red, green, and blue stones embedded in the robot’s chest. Removing all three stones was like taking the batteries out of a machine, and the robot immediately powered down.

I carried the three stones back to the dragon hosts and explained what had happened. Since I did not know what else to do with the stones, I gave them to the dragons. They seemed enthusiastic because, with the robot host powered down, they no longer had to compete against it for customers.

I still felt bad for the robot because it had seemed genuinely cool. It had not attacked me out of simple malice; it had been drawn to Silonis because, unlike the other dragons who ate gold, the robot consumed magical crystals. Afterward, I flew away into the Storyverse, and that was the end of the dream.
