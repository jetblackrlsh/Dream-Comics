# Character Bible - The Device Beyond Time

## Jet

- Black adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears a black hoodie over a colorful “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each outer leg, clean white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build; thoughtful and analytical in the study, alarmed during the glitch, and focused while fighting to regain his identity.
- Keep his skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, pants stripes, shoes, and cross-body bag visually consistent on every page featuring his body.

## Adapt Tech

- Handheld T-shaped futuristic device, sized like a compact tool rather than a sword.
- Dark silver metallic body, horizontal scanner head, bright red scanning aperture, small cyan status lights, and no visible keyboard.
- Can project clean red scan beams and cyan holographic technical diagrams.
- Every temporal user holds the same core design, with only subtle era-appropriate wear or casing accents.
- It is not a smartphone, pistol, hammer, or ordinary sword hilt.

## Silonis - Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life

- Long futuristic crystal sword with a faceted rainbow blade, luminous prismatic edges, an advanced metallic hilt, and a bright red scanner gem.
- Releases sparkling rainbow life energy rather than fire.
- It is a physical living-crystal blade, not a lightsaber.

## Crystalline glitch

- Blocky blue, brown, and green crystal-like corruption made from small geometric cubes and fractured facets.
- Spreads from the unattached Adapt Tech device across Jet's arm, torso, and body without blood or gore.
- Creates doubled digital afterimages and broken spatial fragments while leaving Jet recognizable.

## Temporal Adapt Tech users

The women are distinct people in different eras, not alternate costumes for one visible body. During spirit-hopping, use a subtle violet-white glow in their eyes or around the device to show Jet's connected awareness.

- Ancient desert scholar: adult dark-brown-skinned woman with long micro-braids, a cream-and-gold scholarly robe, turquoise jewelry, and a sandstone observatory filled with star maps.
- Renaissance engineer: adult light-brown-skinned woman with auburn hair in a braided bun, a deep blue dress with bronze tool harness, and a workshop of gears and drawn machines.
- Edo-era researcher: adult East Asian woman with black hair in a neat bun, a slate-blue kimono, and a lamplit archive of scrolls and mechanical curiosities.
- Far-future technician: adult brown-skinned woman with a short cobalt-blue bob, an indigo utility bodysuit, and a luminous city laboratory.
- Keep every woman nonsexualized, capable, curious, and visually distinct. Some investigate the device, some use it to help others, and some flee unseen pursuers.

## Magical study

- Vast private study combining ancient magic and advanced technology: towering bookshelves, open grimoires, brass instruments, crystal specimens, cyan holographic screens, and circular purple runes across a dark polished floor.
- Warm amber lamps contrast with cyan technology, sparkling purple Lucid Light, and Silonis's rainbow glow.
- Avoid random readable decorative text.

## Lucid Light and temporal connection

- Lucid Light resembles sparkling purple energy shining like a star-filled night sky.
- Temporal connections appear as fine violet-white threads joining multiple Adapt Tech devices through darkness, clocks, star fields, and translucent layers of different eras.
- The timeline network should feel mysterious and beautiful, not like a conventional computer diagram.

## Continuity rules

- All eight pages use bright, high-saturation modern-anime comic art with crisp linework and cinematic light.
- Story pages use unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes only.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, signatures, gore, extra limbs, duplicate unintended characters, or blank caption boxes.
