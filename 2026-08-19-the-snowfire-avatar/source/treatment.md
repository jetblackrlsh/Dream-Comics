# Treatment - The Snowfire Avatar

## Logline

When Jet's peaceful snowy sanctuary is invaded by demons he accidentally carries across from Hell, a shocking attack reveals that his lucid-dream body is a robotic avatar—and gives him the power to turn an army of frozen monsters into a prismatic fireworks display.

## Premise

Jet wants another quiet place to think without returning to the magical study from his recent dreams. His dream creates a wall-less living room in a snowy wilderness: a purple couch beside a magical fire pit whose warmth holds back impossible cold.

While resting there, Jet reflects on his fascination with role-playing games. AI lets him play whenever he wants, but human role-play requires coordination, wanted advertisements, and unanswered MUSH mail. He wonders whether writing in-character letters could remain meaningful even without replies, becoming a solo role-playing game of its own.

## Central conflict

Jet begins to suspect that the new setting is only the magical study's coziness in a different form. To test it, he snaps the snow into a hellscape. Flaming demons charge toward him. When he snaps the winter back, the demons remain and are instantly frozen.

The result reveals the environment's extreme power: the cold can freeze hellfire, while the fire pit can protect Jet from that same cold.

## Discovery

Jet discovers that the fire is bonded to him. He can hold it without being burned and carry its warmth beyond the couch. The frozen demons resemble statues until his handheld flame passes too close to one face. The demon's head thaws, and it bites away Jet's human exterior.

There is no gore beneath the missing surface—only a clean robotic face. Jet realizes that this body is an avatar piloted by his consciousness while his waking body remains elsewhere.

## Escalation and resolution

Jet tests the avatar's abilities by charging the diodes in his mechanical eyes. His laser refracts through a frozen demon and detonates it. He then aims at the center of the cluster, using every ice-encased body as another prism.

The snowy forest erupts into a cascading spectacle of lasers, ice crystals, color, flame, and light. The dream ends not in horror but in celebration: the cold becomes a weapon, the bonded fire becomes protection, and the damaged avatar reveals a new power.

## Theme

The dream transforms uncertainty into play. Waiting can become letter-writing, danger can become experimentation, injury can become discovery, and an army of enemies can become the structure of a beautiful solution.
