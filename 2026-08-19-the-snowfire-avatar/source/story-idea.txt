# Story Idea - The Snowfire Avatar

While relaxing on a purple couch beside a magical fire pit in a snowy wilderness, Jet reflects on online role-playing, MUSH correspondence, and whether unanswered in-character letters could become their own solo game. He tests whether his sanctuary is too cozy by transforming the landscape into Hell, then restores the snow and flash-freezes the charging fire demons. A bonded flame lets him explore safely, but accidentally thaws one demon's face. Its bite reveals that Jet's dream body is a robotic avatar. He activates eye lasers, refracts them through the frozen demons, and transforms the danger into a triumphant fireworks display of ice, flame, and prismatic light.
