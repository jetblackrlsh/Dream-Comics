Dream journal entry dated 06/19/2026.

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse, a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025.

My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I.

My Ghostly UFO, named Leon, resembles a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025.

Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit doesn't have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025.

Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds.

Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color with spiky slicked back yellow hair and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025.

Lucid Light is a reality warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered the power of Lucid Light in a dream on 07/19/2025.

Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah is a pale skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that covered her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She wields a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword.

Tecton is Savannah's father: a green skinned, humanoid, muscular, cat ogre, barbarian who dresses in a brown loin cloth and a silver baldric.

Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot.

Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper all met me in early 2026 while adventuring through the Super Cathedral. They became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, and warm brown eyes. I have a rainbow sword, which resembles a colorful futuristic crystal sword, and an energy tether, which resembles a rope made of golden glittery light.

I went to sleep feeling a little dejected, as it seems like most people don't care too much about what I create in the waking world.

A beautiful young woman with an hourglass figure, curly silver permed hair, a shiny green and blue jumpsuit, and piercing blue eyes approaches me.

She offers me fruit called "Num Num Berries" which make you feel whatever you want to feel.

I eat some berries and find myself in a trance.

Second Brain yells out for me to wake up.

My trance is interrupted as I see the woman who offered me the berries is now quite old and actively trying to eat my soul.

Second Brain fires a bolt of Lucid Lightning, a neuro shock, that defeats the succubus hag.

But the berries weren't done twisting my mind.

I get lost in a trance loop where I spend a lot of time with a doctor named Odin, who I seem to trust.

But I start to notice that Odin isn't as smart as I believed him to be, that my own perceptions weren't trustworthy, and that Odin was trying to use my trust to kill me.

I realize that Odin is a hallucination caused by the berries and I am forced to fight him and flee away from him until the effect of the berries wears off.

Overall the dream was a wild ride.

I need to have a more positive mindset before going to sleep.

Negativity can root its way into your dreams and distort things.

I am glad that Second Brain was there to help me in my time of need.
