# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow crystal sword shaped like a colorful futuristic crystal blade, and a golden glittering energy tether that trails like a rope made of light. His Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy shining like a starry night sky. In this entry he is lucid, curious, appreciative of Leon, uneasy in vacant superhero architecture, and finally startled by the alien possession scene.

## Leon

Leon is Jet's Ghostly UFO: a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow, translucent silver-green hull, soft spectral rim lights, and a friendly presence. Leon should look like a vehicle, a companion, and a mobile headquarters all at once. When Jet surfs on top of Leon, Jet stands balanced on the saucer roof as Leon glides through the Storyverse. Leon's green glow should contrast with Jet's purple Lucid Light and golden energy tether.

## Storyverse

The Storyverse is a cosmic void filled with bubble universes, each containing possible story worlds. It should appear as deep space with many luminous bubble-like worlds drifting together, starry purple and blue ambience, and surreal scale. Jet and Leon travel through it freely.

## Lucid Light

Lucid Light is sparkling purple reality-warping energy filled with tiny star-like points. It appears around Jet during sleep paralysis induction, around his hands and body when lucid, and as a cosmic aura during Storyverse flight. It should feel magical, conscious, and dreamlike rather than technological.

## Rainbow Crystal Sword

Jet's rainbow sword is a colorful futuristic crystal sword. It can be worn on his back, held at his side, or gleaming near him as a visual identifier. It is not the main tool of this dream, but it should remain part of Jet's silhouette when possible.

## Golden Energy Tether

Jet's energy tether resembles a rope made of golden glittering light. It trails from him in dynamic arcs while surfing Leon, exploring Super Base World, or reacting to possessed dreamers.

## Superhero Headquarters Montage

The vacant headquarters should look intentionally empty rather than abandoned: clean command rooms, ceremonial halls, hangars, cave platforms, jungle watchtowers, Bermuda Triangle oceanic bunkers, moon base domes, space-station corridors, temples, and castles with no personal gear or hero emblems. The mood starts wondrous and becomes increasingly liminal.

## Mall World

Mall World is an infinite mall that many people around the world claim to dream of. Show endless tiled corridors, glass railings, escalators, storefronts with unreadable signage, atriums, skylights, vending machines, food-court lights, and distant dreamers. Avoid readable store names or logos.

## Super Base World

Super Base World is a secret superhero base inside Mall World that feels like every prior base smashed together into one. Its architecture combines cave stone, castle battlements, rainforest vines, oceanic Bermuda Triangle machinery, space-station metal, temple pillars, moon-base glass domes, training floors, launch tubes, command decks, and hallways that lead into impossible biomes. It should feel beautiful, functional, infinite, and wrong because it is empty.

## Antenna Aliens

The aliens appear as playful small beings or visible alien presences around possessed humans. They may be tiny hovering alien lights, small greenish cosmic mascots, or translucent controllers, but they should not look obviously evil. Their visible effect matters most: humans receive green skin, bright yellow antennae, excited expressions, and overwhelming super strength.

## Possessed Dreamers

Possessed dreamers are ordinary mall visitors transformed by alien control. They have green skin, bright yellow antennae, glowing eyes or excited smiles, and oversized strength effects such as bending steel rails, lifting mall kiosks, or punching through floor tiles. Their durability is mismatched: when Jet accidentally touches one, the person ragdolls dramatically like a loose action figure, without gore.

## Wider Storyverse Crew Context

Jet's broader team includes Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper. They are part of the continuity but are not required to appear in this dream's pages unless a background reference is useful. Keep the visible story focused on Jet and Leon.

## Visual Continuity Notes

Keep Jet's design consistent across every page: dark-brown skin, full beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, "Dream Anime" tee, black athletic pants with white stripe, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and purple Lucid Light. Keep Leon consistent as a ghostly green saucer with an ethereal glow. Do not add issue numbers to the cover or page art.
