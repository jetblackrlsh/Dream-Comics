# 07/24/2026 - Alpha Planet, Two Rainforests

Dream Comics entry for July 24, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet awakens at mouse size inside a vast corridor and is hunted by a long-haired purple tiger woman. He answers by constructing a colossal tiger mech from Lucid Light, only to be mistaken for her pack's Alpha. When the misunderstanding collapses, Jet's Rainbow Crystal Sword heals her claw attacks and creates a barrier that lets him escape. The planet itself then unfurls into the gigantic purple tiger king she serves.

Jet outruns the Alpha, opens an ordinary wooden door in space, and enters a blue-tinted rainforest where snow falls through tropical trees with rounded canopies. A brief waking interruption breaks the dream. When Jet returns and reconstructs the journey, the same rainforest has become an autumn world of red, orange, and yellow leaves. He enters its lava river, becomes a living god of fire and molten stone, and rises above both versions of the landscape as they overlap in his mind. The dream ends with Jet preserving the snowy and autumn rainforests together like photographs taken without a camera.
