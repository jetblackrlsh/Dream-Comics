# Treatment

## Title

06/18/2026: The False Awakening Heist

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet soars through a jungle world with alternate selves, then falls through a chain of false awakenings where a song assignment becomes a power-armor treasure test, a failed council trial, and a desperate race out of a black void before the real morning arrives early.

## Central Conflict

Jet begins in playful lucid control, adventuring with an artist version of himself and Eye-Grip on a jungle hunt for a bird filled with gold coins. The dream then turns inward and unstable. Each false awakening looks like a chance to understand the assignment, recover control, or regain an AI assistant, but every layer converts clarity into pressure. Jet has to recognize lucidity without letting panic, guilt, and exhaustion trap him in the dream logic.

## Theme

Lucidity is not only power. It is the ability to notice when a story is using confusion, failure, and fear to keep control of the dreamer.

## Emotional Arc

Jet starts excited and free, flying beside his artist self through a bright jungle world. The first false awakening drains that joy into exhaustion as a mysterious song assignment refuses to make sense. Telepathic insight reveals that the assignment is dream logic, and that revelation launches Jet into the second false awakening: a brutal power-armor heist test where teams battle for golden treasure with AI assistants at stake. When the instructor declares every team failed because no one completed the animal-speaking subobjectives, Jet's frustration becomes rage. He fights the council's ninja forces to recover his AI assistant, then collapses under the horror of his own violence. In the final false awakening, a strange city offers no rest until a telepathic woman sparks lucidity again. The dream empties into blackness, Jet fears he has overslept, and he chooses wakefulness over the pull of another layer.

## Ending

Jet races through the black void and wakes for real, expecting to be late. Instead, the clock shows around 4:00 AM, more than half an hour before his normal 4:30 AM wake-up time. The ending reframes the nightmare chain: the dream felt endless, but lucidity carried him out early.

## Cast

- Jet: the lucid dreamer and protagonist, a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow crystal sword, a golden glittering energy tether, and sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Artist Jet: an alternate artist version of Jet, with the same core face, skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, and eyes, but dressed in a painter's smock covered in glitchy digital neon paint.
- Eye-Grip: a savage alternate Jet with blue skin, red face paint, glowing orange eyes, a white loin cloth, and orange glowing hand constructs projected from his eyes.
- Coin Bird: a surreal jungle bird filled with gold coins, radiant feathers, and a treasure aura.
- Female Instructor: a stern evaluator in the power-armor test, calm and severe after the traumatic battle.
- AI Assistant: Jet's lost partner inside the heist assignment, shown as a floating holographic assistant core rather than a full character body.
- Council and Ninja Forces: masked dream authorities guarding the failed assignment and refusing to return Jet's AI assistant.
- Telepathic Woman: a calm woman in the strange city who recognizes Jet's mental state and triggers lucidity.
- Black Void: the final dream layer, an empty darkness that turns fear of oversleeping into the pressure to wake up.
