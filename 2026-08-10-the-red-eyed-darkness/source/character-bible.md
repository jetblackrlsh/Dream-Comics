# Character Bible - The Red-Eyed Darkness

## Jet

- African-American man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard and mustache, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Long dreadlocks spill from beneath a black hoodie worn over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt.
- Black athletic pants with one clean white stripe down each outer leg, white high-top sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build, expressive brows, compassionate confidence, and a decisive posture.
- Flies under his own power.

## Silonis

- Jet's Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life and the most powerful artifact in the Storyverse.
- Futuristic sword made from large, faceted, translucent crystals spanning the full rainbow spectrum.
- Radiates prismatic life energy that purifies evil on contact.
- Wherever the blade's light touches the black corruption, it becomes brilliant white, develops luminous cracks, and shatters into clean crystal-like fragments.

## Lucid Light

- Reality-warping energy wielded by Jet.
- Sparkling purple light textured like a deep night sky filled with bright stars.
- On the final pages, Jet shapes it into a large rotating drill or spiral around his upward-reaching body, boring through earth without changing his clothing or physical appearance.

## The red-eyed water tower and darkness

- Begins as a huge industrial black water tower supported by tall metal legs, with one unnatural black eye embedded in its rounded tank.
- Grows to monstrous city-dominating scale; the eye becomes enormous, crimson-red, luminous, and vertically slit.
- Controls glossy psychic darkness resembling a living symbiote made of smoke, liquid tar, tendrils, and reaching hands.
- The same identifiable darkness later returns inside the underground home, descending from the ceiling and surface roots.
- Never becomes a separate humanoid villain. Its identity remains the eye, the tower silhouette, and the connected black tendrils.

## Underground home

- Warm, block-built subterranean refuge inspired by an old Minecraft survival home without using game logos or interface elements.
- Contains a crafting table, red-and-white bed, wooden chests, furnace, refrigerator, shelves, and torchlit stone-and-earth walls.
- One wooden doorway leads into a mine tunnel containing dirt, thorny green berry bushes with bright red berries, wooden supports, rails, and torches separated by ominous shadows.
- The home feels safe, familiar, handmade, and nostalgic until the darkness intrudes.

## Continuity rules

- Jet's skin tone, beard, long dreadlocks, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white shoes, cross-body bag, and Silonis remain consistent on every page.
- Silonis keeps the same faceted rainbow crystal form and never becomes a conventional metal sword.
- The darkness is always glossy black with faint red internal sparks before purification; purified sections are brilliant white with luminous cracks.
- Lucid Light is always sparkling purple and star-filled.
- No gore, speech bubbles, thought balloons, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, creator signatures, or blank caption boxes.
