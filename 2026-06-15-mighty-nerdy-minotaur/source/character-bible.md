# Character Bible

## Jet, Usual Dream Identity

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks. His usual dream outfit is a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. These details can appear in the sleep paralysis transition and as recognizable fragments on his werewolf form.

## Jet, Farm Werewolf Form

For most of this comic, Jet is a tall athletic werewolf with black fur, clean white tiger-like stripes across his body and muzzle, green serpent eyes with slit pupils, a strong wolf snout, pointed ears, sharp but controlled claws, and long dark-brown dreadlocks flowing like a mane from the transformed head. Fragments of his black hoodie, "Dream Anime" tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and cross-body belt bag remain visible as dream-clothing echoes. His rainbow crystal sword is a colorful futuristic crystal blade used for healing, not aggression. His golden energy tether can coil at his waist or trail behind him.

## Lucid Light

Lucid Light is sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. In this story it appears first as a sleep paralysis gateway, then as an aura around Jet's muscles while he tries to match the minotaur's strength. It should look magical and cosmic, not electric.

## Rainbow Crystal Sword

Jet's rainbow crystal sword is a colorful futuristic crystal blade with prismatic light. During the fight, it glows softly and sends rainbow healing light through strained muscles, visibly strengthening Jet as the clash continues. It is not used to cut or wound the minotaur.

## The Mighty Nerdy Minotaur

The Mighty Nerdy Minotaur is a towering muscular heroic minotaur with broad shoulders, tan-brown fur, massive curved horns, square glasses, intelligent expressive eyes, and a confident defender's stance. His outfit blends farm hero and scholar: reinforced leather bracers, a blue sweater vest or academic harness over simple heroic armor pieces, a utility belt with pencils, notebooks, rolled star charts, and a small telescope. He is far stronger than Jet at first, but his face shifts from stern protectiveness to delighted curiosity when the topic becomes multiverse theory.

## Humanoid Cow Villagers

The humanoid cows are upright cow people in practical farm clothes: overalls, straw hats, aprons, plaid shirts, and work boots. They should look frightened but sympathetic, not monstrous. They panic at Jet's werewolf appearance, call for their hero, then gather around with relief and curiosity after the fight ends.

## Farm World

The farm is a bright Storyverse bubble world with golden fields, red barns, wooden fences, hay bales, a windmill, dirt paths, and rolling hills. The sky can contain subtle cosmic hints: faint translucent bubble universes, violet stars, or a dreamlike shimmer showing that the farm is one world inside the Storyverse.

## Visual Continuity Notes

Keep Jet's werewolf form consistent after the cover: black fur, white stripes, green serpent eyes, long dark-brown dreadlock mane, athletic build, dream outfit fragments, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and purple Lucid Light. Keep the minotaur consistent: huge tan-brown body, curved horns, glasses, blue academic vest or harness, leather bracers, notes and star-chart accessories.
