# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: lucid dreamer, solo explorer, and protagonist.
- Age range: adult man.
- Skin and face: dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, expressive heroic face that moves from curiosity to danger to amused confidence.
- Hair: long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie; the dreadlocks must remain visible whenever Jet appears.
- Outfit: black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag.
- Signature props: rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glittering energy tether, sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Aegis spell: a glowing golden-orange shield shaped like a turtle shell, with hexagonal shell plates and a curved protective dome. It can be held as a shield or projected outward to trap an enemy inside a dome.
- Rainbow sword healing: the rainbow crystal sword shines with gentle healing color when zombie magic scratches Jet, protecting his mind and transforming the curse into lion strength.
- Lion strength state: Jet remains visually himself, but his stance becomes powerful and feline, his eyes shine with heroic gold, and faint golden lion-aura shapes appear around his arms and shoulders.
- Continuity rule: do not remove the beard, dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime tee, belt bag, rainbow sword, or golden tether. Aegis should always read as golden-orange turtle-shell magic, not a generic force field.

## Mounted Dragon Head

- Role: first active threat and Aegis demonstration.
- Appearance: huge mounted dragon head on the castle wall, ancient dark red and bronze scales, curved horns, smoke-blackened teeth, glowing ember eyes, iron wall bracket, and scorched trophy plaque with unreadable marks.
- Attack: breathes bright orange fire in a cone across the gallery.
- Defeat: trapped inside Jet's projected golden-orange turtle-shell dome and burned by its own fire, becoming charred and defeated without gore.

## Mounted Mythical Creature Heads

- Role: silent witnesses and implied wider danger.
- Appearance: ornate trophy heads mounted on the stone walls, including griffin, hydra, serpent, unicorn, chimera, wolf, stag, and bird-like monsters. Their glassy eyes glow with old magic.
- Behavior: they watch Jet defeat the dragon and choose not to attack.
- Continuity rule: show them as intelligent living trophies, not ordinary museum decorations.

## Zombie Lion Mounts

- Role: main physical attackers.
- Mounted form: multiple lion trophy heads with torn manes, cracked teeth, cloudy eyes, and green corpse-light inside the mouths.
- Regenerated form: full undead lion bodies grow out from the mounted heads, with spectral green seams, torn golden-brown fur, exposed stone dust and magic cracks without gore, and glowing hungry eyes.
- Magic: claws scratch with zombie curse energy that appears as green-black smoke and tooth-shaped runes. The magic tries to make Jet mindless and hungry, but the rainbow sword's healing light transforms it into heroic lion strength.
- Defeat: knocked across the gallery, through windows, and into stone walls by Jet's amplified punches, sword slashes, and kicks.

## Castle Trophy Gallery

- Role: mysterious dream arena and final peaceful gallery.
- Look: grand stone castle room with high arches, moonlit windows, old red banners, carved columns, polished floor, trophy plaques with unreadable markings, and walls packed with mounted mythical creature heads.
- Lighting: start with cool moonlight and warm torchlight, shift to intense firelight and golden Aegis glow during battle, then settle into quiet blue moonlight and rainbow reflections after victory.
- Continuity rule: keep the room recognizable across pages: the same long gallery, same wall of mounted heads, same windows, and same stone architecture.
