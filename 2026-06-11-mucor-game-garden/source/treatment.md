# Treatment

## Title

06/11/2026: Mucor's Game Garden

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet explores an infinite empty castle, befriends Mucor, a slime-frog who wants a slime room, survives a drift into a world of death games, and transforms the castle courtyard into a garden where play creates new life.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to use the infinite castle's empty space meaningfully, but his lucidity slips into a violent world where games are built around peril. He must reassert lucid control and choose what kind of game his dream will become.

## Theme

Play can either consume life or cultivate it. A lucid dream becomes healthier when imagination turns conflict into creativity and competition into growth.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alone in a vast empty castle, curious but unsure what to build. Meeting Mucor gives the castle warmth and personality. When the dream drifts into deadly games, Jet's urgency and compassion restore his lucidity. He returns with a clearer answer: the castle should become a place where games make the world more alive.

## Ending

Jet converts the castle courtyard into the Game Garden, where cloth spell bags scatter seeds whenever they break. He and Mucor enjoy a playful LARP magic battle, leaving behind the death-game world for a new rule: every spell helps the garden bloom.
