07/27/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025.

My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I. My Ghostly UFO, named Leon, resembles a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025. Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit does not have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025. Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds.

Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color, spiky slicked-back yellow hair, and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025. Lucid Light is a reality-warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered the power of Lucid Light in a dream on 07/19/2025. Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah is a pale-skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that cover her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She wields a futuristic purple-and-blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword. Her father Tecton is a green-skinned, humanoid, muscular cat-ogre barbarian who dresses in a brown loincloth and a silver baldric. My drone Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot. All three met me in early 2026 while I was adventuring through the Super Cathedral. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a “Dream Anime” graphic tee. I wear black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. I have warm brown eyes. I carry a rainbow sword that resembles a colorful futuristic crystal sword and an energy tether resembling a rope made of golden glittery light.

I found myself flying above a dark, blocky planet that appeared to be constructed from a strange reflective material. Its surface resembled a vast world of black mirrors, divided into countless individual cubes. From within every cube shone an eerie red light, giving the entire planet a sinister internal glow. Even from high above it, I could feel menace radiating from the world.

As I approached the planet, I intuitively understood what it was. This was a world inhabited by nightmares—a place where evil and terror lived.

A towering being rose into the air behind me. I recognized him as the King of Nightmares. His entire body was formed from burnt-black vines and thorny branches twisted tightly together into a humanoid shape. A crown of curling vines rested upon his head, and two glowing red eyes stared out from his dark form. Their light perfectly matched the red glow shining from the cubes below us.

The King of Nightmares told me that, if I truly wanted to save people that night, I should destroy the planet. He claimed that the world below us was filled with nightmare beings that harmed people while they slept. Nightmares were often treated as things that were merely frightening, but some nightmares could be genuinely lethal. They could cause people to sleepwalk, leaving them vulnerable to walking into traffic, falling down stairs, or wandering unknowingly into other dangerous situations.

According to him, destroying the planet would prevent those deaths. He framed the situation as a simple heroic mission: if I wanted to be a hero, I needed to wipe out the entire nightmare world.

However, I was lucid, and I could see through the ruse.

I knew that we were in the Storyverse, a multiverse composed of an infinite number of cosmic bubble universes containing every possible kind of story. There was no way this single planet could be the source of every nightmare. It was only one nightmare world among an infinite number of others. Even if I destroyed it, nightmares would continue to exist elsewhere.

If I dedicated myself to eradicating every nightmare world in the Storyverse, the task would never end. The King of Nightmares was presenting me with a false mission—one that could not solve the actual problem.

When I explained this, he immediately tried to manipulate me.

“So you're just going to let people die?” he demanded.

He accused me of being a false hero. If people were being killed by nightmares and I refused to destroy the planet, then according to him, their deaths would be my responsibility.

By that point, I was tired of his games and began considering how I might defeat him. Because his body was composed of burnt vines, I wondered whether he might still be vulnerable to fire. Using my Lucid Light, I ignited his thorny body with pyrokinetic flames.

At first, the King of Nightmares screamed and thrashed as though the fire were hurting him. For a moment, it seemed as if my attack was working.

Then he suddenly lunged toward me.

His burning vines wrapped around my body, and his thorns dug painfully into my skin. As he held me in place, he mocked my reasoning.

“Why would you think a being made from burnt vines would be vulnerable to fire?” he asked. “If fire could destroy me, why would the vines be burnt instead of reduced to ashes?”

As the thorns cut deeper into me, I reached toward the Rainbow Crystal Sword secured across my back. The sword was also known as the Life Sword, and it was one of the most powerful artifacts in the entire Storyverse.

Before I could draw it, one of the King of Nightmares' vines wrapped around the weapon and began pulling it away from me.

The idea of the Life Sword falling into the possession of the King of Nightmares was a worst-case scenario. Its power was far too great to allow someone like him to control it.

Fortunately, I still had my Golden Energy Tether. Unlike the Life Sword, the tether was not an incredibly rare or famous Storyverse artifact, so the King of Nightmares had ignored it.

I summoned the tether and threw it like a lasso, wrapping it around the Rainbow Crystal Sword before the King of Nightmares could pull the weapon completely out of my reach.

The Golden Energy Tether was more than a physical rope. Its true ability was to create mystical connections and bonds between different people and objects. Although I was not yet holding the sword in my hand, the connection created between the tether, the sword, and me allowed me to access the sword's power.

The Life Sword possessed several abilities. It could be used as a conventional weapon, allowing me to slash through enemies. It could manipulate life energy, and it could also generate powerful force fields.

Using the bond created by the tether, I released an explosive pulse of energy from the sword.

A force field expanded outward around me, shattering the thorny vines that had wrapped around my body. At the same time, the sword's life energy flowed through my injuries, healing the cuts left behind by the thorns.

The King of Nightmares was thrown backward by the force of the blast. With his grip broken, I pulled the Rainbow Crystal Sword fully into my hand.

I then turned toward the nightmare planet and raised the sword.

The King of Nightmares initially believed that he had won. He thought I was about to use the sword's power to slash through the world and destroy it. That was what he had wanted from the beginning—not because destroying the planet would eliminate all nightmares, but because it would corrupt me. He wanted to manipulate a hero into committing an act so extreme and destructive that it could never truly be justified.

But I did not use the Life Sword to destroy the nightmare world.

Instead, I used its life energy as a force of purification.

Pure life energy poured from the sword and washed across the planet. Rather than burning the nightmare beings to ash, I transformed them. One by one, the nightmares became dreams.

The entire structure of the world changed around us. The black reflective cubes dissolved into brilliant white clouds illuminated by glowing golden light. The oppressive red glow vanished, replaced by warmth and radiance.

As the newly purified dreams rose from the transformed world, they gathered around me. No longer servants or victims of the King of Nightmares, they joined me in fighting against him.

Together, the dreams overwhelmed the Nightmare King.

They tore his body apart until the twisted mass of burnt vines and thorns was completely dismantled. The remaining pieces scattered through the cosmic void of the Storyverse, drifting away in every direction.

I knew that this probably was not the permanent end of him. The King of Nightmares was an archetypal being, and beings like him could not necessarily be killed in the ordinary sense. Eventually, his scattered thorns would likely find one another, and he would reconstitute himself.

But reforming would take time.

Every moment he remained scattered gave the dreams—and heroes who fought on behalf of dreams—more time to continue protecting people and doing what was right.
