On this new world, there is a utopia of humans where no one receives special treatment. Everyone there treats everyone with equal respect and kindness.

A young orphan troll finds their way into the utopia and is accepted with open arms by the people.

The troll grows up there and is treated just as well as everyone else.

A dark elf arrives in the city and demands to be worshipped, because the elf is immortal and possesses vast magical powers.

The people of the utopia refuse, because they treat everyone equally.

Insulted by their defiance, the dark elf casts a spell that brainwashes all of the humans in the utopia.

The dark elf's magic only works on humans, so the troll confronts the dark elf and requests that he dispel his brainwashing magic at once.

The troll is physically stronger than the dark elf, but the dark elf is clever.

The dark elf pulls out a Universe Splitter device. The Universe Splitter divides the universe based on choices.

The elf puts in two options and activates the device. The universe splits into one world where the dark elf surrenders and retreats, and another world where the dark elf does not.

The way the split resolves, it seems as if their world is the one where the dark elf does not retreat.

The dark elf tells the troll that they should be happy that there exists another universe where the dark elf leaves quietly.

The troll is at first confused on what to do, because technically the world he wants exists out in the multiverse somewhere even though his world is still enslaved.

The troll refuses to give up on his own world and grabs the Universe Splitter.

The troll activates the Universe Splitter again, creating one world where the troll banishes himself in shame and another where he uppercuts the dark elf to the other side of the planet.

The way the split resolves is with their world being the one where the troll uppercuts the dark elf.

The dark elf's spell is broken due to him being too far away from the people he is trying to brainwash.

The humans of the city all turn back to normal, but they question what happened in the other universe where the troll banished himself in shame.

The troll assures them that in that world he likely went beyond the city to request the help of a hero's guild, and that the lesson he learned from the Universe Splitter is that there is always a way to save the day.

Life returned to normal.

The troll was not celebrated as a hero and there were no monuments made in his name.

He was treated like everyone else, and that is exactly how he wanted things to be.
