# Story Idea - The Device Beyond Time

While studying the futuristic hilt of Silonis, Jet identifies Adapt Tech as an everything device that learns by scanning ordinary machines and assimilates with magical crystals. His attempt to make a standalone unit copy the Rainbow Crystal blade causes a violent crystalline glitch, proving that magic cannot be scanned. After shutting the device down, Jet involuntarily inhabits women using Adapt Tech in different eras and realizes that the technology may not have been sent backward through time at all—it may exist loosely across the entire timeline, connecting every person who uses or even contemplates it.
