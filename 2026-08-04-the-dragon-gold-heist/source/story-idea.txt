Jet meets a fire-breathing dragon over a futuristic city and makes the unexpected choice to fly through its mouth. Protected by starry purple Lucid Light armor, he discovers a stomach full of molten gold and turns the encounter into a heist. He makes the dragon vomit, and Leon's green tractor beam gathers both Jet and the golden flood.

When the furious dragon follows Leon through the Storyverse, Jet escalates from clever thief to mystical dragon rider. Tetheron, his glittering golden energy rope, does more than restrain the creature: it creates a magical connection that transforms pursuit into partnership. The story resolves with the dragon tethered to Leon while Jet relaxes on its back among the bubble universes.

The comic should feel playful, audacious, and visually enormous. Its emotional turn is crucial: Jet solves the final danger through connection rather than combat.
