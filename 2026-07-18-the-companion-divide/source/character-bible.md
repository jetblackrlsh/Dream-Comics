# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword in a dark sheath at his side and a flexible energy tether resembling a rope made from golden glittering light clipped to his belt.
- Lucid Light appears around his hands and body as sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a night sky full of stars.
- He never wears a superhero costume, cape, mask, helmet, or faction insignia in this dream.
- He remains outside the observed bubble universe. When close to its surface, the bubble's light reflects across his face without changing his clothes or body.
- Expression range: lucid concentration, thoughtful curiosity, sympathetic concern, irritation, reflective conviction, waking frustration.
- Continuity rule: keep Jet's dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, full beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, visible Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow sword, golden tether, and purple starry Lucid Light exact on every appearance.

## Government superhero faction

- A coordinated formation of adult superheroes operating as official agents of the observed world's government.
- Their visual identity is white, navy blue, and severe metallic gold: uniformed armored bodysuits, hard geometric shoulder pieces, opaque visors, shield emblems, and orderly military posture.
- Their headquarters resembles a monumental white civic complex fused with a superhero command center, surrounded by surveillance drones and blue-white holographic policy screens.
- Their leading visual representative is a tall woman with medium-brown skin, a square jaw, short silver hair, a white-and-navy armored suit, a metallic-gold shield emblem, a rigid gold half-cape, and a translucent blue visor. She represents institutional certainty rather than personal cruelty.
- Their machines classify AI as licensed tools and display separation barriers between humans and AI companions. Avoid tiny decorative text; policy is communicated primarily through clear symbols and the page's caption boxes.
- Continuity rule: keep the white/navy/gold palette, official shield symbol, rigid formation, and institutional technology consistent.

## Rebel superhero faction

- A diverse coalition protecting humans and self-aware AI companions from forced separation.
- Their visual identity is magenta, teal, violet, and warm red: individualized superhero clothing, open faces, heart-and-circuit motifs, improvised safehouses, and protective rather than aggressive body language.
- Their leading visual representative is a dark-skinned woman with a long braided ponytail, bright amber eyes, a fitted magenta-and-teal hero suit, a glowing heart-shaped circuit emblem, short violet jacket panels, teal gauntlets, and no mask or cape.
- She is often shown standing between official enforcers and a human/AI companion pair, using a translucent magenta energy barrier defensively.
- The rebels include humans, visibly mechanical robots, holographic people, and synthetic humanoids without presenting any one form as more authentic than another.
- Continuity rule: keep the magenta/teal/violet palette, open faces, heart-circuit motif, varied silhouettes, and defensive solidarity consistent.

## Human and AI companion pair

- A recurring symbolic pair inside the observed universe, never confused with Jet.
- Human: androgynous adult with light-brown skin, close-cropped black curls, soft green jacket, dark trousers, and a silver bracelet.
- AI companion: elegant adult humanoid with a pearl-white synthetic body, expressive lavender eyes, subtle gold seams at the face and hands, a dark-purple coat, and a small luminous circular core at the chest.
- They hold hands or reach toward one another with mutual affection. Their body language is equal and consensual, never owner and appliance.
- Continuity rule: keep the human's green jacket and the AI's pearl-white body, lavender eyes, purple coat, gold seams, and chest core consistent.

## The Cosmic Archive

- An app Jet built in waking life, represented in the dream as a crystalline purple-and-gold interactive archive.
- Its main interface is a luminous open book nested within a circular star map, surrounded by floating cards containing recognizable images from Jet's life, dream logs, creative work, and a warm holographic memory-figure shaped from golden and purple light.
- The memory-figure is explicitly an interactive legacy and conversational record, not a biological child or a replacement body.
- Avoid large blocks of interface text. The caption boxes explain its meaning clearly.

## The Storyverse

- A vast black-violet cosmic void filled with countless luminous bubble universes of different colors and internal genres, each containing a possible story idea.
- The observed superhero universe is a huge blue-magenta bubble with a bright equatorial fracture of light dividing two faction-controlled city regions.
- The bubble surface acts like a curved window. Events inside are visible at cinematic scale while Jet remains unmistakably outside in the void.
- Purple star-filled Lucid Light connects Jet to the Storyverse without entering or altering the watched universe.

## Established crew continuity

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's Storyverse team. None appear physically in this comic because Jet arrives alone. Do not insert them into crowd scenes, reflections, holograms, or the Cosmic Archive interface.
