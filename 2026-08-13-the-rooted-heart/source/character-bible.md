# Character Bible - The Rooted Heart

## Jet

- African-American man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard and mustache, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Long dreadlocks spill from beneath a black hoodie worn over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt.
- Black athletic pants with one clean white stripe down each outer leg, white high-top sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build, compassionate expression, curious posture, and the ability to fly under his own power.

## The Rooting Hero - ordinary form

- Gender-ambiguous young adult with warm light-brown skin, an oval friendly face, short wavy brown hair, vivid green eyes, and understated features.
- Wears a moss-green casual jacket over a cream shirt, dark charcoal trousers, and brown ankle boots.
- Initially reads as slightly masculine. As the power transformation progresses, the same face, voice, and body proportions become more feminine while all identifying colors and features remain consistent.
- Reserved and startled while protecting their identity, then playful and confident while demonstrating their gift.

## The Rooting Hero - Sasquatch form

- The same vivid green eyes and face remain recognizable beneath thick, soft, warm-brown hair covering the body from shoulders to ankles.
- Friendly and magical rather than monstrous; slightly feminine silhouette after the transformation, sturdy athletic proportions, expressive hands and face.
- Multiple long prehensile hair strands can grow from the hands, back, and body as elegant whips or tethers.
- Glossy balloons in bright red, blue, yellow, green, and purple grow directly from the tips of individual hair strands.
- Rooted hair ends glow with fine golden branching lines. Unrooting and rerooting appear as seamless golden light, never damage or gore.

## Authorities and hospital community

- City authorities wear clean navy-and-silver public-safety uniforms and react with caution rather than aggression.
- Hospital staff wear bright modern scrubs and white coats.
- The children are diverse in age, skin tone, mobility aids, and treatment needs; their reactions are joyful and unafraid.

## Key visual motifs

- Hair is warm brown, flexible, and organic, never tentacles or cables.
- Balloons are glossy, saturated, and visibly attached to hair tips.
- Rooting magic is golden branching light that restores structures and living tissue perfectly.
- The city remains contemporary and intact before and after every demonstration.
- The transplant is represented symbolically through radiant heart-shaped light and seamless golden roots, with no exposed organs, blood, incisions, or gore.

## Continuity rules

- Jet's skin tone, beard, long dreadlocks, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white shoes, and cross-body bag remain consistent.
- The hero's green eyes, brown hair, moss-green and cream clothing accents, and warm-brown Sasquatch hair remain consistent through every form.
- The hero's gender presentation may shift gradually, but the person must remain visibly recognizable.
- No gore, speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, creator signatures, or blank caption boxes.
