# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and can summon a golden glittering energy tether. He wields Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he is alone at first, cautious with the rat-dog, firm with the pickle warlord, and intrigued by the production company interruption.

## Lucid Light

Reality-warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Lucid Light forms the containment orb around the rat-dog, pops like a magical bubble when released, and later reshapes the creature's body and cybernetic eyes. It should feel cosmic, precise, and dreamlike.

## White Rat-Dog

A strange creature with white fur, a rat head, dog teeth, a doglike body, and round solid crimson eyes. It is low to the ground, sinewy, fast, and unpredictable. Its first posture is docile and sniffing, but its aggression returns suddenly with bared teeth and lunging claws.

Continuity notes: Before transformation, the creature should look unsettling but not huge: a strange animal that could fit inside a floating Lucid Light orb like a hamster ball.

## Tinker Beast

The transformed version of the white rat-dog. It now has dark gray fur, glowing green cybernetic eyes, a calmer and more intelligent expression, a brown work apron, and the ability to walk on two legs. It keeps the rat head, dog teeth, and doglike body structure, but its posture is upright and practical.

Continuity notes: The green cybernetic eyes visibly soothe its mind. The brown apron and tinkerer posture are key identifiers. It should feel like a newly advanced ally, not a separate creature.

## Pickle Warlord

A villainous humanoid pickle in a medieval purple button-up shirt. He has a green brined body, angry narrow eyes, a tiny crown-like warlord helm, boots, gloves, and theatrical villain poses. He believes villainy deserves to rule the world and repeatedly tries to recruit Jet.

Continuity notes: The warlord should look absurd but still committed to his own evil speech. His first body is the strongest version; each clone that appears later is smaller, wobblier, and less threatening than the last.

## Pickle Clones

Copies of the Pickle Warlord that multiply every time Jet defeats him. Each clone is weaker than the previous clone, with softer posture, smaller weapons, wobblier limbs, and less confident expressions.

Continuity notes: The comedy should escalate visually. By the final clone wave, the clones are more or less harmless and can barely hold their little medieval props.

## Production Company

A glossy dream-shifted studio office that appears after the clone battle. It has glowing meeting tables, movie posters inspired by Jet's Storyverse adventures, glass walls that show cosmic bubble universes outside, and executives in bright modern suits.

Continuity notes: The meeting should feel like a strange reward for defeating the clones, then be interrupted abruptly by the warlord's drone attack.

## Warlord Drones

Small medieval-futuristic drones used by the weakened Pickle Warlord. They are metallic purple and green, with tiny propellers, glowing red scanner eyes, and little pennants marked with pickle-shaped warlord symbols.

Continuity notes: The drones do the dirty work because the warlord has become too weak to attack directly. They should look more dangerous than the remaining clones, but still belong to the absurd pickle-warlord aesthetic.

## Storyverse

A multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes floating together in a cosmic void.

Continuity notes: This issue begins in a dreamlike Storyverse environment and shifts into a medieval battlefield and then a production office. Keep cosmic bubble universes, surreal portals, and purple Lucid Light accents visible during transitions.
