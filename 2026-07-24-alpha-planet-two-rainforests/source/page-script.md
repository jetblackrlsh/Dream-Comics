# Page Script — Alpha Planet, Two Rainforests

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions must remain readable, clearly separated, long enough to make every story change obvious, and nonredundant. Jet and every recurring design must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 — Cover

One dramatic layered composition centered on lava-form Jet flying above two matching versions of the same rounded-canopy tropical rainforest. The left half is blue and snowy; the right half is warm autumn red, orange, and gold. Behind Jet, an ordinary wooden door stands open in space. The opened Alpha planet looms as an astronomical purple tiger king at upper left, while the long-haired purple tiger woman and the star-filled Lucid Light tiger mech form smaller opposing silhouettes at lower right. A glowing lava river unifies the bottom of the composition. Visible cover text only: “ALPHA PLANET, TWO RAINFORESTS” and “JULY 24, 2026”. No issue number.

## Page 2 — Mouse-Sized but Lucid

Five panels. Jet lies conscious but immobilized during sleep paralysis in a dark bedroom. Sparkling star-filled purple Lucid Light carries him into the continuing Storyverse. He appears at mouse size inside an enormous corridor with towering baseboards, giant floor seams, and distant oversized doors. A humanoid purple tiger woman with long flowing hair spots him and gives chase. Jet runs as her enormous clawed hand reaches down, then remains calm when she catches him gently but firmly in her claws.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and entered the same Storyverse continuity I had explored since June 11, 2025.”
- “I found myself moving through a vast corridor, although it may only have seemed enormous because I had been reduced to the size of a mouse.”
- “A long-haired humanoid purple tiger woman saw me and began chasing me through the towering passage.”
- “She was much larger than I was, and eventually her claws closed around me.”
- “Because I knew I was dreaming, being caught did not make me feel intimidated.”

## Page 3 — The False Alpha

Six panels. Mouse-sized Jet summons sparkling purple star-filled Lucid Light inside the tiger woman's grip. The energy expands around him into the gigantic humanoid tiger mech with Jet visible in its chest cockpit. The mech towers over the astonished tiger woman. She bows slightly and addresses it as Alpha. Jet shrinks the mech until it matches her height, then dismisses the armor and stands before her at normal adult scale. Her reverence becomes fury when she understands the mistake. She lunges and rakes Jet with her claws, but the Rainbow Crystal Sword heals each wound with prismatic light. Final panel: when she reaches for the sword, Jet raises a curved radiant rainbow barrier that stops her claws.

Caption text:

- “I summoned my Lucid Light and constructed a gigantic humanoid tiger mech around my tiny body.”
- “The mech grew until I towered over her, and she stared upward in sudden surprise.”
- “Oh, Alpha. I did not recognize you, she said, mistaking my armor for the leader of her pack.”
- “I shrank the mech to her height and dismissed it, revealing that I was not the Alpha at all.”
- “Imposter, she shouted, but every wound from her claws closed immediately in the Rainbow Crystal Sword's healing light.”
- “When she tried to steal the sword, I created a radiant rainbow barrier that made her attacks useless.”

## Page 4 — The Planet Unfurls

Six dynamic panels. Jet stands behind the rainbow barrier and calmly offers the furious tiger woman a chance to join his next adventure. She refuses and claws at the barrier, insisting that the Alpha will take his sword. Jet flies straight upward inside the shield and crashes harmlessly through the corridor ceiling. He exits the atmosphere into space. Behind him, the entire spherical planet opens along glowing seams and unfurls into the astronomical body of the gigantic purple tiger king, with continents and oceans across his limbs. The Alpha reaches after Jet, but Jet accelerates beyond the planet-sized claws. Final wide panel: alone in space, Jet creates a completely ordinary freestanding brown wooden door with a plain silver metal knob, opens it, and steps through.

Caption text:

- “I told her I was leaving for an adventure and invited her to come with me, but I had no reason to stay while she kept attacking.”
- “She refused. She insisted that her Alpha would want my sword and promised to take it for him.”
- “Protected by the rainbow shield, I stopped arguing, flew upward, and crashed through the ceiling without being harmed.”
- “Behind me, the entire planet opened and transformed into the enormous body of the Alpha, a gigantic purple tiger king.”
- “His true form pursued me through space, but I accelerated beyond his reach.”
- “In the cosmic emptiness, I created an ordinary wooden door with a metal knob, opened it, and stepped into another reality.”

## Page 5 — Snow in the Tropics

Five atmospheric panels. Jet exits the ordinary wooden door into a dense blue-tinted rainforest of giant tropical trunks, vines, ferns, broad leaves, and smooth round canopy crowns while white snow falls everywhere. He studies snow collecting on unmistakably tropical vegetation and follows the matching forest path. Jet discovers the basalt cave and walks beside its bright lava river. He rests safely on a warm ledge near the molten current while only a faint blue gust reaches from the snowy entrance. Final panel fractures like a disrupted image as the dream's colors pull away from Jet, suggesting a brief waking interruption without introducing another location.

Caption text:

- “The door opened into a dense rainforest where tropical trees had strange, smoothly rounded canopies.”
- “The entire landscape was tinted blue, and snow fell through broad leaves, vines, and ferns as if the Amazon had entered winter.”
- “I found a cave containing a river of lava and followed its glowing current deeper underground.”
- “The lava's heat kept me warm while only a faint trace of the cold wind reached me from outside.”
- “I rested beside the molten river until a brief waking interruption pulled me away from the dream.”

## Page 6 — The Reconstructed World

Six panels with visual echoes of pages 4 and 5. Jet returns through purple Lucid Light, visibly determined to rebuild the broken journey. A small comparison panel shows the tiger planet remaining a normal sphere instead of becoming the Alpha, while Jet still finds the ordinary wooden door in space. He enters the same rainforest, but warm sunlight reveals identical rounded tropical canopies filled with orange, red, amber, and yellow leaves. Fallen autumn leaves cover the same winding path beneath vines and ferns. Jet finds the same basalt cave and follows the same lava river. Final large panel: he deliberately steps into the molten current and allows it to rise over his body.

Caption text:

- “I returned to sleep quickly, frustrated by the interruption, and tried to reconstruct the dream from the point where it had broken.”
- “The second version did not repeat perfectly: the planet no longer transformed into the Alpha, although the wooden door in space remained.”
- “Beyond it, the same tropical rainforest was now experiencing autumn instead of winter.”
- “Orange, red, and yellow leaves filled the round canopies and blanketed the ground in a mild, comfortable climate.”
- “I found the cave again and followed the familiar river of lava into its depths.”
- “This time I did not merely rest beside the current. I stepped directly into it and let the lava cover me.”

## Page 7 — God of Fire and Molten Stone

Six transformation panels. Lava surrounds Jet without burning him. Glowing molten lines spread harmlessly across his skin, clothes, beard, dreadlocks, sword, tether, and bag until his exact lava-form design emerges. Jet rises from the current as a powerful living embodiment of black volcanic stone, orange-yellow fire, and molten rock. He launches upward through the cave shaft without damaging it, exits the autumn rainforest, and flies high above the rounded red-and-gold canopies. From the sky, he remembers the same terrain under snow and blue light. Final wide panel shows the autumn landscape beginning to overlap transparently with the snowy version inside his vision.

Caption text:

- “The lava did not burn me. It spread across my skin and transformed every part of my body without causing pain.”
- “I emerged as living fire and molten stone, still myself but filled with the power of the volcanic river.”
- “The transformation felt like transcendence, as though I had become a god of lava.”
- “I flew out of the cave and rose high above the autumn rainforest.”
- “From the sky, I remembered how the same rounded treetops had looked beneath snow and a blue atmosphere.”
- “The winter and autumn versions of the world began to overlap and merge inside my mind.”

## Page 8 — Photographs Without a Camera

Six contemplative panels arranged around one large central aerial image. Lava-form Jet hovers between two matching visions of the same rainforest. On the left, he frames snowy blue round canopies with his hands. On the right, he studies the exact trees covered in red, orange, and gold autumn leaves. Close details pair snow on a broad tropical leaf with fallen autumn leaves beneath tropical vines; another pair compares the same cave entrance and lava glow in both seasons. The central image blends the two landscapes along a graceful boundary without turning either into the other. Final wide panel: Jet floats peacefully above both complete worlds as their colors are preserved like two luminous photographs within a starry memory field.

Caption text:

- “I moved between the two visions like a photographer without a camera, carefully framing every detail with my attention.”
- “I studied the snowy rainforest first: blue air, falling white flakes, and round tropical treetops that should not have belonged to winter.”
- “Then I studied the same landscape in autumn, glowing red and gold above a ground covered with fallen leaves.”
- “Neither version replaced the other. The matching paths, cave, and lava river allowed both impossible seasons to remain connected.”
- “The interruption had changed the dream, but reconstructing it had created a second world worth remembering.”
- “As the dream ended, I preserved both rainforests together inside my memory.”
