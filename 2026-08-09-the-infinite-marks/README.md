# 08/09/2026 - The Infinite Marks

Dream Comics entry for August 9, 2026.

Jet flies above the clouds and discovers the Cloud Tavern, a crossroads where superheroes, fantasy adventurers, and benevolent demons answer emergencies across countless realms. The tavern pays returning heroes in golden crown-shaped Marks by reading the glowing green achievement grids built into their official gear.

When a bartender tries to replace Silonus with an approved sword, Jet protects the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword and proves it can adapt the tavern's technology. The resulting scan does more than recover his past victories: it recognizes that every successful defense of Silonus potentially protects the entire Storyverse. The impossible total transforms an unknown newcomer into a legendary hero before the whole tavern.

The finished package includes the complete journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, and assembled PDF.
