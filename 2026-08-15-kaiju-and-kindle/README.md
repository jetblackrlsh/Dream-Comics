# 08/15/2026 - Kaiju & Kindle

Dream Comics entry for August 15, 2026.

From outside the action, Jet uses Lucid Light to shape a new Storyverse world around Kaiju and Kindle. Kaiju is a brilliant, kindhearted orange-skinned powerhouse whose frightening appearance hides a disciplined protector. Kindle is the rabbit-eared humanoid robot he created to be both his romantic partner and his superhero partner.

When Anti-Tekistan attacks San Francisco, Kaiju becomes a gigantic robotic dragon and destroys a falling nuclear weapon before it reaches the city. The invaders then deploy nanite rifles that force modern technology into analog forms. Kaiju refuses to kill the human soldiers and begins losing ground, but Kindle flies beyond their firing range, connects to the nanites remotely, and disables every weapon at once.

The finished package includes the complete journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, and assembled PDF.
