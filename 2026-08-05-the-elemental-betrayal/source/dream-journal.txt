Dream Journal Reconstruction

I began the dream standing in front of the entrance to a cave. Before I could proceed, vines seized me and held me in place. They belonged to a humanoid vine elemental whose body included a flaming crown. The crown looked almost like hair shaped into a crown, except it was made entirely of fire.

The vine elemental demanded that I relinquish Silonis, my rainbow crystal sword. Silonis is also known as the Life Sword and is one of the most powerful artifacts in the Storyverse. I refused to surrender it. Because the sword was already in my hand, I simply used it to cut through the vines and free myself.

My refusal enraged the elemental. Its temper appeared to coincide directly with the intensity of its flames: the angrier it became, the larger and more fiery its crown grew. It became so furious that the flames ultimately consumed the elemental itself, burning its vine body into ash.

I was shocked. The implication seemed to be that this elemental normally never became that upset, because otherwise it would have burned itself to ash long ago. I began thinking about how strange it was that the being consisted of two elements that were fundamentally dangerous to one another. Fire and living vines simply did not work well together.

I took pity on the elemental and decided to help it. Using Silonis, I cut the elemental into two separate parts: the burned vines and the fiery crown. I then channeled the sword’s life energy into both halves. I transformed the crown into an independent flame elemental, while restoring the charred vines into living, healthy vines as a separate plant elemental.

I told the two elementals that we did not have to fight. I explained that they could learn to live peacefully with themselves in a way that allowed both of them to remain safe. As long as they did not touch one another, they could both live happily together inside the cave. Each one could finally be its full self without constantly fearing that it might destroy its other half.

At first, the two elementals seemed conflicted about whether they could truly live with one another. Then they looked at each other with mischievous smiles. Instead of accepting the second chance I had given them, they both attacked me.

The plant elemental wrapped its vines around me and restrained me once again. The fire elemental anticipated that I would simply cut myself free as before. It grabbed Silonis and began superheating the sword. It told me that I would not be able to use the sword if I could not touch it. The weapon became so hot in my grip that I was forced to let it go.

However, Silonis has a life of its own. I do not need to wield it in the ordinary sense for it to act. The sword began spinning rapidly, almost like a jet turbine. Its rotation generated a tornado that blasted the fire elemental away and snuffed it out.

Silonis returned to my possession. It healed me and created one of its rainbow crystal barrier force fields, which knocked the plant elemental backward and freed me from its vines. I then threw Silonis toward the plant elemental. Once again, the sword performed its rapid spinning maneuver, but this time it did not create a tornado. Instead, it tore through the plant elemental like a mulcher, cutting the being into pieces. Afterward, Silonis flew back to me.

With both elementals defeated, I reflected on the effect Silonis seemed to have on the beings around me. I did not believe that the rainbow crystal sword necessarily had a corrupting influence. However, simply because it was one of the most powerful artifacts in the Storyverse, its presence appeared to make others behave differently than they normally would.

The original elemental had become so enraged over the sword that it destroyed itself. Even after I separated its incompatible halves, restored them, and gave both beings a second chance at life, they were still willing to risk everything in another attempt to take Silonis from me. It showed me that, no matter which Storyverse world I entered, everyone wanted the opportunity to hold the power of life itself within their hands.

After defeating the elementals and reaching that realization, I flew away into the Storyverse. That was the conclusion of the dream.
