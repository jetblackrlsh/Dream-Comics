The Unicode Beneath the World

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis but losing it almost immediately, Jet becomes an obedient prince-like student in a king-governed academy. He nearly completes a magnetic flying machine using spring-loaded coils and rainbow Morphium while chaotic classmates misbehave around him. A thankless errand for his royal father and the king's aloof wife leaves him disappointed, and he slips away from the academy through a guarded rear exit.

When Jet chooses to separate himself from the rowdy students outside, the dream becomes subtly lucid. Unicode characters bleed through the academy until the entire world is replaced by a terminal. Jet understands that the living code on its screen is the source of the reality he just inhabited. He reaches toward the interface, ready to discover whether he can rewrite a dream directly instead of controlling it with Lucid Light, but his alarm wakes him before his first edit.
