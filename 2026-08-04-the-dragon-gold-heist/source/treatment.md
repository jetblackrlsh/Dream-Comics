# Treatment - The Dragon Gold Heist

## Logline

After stealing molten treasure from inside a fire-breathing dragon, Jet must transform a relentless Storyverse pursuit into a magical bond using Tetheron, his golden energy rope.

## Story

Jet soars through a futuristic city and confronts a colossal copper-crimson dragon. Rather than attack, he protects himself with star-filled purple Lucid Light armor and dives directly through the dragon's fire and into its mouth.

Inside the creature, Jet discovers an impossible golden sea. He wonders whether dragons hoard gold because they eat it, then realizes the discovery can become a heist. He signals Leon, climbs the throat, punches the uvula without intending harm, and triggers a spectacular eruption. Leon catches Jet and the molten treasure in a green tractor beam.

The apparent getaway becomes a chase across the Storyverse. Inside Leon, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper, and the crew realize the dragon can follow them between bubble universes. Jet remains confident, announces that he can bond with it, and ejects into open space.

Lucid Light forms winglike jets from his armor. Jet throws Tetheron around the dragon's jaws, but the golden rope is not simply a muzzle. Its true magic creates connections, allowing Jet to tame the creature without hurting it. He lands on its back and guides it behind Leon with the tether as reins.

Jet then connects Tetheron's free end directly to Leon. The dragon follows the UFO through the luminous multiverse while Jet releases the reins, settles comfortably between its shoulders, and enjoys a peaceful Storyverse ride. The end transforms theft and pursuit into companionship.

## Visual Direction

Bright, high-saturation modern anime superhero comic art with cinematic lighting, clean panel borders, readable cream caption boxes, purple Lucid Light, emerald tractor-beam energy, molten metallic gold, and cosmic bubble universes. The dragon remains a colossal copper-crimson creature with charcoal horns, gold eyes, and broad membranous wings throughout.
