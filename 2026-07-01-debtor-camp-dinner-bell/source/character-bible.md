# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: protagonist, lucid dreamer, Storyverse explorer, wielder of Lucid Light, rainbow sword, and golden energy tether.
- Stable waking appearance: man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, warm brown eyes.
- Dream arrival appearance: begins as a hooded ghostly figure with the same Jet silhouette visible beneath pale translucent glow; as the story escalates, his normal Jet details become clearer under the ghostly hood.
- Signature powers and props: rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glitter energy tether, and sparkling purple Lucid Light like a night sky full of stars.
- Expression range: silent horror, tactical focus, calm intimidation, protective resolve.
- Continuity rule: keep Jet recognizable through dark-brown skin, beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow sword, golden tether, and purple Lucid Light, even when he appears ghostly.

## Homeless Woman

- Role: first victim Jet witnesses; emotional anchor for the debtor camp.
- Appearance: tired adult woman with medium-brown skin, gray-brown patched cloak, wrapped hair scarf, worn boots, hollow cheeks, and determined eyes.
- Expression range: exhausted dignity, fear during arrest, relief at liberation.
- Continuity rule: keep the patched gray-brown cloak, head scarf, and worn boots visible whenever she appears.

## Constable

- Role: enforcer who arrests poor citizens for unpaid taxes.
- Appearance: stern medieval officer in dull iron helmet, blue-black tabard with a gold lion crest, leather gloves, heavy boots, baton, and a tax ledger chained to his belt.
- Expression range: cold bureaucracy, nervous fear when the ogre falls.
- Continuity rule: keep the lion-crest tabard and chained ledger visible.

## Lion King

- Role: tyrant antagonist who uses elemental cores to warp the kingdom.
- Appearance: muscular humanoid lion with golden fur, thick red mane, jeweled crown, sharp feline muzzle, predatory amber eyes, and massive claws.
- Robes: crimson and ivory royal robes with many glowing elemental cores visibly sewn into the fabric, each core shining with fire, ice, lightning, wind, earth, or water energy.
- Signature threat: channels the cores to reshape stone, weather, fire, and water around him.
- Expression range: arrogant command, panicked rage, drained humiliation.
- Continuity rule: keep him lion-headed and humanoid, with the crown, red mane, claws, and glowing elemental cores sewn into the robes until the ogre drains them.

## Energy-Vampire Ogre

- Role: summoned greater threat and reluctant replacement ruler.
- Appearance: huge green-gray ogre with tusks, black horns, blackened space-scarred armor, clawed hands, red hungry eyes, and violet cracks glowing across skin and armor.
- Signature threat: drinks energy through mouth and hands, especially elemental cores.
- Expression range: ravenous triumph, throne-hungry arrogance, sudden fear when recognizing Jet, submissive caution.
- Continuity rule: keep the green-gray skin, tusks, black horns, blackened armor, violet cracks, and hunger aura consistent.

## Debtor Prisoners

- Role: exploited citizens forced to mine elemental cores.
- Appearance: mixed group of exhausted miners in patched medieval clothing, wrist shackles, dusty faces, lanterns, picks, and baskets filled with glowing cores.
- Expression range: despair, terror during the royal battle, cautious hope during liberation.
- Continuity rule: keep their clothing rough, dusty, and practical; do not make them look like soldiers.

## Medieval Tax Kingdom

- Role: primary setting.
- Appearance: stone streets, timber houses, market stalls, muddy alleys, castle towers, debtor camp palisades, mine shafts under the camp, and a throne hall warped by elemental power.
- Corruption motif: gold lion crests, tax notices, chained ledgers, elemental cracks in stone, and glowing cores used as wealth and weapons.

## Giant Dinner Bell

- Role: Jet's lucid summoning tool.
- Appearance: enormous bronze dinner bell floating above the debtor camp, carved with dreamlike star patterns, suspended by sparkling purple Lucid Light and struck by a golden energy tether.
- Function: rings at an impossible frequency that attracts ogres across space.
- Continuity rule: it is a bell, not a gong or tower; keep the bronze body, star carvings, purple Lucid Light, and golden tether strike visible.
