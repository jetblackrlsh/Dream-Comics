Jet becomes the number-one hero of a Storyverse city by separating a giant slime monster's soul, purifying it with the Life Sword, and returning it as a benevolent ocean guardian. A fire-powered rival then melts Jet's metallic defense into living liquid metal, but Jet turns that transformation into a restraint that wins their aerial duel. Afterward, a team of flying superheroes introduces him to an unexpectedly magnificent honey-salt-and-spice tortilla chip, ending the dream in friendship and delight.
