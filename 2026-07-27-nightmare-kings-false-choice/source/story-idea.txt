The Nightmare King's False Choice

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet encounters a black mirrored nightmare planet and its ruler, the King of Nightmares. The king insists that Jet must destroy the entire planet to prevent lethal nightmares from harming sleeping people, then tries to shame him as a false hero when he refuses. Jet's awareness of the infinite Storyverse lets him recognize the demand as a manipulative false mission that cannot end nightmares.

Jet's attempt to burn the king fails. The burnt-vine monster traps him in flaming thorns and nearly steals the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword. Jet uses his overlooked Golden Energy Tether to bond with the sword from a distance, release its force field, heal himself, and reclaim the weapon.

Rather than destroy the nightmare world, Jet uses the Life Sword to purify it. The black cubes become radiant clouds, the nightmares become dreams, and the liberated dreams join Jet in tearing apart the King of Nightmares. His archetypal nature means he may eventually return, but every moment he remains scattered gives dreams and their defenders more time to protect people.
