I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void.

Jet traveled alone to a massive rocky purple planet covered in craters. There he met Pop, a pot-bellied purple imp with a skinny diamond-shaped head and bright neon green shorts. Pop could seemingly do anything, kept shapeshifting, and bent reality around himself like a classic cartoon character brought to life. He felt so unrealistic that he became hyper-realistic, as if he and his world were more real than Jet.

Pop wanted to go on an adventure, but Jet kept trying to understand what Pop was instead of choosing an objective. Frustrated, Pop reshaped the world into a superhero planet. Jet quickly found himself inside a Hero Station, where hero officials confronted him because he did not have a Hero Badge. Every hero needed one to operate, and Jet was about to be arrested.

At the last second, Pop transformed himself into a Hero Badge. Jet showed the badge to the other heroes, who immediately changed their tune. They became curious about Jet, his powers, and his recruitment potential. Jet tried to explain his lucid dreaming abilities, but the dream was ending. Before he could explain further, the 4:30 AM alarm went off in the waking world.
