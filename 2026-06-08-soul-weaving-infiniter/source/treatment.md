# Treatment

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet's soul travels through the Storyverse and meets Professor Weird and Infiniter for a reality-bending contest, only to fall into his own woven world: a supernatural comic-book arts academy where danger, pride, and returning lucidity teach him the difference between soul travel and ordinary dreaming.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants the contest to be an act of creation, while Infiniter would rather settle reality warping through football. Professor Weird mediates because either contestant could damage existence. Once Jet begins weaving a blue-thread reality, the conflict turns inward: he must survive the chaotic story he created without losing the clarity that brought him there.

## Theme

Creation is powerful because it can become a world, but lucidity is the thread that helps Jet tell the difference between making a dream and traveling beyond one.

## Ending

Jet regains lucidity inside the haunted comic academy when he questions how long he has been there. The blue threads peel back, revealing Professor Weird and Infiniter beyond the woven reality. Jet returns from the journey with a sober certainty: the cosmic soul travel felt different from the dream he fell into.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins in awe and uncertainty, feeling his soul leave his body and cross a plane that does not feel like a dream. Professor Weird's scan steadies him, letting him engage Infiniter as an equal. Inside the created school, he becomes swept into missions, danger, and pride for the award-winning double. By the end, his lucidity returns as discernment: he does not need to prove the experience to anyone in the moment, only remember the difference clearly.
