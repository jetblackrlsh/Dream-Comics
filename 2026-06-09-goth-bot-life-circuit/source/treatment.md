# Treatment

## Title

06/09/2026: Goth Bot Life Circuit

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet creates a new Storyverse world built for adventure, prints Goth Bot as an AI companion, and learns that the deepest power in any team is not strength but synchronized communication.

## Central Conflict

Jet can create worlds, print companions, and command powerful heroes, but raw power fails when people do not share energy, intention, or information. The peaceful life-energy circuit with Goth Bot becomes the standard that the later hero team cannot meet.

## Theme

A true team works when its members can move as one. Power without communication collapses; connection turns separate beings into a coordinated whole.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alone but lucid, designing a world full of options. He finds calm connection with Goth Bot in the meditation alcove, then loses that clarity when lucidity drifts. The later defense sequence reveals the opposite state: mighty heroes acting separately. Jet ends by rebuilding the broken wall and recognizing that the life circuit was the real lesson.

## Ending

Jet fortifies the house against the giant green squid villain and sees a final split image: chaotic heroes on one side, the serene alcove circuit with Goth Bot on the other. The visual consequence is clear: future teams need the same communication and shared rhythm that made the alcove bond work.
