# 07/17/2026 - Magmagator: Mall World Meltdown

Dream Comics entry for July 17, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet arrives alone in Mall World with augmented-reality display sunglasses and a handheld touchpad. Entering an infinite filthy bathroom causes him to lose lucidity, but an alligator’s transformation into the magma kaiju Magmagator restores it. A yellow-and-blue superhero suit materializes around Jet, yet the monster is too powerful for him to defeat alone. A mysterious purple-skinned counterpart whom Jet instinctively recognizes as his evil twin crashes into the mall and helps him win. The twin reveals that they are aliens sent to fight monsters created by Earth’s future pollution, but claims to be indifferent rather than evil because Earth is not their home. Jet chooses to remain, investigate the polluting companies, and find the resistance opposing them—until an aggressive encounter with a resistance woman ends when his alarm wakes him.
