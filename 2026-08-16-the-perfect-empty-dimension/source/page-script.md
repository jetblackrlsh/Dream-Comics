# Page Script - The Perfect Empty Dimension

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, Silonis, the rescued woman, the slime monster, and both Empty Dimension versions of Jet follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic full-page composition. Jet stands in a rain-slicked city street holding Silonis as rainbow life energy separates the rescued woman from the green slime monster. A huge Porta portal opens behind him into the serene digital Empty Dimension, where translucent observer-Jet watches resident-Jet walking freely among welcoming people. The cover must be one coherent scene, not a collage.

Visible cover text only: “THE PERFECT EMPTY DIMENSION” and “AUGUST 16, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - The Threat Behind the Performance

A clear four-panel page. Panel one shows Jet flying above the city and calmly passing the apparently idle slime monster. Panel two shows the creature slamming an empty section of road with a giant tentacle while Jet challenges the display. Panel three reveals the trapped woman inside the lifted translucent tentacle. Panel four is Jet's close reaction as he turns back and draws Silonis.

Caption text:

- “Flying above the city, I noticed a giant green slime monster—but it was not harming anyone.”
- “When it smashed the road merely to get my attention, I called the threat performative.”
- “Then it raised one tentacle and revealed a woman trapped inside.”
- “I had misjudged the danger. Now I had to save her without killing the creature.”

## Page 3 - A Rescue Neither Wanted

A clear four-panel page. Jet raises Silonis and sends rainbow life-energy ribbons into the woman and monster. Their forms merge into one towering green slime woman. Close panels show the fused being recoiling in horror and Jet understanding that safety alone has not respected either identity.

Caption text:

- “I drew Silonis, the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life, and joined their life energies.”
- “The hostage and her captor became one towering slime woman—safe from attack, but horrified by the fusion.”
- “Neither wanted to remain trapped in a body that denied who they were.”
- “I promised the transformation was temporary. Rescue without consent was not a solution.”

## Page 4 - The Empty Sentence

A clear four-panel page. Jet makes one careful nonviolent cut with Silonis through the rainbow connection, restoring the relieved woman and startled slime monster. He casts Porta. The creature tumbles unharmed through a luminous circular portal while the woman questions Jet. The final panel contrasts a faint vision of the Glimmer's spirit-prison with the calm starry emptiness Jet actually chose.

Caption text:

- “With one careful cut of Silonis, I separated their lives and restored them both.”
- “Then Porta opened a path to the Empty Dimension, where the slime monster could cause no immediate harm.”
- “The woman asked whether I had sent it to the Glimmer, a spirit-prison for magical enemies.”
- “No. The Empty Dimension was simply a place to be alone with its own thoughts.”

## Page 5 - I Followed It In

A clear four-panel transition page. Jet lowers Silonis and reflects while the portal glows beside him. Small visual memories show him happily narrating aloud and enjoying private thought. He then steps voluntarily through Porta. In the final large panel, the Empty Dimension divides him into exactly two versions: translucent observer-Jet and solid resident-Jet.

Caption text:

- “The more I described that solitude, the less it sounded like punishment.”
- “I loved being alone long enough to narrate my life, think freely, and hear myself clearly.”
- “So I opened Porta again—and stepped into the Empty Dimension myself.”
- “On the other side, I split into two: one self to live there, and one self to watch.”

## Page 6 - Freedom to Be Ordinary

A clear four-panel slice-of-life page in the serene digital neighborhood. Resident-Jet moves among familiar welcoming people. He peacefully eats one hearty meal, rests comfortably on a simple floor sleeping setup, and follows his normal routines while nobody stares or criticizes. Observer-Jet watches from a translucent distance. Avoid making the habits comedic or pitiable.

Caption text:

- “The resident me lived among familiar people in a world that felt softly digital.”
- “I ate once a day. I slept on the floor. I followed every unusual routine that felt natural.”
- “No one judged me, questioned me, or demanded an explanation.”
- “This was not the freedom to be a superhero. It was the freedom to be myself.”

## Page 7 - The Siren With My Face

A clear four-panel page as violet morning light approaches. Observer-Jet notices a floating digital clock nearing 8:00 AM and looks toward a thinning glimpse of the waking world. Resident-Jet turns toward him with a warm, persuasive expression and extends an inviting hand. The perfect neighborhood remains genuinely beautiful and safe. The final panel brings the two identical faces close together across a luminous divide.

Caption text:

- “After what felt like a long time, observer-me remembered the waking world.”
- “It was almost eight in the morning. I knew I should get up.”
- “Resident-me answered like a siren: This world is perfect. We can stay and never worry again.”
- “The offer was genuine. Nothing here was secretly cruel—and that made it harder to refuse.”

## Page 8 - I Chose to Wake

A clear four-panel finale. Observer-Jet looks from resident-Jet's offered hand toward a portal showing his real bedroom in warm morning light. He gently refuses and walks through. Resident-Jet remains calm as the digital paradise dissolves into rainbow pixels. The final wide panel shows waking Jet opening his eyes on his simple floor sleeping space, peaceful and purposeful as daylight enters.

Caption text:

- “But acceptance without responsibility was not the whole life I wanted.”
- “I still had people, promises, and work waiting beyond the dream.”
- “I told my other self, "I'm good. I have things I need to do."”
- “Then I let the perfect dimension fade—and chose to wake up.”
