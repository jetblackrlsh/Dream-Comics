On July 13, 2026, Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in a futuristic corridor wearing a business suit. A woman escorts him into the command center of Agentis and identifies him as its director. Jet first assumes he runs a spy organization, but learns that these are AI agents: humanlike visualizations of the many digital agents he uses in waking life.

The crowd makes Jet realize that he is comfortable asking agents to make comics, but not yet comfortable coordinating many agents in parallel or deciding what a larger agent-powered company should do. He considers the rise of solopreneurs, his existing comics site and support options, and a possible unified site for his wider media landscape. Instead of forcing an immediate business plan, he gives Agentis one focused assignment: organize what already exists and sketch possible connections. The command center becomes a clear map, and Jet recognizes that the same circumstances can feel completely different when viewed from a new perspective.
