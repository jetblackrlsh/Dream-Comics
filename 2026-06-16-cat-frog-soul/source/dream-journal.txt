Dream Journal 06/16/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025. My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I. My Ghostly UFO, named Leon, resembles a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025. Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit doesn't have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025. Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds. Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color with spiky slicked back yellow hair and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025. Lucid Light is a reality warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered the power of Lucid Light in a dream on 07/19/2025. Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah is a pale skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that covered her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She is wielding a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword. Her father Tecton is a green skinned, humanoid, muscular, cat ogre, barbarian who dresses in a brown loin cloth and a silver baldric. My drone Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper all met me in early 2026 while adventuring through the Super Cathedral. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, and warm brown eyes. I have a rainbow sword that resembles a colorful futuristic crystal sword, and an energy tether that resembles a rope made of golden glittery light.

I arrive alone on the back of a Shellasaur.

Shellasaurs are dinosaurs with massive shells on their back, similar to snail shells, that they use as their homes.

A giant, subterranean serpent uses its speed, stealth, and stretchy jaws to eat the Shellasaurs into extinction.

I am forced to fly away using my Lucid Light and to watch from space.

I notice something on the ground and fly back down to get a better look.

I discover a humanoid green frog felt puppet, but it is missing its arms and legs.

In past dreams this puppet has represented my soul, but normally it has arms and legs.

Without arms my soul cannot act, and without legs my soul cannot move.

It is trapped in the dangerous land of the serpent.

I use my Lucid Light to give the puppet new life with long cat arms and cat legs.

With the speed, agility, and incredible claws of a cat: Cat Frog Soul outmaneuvers and slays the dreaded subterranean serpent, allowing for the Shellasaurs to potentially one day return to the land in peace.

My biggest takeaway from the dream is that you are what you build yourself to be, and that no matter how bad things get you have the power to reinvent yourself to make things better.
