# Page Script — The Electronic Egg

All story text appears inside rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, or issue numbers.

## Page 1 — Cover

Jet sits on a red couch floating in the starry cosmic void beside Doctor Bear. Above them floats the electronic egg, reflecting a flying sasquatch, a runaway train, a missile, and Midnight Archer drawing her bow. Visible cover text only: “THE ELECTRONIC EGG” and “JULY 12, 2026”.

## Page 2 — The Question

Four panels establish Jet and Doctor Bear on the couch in space, the purple bubbles of the Storyverse, and a vision of Earth inside Jet's Lucid Light.

Caption text:

1. “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrived alone in the Storyverse, where every possible story exists inside a cosmic bubble.”
2. “Doctor Bear sat beside me on a red couch in space, and together we asked what it means to hold the power of a god.”
3. “I love Earth, even though some people there hate me simply because of who I am.”
4. “I would never smite them. But I could turn those who hate and destroy into people who love and create.”

## Page 3 — The Boundary

Four panels visualize loving creators and hateful destroyers as possible paths around Earth, Jet almost touching the vision with Lucid Light, then deliberately withdrawing his hand while Doctor Bear listens.

Caption text:

1. “Changing cruelty into kindness sounds merciful, until I remember that rewriting a mind also steals its freedom.”
2. “In a dream I can control almost anything. Does anyone truly have free will when my thought can become their reality?”
3. “And yet dream people surprise me. They fear, decide, disagree, and offer choices I did not plan.”
4. “If their choices feel real, then my power must make room for them.”

## Page 4 — Friendship by Choice

Five energetic panels: sasquatch flies past with the egg; Jet pursues using Lucid Light; sasquatch recoils protectively; Jet transforms into Blue Cat Jet; the two play catch and the smiling sasquatch freely gives Jet the egg.

Caption text:

1. “A sasquatch in a red jersey and blue cape flew past carrying what looked like a football.”
2. “I followed from curiosity, but he thought I meant to steal it.”
3. “Instead of overpowering his fear, I changed myself into a blue catlike form that felt familiar and safe.”
4. “We played catch, and trust replaced fear because neither of us forced it.”
5. “Then my new friend chose to give me the object: an electronic egg that connected realities and games.”

## Page 5 — The Egg Opens

Four panels: the egg unfolds into a portal; Jet is pulled through a tunnel of game worlds; he lands among superheroes in a vivid city; Manifestor conjures a runaway elevated train and launches a missile.

Caption text:

1. “The gift opened before I could study it, and the electronic egg pulled me through itself.”
2. “I crossed realities like levels in a game and landed beside a team of superheroes.”
3. “Their enemy could manifest ideas into solid reality.”
4. “He created two disasters at once: a runaway train packed with people, and a missile aimed at everyone trying to save them.”

## Page 6 — Power Fails

Five panels show the gold flyer pushing unsuccessfully, blue stone hero bracing against rails, solar knight blasting brakes without effect, Jet gathering Lucid Light but seeing civilians endangered, and Midnight Archer calmly studying missile circuitry and the train's path.

Caption text:

1. “The strongest hero pushed against the train, but its manifested momentum only grew.”
2. “Another shattered the track by accident, making the danger worse.”
3. “Even my Lucid Light offered too many ways to erase the threat—and too many lives I might erase with it.”
4. “Raw power kept answering the wrong question.”
5. “Then Midnight Archer, the only hero without powers, noticed that the two threats could solve each other.”

## Page 7 — The Creative Shot

Large dynamic panels: Midnight Archer fires a violet circuit-pattern trick arrow; it pierces the missile control housing; missile banks away from the heroes toward the empty front of the runaway train; collision destroys both threats in a controlled burst as passengers are shielded; Manifestor flees.

Caption text:

1. “Midnight Archer fired one hacking arrow into the missile's guidance system.”
2. “Her arrow redirected the weapon away from us and toward the empty engine at the front of the train.”
3. “One precise idea destroyed both threats while the heroes shielded every passenger.”
4. “The villain fled from a mortal who had defeated his power with imagination.”

## Page 8 — What Power Is For

Four panels: heroes and passengers celebrate Midnight Archer; Jet sees the egg glowing in his hand; the portal returns him to Doctor Bear and the couch; final wide panel shows Jet and Doctor Bear before Earth and the Storyverse while an arrow-shaped gleam crosses the egg.

Caption text:

1. “Among godlike beings, the powerless hero had been the one who understood the problem.”
2. “Empathy told her whom to protect. Creativity showed her how.”
3. “Back beside Doctor Bear, I finally understood that power is not the same thing as wisdom.”
4. “The best use of godlike power is not to command hearts, but to protect the space in which hearts can choose to change.”
