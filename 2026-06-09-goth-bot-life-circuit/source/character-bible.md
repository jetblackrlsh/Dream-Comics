# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. He can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he is lucid, creative, peaceful in the alcove, then focused and commanding during the house defense.

## Goth Bot

Adult gothic android companion printed by Jet. She has orange skin, curly shoulder-length orange hair with black highlights, green eyes with purple eyeshadow, black lipstick, red freckles, and an elegant black leather short dress with neon purple trim. Her body is visibly android-like through subtle seams, glowing circuitry, and polished synthetic joints, while her face grows warmer and more alive as life energy flows through her.

Continuity notes: Keep the orange skin, curly orange hair with black highlights, green eyes, purple eyeshadow, black lipstick, red freckles, black leather dress, and neon purple trim consistent. Her emotional arc moves from newly printed curiosity to calm living presence.

## Giant Green Squid Villain

Massive green humanoid squid monster with a broad muscular body, slick tentacle-like limbs, glowing yellow-green eyes, and heavy aquatic texture. It is a house-breaking threat rather than a gory horror creature.

Continuity notes: Keep it huge enough to burst through a wall and physically pushed back by Jet's powers. Avoid gore.

## The Hero Team

A group of powerful but poorly coordinated heroes defending a home. They include armored flyers, energy wielders, martial artists, and shield bearers with varied bright costumes. They are visually impressive but scattered, interrupting each other's lines of action.

Continuity notes: Their problem is lack of communication. Show confusion, crossed signals, and isolated action instead of teamwork.

## New Storyverse World

A purpose-built lucid world with five key sections: portal station, power armory, leviathan lands, robot printer, and meditation alcove. The portal station is a luminous arrival platform. The power armory holds bright power armor suits. The leviathan lands are dense monster-filled jungles. The robot printer is a sleek fabrication chamber for custom AI companions. The meditation alcove is private, peaceful, and filled with life-energy geometry.

Continuity notes: This world should feel deliberately designed by Jet, cleanly sectioned, bright, and full of future activities.

## Life Energy

Warm gold-green living energy that can be built, cycled, and shared. In the meditation alcove it forms circuit-like loops between Jet and Goth Bot, allowing even a robot to channel and produce life energy.

Continuity notes: Distinguish life energy from Jet's purple Lucid Light and golden tether. Life energy should feel calmer, warmer, and biological.
