# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears a black hoodie open over a visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white side stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries the rainbow crystal sword of life: a colorful futuristic crystal sword whose translucent blade contains vivid rainbow facets and a bright life-energy glow.
- Uses Lucid Light: sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a night sky full of stars.
- Summons a bright yellow anti-gravity force-field belt around his waist. The belt projects a translucent golden-yellow spherical shield when Jet stretches his arms and legs outward.
- His silhouette, dreadlocks, beard, skin tone, hoodie outfit, belt bag, rainbow sword, and later yellow belt must remain identical on every page.
- Expression range: observant curiosity, moral disgust, calm tactical understanding, focused effort, and satisfied relief.

## Slime gorgons

- A coordinated group of eight monstrous gorgons with feminine humanoid upper bodies, long serpentine lower bodies, and writhing snake hair.
- Every body and every hair-snake is made from glossy translucent slime. Use clearly different saturated colors across the group: emerald green, magenta, cyan, orange, violet, acid yellow, ruby red, and electric blue.
- Faces are expressive, predatory, and smug. Their snake eyes glow chalk-white when charging stone vision.
- Stone-vision attacks are hard-edged pale gray-white beams surrounded by mineral dust and tiny geometric stone fragments.
- Their slime bodies visibly squash and splatter without gore when struck by Jet's spherical shield.

## Returning nightclub patrons

- Golems are friendly blocky humanoids made from varied stone: granite, sandstone, marble, basalt, and crystal-veined rock.
- Gargoyles are friendly winged stone humanoids with expressive faces, horns, claws, and varied gray, green-gray, and warm limestone colors.
- They appear only on the final page and must read as happy returning patrons, not enemies.

## Nightclub

- A large underground neon dance club built for stone creatures: black polished floor, purple and cyan lighting, mirror ball, DJ booth, stone arches, carved gargoyle sconces, massive reinforced stools, and wall reliefs showing golems and gargoyles dancing.
- At first the room is abandoned except for corrosive slime stains, dissolved stone furniture, and the gorgons. On the final page, repaired stone furniture, colorful lights, and a full dance floor make the restoration obvious.
