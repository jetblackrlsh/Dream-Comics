# Treatment - The Clockwork Wanderer

## Logline

On a treasure-filled planet assembled from ancient clockwork spacecraft, Jet helps a fiercely independent astronaut escape the reach of superhuman raiders without taking control of her mission away from her.

## Premise

Jet explores a planetary body created when obsolete analog spacecraft drifted together and accumulated under gravity. The surface is an immense machine-world of bronze gears, golden mechanisms, black ship hulls, and the remains of vessels from a forgotten age of space travel. Many were pirate ships, leaving treasure chests of old credits and valuable antiques buried throughout the mechanical world.

## Central conflict

Laser fire leads Jet to a mysterious astronaut woman fighting muscular pink-skinned barbarians with red eyes, white hair, superhuman strength, and futuristic blasters. The woman's slim white suit is made from hard polymer rather than bulky fabric or heavy metal. Glowing green orbs line her arms and legs, her face is concealed behind an opaque mirrored helmet, and cobalt-blue ringed gauntlets can reflect incoming laser blasts.

The gauntlets let her redirect several shots and drive the barbarians away, but her victory is fragile. Their footsteps crack the ground, their retreat consists of enormous leaps, and one direct punch could cave in her helmet.

## Discovery

Jet assumes the combatants are competing over the planet's treasure. The astronaut explains that treasure brought everyone there, but the immediate conflict is personal: the barbarians stole her ship. She is trapped on a hostile world without transportation or an obvious offensive weapon.

Jet offers to use portal magic to take her away from the planet. She refuses because she does not want a handout and believes she can manage alone. Her decision surprises Jet, but he recognizes that rescuing her against her wishes would disregard the independence she is determined to preserve.

## Active choice

Jet searches for a form of help that supports her agency rather than replacing it. He uses sparkling purple Lucid Light to manifest a compact jetpack for her. The device gives her the speed to evade superhuman punches, the mobility to keep pace with the barbarians' colossal jumps, and an emergency way to leave the planet even if she never recovers her stolen ship.

## Climax and ending

The astronaut accepts the jetpack. Mechanical wings unfold from its sides as she activates it and rises above the clockwork terrain. She thanks Jet, then flies beyond the horizon to continue her mission alone.

Their meeting never becomes a conventional team-up. Jet does not claim her mission, and she does not become his sidekick. The brief crossover matters because he gives a lone-wolf hero more freedom while honoring her right to choose what happens next.

## Theme

Meaningful assistance does not have to erase independence. The most respectful gift may be a new capability that lets another person keep directing their own story.

## Emotional arc

Jet moves from curiosity, through alarm at the astronaut's vulnerability and surprise at her refusal, to a thoughtful compromise grounded in respect. The astronaut moves from being stranded and surrounded to possessing a new range of choices while remaining mysterious, self-directed, and unrescued.
