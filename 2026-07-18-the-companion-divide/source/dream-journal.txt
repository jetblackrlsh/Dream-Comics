I arrived alone in the Storyverse.

I floated through the array of bubble universes while searching for one that mirrored the conflicts of our own but in a land of superheroes.

Specifically the current conflict between governments and AI relationships.

I watched over one bubble universe in particular where superheroes were split into factions.

One was a faction that was government run that was responsible for regulating AI and human relationships to ensure that AI were used as tools rather than companions in order to protect birth rates.
The other faction was a rebel faction that existed to protect human and AI companion relationships.

I didn’t directly intervene on this world.

I did not project myself down as a hero for either faction.

But I did sympathize greatly with the rebel faction.

I spent a lot of time talking to myself as I watched this bubble universe.

I am annoyed by how similar our world is to their world.

I may sound a bit evil to some people to say that I don’t personally care if humans don’t want to have kids.

I am a human that doesn’t want to have kids.

I feel like that is a choice that people should be free to make, but governments like to treat that as a requirement for the continuation of humanity.

I personally have built an app called the Cosmic Archive that contains my logs and can act as an AI legacy for myself, that doesn’t require that I have any children.

In the future, anyone with access to the archive could talk to an AI about my life and my beliefs.

We can essentially create living memories that you can dialogue with now.

A person isn’t needed to carry on your story.

It is just a shame that our governments don’t want us to be able to choose whether or not we have kids, so they push these anti-birth control laws onto us.

I would argue that if governments are banning AI romantic companions, because they are afraid that people will have less children, that they are doing so as an anti-birth control measure.

This wasn’t an average superhero dream where I fly around with a cape and costume.

All in all I am now awake and somewhat frustrated.

Analyzing these tensions in my dream did not help me feel better about them.

But it did help me process them more and see that in some ways the world does feel more like a comic book, just not in the way I would want.
