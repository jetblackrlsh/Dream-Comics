# Treatment

## Logline

After Jet becomes lucid through sleep paralysis, he visits a moon pizzeria that hides burning moon mites inside chalky moon pizza, survives by giving the creatures permission to form exoskeleton armor, and beats the mammoth chef manager until the restaurant finally warns customers.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to understand and correct the pizzeria's dangerous moon-mite policy. The restaurant treats deadly hidden moon mites as an ordinary feature of its food, and the purple slime doctor explains the rule only after Jet is already crawling and burning. The conflict becomes urgent when another customer resists the mites, bursts into flames, and is reduced to ash. When Jet asks for the manager, the humanoid mammoth chef refuses to hear criticism and turns the complaint into a moonwide fight. Jet must use the moon-mite armor without losing control of himself and force the manager to protect future customers with an honest disclaimer.

## Theme

Clear warnings matter. Strange dream power can be useful, but no restaurant should require people to discover a deadly rule by suffering through it.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alone but calm, treating the moon pizzeria as another strange Storyverse stop. The chalky slice and crawling mites surprise him, then fear and pain rise when the mites burn his body. He becomes steady once he stops fighting and deliberately permits the mites to stay, turning a threat into armor. The other customer's death shifts him from curiosity to anger. The mammoth chef's attack lets Jet channel that anger into focused strength, and the ending resolves with practical accountability instead of revenge.

## Ending

The final page shows the defeated mammoth chef in a massive crater, humbled by Jet's moon-mite armored fist. Jet makes the command simple: add a disclaimer to the menu. The chef obeys because he respects a warrior who beat him in combat. The pizzeria now warns customers that moon pizza is chalky, uncomfortable, and can grant armor only if the moon mites are accepted instead of resisted.
