# 08/12/2026 - The Glimmer Prison

Dream Comics entry for August 12, 2026.

While flying over a city, Jet recognizes a mysterious time-displaced woman by her bluish-green eyes—only for the recurring darkness to reveal itself through her body. Jet fuses with Silonis, transforming into a humanoid rainbow-crystal being, and turns the entity's attempted possession into a trap.

After giving the darkness a temporary crystal body, Jet witnesses its true goal: bringing buildings to life so they can replace humanity as the lives that matter. He strips away the stolen life energy, banishes the bodiless spirit into the Glimmer, restores the city, and discovers a new defensive power in his bond with the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life.
