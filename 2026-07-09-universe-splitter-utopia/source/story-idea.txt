Adapt the 07/09/2026 dream journal entry into an eight-page Dream Comics package.

Core adaptation:

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in space. He flies onto a floating couch among the stars and sits with Doctor Bear, a humanoid bear wearing glasses. They discuss the nature of American Monomyth stories, especially the idea of a lone special hero being treated above everyone else. Inspired by this conversation, Jet creates a new Storyverse world and shows it to Doctor Bear.

The new world contains a human utopia where no one receives special treatment. Everyone treats everyone else with equal respect and kindness. A young orphan troll finds their way into the city and is welcomed with open arms. The troll grows up there and is treated just as well as everyone else.

A dark elf arrives and demands worship because he is immortal and has vast magical power. The people refuse because their society treats everyone equally. Insulted, the dark elf casts a spell that brainwashes every human in the utopia. The spell only works on humans, so the troll confronts the dark elf and asks him to dispel the brainwashing at once.

The troll is physically stronger, but the dark elf is clever. The elf uses a Universe Splitter device that divides the universe based on choices. The elf creates one universe where he surrenders and retreats and another where he does not. The visible world resolves as the version where he does not retreat, and he tells the troll to be satisfied that a better universe exists somewhere else.

The troll refuses to abandon his own world. He grabs the Universe Splitter and creates one world where he banishes himself in shame and another where he uppercuts the dark elf to the other side of the planet. The visible world resolves as the version where the troll uppercuts the dark elf. The brainwashing spell breaks because the dark elf is too far away from the humans.

The restored humans wonder what happened in the world where the troll banished himself. The troll says that in that world he likely went beyond the city to request help from a hero's guild. He explains that the Universe Splitter taught him there is always a way to save the day. Life returns to normal. The troll is not celebrated as a special hero and receives no monument. He is treated like everyone else, exactly as he wants.
