# Treatment

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet appears inside a lonely Storyverse castle as a blonde hippie werewolf, follows a mystical pull toward an ordinary teddy bear, and defeats a window-scaling claw monster through peace-sign eye pokes and crushing hugs before the bear reveals hidden colorful energy.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants connection inside an empty castle, but the only being he finds is a silent teddy bear that feels more important than it looks. When a clawed monster destroys the bear, Jet must defend what his intuition says matters while trapped in a form that blocks normal aggression.

## Theme

Intuition can reveal invisible truths, and limits can force more creative solutions than raw power.

## Ending

Jet's crew arrives expecting confusion over a teddy bear, only to see the headless bear leaking radiant color. The final image reframes Jet's strange rage and improvised fight as protection of something real but hidden.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins isolated and curious, becomes mystically protective, erupts into rage when the bear is harmed, transforms that rage into absurd nontraditional action through his hippie werewolf limits, then ends validated by the bear's hidden light.

## Cast

- Jet as the hippie werewolf: the protagonist, currently blonde, wolf-like, and dressed in peaceful hippie clothing instead of his usual dream outfit.
- Teddy bear: silent, apparently inanimate, but mystically charged.
- Claw monster: a massive-clawed intruder who climbs the castle wall and attacks the bear.
- Crew: Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper arrive for the closing reveal.
