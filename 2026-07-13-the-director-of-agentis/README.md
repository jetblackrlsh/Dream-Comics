# The Director of Agentis

Dream Comics entry for July 13, 2026.

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and wakes in a futuristic command center wearing a business suit. A guide identifies him as the director of Agentis, where the people waiting for orders are AI agents made visible. The strange organization reframes Jet's everyday creative habits and helps him imagine a clearer way to coordinate comics, support options, and a possible home for his wider media landscape.

The finished package includes the journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, assembled PDF, and preview artifacts.
