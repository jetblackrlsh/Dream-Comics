# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. He can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he moves from guarded readiness to anniversary joy, then to reverent astral calm and finally blissful heightened awareness.

## Leon

A ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon is both a UFO and a crew home, with a soft supernatural aura, rounded saucer silhouette, luminous green tractor beam, and warm interior party chamber.

Continuity notes: Keep Leon's exterior ghostly, green, and saucer-shaped. Inside Leon, use curved walls, soft green instrument lights, and windows showing cosmic bubble universes.

## Crew

Johnson is an orange humanoid goldfish in a blue astronaut suit without a helmet. Second Brain is a floating brain made of sparkling purple Lucid Light. Skelebot is a humanoid speedster robot. Overdrive is a metallic chrome robot with spiky slicked back yellow hair, glowing green eyes, and a full yellow outfit. Savannah is a pale skinned cyborg woman with long black hair and bangs, glowing red eyes, a futuristic silver bodysuit, and a purple-blue crystal Death Sword. Tecton is a green skinned muscular humanoid cat ogre barbarian in a brown loin cloth and silver baldric. Chipper is a small levitating silver crab robot.

Continuity notes: The crew can appear as a celebratory ensemble; emphasize their silhouettes and key colors rather than crowding every detail into every panel.

## Humanoid Barbarian Frogs

Tall amphibian warriors with green and teal skin, webbed hands, heavy brows, muscular builds, leather straps, bone ornaments, rough spears, and stone axes. They look intimidating in fog, then friendly once the surprise party is revealed.

Continuity notes: They are not villains in this issue; their threat is a setup for the anniversary reveal.

## Berry Octopus

A strange party creature shaped like a small octopus made of glossy berries. It has raspberry, blueberry, and blackberry textures across its tentacles, bright curious eyes, and a soft bioluminescent glow. It emits musky ink punch into cups and later offers Jet a special green psychedelic punch.

Continuity notes: Keep it whimsical and alien rather than gross. The punch should look magical, aromatic, and dreamlike.

## Miniature Amethyst Crystal Castle

A tiny castle carved from glowing amethyst crystal, sitting on a shrine altar. It radiates violet light, star-like reflections, and a sacred calm. It represents gratitude for nightly lucid dreaming.

Continuity notes: Treat it as the visual anchor of the shrine page, not a character with a face.

## White-Hooded Godlike Figures

Faceless women wearing white hoods and flowing white robes. They appear serene, godlike, and ceremonial. Their faces remain hidden in clean white shadow, not horror darkness.

Continuity notes: They should feel sacred and guiding rather than threatening.

## Spring of Life

A luminous sacred pool with clear turquoise water, gold-white ripples, floating petals, and rings of Lucid Light. Swimming through it begins Jet's transformation into a hooded godlike state.

Continuity notes: Use water, white robes, and purple starry Lucid Light as the transition symbols.

## Mountain of Qa

A cosmic mountain peak above creation, surrounded by floating Storyverse bubble universes in a vast void. The summit is quiet, high, crystalline, and sacred, with dawn-gold light and purple Lucid Light around Jet.

Continuity notes: The final image should feel expansive and above all creation, with Jet meditating in bliss rather than fighting.
