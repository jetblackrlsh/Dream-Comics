Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone on a rooftop where he tests several custom superhero suits. Jet-pack armor and built-in weapons keep becoming heavier and less satisfying, so he replaces them with a light yellow anti-gravity force-field belt. The belt allows flight, protection, shockwaves, and focused force beams by responding to the direction in which Jet flexes his limbs.

Jet pairs the belt with a superhero costume: a full-head black mask with white eye lenses, green armored chest padding, black boots and gloves, a black cape with an orange inner layer, and the yellow belt. While enjoying flight above the city, he meets a giant flaming ogre. Jet says he does not want another monster fight, and the ogre explains that he is also a hero and did not come to fight.

The ogre swallows Jet, revealing a comfortable bachelor pad and arcade inside his body. Because damage from the inside makes the ogre stronger, Jet safely punches his bones to power him up. The friendly ogre is named Pyrogre. Jet and Pyrogre become an unusual superhero team that prefers relaxing and enjoying their powers, while remaining willing to help anyone who truly needs them.
