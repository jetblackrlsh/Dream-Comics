# Treatment - The Elemental Betrayal

## Premise

Compassion gives two incompatible halves a second chance, but desire for the Life Sword turns that gift into betrayal. Silonis reveals that its bond with Jet—and its own living will—cannot be defeated simply by taking it from his hand.

## Tone and visual direction

The issue is a vivid, high-saturation modern-anime fantasy adventure. The cave begins in green, amber, and volcanic orange, expands into brilliant rainbow crystal spectacle during the battle, and closes in the star-filled purple-blue Storyverse. Every page must make the action obvious through strong silhouettes, continuous character design, and long readable caption boxes. There are no speech balloons or thought balloons.

## Story structure

Jet reaches a cave and is immediately bound by living vines. Their humanoid owner wears a crown made of fire and demands Silonis, one of the Storyverse's most powerful artifacts. Jet refuses and cuts himself free. The creature's fury makes its crown burn hotter until its own body is reduced to ash.

Pitying the self-destructive being, Jet uses Silonis to separate the burned vines from the surviving flame. The sword's life energy restores them as two independent beings. Jet explains that the plant and fire elementals can coexist peacefully if they simply avoid touching. Their uncertainty turns into shared mischief, and they attack the person who saved them.

The plant elemental binds Jet. Anticipating his escape, the fire elemental seizes Silonis and superheats it until Jet must let go. This appears to complete their victory, but Silonis is alive. The sword spins like a turbine, summons a tornado, and blows out the fire elemental. It returns to Jet, heals him, and raises a rainbow crystal barrier that frees him from the vines. Jet throws Silonis, which becomes a spinning crystal mulcher and tears the plant elemental apart before returning.

With both attackers defeated, Jet considers the deeper danger surrounding the Life Sword. Silonis may not corrupt anyone directly, but its immense power changes how others behave. The original fused being destroyed itself over the sword; its restored halves sacrificed their new lives for another attempt to possess it. Jet flies into the Storyverse understanding that, in every world, someone will want to hold the power of life.

## Ending

The final image resolves the physical threat and preserves the dream's philosophical unease: Jet and Silonis fly safely among the cosmic bubble universes, but the cave recedes below like a warning about the desire their power inspires.
