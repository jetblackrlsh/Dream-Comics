# Page Script - The Sword Remembers

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, Silonis, the librarian, the knight, the darkness, and the library follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

Jet stands between the newly living knight inside a rainbow-crystal shield and the corrupted librarian reaching desperately toward Silonis. The sword flies back into Jet's hand as black tendrils cross the infinite night library. Visible cover text only: "THE SWORD REMEMBERS" and "AUGUST 11, 2026." No issue number.

## Page 2 - The Library of Lives

Jet discovers the infinite archive, sees the knight shelving books, and learns why the librarian rereads her own life book.

Caption text:

- "I found myself inside a library so vast that its shelves seemed to continue forever."
- "A knight carried and shelved books, while a pale-blue-haired woman relaxed behind the front desk."
- "She told me she was reading her life book—one volume among countless books describing every person's life."
- "The past became fixed only after it happened. The future kept rewriting itself as an uncertain estimate."
- "I found the idea deeply fascinating."

## Page 3 - Half Rest

The librarian explains that maintaining the knight prevents either her body or magic from resting completely. Jet offers help through Silonis.

Caption text:

- "The librarian cared for the enormous archive almost alone."
- "By day, she rested her magic but worked with her body."
- "By night, she rested her body but spent her magic animating the armor."
- "She could only ever half rest."
- "I wanted to free her from that burden, so I used Silonis to make the armor genuinely alive."

## Page 4 - Genuine Life

The awakened armor drops its books and charges. Jet contains it before anyone is harmed.

Caption text:

- "The moment the armor became alive, it went ballistic."
- "It dropped the books it had carried for so long and raced straight toward the librarian."
- "I acted before anger could become tragedy."
- "Silonis formed a sealed rainbow-crystal shield around the knight."
- "With the attack contained, I demanded the truth."

## Page 5 - The Knight's Truth

The knight explains that the librarian suppressed its will, then the librarian seizes Silonis as if greeting a lost companion.

Caption text:

- "The knight revealed that the librarian had animated it as a servant."
- "When it eventually asked for freedom, she answered with a spell that suppressed its will."
- "Silonis had restored the freedom she buried—and all the knight's anger with it."
- "Then the librarian seized my sword as if greeting someone she had known before."
- "She called Silonis by name and cried, 'Oh, baby, you've returned.'"

## Page 6 - Silonis Chooses

Silonis rejects the librarian, returns to Jet, and becomes the instrument of a nonlethal purification attempt.

Caption text:

- "The librarian clutched Silonis and spoke of memories the sword did not share."
- "Silonis rejected her."
- "It tore free from her grasp and flew back to me."
- "She chased after it, unable to accept that the sword had chosen."
- "I pierced her with the Sword of Life—not to injure her, but to heal and purify whatever had corrupted her."

## Page 7 - The Same Darkness

Purification succeeds for only a moment. The corruption returns and reveals its repeated pursuit through many hosts.

Caption text:

- "For one moment, the darkness left her."
- "Then it crawled back."
- "Her eyes turned red as the same presence spoke through another stolen face."
- "It had been jumping from person to person, always seeking the Rainbow Crystal Sword."
- "Every host, every obsession, every attempt to separate Silonis from me had belonged to the same darkness."

## Page 8 - Beyond the Window

Jet understands the pattern, refuses to surrender Silonis, and escapes the corrupted archive into the Storyverse.

Caption text:

- "I had mistaken the pursuit for ordinary greed, fame, or hunger for power."
- "Now I understood: one darkness had been moving through many hosts to reach Silonis."
- "I would not leave the Sword of Life with the corrupted librarian."
- "I took flight, passed through the library's immense glass window, and returned to the Storyverse."
- "The darkness remembered the sword. It still did not understand me."
