# Treatment

## Title

05/27/2026: Omega Knight and the Stone Couch

## Logline

Jet challenges the mightiest Knightmare he can imagine, a building-sized black armor giant whose rubies hold the souls of defeated enemies, then uses geokinesis to tear the monster apart, shatter its soul-rubies, and leave the freed spirits as future trouble.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants a harder Knightmare battle than the recent faceless armor fights. Omega Knight is not merely another suit of armor: it is a kaiju-scale black Knightmare empowered by thousands of trapped enemy souls stored in mystical rubies across its armor.

## Theme

Knowing the material of a threat can reveal its weakness. Omega Knight looks unbeatable because of size and legend, but Jet sees stone, metal, and rubies, then turns that understanding into a clean solution.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins with deliberate confidence, choosing the arena and asking for the mightiest Knightmare possible. Omega Knight's scale and history make the challenge feel cosmic, but learning its construction changes the mood from intimidation to technical certainty. Jet defeats it, tests its regeneration, and calmly removes its power source, ending with anticipation rather than fear.

## Ending

Omega Knight loses its magical strength when Jet makes the rubies embedded in its armor burst. The trapped Knightmare souls pour free into the Storyverse arena, promising future challenges that Jet welcomes.
