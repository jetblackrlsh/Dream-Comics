# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life: a colorful futuristic crystal blade with distinct red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet facets. His energy tether resembles a flexible rope made of golden glittering light clipped to his belt.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy shining like a night sky full of stars.
- Jet never wears a cape, mask, helmet, conventional superhero costume, or unrelated armor.
- Expression range: curious interest, cautious skepticism, physical unease, lucid concentration, uncertainty, compassion, shock at his clone, disorientation, renewed confidence, playful surprise, combat focus, victorious relief.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, face, beard, dreadlocks, outfit, bag, sword, and tether on pages 1-4.

## The Jet clone

- An exact physical duplicate of Jet, including dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks beneath a black hoodie, “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, striped black athletic pants, white sneakers, and black cross-body belt bag.
- Emerges alive but weak and bewildered from the torn black bag after being restored by the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life.
- Has no separate sword or energy tether in this episode.
- The clone is a second living person, not a mirror reflection, hologram, corpse, zombie, or evil version.
- On the cover and page 4, Jet and the clone must be visibly distinct as two bodies while remaining exact doubles.

## Comic-book man

- Adult light-brown-skinned man in his late thirties with a narrow oval face, short neatly parted black hair, rectangular amber glasses, brown eyes, and a friendly but slightly evasive smile.
- Wears a burgundy cardigan over a cream button-down shirt, charcoal trousers, and brown shoes.
- Sits at a simple square gray table with two plain chairs in the otherwise empty black void.
- Expression range: conversational enthusiasm, vague self-importance, nervous evasion, then sudden predatory transformation.
- He does not wear publisher credentials, a superhero costume, or rat features before transforming.

## Rat monster

- The comic-book man's transformed state: a massive hulking humanoid rat at least nine feet tall, with coarse charcoal-gray fur, a long hairless pink tail, huge muscular arms, hunched animal posture, red eyes, a long rat muzzle, sharp incisors, torn remnants of the burgundy cardigan, and oversized clawed hands.
- Behaves like a wild animal after transforming. It punches the black bag repeatedly, then scurries away on all fours into the void.
- The attack is violent but entirely non-graphic: no blood, organs, exposed wounds, gore, or bodily fragments.
- The human man and rat monster are the same character and must not appear simultaneously except as a transformation transition.

## Black bag and inner silhouette

- A large glossy black bag-like cocoon, roughly human-torso length, with no label, tie, handle, trash, or gore.
- Before the rat attack, a bright purple-white humanoid silhouette is visible curled safely through its semi-translucent surface.
- After the attack, the bag is flattened and torn but remains non-graphic. Rainbow life energy restores the figure from within and splits the material open cleanly.

## Cookware heroine body

- Adult Black woman superhero with deep brown skin, amber eyes, an athletic build, a heart-shaped face, and thick shoulder-length copper-red curls swept to one side.
- Wears a fitted teal superhero bodysuit with copper-gold armor lines, a short asymmetrical white apron-like armored sash at the hips, dark teal boots, and copper bracers shaped subtly like pan handles.
- A simple circular copper frying-pan emblem sits at the center of her chest. She has no cape, mask, helmet, exposed cleavage, or separate weapons before Jet manifests cookware.
- This is the female body Jet inhabits after regaining lucidity. Internally, the consciousness remains Jet. Her expressions therefore follow Jet's emotional arc: disorientation, recognition, resourceful surprise, fierce focus, and victory.
- Continuity rule: keep her face, skin tone, copper-red curls, teal-and-copper suit, white sash, chest emblem, boots, and bracers identical on pages 1 and 5-8.

## Telekinetic shapeshifter

- Initially appears as an ordinary prospective recruit: androgynous adult with olive skin, short silver hair, gray street jacket, black shirt, and dark pants.
- Transforms into an exact duplicate of the cookware heroine: same deep brown skin, amber eyes, copper-red curls, teal-and-copper suit, white sash, emblem, boots, bracers, proportions, and height.
- The villainous duplicate is distinguished only by a cold aggressive expression, glowing magenta eyes during attacks, and a vivid magenta telekinetic aura around hands and levitated objects.
- Telekinesis appears as clean magenta energy ribbons and force waves that lift, throw, or shatter ordinary environment objects. It is not Lucid Light.
- The shapeshifter never becomes Jet and never copies his male appearance.

## Lucid Light cookware

- Ordinary recognizable metal frying pans, saucepans, stockpots, skillets, lids, and baking trays manifested from sparkling purple star-filled Lucid Light.
- Once formed, each piece looks like polished silver, dark iron, or copper cookware with a thin violet cosmic glow around its edges.
- The objects contain no faces, limbs, magical runes, brand logos, readable labels, gore, or food.
- Jet first hurls individual pieces, then uses a frying pan and stockpot as nonlethal melee weapons, and finally summons hundreds of pieces as a controlled overhead storm.

## The endless void and table

- Absolute black emptiness with no visible ground, sky, horizon, walls, stars, buildings, or landscape.
- Jet, the table, chairs, bag, characters, and Lucid Light cast subtle pools of colored rim light that allow them to be seen.
- The table is a simple square gray table with two plain gray chairs, emphasizing how ordinary the conversation appears inside an impossible setting.

## Superhero recruitment drive

- A bright fast-moving montage environment that replaces the void after Jet loses lucidity.
- Diverse superheroes in colorful original costumes approach ordinary adults in city plazas, apartment courtyards, parks, and storefront streets. Recruits visibly move from confusion to consideration and support.
- Do not include copyrighted or recognizable existing superheroes, logos, franchise costumes, or established Storyverse crew members.

## Established crew continuity

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's Storyverse team. None appears physically in this comic. Do not insert them into the void, recruitment montage, battle setting, reflections, silhouettes, or cover.
