# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. He can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he acts alone in a crowded nightclub crisis.

## Lucid Light Watch

A sleek futuristic transformation watch made of sparkling purple starry Lucid Light, rainbow crystal facets, and a glowing circular dial. The dial projects tiny holographic icons of Jet's crew forms.

Continuity notes: The watch glows brightly whenever Jet selects a form. It allows Jet to become Lucid Light Variants of his crew, abbreviated as LLV forms.

## LLV-Skelebot

Jet transformed into a Lucid Light variant of Skelebot: a heroic humanoid speedster robot built from bone-white robotic limbs, chrome joints, sleek athletic plating, and purple starry Lucid Light seams. Jet's warm brown eyes and ghostly dreadlock silhouettes can appear inside the form to keep his identity clear.

Continuity notes: This form is lean, fast, and aerodynamic. Show speed trails, afterimages, motion streaks, and sparkling purple Lucid Light. It should read as a heroic robotic speedster, not a horror skeleton.

## LLV-Tecton

Jet transformed into a Lucid Light variant of Tecton: a massive green-skinned humanoid cat ogre barbarian with powerful muscles, feline ears and features, a brown loin cloth, a silver baldric, and glowing purple Lucid Light lines. This form can generate and control hardlight holographic blue cyber weapons.

Continuity notes: The defining image is a muscular green cat-ogre hero commanding many glowing blue futuristic weapons into a protective dome. The dome should feel solid, technical, and heroic.

## LLV-Leon

Jet transformed into a Lucid Light variant of Leon: a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow, translucent hull, and purple starry Lucid Light shining inside the saucer.

Continuity notes: LLV-Leon should look like a heroic living UFO. The tractor beam is green, luminous, precise, and strong enough to lift Mr. Stopwatch cleanly off the ground.

## Mr. Stopwatch

A humanoid stopwatch villain with a polished brass-and-chrome clock body, a round stopwatch face for a head and torso, thin mechanical limbs, gloved hands, glossy black shoes, and a smug theatrical posture. His clock face has expressive eyes and a sly grin built into the dial.

Continuity notes: Keep him elegant, tricky, and readable as a living stopwatch. He should smirk during the reveal, panic during the capture, and never look like a normal human.

## Android Guest

A nightclub guest who is secretly an android bomb. He appears at first like a stylish clubgoer in a dark jacket with neon trim, but panels reveal synthetic seams, glowing red warning lights, a chest pressure gauge, and overheating circuitry.

Continuity notes: The android should look more tragic and dangerous than evil. Its pressure reading becomes the visual countdown to the explosion.

## Nightclub Guests

A diverse crowd of stylish Storyverse partygoers in colorful futuristic club fashion, neon accessories, and expressive faces. They are civilians who need rescue.

Continuity notes: Show them moving from confusion to fear to relief. Avoid making any guest resemble the recurring crew unless explicitly transformed through Jet.

## Authorities

Storyverse authorities in sleek silver, navy, and teal rescue armor with helmets, containment cuffs, hover lights, and official-looking energy shields.

Continuity notes: They arrive after the blast is contained. Their role is to secure Mr. Stopwatch while Jet departs.

## Storyverse Nightclub

A neon nightclub floating inside a Storyverse bubble universe. It has saturated magenta, cyan, gold, and violet lights, mirrored floors, holographic speakers, a glowing dance floor, and windows showing cosmic bubble universes outside.

Continuity notes: Even indoors, keep hints of the Storyverse visible through windows, portals, or cosmic reflections. Lucid Light is sparkling purple energy like a night sky full of stars.
