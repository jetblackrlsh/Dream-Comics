# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a rope-like golden glittering energy tether. In this issue, he also gains plant-hero armor: grassy green root armor wrapping his torso, arms, and legs, a living moss cape, small blooming flowers on the armor, and purple Lucid Light sparkling around the transformation.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, belt bag, rainbow sword, and golden tether visible whenever possible, even when the plant armor forms over the outfit.

## Mall Security Knights

Noseless humanoid knights who act as Mall World security. Each wears a red chest plate, blue greaves, metallic gauntlets, and simple silver-gray armor pieces. Their faces have smooth skin where noses should be, making the absence of noses visually obvious. They appear stern, procedural, and defensive rather than monstrous.

Continuity notes: Repeat the red chest plates, blue greaves, and no-nose faces on every knight.

## Plant Hero Power

Not a separate character in the present dream, but a remembered heroic force from the previous dream. It appears as green living armor, braided roots, leaves, flowers, vines, and a moss cape around Jet. Its color palette is fresh grass green, moss green, white flower petals, yellow flower centers, and tiny sparkles of purple Lucid Light.

Continuity notes: The plant power should look protective and restorative, not corrupted or thorny.

## Superhero School Students

A blurred mix of superhero students whose identities are not fully remembered on waking. Show them as distinct silhouettes and partial designs rather than stable named characters: a speedster with bright motion streaks, a floating student with starry energy, a bulky armored rival, a train conductor-like student, and background students in colorful uniforms. Faces can be partially obscured by motion blur, light, distance, or dream haze.

Continuity notes: The ambiguity is intentional. They should feel like possible friends, rivals, or other dreamers, but not like fully identified recurring cast members.

## Mall World and Superhero School

Mall World is an infinite dream mall with endless atriums, escalators, glass railings, glowing storefronts, polished tile floors, and impossible hallways. The superhero school is connected to the mall through a hidden corridor or doorway, with academy halls, training rooms, a sleek train platform, cartoon lounge, cafeteria, and bright heroic emblems.
