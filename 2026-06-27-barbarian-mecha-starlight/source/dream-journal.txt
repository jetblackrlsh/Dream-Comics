Dream journal entry dated 06/27/2026:

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void.

Recurring continuity:
- Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, warm brown eyes, a rainbow futuristic crystal sword, and a golden glittery energy tether.
- Leon is Jet's ghostly UFO, with an ethereal green glow.
- Johnson is an orange humanoid goldfish in a blue astronaut suit without a helmet.
- Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light.
- Skelebot is Johnson's humanoid robot speedster.
- Overdrive is a chrome robot with spiky slicked-back yellow hair, glowing green eyes, yellow clothes, and matter-manipulation powers.
- Lucid Light is sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a starry night sky.
- Savannah is a pale-skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, forehead-covering bangs, glowing red eyes, a futuristic silver bodysuit, and the purple-blue Death Sword.
- Tecton is a green-skinned muscular humanoid cat ogre barbarian wearing a brown loincloth and silver baldric.
- Chipper is a small levitating silver crab robot.

Dream events:
I arrive alone as a muscular barbarian.

I initially am excited to be a barbarian, until I witness how cruel the barbarians are.

I try to stand against the other barbarians, but I am attacked by their giant leader "Big Barbarian".

While I lack the strength to overpower "Big Barbarian", my Lucid Light gives me a mystical edge that allows me to summon a giant barbarian mecha construct that I use to fight all of the barbarians at once.

The barbarians offer me all of their stolen wealth if I agree to leave them alone and never return to their camp.

I refuse their offer and use my Lucid Light to create a transport system of pipes that weave through the sky between every village.

The transport system does not allow barbarians to use it.

Using the transport system I take the barbarians' stolen gold and captive villagers, and transport them back to their respective villages.

I warn the barbarians that if they try anything like this again, that I will return and defeat them.

I ride off on a horse made of starlight to a futuristic superhero base, where Second Brain and I discuss AI pet translators.

We discuss whether said translators would work on wolves and big cats if built into animal power armor, how the translators might make more sense for people to wear instead of their pets, how they could help young animals evolve to have a better understanding of English, and how they could potentially help convince people to stop making their animals fight by allowing them to hear what their dogs are saying during battles.

Overall this dream was a lot of fun, and felt like a proper superhero adventure in a science fantasy world.
