Dream journal entry dated 05/28/2026.

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and enters the Storyverse continuity of lucid dreams that began on 06/11/2025. The Storyverse is a multiverse where possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes floating in a cosmic void. Jet first explored the Elven Village there, then widened his adventures with Leon the ghostly green UFO, Johnson the orange humanoid goldfish in a blue astronaut suit, Second Brain the floating Lucid Light brain, Skelebot the humanoid speedster robot, Overdrive the chrome robot with yellow hair and green eyes, Savannah the pale cyborg swordswoman, Tecton the green cat-ogre barbarian, and Chipper the small levitating silver crab robot.

Jet, a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks under a black hoodie over a Dream Anime graphic tee, faces another Knightmare alone. He wears black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow crystal sword and can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a starry night sky.

This Knightmare is a living suit of knight armor with magnetic abilities. It controls individual pieces of itself from a distance, firing its gauntlets like projectiles and reeling them back to reattach. The fighting style reminds Jet of his first battle against Savannah, so he uses the same tactic: he activates his rainbow crystal sword's shield dome to protect himself from the armor's attacks.

But Knightmares do not tire like normal opponents. They have singular focus, and they are not as mindless as they seem. The Knightmare sends a gauntlet into the ground and makes it pop up inside Jet's dome. Jet is trapped in a confined space of his own making with the gauntlet. He slices it apart, but the Knightmare's magnetism lets it control even the shards.

Jet wonders how small the pieces would need to be before the Knightmare loses control. Then he wonders how small he would need to be before he is no longer a viable target. He compresses the rainbow energy dome while using Lucid Light to shrink smaller and smaller. He becomes so small that he falls out of the bounds of normal reality.

Jet keeps shrinking until he loops around as a giant in an exact copy of reality at a sub-quantum scale. With a mighty stomp, he crushes the magnetic Knightmare under his foot. The sub-quantum copy is entangled with macroscopic reality, so the stomp destroys an entire Storyverse bubble universe in both realities.

As above, so below. Jet knows more Knightmares are out in the Storyverse and looks forward to defeating the strongest among them.
