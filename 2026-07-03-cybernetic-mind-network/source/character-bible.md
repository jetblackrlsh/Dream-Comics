# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. His signature items are a rainbow futuristic crystal sword and an energy tether that looks like a rope made of golden glittery light. When lucid, he is surrounded by sparkling purple Lucid Light that looks like a night sky full of stars. Jet should remain recognizable in every page even when shown as a first-person pilot silhouette, reflection, or variant overlay.

## Skelebot

Skelebot is Johnson's humanoid speedster robot. He has a robotic skeleton body with clean mechanical bones, bright green speed trails, expressive glowing eyes, and athletic runner posture. He moves quickly and precisely. In this comic he assists Jet during the opening healing scene and appears again at the end as a continuity anchor beside Jet in the Storyverse void.

## Restored Pharaoh

The pharaoh begins as a red mummy zombie woman wrapped in ragged ancient bandages, with glowing red curse cracks and a desperate expression. After Jet's rainbow sword heals her, she becomes a beautiful restored pharaoh with warm brown skin, regal gold-and-turquoise Egyptian clothing, a radiant headdress, calm eyes, and clean glowing royal jewelry. The transformation should read as healing, not combat.

## Cybernetic Variants

The variants are alternate versions of Jet and other people seen through cybernetic first-person channels. Jet variants should preserve Jet's face, dark-brown skin, beard, and dreadlocks when visible, but their clothing and life circumstances may change. Cybernetic interfaces should vary from clean holographic overlays to cheap grainy visual noise to jailbroken psychedelic colors. The homeless Jet variant wears worn street clothes and carries a backpack while receiving money from a rich family but being unable to settle anywhere. The psychedelic variant sees the world through intense neon distortions, fractal overlays, and color trails.

## The Magic Solitaire Game

The game is a recurring visual rule that appears across many variants. Players use familiar solitaire-style playing cards in trick-taking rounds to deal magical attacks. The cards produce visible spell effects such as fire, ice, lightning, shields, and dream-colored energy. The game should look easy to understand: five rules shown as five glowing icons or simple caption beats, with cards clearly causing magic.
