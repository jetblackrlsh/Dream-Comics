# Story Idea - The Portal-Blade Assassin

While writing magical theory inside his study, Jet realizes that ordinary spell-inscribed swords and rare crystal-tech blades belong to different tiers of power. He imagines a dangerous portal-crystal world that supplies automated teleportation technology—then an assassin wielding one of its hidden blades begins appearing, slashing, and vanishing. Jet uses Silonis to sense the attacker's life energy, parries every arrival, and sends the assassin through Porta into the Empty Dimension, trusting that genuine freedom will persuade them not to return.
