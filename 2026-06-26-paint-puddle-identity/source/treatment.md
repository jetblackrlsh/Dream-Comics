# Treatment

## Logline

After sleep paralysis launches Jet into a paint-splattered sketch, he escapes into a spinning cosmic museum, survives being reduced to paint and rebuilt as blocky matter, then reshapes himself into clay to claim his identity on his own terms.

## Central Conflict

Jet's body is repeatedly defined by the spaces and beings around him: first as a figure trapped in a sketch, then as a puddle poured into a machine, then as a blocky reconstruction. He has to reject externally assigned forms and deliberately choose a self-made shape.

## Theme

Identity is not the place that contains you, the form a machine assigns you, or the role another being pours you into. Identity becomes strongest when it stays flexible enough for self-definition.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alone and disoriented inside a crinkled sketch. He becomes unstable when the museum turns him into paint, powerless when the squid pilot pours him into a reconstitution machine, and frustrated when the machine gives him a rigid block body. The turning point comes when he realizes that lucidity does not mean accepting the next form offered to him. By reshaping into clay, he becomes calm, deliberate, and self-authored.

## Ending

Jet stands in the white, purple, and gold museum as a clay version of himself, with the failed block shell and paint puddle behind him. The spinning hallways slow down, and the final image shows him sculpting his own face and posture while the Storyverse bubbles glow beyond the skylight.
