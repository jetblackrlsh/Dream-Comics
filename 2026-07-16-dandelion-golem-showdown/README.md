# 07/16/2026 - Dandelion Golem Showdown

Dream Comics entry for July 16, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet arrives alone in a bright city and combines his golden energy tether with Lucid Light to swing, fly, and flip without depending on gravity. A purple spike-covered villain attacks him for the rainbow crystal Sword of Life, pins Jet's feet with enormous underground claws, and boasts that the attack can continue forever. Jet cuts himself free, keeps the shattered claw fragments, transforms them into dandelions with Lucid Light, and blows the seeds back at the villain. The seeds become dandelion golems that divide every time they are struck, overwhelming the villain until he surrenders.

The finished package includes the journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, assembled PDF, and preview artifacts.
