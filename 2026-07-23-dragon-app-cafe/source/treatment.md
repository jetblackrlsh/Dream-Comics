# Treatment

## Logline

When lucid dreamer Jet tries to design an internet café where ordinary people use AI coding agents to build and share their own apps, an unexpected dragon transformation pushes him to combine communal invention with magical candies and safe superpower training—until his mysterious partner reveals that making Jet the world's only dragon café owner was her competitive strategy all along.

## Central conflict

Jet wants to explore a practical creative idea inside a dream: a café that gives people affordable access to AI coding agents and grows through the applications its visitors make. The dream keeps insisting on spectacle by transforming him into a humanoid dragon. Jet initially treats the transformation as an annoying interruption, but then chooses to integrate fantasy into the business without abandoning the clear, useful idea that interested him. The woman creates a second tension when she reveals that other fantastical internet cafés already exist. Jet resolves both problems by recognizing that the strongest concept is not technology or magic alone, but a safe place where ordinary people can become creators in several different ways.

## Theme

The best imaginative spaces do not force a choice between practical invention and fantasy. Technology can let people create new tools, while fantasy can let them explore new versions of themselves. A shared environment becomes more valuable when every visitor contributes something to the next visitor's experience.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins curious, analytical, and excited to reason through an unusual business model. He becomes annoyed and physically disoriented when purple scales and orange wings interrupt the discussion. After deciding to take the dream's hint, irritation becomes playful invention: magical candy solves the missing food question, and private training rooms make temporary powers safe. The woman's confession briefly reframes Jet as part of her marketing plan, but her logic earns his acceptance. Jet ends proud and delighted because the café's identity now comes from a coherent union of everything that fascinated him.

## Continuity

This dream belongs to the Storyverse continuity that began on June 11, 2025. Jet begins with his established dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks beneath a black hoodie, visible “Dream Anime” graphic tee, black athletic pants with one white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden glittering energy tether, and sparkling purple star-filled Lucid Light.

The transformation changes Jet's skin into purple dragon scales and gives him a dragon-like face, orange membrane wings, a purple tail, clawed hands, and clawed feet. His beard, dreadlocks, warm brown eyes, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, belt bag, and overall identity remain clearly recognizable. The transformed design persists from page 4 through the ending.

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper remain Jet's established crew, but none appears physically in this focused episode. Jet's rainbow sword and golden tether remain on his person but are not used.

## Ending

The final page shows the fully opened Dragon App Café operating as one connected ecosystem. Visitors build apps at AI-agent computers, add finished projects to a luminous communal library, choose colorful power candies, and safely practice temporary abilities inside transparent private training rooms. The woman proudly presents the bustling space while dragon-form Jet welcomes newcomers beneath a sign that identifies the café by title and date, without an issue number. The visual consequence is unmistakable: each visitor enters with an idea and leaves behind something that makes the café richer for everyone who comes next.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: Dragon-form Jet presides over one spectacular café containing AI creation stations, the communal app library, power candies, and safe training rooms.
2. Jet induces lucidity, arrives in an empty building, meets the waiting woman, and explains why the vanished American internet-café experience fascinates him.
3. Jet develops the app-creation café: admission includes AI coding agents, every visitor builds an idea, and completed apps join a growing public library with a popularity display.
4. Jet unexpectedly transforms into a purple humanoid dragon with orange wings, becomes annoyed by the fantasy interruption, and decides to take the dream's hint.
5. Jet adds colorful chewy candies that grant temporary superpowers, turning the café's food counter into another form of creative experimentation.
6. Jet designs private reinforced training rooms where customers can safely test powers on inanimate targets, never on one another.
7. The woman reveals that she transformed Jet because magical internet cafés already exist and a dragon proprietor is her marketing strategy; Jet accepts the logic and explains their larger advantage.
8. The Dragon App Café opens: visitors create and share apps, sample powers, train safely, and leave new experiences behind for the next person.
