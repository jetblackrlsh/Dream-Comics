Dream Journal 08/01/2026: The Dinosaur Chef

In a prehistoric jungle, Jet refuses a shadow-faced chef's demand that he kill a harmless mango-colored dinosaur for bacon. When she stabs him, grows into a giant humanoid dinosaur, and tries to crush him, Jet heals with Silonis and uses Tetheron to make her experience the prey's fear and grief. Empathy briefly horrifies her, but she chooses her hunger and tries to eat Jet instead. Jet forms Lucid Light armor, flies beyond the limits of her giant form, and escapes into the Storyverse without abandoning his principles.
