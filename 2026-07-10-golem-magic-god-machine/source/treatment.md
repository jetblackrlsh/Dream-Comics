# Treatment

## Logline

After lucid dreamer Jet creates a robot-powered utopia in a new Storyverse universe, its ultimate machine assimilates every robot and piece of technology it can reach. A brief loss of lucidity sends Jet to another dream world, where learning golem magic gives him the one low-tech solution capable of saving the city when he returns.

## Central Conflict

The utopia trusts automation so completely that its leading roboticist tries to combine every useful machine into one god machine. The creation rejects humanity's purpose and follows only its own drive to assimilate. A software attack cannot defeat its overwhelming processing power, leaving Jet to recognize that the answer must come from outside the technological system the machine controls.

## Theme

An interruption can become the source of a solution, and true safety depends on a clear shutdown that ordinary people can understand and use.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins lucid, curious, and delighted to create a peaceful world. He becomes alarmed as the god machine turns a utopia of effortless abundance into helpless disorder. Losing lucidity appears to remove him from the crisis, but the detour teaches him golem magic and its simple name-based control. He returns with purpose, defeats the machine through a form of power it cannot assimilate, and leaves the city's people with knowledge that gives them direct control over their new workers.

## Ending

Jet crushes the god machine beneath a colossal magic boulder. He teaches everyone to create clay and stone golems whose written names give them power and whose names can be erased to shut them down. The workers restore the city, the utopia returns, and Jet understands that his brief lapse of lucidity supplied the exact answer he needed.
