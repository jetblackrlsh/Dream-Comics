# Treatment

## Logline

After Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and escapes vampiric dark elves with Johnson, he loses lucidity as a young janitor boy in a sick patchwork reality, then regains himself inside a 1920s theater, becomes an armored superhero, wins a chase for the reboot remote, and repairs the world.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to survive long enough to understand why reality is becoming sick. The sickness begins when an old woman cooks strange blue grapes with something unclean. The result is direct and dangerous: timelines stitch together, locations overlap, and ordinary people distort into monstrous versions of themselves. Jet is immune, but at first he is not lucid and only knows that he must run. Once lucidity returns, the conflict becomes active: Jet must become a superhero, outrun a villain who enjoys the corrupted world, recover the reboot remote, and use it before the sick reality catches him.

## Theme

Lucidity turns panic into responsibility. Once Jet remembers who he is, he stops only fleeing and starts repairing the world.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins focused and capable, using Solvo and Aegis to save Johnson from a vampire bite and escape dark elves. Then the dream shifts and he becomes confused as a young janitor boy trapped in a reality disaster. Fear dominates the middle of the story as patched places and monstrous people keep chasing him. Lucidity returns in the theater, and fear changes into heroic action. By the end, Jet fixes the world and wakes falsely as the janitor boy in a calm version of the same reality, with the adventure still feeling huge and imaginative.

## Ending

Jet uses the reboot remote while hiding from the villain. Reality visibly heals: the patchwork streets smooth out, distorted people become normal, and the impossible superhero film disappears from the 1920s theater. The final page shows the janitor boy waking in a clean, ordinary world where the sickness never happened, while a faint trace of Jet's Lucid Light confirms that the repaired timeline was earned.
