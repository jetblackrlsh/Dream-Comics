# Character Bible

## Jet — casual lucid form

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- At arrival, wears futuristic wraparound augmented-reality sunglasses with translucent glowing blue display lenses. He holds a thin portable black-and-blue touchpad small enough for one hand, with a luminous grid and a cursor gesture controlled by his other hand.
- Carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword, a flexible golden glitter-light energy tether, and sparkling purple star-filled Lucid Light.
- Continuity rule: before the hero suit appears, keep Jet’s dark-brown skin, full beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white sneakers, belt bag, blue AR glasses, rainbow sword, and golden tether exact.
- Expression range: lucid focus, curious delight, dazed confusion, disgust, restored confidence.

## Jet — yellow-and-blue superhero form

- Same adult Jet with the exact same dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, full beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks. His face, hair, and identity never change.
- His casual clothing is replaced by a sleek fitted superhero bodysuit with a vivid royal-blue base, bold golden-yellow chest and shoulder panels, yellow forearm guards, yellow boots, and a glowing yellow starburst emblem at the sternum. No cape, helmet, mask, or exposed underwear.
- The blue AR lenses remain as narrow translucent display sunglasses so Jet’s eyes and face are still visible.
- His powers appear as golden-yellow flight energy edged with sparkling purple, star-filled Lucid Light. He retains the rainbow crystal sword and golden energy tether at his belt, though the kaiju fight relies primarily on flight, strength, and energy blasts.
- Continuity rule: keep the hero suit’s exact royal-blue and golden-yellow pattern, starburst emblem, uncovered head, beard, long dreadlocks, blue lenses, and power colors consistent on every hero-form page.

## Jet’s supposed evil twin

- Adult masculine-presenting humanoid who clearly resembles an alternate version of Jet: the same broad facial structure and full beard, but with saturated purple skin and glowing pale-violet eyes.
- Has a large rounded black afro with one unmistakable jagged white lightning-shaped highlight through the front-left section.
- Wears a sleek red-and-black superhero suit: deep black torso and legs, crimson-red shoulders, gloves, boots, and angular side panels, with a red lightning emblem at the sternum. No cape, helmet, or mask.
- Powers appear as crimson-red force and violet electrical arcs. His flying posture is aggressive, his expression guarded, and his attitude detached rather than sadistic.
- Continuity rule: purple skin, full beard, rounded black afro, white lightning highlight, red-and-black suit, red chest emblem, and violet-red energy never change.

## Magmagator

- Begins as a realistic dark swamp-green alligator emerging impossibly through cracked bathroom tiles.
- Rapidly grows into a colossal alligator kaiju large enough to smash through multiple mall levels. Its hulking crocodilian body is made from black volcanic rock plates split by brilliant orange-yellow magma fissures.
- Has a broad alligator snout, molten yellow eyes, jagged obsidian teeth, furnace glow inside its mouth, massive clawed legs, and a long lava-shedding tail. It remains recognizably an alligator and never becomes humanoid or draconic.
- Breathes a focused torrent of fire and molten debris. Its heat buckles steel railings, melts signs, and cracks tile without showing casualties or gore.
- After defeat, its magma glow dims and its body collapses into cooling black volcanic stone.

## Resistance woman

- Adult woman with light-brown skin, large dark eyes, and very long voluminous curly black hair framing her face and falling past her shoulders.
- Wears a fitted sleeveless light-brown knee-length dress with simple matching shoes; attractive but grounded, with no superhero costume or weapons.
- Her resistance affiliation is shown by a small hidden teal circuit-shaped insignia card she briefly reveals.
- Her body language is confident, assertive, and increasingly invasive as she steps into Jet’s space and reaches toward his chest emblem. Jet remains politely uncomfortable and leans away.
- Continuity rule: light-brown skin, long curly black hair, tight light-brown dress, teal insignia, and aggressive flirtation remain clear without sexual nudity or coercive physical contact.

## Mall World

- An enormous late-1980s-to-futuristic shopping complex with endless stacked balconies, pastel neon signs, glass elevators, food courts, tiled corridors, artificial palms, skylights that reveal no real sky, and passages that repeat beyond sight.
- The infinite bathroom is filthy but non-graphic: endless rows of stained beige tiles, grimy sinks, broken stall doors, flickering fluorescent tubes, leaking pipes, shallow puddles, and corridors that repeat into fog.
- The bathroom expands and breaks open into the main atrium during Magmagator’s growth, making the action geographically clear.

## Storyverse continuity

- The Storyverse is a cosmic void filled with luminous bubble universes containing every possible story. It appears only briefly in the sleep-paralysis transition.
- Lucid Light is reality-warping purple energy sparkling like a night sky full of stars.
- Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet’s team but do not physically appear because Jet arrives alone.
