# Treatment - The Molten Barricade

## Logline

Hunted through a city and a treacherous cave by fire-bullet goblins, a colossal troll, and a greedy dragon, Jet survives by trusting his living Rainbow Crystal Sword and turning the goblins' own flames into the force that seals his escape.

## Central conflict

A band of goblins wants to steal Silonis, one of the most powerful artifacts in the Storyverse. Their enchanted guns transform ordinary bullets into curving fire projectiles, while their enormous troll can physically reach Jet even in flight. When a cave refuge becomes a trap after a dragon betrays him, Jet must protect Silonis and find an exit despite armed goblins inside the cave and the troll blocking its only visible entrance.

## Theme

Adaptability turns an enemy's advantage into a path to freedom. Silonis protects Jet through autonomous loyalty, and Jet's remembered geomancy transforms the goblins' destructive fire into the heat that strengthens his defense.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins confident and almost playful, easily dodging the goblins' enchanted bullets. The troll's surprise strike breaks that confidence and forces him into a desperate escape. Silonis proves itself an equal partner by catching Jet and independently shielding him. The dragon briefly offers hope, then deepens the danger through betrayal. Trapped in darkness, Jet reaches back to one of his earliest learned powers, regains control through geomancy, and converts the goblins' relentless assault into a stronger barrier. He ends airborne and free, with Silonis safely beside him.

## Ending

The fire bullets partially melt and weld Jet's boulder barricade into a glowing molten wall that the goblins cannot cross. The geomancy has also opened a skylight in the cave ceiling. Jet launches upward with Silonis and escapes through the new opening into the Storyverse, leaving the goblins, troll, and treacherous dragon trapped behind their own choices.
