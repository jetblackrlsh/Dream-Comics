Jet flies through a modern city with Silonis, his living Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life, while gun-toting goblins pursue him with enchanted bullets that turn into homing fireballs. A colossal troll knocks Jet from the sky, but Silonis catches him with a force field and then autonomously deflects the goblins' projectiles while Jet escapes at super speed.

Jet takes shelter inside a cave, where a seemingly friendly dragon demands Silonis as payment. The sword repels the dragon and returns to Jet, so the dragon betrays them by calling the goblins into the cave. A troll blocks the entrance while the goblins search the darkness.

Remembering the geomancy he learned in an earlier Storyverse adventure, Jet pulls boulders from the ceiling and forms a barricade. The goblins' fire bullets melt and weld the stones into an even stronger molten wall. The missing rocks leave a skylight above Jet, allowing him and Silonis to fly free and escape into the Storyverse.
