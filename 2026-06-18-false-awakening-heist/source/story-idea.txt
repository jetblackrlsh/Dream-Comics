Dream journal entry dated 06/18/2026.

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. Jet's team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper, and Jet. Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, warm brown eyes, a rainbow crystal sword, and a golden glittering energy tether.

Jet flew alongside an artist version of himself, dressed in a painter's smock covered in glitchy digital neon paint, on a jungle world. There he helped Eye-Grip, a savage blue-skinned version of Jet with red face paint, glowing orange eyes, a white loin cloth, and orange glowing hand constructs from his eyes, hunt a bird filled with gold coins.

The initial lucid dream was fun and adventurous, but several false awakenings became nightmare-like.

In the first false awakening, Jet felt extremely tired and distraught while trying to understand an assignment involving a song. He telepathically discovered the answer, which revealed that he was dreaming.

The second false awakening launched Jet into a world where he was actively completing that assignment. It involved a heist in which several teams with power armor abilities and AI assistants were forced to battle each other to claim golden treasure. Jet won a small amount of treasure after a traumatic battle, but a female instructor informed him that all teams failed to complete any subobjectives. The subobjectives all involved talking to animals, connecting back to the song from the first false awakening.

Jet went to the council responsible for the test and begged to have his AI assistant back. When they refused, he fought them in a fit of rage using his power armor suit. After brutally beating the council's ninja forces, he became distraught and fell into despair.

In the third false awakening, Jet found himself in a strange city searching for a place to recover. He was mentally unstable from the previous encounters until he stumbled upon a telepathic woman who triggered lucidity again. The dream became a black void, and Jet feared he had overslept. He raced out of the darkness and woke for real, only to discover he had woken more than half an hour earlier than normal, closer to 4:00 AM instead of his usual 4:30 AM.
