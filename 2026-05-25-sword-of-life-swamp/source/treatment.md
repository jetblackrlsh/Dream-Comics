# Treatment

## Title

05/25/2026: Sword of Life Swamp

## Logline

Jet and the moss-caped spirit of a plant hero enter a cyborg's magical sword shop, where Jet's rainbow crystal blade is revealed as the Sword of Life, triggering a violent pursuit that only ends when a mysterious swamp woman named Whittmore deceives the alien rulers on their behalf.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to travel peacefully with the plant hero spirit through a new Storyverse world. The cyborg shopkeeper wants the Sword of Life and the location of Savannah's Death Sword, turning a discovery into a theft attempt and a robot-police chase.

## Theme

Life protects life. The plant spirit's bond saves Jet from violence, and Whittmore's bond with nature saves him from a world ruled by surveillance and machines.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins curious and alert, trusting the plant spirit as a guide. The sword scan reframes his rainbow blade as a powerful cosmic artifact, and the cyborg's betrayal forces him into decisive self-defense. The robot chase pushes him from confidence into urgency. Whittmore's intervention restores wonder and gratitude, but the 6 AM alarm cuts the moment short before Jet can fully understand who she is.

## Ending

Whittmore's false message convinces the alien rulers to call off the robots. Jet is left with the mystery of her name and motive, then wakes before he can thank her, carrying the unresolved feeling into morning.
