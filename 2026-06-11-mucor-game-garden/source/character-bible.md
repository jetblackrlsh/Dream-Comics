# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. He can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he moves from lonely curiosity to protective lucid focus to relaxed creative play.

## Mucor

Humanoid frog made entirely of glossy translucent green slime. He has a frog-like head with wide friendly eyes, soft rounded amphibian features, webbed hands and feet, and a body that can stretch, puddle, and reshape itself. His body looks wet, luminous, elastic, and semi-transparent, with darker green ripples inside the slime. He is playful, polite, and delighted by simple gifts.

Continuity notes: Mucor is not merely coated in slime; he is made of slime. Show his hand morphing around the robot bag clip to reveal his true nature. Keep him friendly and non-threatening.

## Robot Bag Clip

Small toy-like robot bag clip with a metallic body, tiny clamp loop, cute mechanical face, and bright accent lights. It is too small to fit around Jet's wrist, but Mucor can reshape his slime hand and wear it like a bracelet.

Continuity notes: Treat the clip as the object that begins the friendship and reveals Mucor's slime anatomy.

## Infinite Empty Castle

A vast, dreamlike stone castle with endless corridors, tall arched windows, oversized doors, empty chambers, moonlit blue-gray stone, and occasional purple Lucid Light reflections. It has more space than Jet knows what to do with.

Continuity notes: The castle should feel lonely at first, then increasingly personalized by Mucor's slime room and Jet's Game Garden.

## Death Game World

A harsh arena-like world where innocent people are forced through life-or-death games. It uses red warning lights, brutal obstacle architecture, countdown screens without readable text, and cold mechanical traps. Keep the danger intense but non-gory.

Continuity notes: This world is a contrast to the Game Garden. It should feel oppressive, artificial, and designed around fear.

## Game Garden

The transformed castle courtyard: green paths, flower beds, climbing vines, training circles, fantasy banners, and cloth spell bags filled with seeds. When spell bags burst during playful LARP battles, seeds scatter into the soil and help the garden bloom.

Continuity notes: The Game Garden is the moral answer to the death games. Its magic battles should feel energetic, safe, and generative.
