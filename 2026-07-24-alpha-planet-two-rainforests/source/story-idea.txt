On July 24, 2026, Jet induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and entered the continuing Storyverse. Reduced to mouse size inside a vast corridor, he was chased and caught by a long-haired humanoid purple tiger woman. Jet used star-filled purple Lucid Light to build a gigantic humanoid tiger mech, which caused the tiger woman to mistake him for the Alpha of her pack. When Jet revealed himself, she attacked and tried to steal his Rainbow Crystal Sword, but the sword healed every wound and formed a radiant rainbow barrier.

Jet escaped through the ceiling and into space. The entire planet unfurled into the Alpha's true form: a gigantic purple tiger king. Jet outran him, created an ordinary wooden door in space, and entered a blue-tinted tropical rainforest where snow fell among trees with rounded canopies. He found a lava river inside a cave and rested beside its warmth.

A brief waking interruption broke the dream. Jet returned and reconstructed the journey, but this time the Alpha planet did not transform and the rainforest was experiencing autumn. Jet found the lava river again, submerged himself, and became a powerful living form of lava, fire, and molten stone. Flying above the landscape, he held both versions of the impossible rainforest in his mind—the snowy blue world and the warm red-and-gold autumn world—studying and preserving them like a photographer without a camera.
