Jet becomes lucid inside a God dream and creates a Storyverse world in which data centers use swamp water containing frog DNA for cooling. Pollution transforms people into humanoid frog Dynamancers with magic tied to one governing concept, turning the real debate over data centers into a literal conflict over superheroes, supervillains, empowerment, and risk. Jet then loses lucidity and is swept into a modern academy dormitory filled with retro 1990s technology. Paramilitary guards escort a former middle-school bully into Jet's room to repair its failing monitor power. The man has accidentally killed someone and asks Jet to sign a release form recognizing his good deeds, but Jet tearfully refuses while allowing that someone else may eventually grant him freedom. After Jet compares him to a demon-fighting television character in a similar redemption trial, red-and-blue symbiote tendrils transform the bully into a demon. Jet escapes the attack by using a bizarre full-sized motorized zip-line toy to kick the creature through a window. The victory returns Jet to lucidity above the unchanged frog world, where he reflects on the unexpected story, senses morning, and wakes.
