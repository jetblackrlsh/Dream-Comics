# Treatment

## Logline

Jet arrives on a yellow-sunset alien world and meets Bradlynn, the impatient clerk of the Dream Station, where dreamers choose between quick fantasy destinations and the deeper work of exploring dreams as real worlds.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to stay curious about the strange world already in front of him, while Bradlynn expects dreamers to file a simple fantasy request and move on.

## Theme

Dreams can be escapes and parties, but they can also be worlds with their own geography, workers, rules, and mysteries worth exploring.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alone and newly lucid, becomes fascinated by the alien Dream Station system, resists the pressure to choose a fashionable destination, and wakes into a reflective false awakening where he recognizes that exploration is what gives his lucid dreams meaning.

## Ending

In the false awakening apartment, Jet explains Bradlynn and the Dream Station to two women with different reactions. The comic ends with Jet choosing dream adventure over default distraction, while still acknowledging that both experiences have their place.
