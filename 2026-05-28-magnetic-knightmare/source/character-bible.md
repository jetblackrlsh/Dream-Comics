# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow sword, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he fights alone with focused curiosity. His shield dome should be rainbow crystal energy. His shrinking should combine the compressing rainbow dome with purple starry Lucid Light.

## Magnetic Knightmare

A living suit of medieval knight armor with no body inside. The armor is dark gunmetal and silver with magnetized seams, teal-blue magnetic field lines, violet sparks, and floating metal filings around it. It can separate and control its gauntlets, plates, shards, and fragments from a distance, then reel them back into place.

Continuity notes: The Knightmare's key identifier is magnetic control over its own body pieces. Show gauntlets flying like missiles, armor plates hovering, fragments orbiting, and shards obeying the Knightmare even after Jet cuts them apart. It is persistent, singularly focused, and clever enough to send a gauntlet underground into Jet's shield dome.

## Rainbow Shield Dome

A hemispherical rainbow crystal energy barrier projected from Jet's sword. It glows in layered prismatic colors, looks faceted like glass, and can compress around Jet like a shrinking bubble.

Continuity notes: The shield begins as protection, becomes a trap, and finally becomes the mechanism Jet uses to shrink out of normal reality.

## Storyverse Bubble Universe

The battlefield is a cosmic Storyverse bubble universe: a floating reality bubble in a dark void filled with other distant bubble universes. Inside this bubble is a fractured dream battlefield with metallic ground, stony ridges, and a sky streaked by magnetic auroras.

Continuity notes: The final pages must show two scales at once: ordinary macroscopic Storyverse reality and the exact sub-quantum copy where Jet becomes gigantic. The destroyed bubble universe should feel immense and mythic, not casual.
