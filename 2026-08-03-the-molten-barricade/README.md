# 08/03/2026 - The Molten Barricade

Dream Comics entry for August 3, 2026.

Jet races through a city with Silonis, his living Rainbow Crystal Sword, while gun-toting goblins fire enchanted bullets that become homing fireballs. A nearly skyscraper-sized troll knocks him from the sky, but Silonis cushions the fall with a force field and autonomously protects him as he escapes at super speed.

Jet hides inside a cave and meets a dragon that offers shelter only if he surrenders Silonis. When the living sword repels the dragon and returns to Jet, the treacherous creature calls the goblins to the cave. With the goblins searching inside and the troll blocking the entrance, Jet remembers the geomancy he learned during an early Storyverse adventure.

He tears boulders from the ceiling to form a barricade. The goblins' enchanted fire bullets only melt and weld the rocks into a stronger molten wall, while the newly opened ceiling becomes Jet's escape route. Jet and Silonis fly through the skylight and return safely to the Storyverse.
