Dream Journal Reconstruction

The dream began with me riding on the back of a giant dragon that was flying through the sky above an enormous forest toward a castle. The dragon was not made of ordinary scales or flesh. Its body seemed to be made from a soft, quilted blanket material. It was primarily white and covered in an intricate blue pattern resembling the blue-and-white designs I had seen on the fine glassware or good china that people displayed in china cabinets. Silver and gold accents were woven into the design as well, although white and blue remained the dominant colors.

I was not alone on the dragon. Beside me was an elven princess with very pale skin, pointed ears, and long silver hair. She wore a dress bearing exactly the same white, blue, silver, and gold pattern as the dragon. Her most striking feature was her eyes. They did not merely resemble sapphires; literal sapphire jewels sat inside her eye sockets in place of ordinary eyes. The sight was stunning and somewhat off-putting, but the jeweled eyes also seemed strangely fitting for an elf.

A knight accompanied us as well. He was also an elf with pointed ears, although his skin had a more ordinary peach-colored complexion. He wore silver armor and had long brown hair despite being noticeably bald across the top of his head.

I immediately commented on the princess’s remarkable sapphire eyes. She responded with a small, bashful laugh, almost as though she were blushing. The knight became angry, and the princess explained that he was her boyfriend—although, judging by her phrasing, he was soon going to be her ex-boyfriend. When the knight angrily began moving toward her, I stepped between them.

We were still standing on the dragon’s back when I summoned a ring made from my sparkling purple Lucid Light. The knight indignantly demanded, “How dare you use magic in my presence?” He snatched the ring from my finger and placed it on one of his own.

I grinned at him. The Lucid Light ring slid from his finger onto his back, transformed into a parachute, and opened with enough force to launch him completely off the dragon.

The princess stared at me and asked what I had done. I explained that I had originally intended to use the ring as a cool focus from which I could summon different constructs, but once the knight stole it, I could not resist changing my plan. I assured her that I did not think he was dead. I had deliberately turned the ring into a parachute so that he would descend safely into the forest below.

Instead of being relieved, the princess insisted that we had to retrieve him. We had been flying toward the castle because the surrounding woods were completely swarmed with hostile orcs.

Realizing the danger I had unknowingly placed him in, I could only respond, “Oh.”

The princess steered the quilted dragon around and began descending toward the forest. As the dragon turned, an orc attacked it from below. I could not remember exactly what struck the dragon. It might have been some kind of enchanted axe hurled into the air, but whatever it was cut straight through one of the dragon’s fabric wings.

With the wing torn, the princess struggled to control the dragon and guided it into a crash landing inside a clearing in the woods. Once we were down, she summoned needles and thread directly from her fingers. She told me that she could use her magic to sew the dragon back together, but I needed to find the knight and bring him back before the clearing was overrun by orcs.

I told her that would not be a problem. The knight was still wearing the Lucid Light ring I had created. I summoned another ring onto my own hand and explained that it was the first ring’s sister. I could make the paired rings track one another and pull the knight back to us.

I activated the ring, but the magic worked in the reverse direction from what I expected. Instead of pulling the knight toward me, it telekinetically yanked me away from the princess and through the forest. I shot rapidly between the trees until I crashed into the back of an orc.

The knight was standing at the center of a circle of hostile orcs. Because the orcs surrounded him on every side, I had not been pulled all the way to the knight. I had collided with the outer wall of the creatures encircling him.

The orcs turned and tried to strike me. I raised my Lucid Light ring into the air and summoned a physical manifestation of the bond between my ring and the knight’s ring. A massive sparkling-purple Lucid Light chain appeared, connecting the two of us. The chain blocked and parried the attacking orcs’ blows. When their weapons struck it, a powerful shockwave erupted and knocked them backward.

That gave me an idea. I grabbed the Lucid Light chain and began whirling the knight around as though he were a human flail. The knight still had his sword, so as I swung him from one orc to another, he used his blade to slash into each of them. We transformed the magical connection between us into an improvised team attack.

For the finishing move, I pulled hard enough to break the chain. The released force sent the knight spinning like a human Beyblade. With his sword extended, he sliced through the remaining orcs and knocked them away with his blade.

The maneuver was incredibly cool, and fighting together in such a spectacular way completely changed the knight’s attitude toward me. Despite our earlier confrontation, the successful team attack endeared me to him.

After defeating the orcs, the knight and I made our way back through the forest to the clearing. By the time we returned, the princess had finished sewing the dragon’s torn wing back together. The three of us were reunited beside the repaired quilted dragon, and that was where the dream ended.
