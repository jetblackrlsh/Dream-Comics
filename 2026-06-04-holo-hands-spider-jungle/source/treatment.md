# Treatment

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet wakes alone in a dense Storyverse jungle with his hands replaced by green metal stumps, then survives a swarm and a colossal queen spider by transforming green holo-light prosthetics into whatever tool the moment demands.

## Central Conflict

Jet must learn whether the loss of his hands is a weakness or a new lucid ability. The jungle keeps escalating: vines block his path, a giant spider ambushes him, a swarm surrounds him, and the queen spider counters his strongest ranged attack with a stronger screech.

## Theme

Lucidity turns limitation into invention. Jet survives because he adapts faster than the dream can trap him.

## Ending

After defeating the queen spider with hook prosthetics, Jet changes the hooks into rocket hands and flies above the jungle. The final image shifts from violent survival to peaceful wonder: a living world spreads beneath him, bright and whole.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins isolated and physically altered, unsure what his green metal stumps can do. Concentration turns anxiety into control as he creates shears, a machete, a soundwave blaster, and hooks. The queen spider forces him into a close-quarters choice, but once he wins, he transforms the same power into flight and ends in calm bliss.
