# Treatment - The Vengeful Swarm

## Logline

When the ghosts of countless insects fuse into a superpowered humanoid that repeatedly disables his Lucid Light, Jet escapes the cycle not by overpowering them, but by awakening his living Sword of Life and blessing the vengeful spirits into peace.

## Central conflict

Jet initially treats an ocean of angry insect ghosts as a combat problem and uses Lucid Light to compress them into one target. That decision creates a more dangerous foe: a six-to-seven-foot humanoid with the combined strength, speed, durability, instincts, and hive intelligence of countless species. It constructs an inhibitor chip, implants it in Jet's neck, and traps him in a looping confrontation without his most versatile power. Jet must discover a solution that works even while Lucid Light is unavailable—and then understand why victory does not require destroying his attackers.

## Theme

Power can end an attack, but understanding can end the reason for it. Jet accepts that the insects' anger comes from a genuine sense of having been wronged. Compassion does not excuse the attack; it transforms the conflict by releasing pain instead of perpetuating violence.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins thoughtful and confident, turning a terrifying flood of ghost bugs into a single opponent he believes he can manage. The humanoid's speed and technological intelligence immediately reverse that control by shutting down Lucid Light. Repeated wake-and-return cycles create frustration and apparent hopelessness. Jet's breakthrough comes when he remembers that his tools are independent allies: Psilones brings the inhibitor to life and frees him. With his power restored, Jet chooses reflection over retaliation, recognizes the swarm's grievance, and draws upon his spiritual history to bless the spirits. Fear and struggle give way to accountability, mercy, and luminous peace.

## Ending

Jet places an open hand against the ghost-bug humanoid and sends a warm blessing through every insect spirit inside it. Their collective anger dissolves; the humanoid separates into thousands of gentle lights that rise into a brilliant afterlife. Jet stands unharmed beneath the clearing sky, having ended the repeating cycle without destroying anyone again.
