# Treatment

## Logline

Jet visits the free Question Store, where a brilliant Houndstress helps him discover the joy of dream photography until a photo exposes her demonic side and her obsession with stealing the Life Sword to solve the meaning of life.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to explore a strange dream idea with curiosity and play, while the Hell Houndstress wants to seize the Life Sword and force an answer to the ultimate question through power.

## Theme

Life and death are both temporary states in a world of dream magic, but meaning comes from how a person chooses to live after receiving another chance.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins amazed and playful, becomes protective when the Life Sword is threatened, then shifts from combat to compassion. The Houndstress moves from helpful curiosity to demonic obsession and finally to humility, choosing the joy of her store over monstrous ambition.

## Ending

The Life Sword resurrects the Houndstress. Jet explains that life is fleeting, death is not absolute, and meaning is found in what one does with life. The Houndstress tames her demonic side and peacefully enjoys running the Question Store.
