Silonis and Tetheron

My dream began in a modern city that felt very similar to the ordinary waking world. I was considering a hypothetical question: if I could bring only one of my two primary dream artifacts into the waking world, which one would I choose?

The choice was between the Rainbow Crystal Sword, commonly known as the Life Sword, and my Golden Energy Tether.

At first, the Life Sword seemed like the obvious answer. It was one of the two most powerful artifacts in the entire Storyverse, rivaled only by the Death Sword. The Life Sword could channel life energy into living beings and inanimate objects. It could heal injuries, bring objects to life, and even purify evil beings by transforming their nature from evil into good.

It was also still a sword, capable of cutting through things when necessary, although its healing abilities often seemed even more powerful than its destructive ones. It could generate protective force fields, and I could ride it through the air like a hoverboard. The sword itself was alive and could communicate with me, making it more than a weapon or tool. It was also a companion.

Leaving it behind would therefore mean more than giving up one of the most powerful artifacts in existence. It would feel as though I were abandoning a living being. There was also the question of what might happen if the Life Sword fell into the wrong hands.

I was not genuinely planning to stop using either artifact. I was simply imagining what I would do if I somehow had to choose only one of them to possess in the waking world. Either object would allow me to accomplish things that would otherwise be completely impossible.

The Life Sword seemed overwhelmingly useful. The ability to fly on it and create force fields alone would give me powers resembling those of a flying-brick superhero. When its healing abilities and its power to animate objects were added to that, the sword seemed almost godlike. It made perfect sense that it was considered one of the greatest artifacts in the Storyverse.

However, as I continued thinking, I began to reconsider the Golden Energy Tether.

The tether appeared to have fewer defined powers than the Life Sword, but it was not truly limited. In some ways, it might have had even more potential applications because its primary ability was to create magical bonds between anything it connected.

For example, I could attach one end of the tether to a laptop and the other end to an inanimate object such as a chair. The magical connection could then allow me to use the laptop to reprogram the chair. By creating a bond between two things, the tether could change the very nature of how those things interacted.

The tether was also reusable. I could create a connection between two objects and then remove the tether without breaking the magical bond. The laptop and chair could remain mystically linked forever, while I continued using the tether to form entirely new connections elsewhere. Its limitations depended almost completely upon what objects were available and how creatively I could imagine connecting them.

It also had powerful traversal capabilities. Although I could not ride it through the air like the Life Sword, I could use it as a grappling line. Whenever I threw it toward something, it seemed almost impossible for me to miss. I did not possess any special real-world talent for throwing lassos, but the tether appeared capable of sensing my intentions and wrapping itself perfectly around whatever target I selected.

It could seemingly extend for as great a distance as necessary and then retract again afterward. I could swing from it like Spider-Man using a web line or rapidly pull myself toward the top of a tall building. I could throw it with complete confidence that it would catch something and support me.

The more I considered it, the more I realized that the tether's magical bonding ability had almost limitless utility.

While I was contemplating the relative value of the two artifacts, a masked thief suddenly appeared. He wore a black suit with a red tie and a top hat decorated with a red ribbon. His face was concealed behind a porcelain-white demonic mask.

The thief grabbed the Golden Energy Tether from me.

The modern city around us immediately shattered, and we were transported into the open space of the Storyverse. Floating bubble universes surrounded us, suspended among twinkling purple stars scattered throughout the cosmos.

I still possessed the Life Sword, while the masked thief now held the Golden Energy Tether. Our confrontation became a direct conflict between the two artifacts, almost as though the battle itself would determine which of them was superior.

However, I could immediately tell that the thief did not understand how to use the tether properly. He treated it as though it were merely a magical rope or whip. This was something I had noticed during several of my previous adventures. People frequently underestimated the Golden Energy Tether because they did not comprehend its true function. Even when they recognized that it was magical, they often assumed its only purpose was to bind or strike things.

The thief attempted to wrap the tether around me and restrain me with it.

I used the Life Sword to cut through the tether and free myself.

The thief celebrated, believing he had tricked me into destroying one of my own most important artifacts. He acted as though I had been forced to sacrifice the tether in order to escape and would now have to live without it.

However, I had never believed that I was sacrificing anything.

I gathered the severed pieces of the Golden Energy Tether and charged toward the masked thief. As I ran, I channeled the Life Sword's restorative energy into the damaged tether. The pieces reconnected, and the tether became whole again.

With both artifacts restored to me, I defeated the masked thief. I mocked him for believing that I had carelessly destroyed the tether, explaining that I had known the Life Sword would be capable of restoring it.

As the tether was healed, I sensed that something greater had occurred. The Life Sword had not merely repaired an inanimate magical object. Its life energy had imbued the Golden Energy Tether with a life of its own.

For the first time, I learned the true names of both artifacts - not the titles other people had given them, but the names by which the artifacts knew themselves.

The Rainbow Crystal Sword, the artifact commonly called the Life Sword, was truly named Silonis.

The Golden Energy Tether was now named Tetheron.

I was uncertain whether Tetheron had always possessed a hidden life and a name of its own or whether it had only become alive after Silonis restored it. However, I believed the Life Sword's energy had done more than simply mend the tether. It had awakened something within it.

By the end of the dream, I no longer viewed the choice between the two artifacts as obvious. Silonis remained one of the most powerful objects in the Storyverse, but Tetheron's potential was equally extraordinary in a different way.

One offered immense life-giving power, protection, healing, and flight. The other offered limitless creative possibilities through the formation of magical bonds.

Both artifacts were incredibly capable.

More importantly, both of them were now alive, and both carried tremendous personal significance for me.
