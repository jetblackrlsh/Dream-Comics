# Page Script — The Clone in the Void and the Superhero Recruitment Drive

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions must remain readable, clearly separated, long enough to make every story change obvious, and nonredundant. Jet and every recurring design must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 — Cover

One dramatic layered composition about duplicated identities. Human Jet stands centered in the endless black void, gripping the glowing Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life. His newly revived exact clone rises from the torn black cocoon at lower left. The hulking charcoal rat monster looms behind the ordinary table at upper left. At right, heroine-bodied Jet faces the shapeshifter's exact female duplicate while hundreds of metal pots and pans spiral overhead through purple Lucid Light and magenta telekinesis. The visual path moves from two identical male Jets to two identical female heroes. Visible cover text only: “THE CLONE IN THE VOID”, “SUPERHERO RECRUITMENT DRIVE”, and “JULY 25, 2026”. No issue number.

## Page 2 — The Pitch in Nothingness

Five panels. Jet lies conscious but immobilized during sleep paralysis in a dark bedroom while sparkling purple Lucid Light gathers around him. He enters the absolute black void and stands alone with no ground, sky, or horizon. A simple square table and two chairs appear, with the friendly comic-book man already seated. Jet sits across from him as the man asks for a comic pitch. Jet remembers his friend's wish to create a comic, then leans forward skeptically after learning that the stranger is not a publisher and can only send a recommendation.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and entered the same Storyverse continuity I had explored since June 11, 2025.”
- “I found myself alone in an endless void with no visible ground, sky, or horizon.”
- “A man sat at an ordinary table and told me he was involved with comic books, so I joined him.”
- “When he asked for a pitch, I considered promoting a comic idea that my friend wanted to create.”
- “He admitted that he worked for no publisher. He could only message one and recommend creators he believed deserved attention.”
- “His offer sounded potentially useful, but I still did not know how much influence he truly possessed.”

## Page 3 — Something Inside

Six panels. Still seated in the void, Jet suddenly feels his stomach swell beneath the hoodie while a pulsing ring of light suggests the waking-body heartbeat. Jet stands, grips the impossible enlarged stomach, and recognizes that a real physical sensation is shaping the lucid dream. He summons sparkling star-filled purple Lucid Light and painlessly draws a luminous mass out of his body with no blood or gore. The mass changes into the glossy black bag-like cocoon; a purple-white humanoid silhouette is curled inside. The comic-book man rises and transforms violently but non-graphically into the hulking charcoal rat monster. Final large panel: the rat monster repeatedly punches the cocoon while shocked Jet watches, and the inner silhouette is battered but never shown graphically.

Caption text:

- “A swollen pulse from my waking stomach entered the dream and made it feel as though a living being were growing inside me.”
- “I knew that I could not truly be pregnant, but the physical sensation felt disturbingly real.”
- “Because I was lucid, I used sparkling purple Lucid Light to pull the mass from my body without pain or injury.”
- “It became a large black cocoon, and a glowing humanoid silhouette was visible through its surface.”
- “Before I could understand it, the comic-book man transformed into a massive, animalistic rat monster.”
- “The creature pummeled the being inside the bag again and again, then scurried away into the darkness.”

## Page 4 — The Other Jet

Six panels. The rat monster vanishes on all fours into the void, leaving Jet beside the flattened black cocoon. Jet draws the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life and directs brilliant prismatic life energy into it. The silhouette regains shape and the cocoon tears open cleanly. A second Jet emerges alive: exact face, skin, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, sneakers, and bag, but no sword. Jet and the clone stare at one another in shock. The black void then fragments into a bright rapid montage as Jet's lucidity fades. Final two panels show diverse original superheroes asking confused ordinary adults to become heroes; some recruits begin nodding with new understanding.

Caption text:

- “The rat fled like a wild animal and left me alone with the battered cocoon.”
- “I carried the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life, so I directed its restoring energy into the unknown victim.”
- “Rainbow light repaired the body, and the black material split open without blood or gore.”
- “The being I had revived was an exact clone of me—another Jet wearing my own face and clothes.”
- “My lucidity faded, and the dream accelerated into a montage of superheroes recruiting ordinary people.”
- “Many people were confused at first, but some began to understand that becoming a hero could be a choice.”

## Page 5 — The Heroine and Her Duplicate

Five panels. In a bright city plaza during the recruitment drive, Jet's awareness snaps back inside the cookware heroine's adult female body. She looks down at the deep brown hands, teal-and-copper suit, white apron-like sash, copper bracers, and frying-pan chest emblem, then sees her copper-red curls in a reflective window. A subtle purple Lucid Light reflection of male Jet overlays her mind, making clear that Jet remains internally himself. She approaches the silver-haired prospective recruit. The recruit smiles coldly and transforms into an exact copy of the heroine. Final split panel: two visually identical cookware heroines face each other, while the villainous duplicate's magenta eyes and aura reveal telekinesis.

Caption text:

- “When I regained lucidity, I discovered that I was no longer inside my normal body.”
- “I had become a woman in a teal-and-copper superhero suit, apparently one of the heroes leading the recruitment drive.”
- “Although the body and face were unfamiliar, my awareness remained completely my own. Internally, I was still Jet.”
- “I approached someone who seemed like a possible recruit, only to discover that they were already a shapeshifting supervillain.”
- “The shapeshifter copied the female hero I was inhabiting, creating an exact duplicate of my current appearance.”
- “Only the duplicate's magenta telekinetic aura revealed which of us was the attacker.”

## Page 6 — Lucid Light, Unexpected Form

Six dynamic panels. The magenta-aura duplicate attacks from a distance, ripping benches, signs, and loose objects into the air without touching them. Heroine-bodied Jet dives behind cover while a magenta force wave smashes nearby pavement, then runs through the plaza as objects narrowly miss her. She pauses, looks at her unfamiliar hands, and remembers that lucidity—not physical appearance—allows her to wield Lucid Light. Star-filled purple energy ignites around her copper bracers. Instead of a conventional weapon, a frying pan, saucepan, stockpot, and lid materialize in midair. Final wide panel: Jet hurls the glowing cookware at the stunned duplicate and breaks the villain's concentration.

Caption text:

- “The duplicate possessed telekinesis and attacked me from a distance without needing to come close.”
- “I struggled to escape the flying objects and force waves while searching for a way to fight back.”
- “Then I remembered that an unfamiliar body could not prevent me from using Lucid Light.”
- “Sparkling purple power gathered around my hands, ready to become whatever my lucid mind demanded.”
- “For reasons I did not yet understand, I manifested an assortment of pots and pans.”
- “The absurd attack caught the shapeshifter completely off guard and broke their telekinetic rhythm.”

## Page 7 — Cookware Combat

Six action panels. Heroine-bodied Jet confidently summons more star-edged metal cookware. She catches a large frying pan in one hand and a stockpot in the other, rushes the identical duplicate, and blocks a magenta force blast with a pot lid. Jet sweeps aside levitating debris with the frying pan, strikes the duplicate's armored shoulder nonlethally, and follows with a stockpot swing. The duplicate uses telekinesis to suspend several pans, but Jet yanks them free with Lucid Light and sends them ricocheting around the villain. Final large panel: Jet drives the duplicate backward beneath a widening purple cosmic vortex filled with hundreds of forming cookware silhouettes.

Caption text:

- “The cookware felt connected to the heroine's own theme, so I accepted the dream's strange logic instead of resisting it.”
- “I created more pans, rushed the duplicate, and used a metal lid to block the next telekinetic blast.”
- “At close range, their copied appearance and distant power no longer gave them control of the fight.”
- “I struck with frying pans, pots, and other kitchen implements while the villain struggled to defend themself.”
- “When telekinesis seized my weapons, Lucid Light pulled them back under my command.”
- “Then I raised my power toward the sky and prepared one overwhelming final attack.”

## Page 8 — The Cookware Storm

Six panels arranged around a large vertical climax. Heroine-bodied Jet raises both hands as a massive circular storm of hundreds of polished pans, pots, lids, trays, and stockpots fills the sky in sparkling purple Lucid Light. The magenta-aura duplicate tries to stop the first wave telekinetically, but the volume overwhelms the defense. Cookware rains down in controlled nonlethal impacts and accumulates around the duplicate. The shapeshifter becomes completely buried beneath a mountain of metal, with one fading magenta hand briefly visible before disappearing. Jet stands victorious on the edge of the pile, clearly still in the heroine body, while the purple energy fades. Final panel: an alarm motif fractures the dream into warm bedroom morning light as Jet awakens in his normal male body.

Caption text:

- “Hundreds of pots and pans gathered above us inside a massive storm of Lucid Light.”
- “The shapeshifter tried to hold the first wave back with telekinesis, but there were too many objects to control.”
- “Cookware crashed down one piece after another until the duplicate vanished beneath a mountain of metal.”
- “The villain had copied my temporary body, but they could not copy the lucid awareness that made my choices my own.”
- “With the shapeshifter completely defeated, my alarm began to sound.”
- “The recruitment drive dissolved, the Storyverse released me, and I woke up.”
