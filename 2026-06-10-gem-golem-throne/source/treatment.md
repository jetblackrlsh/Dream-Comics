# Treatment

## Title

06/10/2026: Gem Golem Throne

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet tests his immortality in a forest, survives a strange Were-Owl abduction through shapeshifting, and earns a jeweled underground kingdom by proving to suspicious gem golems that he is a construct unlike any they have known.

## Central Conflict

Jet's freedom and playful body control clash with a world that judges identity by material. The gem golems distrust him because he looks organic, then revere him after his action-figure immortality reveals a body that can be dismantled and rebuilt.

## Theme

Identity is not limited to surface material. In a lucid dream, transformation can turn a threat into a language others understand.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alone, curious, and fearless, testing immortality as a private experiment. His wild freedom as a Were-Rat is interrupted by the familiar Were-Owl woman, forcing him to improvise. Underground, the hostile gem golems become an audience for his strange plastic-body trick, and Jet moves from hunted shape-shifter to recognized ruler.

## Ending

The gem golems gently rebuild Jet, accept him as a rare construct made from unknown material, and seat him on a gem throne deep beneath the forest. The final image turns the earlier dismemberment threat into a ceremonial coronation.
