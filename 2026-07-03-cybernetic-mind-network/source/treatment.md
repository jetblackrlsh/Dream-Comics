# Treatment

## Logline

After Jet and Skelebot heal a red mummy zombie woman into a restored pharaoh, Jet enters a cybernetic multiversal mind network where he pilots infinite variants, studies a five-rule magic solitaire combat game, and struggles to decide whether the most realistic psychedelic feed is only a dream or a real system.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to understand the cybernetic mind network while his lucidity keeps slipping. The network offers endless first-person variant lives, but each feed has different limits, distortions, and rules. Some cybernetics are clear, some are cheap and grainy, and one jailbroken psychedelic feed feels so realistic that Jet questions whether the phenomenon might exist beyond the dream. The conflict is not a villain to defeat; it is Jet trying to remain lucid long enough to map the system before the channels overwhelm him.

## Theme

Lucidity is not only control. Sometimes it is the discipline to observe a strange system clearly before deciding what it means.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins with confident lucid power, using his rainbow sword to heal a cursed figure with Skelebot beside him. Once the cybernetic channels open, his confidence becomes fascination and confusion. The homeless variant makes the network feel morally strange and personal. The psychedelic variant makes the network feel frighteningly real. The repeated magic solitaire game gives Jet one clear pattern to hold onto. By the end, Jet accepts that he does not fully understand the network yet, but he leaves with a concrete memory and a reason to watch for it again.

## Ending

Jet pulls back from the infinite channel wall, still holding the memory of the five-rule card game and the uncanny realism of the jailbroken feed. He stands in the Storyverse void with Skelebot, looking toward countless cybernetic bubble universes, and clearly wonders whether he will encounter the phenomenon again.
