# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries the Life Sword, a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword, and a flexible energy tether resembling a rope made from golden glittering light clipped to his belt.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy shining like a night sky full of stars. When used as laser vision, it forms two narrow purple star-filled beams from his eyes.
- Flight leaves a faint purple starry wake, but Jet never wears a cape, mask, conventional superhero costume, or helmet.
- Metal form: the same recognizable man, clothes, beard, and dreadlock silhouette coated in seamless reflective chrome armor, with warm brown eyes still visible.
- Liquid-metal form: a conscious, reflective silver humanoid stream retaining suggestions of his bearded face and dreadlock silhouette, able to wrap around another person and solidify without gore.
- Expression range: lucid confidence, alarm, sudden realization, compassionate resolve, competitive concentration, dry humor, curiosity, delighted surprise.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact human outfit and identifiers whenever he is in normal form. After reforming, every garment and prop returns intact.

## Giant blue slime monster / ocean guardian

- Initially a colossal translucent cobalt-blue half-orb of slime, several stories tall, with twelve long flexible tendrils, two glowing cyan oval eyes, a broad dark mouth, and wet iridescent highlights.
- It moves by rippling across streets and striking with tendrils. It must remain a single half-orb creature, not a humanoid, octopus, dragon, or collection of smaller blobs.
- Soul form: a compact turquoise-blue vapor being with the same two cyan oval eyes and mouth, wispy tendril shapes, and a faint spectral outline. It can cough, recoil, and fly under its own power.
- Purified form: the same body and silhouette, but its eyes soften, its mouth becomes friendly, and golden-rainbow light glows gently within its blue slime.
- In the ocean it spreads across a much larger area while retaining the half-orb center and twelve tendrils, catching ordinary fish for food and protectively surrounding boats without attacking people.
- Continuity rule: keep the cobalt-blue translucent body, half-orb center, twelve tendrils, and cyan eyes consistent before and after purification; only demeanor and inner glow change.

## Crimson-haired rival superhero

- Adult woman with a thin but athletic professional-boxer build, light olive skin, sharp green eyes, and very dark crimson-red hair tied in a long high ponytail.
- Wears a green professional-wrestler-inspired two-piece superhero costume: emerald sleeveless crop top with gold piping, matching high-waisted shorts over opaque dark-green athletic tights, green wrist wraps, and tall emerald boots. The costume is athletic and practical rather than sexualized.
- Fights with disciplined airborne boxer punches, knees, and powerful kicks. She has no weapons, cape, mask, or armor.
- Fire form: her entire dark crimson ponytail ignites into controlled red-orange flame while retaining its ponytail silhouette; both fists and boots burn with the same energy.
- Expression range: proud challenge, fierce competitive joy, surprise, overconfidence, shock, exhausted unconscious calm.
- Motivation: she is not evil and does not threaten civilians. She fights Jet only because his victory displaced her as the publicly recognized number-one hero.
- Continuity rule: keep her long high crimson ponytail, thin athletic build, green two-piece costume over dark-green tights, gold trim, green eyes, wrist wraps, and emerald boots exact in every appearance.

## The Sky Snack Society

- A friendly group of four adult flying superheroes whose purpose is tasting and reviewing potato chips rather than fighting crime.
- Leader: cheerful Black woman with short lavender curls, round gold goggles on her forehead, a bright orange-and-purple flight suit, white gloves, and a small chip satchel.
- Other members: a broad East Asian man in a teal-and-yellow wingless flight suit; a slim brown-skinned nonbinary hero with silver buzz-cut hair and a magenta-and-blue suit; and a stocky white woman with strawberry-blonde braids and a cobalt-and-lime suit.
- All four fly without wings, capes, helmets, weapons, or visible propulsion. They carry resealable metallic snack pouches and small blank rating cards with star symbols but no readable decorative text.
- Their signature chip is a golden triangular tortilla-style potato chip with visible salt crystals, a glossy honey sheen, and tiny black and red pepper flecks.
- Continuity rule: keep the four-person membership, individual silhouettes, bright costume palettes, snack pouches, and friendly expressions consistent.

## Superhero city and ocean

- A bright coastal metropolis of white, turquoise, and coral high-rises, elevated monorails, crowded civic plazas, and a wide blue ocean beyond the harbor.
- Early destruction is localized: dented streets, overturned empty cars, water splashes, and fleeing crowds without visible injury or death.
- After the slime monster is relocated, the city becomes calm and celebratory while the huge friendly guardian remains clearly visible offshore.

## Established crew continuity

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's Storyverse team. None appear physically in this comic because Jet arrives alone. Do not insert them into crowds, skies, reflections, or background cameos.
