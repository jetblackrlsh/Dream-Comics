# 08/19/2026 - The Snowfire Avatar

Dream Comics entry for August 19, 2026.

In a snowy wilderness, Jet creates a cozy wall-less sanctuary around a purple couch and a magical fire pit while reflecting on the difficulty of finding human role-playing partners online. When he tests whether the setting is too peaceful by briefly transforming it into Hell, the returning cold flash-freezes the charging fire demons.

Jet discovers that the fire is bonded to him and can protect him from cold powerful enough to freeze hellfire. After accidentally thawing one demon's face, the creature bites away his human exterior and reveals a robotic dream avatar beneath. Jet activates eye lasers, refracts them through the frozen demons, and turns the entire forest into a spectacular chain reaction of ice, color, flame, and light.

The finished package includes the complete journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, and assembled PDF.
