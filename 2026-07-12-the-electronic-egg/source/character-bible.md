# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Black hoodie worn open over a black “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt; black athletic pants with one white side stripe; white sneakers; black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries a colorful futuristic crystal rainbow sword and a rope-like energy tether made of golden glittery light.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy shining like a star-filled night sky.
- Thoughtful, warm, curious, and emotionally expressive. Never lighten his skin, shorten his dreadlocks, remove his beard, or replace his outfit.

## Doctor Bear

- Large humanoid brown bear with kind amber eyes, rounded muzzle, expressive brows, and a calm scholarly posture.
- White doctor's coat over a teal shirt, dark trousers, stethoscope, and small round spectacles.
- Gentle, attentive, never threatening.

## Flying Sasquatch

- Huge, friendly brown-furred sasquatch with a broad face and expressive dark eyes.
- Bright red athletic jersey, flowing royal-blue cape, bare furry legs and feet.
- Initially frightened and protective, then playful and trusting.

## Blue Cat Jet

- Jet transformed into a tall, broad humanoid blue cat with thick cobalt fur and feline ears, while retaining Jet's warm brown eyes, full beard shape translated into darker blue facial fur, long dreadlock-like mane, black hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped black pants, white sneakers, and belt bag.
- Friendly open hands and playful body language.

## Midnight Archer

- Mortal adult woman with pale skin, sharp green eyes, and a confident focused expression.
- Gothic black hood with small cat-ear points, chin-length black hair, dark plum lipstick, fitted black-and-deep-purple armored archer suit, silver cat emblem, black gauntlets and boots.
- High-tech black bow with violet energy accents and a quiver of distinctive trick arrows.
- Athletic but recognizably human; no aura, flight, or innate superpowers.

## Manifestor

- Lean supervillain in angular crimson-and-white armor, smooth white mask with a red vertical visor, and a ragged crimson cape.
- Manifests solid objects through geometric red energy constructs.

## Superhero team

- A small background ensemble of visually godlike heroes: a gold-armored flying woman, a massive blue stone man, and a glowing solar knight.
- Their powers are spectacular but ineffective against the linked train-and-missile crisis.

## Electronic egg

- Football-sized ovoid device with pearl-white segmented metal, cyan and magenta circuit seams, a dark glass equator, and tiny portals showing alternate realities and game worlds.
