# 08/26/2026 - The Sanctuary Ladder

Dream Comics entry for August 26, 2026.

Jet enters a lucid spirit-hopping dream inside the body of a black werewolf. He stands within a living sanctuary on cold pink liquid glass that moves in waves without carrying him. When he questions why he is someone else, the rainbow crystal sword reveals that the werewolf is another of its holders from across time.

Jet leaves the sanctuary by climbing down an impossible ladder from an enormous black floating rock toward a distant city. When the ladder touches the ground, he loses lucidity and becomes himself inside a strange academy. After an ignored video, a missed birthday message, a false awakening, and a beautiful art installation made from potentially dangerous insulation, ominous women begin pursuing him with utensils while inexplicably ignoring another man.

Jet escapes into a stairwell and attempts to stop one retreating woman with Lucid Light. Remembering his power restores his lucidity and returns him to the elevated ladder, now in the body of a red-and-yellow fire elemental. He realizes that the floating sanctuary is a lucid realm, the city is a grounded non-lucid dream, and the ladder physically represents the connection between those states.

The finished package includes the complete journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, and assembled PDF.
