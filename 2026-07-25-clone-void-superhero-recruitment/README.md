# 07/25/2026 - The Clone in the Void and the Superhero Recruitment Drive

Dream Comics entry for July 25, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet enters an endless black void and meets a stranger at a table who asks him to pitch a comic-book idea. A strange pulsing sensation from Jet's waking body becomes an impossible pregnancy inside the dream. Jet uses Lucid Light to remove the mass safely, and it becomes a black bag containing a glowing humanoid silhouette. The stranger transforms into a giant rat monster, brutally pummels the bag, and flees. Jet revives the victim with the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life and discovers an exact clone of himself.

After Jet loses lucidity, the dream becomes a montage of superheroes recruiting ordinary people. When his awareness returns, Jet is inhabiting the body of a cookware-themed female superhero and remains internally himself. A supposed recruit proves to be a telekinetic shapeshifter, copies the heroine's appearance, and attacks. Jet remembers that his Lucid Light is independent of his appearance, manifests pots and pans, overwhelms the duplicate in close combat, and buries the villain beneath a storm of cookware before waking to his alarm.
