# Treatment - The Doorman of the Lava Sky

## Logline

An amnesiac dimensional traveler called the Doorman must outwit a predatory pantheon, survive a divine bargain, and combine unlimited god-magic with Lucid Light to break the lava prison surrounding an entire world.

## Premise

Jet steps out of a freestanding dimensional elevator into a surreal rocky desert. Black-bone castles cover the landscape, and moving red, orange, violet, and golden lava seals the sky like a gigantic lava lamp.

Autumn, a woman in a reddish-brown Victorian dress patterned with golden leaves, claims that she is Jet's wife. She says that his recurring memory problems are annoying because they have already discussed their relationship and their many journeys together. Jet remembers none of it. Autumn identifies him as the Doorman, the dimensional elevator's operator and an experienced traveler between worlds.

## Inciting mission

Autumn summons Apollo. The solar god descends through a partial opening in the lava sky and explains that the planet has been completely deprived of sunlight. He tells Jet that restoring the world requires defeating Meltronome, the god of lava.

After delivering the Doorman's title and arranging the mission, Autumn disappears. Jet continues alone, unsure whether she left with Apollo.

## First obstacle

A gigantic black-bone dinosaur skeleton attacks and swallows Jet. Its rib cage is hollow, so Jet is not digested. He forms controls from sparkling purple Lucid Light and pilots the skeletal dinosaur like a mech.

The attack draws the god of blades, a towering deity associated with black bones and cutting weapons. The blade god initially believes Jet intends to kill the whole pantheon or seize Meltronome's supreme position. Jet explains that he wants neither extermination nor rulership. Once the god realizes that Meltronome's vacant position could improve his own rank, he allows Jet to pass and gives him a divine black-bone blade.

## Divine bargain

Jet is transported to a magical academy, where many people wait to meet a hulking gray-skinned man in a business suit. The divine blade lets Jet bypass the line. The man is the god of deals, whose apparently unlimited magic is constrained by bargains, transfers, and sacrifices.

The god transfers his vast magical power into Jet. The bargain's price falls upon another person at the academy, whose lightning and rhinoceros abilities the deal god consumes. The gray businessman transforms into a huge humanoid rhinoceros surrounded by blue-white electricity.

Jet understands the bargain's hidden logic. In a world where gods compete and kill one another, a deal-maker with conditional magic is vulnerable and permanently targeted. He has surrendered godhood to Jet while stealing direct combat abilities so that he can remain powerful without occupying a dangerous position in the pantheon.

## Reversal

Although the transformed rhinoceros has escaped divine politics, he remains cruel and dangerous. Everyone in the academy fears that he will kill them.

Jet uses his amplified magic to cast Porta. Two linked purple portals divide the rhinoceros spatially without blood or injury. His legs wander somewhere in the distant desert while his upper body remains alive, angry, and harmless inside the academy.

## Climax

The former god of deals could use his magic only through bargains and sacrifices. Jet already possessed Lucid Light, Porta, and other Storyverse magic before receiving that power. Once the divine energy belongs to him, he can channel it without the old restrictions.

Jet reaches across the world with an immense Lucid Light spell and casts Meltronome away. The colossal lava deity vanishes, and the flowing molten shell around the planet breaks apart.

## Ending

True sunlight floods the desert for the first time. Apollo rises through the open sky like the sun itself and declares that he is now the world's supreme god.

Jet does not know why Apollo wanted the throne, but the prior pantheon was openly violent and predatory. He hopes the new reign will be kinder. His mission ends with sunlight restored, while the greater mystery remains: a wife named Autumn, a dimensional elevator, and an entire life as the Doorman that he still cannot remember.

## Theme

Power is shaped by the rules governing its use. A seemingly omnipotent god can be trapped by conditions, while an outsider becomes transformative because he is not bound by the world's assumptions.

## Emotional arc

Jet moves from intimate disorientation, through cautious negotiation and intellectual understanding, into decisive protection of innocent people. His final victory restores a world but leaves his own missing identity unresolved, turning triumph into the beginning of a larger myth.
