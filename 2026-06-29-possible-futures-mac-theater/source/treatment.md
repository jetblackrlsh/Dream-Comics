# Treatment

## Logline

After sleep paralysis opens a lucid gate, Jet crosses from a future where robots redefine family to an edible ocean and then into a theater world where a false awakening and a poisoned drink force him to prove that lucidity is also a matter of knowing himself.

## Central Conflict

Jet is swept through imaginative worlds that each test a different kind of certainty: what future robot relationships could mean, whether impossible sensory delight can be trusted, and whether a stage life made convincing by false awakening can make him forget his real values. The jealous co-star's poisoning attempt turns the dream from wonder into danger, but the trap only works if Jet abandons self-knowledge.

## Theme

Lucidity is not only control over the dream; it is the ability to recognize what fits your identity and what does not.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins reflective and analytical in a conjunction of possible futures, considering companion robots, worker-bots, and family. The dream becomes playful and sensory when he swims through a thick macaroni-and-cheese ocean beside a cracker-crust beach. The theater world then gives him public praise and a genius identity, making the false awakening feel seductively real. When the jealous co-star offers him a poisoned alcoholic drink, Jet's grounded refusal cuts through the illusion. He ends calm and lucid, using Lucid Light to summon the authorities and leave the strange adventure with joy instead of fear.

## Ending

Jet recognizes the poisoned drink as impossible for him to accept, regains lucidity, raises sparkling purple Lucid Light, and calls in dream authorities. The final page shows the co-star being taken away as the theater transforms back into the Storyverse, with Jet smiling because the wild sequence of worlds became another lucid victory.
