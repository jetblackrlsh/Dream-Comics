# Treatment

## Logline

After a hostile tiger woman draws lucid dreamer Jet into conflict with a planet-sized Alpha, Jet escapes through an ordinary door in space and discovers a snowy tropical rainforest—then a waking interruption lets him reconstruct the same world in autumn, enter its lava, and preserve both impossible landscapes within his memory.

## Central conflict

Jet's first conflict is physical and immediate: he is mouse-sized, pursued by a much larger purple tiger woman, and accused of impersonating her Alpha after his Lucid Light tiger mech causes a misunderstanding. His lucidity keeps him from being intimidated, while the Rainbow Crystal Sword makes the tiger woman's claws useless and protects him from her attempt to steal it. The conflict expands when her leader is revealed to be the planet itself, forcing Jet to leave that reality entirely.

The deeper conflict arrives after Jet finds peace in a snowy rainforest and then wakes briefly. The interruption breaks an experience he wanted to continue. Jet responds by reentering sleep and deliberately reconstructing the route. He cannot perfectly restore the first dream: the Alpha transformation is absent and the rainforest has changed from winter to autumn. Instead of treating the variation as failure, Jet enters the lava, transforms himself, and chooses to hold both worlds together in memory.

## Theme

An interrupted creative experience does not have to be recovered as an exact copy to remain valuable. Reconstruction can produce a second vision that enriches the first. Lucidity gives Jet power over danger, but his most meaningful act is attention: he studies two impossible landscapes carefully enough that neither is lost.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins lucid, calm, and unintimidated even while physically tiny. He becomes inventive and playful when he constructs the tiger mech, then firm when the tiger woman refuses his invitation and continues attacking. Escape brings wonder as the planet becomes the Alpha and an ordinary door opens into an impossible snowy rainforest. Rest beside the lava gives Jet warmth and peace.

The waking interruption creates frustration because it breaks the experience. Jet returns determined to reconstruct the dream, initially noticing what has failed to repeat. Curiosity replaces frustration when he discovers the autumn rainforest. By entering the lava and becoming a living fire form, he fully commits to the altered version. He ends contemplative and fulfilled, preserving both seasonal worlds instead of choosing one as the correct dream.

## Continuity

This dream belongs to the Storyverse continuity that began on June 11, 2025. Jet has dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the open hoodie over a visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. His Rainbow Crystal Sword is a colorful futuristic crystal blade. His energy tether is a rope made of golden glittering light. Lucid Light is sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a night sky full of stars.

Jet is reduced to mouse size on page 2. His physical proportions and outfit remain unchanged; only his scale relative to the corridor and tiger woman changes. The Lucid Light tiger mech on page 3 is translucent violet armor filled with stars, built around the tiny Jet. When he shrinks the mech, Jet returns to the tiger woman's normal adult scale and remains that size for the rest of the dream.

The lava transformation begins on page 6 and persists through the ending. Jet remains recognizable by his face, beard, long dreadlock silhouette, hoodie-shaped upper-body contour, pants silhouette, belt bag, sword, and tether, but his skin and clothes appear transformed into black volcanic stone crossed by brilliant orange-yellow molten fissures. His dreadlocks become flowing strands of dark volcanic rock tipped with flame, his eyes glow warm gold, and heat surrounds him without harming the rainforest.

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper remain Jet's established crew, but none appears physically in this focused episode. Do not insert them into crowds, space, cave reflections, or landscape backgrounds.

## Ending

The final page visually combines the same rainforest in two equally vivid forms. On one side, snow falls beneath a blue atmosphere across round tropical canopies. On the other, red, orange, and yellow leaves fill a mild autumn landscape. Lava-form Jet moves between the two visions high above the matching terrain, framing details with his hands like a photographer without a camera. The overlap becomes a paired memory rather than a continuity error. The visual consequence is clear: waking interrupted the first dream, but Jet's reconstruction created a second impossible world worth preserving beside it.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: lava-form Jet flies between the snowy blue and autumn red-gold versions of the rounded-canopy rainforest, while the Alpha planet, tiger woman, Lucid Light mech, wooden space door, and cave lava form a layered record of the journey.
2. Jet induces lucidity, enters the Storyverse at mouse size in a vast corridor, and is caught by the long-haired purple tiger woman without feeling afraid.
3. Jet constructs a gigantic Lucid Light tiger mech, is mistaken for the Alpha, exposes the misunderstanding, survives the tiger woman's claws through the healing sword, and blocks her attempt to steal it.
4. Jet stops arguing, escapes through the ceiling, watches the planet unfurl into the enormous Alpha, outruns him, and enters an ordinary wooden door standing in space.
5. Jet explores the blue-tinted snowy rainforest with rounded canopies, finds the lava cave, rests in its warmth, and is pulled away by a brief waking interruption.
6. Jet reenters and reconstructs the route, discovers that the Alpha transformation is absent and the rainforest is now in autumn, then steps into the lava river.
7. The lava transforms Jet into a living god of fire and molten stone; he flies above the autumn landscape as his memory of the snowy version begins overlapping it.
8. Jet studies both seasonal rainforests like a photographer without a camera and preserves them together in memory as the dream ends.
