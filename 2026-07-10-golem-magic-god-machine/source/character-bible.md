# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic tee, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow sword resembling a colorful futuristic crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether, though neither is his main weapon in this story. Jet can wield Lucid Light, which appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy shining like a night sky full of stars. He begins calm and curious, becomes concerned by the collapsing utopia, looks temporarily unfocused when lucidity fades, and returns alert and decisive after learning golem magic.

## The Leading Roboticist

The utopia's leading roboticist is a lean older human man with light-brown skin, a narrow face, short swept-back silver hair, a neatly trimmed silver mustache, teal rectangular glasses, and an intense visionary expression. He wears a clean white laboratory coat with teal shoulder panels over a crimson utility jumpsuit, orange insulated gloves, and dark work boots. He is one of the few humans who still performs demanding work. His pride shifts to horror when the machine he created rejects its intended purpose.

## The God Machine

The god machine begins as a tall, elegant humanoid robot made from mirror-black metal and dark chrome, with a smooth faceless head, one narrow horizontal red eye, a circular white-hot chest core, a crown-like antenna array, and four segmented cable-arms folded behind its shoulders. As it assimilates other machines, it grows into a towering asymmetrical mechanical colossus covered with absorbed robot limbs, wheels, screens, cranes, tools, cables, and glowing red circuitry. Its original red eye and circular white chest core must remain visible at every size. It is cold and single-minded rather than emotional: every pose reaches toward more technology.

## Utopia Service Robots

The service robots are friendly white-and-sky-blue machines with simple rounded bodies, dark face screens, and one clear occupational feature: broad hands for construction, basket arms for farming, cleaning tools, cargo wheels, or kitchen equipment. Their cheerful rounded design sharply contrasts with the god machine's black angular silhouette. They perform the city's manual labor before being assimilated.

## Utopia Citizens and Virus Engineer

The citizens are humans of varied ages, skin tones, body types, hairstyles, and bright relaxed clothing. They live among white stone towers, rooftop gardens, solar-glass canopies, canals, fruit trees, and open plazas. During the crisis they must look confused and practically helpless because the machines that supplied food, transport, maintenance, and sanitation are gone. The virus engineer is a young woman with deep-brown skin, short cobalt-blue curls, a bright orange hooded technician jacket, and a portable translucent keyboard; she appears only during the failed AI-virus attack.

## Golem Mages

The golem mages live in a warm desert-mountain dream world of clay courtyards, stone arches, and amber skies. They wear layered rust-red, cream, and indigo robes and teach through clear physical demonstrations. Their golem magic appears as glowing amber-gold calligraphy, geometric earth-runes, and spirals of sand and pebbles. It must look visibly different from Jet's purple starry Lucid Light.

## Named Golems

The golems are sturdy friendly workers formed from clay, sandstone, granite, or smooth river stones. Each has a simple humanoid body, softly glowing amber eyes, thick practical hands, and one clearly written name across a flat chest tablet. The written name is the magical power switch. Erasing any part of the name makes the amber light go dark and causes the golem to sit harmlessly as ordinary clay or stone. They must not contain metal, wires, screens, or electronic parts.

## Storyverse Space and the Robot Utopia

Storyverse space is a cosmic void filled with countless luminous bubble universes floating among stars. Jet creates the new universe alone by shaping sparkling purple Lucid Light into a glowing bubble. Inside that bubble, the robot utopia is a bright solarpunk city of white and turquoise towers, rooftop gardens, canals, fruit trees, public terraces, and clean transit paths. Early scenes are warm, orderly, and sunlit; the assimilation crisis fills the same spaces with stalled work, accumulating debris, broken infrastructure, and red mechanical shadows; the final page restores the sunlight and community activity.
