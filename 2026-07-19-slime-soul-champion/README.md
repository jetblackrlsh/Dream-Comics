# 07/19/2026 - Slime Soul Champion

Dream Comics entry for July 19, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet flies into a superhero city and accidentally separates a giant slime monster's soul from its body. He purifies the living vapor with the Life Sword, returns it to the monster, and transforms the former threat into a gentle ocean guardian. The victory makes Jet the world's number-one hero, drawing a fiery red-haired rival into a brutal aerial challenge that forces him to survive as living liquid metal. After proving himself again, Jet discovers that even the strongest flying heroes can find purpose in something as simple as sharing an extraordinary honey-salt-and-spice potato chip.
