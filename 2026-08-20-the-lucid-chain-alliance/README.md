# 08/20/2026 - The Lucid Chain Alliance

Dream Comics entry for August 20, 2026.

Jet travels above an immense forest on a living quilted dragon patterned like blue-and-white china. An elven princess with literal sapphire eyes guides the dragon toward a castle while her jealous silver-armored knight objects to Jet's magical intervention. When the knight steals Jet's Lucid Light ring, Jet turns it into a parachute and launches him safely into the woods—only to learn that the forest is swarming with hostile orcs.

After an orc attack tears the dragon's fabric wing, the princess crash-lands and magically sews it back together. Jet uses a sister ring to find the knight, but the paired magic pulls Jet backward through the trees. Surrounded by orcs, Jet materializes the bond between the rings as a massive Lucid Light chain. He and the knight transform their conflict into cooperation, using the chain for a spectacular human-flail and Beyblade sword attack before returning as allies to the repaired dragon.

The finished package includes the complete journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, and assembled PDF.
