# Page Script - The Patron's Stew

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. All characters, artifacts, and settings follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic full-page composition. Muddy Jet raises the glowing Rainbow Crystal Sword in the dark swamp. Second Brain floats at his shoulder with purple bat wings spread. Behind them, the enormous giant bat recoils inside a gold-white beam from Apollo's chariot breaking through black clouds. The flooded mineshaft gapes below.

Visible cover text only: “THE PATRON'S STEW” and “AUGUST 27, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - Three Choices

A clear three-panel setup page. Show Jet considering the three requirements inside the swamp; Apollo leaning from his sun chariot with a bemused shrug; then Jet standing with Second Brain and the Rainbow Crystal Sword as his complete warlock trio.

Caption text:

- “Inside a dark swamp, I decided that becoming a warlock required a patron, a familiar, and an arcane focus.”
- “I chose Apollo because he had called me a doorman of dimensions and sent me on a quest before.”
- “Apollo accepted; Second Brain became my familiar, and the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life became my focus.”

## Page 3 - Beyond the Sun

A clear three-panel quest page. Show Apollo pointing toward the dark mineshaft; Jet and Second Brain entering while the last sunlight fades behind them; then Second Brain hovering anxiously as Jet confidently raises the Sword in near-total darkness.

Caption text:

- “Apollo immediately sent us into a dark mineshaft hidden within the swamp.”
- “Second Brain warned that a sun patron could give us little power where no sunlight reached.”
- “I believed that was exactly why Apollo needed his warlock to enter a place beyond his domain.”

## Page 4 - Still Water

A clear three-panel hazard page. Show Jet and Second Brain suddenly submerged in opaque green-black water; the Sword producing a prismatic explosive barrier that blasts them into a dry tunnel; then green-gold healing light cleansing them while Jet studies the filthy water with alarm.

Caption text:

- “The tunnel dropped us into deep still water before either of us could react.”
- “I used the Sword to explode us free, then healed us against whatever contamination touched us.”
- “Old mine water could carry deadly bacteria or viruses, so I treated the danger as real.”

## Page 5 - The Theft

A clear three-panel escalation page. Show the giant animal bat diving for the Rainbow Crystal Sword; Jet gripping the hilt while the bat carries him through the mineshaft; then the bat flinging muddy Jet into shallow swamp water as Second Brain races after them.

Caption text:

- “Before we recovered, a gigantic bat swooped from the darkness and seized the Sword.”
- “I refused to let go, so the creature carried me out of the mine with Second Brain behind us.”
- “It hurled me into the shallow swamp, but I rose muddy and ready to fight.”

## Page 6 - Patron and Familiar

A clear three-panel battle page. Show Apollo parting the clouds and pinning the giant bat in a gold-white sunbeam; Second Brain transforming into purple starry energy and merging with the Sword; then muddy Jet making two swift upward slashes that sever both wings in non-gory bursts of rainbow crystal light and violet lightning.

Caption text:

- “Apollo parted the clouds, and his sunlight stunned the bat's sensitive eyes.”
- “Second Brain merged with my arcane focus, charging its crystal blade with lucid lightning.”
- “Two rainbow slashes severed the bat's wings without killing it.”

## Page 7 - The Real Quest

A clear three-panel reveal page. Show the wingless bat hobbling safely toward the dark mineshaft; Apollo reaching from his sun chariot to collect the two intact wings; then Apollo disappearing into the clouds while Jet and Second Brain stare upward in disbelief.

Caption text:

- “The wounded bat escaped into the mine instead of dying beneath the sun.”
- “Apollo collected its wings and thanked us for obtaining ingredients for his stew.”
- “Second Brain was horrified that our deadly first quest had only been a patron's grocery errand.”

## Page 8 - Mercy with Consequences

A clear three-panel moral resolution page. Show Jet approaching the shadowed mine with the Sword glowing in healing green-gold light; a symbolic imagined vision of Apollo repeatedly taking magically regrown wings from the same bat; then Jet lowering the Sword beside Second Brain while the bat rests safely in darkness and a thin natural dawn glows beyond the swamp.

Caption text:

- “I considered restoring the bat's wings with the Sword of Life.”
- “Second Brain warned that Apollo could order me to harvest every pair that magically regrew.”
- “I left the bat to heal naturally, refusing to turn mercy into endless exploitation.”

## Caption checklist

- Page 1: 2 visible text elements.
- Pages 2-8: exactly 3 caption boxes per page.
- Every caption is required verbatim, unnumbered, and enclosed in a complete rectangular cream box.
