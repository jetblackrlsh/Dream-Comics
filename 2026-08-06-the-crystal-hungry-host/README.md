# 08/06/2026 - The Crystal-Hungry Host

Dream Comics entry for August 6, 2026.

At a cosmic nightclub, Jet must choose one humanoid dragon host. He selects an elegant customizable robot, upsetting the organic dragon workers who fear that the tireless machine will leave them hungry. Jet responds with compassion by calling Leon to deliver cups of molten gold for every host he did not choose.

The robot has a hunger of its own. In a private VIP lounge, its sensors identify Silonis as edible magical crystal. Jet's illusion cannot fool the machine, so he uses geomancy to remove its red, green, and blue power stones. The robot shuts down, Silonis remains safe, and Jet departs with sympathy for every creature whose choices were shaped by hunger.
