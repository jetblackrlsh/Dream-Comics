# 08/10/2026 - The Red-Eyed Darkness

Dream Comics entry for August 10, 2026.

Jet encounters a living black water tower whose enormous eye turns red as psychic darkness closes around Silonis. The Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life purifies the evil wherever it strikes, turning the corruption white and shattering the tower—but the darkness survives long enough to follow Jet into a nostalgic underground home inspired by Minecraft.

After enjoying the familiar refuge and experimenting with berry recipes, Jet realizes that fleeing deeper into torchlit mines would only trade one danger for many more. He rejects the routes the darkness expects him to take, shapes sparkling purple Lucid Light into a drill, escapes straight through the earth, and flies beyond the sky into the Storyverse.

The finished package includes the complete journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, and assembled PDF.
