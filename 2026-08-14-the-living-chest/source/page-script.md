## Page 1 - Cover

Jet stands at the ruined entrance of the buried monster zoo in his dramatic living-chest fusion, his recognizable human body interwoven with dark wood, gold hardware, and spilling coins. Silonis is planted in the ground, sending radiant life energy beneath him. Behind Jet, the violet dragon searches the collapsed cages, the mole monster erupts through rubble, and the luminous looping portal seals the cave. Visible cover text only: "THE LIVING CHEST" and "AUGUST 14, 2026." No issue number.

## Page 2 - A Dungeon That Made Sense

A clear past-to-present page shows Jet thinking about dungeons while Lucid Light constructs a monster zoo, the magical wards failing, visitors evacuating, and the whole attraction sinking underground.

Caption text:

- "I had always wondered why fantasy treasure caves were called dungeons. Dungeons were prisons; treasure belonged in vaults."
- "So my Lucid Light built a history that made the monsters and money belong together."
- "The dungeon had once been a public monster zoo, protected by magical containment wards."
- "When the wards failed, the zoo was evacuated and buried underground before its creatures could escape."

## Page 3 - The Insurance Claim

In a modern office, the insurance executive explains the buried earnings to Jet. A map and magical projection show the ruined zoo, the trapped monsters, and the insured chest of gold. Jet accepts.

Caption text:

- "The zoo's owners had fled too quickly to recover that day's earnings."
- "Because the lost gold was insured, the insurance company had already paid for it."
- "A bald executive with a gray beard hired me to retrieve the company's money."
- "I agreed—but I had no intention of fighting blindly through an underground zoo."

## Page 4 - A Pulse of Life

At the rubble-covered entrance, Jet excavates a tunnel with Lucid Light, plants Silonis in the earth, and sends rainbow life energy through a cutaway view of the ground until it reaches the chest.

Caption text:

- "At the ruined site, I opened a passage into the buried attraction."
- "The darkness might contain any number of surviving monsters, so I chose a safer search."
- "I drove Silonis, the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life, into the ground."
- "Its pulse followed the earth until it found the treasure—and brought the chest to life."

## Page 5 - Two Monsters, One Chest

Deep inside the zoo, the awakened chest opens amber eyes while held in the violet dragon's claws. A cutaway shows Jet seeing through the chest as the mole monster tunnels toward him.

Caption text:

- "Through the living chest's new eyes, I saw an enormous dragon holding the gold."
- "The dragon immediately realized that its treasure had awakened."
- "Worse, the life pulse had alerted a mole monster beneath my feet."
- "I had to move the chest before the dragon reacted—and escape before the mole reached me."

## Page 6 - Become the Treasure

Jet activates the life-energy fusion. The chest is ripped from the dragon in a stream of gold and wood, travels through glowing underground energy, and merges into Jet's body as heroic wooden-and-golden armor.

Caption text:

- "Then I remembered that my life energy could fuse me with another living thing."
- "The chest was alive now, so distance no longer had to matter."
- "I fused with it and tore the treasure instantly out of the dragon's grasp."
- "In one transformation, I became a man of flesh, polished wood, and gold."

## Page 7 - The Door to Nowhere

Fused Jet backdashes from the cave in a burst of purple Lucid Light as the mole erupts nearby and the dragon charges through the tunnel. Jet casts a brilliant circular portal across the entrance; diagram-like panel action shows both monsters entering and being returned to the same side.

Caption text:

- "The missing treasure enraged the dragon, and the mole monster was almost upon me."
- "I backdashed away from the entrance with a burst of Lucid Light."
- "Then I sealed the tunnel with a portal that led nowhere."
- "Anything entering it returned to the same side, forever unable to cross."

## Page 8 - Claim Recovered

At sunrise outside the secured ruins, Jet ends the fusion and the complete chest separates safely from his body. The final panels show him delivering the gold to the astonished executive and walking away with Silonis.

Caption text:

- "Once I was safe, I released the fusion and separated from the treasure."
- "The chest returned intact, filled with the monster zoo's long-buried earnings."
- "I delivered the gold to the insurance company without fighting either monster."
- "The dungeon made sense—and so did the way I conquered it."
