# Treatment

## Logline

After a superhero game satisfies Jet's appetite for action but leaves him craving story, Johnson builds Superhero Mandroid, a rocket-fisted guardian who must win astral thread from a hypnotic cyclops witch before he can seal nightmare portals across the Storyverse.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to turn raw superhero spectacle into a purposeful protector for dreamers. The missing astral thread forces Jet, Johnson, and their new robot into a magical confrontation where direct combat is less important than identifying the witch's source of control.

## Theme

Power becomes meaningful when it is given a mission. Superhero Mandroid is not created just to punch monsters; he is created to protect the boundary between dreams and nightmares.

## Ending

Superhero Mandroid's immunity to hypnosis lets him destroy the witch's blue crystal amulet. The witch surrenders the astral thread, Johnson installs it, and Mandroid launches through the Storyverse to sew shut nightmare portals for sleeping dreamers.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins with the excitement of new superhero powers but feels the missing weight of story. By the end, he has transformed that feeling into a living narrative: a protector with a clear purpose, tools, and first mission.

## Cast

- Jet: Lucid dreamer and Storyverse adventurer who turns mixed reality superhero action into a dream-world mission.
- Johnson: Orange humanoid goldfish engineer in a blue astronaut suit, builder of Superhero Mandroid.
- Superhero Mandroid: White-skinned humanoid robot superhero with slicked-back brown hair, red unitard, yellow "SHM" chest insignia, yellow cape, huge mechanical green fists, holo shield, and rapid regenerating rocket punches.
- Cyclops Witch: Light purple-skinned witch with one glowing red eye, shoulder-length white hair, dark purple outfit, and a glowing blue crystal amulet that powers hypnotic whispers.
