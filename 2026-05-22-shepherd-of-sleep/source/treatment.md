# Treatment

## Title

05/22/2026: The Shepherd of Sleep

## Logline

After sleep paralysis opens a lucid path through the Storyverse, Jet searches for an unfinished superhero idea named Captain Dream and instead finds the fearless Shepherd of Sleep, a godlike dream cowboy defending cloud sheep from lightning-wrapped nightmare wolves.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to understand and complete a rough character idea, but the dream refuses the first draft. The world reveals a truer form of Captain Dream, then tests that identity through an attack on the flock he protects.

## Theme

Dream creation is discovery. A lucid dreamer can arrive with an idea, but the Storyverse can answer with the version that was waiting underneath.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alone and investigative, expecting to meet a power-armored superhero. He becomes disoriented when the dream replaces that concept with a cloud island, sacred temples, and a calm shepherd. Awe replaces confusion when the Shepherd stands fearless against the nightmare wolves, and Jet leaves with respect for the dream's deeper design.

## Ending

The Shepherd of Sleep drives the nightmare wolves back with blue psychic blasts from his wooden staff. The cloud sheep settle, the temples glow, and Jet realizes his rough idea has become something stronger than what he imagined before sleep.
