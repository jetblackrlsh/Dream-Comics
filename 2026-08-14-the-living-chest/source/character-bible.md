# Character Bible - The Living Chest

## Jet

- African-American man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard and mustache, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Long dreadlocks spill from beneath a black hoodie worn over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt.
- Black athletic pants with one clean white stripe down each outer leg, white high-top sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build, alert posture, and a thoughtful expression that becomes fiercely confident when his plan works.
- Wields sparkling purple Lucid Light and Silonis, a large colorful futuristic Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life.

## Jet - Living Chest fusion

- Jet remains unmistakably himself: the same dark-brown face and skin, warm eyes, full beard, long dreadlocks, hoodie silhouette, pants, shoes, and cross-body bag.
- One shoulder, forearm, thigh, and side of his torso become polished dark treasure-chest wood with bright gold bands, hinges, corner plates, locks, and seams.
- Gold coins and radiant life energy glow through gaps in the wooden armor; the transformation is heroic and elegant, never grotesque.
- Silonis and the purple Lucid Light remain visible so the fusion reads as one of Jet's powers rather than a separate armored character.

## Insurance executive

- Older bald man with medium-brown skin, a neatly trimmed gray beard, calm dark eyes, and a stocky office-worker build.
- Wears a tailored cobalt-blue business suit, white shirt, green striped tie, black dress shoes, and a small gold insurance-company lapel pin.
- Professional and matter-of-fact, presenting a bizarre monster-recovery mission as ordinary claims work.

## The living treasure chest

- Large antique chest made from dark polished wood with heavy gold bands, ornate gold corner plates, a large central keyhole, and piles of bright coins inside.
- When awakened, two small amber-gold eyes appear in the wood above the lock; its lid and hinges move like an expressive face.
- The chest never grows limbs. Its life is communicated by eyes, lid movement, golden glow, and Jet's remote vision through it.

## Dragon

- Enormous ancient cave dragon with jagged midnight-violet scales, obsidian horns, glowing violet eyes, and purple fire between its teeth.
- Crouches among broken monster cages and buried gold deep inside the zoo.
- Powerful and furious when the living chest vanishes, but never reaches Jet before the escape.

## Mole monster

- Massive black-furred subterranean beast with a pink star-shaped nose, glowing violet eyes, huge ivory digging claws, and dirt-covered shoulders.
- Burrows through earth and rubble toward the life-energy pulse, throwing rocks and soil behind it.
- Reads clearly as a distinct ground threat, never as a normal small mole.

## The buried monster zoo

- Once-colorful magical attraction now collapsed underground: arched stone corridors, bent gilded cages, faded ward runes, torn banners, rubble, roots, and scattered coins.
- Flashbacks show the zoo alive with visitors, colorful containment wards, and varied fantasy creatures safely behind cages.
- Present-day scenes use deep indigo shadows, violet Lucid Light, rainbow sword glow, and gold highlights.

## Continuity rules

- Jet's skin tone, beard, long dreadlocks, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white shoes, cross-body bag, Silonis, and Lucid Light remain consistent.
- The fusion adds wood and gold to Jet without hiding his identity or replacing his established clothes completely.
- The executive's bald head, gray beard, blue suit, and green striped tie remain consistent.
- The chest's dark wood, gold bands, central keyhole, and amber eyes remain consistent.
- No gore, speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, creator signatures, or blank caption boxes.
