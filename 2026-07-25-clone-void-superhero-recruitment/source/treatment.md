# Treatment

## Logline

After lucid dreamer Jet revives a mysterious victim in an endless void and discovers his own clone, a loss of lucidity casts him into a superhero recruitment drive inside an unfamiliar female body, where he must defeat a telekinetic shapeshifter by trusting that his identity and Lucid Light remain his own.

## Central conflict

Jet's first conflict is the collision between waking sensation and dream logic. A pulsing feeling in his real stomach becomes an impossible pregnancy inside the void. Although disturbed, Jet knows he is dreaming and uses Lucid Light to remove the mass without blood or pain. His attempt to understand it is interrupted when the apparently harmless comic-book man transforms into a giant rat monster and attacks the life inside. Jet does not prevent the attack, but he takes responsibility for its consequence by using the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life to restore the victim. The revelation that the being is an exact Jet clone turns the strange bodily sensation into an encounter with another self.

The deeper conflict begins after Jet loses lucidity. A fast superhero recruitment drive separates heroism from destiny: ordinary people can decide to become heroes even when the idea initially confuses them. When lucidity returns, Jet is no longer physically recognizable as himself. He is inhabiting a cookware-themed female superhero, and a telekinetic shapeshifter copies that body exactly. The enemy possesses the familiar appearance and the obvious combat advantage. Jet wins by recognizing that neither his unfamiliar body nor the villain's copied surface can take away the lucid awareness that makes him himself.

## Theme

Identity is not limited to appearance. A clone can share Jet's face without replacing him, and a shapeshifter can copy his temporary body without gaining his awareness. Heroism is also not limited to conventional powers or predetermined roles. People can choose to become heroes, and Jet can turn even absurd dream imagery—pots and pans—into an effective expression of will.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins curious and cautiously optimistic during the comic pitch conversation. The pulsing sensation makes him unsettled, but lucidity gives him enough control to act. He becomes uncertain when the rat monster attacks, then compassionate and purposeful when he revives the unknown victim. The sight of his own clone leaves him shocked.

The recruitment montage expands the dream from solitary mystery into communal possibility. Jet regains lucidity disoriented by his unfamiliar female body, then feels cornered when a telekinetic duplicate attacks. Remembering Lucid Light restores his confidence. His first accidental cookware manifestation is bewildering and funny; he rapidly accepts the dream's logic, becomes inventive and aggressive, and finishes with a spectacular, decisive victory. The alarm ends the dream at the exact moment the villain is fully contained.

## Continuity

This dream belongs to the Storyverse continuity that began on June 11, 2025. Jet has dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the open hoodie over a visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. His Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life is a colorful futuristic crystal blade. His energy tether is a rope made of golden glittering light. Lucid Light is sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a night sky full of stars.

The clone has Jet's exact human design and outfit. The cookware heroine is a separate adult female body with a stable superhero design; Jet's internal awareness occupies it from the recruitment sequence through awakening. The shapeshifter copies that heroine exactly, distinguished only by hostile expression and magenta telekinetic energy. The cookware created from Lucid Light is ordinary-looking metal cookware edged by sparkling purple starlight.

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper remain Jet's established Storyverse team, but they do not appear physically in this focused dream. Do not insert them into the void, recruitment crowds, battle, reflections, or cover.

## Ending

The final page shows the shapeshifter completely buried beneath a mountain of metal cookware while heroine-bodied Jet stands above the pile with Lucid Light fading from raised hands. An alarm motif fractures the dream into morning light. The visual consequence is unmistakable: the duplicate copied the heroine's body and possessed stronger conventional powers, but Jet's lucid identity produced the unpredictable choice that defeated them.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: Jet, his revived clone, the rat monster, two identical female superheroes, and a cosmic storm of cookware form a layered portrait of copied identities.
2. Jet induces lucidity, meets the comic-book man at a table in the void, and cautiously discusses pitching a friend's comic.
3. A waking-body pulse becomes an impossible pregnancy; Jet removes the mass with Lucid Light, and the man becomes a rat monster that pulverizes the bag.
4. Jet revives the victim, discovers his own clone, loses lucidity, and passes through a montage of superheroes recruiting confused ordinary people.
5. Jet regains lucidity inside a cookware-themed female superhero, approaches a recruit, and learns that the person is a telekinetic shapeshifter who copies her exact appearance.
6. The shapeshifter attacks from a distance; Jet remembers his Lucid Light and surprises both of them by manifesting and hurling pots and pans.
7. Jet accepts the cookware theme, rushes the duplicate, and wins the close-range struggle with frying pans and pots while telekinesis tears through the scene.
8. Jet summons a massive cookware storm, buries the shapeshifter, and wakes as his alarm dissolves the victorious dream.
