Jet rides a giant living dragon made from quilted blanket fabric and decorated like blue-and-white fine china. An elven princess with literal sapphire gemstones for eyes and her jealous knight accompany him. After Jet turns a stolen Lucid Light ring into a parachute and accidentally ejects the knight into an orc-filled forest, an attack tears the dragon's fabric wing. The princess repairs it with magical needles and thread while Jet follows a sister ring to rescue the knight. The paired rings pull Jet backward into an orc ambush, where their connection becomes a massive Lucid Light chain. Jet swings the sword-wielding knight like a human flail, then breaks the chain to send him spinning like a Beyblade through the remaining orcs. Their absurdly effective team attack transforms rivalry into friendship.
