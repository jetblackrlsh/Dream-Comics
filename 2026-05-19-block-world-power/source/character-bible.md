# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a lucid dream traveler with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow sword that resembles a colorful futuristic crystal sword and an energy tether that resembles a rope made of golden glittery light. In this issue he also wears or holds sleek augmented-reality glasses with transparent lenses and purple Lucid Light reflections. Keep his dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, tee, pants stripe, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow sword, golden tether, and AR glasses consistent.

## Jet Variants

The doppelgangers are alternate Storyverse versions of Jet. They share Jet's dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks, warm brown eyes, and relaxed analytical presence, but each has a distinct outfit silhouette: lab-coat Jet, armored Jet, cyberpunk Jet, mage Jet, astronaut Jet, and streetwear Jet. They should read as variants of the same person, not unrelated characters.

## Storyverse

The Storyverse is a cosmic void full of floating bubble universes, each one containing a possible story. Purple Lucid Light sparkles like a star-filled night sky around portals, prototypes, and moments of reality warping. The doppelganger brainstorming world should look like a circular conference space suspended among many story bubbles.

## Block World AR Glasses

The device is a pair of sleek futuristic augmented-reality glasses connected to real-time interactive AI world models. The glasses superimpose a miniature world of blocky superhumans into Jet's environment. The AR overlay appears as luminous grid lines, voxel terrain, floating simulation windows, and tiny heroic figures standing on tables, floors, notebooks, and lab equipment. Avoid readable interface text except the required caption boxes.

## Block World

Block World is a miniature augmented reality realm inhabited by blocky, voxel-styled superhumans. Characters are small, toy-like, and square-edged, but they still have expressive anime faces, dynamic poses, and glowing elemental effects. The environment should mix physical real-world surfaces with AR-projected voxel cities, block mountains, cosmic portals, and tiny battlefield props.

## World Guard

World Guard is Block World's main hero. He is a miniature blocky superhuman with a square heroic jaw, bright determined eyes, a compact muscular voxel build, and white-and-teal armor marked by a circular elemental chest emblem. The World Power surrounds him as a rotating aura of all elements of the universe: fire, water, wind, stone, lightning, ice, plants, metal, starlight, gravity rings, and rainbow cosmic particles. He is brave but cautious around Space Succubus because direct contact lets her drain him.

## Woofer

Woofer is a clairvoyant, cowardly were-wolf hero from Block World. He is a small blocky were-wolf with gray-brown fur, oversized anxious eyes, pointed ears, a short snout, a hunched nervous posture, clawed hands, and a tattered blue scarf. His future visions appear as pale blue crystalline afterimages, branching path lines, and ghostly silhouettes of incoming attacks. When World Guard shares power with him, Woofer keeps his nervous face but gains a bright elemental aura around his claws and scarf.

## Space Succubus

Space Succubus is a multiversal vampire and World Guard's greatest enemy. Keep her menacing and cosmic rather than explicit: a tall regal vampire sorceress with deep violet skin, star-field black hair, glowing magenta eyes, batlike cosmic wings, sharp fangs, black-and-crimson armored bodysuit, and a crown of orbiting dark moons. Her draining power appears as red-black nebula tendrils reaching for World Guard's elemental aura. No nudity, no fetish styling, no sexualized posing.

## The World Power

The World Power represents all elements of the universe. It should appear as a layered elemental aura: fire ribbons, water spirals, wind arcs, stone fragments, lightning forks, ice crystals, vines, metal shards, starlight, gravity rings, and rainbow cosmic energy. It begins around World Guard, then visibly streams as a partial transfer into Woofer.
