# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow sword that looks like a colorful futuristic crystal sword and can wield Lucid Light, which appears as sparkling purple energy shining like a night sky full of stars. Jet's expressions move from curious wonder, to alarm, to focused creative responsibility, to regretful reflection.

## Insulation Annie

Insulation Annie is a wandering explorer hero created from Jet's Lucid Light. She is a sturdy adult woman with wind-chapped fair skin, bright gray-blue eyes, short copper hair tucked under a fur-lined teal explorer hood, and a practical arctic travel outfit: teal parka, cream scarf, brown gloves, utility belt, dark snow pants, and heavy boots. Her signature power is an expanding back shield made of pale cream insulation putty. When activated, the putty swells from her upper back into a huge soft barrier that can absorb attacks and seal openings. Annie should look brave, compassionate, and tragic, never monstrous.

## Igloo City People

The arctic city people wear layered seal-skin parkas, fur-trimmed hoods, mittens, snow boots, beadwork, and auralite crystal necklaces or trade pouches. They live in a clustered city of igloos beneath green and violet auroras. They barter with purple-gold auralite crystals, play seal-skin drums, laugh together, and gather as one large family. During danger, their faces show shock, fear, gratitude, and later reverence.

## Auralite Dinosaurs

The cave dinosaurs are large predatory dinosaurs with heavy natural armor plating that resembles jagged auralite crystal. Their armor is purple, amber, red, and gold, glowing faintly like valuable crystal veins. They have broad jaws, clawed feet, long tails, and geothermal steam rising around them. They should appear dangerous and animalistic, not intelligent villains. Their crystal-like armor lures miners and prey too close.

## Auralite

Auralite is a prized crystal currency in the city. It appears as faceted purple, gold, red, and amber crystals with magical-looking inner light. The people treat it as if it is literal magic because enough auralite can persuade almost anyone to do anything. It appears in market stalls, mining carts, trade pouches, cave walls, and dinosaur armor.

## Arctic City

The city is a dense cluster of igloos on blue-white snow under sweeping auroras. The people gather around seal-skin drums, carved bone tools, sleds, market blankets, auralite crystal trays, and glowing lanterns. The city should feel cold but joyful before the sinkhole opens, then warm and threatened when orange geothermal light rises from the broken ground.

## Geothermal Sinkhole

The sinkhole is a wide cracked opening in the middle of the igloo city. Orange geothermal light and steam rise from below. At first the people warm their hands around it like a communal fire. Later, the same heat allows the auralite-armored dinosaurs to enter the city. After Annie's sacrifice, the hole is sealed by her expanded insulation putty, forming a pale cream mound that blocks the warmth and keeps the dinosaurs away.
