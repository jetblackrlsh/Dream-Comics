After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet arrives alone on a beach and feels drained. He flies into space, consumes a lifeless system's star, becomes a flaming cosmic giant, and kicks the darkened star beyond the observable universe. There he rediscovers the homeless Minicubes, colorful jelly-cube beings with fuzzy antennae who survived the death of another reality. Jet compassionately creates a grassy Storyverse paradise for them, but their grateful attempt to assimilate him becomes dangerous. He gives them the star power instead, extinguishes the resulting flames with Lucid Light, then flees when the Minicube slime assimilates the entire planet and pursues him. A tiny Porta portal returns Jet to the beach, where a chef with a giant tortilla-chip head serves extraordinary shakes and spicy-honey meat. Jet finally satisfies his hunger and relaxes.
