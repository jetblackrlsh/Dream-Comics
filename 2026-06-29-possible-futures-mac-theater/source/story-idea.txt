Adapt the 06/29/2026 lucid dream journal entry into a complete Dream Comics short comic.

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in a conjunction of possible futures. He thinks through how intelligent humanoid robot companions might reshape family, work, and personal identity, especially the divide between companion-bots and worker-bots. The dream shifts into a surreal ocean of thick macaroni and cheese with a beach made of cracker crust, where Jet can swim without worrying about buoyancy and simply enjoy the impossible flavors. The dream shifts again into a theater world where Jet performs in a play, wins an award, impresses people with a futuristic watch he built, and then experiences a false awakening that makes the stage world feel real. A jealous co-star tries to poison his drink, but the trap gives itself away because it is alcohol and Jet never drinks alcohol. That recognition restores his lucidity, and he uses Lucid Light to call the authorities.
