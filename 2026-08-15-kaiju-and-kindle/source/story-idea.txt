# Story Idea - Kaiju & Kindle

Jet uses Lucid Light as a creator-observer to build a Storyverse world around Kaiju, a gentle orange-skinned shapeshifting giant, and Kindle, the rabbit-eared humanoid robot he created as his romantic and superhero partner. When the anti-technology nation Anti-Tekistan attacks San Francisco first with a nuclear weapon and then with analogizing nanite rifles, Kaiju's overwhelming strength saves the city but cannot safely stop the human army. Kindle completes the victory by outthinking the soldiers and remotely disabling every nanite without harming anyone.
