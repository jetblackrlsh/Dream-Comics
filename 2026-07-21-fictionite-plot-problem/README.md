# 07/21/2026 - The Fictionite Plot Problem

Dream Comics entry for July 21, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet enters a museum in an unfamiliar Storyverse city and discovers Fictionite, a crystal said to make fictional impossibilities possible. When a gang of advanced robotic bikers steals it, Jet refuses to play the expected hero because the thieves, the world, and Jet's own powers are already impossible. A purple-haired villain steals the crystal in turn, but Jet challenges her to demonstrate one genuinely new ability. His logic defeats the plot more thoroughly than a battle could, and a summoned birthday party turns one confused biker from an enemy into a cake-eating companion.
