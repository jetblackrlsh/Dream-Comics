# Character Bible - The Sanctuary Ladder

## Jet

- Adult Black man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Black hoodie over a vivid Dream Anime graphic T-shirt.
- Black athletic pants with a white side stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Large translucent blue augmented-reality glasses resembling oversized sunglasses.
- Smartphone mounted in a black wrist holder, connected to the glasses by a visible yellow cord.
- Curious and empathetic in calm scenes; wary and urgent during the pursuit; astonished when lucidity returns.

## Black werewolf

- Tall, powerfully built humanoid werewolf covered in deep black fur.
- Long black mane, pointed ears, glowing amber eyes, clawed hands and feet, long heavy tail.
- Dark fitted fantasy trousers and subtle black harness details; no armor that obscures the silhouette.
- Carries the rainbow crystal sword after summoning it.
- Quiet, observant posture in the sanctuary; careful and deliberate on the ladder.

## Fire elemental

- Tall humanoid body composed entirely of intertwined red, orange, and yellow flame.
- Athletic masculine silhouette with a bright yellow-white inner glow and red-orange outer fire.
- No ordinary clothing; facial features and hands remain readable through the flame.
- Clings to the sky ladder while shedding sparks, then stands as one of Jet's symbolic bodies.

## Rainbow crystal sword

- Large futuristic crystal blade made of clean faceted rainbow segments from red through violet.
- Dark ornate hilt with subtle metallic gold accents.
- Emits a bright multicolored glow and thin luminous trails.
- Must remain visually consistent and unmistakable on every page where it appears.

## Ponytailed woman

- Adult woman with long black hair tied in a high ponytail.
- Black-and-white horizontally striped long-sleeve shirt, black knee-length skirt, black socks, simple shoes.
- Wide hips and an evasive, alarmed posture.
- She retreats from Jet rather than joining the pursuing mob.

## Red-aura artists

- Diverse group of adult women dressed mostly in black practical artist clothing.
- Each carries an ordinary kitchen or serving utensil such as a whisk, spatula, spoon, tongs, or rolling pin.
- Surrounded by vivid red-orange flame-like auras.
- Their expressions shift from cold suspicion to shrieking pursuit.

## Baseball-cap man

- Light-skinned adult man in a green T-shirt, gray shorts, white baseball cap, and white sneakers.
- Calmly looks at his phone while the mob ignores him.

## Lone video watcher

- Adult woman with long straight black hair and dark clothing.
- Sits facing a television in an academy bedroom or viewing room.
- Seen mainly as a blue-lit silhouette; quiet and uncanny rather than overtly threatening.

## Living sanctuary

- Vast rounded organic enclosure like the inside of a compressed living sphere.
- Cold liquid glass floor in luminous saturated pink, moving in waves without carrying its occupant.
- Pale-blue cellular ceiling that pulses gently.
- Irregular glossy black rock formations protrude through the liquid floor.
- No visible artificial lamps; the space is self-luminous.

## Floating world and ladder

- Sanctuary exterior is an enormous smooth black flattened-sphere rock floating above a distant city.
- Other rocky islands hover among clouds.
- A long narrow ladder descends from the sanctuary, able to extend toward or disconnect from the city.
- The city is modern, dense, and impossibly far below.

## Academy and installation

- Modern academy interior with large windows, tables, students, screens, and a display case of colorful action figures.
- Installation consists of colorful pink, yellow, blue, and gray fibrous insulation slabs suspended on a maze of black metal bars.
- The beautiful color arrangement should still look physically irritating and unsafe on close inspection.

## Lucid Light

- Sparkling purple reality-warping energy, like a night sky filled with stars.
- Appears as controlled luminous threads and crystalline fractures, never as fire.
