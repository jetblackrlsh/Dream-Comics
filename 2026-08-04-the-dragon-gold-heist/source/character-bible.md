# Character Bible - The Dragon Gold Heist

## Jet

- Black man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Black hoodie over a colorful Dream Anime graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white side stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Heroic, playful, inventive, and calm under pressure. His face and clothing remain recognizable even when armored.
- Lucid Light armor is a sleek, translucent purple-black full-body energy shell filled with tiny stars. It may leave his head visible for recognition. On page 6 it forms two radiant jetpack-like wings.

## Tetheron

- Jet's energy tether: a flexible rope made of glittering golden light.
- It can be thrown as a lasso and creates a visible magical bond between connected beings or objects.
- It is never a physical chain and never harms or cuts the dragon.

## Leon

- Jet's ghostly UFO: a polished silver-blue flying saucer surrounded by an ethereal emerald-green glow.
- A transparent green dome and a wide circular underside emitter are signature shapes.
- Its tractor beam is a translucent green cone containing swirling spectral ribbons.

## The Dragon

- A colossal Western dragon, large enough to dominate the city skyline.
- Copper-crimson scales with brighter gold scale edges, charcoal swept-back horns, luminous gold eyes, a pale gold armored underbelly, and broad red-orange membranous wings.
- Its fire is orange-white at the core and red at the edges.
- Its stomach is an immense organic cavern containing rivers and pools of molten gold; the visual is surreal rather than anatomical or gory.
- Initially fierce and relentless, but calmed by Tetheron's magical connection. Keep the same colors, horns, eyes, and wing shape on every page.

## Savannah

- Pale-skinned cyborg woman with long black hair and forehead-covering bangs, glowing red eyes, and a fitted futuristic silver bodysuit with violet details.
- Appears briefly inside Leon during the chase.

## Tecton

- Green-skinned, muscular humanoid cat-ogre barbarian with feline ears and facial structure.
- Brown loincloth and silver baldric. Appears briefly inside Leon during the chase.

## Chipper

- Small levitating silver crab robot with a round body, tiny pincers, and blue-white lights.
- Appears briefly inside Leon during the chase.

## Storyverse

- A cosmic black-violet void filled with nebulae, sparkling stars, floating islands, and transparent bubble universes containing different worlds.

## Lettering and Layout

- Complete 4:5 portrait comic pages with clean white gutters and varied cinematic panels.
- All narrative and spoken text appears in unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes with crisp black lettering.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, subtitles, watermarks, logos, or issue numbers.
- Caption wording must match the page script exactly and remain readable at full-page viewing size.
