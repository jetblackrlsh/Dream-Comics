# Character Bible - The Glimmer Prison

## Jet

- African-American man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard and mustache, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Long dreadlocks spill from beneath a black hoodie worn over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt.
- Black athletic pants with one clean white stripe down each outer leg, white high-top sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build, compassionate expression, decisive posture, and the ability to fly under his own power.

## Jet fused with Silonis

- A heroic humanoid version of Jet formed from large, faceted, translucent rainbow crystals, retaining his beard silhouette, long dreadlock silhouette, warm expression, athletic proportions, and recognizable facial structure.
- The black hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, pants, shoes, and cross-body bag are translated into layered rainbow-crystal forms rather than disappearing.
- His chest and hands radiate brilliant prismatic life energy; Silonis is no longer held separately while the fusion lasts.

## Silonis

- Jet's Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life and one of the most powerful artifacts in the Storyverse.
- Futuristic sword made from large, faceted, translucent crystals spanning the full rainbow spectrum.
- Radiates prismatic life energy capable of healing, creating temporary living constructs, siphoning life energy, and restoring victims.

## The flying woman

- Light-complexioned woman with familiar bluish-green eyes and brown braided hair gathered into one long ponytail.
- Wears a soft-looking mustard-colored felt jacket with a tailored 1940s or 1950s silhouette, a cream blouse, dark brown skirt, and vintage shoes.
- Flies under her own power. When possessed, her eyes glow red and branching black veins spread across her face.

## The darkness

- Bodiless possessing spirit made from glossy black smoke, liquid tar, roots, tendrils, and branching veins threaded with crimson light.
- Its presence is identified by red eyes inside a host and darkness spreading across the host's face.
- After Jet's counterattack, it occupies a separate humanoid body made from dark-edged translucent rainbow crystal, with blazing red eyes and black corruption moving inside the facets.
- Its final bodiless soul is a compact black-and-crimson ghostly mass with grasping tendrils.

## Living buildings

- Modern city towers, apartments, offices, and storefronts animated by prismatic life energy.
- Windows resemble luminous eyes; entrances become mouths; walls bend into arms and legs while remaining recognizably architectural.
- Their movement is enormous and destructive but never gory.

## The Glimmer

- An alternate prison dimension for extremely powerful magical beings in ghostly forms.
- An endless dark-violet void filled with pale blue-violet glimmers, translucent prison currents, distant bodiless magical silhouettes, and no physical surfaces or objects to possess.

## Continuity rules

- Jet's skin tone, beard, long dreadlocks, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white shoes, and cross-body bag remain consistent before fusion.
- Crystal Jet must remain recognizably Jet and retain crystal translations of all key clothing silhouettes.
- Silonis is always a faceted rainbow-crystal sword, never metal.
- The woman retains the same braid, mustard vintage jacket, facial structure, and vintage clothing.
- The darkness's red eyes, black tendrils, and crimson veins remain visually consistent in every form.
- No gore, speech bubbles, thought balloons, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, creator signatures, or blank caption boxes.
