# Treatment

## Title

06/21/2026: Aegis Trophy Gallery

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet enters a mysterious castle alone, survives a fire-breathing mounted dragon head by turning its own flames back with Aegis, and uses the zombie lion magic meant to corrupt him as a source of heroic strength.

## Central Conflict

The castle trophy gallery is beautiful, unknown, and hostile. Its mounted mythical creature heads are not dead trophies but enchanted attackers. Jet's first threat is a dragon head that breathes fire from the wall, forcing him to use Aegis as both defense and counterattack. After the dragon defeats itself inside a golden turtle-shell dome, most trophies back down, but the zombie lion mounts regenerate full bodies and try to turn Jet into a mindless zombie. Jet has to resist corruption, transform the attack into strength, and clear the gallery without losing himself.

## Theme

Lucidity turns a threat's logic against itself. The dragon's fire becomes its own defeat, and the zombie lions' curse becomes lion strength in Jet's body.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins curious and alone, stepping into a castle with the tense excitement of lucid exploration. The mounted heads make the room feel like a museum that remembers being alive. The dragon's fire forces immediate focus, and Jet answers with Aegis instead of panic. His confidence rises when the dragon is trapped by its own flames, but the zombie lions create a more intimate danger by scratching him with corruption magic. For a moment the dream threatens to overwrite his mind, but the rainbow sword's healing power keeps Jet lucid. The attempted curse becomes power, and Jet's fear turns into joyfully physical confidence. By the end, the gallery is peaceful again, and Jet can enjoy the trophies despite never learning who created the castle.

## Ending

Jet defeats the last zombie lion with punches, sword slashes, and kicks that send the undead beasts through windows and into stone walls. The other mounted mythical creature heads remain still, having decided not to challenge him. Jet stands alone in the quiet gallery beneath moonlit broken windows, rainbow sword glowing gently and Aegis fading from his hand, amused that the mystery of the castle's owner matters less than the fact that he defended himself and had fun.

## Cast

- Jet: the lucid dreamer and protagonist. He is a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow futuristic crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. His Aegis spell appears as a glowing golden-orange shield shaped like a turtle shell, often with hexagonal shell plates and a curved protective dome.
- Mounted Dragon Head: an enchanted trophy dragon head fixed to the castle wall. It has ancient scales, curved horns, glowing ember eyes, smoke leaking from its jaws, and enough living magic to breathe fire.
- Mounted Mythical Creature Heads: other enchanted wall trophies, including griffin, hydra, serpent, unicorn, chimera, wolf, and bird-like heads. They are watchful, intelligent, and dangerous, but most choose not to attack after seeing the dragon defeated.
- Zombie Lion Mounts: undead lion heads mounted on the wall that regenerate complete zombie lion bodies. They have torn spectral manes, greenish corpse magic, bone-and-fur bodies, glowing hungry eyes, and claws that carry zombie magic. Their magic fails to consume Jet and instead grants him lion strength.
- Castle Trophy Gallery: a mysterious stone hall lined with mounted mythical heads, carved pillars, moonlit windows, old banners, trophy plaques with unreadable markings, and enchanted firelight. The owner and purpose remain unknown.
