# Character Bible - The Dynamancer Interruption

## Jet

- Adult Black man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears a black hoodie over a colorful Dream Anime graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Curious, compassionate, emotionally honest, imaginative, and resourceful.
- In lucid scenes, sparkling purple Lucid Light resembles a star-filled night sky and frames him as the creator-observer of the frog world.
- In academy scenes, he retains the same exact face, skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, clothing, sneakers, and bag.
- Keep every visible identifier consistent across all eight pages.

## Frog Dynamancers

- Humanoid frogs with expressive amphibian faces, large golden-green eyes, green skin in varied natural shades, webbed hands, and athletic upright bodies.
- Wear vivid modern fantasy-tech clothing that blends hooded streetwear, light armor, belts, and glowing conceptual sigils.
- Love Dynamancer: rose-and-magenta clothing accents and luminous heart-shaped energy.
- Knowledge Dynamancer: indigo-and-cyan clothing accents, a glowing open book, geometric diagrams, and connected symbols.
- Elemental Dynamancer: amber-and-blue clothing accents with intertwined water and light energy.
- Conceptual effects must look powerful and magical without readable decorative text.

## The former bully

- An unnamed adult man from Jet's school years. Protect his real-world identity by keeping his ordinary human face partially shadowed, turned away, or obscured by framing.
- In human form, wear simple neutral modern clothing and a guarded expression. Do not add a name, identifying uniform, logo, or invented personal details.
- His ordinary actions should remain clear: repairing the monitors, offering the release form, and reacting to Jet's refusal.

## Demonic symbiote form

- The former bully's body becomes large and muscular beneath wet red-and-blue tendrils.
- The tendrils resemble living slime and wrap around his arms, torso, and face while preserving a threatening humanoid silhouette.
- White eyes, sharp organic teeth, extended claws, and whipping tendrils make the transformation demonic and symbiote-like.
- Show no gore. The defeat through the window is forceful but non-graphic.

## Armored paramilitary guards

- Anonymous background figures in dark charcoal tactical armor, sealed helmets, and restrained red status lights.
- Escort the human bully into the dorm and then disappear before the attack.
- Keep them clearly distinct from superheroes or futuristic robots.

## Motorized zip-line toy

- A bizarre full-sized 1990s-style motorized toy with a red-and-blue plastic motor housing, metal handle, overhead cable, foot platform, chunky switches, and toy-like industrial design.
- Large enough for Jet to ride through the hallway.
- Keep the same red-and-blue device design on the cover and Page 7.

## Frog world

- A luminous wetland surrounding a massive futuristic data-center complex.
- Cooling channels carry swamp water among server towers, lily pads, pipes, and neon infrastructure.
- Humanoid frog Dynamancers manifest colorful conceptual powers beneath a cosmic Storyverse sky.
- The world appears on Pages 1-4 and returns unchanged on Page 8.

## Academy and dormitory

- Present-day stone-and-concrete academy with dorm rooms and long hallways.
- Packed with authentic-looking 1990s CRT monitors, VCRs, stereo systems, tangled cables, plastic toys, and retro electronics.
- Monitor power flickers during the setup. The hallway ends at a large high window that shatters during the climax.
- Avoid readable brand names and logos.

## Continuity rules

- All eight pages use vivid, high-saturation modern-anime comic art with crisp linework, luminous purple magic, expressive faces, cinematic depth, and clear action.
- Story pages use exactly three unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, signatures, gore, blank caption boxes, or decorative gibberish.
- Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from every outer edge.
- Jet's exact appearance and clothing never change.
- The frog world dominates Pages 2-4 and returns on Page 8.
- The ordinary former bully appears on Pages 5-6; his face remains intentionally unidentifiable.
- The red-and-blue demon appears on Pages 6-7.
- The red-and-blue motorized zip-line toy appears on Pages 1 and 7.
