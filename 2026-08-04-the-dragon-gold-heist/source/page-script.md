# Page Script - The Dragon Gold Heist

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, long enough to make every development obvious, and nonredundant. Jet, Leon, Tetheron, the dragon, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper, Lucid Light, and the Storyverse must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

One dramatic vertical composition above the futuristic city. Armored Jet flies beside the colossal copper-crimson dragon while a torrent of molten gold spirals upward toward Leon's emerald tractor beam. Glittering golden Tetheron curves from Jet toward the dragon's jaws, foreshadowing their bond. Storyverse bubble universes shimmer behind the skyline. Visible cover text only: "THE DRAGON GOLD HEIST" and "AUGUST 4, 2026." No issue number.

## Page 2 - Into the Fire

Six panels. Jet flies like a superhero between bright city towers. The dragon approaches head-on and breathes fire. Purple star-filled Lucid Light wraps Jet in armor. Instead of dodging or attacking, he accelerates through the flame. The final panel shows him diving into the dragon's enormous open mouth.

Caption text:

- "I began the dream flying through a city like a superhero."
- "A massive dragon appeared above the skyline and flew directly toward me."
- "When it opened its mouth, a furnace of fire filled my path."
- "I surrounded my body with a suit of starry purple Lucid Light armor."
- "Instead of fighting the dragon, I accelerated straight through the flames."
- "Protected by the armor, I flew through its open mouth and down its throat."

## Page 3 - The Hoard Within

Six panels. Jet descends through a surreal organic tunnel into an immense stomach cavern. Molten gold pours from ledges and fills a radiant lake around him. He hovers safely above it in Lucid Light armor while imagining a dragon eating treasure and a bear storing food. He grins at the heist idea. In the final panel, a purple signal shoots upward toward Leon above the city.

Caption text:

- "The dragon's stomach was an enormous cavern filled with molten gold."
- "I wondered whether dragons hoarded treasure because they actually ate it."
- "They could not spend gold at a market, so perhaps it was stored food."
- "Whatever the reason, my Lucid Light armor kept me safe above the liquid metal."
- "Surrounded by the dragon's fortune, I realized this could become an interesting heist."
- "I sent Leon a Lucid Light signal and told my ghostly green UFO to get into position."

## Page 4 - The Golden Eruption

Six panels. Jet flies upward through the throat, finds the uvula, and punches it like a training bag without causing visible injury. The dragon gags above the city. A spectacular non-gory flood of molten gold erupts from its mouth with armored Jet riding the wave. Leon opens a green tractor beam that catches Jet and every stream of gold.

Caption text:

- "I flew back up through the dragon's throat until I reached its uvula."
- "I punched it like a training bag - not to cause harm, but to make the dragon throw up."
- "The plan worked, and the dragon expelled me in a spectacular flood of molten gold."
- "Above the city, Leon opened his emerald tractor beam."
- "The beam pulled me safely toward the ship."
- "It also collected the liquid treasure, completing the strangest heist of my dreams."

## Page 5 - Pursuit Across Worlds

Six panels. Inside Leon, molten gold rests safely in a contained glowing cargo reservoir. The copper-crimson dragon chases the UFO out of the city and between Storyverse bubbles. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper study the pursuit through the viewport. Jet calmly explains his plan while the crew reacts with uncertainty. The last panel frames Jet's confident expression with the spoken line.

Caption text:

- "Inside Leon, we now carried a huge amount of gold stolen from a dragon."
- "Our escape seemed successful - until the furious dragon followed us into the Storyverse."
- "It crossed from world to world, so Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper knew we could not shake it."
- "While the crew searched for an escape, I told them I could bond with the dragon."
- "They did not understand how I could possibly tame the creature chasing us."
- "I smiled and said, 'Oh, ye of little faith. I got this.'"

## Page 6 - Tetheron's Bond

Six panels. Leon ejects Jet into the cosmic void. His Lucid Light armor unfolds radiant purple jetpack wings. He races at the oncoming dragon but veers past its teeth. Jet casts Tetheron as a golden lasso around the closed jaws. The final panels show warm gold magic linking Jet's heart, the rope, and the dragon's heart as its eyes soften.

Caption text:

- "I had Leon eject me from the ship and faced the dragon in open space."
- "My Lucid Light armor expanded into a pair of radiant jetpack wings."
- "This time I refused to let the dragon swallow me."
- "I threw Tetheron, my glittering golden energy rope, around its jaws like a lasso."
- "The tether was more than a muzzle: its true power was creating magical connections."
- "Holding one end, I formed a mystical bond with the dragon and tamed it without a fight."

## Page 7 - Dragon Rider

Six panels. Jet lands firmly between the dragon's shoulders. Tetheron loops gently around the jaws and glows without cutting the scales. Jet first holds the rope like reins. The dragon follows Leon past multiple bubble universes. Jet's posture changes from braced to confident as the dragon accepts the guidance.

Caption text:

- "I landed on the dragon's back and planted myself firmly between its shoulders."
- "At first, I held Tetheron like the reins someone would use to guide a horse."
- "The golden connection kept the dragon calm and responsive beneath me."
- "Leon flew ahead while my new mount followed through the Storyverse."
- "A chase that had crossed countless worlds had become a peaceful ride."
- "Then I realized Tetheron could make the journey even easier."

## Page 8 - Connected Through the Storyverse

Six panels with a triumphant vertical resolution. Jet throws Tetheron's free end toward Leon. Golden light clicks into the UFO's underside, linking ship, dragon, and rider. Jet releases the reins and relaxes across the dragon's shoulders while it automatically follows Leon. The final wide panel shows them traveling together among brilliant cosmic bubbles, with the contained stolen gold glowing inside Leon.

Caption text:

- "I tossed Tetheron's free end forward and connected it directly to Leon."
- "The same magic now linked my ghostly UFO, the dragon, and me."
- "With Leon guiding the route, the dragon would automatically follow."
- "I no longer needed to steer, so I released the reins and settled comfortably on its back."
- "The creature that had tried to burn and swallow me now carried me between worlds."
- "Relaxed above the Storyverse, I enjoyed the ride created by connection instead of combat."
