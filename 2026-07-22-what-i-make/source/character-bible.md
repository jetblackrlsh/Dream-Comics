# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: lucid dreamer, protagonist, creator, and narrator.
- Appearance: adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath his hood.
- Outfit: black hoodie worn open enough to reveal a “Dream Anime” graphic tee, black athletic pants with a single white side stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Signature tools: the Sword of Light, a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword; an energy tether resembling a rope of golden glittering light; sparkling purple Lucid Light that shines like a star-filled night sky.
- Posture and expressions: relaxed and observant in the jacuzzi; wide-eyed wonder in the aurora; calm defensive focus during the pursuit; soft, introspective warmth while drawing; open, joyous confidence while singing.
- Continuity rule: maintain the same skin, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, shirt, pants stripe, sneakers, and belt bag on every page. The hoodie and clothing may be wet after the jacuzzi but never redesigned.

## Sentient flying jacuzzi

- Role: Jet's friendly, attentive relaxation companion and transport.
- Appearance: a large rectangular-to-oval wood-paneled outdoor jacuzzi filled with steaming warm water. Rainbow RGB lights on its front form two friendly eyes and a smiling mouth, arranged somewhat like a benign jack-o'-lantern face without being frightening.
- Flight system: several compact metallic hover pads beneath the wooden tub emit soft cyan-white antigravity light.
- Personality through expression: the RGB face appears caring, eager to help, startled when shot, relieved after being healed, and content while listening to Jet's final song.
- Continuity rule: always show the warm wood slats, steaming water, friendly rainbow-light face, and underside hover pads when airborne.

## Steampunk cowboy

- Role: brief antagonist who insists on dragging Jet into an unexplained war.
- Appearance: lean adult man with a weathered face, curled brown mustache, long brown duster, brass-trimmed leather vest, red neckerchief, brown cowboy hat, gloves, boots, and goggles resting above his eyes.
- Weapon: long brass-and-wood steampunk musket that fires bright amber projectiles.
- Expression: relentless, hostile, and astonished when Jet takes control of the horse.

## Flying steampunk horse

- Appearance: horse-shaped analog machine built from dark iron plates, exposed brass gears, copper pipes, small steam vents, and articulated metal legs tucked into flight posture. Amber furnace light glows behind its rib-like side plates.
- Flight system: short mechanical wings and steam jets.
- Interface event: Jet's golden tether connects to its brass chest mechanism, making internal gears and self-destruct controls glow gold.

## Steampunk war train

- Appearance: enormous armored black-iron locomotive crossing the Arctic on elevated rails, covered in brass pipes, smokestacks, gears, red-orange furnace windows, rotating cannons, and armored cars.
- Role: environmental threat from an unexplained battle. Its insignia and combatants remain unreadable and unidentified.

## Purple felt notebook and magical pencil

- Notebook: handmade purple felt cover stitched from several soft patchwork pieces with visible colorful thread; thick cream pages.
- Pencil: ordinary pencil silhouette transformed by a glittering pink magical glow.
- Drawing: a leafy green plant stalk with no conventional blossom; a radiant star at the top; circular bands of coral, orange, gold, pink, and violet sunset color spreading outward; watercolor texture with delicate ink and pencil details.

## Living vine guitar

- Appearance: green vines, leaves, flowers, and flexible stems curl around Jet's forearms and torso to form the body, neck, strings, and tuning pegs of a playable guitar. It remains visibly alive and botanical, never becoming a wooden commercial instrument.
- Continuity rule: it grows directly from vines around Jet's arms and remains intertwined with him through the final page.
