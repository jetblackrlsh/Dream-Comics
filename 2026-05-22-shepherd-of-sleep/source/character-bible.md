# Character Bible

## Jet

- Identity: lucid dream explorer, man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Outfit: black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Signature gear: rainbow sword shaped like a colorful futuristic crystal sword, and a golden glittering energy tether.
- Silhouette: grounded explorer moving through cosmic scale, alert posture, one hand often raised as if testing dream physics.
- Expression range: focused lucidity, investigative curiosity, surprise, awe, reverent respect.

## The Shepherd of Sleep

- Identity: the true form of the rough idea Jet called Captain Dream; known in his land as the Shepherd of Sleep.
- Appearance: godlike adult man with shoulder-length silver hair, shimmering blue eyes, calm face, long flowing white robes, and a wooden staff.
- Signature prop: wooden staff used with the stance and precision of a seasoned cowboy's shotgun, firing crackling blue psychic energy.
- Powers: travels between dream worlds, communicates telepathically with dreamers, controls psychic energy.
- Body language: fearless, relaxed, protective, practiced, never flinching.
- Visual motif: blue psychic lightning, soft white cloud glow, sacred shepherd aura, calm eyes in the middle of danger.

## Cloud Sheep

- Appearance: fluffy sheep made of white and pale silver clouds, soft blue sleepy eyes, tiny vapor curls, drifting wool edges.
- Role: the flock people count to fall asleep; peaceful, vulnerable, and central to the Shepherd's purpose.
- Body language: drowsy calm at first, panicked during the wolf attack, then safely gathered after the battle.

## Nightmare Wolves

- Appearance: lean predatory wolves made of pink and black storm clouds, jagged lightning cracks, glowing eyes, vapor fangs, living-storm silhouettes.
- Role: nightmare attackers that threaten the cloud sheep and reveal the Shepherd's true courage.
- Body language: fast, hungry, electric, circling the flock, lunging through the pastures.

## Worlds

- Storyverse: cosmic black void filled with floating bubble universes and sparkling purple Lucid Light like a night sky full of stars.
- Cloud island: floating island made of clouds with pale gold sunlight, white cloud temples, cloud fences, and fenced pastures of cloud sheep.
- Battle atmosphere: serene dream heaven interrupted by pink-black lightning storms, then restored to calm blue-white light.
