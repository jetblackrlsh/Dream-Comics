# Treatment - The Infinite Marks

## Logline

When a realm-crossing tavern refuses to recognize Silonus as legitimate heroic equipment, Jet protects the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword, teaches it to copy the tavern's achievement grid, and reveals that defending the artifact has earned him an effectively infinite heroic legacy.

## Premise

Jet flies higher than usual, passes through the clouds, and discovers the Cloud Tavern in a realm between realms. Superheroes in capes mingle with armored adventurers, elves, magical warriors, and benevolent demons. Screens announce emergencies across countless worlds, while three enchanted doors control ordinary arrivals, mission departures, and returning heroes.

The tavern rewards completed missions with crown-shaped golden currency called Marks. Official weapons and equipment are covered by a glowing neon-green circuit grid that tracks successful strikes, defeated creatures, captured villains, and other forms of heroism. The recorded history determines each returning hero's payment.

## Central conflict and theme

A bartender dismisses Silonus because it lacks the official grid and reaches to take it, offering Jet a tavern-approved sword instead. Jet refuses and protects Silonus with a rainbow crystal force field. The immediate conflict is not simply about currency; it is about whether an institution's measuring system can recognize forms of heroism larger than the categories it was designed to count.

The theme is that true value does not begin when a system learns how to measure it. Silonus adapts the tavern's technology and imports Jet's prior accomplishments, but the extraordinary result comes from redefining what counts as protection. Keeping an artifact capable of reshaping the Storyverse away from evil is itself a multiversal act of defense.

## Tone and visual direction

A bright, high-saturation modern-anime superhero fantasy. The Cloud Tavern combines warm golden timber, floating cloud terraces, stained-glass portals, guild banners, magical weapons, and futuristic screens. Official equipment carries a vivid neon-green circuit lattice. Silonus remains a faceted rainbow crystal sword whose prismatic colors visibly merge with that circuitry. Crown-shaped Marks spill in brilliant gold against purple, cyan, green, and amber lighting.

## Story structure

Jet flies through the clouds and enters the Cloud Tavern. He explores its mixed heroic culture and studies the three doors. He learns how heroes answer alerts, return from missions, scan their registered gear, and receive Marks.

At the bar, an attendant notices Silonus and tries to take it because the sword is not official tavern equipment. Jet blocks the hand with a rainbow crystal force field and refuses the replacement. He then uses the red gem in Silonus's hilt to scan the glowing grid on the offered sword.

Silonus adapts. Neon-green circuitry spreads through its rainbow crystal blade, and the sword generates a backlog of Jet's earlier accomplishments. The scanners expect little or nothing from a newly upgraded weapon, but the result registers as effectively infinite.

Jet explains that protecting Silonus from evil does not save only one person or realm. Each successful defense potentially protects the entire Storyverse, and he has defended the sword almost constantly. The tavern's system translates that multiversal responsibility into a number too vast to contain.

## Ending and emotional arc

Jet begins as an unknown newcomer who does not understand the tavern's currency. By the end, the assembled heroes recognize that the sword they dismissed carries a history larger than their institution can measure. Jet does not need the tavern's replacement weapon or approval; Silonus adapts without surrendering its identity, and its impossible record makes his experience undeniable.
