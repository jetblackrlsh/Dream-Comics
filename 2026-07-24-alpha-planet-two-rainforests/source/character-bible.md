# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries a Rainbow Crystal Sword: a colorful futuristic crystal blade emitting healing prismatic light. His energy tether resembles a flexible rope made of golden glittering light clipped to his belt.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy shining like a night sky full of stars.
- Jet never wears a cape, mask, helmet, conventional superhero costume, or unrelated armor.
- Expression range: lucid calm, playful confidence, focused defense, firm resolve, cosmic wonder, peaceful rest, frustration at waking, renewed curiosity, transcendent awe, contemplative attention.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, face, beard, dreadlocks, outfit, bag, sword, and tether whenever he is in human form.

## Mouse-sized Jet

- The exact standard Jet design reduced to roughly the height of the purple tiger woman's palm.
- His body proportions, face, beard, long dreadlocks, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white sneakers, belt bag, sword, and tether remain unchanged.
- The vast corridor must communicate his scale through towering baseboards, huge floor seams, distant oversized doors, and the tiger woman's enormous claws.
- He returns to normal adult scale only after shrinking and dismissing the tiger mech on page 3.

## Lucid Light tiger mech

- A gigantic upright humanoid tiger-shaped mech constructed entirely from Jet's Lucid Light.
- Translucent violet armored plates filled with sparkling stars; broad tiger head, pointed ears, luminous white-violet eyes, darker cosmic stripes, huge mechanical paws, articulated claws, powerful shoulders, and a long segmented energy tail.
- A clear starry chest cockpit visibly contains mouse-sized Jet in his exact outfit, proving that the mech is built around him rather than transforming his body.
- It begins many times taller than the purple tiger woman. Jet then shrinks it to her height before dismissing the armor in violet starlight.
- It is mystical dream machinery, not a normal metallic robot and not the Alpha's true form.

## Purple tiger woman

- Adult humanoid purple tiger woman with athletic proportions, vivid violet fur, darker indigo tiger stripes, feline muzzle, pointed tiger ears, long flowing indigo-purple hair reaching below her shoulders, bright gold eyes, and long ivory claws.
- Wears a fitted dark-plum sleeveless warrior tunic, wrapped forearm guards, a black-violet waist sash, and simple dark boots that leave a feline silhouette.
- Initially gigantic relative to mouse-sized Jet. After Jet shrinks the mech and returns to normal adult scale, she stands only slightly taller than him.
- Expression range: predatory focus during the chase, startled reverence toward the mech, confusion, furious recognition, possessive determination toward the sword, frustrated rage behind the barrier.
- Continuity rule: her long hair, purple fur, dark stripes, gold eyes, claws, plum warrior clothing, and sash remain identical through pages 1-4.

## The Alpha

- The tiger pack's true leader: a cosmic purple tiger king whose dormant form is an entire spherical planet.
- When revealed, the planet's crust and clouds unfurl into a colossal upright humanoid tiger body with vast violet fur, midnight-purple stripes, blazing gold eyes, a thick regal mane, planet-scale claws, and continents and oceans still visible across his body.
- His head has a natural crown-like arrangement of golden horn-shaped fur crests, signaling kingship without a separate metal crown.
- He is larger than cities and mountains, with the opened planetary sphere becoming his torso and limbs. His scale must remain unmistakably astronomical.
- He never enters the wooden door or either rainforest. In the reconstructed dream, the planet remains spherical and does not transform.

## Rainbow Crystal Sword and barrier

- A colorful futuristic crystal sword with distinct red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet facets.
- Healing appears as clean rainbow light instantly closing Jet's claw wounds without scars or gore.
- The defensive barrier is a tall curved wall of radiant prismatic energy between Jet and the tiger woman. It is transparent enough to see her frustrated attacks but solid enough that her claws cannot pass.
- The sword stays with Jet through the space escape, rainforest exploration, and lava transformation.

## Ordinary door in space

- A completely ordinary freestanding brown wooden household door with a rectangular frame, subtle wood grain, and one plain round silver metal knob.
- It stands alone in the black cosmic void with no wall, glowing portal ring, magic runes, sci-fi mechanism, platform, or building around it.
- When open, its threshold shows the seasonal rainforest on the other side.

## Snowy blue rainforest

- Dense tropical rainforest with enormous trunks, tangled vines, ferns, broad leaves, and distinctive treetops whose canopies form smooth round clustered crowns.
- The entire atmosphere is tinted deep blue and cyan. Fresh white snow falls through tropical vegetation and rests on round canopies, leaves, roots, and the forest floor.
- The temperature appears wintry outdoors. The spatial layout must match the autumn version: same rounded trees, same winding path, same rock formations, same cave entrance, and same high aerial silhouette.
- There are no buildings, inhabitants, crew members, or ordinary evergreen forests.

## Autumn rainforest

- The exact same tropical landscape and rounded-canopy tree layout as the snowy blue rainforest, now in mild comfortable autumn.
- Leaves and canopy crowns glow in saturated orange, red, amber, and yellow. Fallen leaves blanket the ground beneath vines, ferns, and broad tropical plants.
- No snow remains, the blue winter tint is replaced by warm gold sunlight, and the cave entrance occupies the same location.
- It must still look unmistakably like a dense tropical rainforest, not a North American deciduous forest.

## Lava cave and river

- A deep volcanic cave beneath both versions of the rainforest, with the same black basalt walls, arched rock formations, ledges, and a bright orange-yellow river of lava flowing deeper underground.
- In the snowy version, faint blue cold wind reaches only the cave mouth while lava casts a warm amber refuge over Jet.
- In the autumn version, the same current becomes Jet's transformation path. The lava is powerful and luminous but causes him no pain, burns, smoke inhalation, or gore.

## Lava-form Jet

- Jet transformed into a powerful living embodiment of lava, fire, and molten stone while remaining unmistakably himself.
- His face, full beard, long dreadlock silhouette, warm features, hoodie-shaped torso contour, pants silhouette with side stripes, cross-body bag, Rainbow Crystal Sword, and golden tether remain identifiable.
- His body and clothing surfaces become dark obsidian-black volcanic stone crossed by brilliant orange-yellow molten fissures. His beard is dark ember-rock, his dreadlocks become flowing volcanic strands tipped with flame, and his eyes glow warm gold.
- A controlled corona of orange fire and drifting sparks surrounds him. He can fly and leaves a subtle molten-gold wake, but he has no wings, cape, crown, armor, extra limbs, or giant size.
- He never burns or damages the rainforest. His final mood is wonder and concentrated visual attention, not rage.

## Established crew continuity

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's Storyverse team. None appears physically in this comic because Jet experiences the dream alone. Do not insert them into the corridor, space, reflections, seasonal rainforests, cave, or cover.
