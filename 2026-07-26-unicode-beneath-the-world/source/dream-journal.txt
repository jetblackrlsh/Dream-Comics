07/26/2026

The Unicode Beneath the World

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse, a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025.

My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I. My Ghostly UFO, named Leon, resembles a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025. Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit does not have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025. Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds.

Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color, spiky slicked-back yellow hair, and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025. Lucid Light is a reality-warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered the power of Lucid Light in a dream on 07/19/2025. Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah is a pale-skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that cover her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She wields a futuristic purple-and-blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword. Her father Tecton is a green-skinned, humanoid, muscular cat-ogre barbarian who dresses in a brown loincloth and a silver baldric. My drone Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot. All three met me in early 2026 while I was adventuring through the Super Cathedral. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a “Dream Anime” graphic tee. I wear black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. I have warm brown eyes. I carry a rainbow sword that resembles a colorful futuristic crystal sword and an energy tether resembling a rope made of golden glittery light.

I lost lucidity almost immediately. I found myself attending a strange academy that was governed by a king. The king was also my father, and I served him with an obedience that seemed built into my role within the dream. He was married to a snooty Asian woman who carried herself like a queen, and the two of them regularly made requests of me. Even when I did not particularly want to help them, I usually did what they asked out of respect for my father and his authority as king.

One of my main responsibilities at the academy was completing a project for a class about flying machines. Our assignment was to construct a device capable of flight using magnetic coils. The instructors seemed like a blend of several familiar people, including Mr. Carter, an art teacher I had known a long time ago. He was a cool, dapper man with blue eyes and distinctive facial hair, including a curled mustache. He had such a relaxed personality that I could imagine him using a word like “groovy” completely unironically. The other instructors resembled a couple of famous people I regularly watched discussing artificial intelligence online, although their appearances were not exact representations of their real-life likenesses.

The classroom around me was extremely rowdy. Students shouted, talked over one another, and did things they clearly were not supposed to be doing. I did not allow their behavior to distract me. I remained intensely focused on building my flying machine.

Among the materials available to me was a rainbow-colored goo. During the dream, I may have interpreted it as some kind of soap or ordinary slime, but it strongly resembled Morphium, a magical substance I had discovered during earlier lucid-dream adventures. Morphium was a rainbow-colored material capable of being shaped into different creations, almost like a precursor to the Lucid Light I would later learn to manipulate. I combined the rainbow substance with spring-loaded magnetic coils, carefully assembling the different pieces in the hope that they would generate enough force to make the machine fly.

The academy may have contained another dream substance as well: Amnesium, a dangerous material that erased memories. Morphium and Amnesium were both substances I had encountered during my earlier lucid journeys, although Amnesium was particularly difficult to remember because forgetting it was part of its nature.

Despite the chaos around me, I came extremely close to completing my flying machine. I could sense that the underlying idea was sound, but the device never quite worked. Rather than becoming discouraged or dismantling it, I placed the unfinished machine inside my bag and moved on. It felt less like a permanent failure and more like a project I might return to later.

At some point, I performed another task for my father and his wife. I followed their instructions and gave them whatever they had requested, even though I did not feel especially enthusiastic about doing it. Once they received what they wanted, however, they showed almost no interest in me or in the effort I had made. They simply fell asleep. I was left without acknowledgment, feedback, or even a meaningful reaction. Their indifference disappointed me, especially after I had gone out of my way to fulfill their wishes.

When the school day finally ended, leaving the academy did not feel straightforward. It seemed as though I was not truly supposed to leave, forcing me to slip away through a back entrance and sneak past security. On the other side of the door, I encountered some of the rowdy students from my class. They were gathered around and socializing while waiting to be picked up, almost like students standing in a car-rider line.

I listened to fragments of their conversations, but I had no desire to join them. It was not that I opposed friendship or wanted to remain alone. I simply did not think I liked any of these particular people. I distanced myself from the group and began moving away.

As I separated myself from the students, my awareness changed.

This was when the dream became subtly lucid. I did not immediately experience the clear realization that I was asleep and dreaming, and I did not summon my usual sparkling purple Lucid Light. Instead, I developed a growing awareness that the world around me was artificial. Something about reality felt fundamentally wrong. The objects and people surrounding me no longer seemed to be made from ordinary matter.

Characters and symbols began appearing across my vision.

At first, the transformation was gradual. The environment remained partially visible, but strange lines of encoded information started bleeding through its surface. Then the entire scene shifted. The students, the pickup area, and the academy disappeared, leaving me seated before a terminal and staring at a screen filled entirely with Unicode characters.

I needed a moment to recognize what I was seeing. Once I did, I understood with absolute certainty that the code represented the world I had just inhabited. I was no longer merely standing inside that reality. I had reached the hidden controls beneath it.

The Unicode did not appear to be simple, static text. It seemed capable of describing motion, transformation, behavior, and change. The characters represented a living system—a world that could continuously update even though its foundation appeared to be written code. I could see that the academy, the king, the students, the magnetic coils, and perhaps even the Morphium had all emerged from whatever information was displayed before me.

I stared into the code, trying to decipher how it worked. I knew what it was, but I did not yet understand how each character corresponded to the world. I had achieved awareness of the dream's artificial nature and gained access to the source governing it, but I had not yet discovered how to edit that source.

I felt that I was standing at the threshold of a deeper form of lucidity. Rather than controlling the dream by summoning Lucid Light, I might be able to alter reality directly by rewriting its underlying code. I only needed enough time to understand the interface.

Before I could begin moving the characters or testing what would happen if I changed them, my alarm went off.

The terminal vanished, and I awoke with the sense that I had reached the foundation of the dream world—only to be pulled away moments before I could learn how to rewrite it.
