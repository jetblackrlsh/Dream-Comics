Lucid inside the unfamiliar body of a muscular half-white, half-Asian prisoner, Jet performs forced mining labor in an isolated rocky-desert prison while wearing orange jumpsuit pants and a metallic exoskeleton harness. The machine lets him shatter ore-bearing rocks with his fists, but it also makes escape seem possible. Jet reasons that the unknown prisoner must have earned extraordinary trust, then refuses to remain trapped by a life he did not choose. He runs toward a distant evergreen forest. The warden shoots him, revealing that the exoskeleton grants strength and durability but not bulletproof invulnerability. As he dies, Jet summons the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life through his chest. The sword splits his departing soul from the body and pulls it back inside, resurrecting him. The frightened warden activates a prison turret, but Jet creates a shimmering prismatic crystal shield that deflects its bullets. Combining the Life Sword's power with the exoskeleton's strength, he launches himself beyond the turret's reach and disappears into the pine forest, free.
