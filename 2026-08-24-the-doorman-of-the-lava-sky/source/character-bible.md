# Character Bible - The Doorman of the Lava Sky

## Jet, the amnesiac Doorman

- Adult Black man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Wears a black hoodie over a colorful Dream Anime graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Intelligent, curious, skeptical, compassionate, and calmly resourceful even when surrounded by gods.
- Sparkling purple Lucid Light resembles a star-filled night sky and forms solid controls, energy, and spell effects around his hands.
- Porta appears as paired circular portals rimmed with sparkling purple Lucid Light. Anything crossing them remains intact and connected across space.
- Keep his face, skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, shirt, pants, sneakers, and belt bag consistent on every page.

## Autumn

- Adult woman with medium-length curly brown hair, warm fair-to-olive skin, and elegant heavy makeup.
- Wears a fitted reddish-brown Victorian dress covered in clear golden leaf patterns, with long sleeves and a full skirt.
- Carries herself like Jet's familiar, mildly exasperated wife even though he does not remember her.
- Uses golden autumnal magic to summon Apollo.
- Keep her hair, makeup, reddish-brown dress, golden leaf pattern, and poised posture consistent.

## Apollo

- Radiant adult Greek solar god with sun-bronzed skin, long flowing golden hair, a luminous golden laurel crown, and white-and-gold classical armor over a flowing white tunic.
- Travels in an ornate golden chariot drawn by two brilliant white horses.
- Surrounded by warm sun rays and circular golden light.
- Authoritative and magnificent, but his desire to become the world's supreme god remains morally ambiguous.

## The god of blades

- Towering divine warrior made from polished obsidian-black bones and layered blade-shaped armor.
- Crown resembles long black bone knives; glowing amber eyes; carries a long black-bone sword.
- Controls animated black-bone dinosaur skeletons and inhabits castles constructed from the same blade-like material.
- Suspicious and intimidating but openly opportunistic once Meltronome's defeat can improve his divine rank.
- His exact name is uncertain and should not appear as readable text in the comic.

## The god of deals

### Gray businessman form

- Enormous muscular gray-skinned man with a broad bald head, heavy brow, dark eyes, and an immaculate charcoal business suit with a black tie.
- Calm, calculating, and cordial only while arranging a useful bargain.
- His magic appears as luminous gold contractual sigils, linked rings, and streams of transferred energy.

### Lightning-rhinoceros form

- The same massive body transformed into a gray humanoid rhinoceros with one large horn, electric-blue eyes, and an extremely muscular build.
- Torn remnants of the charcoal suit and black tie remain recognizable.
- Blue-white lightning crackles across his shoulders, arms, and horn.
- After Porta separates him spatially, show no blood or injury: his upper body ends cleanly inside one glowing portal while both intact legs emerge through a second portal in the desert.

## Meltronome

- Colossal humanoid god composed of molten lava, black volcanic crust, and glowing orange-red cracks.
- His vast face and upper body form within the lava-lamp sky surrounding the planet.
- Appears ancient, oppressive, and large enough to make the whole sky feel like his body.
- When defeated, his form dissolves and is cast beyond the breaking molten clouds without gore.

## Black-bone dinosaur

- Gigantic animated tyrannosaur skeleton made entirely from glossy obsidian-black bones.
- Empty rib cage and skull contain no flesh or organs.
- Purple Lucid Light controls appear inside the rib cage when Jet pilots it as a mech.
- Keep the skull, rib cage, tail, and black-bone material visually consistent.

## Dimensional elevator

- Freestanding antique brass elevator cab standing alone in the desert with no building or shaft.
- Open doors reveal warm amber interior light and subtle impossible cosmic depth.
- Serves as the visual symbol of Jet's forgotten life as the Doorman.

## The world

- Rocky tan desert with scattered sand, black stones, and colossal Gothic castles built from polished black bones.
- The initial sky resembles a huge lava lamp: flowing red, orange, magenta, violet, and gold molten blobs completely block the sun.
- After Meltronome's defeat, the molten sky tears open to reveal clean blue atmosphere and brilliant golden sunlight.
- The magical academy has towering stone halls, arched windows, crowded students, and a central chamber used for divine bargains.

## Continuity rules

- All eight pages use bright, high-saturation modern-anime comic art with crisp linework, luminous purple magic, electric-blue lightning, golden sunlight, expressive faces, and cinematic depth.
- Story pages use unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes only.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, signatures, gore, blank caption boxes, or decorative gibberish.
- Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from every outer edge.
- Autumn appears on pages 1-3 and may appear only as a faint memory motif on page 8.
- The god of blades and black-bone dinosaur dominate page 4.
- The god of deals begins in gray businessman form on page 5 and remains in lightning-rhinoceros form on page 6.
- Jet receives unlimited gold-white divine energy on page 5; that energy mixes visibly with his existing sparkling purple Lucid Light on pages 6-8.
- The lava sky remains intact through page 6, breaks apart on page 7, and is completely replaced by sunlight on page 8.
