# Treatment - The Glimmer Prison

## Logline

When the recurring darkness tries to possess Jet after he fuses with Silonis, he transforms the invasion into a trap—but must stop the entity's newly animated buildings before it replaces human life with living architecture.

## Premise

Jet encounters a flying woman dressed as if she stepped out of the mid-twentieth century. Her braided brown hair, mustard felt-like jacket, and familiar bluish-green eyes draw him closer. When Jet says he recognizes those eyes, they turn red and black veins spread across her face. The darkness pursuing Silonis has found another host.

Jet fuses the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life into himself, becoming a faceted humanoid rainbow-crystal being. The darkness believes he has made possession easier and leaps into him. Jet anticipated the attack. He fires life energy from within, ejecting the spirit and wrapping that energy around it as a separate rainbow-crystal body.

## Central conflict and theme

Jet chooses understanding before destruction. He gives his enemy enough freedom to reveal its values, then judges it by what it chooses to do. The darkness sees people as disposable shells and buildings as the lives that deserve liberation. Its twisted reversal of hierarchy turns compassion for created things into contempt for human beings.

## Escalation

The darkness animates towers, apartments, and offices throughout the city. Living buildings tear themselves loose, bend like giants, and attack fleeing people. Jet realizes that the entity treats human bodies as houses it may occupy, so it wants buildings to become the dominant living beings while humanity becomes expendable.

## Ending and emotional arc

Jet siphons the life energy from the darkness's construct until only its bodiless soul remains. Through the Porta spell, he opens a portal into the Glimmer, a ghostly prison dimension containing no physical bodies for the possessor to inhabit. He then drains life from the rampaging buildings, heals their victims, and restores the city. Though the rainbow-crystal body may remain a dangerous loose thread, Jet leaves with a new lesson: fusion with Silonis can turn possession itself into a weapon against the possessor.
