THE FICTIONITE PLOT PROBLEM

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in an unfamiliar Storyverse city. Inside a museum, he finds Fictionite, a crystal supposedly able to make fictional impossibilities possible. Advanced robotic bikers crash through the wall and steal it, but Jet refuses to stop them. He explains that they and their world already depend on fictional impossibility, so the crystal has no clear additional function. A flamboyant purple-haired woman steals the crystal from the bikers and claims she will reveal its true power, but she cannot name or demonstrate anything new that it enables. Jet proves his point by freely summoning balloons, two chocolate ice cream cakes, and a crowd for a birthday party. The woman panics and flees, while Jet befriends the robotic biker and enjoys the cake.

The story is a lucid superhero comedy about a hero defeating a bad plot by asking what its magical object actually changes.
