# Treatment

## Logline

In a Storyverse desert, Jet faces a pack of wolves corrupted by dark Lucid Light crystals, loses the Sword of Life to their werewolf cowboy leader, and uses the Lucid Light Watch to chain Savannah's purification, Skelebot's speed, and Chipper's agility into a clean recovery.

## Central Conflict

Jet begins confident because the Sword of Life can heal the damage from corrupted animals, but the werewolf cowboy steals it and turns that confidence into danger. The real problem is no longer surviving the wolves; it is stopping the werewolf from escaping with one of the Storyverse's most powerful healing weapons.

## Theme

The right tool matters more than raw power. Jet survives because he does not cling to one weapon or one form; he reads the problem, switches roles, and uses each Lucid Light Variant for the exact job it can do best.

## Ending

LLV Chipper dives under the animated sand steed, cuts away its legs, and collapses it into ordinary desert sand. The werewolf crashes, drops the Sword of Life, and falls unconscious. Jet returns to his normal form, reclaims the Sword of Life, and stands over the quiet desert as the purified wolves scatter safely into the dawn.

## Emotional Arc

Jet starts alone but unafraid, trusting the Sword of Life and the previous day's victory over a corrupted dog. The theft of the sword forces a brief panic and physical vulnerability. His focus returns when he treats the Lucid Light Watch as a tactical crew roster, ending the issue controlled, decisive, and more confident in the watch's place in his solo adventures.
