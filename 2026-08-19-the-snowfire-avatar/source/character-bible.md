# Character Bible - The Snowfire Avatar

## Jet

- Black adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears a black hoodie over a colorful “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each outer leg, clean white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build; relaxed and thoughtful in the sanctuary, curious during the experiment, startled during the attack, then exhilarated and purposeful during the laser chain reaction.
- Keep his skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, pants stripes, shoes, and cross-body bag visually consistent on every page.

## Robotic dream-avatar layer

- Beginning after the demon bite on page 5, the left side of Jet's face has a clean, non-gory opening in the human exterior that reveals sleek silver-black robotic plating beneath.
- The exposed robotic face preserves Jet's recognizable facial structure, beard outline, dreadlocks, and identity.
- One mechanical eye contains bright blue-violet diodes that can charge and project a narrow prismatic laser.
- No blood, bone, exposed muscle, skull imagery, or horror framing.
- The exposed robotic layer remains visible and consistent on pages 6-8.

## Snowy sanctuary

- A magical winter forest at night with deep blue snow, dark evergreen trees, and continuous snowfall.
- A plush royal-purple couch and a circular stone fire pit create a wall-less living room in the snow.
- The fire pit burns with intense orange-gold magical flame and a subtle circular protective glow.
- The couch, pit, and surrounding forest retain the same shapes and colors whenever the dream returns to the sanctuary.

## Bonded flame

- Bright orange-gold magical fire with violet sparks at its edges.
- It does not burn Jet and can rest safely in his bare hand.
- Its warmth creates a visible amber protective aura around Jet when he walks into the deadly cold.

## Fire demons

- Muscular humanoid figures made from black volcanic stone, glowing red-orange cracks, and roaring head-and-shoulder flames.
- Menacing but not gory; sharp ember eyes and expressive monstrous faces.
- In the snowy wilderness they are encased in thick translucent cobalt-blue ice, frozen in dynamic running poses.
- When one face thaws, only its head flame and jaw regain motion while its body remains trapped.
- During the finale, their ice shells act as prisms and burst into non-gory shards, sparks, and colorful magical flame.

## Hellscape

- The same spatial arrangement as the snowy sanctuary, but transformed into cracked black ground, red sky, lava light, distant flames, and charging fire demons.
- The purple couch and fire pit remain unchanged, making the transformation unmistakable.
- No readable decorative text.

## Laser refraction and finale

- Jet's eye laser begins as a concentrated blue-violet beam and splits into saturated rainbow rays inside the ice.
- Refracted beams cascade from one frozen demon to another like light crossing crystal prisms.
- Explosions combine ice crystals, orange flame, magenta and cyan light, and rainbow streaks.
- The final effect feels celebratory and spectacular rather than violent or horrific.

## Continuity rules

- All eight pages use bright, high-saturation modern-anime comic art with crisp linework and cinematic lighting.
- Story pages use unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes only.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, signatures, gore, extra limbs, duplicate unintended characters, or blank caption boxes.
- Keep all captions and complete caption-box borders inside a generous 6% safe margin from every outer edge.
