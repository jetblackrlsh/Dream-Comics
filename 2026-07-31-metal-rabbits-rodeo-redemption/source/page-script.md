# Page Script - The Metal Rabbit's Rodeo Redemption

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, long enough to make every development obvious, and nonredundant. Jet, Silonis, the Metal Rabbit, every summoned creature, and all recurring settings must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

One dramatic vertical composition in the purple-fogged black void. Jet stands calmly in the lower foreground with sparkling purple Lucid Light around one hand and Silonis blazing in the other. Behind him towers the grimy orange-metal skeletal rabbit with glowing red eyes, long ears, and two buck teeth. Zombie clowns with chainsaws, the many-armed black spider, and a surging mound of tiny mole men emerge through the fog as secondary threats. In the upper background, a gigantic orange-gold metal carrot splits the darkness with hopeful light, hinting at the nonviolent resolution. Visible cover text only: “THE METAL RABBIT'S RODEO REDEMPTION” and “JULY 31, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - A Monster Looking for Fear

Six panels. Jet appears in the empty black void and studies the strange Metal Rabbit with fascination. It approaches and asks whether he is afraid. Jet calmly answers that he is lucid and in control. The rabbit raises both arms as purple-black fog floods the void and swallows the horizon. The last panel contrasts the rabbit's threatening pose with Jet's unimpressed expression.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and immediately entered an empty, dark void.”
- “There I met a grimy skeletal rabbit made of orange metal, with glowing red eyes and two enormous buck teeth.”
- “Its design fascinated me far more than it frightened me.”
- “The rabbit approached and asked whether I was afraid of it. I told it that I was not.”
- “I knew that I was lucid and in control, while its entire goal was simply to scare me.”
- “It raised its arms and filled the darkness with purple fog, hiding whatever might exist beyond my sight.”

## Page 3 - Clowns, Chainsaws, and the Sword of Life

Six panels. Zombie clowns claw out of the black ground. When Jet remains calm, they reach inside their own rib cages and pull out chainsaws. They surround Jet and strike, but he summons Silonis. Rainbow-white life energy immediately repairs every stylized injury. The rabbit watches in frustrated disbelief while Jet stands restored and confident amid useless chainsaw attacks.

Caption text:

- “When the fog failed, the rabbit summoned decayed skeletal clowns from beneath the ground.”
- “I had never found clowns especially frightening, so the rabbit armed them with chainsaws pulled from their own rib cages.”
- “I summoned Silonis, my living rainbow crystal Sword of Life.”
- “Instead of retreating, I let the clowns strike me so the rabbit could see that physical attacks were useless.”
- “Every injury vanished beneath the rainbow life energy radiating from Silonis.”
- “The rabbit's nightmare creatures could hurt me for an instant, but the Sword of Life would always make me whole.”

## Page 4 - The Sword Steals Itself Back

Six panels. A black six-armed humanoid spider swings through the fog and catches Silonis with one adhesive palm. The Metal Rabbit cackles while the clowns close around an unarmed Jet. Jet calmly explains through narration that Silonis is alive. The sword twists the spider through the air and slices off the trapped hand in one clean, non-gory beat. Silonis flies back to Jet with the detached black hand still stuck to its blade. Jet creates a transparent faceted rainbow-crystal dome around himself.

Caption text:

- “The rabbit changed tactics and summoned a black, many-armed spider that swung past me at tremendous speed.”
- “One adhesive palm caught Silonis and held the sword fast while the rabbit cackled and the clowns closed in.”
- “Stealing Silonis did not make me helpless. The Sword of Life possessed a living will and could fly without me.”
- “When the spider could not release it, Silonis twisted through the air and sliced away the trapped hand.”
- “The sword returned to my grasp, carrying the harmless black hand still stuck to its blade.”
- “I sealed myself inside a nearly indestructible dome of rainbow crystal so nothing else could reach me.”

## Page 5 - The Weakness Beneath My Feet

Six panels. The rainbow dome gleams above and around Jet, but a small mole man bursts through the open ground inside it. More follow until nearly one hundred swarm over Jet and pile him down. Jet is surprised for the first time as their combined weight threatens to crush him. He drops the dome, launches upward with sparkling purple Lucid Light, and releases a huge circular shock wave. The mole men, clowns, and spider are thrown safely away while the rabbit remains standing.

Caption text:

- “The rabbit noticed the one weakness in my dome: the barrier did not extend beneath my feet.”
- “A puppy-sized mole man erupted inside it, followed by another, and then nearly one hundred more.”
- “They were weak alone, but together they covered my body and threatened to crush me beneath their weight.”
- “For the first time, one of the rabbit's attacks genuinely surprised me.”
- “I dismissed the dome, carried the swarm high into the air, and released a powerful shock wave of Lucid Light.”
- “Sparkling purple force scattered every summoned creature, yet the Metal Rabbit still stood laughing in the void.”

## Page 6 - I Did Not Need to Escape

Five panels. The rabbit's red eyes intensify and it declares that nothing Jet does will let him escape. Its orange-metal body grows until it towers like a giant over Jet. Jet looks up without fear and answers that he does not need to escape. He aims sparkling purple Lucid Light into the distance. The final tall panel reveals that the energy has forged a gigantic orange-gold metal carrot with faceted green-metal leaves.

Caption text:

- “The rabbit's red eyes blazed brighter as it declared that nothing I did would ever let me escape.”
- “Its grimy orange-metal body expanded until the creature towered over me as a giant.”
- “I looked up at it and answered, ‘I don't need to escape.’”
- “Instead of attacking, I fired my Lucid Light into the distance and used it to create something entirely new.”
- “A gigantic metal carrot rose from the darkness, shining like an impossible gift.”

## Page 7 - A Better Calling

Six panels. The giant rabbit's threatening posture vanishes. It rushes to the carrot, leans close in astonishment, and gratefully asks whether it is for him. Jet confirms that it is. The rabbit cannot understand why Jet would give a gift after everything it did. Jet gently admits that its frightening career has not gone well and suggests becoming a rodeo cowboy instead. The final panel shows the rabbit's red eyes wide with sudden hope as a luminous imagined rodeo arena and bucking mechanical bull appear behind it as a possibility, not a literal new setting.

Caption text:

- “The giant rabbit's entire disposition changed the moment it saw the metal carrot.”
- “Its menace vanished. It rushed to the gift and asked with astonished gratitude, ‘Is this for me?’”
- “I told it that the carrot was truly meant for it.”
- “The rabbit could not understand my kindness after spending the entire encounter trying to scare me.”
- “I admitted that frightening people was not working and suggested a better path: become a rodeo cowboy.”
- “Beneath the grime, metal bones, and red eyes, I sensed a strange southern charm—and the rabbit suddenly looked hopeful.”

## Page 8 - Howdy, Partner

Six panels with a warm luminous resolution. Jet and the still-giant rabbit face one another beside the shining carrot. The rabbit asks whether Jet truly believes it has what it takes; Jet confidently says yes. A hopeful inset shows the rabbit imagining itself mastering a rodeo while retaining its exact orange-metal form. Jet and the rabbit exchange a cheerful “Howdy” through rectangular caption narration, not speech bubbles. Jet rises into the air and leaves the rabbit waving beside its carrot. The final wide panel shows the dark void opening into the brilliant Storyverse as Jet flies toward countless colorful bubble universes.

Caption text:

- “The rabbit asked whether I truly believed it had what it took to become a rodeo cowboy.”
- “I told it that I did. Its strange southern charm suited that future far better than a life spent manufacturing fear.”
- “When it was time to part, both of us naturally slipped into slight southern accents.”
- “We exchanged a cheerful ‘Howdy,’ and the rabbit finally allowed me to leave.”
- “The dark void became less oppressive as I flew farther away from my former adversary.”
- “I emerged into the bright Storyverse and continued toward its distant bubble universes, leaving a hopeful new cowboy beside his metal carrot.”
