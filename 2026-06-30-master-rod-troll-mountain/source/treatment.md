# Treatment

## Logline

After sleep paralysis opens a lucid gate, Jet enters a reverse-water troll world where a divine lightning staff seems like the answer to his climate grief, but claiming it forces him to outthink a selfish speedster and overload an energy-vampire ogre before accepting that no dream relic can replace waking responsibility.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants a miracle powerful enough to save his waking world from climate doom. The trolls' Master Rod appears to offer that hope, but the quest is complicated by a purple-lightning speedster who wants the staff for himself and an energy-vampire ogre drawn by Jet's elemental bike. The real conflict becomes whether Jet can use lucid power responsibly without mistaking a mythic object for an easy answer.

## Theme

Lucid power can solve dream dangers, but grief for the waking world demands honesty instead of false certainty.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alone and heavy with climate anxiety in a beautiful world that seems kinder than his own. The trolls' faith in the Master Rod gives him hope, and the race up the mountain turns that hope into focused action. When the energy-vampire ogre attacks, Jet's fear becomes tactical clarity: he recognizes what the monster really wants and uses the staff to protect everyone. After the victory, he gives the staff away because he understands that its promise was never simple salvation. The dream's later world-shifts return him to wonder and urgency, but the final wake-up leaves the emotional truth intact.

## Ending

Jet launches the overloaded energy-vampire ogre through a Porta portal so it can explode harmlessly far away, then hands the Master Rod to the confused speedster. The final page shows the dream fragmenting through a virtual anime battle world and a wrestler's downhill dinosaur rescue before Jet snaps back to the troll meadow and wakes at 4:30 AM, carrying neither the staff nor an easy answer, but still carrying lucid resolve.
