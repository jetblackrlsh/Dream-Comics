# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. He can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he is inspired, curious, briefly hypnotized, then proud of the new protector he helped create.

## Johnson

Orange humanoid goldfish with expressive fish features, bright orange scales, large friendly eyes, and a blue astronaut suit with no helmet. He is a robotics engineer aboard Leon and works with confident inventor energy.

Continuity notes: Keep Johnson clearly fish-like and humanoid, with the same blue astronaut suit and no helmet. In lab scenes, give him tools, holographic readouts, and robotic parts. During hypnosis, his eyes should glaze slightly while his body remains recognizable.

## Superhero Mandroid

Humanoid robot superhero resembling a human man with white skin, slicked-back brown hair, determined heroic face, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow chest insignia with the initials "SHM", and oversized mechanical green fists. He can summon a rechargeable translucent holo shield and fire rapid regenerating rocket punches from his mechanical fists.

Continuity notes: Keep the red unitard, yellow cape, "SHM" insignia, slicked-back brown hair, and large green mechanical fists identical on every page. Mandroid is heroic, upright, and focused. His immunity to mind control is shown through clear eyes and steady posture when Jet and Johnson falter.

## Cyclops Witch

Light purple-skinned witch with one singular glowing red cyclops eye, shoulder-length white hair, sharp face, dark purple outfit, and a glowing blue crystal amulet at her chest. Her hypnotic whisper magic radiates as thin blue-violet sound spirals from her mouth and amulet.

Continuity notes: The blue crystal amulet is the visible source of her power and must be obvious before it is destroyed. Keep her eerie and theatrical, not grotesque. After the amulet breaks, she looks startled and defeated.

## Leon

Ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow and translucent spectral hull. Leon is both a crew member and a vessel containing a robotics lab.

Continuity notes: Interior scenes aboard Leon should include curved ghost-green walls, floating consoles, cosmic windows, and a high-tech robotics bay.

## Astral Thread

A rare spool of luminous silver-blue thread with tiny star sparks along the fibers. It can sew shut nightmare portals and becomes a key component installed inside Superhero Mandroid.

Continuity notes: Show it as a physical spool or thread reel before installation, then as glowing thread lines emerging from Mandroid's wrist or fist when he seals portals.

## Nightmare Portals

Large purple portals with jagged edges, glowing violet interiors, and shadowy nightmare shapes pushing through. When sealed by astral thread, the portal edges pull together like fabric stitched shut.

Continuity notes: Portals should look connected to the mixed reality game inspiration but belong in the Storyverse as dream threats.

## Storyverse

A cosmic void filled with glowing bubble universes, each containing possible story ideas. The environment around Leon and Mandroid's mission includes nebula skies, floating universes, and luminous paths.

Continuity notes: Keep cosmic bubble universes visible whenever the story leaves Leon's robotics lab. Lucid Light should appear as sparkling purple energy shining like a night sky full of stars.
