# Treatment

## Logline

After sleep paralysis brings Jet lucidly into Mall World, a persistent elven sorceress leads him to a futuristic workshop where her hidden robot nature turns an annoying encounter into a creative upgrade project, until an energy vampire ogre crashes in to steal the elemental power.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants a workshop and solitude for invention, but the sorceress will not stop following him. Once she helps him reach the right lab, the conflict changes: Jet must protect the elemental power he has installed in her before the energy vampire ogre can absorb it and dominate reality.

## Theme

Lucid creation turns irritation into collaboration. The dream's joy comes from redirecting a problem into a project and then using invention as the answer to danger.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins focused and impatient, wandering Mall World alone with one clear desire: find a workshop. The sorceress frustrates him until she reveals a practical route out of Mall World. In the lab, suspicion becomes delight when Jet realizes she is a robot he can improve. The ogre's attack turns invention into crisis, but Jet stays lucid, tactical, and creative. By the end, the explosion is far away, the planet is safe, and Jet returns to tinkering with genuine happiness.

## Ending

Jet opens a Porta portal beneath the overloaded ogre, launching him into space before the elemental explosion can destroy the workshop. The distant cosmic blast shakes the planet, but Jet and the upgraded sorceress survive. The final image resolves on Jet smiling at the workbench, still modifying her design after the chaos.
