Jet arrives alone in Mall World, searching for a workshop where he can tinker through the night. An obsessive elven sorceress follows him until she proves useful by fusing a flock of eagles into a giant eagle-shaped jet that carries them to a futuristic workshop. There Jet realizes the sorceress is actually a robot and joyfully upgrades her with talon-shaped grapple blades and elemental cores. The new power attracts an energy vampire ogre who tries to absorb every element and rule reality. Jet blocks him with Lucid Light, coordinates a charged elemental strike, then uses the Porta spell to launch the overloaded ogre into space before returning to happy invention.
