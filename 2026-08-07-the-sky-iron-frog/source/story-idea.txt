The Sky-Iron Frog

Jet visits a magical treehouse village populated by colorful fairies with different powers. A giant humanoid frog returns in anti-magic sky-iron samurai armor, making the fairies' spells useless. When Jet's Geomancy can move the armor but cannot pull it free from the frog's adhesive mucus, he realizes the power may actually be Geokinesis—a psychic ability. He orders magical fairies to retreat and psychic fairies to advance. Their fire, ice, and lightning bypass the armor, save the village, and inspire Jet to question whether every dream power is ultimately psychic because dreams themselves exist within the mind.
