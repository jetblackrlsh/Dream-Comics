# Treatment - The Rooted Heart

## Logline

When Jet discovers a secret hero whose balloon-tipped hair can uproot an entire city, the alarming demonstration draws official suspicion—but the hero proves that their strangest power is most heroic when used to delight children and perform impossible life-saving transplants.

## Premise

While flying through a city, Jet notices an ordinary-looking stranger standing on a rooftop. A sudden dream download tells him that this person possesses a hidden power. The stranger is startled that Jet can see through their secret identity but admits that they are a superhero.

The hero grows long hairs from their body and forms colorful balloons at the tips. They cover themselves in thick fur like a friendly Sasquatch, extend hair into a whip, swing between buildings, and float by growing a large balloon from their back. Their voice and body proportions seem to shift from masculine to feminine as the transformation deepens, although Jet cannot tell whether this is another power or dream logic.

## Central conflict and theme

The hero's abilities look absurd at first and terrifying at full scale. Jet must decide whether power should be judged by its maximum destructive potential or by the purpose its wielder chooses. The story argues that compassion—not combat—is the truest measure of heroism.

## Escalation

The hero roots one detached hair into a building, unroots the structure, and lifts it with an enormous balloon. Jet fears that the plumbing and electrical connections have been destroyed, but the hero reroots the building and restores every connection perfectly. They then unroot and lift the entire city before returning it safely to the ground. Authorities arrive and demand proof that such overwhelming power is genuinely used to help people.

## Ending and emotional arc

At a hospital, the fur-covered hero makes balloons for delighted sick children. They then demonstrate their most extraordinary gift: seamlessly unrooting a patient's failing heart and rooting a viable donor heart into place, restoring the patient without the dangers and inconsistencies of conventional surgery. Jet leaves amazed that someone capable of lifting cities chose not fame or battle, but a compassionate niche where every strange ability becomes wonderful.
