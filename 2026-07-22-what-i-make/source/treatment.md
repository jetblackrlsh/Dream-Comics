# Treatment

## Logline

After a friendly flying jacuzzi carries lucid dreamer Jet through the Arctic aurora and into an unwanted steampunk attack, he escapes to a floating living room where one private drawing grows into a living guitar and a declaration that no audience, critic, or angry stranger can own his imagination.

## Central conflict

Jet wants to relax without feeling that a peaceful dream is less worthwhile than one of his superhero adventures. The first half externalizes that tension: the moment he questions whether the jacuzzi is exciting enough, it flies into the aurora and attracts a war train and an aggressive cowboy. Jet survives the spectacle but does not embrace the war. The deeper conflict arrives in the floating living room, where he must decide whether a quiet act of creation can matter without action, danger, approval, or an audience. Drawing for himself answers that question.

## Theme

Creation has private value. A work does not need an audience to matter, and other people's anger does not grant them ownership over another person's imagination. Peaceful creativity is not lesser than heroic spectacle; it is another expression of lucid freedom.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins physically comfortable but mentally restless, comparing relaxation with his past adventures. Wonder overtakes that doubt as the jacuzzi carries him through the tingling green and pink aurora. The train and cowboy turn wonder into unwanted danger, so Jet decisively protects himself and his friendly host without joining their war. In the floating living room, he returns to stillness with greater intention. Nostalgia and satisfaction arise as he draws something only for himself. That inward pleasure becomes outward music, confidence, and joyful creative defiance.

## Continuity

This dream belongs to the Storyverse continuity that began on June 11, 2025. Jet retains his established dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks beneath a black hoodie, visible Dream Anime graphic shirt, black athletic pants with one white side stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, rainbow crystal Sword of Light, golden glittering energy tether, and sparkling purple star-filled Lucid Light.

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper remain Jet's established crew, but none appears physically in this episode. The sentient jacuzzi is the supporting character. The war train and cowboy are an unexplained local conflict Jet explicitly refuses to join.

## Ending

Jet stands inside the three-walled floating living room beneath a blue sky and fluffy pink clouds. The living vine guitar remains wrapped around his arms, the friendly jacuzzi hovers nearby, and the notebook lies open to the star-flower painting. Jet sings the hook, “Cry me a river, bleed me a lake. You can’t take or control what I make.” The final image makes the consequence visible: a private sketch has transformed the entire sky into a personal stage, yet the open notebook remains the quiet heart of the experience.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: Jet strums the living vine guitar above the aurora while the friendly flying jacuzzi, star-flower notebook, and distant steampunk pursuit form the story's visual layers.
2. Jet induces lucidity, relaxes in the sentient jacuzzi in the Arctic, worries quietness may feel dull, and rises into the aurora.
3. Jet experiences the physical beauty of green and pink aurora before an armed steampunk war train begins firing from below.
4. A cowboy on a flying steampunk horse wounds Jet and the jacuzzi; Jet creates a healing barrier, then uses his tether to interface with and destroy the horse.
5. Jet reaches an open floating living room, chooses drawing over television, and feels nostalgic warmth when he opens a purple patchwork felt notebook.
6. Jet uses a pink magical pencil to create a watercolor-like green stalk topped by a star and discovers the private meaning of placing part of himself on a blank page.
7. Living vines form a guitar around Jet's arms as the sky changes to blue and pink; he begins singing about creation, freedom, and the limits of other people's anger.
8. Jet sings the hook beside the friendly jacuzzi and open notebook, fully claiming the personal freedom to make what belongs to him.
