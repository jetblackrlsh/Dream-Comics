# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears a black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries a rainbow sword shaped like a colorful futuristic crystal blade and an energy tether that looks like a rope of golden glittery light.
- In this comic, Jet is alone. His posture shifts from thoughtful stillness, to athletic ascent, to controlled lucid power, to relaxed euphoria.
- Visual motifs: rainbow imagination energy, golden tether light, purple starry Lucid Light accents, warm brown eyes focused with calm lucidity.

## Sleep Paralysis Demon

- A shadowy humanoid pressure-demon with a flattened upper body, long thin arms, smoky black-purple skin, glowing hollow violet eyes, and a mouth like a dark suction vortex.
- It crouches on Jet's chest in liminal panels, trying to drink imagination as colored vapor from him.
- It should feel frightening but not gory: no blood, no wounds, no explicit injury. When overloaded, it stretches like an overinflated balloon and pops into harmless shadow confetti and rainbow vapor.

## Idea Space

- Not a character, but a recurring visual presence: cosmic bubble universes and floating idea symbols suspended in a white void.
- Playing-card staircase consists of giant crisp cards floating upward: kings, jacks, number cards, aces, and a glowing queen card that becomes the symbolic key.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple energy like a night sky full of stars. Rainbow imaginary energy appears brighter, warmer, and more prismatic than Lucid Light.
