# 07/29/2026 - Sol Against the Ghost Armada

Dream Comics entry for July 29, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet returns to the medieval Storyverse village protected by the Blue Enchantress. Their exploration beneath the village has revealed two distinct forms of rainbow Life Crystal: shallow crystals that can animate lifeless objects, and naturally living crystal people with minds and a civilization of their own. Jet realizes that his independently living, life-giving, technologically advanced Life Sword combines both forms.

Because the exposed mines have attracted raiders, Jet bonds his pocket magic mirror to the Blue Enchantress's mirror, summons Leon, and hides the entire bubble universe inside a pocket dimension beyond conventional Storyverse travel. An arriving pirate armada attacks Leon's gigantic mecha form, but Leon turns intangible. The ships' cannon fire passes through him and destroys the pirates on the opposite side.

An even larger ghost cruiser then charges toward Leon. Jet uses the Life Sword to restore the spectral warship to physical life, allowing Leon to phase through it and then possess it. Inside the captured cruiser, Jet fights its undead vampiric captain. After the captain drains Jet's life, evades the Life Sword as mist, and shatters his own claws against Aegis, Jet discovers that Lucid Light can be fired as an instantaneous laser.

The laser stuns the captain long enough for Jet to invent a new spell. “Sol” summons a miniature sun whose concentrated sunlight destroys the vampire. Jet and Leon end the dream with the hidden universe safe, permanent mirror communication established, the greatest pirate cruiser they have ever seen under their control, and two new abilities ready for future adventures.
