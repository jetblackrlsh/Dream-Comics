# 08/07/2026 - The Sky-Iron Frog

Dream Comics entry for August 7, 2026.

Jet explores a luminous village where fairies of many colors live inside magical trees. After the fairies repel a giant frog kaiju, it returns wearing black sky-iron samurai armor that nullifies their spells. Jet tries to tear the armor away with Geomancy, but the frog's adhesive mucus holds it in place.

That failure reveals the solution: if Geomancy can move anti-magic armor, perhaps the power is really Geokinesis. Jet reorganizes the defenders, sending magical fairies to safety and psychic fairies to the front. Pyrokinesis, cryokinesis, and electrokinesis bypass the armor, defeat the frog, and leave Jet wondering whether every dream power is ultimately a power of the mind.
