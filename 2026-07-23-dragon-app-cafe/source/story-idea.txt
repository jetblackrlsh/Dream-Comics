On July 23, 2026, Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and enters the continuing Storyverse. He arrives in a mostly empty commercial building where a mysterious woman is waiting. Jet explains his idea for a new kind of internet café: instead of paying only to play preinstalled games, every visitor arrives with an app concept and receives access to computers equipped with AI coding agents. Completed apps become part of a growing public library, so customers are both users and creators.

During the explanation, Jet's skin turns purple and orange wings emerge from his back, transforming him into a humanoid dragon. The interruption annoys him because he sincerely wants to explore the café as a business thought experiment. Jet takes the transformation as a hint that the dream expects more fantasy, so he expands the idea. The café will sell colorful chewy candies that temporarily grant different superpowers, along with private training rooms where customers can safely test those abilities on inanimate targets without fighting anyone.

The woman reveals that she transformed Jet deliberately. Fantastical internet cafés already exist throughout the dream world, and she wants their business to stand out as the only one run by a humanoid dragon. Jet accepts her reasoning and points out that their café will now be unique in four connected ways: AI-assisted custom app creation, a communal library of visitor-made apps, temporary superpower candies, and safe private power-training rooms.
