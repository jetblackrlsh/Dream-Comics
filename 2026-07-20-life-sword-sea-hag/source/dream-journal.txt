07/20/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse, a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes floating together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular Storyverse world called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025.

My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper, and me. Leon is my Ghostly UFO, resembling a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow, and first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025. Johnson resembles an orange humanoid goldfish and wears a blue astronaut suit without a helmet; I befriended him on 08/03/2025. Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him on 09/06/2025, and on that same day he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse, beginning my adventures into other Storyverse worlds. Skelebot is a humanoid speedster robot Johnson created on 09/21/2025. Overdrive is a metallic chrome robot with spiky slicked-back yellow hair, glowing green eyes, a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They can manipulate matter and were created in a dream on 10/20/2025.

Lucid Light is reality-warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered it on 07/19/2025.

Savannah is a pale-skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs covering her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She wields a futuristic purple-and-blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword. Her father, Tecton, is a green-skinned, muscular humanoid cat-ogre barbarian who wears a brown loincloth and silver baldric. Chipper is my small levitating silver crab robot. I met Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper while adventuring through the Super Cathedral in early 2026, and they became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie worn over a “Dream Anime” graphic tee. I wear black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag, and I have warm brown eyes. I carry a rainbow sword resembling a colorful futuristic crystal sword and an energy tether resembling a rope made of golden glittery light.

I arrived alone in the city and flew around like a superhero. While exploring, I encountered a blue slime creature whom I had assisted in a previous dream. When I originally met him, he resembled a giant half-orb-shaped mass of blue slime, but he was capable of shapeshifting into different forms. Since our previous encounter, he had become the king of the sea.

The slime creature now appeared as a gigantic, faceless blue humanoid with a crown-shaped head that symbolized his new status as the king of Atlantis. Despite how powerful he had become, he was dealing with a massive pollution kaiju that he simply could not defeat on his own. The kaiju was an enormous mutant monster that had apparently been created by pollution contaminating the ocean.

The slime creature grew to an even greater size, and I climbed onto his shoulders so that we could confront the pollution kaiju together. As we approached the monster, I decided that I might be able to purify it using my rainbow crystal sword, the Life Sword. The Life Sword was one of the most powerful artifacts in the Storyverse, and I believed that its life-giving energy might be able to cleanse the pollution from the kaiju.

However, as soon as I lifted the Life Sword into the air, a sea hag suddenly flew past me on a witch's broom. She snatched the sword directly out of my hand before I could use it. The sea hag immediately began channeling the power of the Life Sword, using its energy to resurrect an army of skeletal pirate warriors. The revived pirates began attacking the city, creating an entirely new crisis while the pollution kaiju continued threatening the sea.

For a moment, it seemed as though all hope was lost. The sea hag now possessed one of the most powerful artifacts in the Storyverse, and she was using it to create an undead army. However, I still had my golden energy tether. The tether functioned somewhat like a lasso, but it could also act as a conduit that transferred energy from one place to another.

I threw the golden energy tether around the Life Sword while the sea hag was still holding it. Once the tether was attached, I focused my Lucid Light into the golden energy line. The tether carried my Lucid Light directly into the sword and shocked the sea hag through it. The surge of energy caused her to lose her grip on the Life Sword and fall from her broom.

As the sword slipped from her hand, I used the tether to pull it back toward me and caught it. Now that I had reclaimed the Life Sword, I turned its power against the army of resurrected skeletal pirates. I used the sword to drain the life energy that had been sustaining all of the undead warriors. One by one, the skeletal pirates collapsed as their borrowed life force flowed back into the Life Sword.

Absorbing the energy from the entire pirate army supercharged the sword. I aimed the Life Sword at the pollution kaiju and released all of the accumulated power in a massive blast. The supercharged life energy struck the kaiju and completely eliminated it, saving both the city and the sea from the monster.

The slime hero and I understood where the pollution kaiju had come from because it had clearly been created by pollution. However, we were both confused about how the sea hag factored into everything. Her arrival seemed unrelated to the kaiju, so I decided to investigate and determine what she had actually been trying to accomplish.

I went to check on the sea hag after the battle. She had died from falling off her broom, but the broom itself was still intact. I attached my golden energy tether to the broom and used the tether to form a magical bond with it. This connection allowed me to control the broom and sense where it belonged.

I climbed onto the broom and allowed it to carry me back to the sea hag's hidden treasure cove. The broom knew the way and flew me directly to a cave filled with treasure. Once I arrived, I discovered that the sea hag had already possessed a small army of skeletal pirates before stealing the Life Sword. She had wanted the sword so that she could resurrect an even larger army.

The sea hag had been hoarding treasure inside the hidden cove, and she intended to use her expanded pirate army to raid and pillage the city for even more riches. Discovering the treasure cove finally revealed her true motivation. Her appearance during the battle with the pollution kaiju had largely been a coincidence. She had simply recognized an opportunity to steal the Life Sword and use it to increase the size of her undead forces.

While exploring the treasure cove, I was attacked by the sea hag's familiar. The familiar was a humanoid red crab monster who fought like a warrior. He possessed cursed claws that I believed could potentially damage either my golden energy tether or the rainbow crystal Life Sword. Because I did not want to risk losing or damaging either artifact, I was reluctant to use them directly against him.

Instead, I focused my Lucid Light and used it to summon a pair of glowing claws around my hands. I prepared to fight the crab familiar using the Lucid Light claws, but I was also surrounded by the remaining skeletal pirate warriors who had been stationed inside the treasure cove.

The slime hero was no longer with me, so I had to face the crab familiar and the entire group of skeletal pirates alone. It became another moment where it seemed as though all hope might be lost. I was badly outnumbered, and I still needed to avoid being struck by the familiar's cursed claws.

Then I remembered the magical bond I had formed with the sea hag's broom. I called the broom to my side and commanded it to fight alongside me. The broom immediately began flying around the treasure cove on its own. It swept across the ground and kicked up clouds of dust, creating a thick smokescreen that obscured the battlefield.

The broom used its wooden handle like a weapon, battering the skeletal pirates and knocking them away from me. It flew rapidly between the warriors, striking them one after another and almost single-handedly keeping the entire pirate army occupied. With the broom fighting the skeletal warriors, I finally had an opportunity to concentrate on the crab familiar.

The dust and smoke partially blinded the familiar, but I still had to move carefully and avoid the swipes of his cursed claws. I had already taken some damage while fighting the skeletal pirates, but I was able to use the Life Sword's power to heal my injuries without risking the sword in direct combat.

I attacked the crab familiar with my Lucid Light claws. Each time my claws struck his shell, they released powerful waves of heat. The repeated impacts caused the inside of his shell to become hotter and hotter. Eventually, the heat became so intense that the familiar could no longer remain inside his armored shell.

The humanoid crab molted, abandoning his shell in an attempt to escape the heat. However, without the protection of his shell, his body was left vulnerable. I attacked him again with my Lucid Light claws and defeated him.

Meanwhile, the enchanted broom finished overwhelming the remaining skeletal pirate warriors. With the crab familiar defeated and the last of the sea hag's undead forces destroyed, the threat of her pirate army was finally eliminated. Through my alliance with the slime king, the power of the Life Sword, my golden energy tether, my Lucid Light claws, and the sea hag's own broom, I defeated all of her remaining forces and saved the city.
