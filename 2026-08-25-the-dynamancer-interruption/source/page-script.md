# Page Script - The Dynamancer Interruption

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, the frog Dynamancers, the unnamed former bully, the red-and-blue demon, the paramilitary guards, the motorized zip-line toy, the frog world, and the academy follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic full-page composition. Jet rides the red-and-blue motorized zip-line toy through a seamless collision of two worlds. Humanoid frog Dynamancers manifest love, knowledge, water, and light around a swamp-cooled data center on one side. The red-and-blue demon crashes through an academy window among CRT monitors on the other. Sparkling purple Lucid Light and cosmic Storyverse bubbles connect the scene.

Visible cover text only: “THE DYNAMANCER INTERRUPTION” and “AUGUST 25, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - A World Made Like a Comic

A clear three-panel lucid setup page. Show Jet becoming lucid in a star-filled contemplative Storyverse space; Jet shaping a new cosmic bubble world with Lucid Light; then a close view of the imagined world waiting to be transformed.

Caption text:

- “I became lucid inside a God dream, where I could create and study an entire Storyverse world.”
- “I wondered what overnight event could make reality feel more like a living comic book.”
- “My answer was simple and dangerous: let modern technology give ordinary people literal superpowers.”

## Page 3 - Birth of the Dynamancers

A clear three-panel origin page. Show a data center drawing swamp water through cooling pipes; frog DNA glowing inside polluted water; then several humans transforming harmlessly into upright humanoid frog Dynamancers whose conceptual powers awaken.

Caption text:

- “Data centers cooled themselves with swamp water carrying traces of frog DNA.”
- “Their pollution transformed people into humanoid frogs with powers tied to a single concept.”
- “I called them Dynamancers—love, knowledge, and countless other ideas made physically real.”

## Page 4 - A Divided Superpowered World

A clear three-panel social-conflict page. Show joyful frog Dynamancers using constructive conceptual powers; fearful citizens watching a dangerous silhouetted frog villain; then supporters and opponents divided outside the glowing data center while Jet observes from above.

Caption text:

- “Some saw the Dynamancers as humanity's next great era, powered by imagination.”
- “Others feared that every new superhero would also make room for a supervillain.”
- “Even literal superpowers could not unite a world already divided over data centers.”

## Page 5 - The Release Form

A clear three-panel academy setup page. Show Jet inside a retro-filled dorm with failing CRT monitors; paramilitary guards escorting the intentionally unidentifiable former bully inside; then the man repairing the monitors and presenting an official-looking release form.

Caption text:

- “Then I lost lucidity and found myself at a modern academy filled with relics from the 1990s.”
- “Armored guards brought a former school bully into my dorm to repair its failing monitors.”
- “He had accidentally killed someone, and one signed release form could restore his freedom.”

## Page 6 - The Boundary Becomes a Demon

A clear three-panel emotional-reversal page. Show the man offering the form while Jet cries and refuses; Jet explaining the comparison to a demon-fighting television character; then red-and-blue symbiote tendrils beginning to transform the man as the guards vanish.

Caption text:

- “He wanted my signature because good deeds were supposed to prove he deserved another chance.”
- “Crying, I refused. Redemption was possible, but I could not be the person who granted it.”
- “I compared him to a demon-fighting character whose life also depended on doing good.”

## Page 7 - The Motorized Escape

A clear three-panel action-climax page. Show the fully transformed red-and-blue demon attacking through the dorm; Jet setting up the full-sized motorized zip-line toy while pursued; then Jet riding it through the hallway and kicking the demon through the high window.

Caption text:

- “The comparison became reality as red-and-blue symbiote tendrils transformed him into a demon.”
- “He chased me through the dorm, turning everything he found into a weapon.”
- “A motorized zip-line toy became my escape—and the weapon that sent him through a window.”

## Page 8 - Back Above the Frog World

A clear three-panel resolution page. Show Jet instantly returning to his lucid creator's vantage point; the unchanged frog Dynamancers continuing beneath him; then Jet peacefully observing the cosmic bubble as morning light approaches.

Caption text:

- “The moment he fell, I returned to lucidity above the frog world I had created.”
- “Nothing there had changed, though I survived an entire story while my control was gone.”
- “I watched the Dynamancers once more, sensed morning approaching, and chose to wake.”

## Caption checklist

- Page 1: 2 visible text elements.
- Pages 2-8: exactly 3 caption boxes per page.
- Every caption is required verbatim, unnumbered, and enclosed in a complete rectangular cream box.
