# 08/24/2026 - The Doorman of the Lava Sky

Dream Comics entry for August 24, 2026.

Jet steps from a freestanding dimensional elevator into a desert of black-bone castles beneath a sky sealed by living lava. His companion Autumn insists that she is his wife and that he is the Doorman, a veteran traveler who operates the elevator, but Jet remembers neither her nor their supposed adventures together.

Autumn summons Apollo, who explains that the lava god Meltronome has blocked all sunlight from the world. After Apollo assigns Jet the mission of clearing the sky, Autumn disappears. Jet survives an attack from a hollow black-bone dinosaur by turning it into a Lucid Light mech, then negotiates with the god of blades. Because Meltronome's defeat would improve the blade god's place in the divine hierarchy, he grants Jet safe passage and a divine bone blade.

At a magical academy, the blade gives Jet access to a gray-skinned god of deals. The god transfers his unlimited but conditional magic into Jet, then steals another person's lightning-rhinoceros abilities as the price of the bargain. Jet realizes that the deal-maker has traded dangerous godhood for simpler combat power, but the transformed rhinoceros threatens everyone at the academy. Jet uses Porta to strand the creature's legs in the desert while leaving its unharmed upper body trapped and harmless.

Because Jet's existing magic does not require bargains or sacrifices, he can wield the transferred divine power without the deal god's old restrictions. He casts Meltronome away, breaks the molten sky, and restores sunlight. Apollo rises like the sun and declares himself the world's new supreme god. Jet can only hope the new reign will be kinder as his strange life as the amnesiac Doorman remains unresolved.
