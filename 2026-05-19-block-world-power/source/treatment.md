# Treatment

## Logline

Lucid in the Storyverse, Jet joins his doppelgangers to prototype augmented-reality glasses that reveal Block World, where World Guard must share the elemental World Power with cowardly clairvoyant Woofer to surprise the multiversal vampire hunting him.

## Central Conflict

The issue has two nested conflicts. In the outer dream, Jet and his variants are trying to invent a device that turns AI world models into an explorable augmented reality. In the inner Block World adventure, World Guard faces Space Succubus, a predator who can instantly drain his World Power if he fights her directly. The only viable strategy is trust: World Guard must give part of his power to Woofer, whose future sight lets him dodge the vampire but whose fear keeps him from acting alone.

## Theme

Power becomes more dangerous when hoarded and more surprising when shared. The dream's invention is exciting because it lets stories spill into the room, and the Block World battle is thrilling because victory depends on cooperation that the villain never imagined.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins as a lucid observer entering a brainstorming world. He becomes a collaborator among doppelgangers, then a fascinated tester watching tiny blocky heroes move through his environment. As World Guard and Woofer face Space Succubus, Jet shifts from amused spectator to invested fan. The battle climaxes with World Guard giving Woofer enough World Power to act. Jet wakes up right when the story is most exciting, disappointed because the miniature world had started to feel real.

## Ending

Woofer, trembling but empowered, launches a surprise counterattack with a borrowed spark of the World Power. Space Succubus reels, shocked by an act of shared power she has never encountered. The final page fractures into Jet waking in bed while the AR glasses glow in memory, leaving the Block World battle unresolved and making Jet want the next issue.
