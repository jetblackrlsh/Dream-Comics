# Treatment - Silonis and Tetheron

## Logline

When a masked thief steals Jet's Golden Energy Tether and turns a private comparison into a cosmic duel, Jet deliberately cuts the tether with his living Life Sword, restores it with life energy, and discovers that both artifacts possess true names and lives of their own.

## Central Conflict

Jet begins with an apparently practical question: if he could bring only the Life Sword or the Golden Energy Tether into the waking world, which would be more valuable? The Life Sword offers healing, protection, flight, animation, purification, and companionship. The tether offers traversal and magical bonds whose possibilities depend almost entirely on Jet's creativity. The masked thief externalizes the question by stealing the tether and forcing it into conflict with the sword.

## Theme

Power cannot be measured only by a list of abilities, and living companions cannot be reduced to competing tools. Silonis represents immense life-giving capability; Tetheron represents connection and open-ended possibility. Their differences make them complementary rather than replaceable.

## Ending

Silonis restores the severed Golden Energy Tether and awakens it as Tetheron. Jet defeats the thief while wielding both artifacts together. Floating among the Storyverse's bubble universes, Jet hears both true names and understands that the original choice was incomplete: both artifacts are alive, personally significant, and extraordinary in different ways.

## Emotional Arc

Jet moves from analytical comparison, to fascination with the tether's underestimated potential, to urgent confidence during the theft, to vindication when his restoration plan succeeds, and finally to wonder and affection when Tetheron awakens. The ending replaces the language of possession with the language of companionship.

## Cast

- Jet: lucid protagonist and caretaker of both artifacts.
- Silonis: living Rainbow Crystal Life Sword, already capable of thought and communication.
- Tetheron: Golden Energy Tether, awakened or revealed as living when restored.
- Masked thief: arrogant obstacle who mistakes Tetheron for a simple magical rope.

## Visual Progression

The comic begins in a recognizable modern city with clean daylight and hypothetical demonstrations of each artifact. The thief's arrival fractures the setting into the deep indigo cosmic void of the Storyverse. The battle moves from stark red-black menace to radiant rainbow and gold as Silonis restores Tetheron. The final page is calm and luminous, with both living artifacts hovering beside Jet as equals.
