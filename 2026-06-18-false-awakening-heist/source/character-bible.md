# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow crystal sword shaped like a colorful futuristic crystal blade, and a golden glittering energy tether that trails like a rope made of light. His Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy shining like a starry night sky. In this entry he moves from playful lucidity to exhaustion, rage, guilt, panic, and finally focused wakefulness.

## Artist Jet

Artist Jet is an alternate version of Jet with the same dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks. He wears a painter's smock covered in glitchy digital neon paint, with splashes of cyan, magenta, lime, and orange that look like broken pixels and wet brush strokes at the same time. His posture is buoyant and creative. He flies beside Jet in the jungle world as a friendly mirror self.

## Eye-Grip

Eye-Grip is a savage alternate Jet with blue skin, red face paint across the cheeks and forehead, glowing orange eyes, and a white loin cloth. He is athletic, crouched, intense, and predatory, with orange glowing hand constructs projected from his eyes. The constructs should look like spectral orange hands made of light, reaching outward from Eye-Grip's gaze to grab prey. He should read as wild and dangerous, but still recognizably an alternate Jet through beard shape, dreadlock silhouette, and facial structure.

## Coin Bird

The coin bird is a vivid jungle creature with colorful tropical feathers, a glowing belly or broken-feather gaps revealing golden coins inside, and a trail of falling gold coins as it flees. It should look magical and dreamlike rather than wounded or gruesome.

## Jungle World

The jungle world is lush, saturated, and adventurous: towering emerald trees, massive leaves, sun shafts, vines, ancient stone platforms, waterfalls, glowing insects, and a sense that gravity can be bent by lucid flight.

## False Awakening Bedroom and Assignment Space

The first false awakening should feel emotionally heavy and semi-real: a dim bedroom, desk, paper assignment, headphones or music imagery, tired posture, and dream symbols from a song assignment curling into the room. The assignment does not need readable text; use unreadable marks, musical staff shapes, and symbolic animal silhouettes instead.

## Power Armor Jet

In the second false awakening, Jet wears power armor over his usual silhouette. The armor should preserve Jet's dark-brown skin, beard, dreadlocks, warm eyes, and belt-bag silhouette where possible, while adding sleek black and purple armor plates, glowing purple Lucid Light seams, a rainbow sword mount, and golden tether energy. The armor looks strong but emotionally unstable during the council fight.

## Teams With Power Armor

The opposing teams in the heist test wear varied power armor with distinct silhouettes and AI assistant holograms hovering near them. Avoid readable team logos. Their abilities should be implied through colored energy, shields, boosters, and utility drones.

## AI Assistant

Jet's AI assistant appears as a small floating holographic core, a glowing blue-white geometric face or orb with interface shards around it. It should feel like a trusted partner being taken away rather than just a device.

## Female Instructor

The female instructor is an adult woman with composed posture, sharp eyes, and a professional evaluator outfit with sleek futuristic academy details. She is not cruel in expression; she is severe, disappointed, and formal while telling the teams they failed.

## Council and Ninja Forces

The council are dream authorities seated in a formal chamber of dark stone, gold treasure light, and glowing assignment symbols. Their ninja forces wear masked black armor with muted silver highlights and move in coordinated groups. The council should feel bureaucratic and mythic, not like ordinary villains.

## Telepathic Woman

The telepathic woman is calm, grounded, and compassionate. She stands in a strange city of tilted towers, empty streets, neon windows, and fog. Her eyes glow softly with blue-violet telepathic light, and translucent thought-wave rings radiate from her toward Jet. She triggers lucidity without aggression.

## Black Void and Real Awakening

The black void is almost empty, with Jet's purple Lucid Light, golden tether, and fragments of prior worlds dissolving behind him. The real awakening should show a grounded bedroom clock reading around 4:00 AM, with a small visual echo of the normal 4:30 AM wake-up time as a ghosted expectation, not as a dominant text element.

## Visual Continuity Notes

Keep Jet's design consistent across every page: dark-brown skin, full beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, "Dream Anime" tee when not armored, black athletic pants with white stripe, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and purple Lucid Light. The cover and every page must avoid issue numbers. All story text belongs inside generated caption boxes in the page art.
