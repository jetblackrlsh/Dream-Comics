# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: protagonist, lucid dreamer, Storyverse explorer, performer, inventor, and wielder of Lucid Light.
- Stable appearance: man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, warm brown eyes.
- Signature powers and props: rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glitter energy tether, sparkling purple Lucid Light like a night sky full of stars, and a futuristic watch that he built during the theater-world sequence.
- Expression range: thoughtful curiosity, delighted sensory joy, surprised theatrical pride, suspicious focus, calm lucid authority.
- Continuity rule: keep Jet recognizably Jet on every page through skin tone, full beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, white stripe pants, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow sword, golden tether, and purple Lucid Light.

## Companion-Bot

- Role: symbolic future robot companion in Jet's possible-futures theory.
- Appearance: warm humanoid household robot with pearl-white and brushed-silver plating, soft teal eyes, rounded friendly face, gentle posture, and subtle family-home design cues.
- Function: represents intimate companionship, friendship, and family-like presence rather than wage labor.
- Continuity rule: keep the companion-bot warm, approachable, and distinct from the worker-bot.

## Worker-Bot

- Role: symbolic future labor robot in Jet's possible-futures theory.
- Appearance: sturdy orange-and-slate humanoid robot with practical tool arms, industrial backpack module, work gloves built into its hands, and confident task-focused posture.
- Function: represents rented business labor, job niches, and purpose-built utility.
- Continuity rule: keep the worker-bot practical and industrial, not emotionally styled like the companion-bot.

## Jealous Co-Star

- Role: antagonist of the theater-world sequence.
- Appearance: stylish stage actor with pale skin, narrow face, slick black hair, sharp eyebrows, burgundy velvet stage jacket, black shirt, white gloves, and a fake congratulatory smile.
- Signature prop: poisoned alcoholic drink in a glittering glass on a tray.
- Expression range: forced friendliness, hidden envy, alarm when exposed.
- Continuity rule: keep the burgundy jacket, slick black hair, white gloves, and suspicious tray consistent.

## Dream Authorities

- Role: lucid authority figures called by Jet through Lucid Light.
- Appearance: calm futuristic officers made of clean silver armor and translucent purple star-light, with rectangular badges of Lucid Light and non-threatening but firm posture.
- Function: arrive only after Jet regains lucidity and calls them.
- Continuity rule: they should look like dream-law officers summoned by Lucid Light, not ordinary police.

## Possible-Futures Conjunction

- Role: opening setting and thought-space.
- Appearance: cosmic intersection of branching timelines, holographic family homes, robot design showrooms, business kiosks, apartment interiors, and Storyverse bubble universes floating beyond glassy horizon lines.

## Macaroni-and-Cheese Ocean

- Role: playful sensory dream world.
- Appearance: a vast thick golden-orange macaroni-and-cheese ocean with visible curved pasta, glossy cheese waves, steam curls, and a beach made of tan cracker crust crumbs under a bright surreal sky.

## Theater World

- Role: final setting, where praise and false awakening make the dream feel real.
- Appearance: ornate futuristic theater with golden stage lights, red curtains, award podium, audience silhouettes, backstage dressing room, and surreal hints of Storyverse stars behind the set.
