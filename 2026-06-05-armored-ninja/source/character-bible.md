# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. He can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he is alone in a dream city, contemplative and precise, using Lucid Light to construct a new hero rather than fight directly.

## Armored Ninja

Asian man with short messy black hair, sharp blue eyes, athletic build, focused expression, and a grounded street-hero presence. He wears a cobalt blue armored vest, cobalt blue armored knee-high combat boots, cobalt blue armored shorts, black armored gloves with raised gray knuckles, a black unitard under layer, and a large black round carbon shield strapped to one arm.

Continuity notes: Keep the short messy black hair, blue eyes, cobalt armor, black underlayer, raised gray glove knuckles, knee-high combat boots, and large black round carbon shield consistent on every page. The boots are his signature movement device: show him grinding across rails, curbs, roof edges, and pipes with bright blue speed streaks. His gloves channel forward momentum into heavy impacts. His shield is defensive against blades and fists, not bullets, and can be thrown like a chakram before ricocheting back.

## Superhero Mandroid

Humanoid robot superhero resembling a human man with white skin, slicked-back brown hair, determined heroic face, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow chest insignia with the initials "SHM", and oversized mechanical green fists.

Continuity notes: Superhero Mandroid should appear only as a memory, inspiration, mural, or symbolic silhouette in this issue. Keep his red unitard, yellow cape, "SHM" insignia, slicked-back brown hair, and large green mechanical fists recognizable when shown. He represents the larger heroic model that inspires Armored Ninja without overshadowing him.

## City Attackers

Anonymous hand-to-hand criminals in dark street clothing, carrying knives, clubs, and improvised weapons. They are dangerous at close range but not cosmic villains.

Continuity notes: Keep them visually simple so Armored Ninja remains the focus. Avoid firearms because Armored Ninja's shield is not bulletproof. Their role is to prove his defense, mobility, and restraint.

## Dream City

A modern lucid dream city at night with neon signs, wet streets, rails, curbs, rooftop pipes, fire escapes, elevated walkways, and deep blue-purple sky glow. The city exists inside the Storyverse but feels grounded and urban.

Continuity notes: Rails, curbs, ledges, and pipes are essential props because Armored Ninja uses them to grind for speed and momentum. Use subtle sparkling purple Lucid Light in the sky and reflections to show Jet's dream control.
