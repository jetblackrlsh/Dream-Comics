# Treatment

## Logline

After Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis, he appears as a vampire variant of himself in King Blud's castle cellar, defends his right to one daily Blud Shake, survives holy knights who can wound mist, and escapes sunrise by turning his enemies and allies into burrowing wolves and sheltering bats.

## Central Conflict

Vampire Jet wants one Blud Shake a day so he and Vampire Johnson can feed without drinking real blood. King Blud owns the cellar and refuses to share, then escalates from threat to violence by swinging a mighty axe and summoning holy-weapon knights. The conflict becomes more dangerous when the knights can cut chunks from Jet and Johnson's mist forms, creating a tiny fusion that is powerful but hungry and hard to control. By sunrise, survival depends on Jet making a direct choice: turn the final strongest knight into a vampire, command everyone into wolf form, and get them underground before the sun destroys them.

## Theme

Lucid control is not only about power. It is about staying practical under weird rules, using vampire abilities quickly, and choosing survival before pride.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins calm and confident because the Blud Shake arrangement seems simple and fair. King Blud's attack lets Jet show overwhelming vampire strength, but the holy knights prove the dream has rules that can still hurt him. Jet becomes worried when the mist chunks create a tiny feral fusion that might devour the cellar supply. Outside the castle, sunrise shifts the feeling from dominance to danger. Jet ends decisive and clear-headed, turning the final knight and leading every vampire creature into shelter just in time.

## Ending

The final page resolves the danger visually: the strongest knight has become one of the vampires, the group shifts into wolves, digs shelter holes, then becomes bats and dives underground before sunrise can burn them away. The last image shows the sealed dark holes beneath the morning light, making it obvious that Vampire Jet, Vampire Johnson, the tiny fusion, and the new vampire knight survived to feast another day.
