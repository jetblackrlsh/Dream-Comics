# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: lucid dreamer and protagonist.
- Age range: adult man.
- Skin and face: dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, expressive face that moves from discouraged to entranced, shocked, suspicious, and finally relieved.
- Hair: long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie; the dreadlocks must remain visible in every page where Jet appears.
- Outfit: black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag.
- Signature props: rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glittering energy tether, sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Silhouette and posture: grounded heroic stance when lucid, slumped shoulders when dejected or trapped, defensive running posture in the Odin chase.
- Continuity rule: do not remove the beard, dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime tee, belt bag, rainbow sword, or golden tether unless the page explicitly shows dream distortion trying and failing to obscure them.

## Second Brain

- Role: loyal protector and lucid warning system.
- Body: floating brain made of sparkling purple Lucid Light, with a translucent starry glow like a night sky.
- Face and expression: no human face; emotion is conveyed by glow intensity, electric arcs, posture in the air, and halo-like ripples.
- Signature power: Lucid Lightning neuro shock, shown as purple-white lightning bolts with starry particles and brainwave-shaped arcs.
- Continuity rule: always make him clearly a floating brain of Lucid Light, not a robot, skull, or ordinary organ.

## Berry Woman / Succubus Hag

- Role: tempter and first antagonist.
- False young form: beautiful young woman, hourglass figure, curly silver permed hair, shiny green and blue jumpsuit, piercing blue eyes, poised and inviting posture.
- True hag form: elderly soul-eating hag with the same silver curls turned wild, the same green-blue jumpsuit tattered and shadowed, piercing blue eyes now cold and predatory, long grasping hands, smoky soul-hunger aura.
- Signature prop: Num Num Berries, glowing surreal fruit colored deep magenta, blue, and green with tiny star-like seeds.
- Continuity rule: the young form and hag form should clearly be the same entity through shared silver curls, blue eyes, and green-blue outfit.

## Doctor Odin

- Role: hallucinated false helper and second antagonist.
- First appearance: calm trusted doctor with silver hair, neat short beard, pale blue eyes, white doctor's coat over dark clothes, and a staff-like medical cane.
- Distorted appearance: same doctor design, but masklike smile, warped proportions, predatory shadows, and medical tools that become sharp surreal dream shapes without gore.
- Setting motif: sterile clinic corridors that loop into each other, floating charts with unreadable dream symbols, impossible doors, and exam lights like watching eyes.
- Continuity rule: Odin should remain visually consistent as the same doctor while the environment reveals he is a berry hallucination.

## Leon

- Role: Ghostly UFO and visual anchor for the Storyverse ending.
- Body: ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow.
- Setting motif: Storyverse cosmic void filled with bubble universes.
- Continuity rule: Leon appears briefly and should be unmistakably a green-glowing ghostly UFO, not a solid spaceship.
