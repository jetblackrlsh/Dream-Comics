# 07/14/2026 - Pyrogre's Bachelor Pad

Dream Comics entry for July 14, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet tests increasingly heavy superhero power suits on a city rooftop. Wearing a new green, black, orange, and yellow superhero costume, Jet meets Pyrogre—a giant flaming ogre whose intimidating arrival hides a friendly fellow hero, a surreal internal arcade bachelor pad, and a power that grows through damage from within.
