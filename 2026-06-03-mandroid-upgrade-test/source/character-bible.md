# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. He can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he is excited by new ideas, focused during testing, and realistic about the need for more training.

## Johnson

Orange humanoid goldfish with expressive fish features, bright orange scales, large friendly eyes, and a blue astronaut suit with no helmet. He is a robotics engineer aboard Leon and works with confident inventor energy.

Continuity notes: Keep Johnson clearly fish-like and humanoid, with the same blue astronaut suit and no helmet. In lab scenes, give him tools, holographic readouts, rocket-fist schematics, material-grade meters, and nanite control consoles.

## Superhero Mandroid

Humanoid robot superhero resembling a human man with white skin, slicked-back brown hair, determined heroic face, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow chest insignia with the initials "SHM", and oversized mechanical green fists. He can use a translucent holo shield, regenerating rocket punches, astral thread for closing nightmare portals, and the new rocket-fist upgrades from this issue.

Continuity notes: Keep the red unitard, yellow cape, "SHM" insignia, slicked-back brown hair, and large green mechanical fists identical on every page. The new nitro rocket fists should leave orange-blue exhaust trails. The light propulsion rocket fists should leave bright white-gold exhaust trails. The nanite upgrade should visibly expand his mechanical green fists with swarming silver nanites, making them heavier and harder to aim.

## Leon

Ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow and translucent spectral hull. Leon is both a crew member and a vessel containing a robotics lab.

Continuity notes: Interior scenes aboard Leon should include curved ghost-green walls, floating consoles, cosmic windows, and a high-tech robotics bay. The lab should feel like the same place where Superhero Mandroid was created on 06/02/2026.

## Rocket Fist Test Materials

Three numbered training blocks used in Leon's robotics lab. Grade one is matte blue-gray and barely reacts to normal rocket fists. Grade two is darker steel with orange stress lines that cracks under nitro propulsion. Grade three is dense black crystal-metal with violet highlights that shatters under light propulsion.

Continuity notes: Use large clear visual numbers or color bands for the grades if possible, but do not rely on tiny labels for story clarity. The damage state should escalate visibly from unfazed to cracked to shattered.

## Storyverse

A cosmic void filled with glowing bubble universes, each containing possible story ideas. Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple energy shining like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep cosmic bubble universes visible through Leon's lab windows. Use Lucid Light sparingly as dream-entry energy and background sparkle, while the central visual focus stays on robotics testing.
