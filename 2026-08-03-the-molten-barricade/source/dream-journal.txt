I began the dream flying through a city like a superhero, soaring between the buildings with Silonis, the Rainbow Crystal Sword, also known as the Life Sword. Silonis was one of the most powerful artifacts in the Storyverse, and a group of goblins was determined to steal it from me.

The goblins carried ordinary-looking guns, but their ammunition had been enchanted with fire magic. Whenever they fired into the air, the bullets transformed into flaming projectiles that curved through the sky and chased after me like miniature fireballs.

They were trying to shoot me down, but I was not especially worried about them. Their attacks were easy enough to dodge while I was flying. The fire bullets were mostly a distraction.

The real threat was the goblins’ muscle: a gigantic troll wielding an enormous wooden club.

The troll was not quite tall enough to tower over skyscrapers, but it was still massive—perhaps half the height of one. It was shorter than a traditional kaiju, although calling it one would not have felt entirely inaccurate.

While I focused on weaving through the goblins’ flaming bullets, the troll swung its club and struck me out of the sky.

The impact sent me crashing toward the ground. As I fell, I used Silonis to summon a force field beneath me. The barrier caught me and softened the impact, preventing the fall from seriously injuring me.

Now that I was on the ground, I needed to escape quickly.

Using my Lucid Light, I propelled myself forward at super speed. The goblins chased after me, continuing to fire their enchanted bullets, while the giant troll lumbered behind them.

The troll was extremely slow because of its enormous size, so I quickly left it behind. The goblins, however, were surprisingly fast. They kept pace with me and continued shooting fire bullets in my direction.

Fortunately, Silonis had a mind of its own.

The Rainbow Crystal Sword flew out of my hands and began circling around me as I ran. It independently intercepted and deflected the flaming projectiles, protecting me while I focused on moving at super speed.

We raced down a long road until I reached a turn. Once I rounded the corner, the goblins temporarily lost sight of me, giving Silonis and me a brief moment of relief.

Nearby, we discovered the entrance to a massive cave.

Hoping to hide from the goblins and the troll, we ran inside. The cave was dark, and deep within it we encountered a dragon.

At first, the dragon seemed somewhat friendly. We explained that goblins were chasing us and trying to steal the Rainbow Crystal Sword.

The dragon offered to let us remain inside the cave, but only under one condition: I had to give it Silonis.

The dragon reached out and grabbed the sword.

Silonis immediately summoned a force field around itself. The barrier violently repelled the dragon, forcing it backward, and the sword flew out of its grasp and returned to my hands.

Angered, the dragon shouted toward the cave entrance, revealing our location.

“They’re in here!”

After betraying us, the dragon crawled deeper into the cave and disappeared, apparently deciding that it no longer wanted to be involved.

Moments later, the goblins entered through the mouth of the cave with their guns raised. Outside, the giant troll positioned itself directly in front of the entrance, using its enormous body to block our escape.

Because the cave was so dark, Silonis and I were able to hide within the shadows. We watched as the goblins searched for us, trying to come up with a plan before they discovered our location.

Then I remembered something from one of my earliest adventures in the Storyverse.

I had learned geomancy.

Realizing that earth magic was perfectly suited for the situation, I used my geomancy to tear loose a mass of rocks from the cave ceiling. The stones crashed down between the goblins and me, forming a thick barricade of boulders.

The goblins immediately began firing at the barrier from the opposite side. I could hear the rapid blasts of their guns and the impacts of the enchanted fire bullets striking the rocks.

Their magic did not destroy the barricade.

Instead, the intense heat began partially melting and welding the stones together. Rather than weakening the structure, the goblins’ fire bullets were turning it into an even stronger molten barrier that they could not cross.

Pulling the rocks from the ceiling had also created a large opening above me, almost like a skylight in the cave.

The barrier now separated me from the goblins, while the opening provided a new escape route.

I launched myself upward and flew through the hole before the troll could react. Rising high above the cave, I accelerated into the sky with Silonis beside me.

Together, we escaped into the Storyverse, leaving behind the gun-toting goblins, their enchanted fire bullets, the giant troll with its wooden club, and the treacherous dragon hidden deep within its cave.
