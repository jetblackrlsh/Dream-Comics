# Character Bible - The Elemental Betrayal

## Jet

- Dark-brown-skinned Black man with warm brown eyes.
- Full dark-brown beard and mustache.
- Long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Black hoodie over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt.
- Black athletic pants with a clean white side stripe, white high-top sneakers, black crossbody belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build; compassionate, calm under pressure, decisive in combat.
- May fly with a subtle aura of star-filled purple Lucid Light when entering the Storyverse.

## Silonis, the Life Sword

- A large colorful futuristic crystal sword with a brilliant faceted rainbow blade.
- The blade shifts through red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, violet, and magenta crystal light.
- Angular silver-and-black crystalline guard with a warm gold core; dark practical grip.
- Alive and capable of independent flight, rapid turbine-like spinning, life healing, and rainbow crystal force fields.
- Always radiant and unmistakably precious; never depicted as a plain metal sword.

## Fused vine-and-fire elemental

- Tall humanoid body woven from thick living green vines, leaves, roots, and woody tendrils.
- A regal crown-shaped mass of orange-gold living flame replaces hair.
- The fire visually intensifies with anger and threatens the plant body beneath it.
- Expressive ember eyes; aggressive posture; no clothing or weapons.

## Plant elemental

- Independent humanoid made entirely from restored emerald vines, leaves, and pale blossoms.
- No fire remains anywhere on the body.
- Long tendril arms can wrap and restrain Jet.
- Initially cautious, then displays a sharp mischievous smile before betrayal.

## Fire elemental

- Independent humanoid formed completely from orange, gold, and white living flame.
- No vines, wood, or leaves remain anywhere on the body.
- Crown-like flame silhouette echoes the original fused being.
- Can grasp and superheat Silonis without being burned; ultimately vulnerable to the sword's tornado.

## Cave and Storyverse

- Cave entrance: mossy black stone, hanging roots, luminous crystals, daylight behind Jet and amber light within.
- Cave interior: broad enough for dynamic combat, with rocky ledges and drifting embers.
- Storyverse: cosmic void filled with many luminous bubble universes, purple-blue starlight, nebulae, and crystalline sparkles.

## Continuity rules

- Jet's face, hair, clothes, crossbody bag, and skin tone remain consistent on every page.
- Silonis retains the same rainbow crystal blade and angular hilt on every page.
- After the separation, the plant elemental has no flame and the fire elemental has no vegetation.
- No speech bubbles, thought balloons, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, or creator signatures.
