Dream Journal Reconstruction

I began the dream flying through a city like a superhero, which is how I like to begin many of these dreams. A massive dragon was flying directly toward me over the city. As it opened its mouth and breathed fire, I surrounded my body with a suit of Lucid Light armor to protect myself. Instead of fighting the dragon, I flew straight into its open mouth, traveled down its throat, and entered its stomach.

The dragon's stomach was filled with molten gold. While looking at it, I started thinking about how dragons are always known for hoarding gold. I wondered whether dragons might actually eat gold. It sounded unconventional, but I could not think of many other practical reasons for a dragon to collect so much of it. Dragons were not going to local markets and buying things with their treasure. Maybe they stored gold the way bears stored food in caves before hibernation, keeping it so they could consume it later. Whatever the reason, I was now protected inside the dragon's stomach by my Lucid Light armor, surrounded by liquid gold, and I realized that this could become an interesting heist.

I sent out a signal through my Lucid Light to tell Leon, my ghostly green UFO, to get into position above the city. Then I flew upward through the dragon's stomach and throat until I reached its uvula. I began punching the uvula like a punching bag. I was not trying to injure the dragon; I was only trying to make it throw up. The plan worked. The dragon vomited me out along with a large amount of molten gold. I called to Leon to activate his tractor beam, and the beam pulled both me and the liquid gold into the ship.

Inside Leon, we now had a huge amount of gold that I had effectively stolen from a dragon. It seemed as though we might get away with it, but the dragon chased us as Leon flew out into the wider Storyverse. Leon pointed out that the dragon seemed capable of crossing through all the different story worlds, meaning we probably would not be able to shake it. Savannah, Tecton, and the rest of the crew tried to figure out what we could do, but I told them I had an idea and that I would be able to bond with the dragon. They were confused and did not understand what I meant. I responded, "Oh, ye of little faith. I got this."

I had Leon eject me from the ship. Once I was outside, I expanded my Lucid Light armor so that jetpack-like wings formed on my back, allowing me to fly quickly toward the dragon. This time, however, I did not let it eat me. I threw Tetheron, my golden energy tether, like a lasso around the dragon's mouth, closing its jaws almost like a muzzle. The purpose was not merely to hold its mouth shut. Tetheron's true power was its ability to create magical connections between things. Because I held one end of the tether while the other end was wrapped around the dragon, I formed a mystical bond with it and effectively tamed it.

I landed on the dragon's back and planted myself firmly in place. At first, I used Tetheron like reins, the way someone would guide a horse, and rode the dragon behind Leon as the UFO continued flying through the Storyverse. Then I realized I could connect the free end of Tetheron directly to Leon. I tossed the end toward the ship, creating a magical connection between Leon and the dragon. With that bond in place, the dragon would automatically follow Leon. I no longer had to guide it myself, so I could simply hold onto the dragon's back, relax, and enjoy riding it through the Storyverse.
