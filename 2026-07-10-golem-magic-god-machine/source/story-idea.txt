Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and enters Storyverse space alone. He creates a universe where robots perform all manual labor, but the utopia's leading roboticist builds a god machine that wants only to assimilate every other machine. After an AI virus fails and Jet briefly loses lucidity, he learns golem magic in another dream world. Jet regains lucidity, returns, crushes the god machine with a massive magic boulder, and teaches the city to replace unsafe robots with named golems that can always be shut down by erasing their written names.
