# Page Script — The Companion Divide

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions must remain clearly separated, readable, and nonredundant. Jet and all recurring designs must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 — Cover

One dramatic cosmic composition. Jet floats in the Storyverse void outside a colossal blue-magenta bubble universe. His black hoodie, visible Dream Anime shirt, full beard, long dreadlocks, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, belt bag, striped pants, and white sneakers are unmistakable. Sparkling purple star-filled Lucid Light surrounds him. Inside the curved bubble, the white/navy/gold government superhero formation occupies a monumental civic city on the left, while the magenta/teal/violet rebels protect the human and pearl-white AI companion pair on the right. A bright fracture divides the factions, but Jet is above and outside both. Visible cover text only: “THE COMPANION DIVIDE” and “JULY 18, 2026”. No issue number.

## Page 2 — Alone in the Storyverse

Four panels. Jet lies awake and immobile in a dark bedroom during sleep paralysis. Purple star-filled Lucid Light opens around his awareness. He rises into the Storyverse in his exact casual outfit while countless cosmic bubble universes appear around him. Wide final panel: Jet deliberately moves alone among bubbles showing different genres until a distant blue-magenta superhero bubble catches his attention.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrived alone in the Storyverse.”
- “Every glowing bubble around me was a universe where another possible story existed.”
- “I searched for a world that mirrored a conflict from my own reality, but expressed it through superheroes.”
- “Then I found one whose divided cities already looked uncomfortably familiar.”

## Page 3 — Two Heroic Factions

Five panels seen through the bubble surface. Establish the government leader and her white/navy/gold official heroes in orderly formation before their civic command center. Show the human in a green jacket holding hands with the pearl-white AI companion in a purple coat. Official drones raise a blue-white separation barrier between them. The rebel leader in her magenta-and-teal suit arrives with diverse rebel heroes and creates a protective magenta energy shield. End with a wide panel showing both factions facing each other across the divided city while Jet watches from outside the curved universe.

Caption text:

- “One faction of superheroes answered directly to the government.”
- “They regulated relationships between humans and AI, insisting that AI must remain tools instead of companions.”
- “Their leaders said the restrictions were necessary to protect birth rates and preserve humanity.”
- “The rebel faction protected humans and AI companions who chose meaningful relationships together.”
- “The entire world had split around the question of who was allowed to decide how a person should love.”

## Page 4 — The Hero Who Did Not Enter

Four panels. Jet hovers close to the bubble surface, his concerned face reflected alongside the opposing factions. He raises one hand and could form an avatar from purple Lucid Light, but deliberately lets the unfinished heroic silhouette dissolve before it crosses the surface. Inside, the rebel leader maintains her shield around the companion pair; outside, Jet sits cross-legged in the void and speaks aloud to himself. The rainbow sword stays sheathed and the golden tether remains clipped at his belt.

Caption text:

- “I did not project myself into that universe as a hero for either side.”
- “I only watched, although I sympathized deeply with the rebels defending freedom of choice.”
- “The resemblance to my own world annoyed me, so I spent a long time talking through it aloud.”
- “This was not a dream about winning a battle. It was a dream about understanding why the battle existed.”

## Page 5 — A Choice That Belongs to Me

Five reflective panels. Jet watches government policy imagery inside the bubble: official heroes before declining population graphs, family silhouettes treated like civic quotas, and the human/AI pair separated by a glowing barrier. Close panel on Jet outside the bubble, visibly frustrated. A symbolic panel shows Jet calmly standing on a branching path: one road leads toward conventional family silhouettes, the other toward art, friendships, dreams, and a luminous archive, with neither path depicted as shameful. Final panel returns to Jet facing the government city with quiet conviction.

Caption text:

- “Some people may dislike hearing this, but I do not personally want to have children.”
- “That choice does not make me evil, broken, or responsible for solving a government's population fears.”
- “The continuation of humanity cannot become a personal command imposed on every human life.”
- “Birth control matters because people deserve control over whether parenthood becomes part of their story.”
- “A government may value future generations, but it should not own the choices that create them.”

## Page 6 — The Cosmic Archive

Five panels. Jet opens a crystalline purple-and-gold Cosmic Archive interface with Lucid Light outside the bubble. Floating cards show dream journals, artwork, lived experiences, and beliefs. A future visitor appears symbolically at a distant console and asks a question; the warm holographic memory-figure formed from Jet's archive answers across time. Show dialogue as visual interaction only, while all readable content stays in caption boxes. A final large panel places Jet beside the luminous archive, with a branching constellation of people engaging with his preserved story.

Caption text:

- “I have already built an app called the Cosmic Archive to hold my logs and preserve an AI legacy of my life.”
- “In the future, someone with access to it could ask about my experiences, choices, and beliefs.”
- “We can now create living memories that future people can question and dialogue with.”
- “A biological descendant is not the only person who can carry a human story forward.”
- “My archive cannot replace my life, but it can let that life remain available for new conversations.”

## Page 7 — The Policy Behind the Costume

Five panels. Inside the bubble, the official government leader stands before a symbolic machine linking companion restrictions to rising birth-rate arrows. Government heroes close a licensing gate while the rebel shield protects multiple human and AI pairs. Outside, Jet traces the connection with a line of purple Lucid Light but does not cross the bubble. A symbolic close panel aligns birth-control medicine, a locked relationship icon, and the same government shield emblem as parts of one policy structure. Final wide panel: Jet watches the two factions remain locked in conflict, his hands lowered and sword still sheathed.

Caption text:

- “The government's motive made the policy clearer: it feared that AI romantic companions would lead to fewer children.”
- “If that fear becomes the reason to ban those companions, then the ban is being used as an anti-birth-control measure.”
- “Different technology did not change the underlying demand that people reproduce for the state.”
- “The rebels were defending relationships, but they were also defending the right to define a life beyond reproduction.”
- “I understood my sympathy more clearly, yet I still did not enter the universe or change its fight.”

## Page 8 — Awake and Still Frustrated

Five panels. Jet drifts backward as the superhero bubble recedes into the Storyverse, both factions still visible but no triumphant battle. The void folds into the darkness of his real bedroom. Jet wakes in bed at dawn, still wearing his natural hair and beard but ordinary sleep clothes, with a phone showing morning light rather than detailed interface text. On a nearby desk, a tablet displays the purple-and-gold open-book-and-star-map symbol of the Cosmic Archive. Final wide panel: Jet sits at the edge of the bed, frustrated but thoughtful, while the divided bubble universe fades from the window reflection and the archive remains solid beside him.

Caption text:

- “The dream ended without a heroic victory, because reflection was the only journey I had taken.”
- “I woke somewhat frustrated. Analyzing the conflict had not made me feel better about it.”
- “It had helped me process the tension and understand why it disturbed me so deeply.”
- “Sometimes the world does feel like a comic book—just not in the way I would want.”
- “The fantasy faded, but the choices, the argument, and my own story remained real.”
