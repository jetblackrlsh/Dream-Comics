# Page Script — Dandelion Golem Showdown

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Jet, the villain, the Sword of Life, the golden tether, Lucid Light, and the dandelion golems must follow the character bible exactly on every appearance.

## Page 1 — Cover

Dynamic vertical city composition. Jet swings high across the foreground on his glittering golden energy tether while sparkling purple Lucid Light holds him aloft. His long dreadlocks and open hoodie stream behind him, and his rainbow crystal Sword of Life gleams across his body. Far below, the purple spike-covered villain is almost hidden by dozens of multiplying dandelion golems and a storm of white seeds. Visible cover text only: “DANDELION GOLEM SHOWDOWN” and “JULY 16, 2026”. No issue number.

## Page 2 — Swinging Without Gravity

Four panels. A dark sleep-paralysis bedroom dissolves into a sparkling purple lucid threshold and a brief glimpse of Storyverse bubbles. Jet arrives alone on a high city rooftop in his exact hoodie outfit. He fires the golden energy tether toward a glass tower and swings out over the street. In the final wide panel he releases the tether, remains peacefully suspended on purple star-filled Lucid Light, then pushes himself forward into another swing.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrived alone in a city within the Storyverse.”
- “I cast my golden energy tether toward a skyscraper and swung out above the streets.”
- “Gravity and momentum did not control the arc. My Lucid Light held me safely in the air whenever the tether went slack.”
- “I could float, push myself forward, and begin another swing from any position I chose.”

## Page 3 — The Sword Thief

Five panels. Jet performs a joyful series of forward flips between skyscrapers, using purple Lucid Light to pause upside down and golden tether arcs to launch again. The purple spike-covered villain watches from a rooftop and focuses on the rainbow Sword of Life. The villain leaps into Jet's path and sweeps enormous claws toward him. Jet twists away in midair and draws the rainbow sword as both descend toward an empty intersection.

Caption text:

- “With Lucid Light removing the danger of falling, I could perform as many flips as I wanted without a care in the world.”
- “Then a purple villain covered in spikes saw the rainbow crystal sword at my side.”
- “The Sword of Life was the only prize they wanted, and their freakishly long claws reached for it.”
- “My joyful flight became a fight before I ever touched the street.”

## Page 4 — Claws Beneath My Feet

Five panels. The villain lands and stabs all ten long claws into cracked pavement. Beneath-street cutaway panel clearly shows the claws bending and traveling through the earth toward Jet. Two clusters of giant claw tips erupt directly beneath Jet's white sneakers and pierce through the soles enough to pin both feet in place without gore. Jet grimaces but stays upright. He swings the rainbow Sword of Life in two bright arcs, cuts through every restraining claw, steps free, and catches several shattered dark-violet claw fragments in one hand.

Caption text:

- “The villain drove every claw into the street, but the attack did not stay beneath their own hands.”
- “The claws traveled underground, rose beneath me, and stabbed through my shoes to hold both feet in place.”
- “I used the Sword of Life to cut through the restraints before the villain could take the blade from me.”
- “When I stepped free, I quietly collected several pieces of the shattered claws.”

## Page 5 — The Wrong Fragments to Lose

Four panels. The villain stands across the damaged intersection with shortened jagged claws already starting to extend again. A rectangular quote-caption presents the taunt. Jet calmly opens his palm to reveal the captured claw shards while the Sword of Life remains in his other hand. Another quote-caption presents Jet's answer. Purple star-filled Lucid Light coils around the shards, softens their sharp edges, and transforms each fragment into a full white dandelion with a golden center and green stem.

Caption text:

- “The villain mocked me: ‘My claws will keep coming. I can do this all day.’”
- “I showed them the broken pieces and answered: ‘You should never have let me get hold of your claw fragments.’”
- “Lucid Light wrapped the sharp purple shards in a tiny night sky.”
- “I changed every piece of the villain's weapon into a living dandelion.”

## Page 6 — Seeds Become Golems

Five panels. Jet gathers the transformed dandelions before his face and blows gently. Hundreds of luminous white seeds stream forward on purple-and-gold wind. The villain slashes at the harmless-looking seeds. Each seed that touches the street sprouts into a knee-high dandelion golem made from green stems, leaves, roots, and a round white seed head. The first group surrounds the villain's legs as more seeds continue landing.

Caption text:

- “I raised the dandelions and blew their seeds toward the villain.”
- “The drifting seeds looked soft, but each one carried a clear command shaped by Lucid Light.”
- “Wherever a seed landed, roots and stems joined together into a small dandelion golem.”
- “Within moments, an entire living squad had formed around the villain.”

## Page 7 — Every Hit Makes More

Five increasingly crowded panels. The villain slices one dandelion golem in half, but it bursts into seeds and reforms as two complete golems. A spike smashes three more, producing six. The villain attacks wildly in every direction as the crowd doubles again and again. The golems climb harmlessly over the villain's claws, spikes, shoulders, and legs, holding the arms down with braided stems. Final wide panel shows hundreds of small dandelion golems filling the intersection while the exhausted villain is almost completely buried but unharmed.

Caption text:

- “The villain struck the first golem, expecting one slash to destroy it.”
- “Instead, the scattered seeds formed two complete golems where one had stood.”
- “Every new hit divided more of them, so every act of aggression doubled the problem.”
- “The villain kept attacking until the intersection became a moving field of dandelions.”
- “Soon there were too many golems to fight, escape, or even count.”

## Page 8 — Surrender Beneath the Seeds

Four panels. The villain finally stops struggling and raises both hands while dandelion golems step back enough to reveal a clear surrender. Jet lowers the rainbow Sword of Life without striking. The golems relax into a peaceful field of ordinary dandelions across the repaired-looking intersection. Jet launches upward with purple Lucid Light, catches a golden tether line, and resumes a joyful midair flip over the city. A small symbolic silhouette of the legendary Monkey King blowing hairs that become helpers appears only as a golden cloud-like inspiration motif behind the final aerial panel, not as a physically present character.

Caption text:

- “At last the villain stopped attacking, raised both hands, and surrendered to the army their own blows had multiplied.”
- “I let the golems become a peaceful field of dandelions and ended the fight without taking the villain's life.”
- “The trick was inspired by the Monkey King, who could blow pieces of his hair into many helpers.”
- “I swung back into the sky, delighted that lucidity had let me combine flight, swinging, and one wonderfully strange idea.”
