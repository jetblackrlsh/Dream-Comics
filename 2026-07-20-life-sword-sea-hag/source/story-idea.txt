07/20/2026 - Life Sword: Sea Hag Siege

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in a coastal Storyverse city. A blue slime creature he helped in an earlier dream has since become a gigantic faceless humanoid king of Atlantis, but even he cannot defeat a pollution kaiju created by ocean contamination. Jet rides the slime king's shoulders and prepares to purify the kaiju with the rainbow crystal Life Sword.

A sea hag flies past on a broom, steals the Life Sword, and uses it to resurrect skeletal pirates. Jet lassos the sword with his golden energy tether, channels sparkling purple Lucid Light through the line, shocks the hag loose, and pulls the sword back. He drains the pirates' borrowed life force into the sword and unleashes it as a blast that destroys the pollution kaiju.

The hag dies in her fall, leaving her broom intact. Jet bonds the tether to the broom and follows it to her hidden treasure cove, where he learns her appearance during the kaiju battle was opportunistic: she wanted a larger undead army to raid the city for riches. Her humanoid red crab familiar attacks with cursed claws that might damage Jet's artifacts, so Jet summons glowing Lucid Light claws instead. Surrounded by the crab and the cove's remaining pirates, Jet commands the broom to fight beside him. The broom creates a dust screen and batters the skeletons while Jet heals with the Life Sword, superheats the crab's shell through repeated Lucid Light strikes, forces him to molt, and defeats his exposed form. The broom eliminates the last pirates, ending the sea hag's threat and leaving the city and sea safe.
