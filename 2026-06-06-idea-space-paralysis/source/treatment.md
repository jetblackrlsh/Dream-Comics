# Treatment

## Logline

Alone in a white void, Jet turns a strange thought about playing cards into a lucid ascent through Idea Space, then defeats the sleep paralysis demon draining his imagination by overfeeding it rainbow energy.

## Central Conflict

Jet's mind is reaching toward abstract discovery while a parasitic sleep paralysis demon tries to turn his imagination into fuel. The conflict is not physical strength against strength; it is control, sensation, and creative overflow against a force that can only consume.

## Theme

Ideas feel discovered because imagination can become a place. Lucidity turns even paralysis into a tool when Jet chooses how much sensation and energy his body receives.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins isolated and analytical in a blank void. His odd realization about the Queen card opens a symbolic path upward. The demon interrupts that discovery with pressure and fear, but Jet recognizes that being aware of his body is not weakness. He transforms the draining attack into a controlled surge of rainbow imagination and ends in calm, euphoric ownership of his senses.

## Ending

The demon bursts harmlessly into colored fragments. Jet floats above the card staircase in the white void, goosebumps and tingling light shown as radiant ripples around his body while Idea Space bubbles shimmer in the distance.
