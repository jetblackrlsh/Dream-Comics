# Treatment

## Title

06/14/2026: Peace at the Peak of Qa

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet transforms alone inside a castle into a black-and-white striped werewolf, follows pure desire back to the peak of Qa above creation, and rejects a disembodied voice's accusation that the summit is about control by choosing peace, shared elevation, and calm power.

## Central Conflict

Jet's heightened werewolf awareness carries him from isolation to the sacred peak of Qa. A voice interprets his return as hunger for control and authority, forcing Jet to define what the mountain means to him: not domination over creation, but proof that peace can be reached and shared.

## Theme

True power is not control over others. It is the calm awareness that helps lift others toward peace.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins suspended in sleep paralysis, then isolated in a castle as his body changes. The werewolf form first feels strange and dangerous, but the surge of lucid energy sharpens his awareness instead of scattering it. Desire moves him to Qa, where the voice challenges his motive. Jet answers with conviction, settles into the summit, and ends in a balanced state where power and peace feel inseparable.

## Ending

Jet sits on the peak of Qa above the Storyverse, no longer arguing with the voice. Purple Lucid Light, green eyes, and dawn-gold summit light ripple outward through the bubble universes below, visually suggesting that his peace helps elevate others along the way.

## Cast

- Jet in werewolf form: the protagonist, transformed from his usual dream self into a black-furred werewolf with white stripes and green snake-like eyes.
- The voice: a disembodied presence made visible through dark caption-like energy and symbolic shadow forms, challenging Jet's motive without becoming a physical villain.
- The mountain of Qa: a sacred crystalline summit towering over creation and the Storyverse bubble universes.
