# Character Bible - The Infinite Marks

## Jet

- African-American man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard and mustache, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Long dreadlocks spill from beneath a black hoodie worn over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt.
- Black athletic pants with one clean white stripe down each outer leg, white high-top sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build, calm confidence, expressive brows, and a compassionate but unyielding posture whenever Silonus is threatened.
- Flies under his own power.

## Silonus

- Jet's Rainbow Crystal Life Sword and one of the Storyverse's most powerful artifacts.
- Futuristic sword made from large, faceted, translucent crystals spanning the full rainbow spectrum.
- A small round red scanning gem is embedded in the hilt.
- Before adapting, it glows with pure prismatic rainbow energy.
- After adapting, the same crystal blade and silhouette remain unchanged while a luminous neon-green circuit grid runs across every facet.
- Generates translucent, faceted rainbow crystal force fields.

## Cloud Tavern bartender

- Androgynous adult tavern attendant with warm medium-brown skin, pointed elf-like ears, short silver hair, amber eyes, and a polished burgundy-and-gold vest over a cream shirt.
- Wears several small golden crown-shaped Marks on a belt chain.
- Initially confident and patronizing while offering a tavern-approved sword, then visibly stunned by Silonus's scan.
- Must never appear monstrous or purely evil; their suspicious attempt to take Silonus is real, but their role is that of a gatekeeper inside the tavern's system.

## The Cloud Tavern

- Vast floating tavern built on luminous clouds in a realm between realms.
- Warm golden wood, stone hearths, guild banners, floating lanterns, stained-glass portal windows, cloud balconies, and futuristic blue-green alert screens coexist naturally.
- Patrons include colorful masked superheroes, caped powerhouses, armored fantasy adventurers, elves, robed mages, dwarves, and benevolent horned demons.
- Three monumental doors stand together: left arrival door for returning heroes, center ordinary cloud entrance, and right departure portal for missions.

## Tavern equipment and Marks

- Every official weapon, gadget, medieval sword, magical staff, and futuristic device has the same unmistakable glowing neon-green circuit grid.
- The offered official sword is a well-made silver longsword with that green grid, visually ordinary beside Silonus.
- Marks are flat pieces of polished gold shaped exactly like small crowns.
- The achievement scanner is a magical-technological arch and counter with emerald circuitry, floating holographic numbers, and a tray for paying Marks.
- The infinite result is communicated as rapidly multiplying golden crown Marks and an emerald display expanding beyond finite digits.

## Recurring background heroes

- A stocky red-bearded dwarf in steel-and-leather armor arm-wrestles an enormous blue-and-gold costumed superhero.
- A violet-robed magical superhero reviews a glowing pact beside a benevolent crimson-skinned horned demon in noble black-and-gold guild armor.
- Other patrons remain a coherent mix of original superhero and fantasy archetypes.

## Continuity rules

- Jet's skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white shoes, cross-body bag, and Silonus remain consistent on every page.
- Silonus keeps the same crystal form before and after adaptation; only the neon-green circuit lattice is added.
- The bartender's silver hair, pointed ears, burgundy-and-gold vest, cream shirt, and crown-Mark chain remain consistent.
- The tavern architecture, three-door arrangement, green-grid equipment, and crown-shaped Marks remain visually consistent.
- No gore, speech bubbles, thought balloons, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, creator signatures, or blank caption boxes.
