# 07/22/2026 - What I Make

Dream Comics entry for July 22, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet relaxes in a friendly sentient jacuzzi in the Arctic. The jacuzzi carries him through the Aurora Borealis, but a steampunk war train and a cowboy on a flying mechanical horse force Jet to defend their peaceful flight. After escaping the unwanted battle, Jet reaches a living room floating in the sky and discovers a quieter kind of dream power: drawing something meaningful only for himself. His star-flower picture grows into a living vine guitar, and Jet sings beneath pink clouds about every creator's freedom to own their imagination.

The finished package includes the journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, assembled PDF, and preview artifacts.
