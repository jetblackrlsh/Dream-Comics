# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and can wield geokinesis and Lucid Light.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow sword, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he fights with confident composure, using stone-and-metal manipulation rather than sword strikes. His geokinesis should appear as stone fragments, bent metal seams, pressure cracks, and controlled slabs of arena rock moving under his command.

## Omega Knight

Omega Knight is a black Knightmare: an animate suit of massive black medieval plate armor with no visible body inside. It is as big as a large building, broad enough to face a kaiju, with a horned or crowned great helm, huge pauldrons, giant gauntlets, and a titanic sword or clawed hands. Mystical rubies cover its armor like glowing red soul-prisons.

Continuity notes: The rubies are the key identifier. They should glow crimson and contain ghostly silhouettes or tiny trapped faces of defeated Knightmares. Omega Knight should look ancient, victorious, and overloaded with stolen power. When damaged, it breaks into black metal plates, ruby shards, and stone-like deposits, then reforms until the rubies burst.

## Freed Knightmare Souls

The souls trapped in Omega Knight's rubies resemble pale spectral silhouettes of fallen animate armor, ghostly helmets, floating gauntlets, and translucent knight shapes. They should look newly liberated, not peaceful: a swirling army of potential future challengers.

Continuity notes: Use cool blue-white and pale green spectral light to contrast with Omega Knight's crimson rubies and Jet's purple Lucid Light.

## Storyverse Arena

The fight takes place in a colossal dream arena made from floating medieval stone platforms inside the Storyverse void. Cosmic bubble universes drift in the distance. Cracked columns, giant stone blocks, and fragments of black armor float when Jet uses geokinesis.

Continuity notes: The arena should feel huge enough for a building-sized opponent, with Jet often shown small against Omega Knight's scale. The flying stone couch is a deliberately strange dream object: a couch sculpted from rough gray stone, hovering like a casual throne or flying vehicle.
