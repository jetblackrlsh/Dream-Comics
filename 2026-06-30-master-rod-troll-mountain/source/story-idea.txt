Adapt the 06/30/2026 lucid dream journal entry into a complete Dream Comics short comic.

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in a beautiful green troll world filled with pink flowers, reverse-flowing rivers, waterfalls that climb upward, and a giant mountain at the edge of the world. The friendly trolls notice that he is sad because he has heard that global warming has passed the point of no return on his waking world. Their god, The Master, leaves a mystical lightning staff called the Master Rod on the mountain peak, and the trolls send seven lifelong climbers with Jet to claim it because it might be able to save his world.

One climber is a selfish speedster with purple lightning powers who does not want to share the staff. Jet uses Lucid Light to summon a motorized mountain bike powered by elemental cores, races past the speedster, and claims the Master Rod. The bike's elemental cores attract an energy-vampire ogre from space. Jet knows the ogre is after the elemental energy, not the staff. He lets the ogre grab the bike, strikes it with the Master Rod's lightning, overloads the ogre with too much elemental power, launches the unstable ogre into the sky, and casts Porta to send the ogre somewhere far away before the explosion can hurt anyone.

Jet gives the staff to the confused speedster and admits that it probably cannot save his waking world after all. The dream shifts through a virtual anime battle world of black holes and energy-absorption bubbles, then into a drama television world where a female wrestler arch-nemesis has trapped baby dinosaurs in an energy construct plane rolling downhill at full throttle. Before Jet can save them, he returns to the grassy troll world and wakes to his 4:30 AM alarm.
