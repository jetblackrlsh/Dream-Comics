# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: protagonist, lucid dreamer, Storyverse explorer, wielder of Lucid Light, rainbow sword, and golden energy tether.
- Stable appearance: man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, warm brown eyes.
- Signature powers and props: rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glitter energy tether, and sparkling purple Lucid Light like a night sky full of stars. On later pages, the Lucid Light becomes branching purple Lucid Lightning.
- Vapor form: a translucent cloud shaped like Jet's face, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie silhouette, rainbow sword glow, and purple starry energy, so the reader still understands that the vapor is Jet.
- Expression range: urgent focus, shock, fear while vaporizing, calm command as vapor, relieved triumph.
- Continuity rule: keep Jet recognizable through dark-brown skin, beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow sword, golden tether, and purple Lucid Light whenever he is solid. When vaporized, preserve the Jet-shaped cloud silhouette and purple Lucid Light.

## The Liquifier

- Role: antagonist who secretly controls phase states.
- Appearance: sleek androgynous supervillain with reflective mercury-like silver skin, teal glass armor plates, glowing cyan eyes, liquid-metal hair flowing backward, long clawed fingers, and a shifting body edge that can become liquid, mist, or solid.
- Power: can change anyone they touch into a different phase state, including vapor. They can also turn themselves into liquid or gas.
- Gas form: a dangerous silver-cyan mist with a faint villain face and claw-like tendrils.
- Expression range: smug invisibility, predatory reveal, panic when Sucker inhales them, terror as Void Man's pocket dimension opens.
- Continuity rule: keep the reflective silver body, cyan glow, and phase-shifting mist/liquid edges consistent.

## Ghost Cat

- Role: surviving phasing hero and psychic guide.
- Appearance: psychic humanoid cat alien woman, sleek lavender-gray fur, feline face, large luminous violet eyes, pointed ears, white and violet superhero bodysuit, translucent psychic aura, and graceful floating posture.
- Powers: phasing and telepathy, shown with semi-transparent body edges and violet psychic rings.
- Expression range: calm warning, alarm at the gas reveal, protective resolve.
- Continuity rule: keep her cat-alien face, lavender-gray fur, violet eyes, and white-violet suit visible.

## Leon

- Role: Jet's ghostly UFO ally and surviving phasing hero.
- Appearance: ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow, translucent hull, soft saucer lights, and a friendly protective presence.
- Powers: phasing and ghostly flight.
- Expression range: shown through tilted posture, glow intensity, and motion.
- Continuity rule: keep Leon as a green glowing ghost UFO, not a robot or conventional spaceship.

## Skelebot

- Role: skeletal robot speedster ally and surviving phasing hero.
- Appearance: humanoid skeletal robot with white bone-like armor over chrome joints, green speed lights, narrow glowing eyes, and runner's stance.
- Powers: super speed and emergency phasing in this dream.
- Expression range: alert, startled, fast protective movement.
- Continuity rule: keep the skeletal robot silhouette and green speed streaks visible.

## Vaporized Heroes

- Role: lost protectors of the superhero world.
- Appearance: many ghostly colored gas silhouettes in the storm clouds, each with faint superhero capes, masks, symbols, armor, or energy outlines.
- Restored form: bright varied superhero figures returning from lightning bolts into the city streets.
- Continuity rule: make it very clear that the vapor clouds are heroes waiting to be restored, not ordinary weather.

## Sucker

- Role: restored hero who pulls The Liquifier into position for the finish.
- Appearance: humanoid lollipop superhero with a round red-and-white spiral candy head, stick-like neck, colorful heroic bodysuit, white gloves, boots, and exaggerated inhaling cheeks.
- Power: super breath strong enough to inhale gas or liquid enemies from a distance.
- Expression range: determined, straining, victorious.
- Continuity rule: keep him obviously a humanoid lollipop, with spiral candy head and super breath gusts.

## Void Man

- Role: restored hero who contains The Liquifier.
- Appearance: pitch-black humanoid energy being with starry edges, glowing white eyes, long athletic silhouette, and a circular pocket dimension opening in his chest like a black galaxy rimmed with blue-purple light.
- Power: absorbs threats into the pocket dimension in his chest.
- Expression range: silent focus, decisive interception, contained victory.
- Continuity rule: keep him as a black energy being with the visible chest void.

## Superhero City

- Role: primary setting.
- Appearance: bright comic-book metropolis with damaged skyscrapers, empty hero billboards, abandoned capes, cracked streets, improvised HQ inside a battered office building, storm clouds full of vaporized heroes, and restored heroes returning with lightning.
- Corruption motif: silver-cyan mist from Liquifier and empty city streets where heroes should be.
