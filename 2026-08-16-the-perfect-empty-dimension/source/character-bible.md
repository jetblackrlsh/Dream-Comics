# Character Bible - The Perfect Empty Dimension

## Jet

- African-American adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears a black hoodie over a colorful “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with a single white stripe down each outer leg, clean white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build, expressive face, calm intelligence, compassionate restraint, and confident flying posture.
- Keep his skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, pants stripe, shoes, and cross-body bag visually consistent on every page.
- The dream contains three visually related states: ordinary Jet in the city, translucent observer-Jet in the Empty Dimension, and solid resident-Jet in the Empty Dimension. Never show more copies than the page script requires.

## Silonis - Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life

- Long futuristic crystal sword with a faceted rainbow blade, luminous prismatic edges, a high-technology metallic hilt, and a bright red scanner gem.
- Releases sparkling rainbow life energy rather than destructive fire.
- When fusing or separating life energy, use clear ribbons of rainbow light that visibly connect or divide the two beings without gore.
- Silonis is an artifact, not an ordinary steel sword and not a lightsaber.

## Porta portals

- Large circular doorways made from luminous violet-blue geometry, rainbow sparks, and a deep starry interior.
- The portal into the Empty Dimension reveals a peaceful, slightly uncanny digital neighborhood with glowing architectural edges and floating pixels.
- The portal is magical and cleanly circular, never a mechanical doorway.

## Rescued woman

- Adult woman with medium-brown skin, shoulder-length voluminous black curls, large brown eyes, an off-white long-sleeved top, blue jeans, and white sneakers.
- Frightened while trapped, horrified during the fusion, then visibly relieved after separation.
- She remains fully human before and after the fusion and is never injured or sexualized.

## Green slime monster

- Gigantic amorphous translucent emerald-green creature with a broad gelatinous body, expressive round eyes, a large mouth, and multiple flexible slime tentacles.
- Initially appears almost harmless and attention-seeking, then reveals the trapped woman.
- It is dangerous but not gory, decayed, skeletal, or demonic.
- After separation, it looks startled and chastened before being sent through Porta.

## Fused slime woman

- A single towering humanoid woman made from translucent emerald slime, visibly combining the rescued woman's curly-hair silhouette and human proportions with the monster's gelatinous material and expressive green features.
- Her face and body language communicate that both consciousnesses are horrified by the involuntary form.
- She must read as one fused being, not two people standing together and not a woman merely wearing slime.

## Observer-Jet and resident-Jet

- Observer-Jet is a translucent violet-blue version of Jet with softly glowing edges, calm watchful posture, and the complete standard outfit rendered like a spirit or hologram.
- Resident-Jet is solid and visually identical to ordinary Jet, but increasingly relaxed and warmly persuasive.
- On the siren page, resident-Jet is inviting rather than monstrous. The danger is the comfort of his argument, not a hidden transformation.

## Empty Dimension

- A peaceful, softly digital version of an ordinary neighborhood: modern homes, gardens, familiar people, glowing architectural outlines, floating pixel motes, and a serene violet-blue sky.
- The world is genuinely pleasant and accepting, not ruined, frightening, or obviously false.
- Its subtle uncanniness comes from perfect order, gentle digital light, and observer-Jet watching from outside ordinary interaction.

## Continuity rules

- All eight pages use bright, high-saturation modern-anime comic art with crisp linework and cinematic light.
- Story pages use unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes only.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, signatures, gore, extra limbs, duplicate unintended characters, or blank caption boxes.
