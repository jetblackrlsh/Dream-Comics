# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. He can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue, his body can harmlessly divide into glossy toy-like plastic segments and snap back together, like a living action figure.

## Jet's Action-Figure Form

Jet remains visibly himself, but his body separates into clean, non-gory, toy-like plastic pieces with ball joints, smooth seams, and glossy molded surfaces. The pieces should read as harmless magical immortality rather than injury.

Continuity notes: Never show gore, blood, exposed anatomy, or pain. The effect should look like a living action figure being popped apart and reassembled.

## Jet's Were-Rat Form

Jet transforms into an agile humanoid Were-Rat with dark-brown fur, expressive warm brown eyes, long dreadlock-like fur tufts, a lean athletic build, a pointed rat muzzle, clawed hands, digitigrade legs, and a long tail. Shreds or stylized echoes of Jet's black hoodie and belt bag can remain so the form connects visually to Jet.

Continuity notes: Keep the form wild and free rather than monstrous. It should look fast, alert, and lucid.

## Jet's Slime Form

Jet becomes a glossy translucent purple-black slime with faint golden glitter from his energy tether and small star-like specks of Lucid Light inside. The slime can squeeze between claws and seep through forest soil into tunnels.

Continuity notes: The slime should feel fluid, clever, and controlled, not gross or horror-based.

## Were-Owl Woman

Familiar-looking owl shapeshifter woman with large amber owl eyes, feathered hair and winglike arms, curved talons, and elegant purple and gold robes. She is powerful enough to snatch Were-Rat Jet from the forest, but her presence should remain mysterious instead of fully villainous.

Continuity notes: Keep the purple and gold robes consistent. Her silhouette should be recognizable: sweeping wings, owl face, talons, regal robes.

## Gem Golems

Underground constructs made of rocks, jewels, crystal plates, ore, and glowing mineral veins. Each golem has a slightly different gemstone palette: emerald, ruby, sapphire, amethyst, citrine, quartz, and obsidian. They are suspicious guardians of a jewel mine, then respectful subjects once they accept Jet as a rare construct.

Continuity notes: Make them heavy, faceted, and ancient. Their hands should be large enough to pull action-figure Jet apart and later carefully reassemble him.

## Forest And Jewel Mine

The forest is deep, moonlit, and saturated with lucid green and violet light. Beneath it, tunnels open into a vast underground mine glittering with jewel seams, crystal columns, gemstone bridges, ore veins, and a throne grown from many colored gems.

Continuity notes: The above-ground scenes should feel wild and kinetic. The mine scenes should feel luminous, mineral, and ceremonial.
