# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible "Dream Anime" graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries Silonis, the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword: a colorful futuristic crystal sword with a broad translucent faceted blade containing saturated red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet bands, a dark silver geometric technological hilt, tiny flowing sparks inside the blade, and a radiant rainbow-white aura.
- Carries Tetheron, the Golden Energy Tether: a flexible rope made entirely from glittering golden light. It forms a bright lasso when thrown and is never a solid cable, chain, grappling hook, or ordinary rope.
- Expression range: thoughtful comparison, imaginative curiosity, surprise, urgent focus, knowing confidence, amused defiance, protective determination, wonder, and affectionate recognition.
- Jet never wears a crown, cape, mask, helmet, conventional superhero costume, medieval armor, or unrelated clothing.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, shirt, pants, sneakers, belt bag, sword, and tether on every page.

## Silonis - Rainbow Crystal Life Sword

- One consistent independently living futuristic rainbow crystal sword.
- Broad translucent faceted blade containing saturated red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet bands; dark silver geometric hilt; rainbow-white aura; tiny flowing sparks inside the blade.
- Can hover beside Jet and angle itself expressively while communicating.
- Healing and restoration appear as warm rainbow-white life energy with strong gold and green currents, branching organic light, and tiny prismatic cells.
- Force fields are transparent spherical rainbow-white barriers threaded with gold and emerald life energy.
- When Jet rides Silonis, the sword flies horizontally beneath his white sneakers like a hoverboard while remaining unmistakably a sword.
- Silonis can heal injuries, animate objects, purify evil beings, cut physical or magical material, create force fields, fly independently, and carry Jet.
- Never depict Silonis as a metal broadsword, katana, ordinary fantasy sword, double-bladed weapon, or Savannah's purple-blue Death Sword.

## Tetheron - Golden Energy Tether

- A supple, reusable rope made entirely from bright golden glittering energy.
- It can extend to any required distance, retract afterward, and form a clean glowing lasso around the exact target Jet intends to catch.
- Its defining power is the creation of persistent magical bonds between connected things. A completed bond can remain after Tetheron is removed and reused elsewhere.
- Magical bonds appear as luminous gold lines, linked sigils, and synchronized pulses shared by both connected objects.
- Before awakening, Tetheron moves with uncanny precision but is treated as an artifact. After Silonis restores it, its light becomes warmer and more expressive, with a subtle constellation-like face or paired eye-lights forming briefly within the flowing gold energy. It remains a flexible tether rather than becoming humanoid.
- Severed pieces remain made of golden light, dimmed at the cut ends but still visible and recoverable.
- Never depict Tetheron as a solid cable, chain, grappling hook, whip with a handle, snake, or ordinary rope.

## Masked thief

- Tall, lean adult man wearing a fitted black suit, crisp white shirt, narrow red tie, black gloves, polished black shoes, and a black top hat wrapped with a vivid red ribbon.
- His entire face is concealed by a smooth porcelain-white demonic mask with elegant angular cheekbones, a narrow black mouth, two short swept-back horn shapes built into the mask, and deep crimson eye slits.
- His posture is theatrical, smug, and overconfident. He flourishes Tetheron like a common whip and celebrates too early after Jet cuts it.
- Expression must be conveyed through body language, tilted mask angles, and glowing eye slits because the mask never comes off.
- Never depict him as a literal demon, monster, clown, caped magician, armored villain, or unmasked human.

## Modern city

- A clean contemporary downtown resembling the waking world: glass-and-steel office towers, brick storefronts, sidewalks, crosswalks, street trees, parked cars, rooftop ledges, and bright natural daylight.
- The city is initially stable and realistic. When the thief steals Tetheron, the streets and buildings fracture into floating shards that dissolve into cosmic starlight.

## Storyverse cosmic void

- Deep indigo-black space filled with twinkling purple stars and many colorful translucent cosmic bubble universes at different distances.
- Each bubble contains a faint suggestion of a different story genre and world.
- The confrontation takes place on floating fragments of the former city until those fragments fully dissolve.
- Avoid conventional empty outer space, realistic solar-system planets, spacecraft, or inserting Jet's absent crew.

## Artifact comparison visions

- Silonis's capabilities appear in clear inset demonstrations: healing an injured person, awakening a wooden chair with friendly eyes and movement, purifying a shadowy hostile figure into a peaceful radiant form, projecting a spherical force field, and carrying Jet above the city.
- Tetheron's capabilities appear in clear inset demonstrations: connecting a laptop to a chair so glowing code and golden sigils animate the chair, leaving that bond intact after the tether is removed, lassoing a distant rooftop with perfect precision, swinging Jet between towers, and retracting into his hand.
- These are hypothetical visual demonstrations, not separate characters or unrelated subplots.

## Established Storyverse crew continuity

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's Storyverse team. None appears physically in this focused comic. Do not insert them into the city, bubble universes, reflections, demonstrations, battle, cover, or ending.
