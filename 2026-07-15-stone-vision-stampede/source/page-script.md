# Page Script — Stone Vision Stampede

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Jet, his equipment, the slime gorgons, and the nightclub must follow the character bible exactly on every appearance.

## Page 1 — Cover

Dynamic vertical cover composition inside the neon stone-creature nightclub. Jet runs at high speed inside a translucent golden-yellow spherical force field, his limbs stretched to maintain the sphere, long dreadlocks and open hoodie flying with the movement. His rainbow crystal sword is secured diagonally across his torso. Eight differently colored slime gorgons recoil and splatter harmlessly as the shield rolls through them, while pale stone-vision beams ricochet as flashes against the sphere without penetrating it. Purple star-filled Lucid Light spirals around the bright yellow belt. Visible cover text only: “STONE VISION STAMPEDE” and “JULY 15, 2026”. No issue number.

## Page 2 — Lucid Arrival

Four panels. A dark sleep-paralysis bedroom view gives way to a sparkling purple lucid threshold. Jet steps through alone in his exact hoodie outfit and arrives on the empty nightclub dance floor. He studies abandoned reinforced stools, carved golem-and-gargoyle wall reliefs, and wet multicolored slime trails. Final wide panel shows Jet small beneath the silent mirror ball, holding the rainbow sword but not yet drawing it.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis, crossed into the Storyverse, and arrived alone inside a nightclub.”
- “The lights and music still worked, but the dance floor was empty and every seat looked built for someone made of stone.”
- “Carvings of golems and gargoyles covered the walls, while fresh trails of colored slime crossed the floor.”
- “The club was not truly abandoned. Its new occupants were watching me from the darkness.”

## Page 3 — What Happened to the Stone Patrons

Four panels. Eight multicolored slime gorgons pour out from behind the DJ booth, ceiling arches, and dissolved stone furniture. A close view establishes their translucent colored bodies and writhing slime snake hair. One smug gorgon gestures toward a half-dissolved granite stool and a pitted gargoyle statue. A clear visual flashback inset shows the gorgons enveloping golem and gargoyle silhouettes and dissolving their stone surfaces, with the terrified stone patrons fleeing through the exit.

Caption text:

- “Eight gorgons flowed from the shadows, each one shaped from a different color of living slime.”
- “They bragged that golems and gargoyles had once filled this nightclub every night.”
- “The gorgons had wrapped their corrosive bodies around the stone patrons and feasted on the rock itself.”
- “Anyone who escaped had abandoned the club, leaving the predators in control of a place that was never meant for them.”

## Page 4 — The Trap Explained

Five panels. Jet faces the gorgons with the rainbow crystal sword now drawn. A rectangular quote-caption captures his direct question. One gorgon points at the sword while a small demonstration inset shows a single stone beam reflecting off its rainbow blade; another inset shows green life energy restoring a cracked stone patch on Jet's arm. The gorgons spread into a perfect circle around him. Every snake-haired head aims inward while Jet remains centered and alert.

Caption text:

- “I asked them, ‘If you turn prey to stone before eating it, why have you not already used stone vision on me?’”
- “They answered that my rainbow crystal sword of life could restore me after one successful blast.”
- “The same blade could also reflect one stone beam directly back at the gorgon who fired it.”
- “That was why no single gorgon wanted to attack first.”
- “Instead, all eight surrounded me and aimed their snake hair from every direction at once.”

## Page 5 — Too Many Beams

Four panels. Overhead panel: Jet is trapped in the precise center of the gorgon circle. Close panels show dozens of slime snakes opening their mouths as gray-white mineral light builds in each throat. Jet looks from the sword to the converging attack angles and understands that he cannot block them one by one. Final panel moves close to his calm eyes as purple star-filled Lucid Light begins shining beneath his hoodie and around his empty waist.

Caption text:

- “Their strategy was obvious: they would fire more stone blasts than I could reflect or heal from.”
- “A strike from every side would make the sword's usual defense useless.”
- “They had carefully planned around everything they could see in my hands.”
- “But they had mistaken my visible equipment for the full potential waiting inside my lucid mind.”

## Page 6 — The Spherical Defense

Five panels. Jet releases sparkling purple Lucid Light filled with tiny stars. It condenses around his waist into the bright yellow anti-gravity force-field belt. He thrusts both arms and both legs outward in a powerful star-like stance. A translucent golden-yellow sphere snaps into place around his entire body. All gorgons fire simultaneously from every direction; dozens of pale stone beams explode against the sphere and create gray-white mineral flashes, but none penetrate and none reflect back. Final panel shows Jet completely human and unharmed inside the shield while the frustrated gorgons recoil.

Caption text:

- “I used Lucid Light to summon a yellow anti-gravity force-field belt around my waist.”
- “Then I stretched my arms and legs outward, projecting its protection into a complete sphere around me.”
- “Every snake fired at the same instant, filling the nightclub with enough stone vision to petrify me many times over.”
- “The force field stopped every beam before it touched my body.”
- “It did not reflect the attacks, but perfect protection gave me time to create a different counterattack.”

## Page 7 — The Hamster-Ball Charge

Five panels. Jet plants his white sneakers against the inside surface of the golden sphere and begins running. The sphere rolls forward like a giant hamster ball, cracking only the already-damaged floor. It bowls through green and magenta gorgons, flattens cyan and orange gorgons into harmless slime puddles, and sends the remaining colors splashing away from the dance floor. Jet changes direction with athletic control and clears the entire circle. Final wide panel shows eight dazed defeated slime puddles trapped beneath overturned transparent drink tubs while Jet stands unharmed inside the stationary sphere.

Caption text:

- “I planted my feet against the inside of the shield and started running.”
- “The ball-shaped force field rolled with me, turning my defense into a giant lucid hamster ball.”
- “Their slime could dissolve stone, but it could not corrode a reality-warping barrier before I reached them.”
- “I rolled through every attacker, crushing their shapes into harmless puddles and ending the ambush.”
- “Once the last gorgon stopped moving, I contained them and sent word that the nightclub was safe again.”

## Page 8 — The Club Reopens

Four panels. Stone workers repair the pitted arches and reinforced furniture while contained slime gorgons are removed in sealed transparent tanks. A friendly granite golem switches on the neon sign and a limestone gargoyle takes the DJ booth. The dance floor fills with happy golems of many stones and winged gargoyles under colorful light. Jet stands among them in his exact hoodie outfit with the yellow belt still glowing and rainbow sword lowered peacefully. Final wide panel shows Jet leaving through the front doors while the stone patrons celebrate behind him beneath a clear interior title sign reading “STONE NIGHT”.

Caption text:

- “With the slime gorgons defeated, the golems and gargoyles returned to repair the home they had been forced to abandon.”
- “Stone seats were rebuilt, gargoyle lights shone again, and a golem DJ restarted the music.”
- “The nightclub reopened with its intended clientele filling the dance floor instead of fleeing from predators.”
- “I left the celebration knowing that one lucid idea had protected me and restored an entire community.”
