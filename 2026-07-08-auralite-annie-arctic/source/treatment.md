# Treatment

## Logline

After Jet becomes lucid through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in an arctic auralite city, he creates Insulation Annie with Lucid Light so she can save the igloo people from crystal-armored dinosaurs, but Annie's rescue requires her to seal a geothermal sinkhole with her own back forever.

## Central Conflict

The arctic city depends on auralite crystals and careful cave mining, but the same crystals hide a deadly danger. Dinosaur predators with auralite-like armor live in warm underground caves and normally stay away from the frozen city. When a sinkhole opens into a geothermal vent, the people celebrate the sudden warmth before realizing it has invited the dinosaurs into their home. Jet can create a hero with Lucid Light, but the hero he creates must choose a protection strong enough to outlast the attack.

## Theme

A heroic sacrifice can protect a whole community, but it can also trap the hero in a life they did not get to choose.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins curious and alone, exploring the beauty and community of the arctic city. The mood turns urgent when the sinkhole transforms from blessing to threat. Jet feels responsible and uses Lucid Light to create Insulation Annie as the city's champion. Annie acts bravely, accepts the cost, and becomes both beloved and imprisoned. The ending moves from gratitude to sorrow as generations pass, Annie is forgotten, and Jet reflects that his next created hero deserves a happier ending.

## Ending

The final page shows the city safe years later, with only the old insulation plug remaining where Annie once lay. Jet stands under the aurora and admits that Annie's protection lasted, but her fate was cruel because she never got to choose a home of her own.
