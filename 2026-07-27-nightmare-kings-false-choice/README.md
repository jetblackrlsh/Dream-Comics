# 07/27/2026 - The Nightmare King's False Choice

Dream Comics entry for July 27, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet flies above a sinister nightmare planet built from black mirrored cubes glowing red from within. The King of Nightmares, a towering humanoid mass of burnt vines and thorns, orders Jet to destroy the world. He claims that doing so will save sleeping people from lethal nightmares, then accuses Jet of allowing those deaths when Jet refuses.

Because Jet knows the infinite Storyverse contains countless nightmare worlds, he recognizes the command as a false mission. Destroying one world cannot eliminate every nightmare, and an endless crusade of destruction would only corrupt the hero undertaking it. Jet's first attack fails when the burnt-vine king proves resistant to fire, traps him in burning thorns, and nearly steals the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword.

Jet saves the sword with his overlooked Golden Energy Tether. The tether creates a mystical bond that lets him activate the Life Sword from a distance, burst free behind a force field, and heal his wounds. He then rejects the false choice between destroying the planet and doing nothing. Instead, he purifies the nightmare beings into dreams. The transformed dreams join Jet, dismantle the Nightmare King, and scatter his thorns across the Storyverse.
