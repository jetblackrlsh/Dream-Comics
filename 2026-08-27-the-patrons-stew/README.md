# 08/27/2026 - The Patron's Stew

Dream Comics entry for August 27, 2026.

Inside a dark swamp, Jet decides to become a warlock. He chooses Apollo as his patron, Second Brain as his familiar, and the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life as his arcane focus. Apollo accepts the unusual arrangement and immediately sends Jet and Second Brain into a flooded mineshaft beyond the reach of sunlight.

After using the Sword to escape contaminated still water, Jet is carried from the mine by a giant bat trying to steal the artifact. Apollo stuns the creature with sunlight, allowing Second Brain to merge with the Sword and charge its blade with purple lucid lightning. Jet severs the bat's wings without killing it—only to discover that Apollo wanted the wings for stew.

When Jet considers healing the bat, Second Brain warns that instant restoration could enable Apollo to harvest the same creature forever. Jet chooses not to turn compassion into a mechanism for repeated exploitation, leaving the bat to recover naturally.
