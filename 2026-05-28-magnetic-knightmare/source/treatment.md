# Treatment

## Logline

After a magnetic Knightmare slips one of its gauntlets inside Jet's own rainbow shield dome, Jet escapes by shrinking past normal reality, looping into a sub-quantum copy of the Storyverse, and crushing the armored predator with a universe-shaking stomp.

## Central Conflict

Jet is fighting an opponent whose control does not depend on an intact body. The magnetic Knightmare can weaponize separated armor pieces, recall them, and keep attacking even after Jet slices them apart.

## Theme

Control has a scale limit. When an enemy dominates every visible piece of a battlefield, the answer may be to leave the enemy's scale entirely.

## Ending

Jet compresses his shield and shrinks through Lucid Light until he drops below ordinary reality. He reappears as a giant in a sub-quantum copy of the same Storyverse bubble universe and ends the fight with one colossal stomp. Because the tiny copy is entangled with the macroscopic universe, the stomp destroys the bubble universe at both scales.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alert and tactical, recognizing the Knightmare's fighting style as a variation on an old Savannah battle. The fight becomes claustrophobic when his own shield traps him with the gauntlet. Instead of panicking, Jet turns the question of control into a question of scale, then ends with curiosity and excitement about whatever Knightmare could possibly exceed this one's persistence.
