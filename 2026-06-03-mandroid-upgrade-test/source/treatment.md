# Treatment

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet returns to Leon's robotics lab with upgrade ideas for Superhero Mandroid, then works with Johnson to test nitro rocket fists, light propulsion rocket fists, and nanite-enlarged fists against three material grades.

## Central Conflict

Jet and Johnson want to make Superhero Mandroid stronger without making him unsafe or clumsy. Each upgrade solves one problem while creating another: speed raises impact, light propulsion raises risk, and nanite size raises damage while adding weight that slows aim and control.

## Theme

Power needs calibration. A hero's upgrade is only successful when the team understands its limits before battle.

## Ending

Superhero Mandroid shatters grade three material with light propulsion, then proves that nanite-enlarged fists can hit harder even with slower aim. Jet calls the upgrades a success, but the final image keeps the training holograms active, showing that the new gear still needs practice before field use.

## Emotional Arc

Jet starts excited by a day spent thinking and writing about Superhero Mandroid. In the dream, that excitement becomes practical engineering work with Johnson. The tests move from confidence to caution as the nanites reveal weight issues. By the end, Jet feels satisfied because the team has turned an idea into a useful tool without pretending it is fully mastered.
