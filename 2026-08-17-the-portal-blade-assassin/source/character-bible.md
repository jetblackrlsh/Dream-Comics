# Character Bible - The Portal-Blade Assassin

## Jet

- Black adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears a black hoodie over a colorful “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with a single white stripe down each outer leg, clean white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build, calm intelligence, curious expression while theorizing, and focused confidence during combat.
- Keep his skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, pants stripe, shoes, and cross-body bag visually consistent on every page.

## Silonis - Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life

- Long futuristic crystal sword with a faceted rainbow blade, luminous prismatic edges, a high-technology metallic hilt, and a bright red scanner gem.
- Releases sparkling rainbow life energy rather than destructive fire.
- When sensing the assassin, show thin rainbow life-energy contours revealing the enemy's next arrival point.
- Silonis is an advanced crystal artifact, not an ordinary steel sword and not a lightsaber.

## Portal-blade assassin

- Androgynous adult figure with a lean athletic build and unreadable body language.
- Wears layered midnight-indigo ninja armor, a deep hood, a smooth angular black mask with one narrow violet luminous visor, a short dark cloak, armored gloves, and silent boots.
- Wields a sleek blue-violet portal-crystal sword with translucent geometric facets and a dark technological hilt.
- Every arrival sheds violet shards and circular portal geometry; every disappearance leaves a thin blue afterimage.
- Keep the mask, armor, cloak, visor, blade shape, and blue-violet palette consistent. Never reveal a specific gender or redesign the figure.
- In the Empty Dimension, the assassin remains masked but relaxes, kneels peacefully, and sets the portal blade on the ground.

## Magical study

- Vast private study blending ancient magic with advanced technology: towering bookshelves, open grimoires, glowing spell diagrams, crystal specimens, brass instruments, holographic screens, and circular runes across a dark polished floor.
- Warm amber lamps contrast with cyan holograms and rainbow light from Silonis.
- Written diagrams can show symbolic sword silhouettes, crystals, and portal geometry, but avoid random readable decorative text.

## Portal-crystal world

- Alien planet of towering cyan, violet, and sapphire crystals beneath a star-filled sky.
- Industrial mining platforms, protected workers, armored heroic guards, cargo teleporters, and spontaneous portals opening to unpredictable environments.
- The world is beautiful, valuable, and visibly dangerous rather than evil or ruined.

## Porta

- Large circular doorway made from luminous violet-blue geometry, rainbow sparks, and a deep starry interior.
- Jet casts it with one hand while holding Silonis in the other.
- It is magical and cleanly circular, never a mechanical doorway.

## Empty Dimension

- A serene boundless world of soft violet-blue grass, still reflective water, floating lights, warm stars, and distant minimalist architecture.
- It feels safe, private, and accepting—not like a prison, void, dungeon, or wasteland.
- The final image must make the assassin's decision not to return emotionally legible through relaxed posture and the unused sword.

## Continuity rules

- All eight pages use bright, high-saturation modern-anime comic art with crisp linework and cinematic light.
- Story pages use unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes only.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, signatures, gore, extra limbs, duplicate unintended characters, or blank caption boxes.
