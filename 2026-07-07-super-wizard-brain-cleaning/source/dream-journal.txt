I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void.

I look like a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, and warm brown eyes. I started feeling a weird itch sensation on my brain.

Super Wizard Man summoned a mystical salon chair and invited me to sit down for a brain cleaning.

Super Wizard Man scratched, brushed, scrubbed, and washed my brain for me.

The experience was unlike anything I had ever felt before and it helped to relieve the itchy sensation.

It was quite relaxing.

I hope Super Wizard Man can find a way to survive while making a difference in a time that does not truly understand his good intentions.
