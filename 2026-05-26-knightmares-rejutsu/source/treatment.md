# Treatment

## Title

05/26/2026: Knightmares and Rejutsu

## Logline

Jet enters a sleep-paralysis lucid dream already surrounded by faceless armored Knightmares, then invents Rejutsu by refusing magical attacks and using his rainbow crystal sword only to heal his fists until his punches shatter anti-magic armor.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to keep his streak of knight battles alive while proving he can overcome anti-magic defenses without magical offense. The Knightmares are identical, animate suits of reflective armor that exist to absorb punishment and keep advancing.

## Theme

The body can become the spell. Rejutsu turns healing into discipline, letting recovery and repeated effort become the source of power.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins amused and curious about why these faceless knights are so satisfying to fight. The first punches hurt badly, forcing him to commit to the experiment. As the rainbow sword heals his damaged fists, pain becomes feedback, feedback becomes adaptation, and adaptation becomes a martial art. By the end, Jet is not merely winning a brawl; he has named both the enemy and the technique.

## Ending

The final Knightmare collapses into scattered armor as Jet stands with glowing healed fists and the rainbow sword lowered at his side. In waking reflection, he names the faceless enemies Knightmares and the healing-based fighting style Rejutsu.
