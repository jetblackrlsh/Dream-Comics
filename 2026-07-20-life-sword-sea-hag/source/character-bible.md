# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries the Life Sword, a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword glowing with living prismatic light, and a flexible energy tether resembling a rope made from golden glittering light clipped to his belt.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy shining like a night sky full of stars. Lucid Light claws are large translucent violet claw-gauntlets wrapped around his hands, filled with stars and radiating waves of purple-white heat.
- Flight leaves a faint purple starry wake, but Jet never wears a cape, mask, conventional superhero costume, armor, or helmet.
- Expression range: lucid joy, recognition, hopeful resolve, shock, focused improvisation, investigative curiosity, pain, determined concentration, victorious relief.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact outfit, hair, beard, skin tone, belt bag, sword, and tether whenever visible. Damage may briefly scuff his clothes on pages 6-7, but the Life Sword heals him and his outfit is intact by page 8.

## Blue slime king of Atlantis

- The same shapeshifting cobalt-blue slime creature Jet purified in an earlier dream, now the king of the sea.
- Current form: a gigantic, muscular but smooth faceless humanoid made entirely of translucent cobalt-blue slime, towering above buildings and ships. He has no eyes, mouth, nose, clothing, or separate crown.
- His head itself rises into five rounded crown points, making a crown-shaped silhouette that symbolizes his status. Cyan currents and soft golden-rainbow traces move inside his translucent body, recalling his purification.
- Can grow even larger so Jet can stand or crouch securely on one broad shoulder. His hands and proportions remain humanoid, with liquid ripples and droplets at the edges.
- Expression must be communicated through posture: welcoming open palm, worried lowered shoulders, protective stance, and victorious raised fist.
- Continuity rule: never give him a human face, ordinary skin, armor, royal robes, or a removable crown. He remains living blue slime in every appearance.

## Pollution kaiju

- An enormous ocean mutant assembled by contamination: a hulking asymmetrical sea monster taller than the slime king, with black-green tar-coated hide, barnacle armor, jagged coral horns, glowing toxic yellow-green cracks, smokestack-like spines, and tentacles tangled with ghost nets and plastic debris.
- Its broad crocodilian head has multiple toxic green eyes and a cavernous mouth exhaling oily vapor. It rises from dark polluted water beside the clean coastal city.
- It should look tragically corrupted by pollution rather than demonic or undead. When destroyed by purified life energy, it dissolves into clean water, harmless sea plants, rainbow light, and reclaimed debris without gore.

## Sea hag

- Ancient aquatic witch with blue-green wrinkled skin, a hooked face, long tangled white-green seaweed hair, yellow eyes, pointed ears, and a dark teal ragged dress decorated with shells and kelp.
- Flies sidesaddle on a crooked brown wooden witch's broom. She moves with hunched predatory speed and clutches the Life Sword in both hands after stealing it.
- Her magic appears sickly emerald-green and raises skeletal pirates through seawater and cracked streets. She has no wand, crown, wings, or modern clothing.
- Continuity rule: keep the same teal dress, seaweed hair, blue-green skin, shell ornaments, and crooked broom through pages 1, 3, 4, and 5. Her fall is shown at a safe distance with no gore.

## Enchanted broom

- A crooked, weathered brown wooden witch's broom with a curved handle, iron binding bands, and dense dark straw bristles tipped with faint green sparks.
- After Jet bonds it with the golden tether, a small golden knot of light wraps the handle and its green aura becomes mixed with friendly gold.
- It can fly independently, sweep dust into a smoke screen, and wield its handle like a staff. It has no face, eyes, limbs, or speech.
- Continuity rule: the golden tether bond remains visible from page 5 onward, even when the tether stretches or releases back to Jet's hand.

## Skeletal pirate warriors

- Undead human skeletons in varied but consistent decayed pirate gear: torn navy and burgundy coats, tricorn hats, belts, boots, rusted cutlasses, and scraps of seaweed.
- Sickly green life energy glows inside their rib cages and eye sockets while animated. The larger outdoor army collapses when Jet drains that borrowed energy; the smaller cove garrison is battered apart by the broom without gore.
- They never use guns, modern equipment, or speech bubbles.

## Red crab familiar

- A towering humanoid red crab monster, about twice Jet's height, with a broad armored crimson carapace, long jointed legs, glowing orange eyes, and two oversized black-red cursed claws covered in violet runes.
- Fights upright like a powerful warrior and swipes precisely at Jet's artifacts. Heat from Jet's attacks makes the shell glow orange from within.
- Molted form: a pale pink, soft-bodied humanoid crab with the same proportions and orange eyes but no hard shell or cursed outer claws. It is vulnerable but not gory.
- Continuity rule: keep the crab silhouette, red shell, orange eyes, rune-covered cursed claws, and two-times-Jet scale consistent until the visible molt on page 8.

## Coastal city, ocean, and treasure cove

- The city is a bright Atlantis-adjacent coastal metropolis of white, turquoise, and coral towers, sea bridges, plazas, and a vast harbor. Civilians flee safely; no injuries are shown.
- The ocean shifts from polluted black-green near the kaiju to sparkling blue after the Life Sword blast.
- The hidden cove is a vast torchlit sea cave packed with gold coins, jeweled chests, ship figureheads, anchors, and a moored ruined pirate skiff. Dust from the broom can fill the cavern while gold highlights keep the action readable.

## Established crew continuity

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's Storyverse team. None appear physically in this comic because Jet arrives alone. Do not insert them into crowds, skies, reflections, pirate forces, or background cameos.
