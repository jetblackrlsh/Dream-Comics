# Treatment

## Logline

Surrounded by slime gorgons who plan to overwhelm his rainbow sword with simultaneous stone vision, lucid dreamer Jet reveals a hidden force-field power and turns his defense into a rolling counterattack that restores a stolen nightclub to its golem and gargoyle community.

## Central conflict

The slime gorgons believe they have solved Jet's visible defenses: one stone beam might be reflected or healed, but a circle of beams should petrify him faster than his rainbow crystal sword can respond. Jet must survive a coordinated attack from every direction and defeat enemies whose fluid bodies already consumed the club's stone patrons.

## Theme

An enemy who judges only what is visible cannot account for lucid potential. Jet wins by revealing a new power at the exact moment it is needed and by transforming total defense into decisive action.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins alert but curious in the deserted nightclub. The gorgons' cheerful confession turns curiosity into disgust, and their tactical explanation creates immediate danger. Once surrounded, Jet calmly understands both their strategy and their mistake. His Lucid Light summons the right tool, his shield makes him untouchable, and his rolling charge converts pressure into momentum. He ends satisfied not merely because he escaped, but because the club's original community can return.

## Ending

Golems and gargoyles reopen the repaired nightclub beneath a new stone-friendly sign. Jet stands among the celebrating patrons with his rainbow sword lowered and the yellow force-field belt still glowing, watching the place become what it was always supposed to be.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: Jet races inside a golden spherical shield through a neon nightclub while slime gorgons fire stone beams around him.
2. Jet becomes lucid through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in the strangely empty club.
3. Multicolored slime gorgons reveal themselves and boast that they consumed the golems and gargoyles.
4. Jet asks why they have not petrified him; they explain the rainbow sword's healing and reflection advantages while surrounding him.
5. The gorgons charge many stone blasts at once, and Jet recognizes both their strategy and their false assumption.
6. Jet summons the yellow anti-gravity belt with Lucid Light and blocks every blast inside a spherical force field.
7. Jet runs inside the shield like a hamster ball and crushes the slime gorgons.
8. The club reopens for golems and gargoyles, with Jet witnessing the restored community.
