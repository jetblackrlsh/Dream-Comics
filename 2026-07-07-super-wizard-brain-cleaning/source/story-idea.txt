Jet becomes lucid through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in Salem during the witch trials. Super Wizard Man, a wizard superhero inspired by a comic from the future, is being burned at the stake by an angry mob. Jet uses his golden energy tether to pull down a storm cloud, extinguish the fire, and scare the mob away. Jet and Super Wizard Man escape into a hidden underground lair, where Super Wizard Man explains that a future comic inspired him to help people with magic before the world understands superheroes.

Jet warns him that helping people with magic is good, but Salem cannot yet understand what he is trying to be. Then Jet feels a strange itchy sensation on his brain. Super Wizard Man summons a mystical salon chair and performs a magical brain cleaning, scratching, brushing, scrubbing, and washing Jet's brain until the itch fades. Jet leaves relaxed and grateful, hoping Super Wizard Man can survive while making a difference in a time that misunderstands his good intentions.
