The Crystal-Hungry Host

Jet enters a nightclub where every guest chooses a humanoid dragon host. He selects a customizable robotic dragon, provoking the organic dragon hosts, who fear that the tireless machine will leave them hungry. Jet solves their immediate need by calling Leon to deliver cups of molten gold. Inside the VIP lounge, however, the robot reveals that it eats magical crystal and tries to consume Silonis. When illusion cannot fool its sensors, Jet removes the robot's three power stones with geomancy, returns them to the other hosts, and leaves with sympathy for a machine driven by hunger rather than malice.
