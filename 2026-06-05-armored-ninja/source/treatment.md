# Treatment

## Logline

Alone in a dream city and carrying the recent legend of Superhero Mandroid in his mind, Jet uses Lucid Light to create a grounded street-level hero from scratch: Armored Ninja, a cobalt-armored shield fighter built for rails, curbs, momentum, and human-scale protection.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to make a new hero worthy of standing beside Superhero Mandroid without simply copying him. The city itself answers with a street-level crisis: knife-wielding attackers and dangerous rooftops where raw power would be too much, but speed, restraint, and smart defense can save people.

## Theme

Heroism can scale down without becoming smaller. Superhero Mandroid protects dreamers with cosmic technology; Armored Ninja protects one city block at a time with grounded skill, momentum, and courage.

## Ending

Armored Ninja rejects the need to be a lesser version of Superhero Mandroid. After stopping a rooftop ambush, he stands on a rail above the city with his carbon shield returned to his arm, ready to become the city's own protector.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins curious and searching, not for a known ally but for a new role. He shapes Armored Ninja carefully, watches him prove himself under pressure, and ends satisfied that the dream has produced a hero with his own identity.

## Cast

- Jet: the lucid dreamer and creator, alone in the city, wielding Lucid Light and imagination.
- Armored Ninja: the new hero, grounded, agile, cobalt armored, shield-bearing, inspired by Superhero Mandroid.
- Superhero Mandroid: present as an influence, memory, and brief symbolic vision rather than the main actor.
- City attackers: anonymous knife-wielding hand-to-hand criminals who create a street-level test without overwhelming the origin story.
