At a cave entrance, Jet is restrained by a humanoid vine elemental crowned with fire, which demands Silonis—the rainbow crystal Life Sword. Its rage causes its own flaming crown to consume its vine body. Jet compassionately separates and restores the incompatible halves as independent plant and fire elementals, only for both to betray him and attempt to steal Silonis. The living sword acts on its own to save Jet and defeat them. Jet leaves with the realization that proximity to the power of life makes other beings behave differently and risk everything for possession of it.
