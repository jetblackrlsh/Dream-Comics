# Treatment

## Logline

When Mr. Stopwatch turns a crowded nightclub into a ticking trap, Jet uses his Lucid Light Watch to transform through Skelebot, Tecton, and Leon variants, saving the guests from a mobile android bomb and delivering the saboteur to justice.

## Central Conflict

Jet must solve a crisis where speed alone is not enough. The threat begins as a simple evacuation, becomes a hidden mobile bomb, and finally becomes a pursuit of the person who engineered the attack.

## Theme

Heroism is not only about having the fastest answer. Jet survives by changing tactics as soon as the truth of the danger changes.

## Ending

Jet becomes LLV-Leon, lifts Mr. Stopwatch with a ghostly green tractor beam, and drops him into the authorities' waiting containment field. The saved guests watch from the street while Jet flies into the Storyverse void.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins confident but alone. Mr. Stopwatch's wordplay forces him from reactive speed into tactical protection, then into decisive capture. By the end, Jet has proven that the Lucid Light Watch is not just a power source, but a way to choose the right crew lesson under pressure.
