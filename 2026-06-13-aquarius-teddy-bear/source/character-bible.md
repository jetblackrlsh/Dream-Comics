# Character Bible

## Jet, Hippie Werewolf Form

Jet appears in this dream as a tall blonde werewolf with golden-blonde shaggy mane hair, pointed wolf ears, amber-brown eyes, a full wolf muzzle, expressive brows, tawny fur, and a strong athletic build. He wears round tinted hippie sunglasses pushed up on his forehead, a loose tie-dye shirt, a fringed suede vest, faded bell-bottom jeans with patchwork flowers, bead necklaces, peace-symbol jewelry, and bare clawed wolf feet. His posture is gentle and open until the teddy bear is attacked, then his rage shows through clenched teeth and bristling fur. Because of his hippie form, he does not use weapons or conventional strikes; he fights with a peace-sign eye poke, an overwhelming hug, and a shove through the window.

## Teddy Bear

The teddy bear is small, worn, soft, and honey-brown with button eyes, stitched seams, a faded blue bow, and a faint hidden aura. It is not alive and does not speak. After its head is sliced off, its body leaks colorful radiant energy like rainbow liquid light, proving there was more to it than anyone could see.

## Claw Monster

The monster is a tall dark-scaled humanoid predator with massive hooked claws, long arms, a narrow snarling face, glowing sickly yellow eyes, jagged teeth, and claw marks across its own body from scaling the castle wall. Its silhouette must emphasize oversized claws. It enters through a high castle window and uses one sweeping claw slash to behead the teddy bear. After Jet's improvised fight, it tumbles backward out of the window in shock.

## Leon

Leon is Jet's ghostly UFO: a flying saucer with an ethereal green glow, translucent edges, and a protective haunted aura. In the closing page, Leon hovers outside or above the castle window as the crew arrives.

## Johnson

Johnson is an orange humanoid goldfish with large fish eyes, fins, and a blue astronaut suit without a helmet. He looks surprised but compassionate when he sees what happened.

## Second Brain

Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. He glows with sparkling purple energy like a night sky full of stars. He studies the leaking teddy bear energy with immediate recognition.

## Skelebot

Skelebot is a humanoid speedster robot with skeletal mechanical limbs, a sleek skull-like metal head, and athletic running posture. He reacts with quick startled body language.

## Overdrive

Overdrive is a chrome robot with spiky slicked-back yellow hair, glowing green eyes, a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. Their matter-manipulating hands glow faintly as they stare at the bear's energy.

## Savannah

Savannah is a pale-skinned cyborg woman with long black hair and bangs covering her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifiers. She carries the Death Sword, a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword, lowered in cautious surprise.

## Tecton

Tecton is a green-skinned muscular humanoid cat ogre barbarian wearing a brown loincloth and a silver baldric. He is large, protective, and visibly puzzled by the teddy bear battlefield.

## Chipper

Chipper is a small levitating silver crab robot drone with compact legs and glowing sensor eyes. He hovers near the leaking bear, scanning the rainbow energy.

## Lucid Light And Storyverse Visuals

Lucid Light is sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. The Storyverse is a cosmic void filled with bubble universes, hinted outside the castle and through Leon's green glow rather than dominating the castle scenes.
