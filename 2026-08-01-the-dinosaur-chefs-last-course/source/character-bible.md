# Character Bible - The Dinosaur Chef's Last Course

## Jet

- Black man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hood.
- Wears an open black hoodie over a clearly visible colorful “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic but natural build. No crown, cape, mask, helmet, or conventional superhero costume until Page 8, when Lucid Light forms a translucent purple power-armor shell over this same outfit.
- Expression range: curious observation, skeptical humor, firm compassion, pain without panic, determined focus, cautious hope, disappointment, and decisive resolve.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy filled with tiny stars, like a living night sky.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hooded outfit, shirt, pants, sneakers, and belt bag on every page.

## Silonis - Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life

- One consistent living futuristic rainbow crystal sword.
- Broad translucent faceted blade containing saturated red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet bands; dark silver geometric technological hilt; radiant rainbow-white aura; tiny flowing sparks inside the blade.
- Healing appears as warm rainbow-white life energy with gold and green currents that closes Jet's stab wound immediately.
- Force fields appear as transparent faceted rainbow crystal threaded by gold and emerald light.
- Never depict Silonis as a metal broadsword, katana, ordinary fantasy sword, double-bladed weapon, or Savannah's purple-blue Death Sword.

## Tetheron - Golden Energy Tether

- One long flexible rope made entirely from glittering golden light, with a bright lasso loop and streams of small star-like particles.
- It can encircle separate beings at once and create a visible magical mind-to-mind connection between them.
- During the empathy transfer, translucent memory images travel along the tether: frightened dinosaur eyes, grieving silhouettes, and the chef's past outdoor kitchen scenes.
- Never depict Tetheron as a physical rope, whip, chain, cable, noose, or weapon cutting into bodies.

## The Dinosaur Chef - Human form

- Tall adult woman wearing a rustic tan-brown wicker dress with woven texture and a very wide matching wicker sun hat.
- Dense clusters of bright red berries dangle from every side of the hat's brim like natural ornaments.
- The hat hides her entire face in supernatural black shadow. Only two glowing red eyes and brighter red pupils are visible; no other facial features can be seen.
- Carries a large chef's knife only after Jet refuses her demand. Her gestures shift from casually inviting to coercive, hostile, and predatory.
- No apron, chef's hat, modern clothing, visible human face, crown, armor, or shoes.

## The Dinosaur Chef - Giant dinosaur form

- The same woman transformed into an enormous green reptilian humanoid dinosaur with powerful legs, clawed hands, a long balancing tail, scaly hide, and a broad predatory dinosaur head.
- She retains the same wicker dress and the exact wide wicker sun hat with dangling bright red berries. Her glowing red eyes remain visible beneath the brim.
- She towers above the jungle canopy but has a fixed maximum size and cannot fly.
- Her human-to-dinosaur transformation should read clearly as one continuous character, not a separate monster.
- Avoid gore, exposed anatomy, torn-off clothing, a quadrupedal body, wings, feathers, horns, or multiple heads.

## The small dinosaur

- Harmless puppy-sized-to-calf-sized bipedal dinosaur with a rounded dopey face, blank gentle eyes, short forearms, sturdy hind legs, and a thick tail.
- Mango-like colors: leaf green across the back and head, rich orange across the belly and flanks, with softer yellow highlights.
- Never attacks, bares teeth, or behaves aggressively. Its innocence and confusion must remain visually obvious.
- During Tetheron's spell, its frightened emotional perspective becomes visible even though its physical posture remains small and vulnerable.

## Prehistoric jungle and outdoor kitchen

- Dense ancient jungle with giant ferns, cycads, mossy trunks, humid golden-green light, distant volcanic rock, and hints of larger dinosaurs far away.
- The outdoor kitchen is an impossible but fully equipped open-air cooking station among the plants: dark wooden counters, stone worktops, hanging metal utensils, iron pans, knives, a grill, and baskets of red berries.
- Do not depict butchered dinosaurs, meat gore, carcasses, blood-covered tools, or graphic food preparation.

## Storyverse

- Deep indigo cosmic space filled with sparkling purple stars and many colorful translucent bubble universes, each containing the faint suggestion of a different world.
- Jet reaches it only in the final page after ascending beyond the Dinosaur Chef's reach.

## Continuity exclusions

Jet's established crew—Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper—does not physically appear in this focused dream. Do not insert them into the jungle, kitchen, memories, cover, or Storyverse ending.
