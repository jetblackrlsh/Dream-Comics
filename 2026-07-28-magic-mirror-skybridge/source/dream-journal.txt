Dream journal entry dated 07/28/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse, a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes floating together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025.

My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I. Leon is my Ghostly UFO, an ethereal green flying saucer that first appeared on 06/11/2025. Johnson resembles an orange humanoid goldfish and wears a helmetless blue astronaut suit; I befriended him on 08/03/2025. Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light whom I created on 09/06/2025; on that day he helped me discover the wider Storyverse. Overdrive is a chrome robot with spiky slicked-back yellow hair, glowing green eyes, yellow clothing and matter-manipulation powers; they were created on 10/20/2025. Lucid Light is sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a star-filled night sky, and I discovered it on 07/19/2025. Skelebot is a humanoid robot speedster created by Johnson on 09/21/2025.

Savannah is a pale-skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, forehead-covering bangs, glowing red eyes, a futuristic silver bodysuit, and the purple-and-blue crystalline Death Sword. Her father Tecton is a green-skinned muscular humanoid cat-ogre barbarian who wears a brown loincloth and silver baldric. Chipper is my small levitating silver crab robot. They met me during adventures through the Super Cathedral and became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a “Dream Anime” graphic tee. I wear black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag, and I have warm brown eyes. My Rainbow Crystal Sword resembles a colorful futuristic crystal sword. My Golden Energy Tether resembles a rope made of golden glittering light.

I found myself in a medieval fantasy town with an unusual goal: I wanted to discover whether the modern advancements we had achieved through artificial intelligence could be recreated through magic in a medieval world.

One of the first things that came to mind was the magic mirror from old fairy tales. Someone could speak to a mirror, cause a face to appear within it, ask questions, hear a voice in response, see images, and sometimes reveal distant events like a live video feed.

The similarities to generative AI fascinated me. Speaking to the mirror resembled using an AI voice assistant. Its ability to create or display images resembled image-generation models, while its visions of faraway places felt like video generation even though it was showing real events.

Golems were an equally obvious parallel to robots. If this fantasy world could combine intelligent magic mirrors with magically animated golems, then it possessed the equivalents of generative AI and robotics. I still needed to understand how medieval villagers could create these things, so I visited the town's enchantress.

The enchantress wore a tall pointed hat and a sparkling blue outfit. Her long messy hair was bright sky blue, her eyes glittered with blue magical light, and her extremely pale skin made the vibrant colors stand out dramatically.

We discussed what would be needed to construct intelligent magic mirrors, golems, and other magical technologies. We discovered a mine filled with life stones directly beneath the town. The stones contained the same essential substance as my Rainbow Crystal Sword—the Life Sword.

The Rainbow Crystal Sword is one of the most important artifacts in the Storyverse. It can bring things to life and directly opposes the Death Sword. Together they are among the most powerful objects anywhere in the Storyverse. Until that moment I had not seriously considered that the Life Sword might have been forged from naturally occurring material. I had treated it as singular rather than something created from a substance that might be mined in certain realities.

Using the mine's crystals, the enchantress and I transformed the town. Large magic mirrors were installed in homes and public areas so villagers could speak to them, ask questions, and receive useful information. We also created portable pocket mirrors that acted as personal magical companions. The magic mirrors became intelligent assistants, while golems performed physical tasks.

At first, everything went remarkably well. Then a villain named Drillov arrived. He wore a futuristic mechanical suit with two enormous drills in place of hands. He drilled near the village to break into the mine. His plan was to steal enough crystals to create his own Life Sword and sell it to the highest bidder.

I used Lucid Light to summon a gigantic glowing hand construct. It seized Drillov and crushed his mechanical suit around him. I was careful not to kill him, but the attack completely disabled his equipment.

The enchantress initially doubted that Drillov could recreate the Rainbow Crystal Sword because it was not powered by magic alone; it also incorporated advanced futuristic technology. I pointed out that Drillov's drilling suit was clearly not medieval magic or life-crystal technology. He already had access to advanced machinery. If he combined that technology with life stones, he might create something extraordinarily dangerous. Even an imperfect copy could be a powerful life-based weapon sold to terrible people.

Drillov soon became the least of our concerns. Three colossal purple alien robot titans crashed down on the outskirts of the village. Their unbelievable size made the dream feel hyper-real. They seemed taller than any building or mountain I had ever seen, and the entire village looked microscopic beside them. Compared with them, we were ants.

I did not know exactly what they wanted. They may have detected the life crystals and come to claim them. Even if every village golem attacked at once, I doubted they could do any meaningful damage.

I remembered that programming had helped me defeat overwhelming threats whenever magic alone was insufficient. If I could hack one alien robot, I could force it to fight the other two. The magic mirrors could function like intelligent coding agents, but the town had no internet or wireless network. A pocket mirror had no obvious way to transmit instructions to a towering machine. The systems were also fundamentally incompatible: extraterrestrial technology on one side, enchantment on the other.

Then I remembered my Golden Energy Tether, a glittering golden lasso whose primary power is creating mystical bonds and connections. I threw it high into the sky and attached one end to a cloud, then connected the other end to my pocket magic mirror.

The clouds gathered, darkened, and condensed into a massive storm above the village. Lightning erupted. A bolt struck one alien titan directly. The lightning carried the mirror's magical information as an electrical signal, creating a bridge between magic and alien technology. Through that connection, the pocket mirror infiltrated the robot's systems and took control.

The hacked titan immediately fought the other two. Their battle was catastrophic, but the controlled robot kept the others occupied and eventually destroyed them. It was destroyed in the process. All three alien robot titans defeated one another, and the village was saved.

The life-stone mine had permanently changed the town's future. Word would likely spread throughout the Storyverse that this medieval village possessed a natural source of the material used to create one of reality's most powerful artifacts. Drillov and the titans might only have been the beginning. If the villagers intended to keep the life stones safe, they would need heroes capable of defending the town from everyone who eventually came searching for them.
