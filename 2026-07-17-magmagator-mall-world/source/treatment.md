# Treatment

## Logline

When an alligator becomes the pollution-born kaiju Magmagator inside the infinite bathrooms of Mall World, lucid dreamer Jet gains a superhero form but must accept help from a mysterious purple-skinned twin before choosing whether Earth is worth defending.

## Central conflict

Jet begins with precise lucid control, then loses both lucidity and confidence in an endless filthy bathroom. Magmagator’s impossible transformation restores his awareness and gives him a yellow-and-blue superhero suit, but raw power is not enough. Jet must work with the stranger he instinctively recognizes as his “evil twin,” then decide what to do after learning that both twins are aliens assigned to fight monsters created by future corporate pollution.

## Theme

Power creates a choice about what deserves protection. A place does not need to be one’s original home to become worth understanding and defending.

## Emotional arc

Jet moves from controlled curiosity to confused powerlessness when the bathroom steals his lucidity. Magmagator’s emergence snaps him back into awareness, and the materialized suit makes him feel heroic, but the failed solo attack humbles him. Fighting beside his supposed evil twin replaces suspicion with reluctant trust. When the twin refuses responsibility for Earth, Jet makes the opposite choice: he remains to investigate the polluters and their resistance. The dream ends with comic social discomfort and the abrupt alarm, leaving Jet awake with the revelation still vivid.

## Continuity

This dream belongs to the Storyverse continuity that began on June 11, 2025. Jet retains his established face, dark-brown skin, beard, dreadlocks, casual outfit, rainbow crystal sword, golden energy tether, and purple starry Lucid Light. Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper remain Jet’s wider team, but Jet explicitly arrives alone and they do not physically appear. Mall World is a recurring shared dreamscape for Jet rather than a newly discovered Storyverse location.

## Ending

After defeating Magmagator and separating from his indifferent twin, Jet stays on Earth to investigate the companies creating future pollution monsters and the resistance fighting them. A resistance woman aggressively approaches him, but his alarm ends the encounter. The final image contrasts Jet waking in bed with a lingering reflection of the yellow-and-blue hero suit and Mall World, showing that he carries the choice to defend Earth out of the dream.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: Jet and his purple twin attack Magmagator inside a shattered neon Mall World.
2. Jet induces lucidity, arrives alone with AR display sunglasses, and creates a handheld touchpad.
3. A bathroom trip steals Jet’s lucidity inside Mall World’s infinite filthy restroom.
4. An alligator becomes Magmagator, restoring Jet’s lucidity and materializing his superhero suit.
5. Jet attacks alone, discovers Magmagator is stronger, and is rescued by his crashing twin.
6. The twins combine their powers and defeat Magmagator.
7. The twin explains their alien origin and the pollution monsters, then leaves while Jet chooses Earth.
8. Jet meets a resistance woman, recoils from her aggressive advance, and wakes to his alarm.
