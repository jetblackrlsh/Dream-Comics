# Page Script — The Nightmare King's False Choice

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions must remain readable, clearly separated, long enough to make every development obvious, and nonredundant. Jet, the King of Nightmares, the planet, the Life Sword, and the Golden Energy Tether must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 — Cover

One dramatic vertical composition. Jet flies in the foreground, his Golden Energy Tether circling his body and connecting to the radiant Rainbow Crystal Life Sword in his hand. He faces the gigantic King of Nightmares rising behind him, clearly made from burnt-black thorn vines beneath a curling branch crown and glowing red eyes. Below them, one half of the spherical planet is still composed of black mirrored cubes glowing red, while the other half transforms into brilliant white clouds and golden light. A few shadow nightmares cross the dividing wave and become luminous dreams. Colorful Storyverse bubble universes float in the deep cosmic void. Visible cover text only: “THE NIGHTMARE KING'S FALSE CHOICE” and “JULY 27, 2026”.

## Page 2 — The Mission That Demanded Destruction

Five panels. Jet lies conscious but immobilized during sleep paralysis, then enters the Storyverse's cosmic void with faint sparkling purple Lucid Light around him. He flies above the spherical nightmare planet and studies its black mirrored cubes glowing red from within. The towering King of Nightmares rises behind him, his red eyes matching the world below. The king gestures toward small inset visions of sleeping people wandering toward stairs and traffic under the influence of nightmare shadows. Final wide panel: the king points downward, demanding planetary destruction while Jet looks back with suspicion rather than obedience.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and entered the Storyverse aware that I was dreaming.”
- “Below me floated a world of black mirrored cubes, each one glowing red from within and radiating menace.”
- “The King of Nightmares rose behind me, a towering body of burnt vines and thorns crowned by curling branches.”
- “He warned that some nightmares could make sleeping people wander into traffic, fall down stairs, or enter other lethal danger.”
- “Then he called the solution simple: if I truly wanted to save people that night, I had to destroy the entire planet.”

## Page 3 — A False Choice Exposed

Six panels. Jet calmly faces the king while translucent Storyverse bubbles multiply around them. In one panel, the nightmare planet is visibly only one bubble-world among countless other nightmare-themed worlds. Jet gestures across the infinity of universes as a projected line from the planet fails to reach the other nightmare worlds, visually disproving that it is the single source. The king leans closer and changes from false solemnity to accusation. A dark rectangular caption presents his quoted challenge. Final panel: the king towers over Jet and labels him a false hero while Jet's hands begin glowing with angry purple Lucid Light.

Caption text:

- “Because I was lucid, I could see the flaw in his mission.”
- “The Storyverse contains infinitely many bubble universes and every possible kind of story. This could only be one nightmare world among countless others.”
- “Destroying it would not end nightmares elsewhere, and trying to erase every nightmare world would become an endless crusade.”
- “The king abandoned logic and turned to guilt.”
- “The King of Nightmares demanded, ‘So you're just going to let people die?’”
- “He called me a false hero and tried to make every future nightmare death my responsibility.”

## Page 4 — Fire Against Burnt Vines

Six panels. Jet launches orange-violet pyrokinetic flames from both hands, surrounded by sparkling purple star-field Lucid Light. The King of Nightmares catches fire, screams, and thrashes theatrically. Jet briefly believes the attack is working. The king suddenly lunges through the flames with red eyes blazing. Burning thorn tendrils coil around Jet's arms, torso, and legs, digging through the hoodie and leaving restrained cuts. Another vine reaches behind Jet and coils around the Life Sword. Final close panel: the king's glowing eyes hover beside the trapped Jet as a caption delivers his mocking explanation.

Caption text:

- “Tired of his manipulation, I tested the weakness that seemed most obvious.”
- “Using Lucid Light, I ignited his thorny body with pyrokinetic fire.”
- “He screamed and thrashed so convincingly that I believed the flames were destroying him.”
- “Then he lunged through the fire and wrapped his burning vines around my body.”
- “The thorns cut deeper while another vine began pulling the Life Sword from my back.”
- “He mocked me: ‘If fire could destroy me, why would my vines be burnt instead of reduced to ashes?’”

## Page 5 — The Bond He Ignored

Six panels. The Life Sword is nearly out of reach in the king's vine while Jet struggles inside the burning thorn coils. Jet notices the Golden Energy Tether at his belt, summons it, and throws a precise glowing lasso. The lasso wraps around the sword's hilt before the king can take it. A clear golden pulse connects Jet's hand, tether, and sword. The sword activates remotely, releasing a rapidly expanding transparent rainbow-white spherical force field that shatters the vines. Warm green-gold life energy closes Jet's thorn cuts as the king is blasted backward. Final panel: Jet pulls the Life Sword fully into his hand and faces the king again.

Caption text:

- “The Life Sword was too powerful to let the King of Nightmares possess it.”
- “He had ignored my Golden Energy Tether because it was neither rare nor famous.”
- “I threw the tether like a lasso and caught the sword before it left my reach.”
- “The tether creates mystical bonds. Its connection let me access the Life Sword even before I held it.”
- “A force field burst outward, shattered the vines, and threw the king away from me.”
- “The sword's life energy healed my cuts as I pulled the weapon safely into my hand.”

## Page 6 — The Third Path

Five panels. Jet turns toward the nightmare planet and raises the Life Sword. Behind him, the King of Nightmares steadies himself and smiles with premature triumph. A dark vision shows what the king expects: Jet slashing the planet apart while his own silhouette becomes corrupted and thorn-crowned. Jet's thoughtful eyes reject that future. The sword changes from a sharp destructive rainbow edge to a warm, expansive gold-green-white radiance. Final large panel: Jet directs a broad wave of pure life energy toward the intact planet as the king recoils in shock.

Caption text:

- “When I raised the Life Sword toward the world, the king believed he had finally won.”
- “He had never cared whether destroying one planet would end nightmares.”
- “His real goal was to manipulate a hero into an atrocity so extreme that it would corrupt me forever.”
- “But destruction and inaction were not my only choices.”
- “Instead of cutting the planet apart, I released the Life Sword's energy as a force of purification.”

## Page 7 — Nightmares Become Dreams

Six panels that flow diagonally with the purification wave. Rainbow-white life energy sweeps across the black cube planet. Cube seams fill with gold and dissolve into brilliant cloud terraces. Inside one cube, a shadow wolf becomes a radiant guardian wolf. In another, a crawling mask becomes a smiling lantern spirit. A winged terror becomes a brilliant dream-bird and an abstract fear-shape becomes a playful star creature. The oppressive red internal glow vanishes completely. Final wide panel: the fully transformed white-and-gold dream world floats below while a diverse multitude of luminous dreams rises and gathers protectively around Jet.

Caption text:

- “Pure life energy washed across the nightmare world without burning its inhabitants to ash.”
- “The black mirrored cubes dissolved into brilliant white clouds illuminated by warm golden light.”
- “One by one, the nightmare beings transformed instead of dying.”
- “Shadow creatures became guardians, terrors became wonders, and every trace of the oppressive red glow vanished.”
- “No longer servants or victims of the Nightmare King, the newly liberated dreams rose from their transformed world.”
- “They gathered around me and chose to fight beside me.”

## Page 8 — Time Won for Dreams

Six panels around a large central battle. Jet leads the luminous dreams toward the stunned King of Nightmares without striking the planet. Dream-birds tear loose crown vines, guardian wolves pull apart his thorn arms, lantern spirits unwind his torso, and star creatures break the remaining knots. The king's red eyes extinguish as his body comes completely apart. Burnt thorns scatter harmlessly through the Storyverse's cosmic void among distant bubble universes. Jet and the dream beings hover protectively above the radiant white-and-gold world. Final quiet panel: one distant thorn drifts alone while Jet watches with sober understanding, the Life Sword lowered and the Golden Energy Tether glowing gently at his side.

Caption text:

- “Together, the dreams overwhelmed the being who had ruled them through fear.”
- “They dismantled his crown, arms, and body until no twisted mass of burnt vines remained.”
- “His thorns scattered through the cosmic void in every direction.”
- “The King of Nightmares was an archetypal being, so this was probably not his permanent end.”
- “Someday his pieces might find one another and rebuild him, but reforming would take time.”
- “Every moment he remained scattered gave dreams and their defenders more time to protect people and keep doing what was right.”
