# Treatment

## Logline

After sleep paralysis launches Jet into the body of a muscular barbarian, he rejects a cruel raider camp, summons a Lucid Light mecha to rescue stolen villagers and gold, then rides a starlight horse to a superhero base where Second Brain helps him turn the adventure toward animal empathy.

## Central Conflict

Jet begins with the thrill of becoming a barbarian, but the fantasy turns sour when he sees that the camp's strength is built on theft and captivity. He cannot beat Big Barbarian through raw muscle, so he has to use lucid creativity instead of matching cruelty with cruelty.

## Theme

Power is only heroic when it protects people who cannot protect themselves. The dream moves from brute force to lucid responsibility, then from rescue to communication and empathy.

## Emotional Arc

Jet starts exhilarated by his barbarian form, becomes disturbed by the camp's cruelty, then feels physically outmatched by Big Barbarian. Lucid Light lets him reclaim agency through invention: first a giant mecha construct, then an impossible sky-pipe transport system. By the end, he is calm and forward-looking, discussing technology that could help people understand animals instead of forcing them into fights.

## Ending

The final page resolves in a futuristic superhero base. Jet and Second Brain study designs for AI translators, wolves and big cats in protective power armor, and a vision of people hearing what animals say during battles. The adventure closes on the idea that the strongest rescue is not just defeating oppressors, but building systems that make cruelty harder to ignore.
