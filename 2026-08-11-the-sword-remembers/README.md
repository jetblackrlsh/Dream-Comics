# 08/11/2026 - The Sword Remembers

Dream Comics entry for August 11, 2026.

Inside an infinite library of life books, Jet meets a pale-blue-haired librarian who can only ever half rest and an animated knight that performs the archive's physical work. Hoping to help, Jet uses Silonis to make the armor genuinely alive—only to unleash its long-suppressed rage and expose the librarian's hidden abuse.

When the librarian recognizes Silonis as if they share a forgotten past, the sword rejects her and returns to Jet. His attempt to purify her reveals a larger threat: the same darkness has been jumping between hosts across his dreams, always trying to take the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life. Jet finally understands the pattern and escapes through the library's immense glass window into the Storyverse.

The finished package includes the complete journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, and assembled PDF.
