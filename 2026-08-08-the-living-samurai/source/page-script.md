# Page Script - The Living Samurai

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, the woman, the mecha, the kaiju, and Andromeda City must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

One dramatic vertical composition in Andromeda City. Jet commands the foreground with Silonis releasing a rainbow life-energy arc. The restored blonde woman emerges beside him in her metallic red-and-yellow bodysuit. The colossal red-and-yellow samurai mecha rises protectively behind them while the black-blue kaiju attacks from the glowing bay. Visible cover text only: "THE LIVING SAMURAI" and "AUGUST 8, 2026." No issue number.

## Page 2 - Andromeda City's Only Guardian

Five panels. Establish the starry night, glowing wireframe city, bay, and towering samurai mecha. Jet flies toward it and discovers the shriveled gray-haired pilot inside its chest cockpit. Visual details make clear that the machine contains her leaking life energy.

Caption text:

- "I arrived in Andromeda City beneath a starry night, where glowing towers looked built from enormous LED lights and exposed wireframes."
- "A red-and-yellow samurai mecha stood guard over the skyline."
- "I flew to meet its pilot, the city's sole defender against the kaiju that repeatedly attacked."
- "Inside, I found a gray-haired woman whose body had been drained by a rare disorder."
- "The mecha was also her life support, preventing her remaining life energy from leaking away."

## Page 3 - The Attempt to Take Silonis

Five panels. Jet reveals Silonis near the open cockpit. The mecha abruptly reaches for the blade. A faceted rainbow crystal shield bursts around it and repels the giant hand. The aged pilot appears desperate while Jet remains compassionate but firm.

Caption text:

- "I told her Silonis, the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword, could heal the damage done to her."
- "Before I could finish explaining, the mecha reached out to seize the blade."
- "A rainbow crystal force field repelled its hand."
- "She believed she needed the sword itself to survive and continue protecting the city."
- "I refused to surrender the Storyverse's most powerful artifact, but I promised to help her."

## Page 4 - The False Choice

Six panels. Show the pilot's bond to the machine, years spent defending the city, her fear of leaving, Jet listening, and a final visual of the woman trapped between Silonis and the skyline she protects. No literal diagrams or labels.

Caption text:

- "She was the only person capable of piloting the mecha, because the machine was bound to her."
- "Healing her body would free her from its life-support system, but not from the city's need for a guardian."
- "She feared that without the mecha, she would need Silonis to protect Andromeda City herself."
- "Her real wish was not simply to become young again."
- "She wanted a life that belonged to her."
- "I realized she had been trapped inside a false choice: sacrifice herself or abandon the city."

## Page 5 - Life for Both

Five panels. Jet raises Silonis and releases one broad prismatic slash through the open cockpit and the mecha's armored body. The aged woman transforms into her healthy blonde form while gold-rainbow light seals her leaking life energy. The mecha's dim eyes ignite and glowing life-energy veins spread through its armor.

Caption text:

- "There was another answer."
- "Silonis could restore life, and I did not need to choose only one recipient."
- "I released a life-energy slash broad enough to strike both the pilot and her machine."
- "The energy sealed the leak draining the woman's body and restored her youth."
- "At the same moment, the empty mecha awakened as an independent living being."

## Page 6 - Her First Step Outside

Five panels. The blonde woman in the metallic red-and-yellow bodysuit opens the cockpit and steps onto the mecha's hand, then reaches solid ground. The living mecha watches protectively with glowing gold eyes. Jet lowers Silonis as she realizes she is free.

Caption text:

- "She emerged as a blonde woman with bluish-green eyes, wearing a metallic bodysuit that mirrored the mecha's red-and-yellow armor."
- "For the first time in her life, she stepped outside the machine."
- "The guardian no longer waited for her commands. It could think, move, and choose for itself."
- "She had expected healing to change her body."
- "Instead, Silonis had changed the meaning of her entire future."

## Page 7 - The Living Samurai Rises

Six panels escalating into a large impact panel. The bay erupts as the black-blue kaiju rises, water cascading from its jagged spines. It damages wireframe towers. The woman expects to return to the cockpit, but the living mecha moves first, leaps across the city, and meets the kaiju at the waterfront by its own choice.

Caption text:

- "Before either of us could celebrate, the bay erupted."
- "A giant kaiju rose from the water and tore into Andromeda City's glowing skyline."
- "The woman had spent her life believing only she could answer such an attack."
- "Now the living samurai moved before anyone ordered it to fight."
- "It charged across the city and met the kaiju with its own will."
- "Andromeda City still had a protector, but its former pilot was finally free."

## Page 8 - Her Life Returned

Five panels resolving into a large final vista. The woman apologizes to Jet and looks toward the mecha battling the kaiju in the bay. Jet lowers Silonis without anger. End with the woman standing independently beside Jet, the protected wireframe skyline behind them, and the living samurai holding the kaiju away from the city.

Caption text:

- "The woman apologized for trying to steal Silonis. She had never imagined it could bring an inanimate machine to life."
- "Her desperation came from believing that survival, duty, and freedom could never exist together."
- "The awakened mecha chose to protect Andromeda City, while she chose what to do with the life returned to her."
- "I had not merely restored her youth."
- "I had given her life back."
