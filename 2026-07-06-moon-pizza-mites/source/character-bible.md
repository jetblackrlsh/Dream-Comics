# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the same recognizable outfit throughout the comic: black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. His posture is alert and grounded. His expressions move from curious, to surprised, to controlled anger. His rainbow sword and golden energy tether are part of his normal identity but are not the focus of this story; the moon-mite armor becomes the main visible power.

## Moon-Mite Exoskeleton Jet

After Jet gives permission for the moon mites to stay, countless tiny silver-white lunar insects lock together over his hoodie and clothes into glossy insectoid armor. The armor has segmented plates, clawed gauntlets, crescent-moon ridges, glowing pale-blue seams, and beetle-like shoulder shells. Jet's face, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie silhouette, and cross-body belt bag remain recognizable beneath and around the armor. The armor can thicken around one arm into a massive armored fist made from concentrated moon mites.

## Purple Slime Doctor

The doctor is a calm purple slime woman who works inside the moon pizzeria. Her body is translucent violet gel with a humanoid female shape, soft rounded features, clear glasses or a medical visor, and a white doctor coat that floats over her slime body. She acts clinically helpful after the danger begins, explaining that the crawling creatures are moon mites and that fighting them makes the pain worse. Her attitude is matter-of-fact, which makes the lack of menu warnings feel even stranger.

## Moon Mites

Moon mites are tiny pale silver insects with many legs, crescent-shaped shells, and faint blue glow. At first they crawl across Jet's skin and clothes like a living rash of glittering lunar bugs. When resisted, they burn. When accepted, they cooperate and form armor. They should appear as both individual crawling insects and swarms that can lock together into exoskeleton plates.

## Other Customer

The other customer is an adult woman sitting at another table in the pizzeria. She has medium-brown skin, short curly black hair, a teal jacket, dark pants, and a half-eaten moon pizza slice. She is important for showing the deadly consequence of resisting the mites. Her fear and pain should be clear, and her death should be visual but not gory: bright flames, smoke, and then a small pile of ash beside her dropped pizza.

## Mammoth Chef Manager

The manager is a towering humanoid mammoth chef with shaggy brown fur, a long trunk, curved ivory tusks, small angry eyes, and a huge white chef hat and apron over a muscular body. He carries a massive pizza peel like a battle weapon and can shift into battle mode, with steam, lunar flour, and sauce splashing around him. He is proud, violent, and unwilling to hear criticism until Jet defeats him.

## Moon Pizzeria

The pizzeria stands on the gray moon under a black starfield, with Earth visible in the sky. Inside, it combines a normal pizza restaurant with lunar absurdity: crater-shaped booths, glowing menu boards, ovens shaped like moon rocks, stacks of flattened moons as pizza crusts, and slices dusted with white chalky lunar powder. The menu initially avoids any clear moon-mite warning. After the battle, the menu has a large obvious disclaimer.
