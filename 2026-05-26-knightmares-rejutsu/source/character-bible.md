# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword that can radiate healing prismatic light. He also has a rope-like golden glittering energy tether, but the tether is secondary in this issue.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow sword, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In action pages, his fists should show progressive damage, healing glow, and then growing muscular power. The sword is used for healing, not for attacking the knights.

## Knightmares

Faceless animate suits of medieval plate armor with reflective silver metal, blank mirrored helmets, and identical silhouettes. They have no visible faces, no mouths, no skin, and no individual markings. Their bodies move like mindless grunts animated by hostile dream logic. Some carry plain swords or shields, but the important visual is the identical faceless armor.

Continuity notes: The Knightmares should look intentionally indistinguishable from one another. Their armor can have scratches, dents, cracked breastplates, and shattered gauntlets as Jet's strikes grow stronger. They should read as animate armor rather than human knights.

## Lucid Light and Rainbow Healing

Lucid Light resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. Jet's rainbow crystal sword emits healing prismatic light when held near his injured fists. The healing effect should feel restorative rather than like an attack: rainbow bands, soft star sparks, rebuilt knuckles, and bright color returning to Jet's hands.

Continuity notes: Avoid making the healing look like a ranged weapon. Rejutsu is powered by healing between strikes, not by blasting the armor with magic.

## Dream Arena

The fight takes place in a lucid Storyverse arena formed from a floating medieval stone courtyard inside a cosmic void. Storyverse bubble universes drift in the distance, and faint purple Lucid Light hangs in the air. The arena should feel like a dream deliberately chosen for a brawl: open space, broken stone, metal debris, and bright unreal lighting.
