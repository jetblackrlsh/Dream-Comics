# Character Bible - The Moonlit Apprentice

## Jet's sheep-dragon dream form

- Fully nonhuman adult humanoid hybrid, built primarily like a tall humanoid black ram or sheep rather than a conventional dragon.
- Long sheep-like muzzle, expressive warm amber eyes, large curled obsidian-black ram horns, dexterous dark hands, and cloven hooves.
- Entire torso, shoulders, upper arms, hips, and legs are wrapped in extremely dense charcoal-black wool. The wool constantly frays into smoky cloud wisps and can become visibly intangible mist.
- Two enormous black batlike dragon wings emerge from his back. Their membranes carry subtle ember-orange veins that glow when he breathes fire.
- Fire glows warmly inside his mouth before he exhales it. Keep the fire controlled and non-gory.
- No human clothing, human face, hoodie, dreadlocks, sword, tether, or separate human Jet appears in this dream. This creature is Jet's first-person dream form.
- Confused by his own body at first, then calm, playful, analytical, compassionate, and confidently mentor-like.
- Keep the sheep muzzle, curled horns, black cloud-wool, amber eyes, cloven hooves, enormous bat wings, and ember accents consistent on every page.

## Wolf knight - armored form

- Adult male humanoid wolf with dark-brown fur, a broad canine muzzle, pointed ears, intelligent blue-gray eyes, and a strong athletic build.
- Initially wears practical medieval steel plate armor over a navy-blue padded gambeson, with dark leather belts and boots.
- Carries a long wooden jousting lance with a steel tip. The lance is thrown at Jet and is not recovered.
- Mounted on a dark chestnut medieval warhorse only during his first appearance. The horse retreats safely from the fire and does not become a recurring character.
- Aggressive and certain at first, then shocked, overheated, and deeply confused when his assigned roles fail.

## Wolf knight - unarmored form

- After the armor becomes dangerously hot, he removes every metal plate and remains in a sleeveless navy-blue padded tunic, dark-brown trousers, leather belt, and boots.
- His fur remains dark brown until he actively channels moonlight.
- Vulnerable posture and lowered ears during his crisis of purpose; skeptical curiosity while listening to Jet's lesson.

## Moonlit apprentice form

- The same wolf's proportions, muzzle, ears, and facial structure remain recognizable.
- His dark-brown fur transforms into pale luminous white fur suffused with silver-blue moonlight.
- His eyes glow clear icy blue. A flowing aura of moon dust, tiny stars, and curved silver light trails surrounds him.
- He never grows wings. His wingless flight must be unmistakable.
- His navy tunic, dark trousers, belt, and boots remain present beneath the moonlight so the transformation does not erase continuity.
- Expression progresses from concentration, to astonishment as his feet lift, to liberated joy while flying beside Jet.

## Medieval-fantasy landscape

- Vast wooded countryside with ancient pines and broadleaf forest, stone roads, rolling hills, occasional distant castle towers, and a luminous twilight sky.
- Early pages use warm late-afternoon light. As the lesson turns cosmic, the sky deepens into blue-violet twilight and a huge bright moon becomes visible.
- Ground scenes occur in a grassy clearing beside the stone road. The wolf's discarded steel armor rests in a safe pile nearby after page 3.

## Fire, cloud, and lunar magic

- Jet's cloud-wool is black, soft, voluminous, and partially transparent at its misty edges. Solid weapons pass through it without blood or injury.
- Jet's fire is bright orange-gold with ember sparks. It heats the wolf's armor but does not burn flesh or cause graphic injury.
- The wolf's lunar magic is silver-blue, cool, radiant, and airy. It originates from his voluntary connection to the moon, not from Jet casting a spell on him.

## Continuity rules

- All eight pages use bright, high-saturation modern-anime comic art with crisp linework and cinematic fantasy lighting.
- Story pages use unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes only.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, signatures, gore, extra limbs, duplicate unintended characters, or blank caption boxes.
- Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from every outer edge.
- The wolf removes his armor on page 3 and never wears it again.
- The wolf remains dark brown until page 7, then remains luminous pale white through page 8.
- The wolf never has wings. Jet always has two enormous batlike wings.
