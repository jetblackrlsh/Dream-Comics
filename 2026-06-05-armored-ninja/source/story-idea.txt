Dream journal date: 06/05/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse, a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void.

In this dream, I am alone in a city. I have spent a lot of my time recently thinking about Superhero Mandroid, a human-like robot superhero with white skin, slicked-back brown hair, a red superhero unitard, a yellow cape, a yellow "SHM" chest insignia, and large mechanical green fists.

As I wander through the dream city, I do not search for Superhero Mandroid. I instead search for a man that I imagine could be his sidekick. I do not put up help wanted posters or conduct interviews. I concentrate on who Superhero Mandroid is and build a new man from scratch with more grounded components.

The man I build is an Asian man with short messy black hair, blue eyes, wearing a cobalt blue armored vest, cobalt blue armored knee-high combat boots, cobalt blue armored shorts, black armored gloves with raised gray knuckles, a black unitard under layer, and a large black round carbon shield strapped to his arm.

His boots allow him to grind across rails, curbs, and similar surfaces to increase speed and combat momentum. While grinding, his armored gloves strike with incredible force by channeling forward momentum into his punches. His shield is not bulletproof, but it is useful for defending against knives and hand-to-hand combatants. He can throw his shield like a chakram and catch it when it ricochets back toward him.

He is a hero inspired by Superhero Mandroid to make a difference in his city. He goes by the name "Armored Ninja".
