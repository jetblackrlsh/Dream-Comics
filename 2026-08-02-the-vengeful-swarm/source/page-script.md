# Page Script - The Vengeful Swarm

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, long enough to make every development obvious, and nonredundant. Jet, Psilones, the ghost bugs, the ghost-bug humanoid, the inhibitor chip, the blessing, and the dream setting must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

One dramatic vertical composition in the indigo-black dreamspace. Jet stands in the foreground with one hand glowing with sparkling purple Lucid Light while Psilones floats protectively behind his shoulder. A colossal liquid wave of luminous insect ghosts curls around him and condenses overhead into the same six-to-seven-foot ghost-bug humanoid, its bright compound eyes staring down. A tiny acid-green circuit motif glows at the back of Jet's neck, foreshadowing the inhibitor. Visible cover text only: “THE VENGEFUL SWARM” and “AUGUST 2, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - The Dead Return

Six panels. A small opening panel shows a roach on Jet's bedroom ceiling before sleep and Jet using bug spray. A thoughtful close-up shows the incident lingering in his mind. The dreamspace opens around him as hundreds of spectral insects appear. Their numbers multiply into enormous liquid-like waves from every direction. Jet remains centered but nearly surrounded. His raised hand ignites with star-filled purple Lucid Light.

Caption text:

- “Before sleep, I killed a roach crawling across my ceiling—but the thought stayed with me.”
- “People kill insects constantly. If bug ghosts sought revenge, no one would face only one spirit.”
- “That thought experiment followed me into a lucid dream.”
- “Countless ghost bugs rushed from every direction, their swarm flowing like a supernatural flood.”
- “Hundreds became thousands, all seeking vengeance for their deaths.”
- “Rather than panic, I activated my sparkling purple Lucid Light.”

## Page 3 - One Impossible Opponent

Six panels. Jet sweeps both hands inward as Lucid Light spirals around the spectral flood. Separate insect waves compress toward one glowing center. The swarm knits itself into feet, limbs, exoskeletal torso, and a compound-eye face. The completed humanoid stands only one foot taller than Jet, clearly six-to-seven feet tall rather than giant. Detail panels emphasize speed, armored insect structure, and coordinated hive awareness. The final panel shows the humanoid launching forward faster than Jet expects.

Caption text:

- “Fighting every spirit separately would have been nearly impossible, so I reshaped the swarm into one target.”
- “Lucid Light pulled the ghost bugs together and compressed them into a humanoid body.”
- “It stood only six or seven feet tall, but countless insect abilities had been concentrated inside it.”
- “Its exoskeleton was resilient, and its strength reflected species able to lift many times their own weight.”
- “Speed, agility, instinct, and advanced hive intelligence moved through one coordinated mind.”
- “Before I could reconsider my plan, the ghost-bug humanoid charged.”

## Page 4 - The Inhibitor

Six panels. The humanoid crosses the dreamspace in a blur, seizes Jet by the neck, and lifts him with one arm. Orbiting insects form precise circuit patterns around its other hand. A small black hexagonal chip with acid-green circuitry assembles instantly. The chip is implanted cleanly into the back of Jet's neck. Jet attempts to summon Lucid Light, but only a few purple sparks fail around his fingers. The final panel shows the humanoid looming while Jet hangs powerless but conscious.

Caption text:

- “Its impossible speed carried it across the dreamspace before I could react.”
- “The creature grabbed me by the neck and lifted me into the air.”
- “Its collective mind understood engineering far beyond any ordinary insect—or person.”
- “In moments, the swarm designed and constructed a computer chip.”
- “It implanted the device into the back of my neck as an inhibitor.”
- “My Lucid Light vanished, leaving me against an enemy with overwhelming power and intelligence.”

## Page 5 - The Repeating Struggle

Six panels arranged as a looping spiral. Jet wakes exhausted in his dim bedroom, closes his eyes, and drops back into the indigo dreamspace. The humanoid attacks again. The chip appears in the back of his neck again. Additional fragments show Jet trying different evasions that all end the same way. At the spiral's center, Jet kneels in concentration and realizes that his independent tools remain available even without Lucid Light.

Caption text:

- “Because I had gone to sleep late, I repeatedly woke feeling that I still needed more rest.”
- “Each time I returned to sleep, the ghost-bug humanoid was waiting for me.”
- “Again and again, it reached me and implanted the inhibitor.”
- “I tried different approaches, but every path returned to the same powerless moment.”
- “Then I realized the chip had disabled one power—not everything I carried.”
- “Even without Lucid Light, I could still call upon Psilones, my living rainbow crystal Sword of Life.”

## Page 6 - The Chip Awakens

Six panels. Psilones materializes horizontally across Jet's back in radiant rainbow light. Jet never touches it; his calm command and the sword's autonomous motion are clear. Psilones turns its point toward the tiny chip at the back of his neck, carefully pierces only the device, and fills it with gold-green life energy. The chip unfolds six mechanical legs, pulls itself free without blood, and scuttles away like a robotic beetle. Purple star-filled light reignites around Jet while the humanoid watches in surprise.

Caption text:

- “Summoning Psilones was like reaching into a utility belt for equipment I already possessed.”
- “The rainbow crystal Sword of Life appeared on my back.”
- “Because Psilones had a life and will of its own, I did not need to draw or wield it.”
- “At my call, the living sword turned and stabbed the inhibitor instead of attacking the swarm.”
- “Its life-giving power awakened the chip, which pulled free, grew tiny legs, and crawled away.”
- “The inhibition vanished, and my Lucid Light returned.”

## Page 7 - A Different Kind of Power

Six panels. Jet faces the humanoid with restored Lucid Light blazing around one fist, then deliberately lets the purple energy fade. Quiet inset images suggest his past: an ordination certificate silhouette, a starry wizard-like ritual, and hands blessing a dark room, without adding literal extra people or specific religious symbols. Jet recognizes frightened and grieving insect faces within the humanoid's body. He lowers Psilones, approaches without aggression, and raises an open empty hand toward the creature.

Caption text:

- “With my power restored, I realized I did not actually need to use it.”
- “More importantly, fighting had been the wrong approach from the beginning.”
- “The bugs believed I had wronged them, and in a sense, they were right: I had killed them.”
- “Their spirits were trapped by anger, pain, and a desire for vengeance.”
- “I remembered my history as an ordained minister and practitioner of mystical blessings.”
- “They did not need to be punished. They needed to be released.”

## Page 8 - The Blessing

Six panels with a luminous resolution. Jet places his open hand against the humanoid's chest and blesses it. Concentric gold-white light travels through the entire insect-built body, softening every pair of eyes. The humanoid gently separates into thousands of peaceful spectral bugs. They rise upward as cyan, mint, violet, and white lights through a radiant opening in the dream sky. Jet watches with quiet relief as the last spirits pass on. The final wide panel shows him standing in a calm starry dawn where the hostile flood once raged.

Caption text:

- “Drawing upon that spiritual part of my history, I chose not to attack the ghost-bug humanoid.”
- “I blessed it.”
- “The blessing passed through the collective form and reached every insect spirit inside.”
- “Their anger dissolved, and their need for vengeance faded.”
- “The humanoid evaporated into light as the ghost bugs finally moved into the afterlife.”
- “By blessing them instead of destroying them, I ended the cycle of violence and allowed the spirits to find peace.”
