# Treatment

## Title

06/17/2026: Super Base World

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet surfs across the Storyverse on Leon and tours impossible vacant superhero headquarters, only to find Mall World's vast Super Base World empty when it feels designed for infinite defenders.

## Central Conflict

Jet loves Leon as a trusted friend and mobile headquarters, but curiosity draws him toward the fantasy of a traditional superhero base. The more empty headquarters he explores, the more the vacancy becomes unsettling. Super Base World is not merely unused; it feels like a place that should be alive with heroes guarding Mall World. When Jet exits and finds aliens controlling dreamers' bodies, the missing defenders suddenly feel less like atmosphere and more like a warning.

## Theme

A headquarters is not only architecture. It is trust, presence, and readiness. Leon may not look like a traditional superhero base, but he is the home that actually shows up with Jet.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins with controlled lucidity and playful curiosity, riding Leon through the Storyverse while imagining what a more classic superhero headquarters might feel like. The tour starts as wish fulfillment: caves, castles, moon bases, temples, and space stations without anyone else's gear in the way. In Mall World, that wonder turns liminal. Super Base World is beautiful and infinite, but its emptiness feels wrong. Jet leaves with questions instead of answers, then immediately meets possessed super-strong dreamers and aliens whose intentions are unclear. His accidental ragdolling of one possessed person turns the dream from exploration into unresolved danger. Jet wakes before the moral situation can be understood, left with renewed appreciation for Leon's reliable presence.

## Ending

Jet touches one green-skinned, antennaed, super-strong possessed person and accidentally sends them ragdolling because their strength and durability do not match. The aliens still seem more playful than cruel, and Jet wakes before he can decide what is really happening. The final image connects the uncertainty back to Leon: the traditional base was magnificent, but the trusted headquarters was the friend carrying Jet home through the Storyverse.

## Cast

- Jet: the lucid dreamer and protagonist, a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow crystal sword, a golden glittering energy tether, and sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Leon: Jet's trusted friend and mobile headquarters, a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon is both vehicle and companion, large enough to feel like a moving base while still elegant, spectral, and saucer-shaped.
- Super Base World: a secret superhero base inside Mall World, combining caves, castles, rainforests, Bermuda Triangle bunkers, space stations, temples, moon bases, hangars, tunnels, command rooms, training arenas, and impossible transition corridors into one liminal patchwork headquarters.
- Mall World: an infinite dream mall filled with endless corridors, glowing storefronts, atriums, escalators, skylights, and crowds of distant dreamers.
- The antenna aliens: small playful aliens or alien presences that control human bodies, giving them green skin, yellow antennae, and huge super strength without matching durability.
- Possessed dreamers: ordinary mall visitors whose bodies are temporarily controlled by the aliens, transformed with green skin, bright yellow antennae, and exaggerated superhero strength.
