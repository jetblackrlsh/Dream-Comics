# Treatment

## Logline

Alone in the Storyverse, Jet survives a lion-sized dog corrupted by Lucid Light by inventing a transformation watch, borrowing Lucid Light variants of Skelebot, Tecton, and Leon, and purifying the beast before Storyverse Animal Control captures the escaped corruption.

## Central Conflict

Jet is alone against a corrupted animal whose raw speed, warp bursts, and shockwave bark overpower direct physical answers. Each transformation teaches him that the beast cannot simply be outrun or restrained; it has to be deprived of traction and cleansed at the source.

## Theme

Solo travel does not have to mean leaving the team behind. Jet can carry the crew's lessons and powers as a toolset, then choose the right answer for the moment.

## Ending

Jet transforms into LLV-Leon, lifts the beast with a green tractor beam, and uses his golden energy tether to connect the suspended animal to his rainbow crystal sword. The sword purifies the corrupted Lucid Light, forcing the corruption into a separate shadow entity that Storyverse Animal Control detains. Jet keeps the Lucid Light Watch as a new solo-adventure tool.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins isolated but alert in the Storyverse. The corrupted beast forces him into frantic improvisation, then each failed tactic sharpens his thinking. By the end, he is calm and inventive, realizing the watch lets him travel alone while still embracing the power of his crew.
