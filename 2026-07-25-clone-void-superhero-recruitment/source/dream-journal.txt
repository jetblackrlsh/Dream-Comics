07/25/2026

The Clone in the Void and the Superhero Recruitment Drive

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream took place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse, a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes floating together in a cosmic void.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a “Dream Anime” graphic tee. I wear black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. I have warm brown eyes. I carry a colorful futuristic Rainbow Crystal Sword and an energy tether resembling a rope made of golden glittery light.

My established Storyverse team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper. Leon is my ghostly green flying saucer. Johnson is an orange humanoid goldfish in a blue astronaut suit without a helmet. Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. Overdrive is a chrome robot with slicked-back yellow hair, glowing green eyes, yellow clothing, and matter-manipulation powers. Skelebot is Johnson's humanoid robot speedster. Savannah is a pale cyborg woman with long black hair, forehead-covering bangs, red eyes, a silver bodysuit, and the purple-blue Death Sword. Her father Tecton is a muscular green cat-ogre barbarian in a brown loincloth and silver baldric. Chipper is a small levitating silver crab robot. None of them physically appeared in this dream.

Lucid Light is the reality-warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy shining like a night sky full of stars. I discovered it on 07/19/2025.

My dream began with me standing alone in an endless void. There was no visible ground, sky, or horizon—only a vast, empty darkness stretching in every direction.

Eventually, I encountered a man sitting at a table in the middle of the void. I pulled up a chair and sat across from him. As we talked, he mentioned that he was involved with comic books.

“Oh, that's cool,” I told him. “I'm into comic books too.”

He then asked me if I could pitch him a comic-book idea.

I thought about a recent conversation with my friend, who had said that he wanted to create a comic. This seemed like it might be a good opportunity to promote my friend's idea instead of pitching one of my own.

Before I committed to anything, however, I asked the man, “Do you work for a comic-book company? Is that why you want me to pitch something to you?”

“No,” he replied. “I don't work for a comic-book company. But I could always message a publisher and tell them, ‘You should check out these creators. They're really good, and they have a cool story that deserves to be shared.’”

I thought that sounded potentially useful, although the situation still felt slightly strange. He did not actually work for a publisher, so it was unclear how much influence he truly had.

While I was still sitting across from him, I began to feel a strange sensation coming from my waking body. My stomach felt swollen and bloated, and I could feel something resembling a heartbeat pulsing inside it.

The sensation translated directly into the dream. It suddenly seemed as though I were pregnant, despite the obvious fact that I am a man. It felt as if there were a living being growing inside my body.

I stood up from the table and grabbed my enlarged stomach.

I was still lucid, so I understood that I was dreaming. Even so, the physical sensation felt incredibly real because I could still feel the strange pulsing in my waking body. I knew that I could not truly be pregnant, but the experience was unsettling.

Using my lucid-dreaming abilities, I summoned my Lucid Light and pulled the bloated mass out of my body.

It did not emerge like an organ, and there was no blood or gore. Instead, the removed mass transformed into something resembling a large black trash bag. Through the surface of the bag, I could see the glowing silhouette of a humanoid figure curled up inside.

I placed the bag on the ground.

Without warning, the comic-book man stood up and transformed into a massive humanoid rat monster. His body became hulking and muscular, with an animalistic posture and the features of an enormous rat.

He immediately began attacking the bag.

The rat monster repeatedly punched it, slamming his fists into the humanoid shape inside over and over again.

I stood there, unsure how to react. I did not understand what the creature inside the bag was, and I had not known that the comic-book man could transform into a giant rat monster. I felt as though I should stop him from killing whatever was inside, but I also remembered that I was carrying the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life, an artifact from the Storyverse that I frequently carry with me throughout my dreams.

Because the sword could restore life, I reasoned that even if the rat monster killed the being in the bag, I could simply revive it afterward.

Once the bag had been thoroughly pulverized, the rat monster suddenly scurried away into the void. In his transformed state, he no longer seemed to possess the same level of intelligence he had shown as a man. He acted more like a wild animal, fleeing immediately after the attack.

I was left alone in the void with the battered bag.

I drew the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life and directed its energy into the bag. Brilliant life energy flowed from the blade, filling the damaged figure within and restoring it.

The bag began to tear open.

When it finally split apart, I discovered that the humanoid inside was a clone of me.

The clone looked exactly as I normally do—as Jet—and was now alive because of the energy from the Rainbow Crystal Sword.

Around this point, I lost my lucidity, and the dream shifted into a fast-moving montage.

I saw groups of superheroes traveling around and attempting to recruit ordinary people to become heroes. It was essentially a superhero recruitment drive, although many of the people they approached did not understand the concept.

Some appeared confused by the idea that someone could simply be recruited into superheroism. Others slowly began to understand and support the effort.

“Okay,” some of them seemed to decide. “Maybe we should become superheroes.”

When I eventually regained lucidity, I discovered that I was no longer in my normal body.

I looked down and realized that I had become a woman. I was apparently one of the superheroes involved in the recruitment drive, traveling around and trying to convince other people to become heroes.

Although I no longer looked like myself, I still possessed my normal awareness. Internally, I was still me.

At some point, I approached someone whom I believed might be a potential superhero recruit. However, I soon discovered that this person was already a supervillain—a shapeshifter capable of assuming other people's appearances.

The shapeshifter copied my current identity.

They did not transform into Jet. Instead, they became an exact duplicate of the female superhero whose body I was currently inhabiting.

I found myself facing someone who looked exactly like me.

Unlike me, however, the shapeshifter possessed telekinesis.

I attempted to escape while the villain attacked me from a distance. Their telekinetic power allowed them to strike and manipulate objects without needing to get close. I struggled to avoid the attacks while trying to determine how I could fight back.

Then I remembered that, despite my unfamiliar body, I was lucid.

Looking different did not prevent me from using my Lucid Light.

I summoned its power, but instead of creating one of my usual weapons or attacks, I manifested an assortment of pots and pans.

I began hurling the cookware at the shapeshifter.

The sudden attack caught them completely off guard. I did not understand why my mind had chosen pots and pans, but I sensed that it had something to do with the female superhero identity I was occupying. Her powers or theme may have been related to cooking, kitchens, or cookware, so the dream's logic naturally expressed my Lucid Light through those objects.

I summoned even more pots and pans and began using them as melee weapons.

I rushed the shapeshifter and repeatedly struck them with the cookware, bashing them with frying pans, pots, and other kitchen implements while they struggled to defend themselves.

Finally, I raised my power toward the sky and summoned a massive storm of cookware above us.

Pots and pans rained down from overhead, crashing onto the shapeshifter one after another. The growing pile completely buried them beneath a mountain of metal cookware.

With the shapeshifter defeated, my alarm began to sound.

The dream dissolved, and I woke up.
