# Treatment

## Title

06/16/2026: Cat Frog Soul

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet arrives alone in a Shellasaur land, witnesses a giant subterranean serpent drive the shelled dinosaurs to extinction, and uses Lucid Light to rebuild a damaged frog soul puppet into Cat Frog Soul, a swift new self capable of ending the serpent's reign.

## Central Conflict

Jet cannot save the Shellasaurs by staying on the ground. The serpent is too fast, too hidden, and too overwhelming, forcing him to retreat into space and watch the loss from above. When he returns, the real danger becomes personal: the green frog puppet that represents his soul is stranded without arms or legs, unable to act or move in the serpent's killing ground.

## Theme

You are what you build yourself to be. When loss leaves the self powerless, reinvention can become survival, action, and restoration.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins lucid and adventurous, riding a Shellasaur through a strange Storyverse world. The dream turns catastrophic as the serpent exterminates the Shellasaurs, and Jet's flight into space becomes a moment of grief and helpless witness. His attention narrows from a planetary tragedy to one small broken figure on the ground: a limbless green frog felt puppet that carries the symbolic weight of his soul. Jet responds not with escape, but with creation. By giving the puppet long cat arms, cat legs, speed, agility, and claws, he turns immobility into motion and helplessness into decisive action.

## Ending

Cat Frog Soul outmaneuvers the subterranean serpent and slays it. The final image holds on Jet, Cat Frog Soul, and the quiet Shellasaur land beneath a Storyverse sky, suggesting that the extinct Shellasaurs may one day return and that the self can be rebuilt after devastation.

## Cast

- Jet: the lucid dreamer and protagonist, a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, long dark-brown dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow crystal sword, a golden glittering energy tether, and sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Shellasaurs: gentle dinosaurs with massive spiral shells on their backs, using the shells as mobile homes. They embody the land's peaceful life before the serpent.
- The subterranean serpent: a giant earth-burrowing predator with speed, stealth, stretchy jaws, and sudden eruptions from below.
- Frog Soul Puppet: a humanoid green frog felt puppet, soft and handmade, initially missing arms and legs. It represents Jet's trapped soul.
- Cat Frog Soul: the rebuilt soul puppet, now with long cat arms, long cat legs, agile posture, and incredible claws, combining frog softness with feline motion and precision.
