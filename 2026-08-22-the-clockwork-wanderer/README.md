# 08/22/2026 - The Clockwork Wanderer

Dream Comics entry for August 22, 2026.

Jet explores a clockwork planet formed when obsolete analog spacecraft drifted together under gravity. The bronze, gold, and black world is a colossal mass of gears, ancient ship hulls, forgotten credits, pirate treasure, and valuable antiques.

Laser fire leads Jet to a mysterious woman in a slim white polymer astronaut suit with an opaque mirrored helmet, glowing green orbs, and cobalt-blue ringed gauntlets. She is fighting muscular pink-skinned, red-eyed, white-haired barbarian women whose footsteps crack the mechanical ground. Her gauntlets reflect their laser blasts, but one direct superhuman punch could still destroy her helmet.

The astronaut explains that the barbarians stole her ship. She refuses Jet's offer of a portal because she intends to survive without being rescued. Jet respects her independence and uses Lucid Light to create a winged jetpack instead. She accepts the tool, thanks him, and flies beyond the horizon to continue her own adventure.
