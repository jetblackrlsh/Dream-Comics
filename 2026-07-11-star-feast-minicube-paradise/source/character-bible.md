# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a visible “Dream Anime” graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow sword resembling a colorful futuristic crystal sword and a golden-glitter energy tether, though neither weapon needs to be drawn unless composition allows. In normal form he is athletic, expressive, compassionate, and unmistakably human.

When Jet consumes the star, he becomes a colossal flaming cosmic version of himself. His face, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie silhouette, athletic pants, cross-body bag silhouette, and white sneakers remain recognizable beneath brilliant orange-gold stellar fire. He must still look like Jet—not a generic bald deity or armored superhero. When he releases the star power, he immediately returns to his exact normal appearance and outfit.

## The Minicubes

The Minicubes are small, living jelly cubes in vivid translucent colors: cherry red, orange, lemon yellow, lime green, cyan, cobalt blue, violet, and pink. Each has two soft fuzzy antennae, a simple expressive face suspended inside its gelatinous body, rounded cube edges, and tiny rippling motions. They are cute and grateful rather than malicious, but their collective instinct is to assimilate visitors with sticky rainbow slime. When united, their colored slime merges into enormous iridescent tendrils and can coat an entire planet.

## The Tortilla-Chip Chef

The beach-shack chef is a friendly humanoid cook with a single enormous golden triangular tortilla chip for a head. The chip has a warm expressive face, toasted speckles, and crisp edges. He wears a clean white short-sleeved chef coat, a red neckerchief, a dark apron, rolled trousers, and sandals. He is welcoming and calm, never frightening. His specialties are tall colorful milkshakes and glossy spicy-honey meat served on rustic platters.

## Lucid Light

Lucid Light is Jet's reality-warping energy. It appears as sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. When it creates the Minicube paradise, it forms a luminous Storyverse world-bubble containing green hills, flowers, clear streams, and a blue sky. When it extinguishes the fires, purple starry waves cool the flames without harming the Minicubes.

## Star Power

The consumed star becomes blazing orange-gold energy inside Jet, transforming him into a giant cosmic god surrounded by solar prominences. After Jet kicks it, the star is a dark, flameless sphere with a faint cracked amber core. When the Minicubes consume the power, orange-gold fire moves from Jet into their slime before being safely extinguished.

## Porta Spell

The Porta spell creates a very small circular portal edged with bright cyan and magenta geometric light. It is only large enough for normal-sized Jet to dive through. The planet and its giant slime tendril cannot fit.

## Recurring Settings

The beach has pale sand, turquoise water, a warm sky, and a rustic open-air food shack made from sun-bleached wood and palm leaves. Storyverse space is a cosmic void filled with stars and countless luminous bubble universes. The Minicube paradise is a grassy planet of rolling emerald hills, wildflowers, clear water, and a bright blue atmosphere before iridescent slime spreads over its surface.
