# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: protagonist, lucid dreamer, inventor, and wielder of Lucid Light.
- Stable appearance: man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, warm brown eyes.
- Signature powers and props: rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glitter energy tether, sparkling purple Lucid Light that shines like a star-filled night sky, and the Porta spell that opens portals.
- Expression range: focused impatience, wary curiosity, excited inventor joy, urgent tactical confidence, relieved happiness.
- Continuity rule: keep Jet recognizably Jet on every page through skin tone, full beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, white stripe pants, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow sword, golden tether, and purple Lucid Light.

## Elven Sorceress Robot

- Role: persistent guide, secret robot, upgrade subject, and battle partner.
- Initial appearance: elven sorceress with pale skin, blue eyes, pink blush, silver-blonde hair, elegant blue robes, and an eagle headdress with feathered gold and white details.
- Robot reveal appearance: the same sorceress design, but with subtle articulated seams, glowing blue circuitry under porcelain-pale synthetic skin, mechanical joints beneath the robes, and a luminous chest panel for elemental cores.
- Upgraded equipment: talon-shaped grapple blades mounted on her forearms, cable launchers that let her swing around the lab, glowing elemental cores in her chest, and a purple-blue talon grapple shot charged with fire, ice, lightning, wind, earth, and water.
- Expression range: obsessive adoration, proud helpfulness, calm robotic focus, heroic determination.
- Continuity rule: keep the blue robes, eagle headdress, blue eyes, silver-blonde hair, pale skin, pink blush, and later visible robotic seams consistent across pages.

## Eagle Jet

- Role: magical transport out of Mall World.
- Appearance: a giant eagle-shaped jet formed from a fused flock of eagles, with feathered wings, a sharp eagle nose, talon landing gear, glowing blue cockpit, gold-white feathers over sleek aircraft panels, and magical wind trails.
- Continuity rule: it should read as both a huge eagle and a futuristic jet, summoned by the sorceress rather than built by Jet.

## Energy Vampire Ogre

- Role: antagonist attracted by elemental power.
- Appearance: huge monstrous ogre with sickly green-gray skin, tusks, heavy black horns, blackened armor, glowing violet cracks across his body, clawed hands, and a dark hungry aura that pulls colored elemental energy toward him.
- Goal: absorb all elemental power and rule reality.
- Expression range: predatory hunger, arrogant confidence, panicked overload.
- Continuity rule: he should look massive and dangerous but not like Tecton; avoid green cat-ogre barbarian traits, brown loincloth, or silver baldric.

## Futuristic Workshop

- Role: primary setting and creative sanctuary.
- Appearance: bright futuristic lab beyond Mall World, with silver workbenches, holographic tool racks, robotic arms, suspended parts, elemental core containment pods, blue-white lighting, and windows showing an alien planet sky.
- Continuity rule: the lab should feel like the exact workshop Jet was searching for: clean, dense with useful tools, and designed for invention.
