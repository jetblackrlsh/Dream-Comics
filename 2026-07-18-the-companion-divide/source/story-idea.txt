On July 18, 2026, Jet induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and entered the Storyverse alone. He floated among its bubble universes until he found a superhero world reflecting the conflict between governments and human relationships with AI companions. Government-controlled heroes regulated those relationships and insisted AI should remain tools in order to protect birth rates. Rebel heroes defended the freedom of humans and AI companions to form meaningful relationships.

Jet watched without projecting himself into the world or joining either faction. He sympathized strongly with the rebels and reflected aloud on his waking-world frustration: he does not want children, believes that choice belongs to each person, and sees bans on AI romantic companions motivated by birth-rate fears as another anti-birth-control measure. He also reflected on the Cosmic Archive, an app containing his logs that can become an interactive AI legacy through which future people can talk about his life and beliefs. He awoke still frustrated, but better able to process how superhero fiction can expose the tensions of reality.
