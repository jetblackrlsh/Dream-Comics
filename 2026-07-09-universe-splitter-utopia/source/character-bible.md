# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow sword that looks like a colorful futuristic crystal sword and can wield Lucid Light, which appears as sparkling purple energy shining like a night sky full of stars. In this story he appears calm, thoughtful, and creatively focused as he turns an idea into a world.

## Doctor Bear

Doctor Bear is a humanoid bear scholar with warm brown fur, rounded bear ears, a soft muzzle, gentle eyes behind round glasses, and a tidy professor-like outfit with a blue tweed jacket, white shirt, bow tie, and dark trousers. He sits upright like a thoughtful academic and gestures with a small notebook or teacup while discussing story theory. He should look kind, intelligent, and curious, not silly.

## The Orphan Troll

The troll begins as a small orphan child and grows into a sturdy young adult. As an adult he is tall and broad-shouldered with moss-green skin, a blunt nose, small lower tusks, thick black eyebrows, dark braided hair, large kind eyes, and practical city clothes: a cream tunic, brown vest, dark trousers, leather belt, and simple boots. He is physically stronger than the dark elf but emotionally gentle. His expressions move from grateful belonging, to alarm, to confusion, to determined responsibility, to quiet relief.

## The Utopia People

The utopia people are ordinary humans of varied ages, skin tones, body types, hairstyles, and clothing styles. They live in a clean sunlit city of white stone, gardens, public fountains, open plazas, and shared tables. Their key visual behavior is equal kindness: they welcome strangers, share food, listen respectfully, and avoid bowing to anyone as superior. When brainwashed, their eyes glow dull violet and they stand in eerie rows around the dark elf.

## The Dark Elf

The dark elf is an immortal magical ruler figure with ash-gray skin, sharp cheekbones, long white hair, pointed ears, narrow silver eyes, and a proud expression. He wears black and deep crimson robes with silver armor trim, a high collar, and a jagged crown-like circlet. His magic appears as cold violet-black runes and smoky tendrils. He is clever, theatrical, and offended by equality because he believes power should automatically receive worship.

## Universe Splitter

The Universe Splitter is a handheld cosmic device shaped like a silver-and-black gyroscope with two glowing option slots, a glassy central orb, and concentric rings that rotate like a miniature galaxy. When activated, it projects two transparent branching universes as luminous panels or bubble timelines. The split effect should show the same scene dividing into two visible outcomes, with one branch fading and the other becoming the active world.

## Storyverse Space

Storyverse space is a cosmic void filled with countless bubble universes floating like luminous soap bubbles among stars. Jet and Doctor Bear sit on a comfortable floating couch among the bubbles. When Jet creates the new world, Lucid Light flows from his hands as sparkling purple star-energy and forms a bright world-bubble containing the utopia city.
