# Page Script — Magmagator: Mall World Meltdown

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Jet, the twin, Magmagator, the resistance woman, the AR glasses, and both superhero suits must follow the character bible exactly on every appearance.

## Page 1 — Cover

Explosive vertical Mall World atrium composition. Yellow-and-blue superhero Jet flies down from the upper left with golden energy around one fist and sparkling purple Lucid Light around his body. His purple-skinned twin rockets from the upper right in the red-and-black suit with violet-red electricity. Below them, the colossal volcanic-rock alligator Magmagator rears through shattered mall balconies, orange magma glowing through its black plates. Jet’s long dreadlocks and full beard remain clearly visible; the twin’s large afro and white lightning highlight are unmistakable. Visible cover text only: “MAGMAGATOR” as the large title, “MALL WORLD MELTDOWN” as a smaller subtitle, and “JULY 17, 2026”. No issue number.

## Page 2 — Lucid Interface

Four panels. Jet lies in a dark bedroom during sleep paralysis as purple starry Lucid Light opens a threshold. Storyverse bubble universes float briefly in a cosmic void. Jet arrives alone on an upper Mall World balcony in his exact casual outfit and blue AR display sunglasses. He materializes a thin handheld touchpad from Lucid Light. A close panel shows his finger moving on the touchpad while a translucent cursor and Mall World directory hover across the blue lenses; the restroom symbol is selected.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and entered the Storyverse alone.”
- “I arrived inside Mall World wearing blue-lens sunglasses that displayed an augmented reality interface.”
- “I materialized a handheld touchpad, giving me precise mouse control over everything the glasses displayed.”
- “Then I needed a bathroom, so I followed the directory into a nearby corridor.”

## Page 3 — The Infinite Bathroom

Five panels. Jet enters a stained bathroom doorway while still lucid. The room stretches into impossible repeated rows of stalls, sinks, lights, pipes, and puddles. The AR directory breaks into visual static, the touchpad dissolves, and purple Lucid Light fades away. Jet looks dazed and disgusted as he walks deeper, unable to remember that this is Mall World. A low final panel shows dark swamp-green alligator eyes and a snout beginning to press upward through cracked tile behind him.

Caption text:

- “The moment I entered the bathroom, my lucidity slipped away.”
- “Filthy sinks, broken stalls, and flickering lights repeated farther than I could see.”
- “It looked exactly like the infinite bathrooms people describe in dreams of Mall World.”
- “I had visited Mall World before, but without lucidity I could not clearly understand where I was.”
- “While I felt powerless and confused, something impossible began rising through the floor.”

## Page 4 — Magmagator Restores Lucidity

Five panels. A realistic alligator tears up through bathroom tiles. It grows rapidly, black volcanic plates covering its body while orange magma fissures blaze open. Walls and ceiling burst outward into Mall World’s multilevel atrium as the creature becomes a colossal alligator kaiju. Jet’s eyes sharpen as purple starry Lucid Light returns around him. His casual clothes transform in bright geometric bands into the exact royal-blue and golden-yellow superhero suit, with his uncovered bearded face, long dreadlocks, narrow blue lenses, and starburst chest emblem clear. In the final panel he launches upward as Magmagator roars below.

Caption text:

- “An alligator emerged from the floor and began growing beyond every reasonable limit.”
- “Its scales became volcanic stone, and rivers of magma burned through its enormous body.”
- “That impossible leap in dream logic made me realize I was dreaming, restoring my lucidity all at once.”
- “I stopped feeling powerless as a yellow-and-blue superhero suit materialized around my body.”
- “I flew straight at the monster I immediately understood as Magmagator.”

## Page 5 — Too Strong Alone

Five panels. Jet blasts Magmagator’s head with golden energy edged in purple stars. The kaiju barely moves and swats him through a decorative mall sign. Jet recovers in the air but a concentrated magma breath forces him back, his suit scorched but intact. Magmagator’s tail pins him against a cracked balcony while he strains unsuccessfully to lift it. In the final diagonal panel, the purple-skinned twin crashes through the skylight like a red-violet meteor and punches the tail away. The twin’s afro and white lightning highlight read clearly.

Caption text:

- “I attacked with flight, strength, and lucid energy, expecting my new power to be enough.”
- “Magmagator absorbed my strongest strike and knocked me across the atrium.”
- “Its molten breath and crushing tail proved that I could not handle the kaiju alone.”
- “Then a purple-skinned stranger in red and black crashed into Mall World and freed me.”
- “I had never met them, but I immediately knew the stranger was my evil twin.”

## Page 6 — Combined Strength

Five dynamic panels. Jet and the twin hover opposite each other for one tense glance, then turn toward the common threat. The twin wraps Magmagator’s jaws in red-violet electrical force while Jet catches the lava tail with golden-purple energy. They spin the monster away from the occupied-looking mall corridors and smash it into the empty central fountain. In the large final panel both twins fire intersecting energy beams—golden starry power and crimson-violet lightning—into the kaiju’s chest, extinguishing its molten cracks until it collapses as harmless cooling volcanic stone.

Caption text:

- “I did not understand the twin’s motive, but there was no time to refuse their help.”
- “My twin restrained Magmagator’s jaws while I stopped its tail from destroying more of the mall.”
- “We moved with the same instincts, each power covering the weakness in the other’s attack.”
- “Our combined energy overwhelmed the monster where neither of us could have succeeded alone.”
- “Magmagator’s molten body cooled into silent black stone, and Mall World was safe.”

## Page 7 — Not Evil, Just Indifferent

Five panels. Amid the cooling stone, Jet faces the twin and asks why an evil counterpart helped. Use a small quote-caption for the question. A stylized explanation panel shows an alien planet and two infant-like escape capsules traveling toward Earth, with adult Jet and the twin as symbolic silhouettes. Another panel shows factories poisoning a future skyline while polluted sludge and smoke form the outlines of multiple monsters, including Magmagator. The twin explains in quote-captions that they are not truly evil and do not care about Earth because it is not their home. The twin flies away through the broken skylight while Jet remains on a Mall World balcony, looking at corporate names on the restored AR interface and a teal resistance symbol, choosing to investigate.

Caption text:

- “After the fight, I asked: ‘Why would my evil twin come here to save me?’”
- “My twin explained that we came from an alien planet and had both been sent to Earth with extraordinary powers.”
- “Our mission was to fight monsters created from the dystopian pollutants of Earth’s future.”
- “They answered: ‘I am not truly evil. I simply do not care about Earth, because Earth is not our home.’”
- “We went separate ways. I stayed to investigate the polluting companies and the resistance trying to stop them.”

## Page 8 — The Resistance and the Alarm

Five panels. In a quieter Mall World corridor, hero-form Jet studies corporate data through his blue AR lenses. The resistance woman with light-brown skin, very long curly black hair, and a fitted light-brown dress steps from a hidden doorway and shows a teal circuit insignia card. She looks admiringly at his glowing starburst emblem and moves close. Jet recognizes that she is pretty but politely leans away as she aggressively reaches toward his chest; there is no kiss or sexual contact. Red digital alarm shapes suddenly fracture the corridor. Final panel: Jet wakes in his real bed as dawn light enters, his phone alarm glowing nearby, while a faint reflection in the window shows the two superhero silhouettes and cooling Magmagator stone.

Caption text:

- “A woman from the resistance found me while I searched for information about the polluting companies.”
- “She had long curly black hair, light-brown skin, and a light-brown dress, and she was clearly attracted to my power.”
- “She was pretty, but she was not necessarily my type.”
- “Her approach became very aggressive, and I leaned away as the dream began breaking apart.”
- “My alarm went off, and I woke before I could learn anything more about the resistance.”
