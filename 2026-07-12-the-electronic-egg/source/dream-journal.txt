Dream journal date: 07/12/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream continues the Storyverse continuity that began on 06/11/2025. I arrived alone in space and sat on a red couch beside Doctor Bear, a humanoid brown bear who is also a doctor. We discussed what it meant to be a god. Divine power seemed to have no inherent morality. I love Earth, even though some people there hate me simply because of who I am. I would not wrathfully smite them with Lucid Light, but I could turn people who hate and destroy into people who love and create. That would violate their free will. Yet in a world where I can control anything, do they truly have free will? Dream characters still seem conscious and able to choose for themselves.

A sasquatch in a red jersey and blue cape flew past carrying what looked like a football. I chased him out of curiosity. He feared I wanted to steal it. I reassured him by turning into a humanoid blue cat version of myself that more closely resembled him, and we played catch. As a token of friendship, he gave me the object. It was an electronic egg that connects realities and games.

The egg pulled me inside. I joined a superhero team fighting a supervillain who could manifest things into reality. The villain sent a runaway train and a missile at us. None of the superpowered heroes could stop the train. Midnight Archer, a gothic cat-themed superhero archer with no powers, hacked the missile with a trick arrow and redirected it into the train. Both threats were destroyed and the villain fled.

Seeing a mortal among godlike beings save everyone showed me that power is not everything. The dream was about power in relation to empathy and a creative mind.

Jet: A man with dark-brown skin, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a Dream Anime graphic tee. He wears black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He has warm brown eyes, a colorful futuristic crystal rainbow sword, and a rope-like energy tether made of golden glittery light. He wields Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a star-filled night sky.
