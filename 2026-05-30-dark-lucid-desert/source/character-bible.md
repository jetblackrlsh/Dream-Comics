# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries the Sword of Life, a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword, and can summon a golden glittering energy tether. He wields Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he is alone in a desert, first confident, then endangered after the sword is stolen, then calm and tactical.

## Sword of Life

A colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword with radiant healing power. Its blade shines with layered rainbow facets, clean white highlights, and healthy Lucid Light that pushes back corruption.

Continuity notes: The Sword of Life is one of the most powerful weapons in the Storyverse. It heals Jet's wounds early in the issue, is stolen by the werewolf's lasso, animates the sand steed when misused, and returns to Jet on the final page.

## Lucid Light Watch

A transformation watch created from Lucid Light. It is a sleek futuristic wrist device made of purple starry energy, rainbow crystal facets, and a glowing circular dial. The dial projects tiny holographic icons of Jet's crew forms.

Continuity notes: This is the same watch from 05/29/2026. It allows Jet to become Lucid Light Variants of his crew, abbreviated as LLV forms. It should glow with sparkling purple Lucid Light whenever Jet selects a form.

## LLV Savannah

Jet transformed into a Lucid Light Variant of Savannah: a pale-skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, blunt bangs covering her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. She wields the Death Sword, a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword. Jet's identity can appear as purple starry Lucid Light within the form or as a small echo of his warm brown eyes in the transformation glow.

Continuity notes: Long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. The Death Sword does not kill the wolves; it kills the corruption infecting them, separating dark crystal energy from living animals.

## LLV Skelebot

Jet transformed into a Lucid Light Variant of Skelebot: a humanoid speedster robot body built from white bone-like robotic limbs, chrome joints, and purple starry Lucid Light seams. Jet's dreadlocks and warm brown eyes can appear as ghostly Lucid Light echoes around the form to maintain identity.

Continuity notes: The form is lean, fast, aerodynamic, and heroic. Show speed trails, afterimages, and desert sand whipping behind him. Avoid making it a horror skeleton.

## LLV Chipper

Jet transformed into a Lucid Light Variant of Chipper: a small levitating silver crab robot with compact claws, tiny flight thrusters, purple Lucid Light seams, and agile hovering movement. The form is much smaller than Jet and can slip under large enemies and vehicles.

Continuity notes: LLV Chipper's key advantages are small size, flight, agility, and claws. The decisive action is flying beneath the sand steed and clawing off its sand legs.

## Cowboy Werewolf

The antagonist first appears as a lone desert cowboy in a weathered hat, long duster coat, boots, gloves, and a coiled lasso. He transforms into a tall muscular werewolf corrupted by dark Lucid Light crystals, with shaggy gray-brown fur, glowing violet eyes, jagged purple-black crystal growths, and cowboy gear torn around the monstrous form.

Continuity notes: The werewolf is the source of the pack's corruption and the leader of the wolves. His lasso steals the Sword of Life. When cornered, he misuses the Sword of Life to animate desert sand into a living horse.

## Corrupted Wolves

A pack of desert wolves infected with dark Lucid Light crystals. They have gray and tan fur, glowing violet eyes, jagged purple-black crystal growths along their shoulders and spines, and smoky starry corruption leaking from their jaws and claws.

Continuity notes: The wolves are dangerous but should read as living animals being controlled by corruption, not evil monsters beyond rescue. After LLV Savannah strikes the corruption, they return to normal wolves and flee or watch calmly from a distance.

## Purified Wolves

The same wolves after the dark Lucid Light corruption is killed. Their eyes are natural, the crystal growths are gone, and their posture shifts from aggressive attack to wary calm.

Continuity notes: The purification should be visually clear: dark crystals shatter or evaporate away from the animals while the wolves remain alive.

## Sand Steed

A living horse animated from desert sand by the stolen Sword of Life. It has a horse silhouette made of swirling tan sand, glowing rainbow veins from the sword's stolen life energy, and hooves that kick up dune waves as it gallops.

Continuity notes: The sand steed is not a real animal. When LLV Chipper cuts off its sand legs, it collapses safely back into a pile of ordinary sand.

## Storyverse Desert

A desert bubble universe inside the Storyverse. It has red-gold dunes, cracked dry earth, distant mesas, cosmic bubble universes faintly visible in the sky, and purple starry Lucid Light corruption spreading through dark crystal veins in the sand.

Continuity notes: Keep the Storyverse identity visible through surreal sky bubbles and cosmic void hints beyond the desert horizon.
