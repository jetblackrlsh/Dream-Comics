On July 22, 2026, Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and enters the continuing Storyverse. He begins inside a friendly wood-paneled sentient jacuzzi in the Arctic. When Jet wonders whether quiet relaxation can compare with superhero adventure, the jacuzzi activates hover pads and flies into the Aurora Borealis. A steampunk war train fires on them, then a cowboy pursues them on a flying mechanical horse. Jet protects and heals himself and the jacuzzi with the Sword of Light, uses his golden energy tether to interface with the horse, and ends the pursuit by triggering its self-destruct system.

Jet then reaches a living room floating in the sky. Instead of watching television, he draws privately in a purple felt notebook using a magical pink pencil. His watercolor-like picture shows a green plant stalk topped by a radiant star with sunset colors around it. The act of making something only for himself feels meaningful. Living vines grow around his arms into a guitar, the sky turns blue with fluffy pink clouds, and Jet sings that other people's anger cannot give them ownership over his imagination. The song's hook is: “Cry me a river, bleed me a lake. You can’t take or control what I make.”
