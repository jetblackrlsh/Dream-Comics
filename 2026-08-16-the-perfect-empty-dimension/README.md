# 08/16/2026 - The Perfect Empty Dimension

Dream Comics entry for August 16, 2026.

While flying above a city, Jet initially dismisses a giant green slime monster's theatrics until it reveals a woman trapped inside its tentacle. He uses Silonis, the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life, to fuse hostage and captor into a single slime woman long enough to neutralize the danger, then restores their separate identities and banishes the creature through Porta.

Jet sends the slime monster to an Empty Dimension intended for solitary reflection—but realizes that solitude sounds appealing to him. Inside, he divides into an observer and a resident self, entering a quietly perfect digital world where no one judges his unusual routines. When the resident self tempts him to remain forever, Jet consciously chooses his waking responsibilities and allows himself to wake.
