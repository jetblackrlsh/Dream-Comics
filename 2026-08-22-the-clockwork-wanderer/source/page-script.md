# Page Script - The Clockwork Wanderer

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, the astronaut, and the clockwork barbarians follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic full-page composition on the clockwork planet. Jet stands amid enormous bronze gears, surrounded by sparkling purple Lucid Light. Above him, the white-suited astronaut woman flies on her unfolded winged jetpack while a cobalt gauntlet reflects red laser fire toward three pink-skinned barbarians. Ancient fused spacecraft form a planetary curve against the stars.

Visible cover text only: “THE CLOCKWORK WANDERER” and “AUGUST 22, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - A Planet Made of Ships

A clear four-panel establishing page. Show ancient analog spacecraft drifting together in space, gravity accumulating them into a sphere, Jet walking across the completed world of gears and hulls, and hints of pirate treasure beneath the machinery.

Caption text:

- “Old analog spacecraft had drifted together until gravity forged their wreckage into a planet.”
- “Bronze gears, golden mechanisms, and black ship hulls turned beneath my feet.”
- “Many of the fused vessels had belonged to pirates, leaving old credits and forgotten antiques buried inside.”
- “I came to explore the clockwork world—until laser fire revealed someone fighting alone.”

## Page 3 - The Mysterious Astronaut

A clear four-panel character-and-danger page. Introduce the astronaut's slim white polymer suit, opaque visor, glowing green orbs, and cobalt ringed gauntlets. Then reveal three muscular pink-skinned barbarian women firing red laser blasts as their feet crack the mechanical ground.

Caption text:

- “The stranger wore a slim white polymer astronaut suit, solid and sleek instead of bulky fabric.”
- “Her mirrored helmet concealed her face, while unexplained green orbs glowed along her arms and legs.”
- “Pink-skinned, red-eyed barbarians surrounded her with laser blasters and overwhelming strength.”
- “Every step cracked the metal beneath them. One direct punch could have destroyed her helmet.”

## Page 4 - A Defense Made of Their Weapons

A clear four-panel action page. The astronaut raises both cobalt ringed gauntlets. Blue circular fields reflect incoming red blasts. Two barbarians are knocked down without gore, and the remaining attackers escape in enormous leaps across the horizon.

Caption text:

- “She carried no blaster of her own, but her cobalt gauntlets could redirect theirs.”
- “Blue energy caught each laser blast and hurled it back toward the attackers.”
- “Two barbarians fell, and the others fled before their own weapons could strike them.”
- “They crossed the horizon in colossal leaps, leaving the astronaut alive—but still stranded.”

## Page 5 - Everyone Wants the Treasure

A clear four-panel conversation-and-worldbuilding page. Jet approaches peacefully. The astronaut gestures toward treasure chests inside an exposed pirate hull. Show old credits and analog antiques while distant rival treasure hunters search the planet.

Caption text:

- “I asked what had brought her to such a dangerous planet.”
- “Treasure had brought everyone: forgotten credits and priceless relics from the analog age of space travel.”
- “I assumed the hunters were fighting because none of them wanted to share the fortune.”
- “The truth was more immediate. The barbarians had stolen her ship.”

## Page 6 - Rescue Refused

A clear four-panel emotional page. Show the astronaut beside an empty landing place, Jet opening a safe purple portal home, her calmly refusing it with one raised hand, and Jet considering her vulnerability without pressuring her.

Caption text:

- “Without her spacecraft, she had no ordinary way to leave the hostile clockwork world.”
- “I offered to open a portal and take her somewhere safe.”
- “She refused the handout, thanked me, and insisted that she could manage alone.”
- “Her answer surprised me, but her independence belonged to her—not to me.”

## Page 7 - Help Without Taking Control

A clear four-panel creation page. Jet shapes sparkling purple Lucid Light behind the astronaut. A compact white, cobalt, and gold jetpack forms. She studies and accepts it. In the final panel its mechanical wings unfold while the green suit orbs and cobalt gauntlets glow.

Caption text:

- “I searched for help that would expand her choices instead of replacing them.”
- “My Lucid Light became a jetpack designed for her suit and her dangerous mission.”
- “It could evade a crushing punch, match a barbarian's leap, or carry her away from the planet.”
- “This time she accepted. Mechanical wings unfolded, ready to answer only to her.”

## Page 8 - Beyond the Horizon

A clear four-panel resolution page ending in a large panoramic panel. The astronaut rises on purple-white thrusters, thanks Jet with a respectful gesture, and flies over the planet's curved horizon. Jet watches from the gears as she becomes a bright distant figure against space.

Caption text:

- “The jetpack lifted her above the gears on wings of metal and Lucid Light.”
- “She thanked me, but she did not ask for a rescuer, a partner, or a sidekick.”
- “Our crossover ended where her next adventure began.”
- “The clockwork wanderer disappeared beyond the horizon—still mysterious, still independent, and free to choose her own way.”

## Caption checklist

- Every caption is required verbatim, unnumbered, and enclosed in a complete rectangular cream box.
