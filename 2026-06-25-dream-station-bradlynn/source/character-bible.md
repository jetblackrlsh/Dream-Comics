# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: lucid dream explorer and narrator.
- Face and body: dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Outfit: black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag.
- Props: compact dream camera used throughout this story, rainbow futuristic crystal sword at his side, golden glittering energy tether visible when the dream threshold appears.
- Expression range: curious, calm, thoughtful, amused by Bradlynn's impatience, quietly determined when choosing exploration.

## Bradlynn

- Role: Dream Station clerk who logs dreamers' desired dreams.
- Face and body: green skin, diamond shaped head, white oval eyes with no visible pupils, lean alien build.
- Outfit: metallic blue beanie, red button up shirt, yellow pants, red shoes.
- Props: clipboard and pen, office desk full of forms, destination cards, filing cabinets, glowing dream route maps.
- Expression range: impatient, dryly unimpressed, relieved by short answers, slightly annoyed when Jet decides to stay.

## False Awakening Woman One

- Role: apartment listener who remembers the Dream Station from her own dreams.
- Design: adult woman with medium-brown skin, short curly auburn hair, round glasses, soft green cardigan, thoughtful posture.
- Expression range: intrigued, half-remembering, quietly amazed.

## False Awakening Woman Two

- Role: apartment listener who would rather party than analyze dream systems.
- Design: adult woman with light skin, long blonde ponytail, shiny purple party jacket, black leggings, bright earrings.
- Expression range: amused, dismissive, restless, eager for a party.

## Key Settings

- Alien planet: yellow sunset sky, futuristic silver city skyline packed with skyscrapers, rural isolated island on the outskirts, docks, bridges, quiet water, metallic rails.
- Dream Station office: functional alien office with silver walls, glowing map screens, stacks of forms, clipboards, destination cards, and route diagrams for dreamers.
- Neon Crystal Nightclub: shown as a tempting destination preview, not the main setting; neon magenta and cyan light, crystalline dance floor, crowded silhouettes.
- False awakening apartment: ordinary modern apartment with warm lamp light, couch, windows, and faint dream afterglow.
