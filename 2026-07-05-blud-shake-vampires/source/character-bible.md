# Character Bible

## Vampire Jet

Vampire Jet keeps Jet's core identity: dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a darker gothic version of Jet's outfit: black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. His vampire traits are sharp fangs, slightly glowing crimson eyes during action, confident predatory posture, and faint sparkling purple Lucid Light around him. He does not carry the story with normal blood drinking; the Blud Shake is his substitute.

## Vampire Johnson

Vampire Johnson is a red humanoid goldfish with a fish face and orange-goldfish silhouette shifted into red vampire coloring. He wears a black version of Johnson's normal astronaut suit without a helmet. He has small fangs, expressive fish eyes, and fast nervous body language. He remains clearly Johnson in every page where he appears, with the same astronaut-suit shape and helmetless fish head.

## King Blud

King Blud is a large imposing humanoid king made from a transparent membrane filled with thick red Blud Shake liquid. His body looks like a powerful walking vessel of the drink, with liquid sloshing inside him. He wears royal castle accents such as a crown, heavy belt, and dark cape, and he carries a mighty two-handed axe until Vampire Jet snaps the handle. He should feel arrogant and threatened by Jet's confidence.

## Tiny Fusion

The tiny fusion is created when holy weapons cut chunks from Vampire Jet and Vampire Johnson's mist forms. It is a small, fast, feral fusion of both of them: miniature dark-brown vampire face and beard hints from Jet, red goldfish features from Johnson, tiny dreadlock-like wisps, black tiny outfit, red fish-vampire skin patches, small fangs, and swirling purple-red mist. It is powerful enough to defeat knights but looks hungry and hard to control.

## Castle Knights

The castle knights wear heavy dark medieval armor with holy silver trim, glowing cross-like weapon engravings, and bright golden-white holy weapons. Their swords, spears, and axes can cut chunks from mist forms, which must read clearly as unusual and dangerous. Most knights are defeated by the tiny fusion inside the cellar.

## Final Knight

The final knight is the strongest knight. He is taller and more imposing than the others, with heavier armor, a torn cape, a furious expression visible through a helmet opening, and the brightest holy weapon. Outside the castle at sunrise, he is dangerous enough that Vampire Jet knows they cannot beat him while sunlight is burning them. After Jet bites him, he becomes a vampire knight with darker armor, fangs, red eyes, and smoke rising from his armor in the sunlight.

## Blud Shake Cellar

The cellar is a gothic castle storage room full of shelves, barrels, crates, and glass bottles labeled visually as red Blud Shakes without relying on readable labels. The drinks are thick red blood-substitute shakes in sealed bottles with wax caps. The setting should make the central rule obvious: this is King Blud's hoarded supply, and Vampire Jet only wants one bottle a day.

## Vampire Transformations

Vampire mist appears as dark purple-red vapor with visible faces and silhouettes inside it. Holy weapons cut little glowing chunks out of the mist. Wolf forms are dark vampire wolves with recognizable color cues: Jet's wolf has dark dreadlock-like mane hints and purple Lucid Light; Johnson's wolf has red fishlike facial markings; the fusion's wolf is tiny and feral; the turned knight's wolf wears broken armor fragments. Bat forms keep the same color cues and dive into dark holes for shelter.
