# Treatment

## Logline

After sleep paralysis sends Jet into a medieval Storyverse kingdom where a lion king enslaves tax debtors to mine elemental cores, Jet summons an ogre-attracting dinner bell and turns a throne-stealing energy vampire into a ruler too afraid to abuse his new power.

## Central Conflict

Jet arrives alone in a fantasy kingdom warped by a tyrant's elemental greed. The king's tax system feeds a debtor camp, and the elemental cores mined by the desperate are sewn into his royal robes. Jet can confront the king directly, but the dream gives him a more lucid solution: make the tyrant's stolen power call something stronger than him. The danger is that the summoned ogre may simply replace one corrupt ruler with another.

## Theme

Power can change hands without justice changing at all, unless someone stronger forces accountability.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins as a quiet ghostly observer, stunned by the cruelty of the debtor camp. His grief sharpens into lucid strategy when he realizes the king's elemental cores are also bait. The falling ogre creates a spectacular reversal, but victory curdles when the ogre moves toward the throne with the same hunger. Jet's reputation from recent ogre battles lets him resolve the dream without another fight: not by becoming king, but by making abuse too costly.

## Ending

The energy-vampire ogre drains the lion king's cores and takes the throne. When he recognizes Jet, the ogre's confidence collapses. Jet stands in purple Lucid Light before the freed prisoners and gives one warning: rule fairly, or be defeated. The final page shows the debtor camp opening, the elemental mines going dark, and the new ruler kept in line by fear of Jet's return.
