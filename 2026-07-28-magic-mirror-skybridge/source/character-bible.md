# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword: a colorful futuristic crystal sword with a broad translucent faceted blade showing saturated red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet, plus a dark silver geometric hilt.
- Carries the Golden Energy Tether: a flexible rope made entirely of glittering golden light. It is never a mechanical cable, chain, grappling hook, or solid fiber.
- Uses Lucid Light: sparkling purple reality-warping energy filled with tiny stars like a night sky.
- Expression range: lucid awakening, analytical curiosity, delighted discovery, responsible concern, focused power, overwhelming awe, frustrated concentration, inventive certainty, sober victory.
- Jet never wears a crown, cape, mask, helmet, conventional superhero costume, medieval armor, or unrelated clothing.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, face, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, shirt, pants, sneakers, belt bag, sword, and tether on every page.

## The enchantress

- Adult woman with extremely pale skin, a narrow expressive face, and very long, messy, voluminous sky-blue hair.
- Thick, uneven sky-blue bangs cross her forehead beneath a tall pointed blue witch hat. Her long messy hair and pointed hat are essential identifying features.
- Eyes glow with brilliant blue magical light, with small sparkling flecks around the lashes.
- Wears a fitted, sparkling royal-blue and azure medieval-fantasy outfit: high-collared tunic, long split sleeves, layered short cape, silver star clasps, dark blue leggings, and blue boots.
- Channels blue magical light through her hands and mirrors. Her blue magic is visually distinct from Jet's purple star-filled Lucid Light and the life stones' rainbow-white radiance.
- Expression range: scholarly fascination, patient explanation, astonishment at the mine, pride in the transformed town, skepticism about Drillov, alarm at the titans, collaborative concentration, determined guardianship.
- Never depict her as elderly, blonde, brunette, armored, robed entirely in black, wearing a broad-brimmed modern hat, or carrying a broom.
- Continuity rule: keep her extremely pale skin, glowing blue eyes, long messy sky-blue hair with bangs, tall pointed hat, and sparkling blue outfit unchanged.

## Rainbow Crystal Sword / Life Sword

- One consistent futuristic crystal sword carried by Jet.
- Broad translucent faceted blade with saturated rainbow colors from red through violet, a dark silver geometric hilt, and a radiant rainbow-white aura.
- The blade visually resembles the natural life stones but is more refined, technological, and precisely engineered.
- It is the life-giving opposite of Savannah's Death Sword, but Savannah and the Death Sword do not physically appear in this comic.

## Life stones and mine

- Naturally occurring translucent crystals with irregular mineral shapes and internal bands of red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet.
- Each crystal glows with warm rainbow-white life energy and contains tiny moving sparks, visibly resembling the material of Jet's sword without looking like finished swords.
- The mine is a vast cavern directly beneath the town, with timber supports, rough stone tunnels, underground streams, and enormous veins of rainbow life crystal.
- Avoid generic diamonds, single-color gemstones, radioactive green rocks, or crystals already shaped like weapons.

## Magic mirrors

- Large mirrors: ornate medieval frames of carved silver-blue metal and dark wood, reflective surfaces filled with calm blue-violet magical light, and a friendly abstract face made from light when answering.
- Pocket mirrors: palm-sized hinged silver mirrors with round reflective glass, blue crystal accents, and the same small friendly light-face when active.
- The mirrors show useful images, maps, diagrams, and distant scenes as part of their enchanted reflections.
- They never look like modern tablets, phones, flat monitors, or glass rectangles without frames. No modern keyboards or printed interface text.

## Village golems

- Human-sized work golems made from warm gray stone blocks, carved medieval joints, blue life-stone cores, and simple friendly faces.
- Different golems carry baskets, lift beams, repair roofs, move carts, and protect villagers.
- They resemble practical magical robots rather than enormous war machines.
- Keep the same gray stone, blue core, and friendly carved facial design across all pages.

## Drillov

- Adult male raider enclosed in a bulky futuristic gunmetal mechanical suit, roughly ten feet tall.
- A small armored cockpit around his head reveals a hard human face with tan skin, shaved dark hair, and one glowing orange cybernetic monocle.
- Both mechanical forearms end in enormous conical steel drills instead of hands. Each drill is longer than his torso and marked by orange heat vents.
- Armor has industrial yellow hazard stripes, heavy pistons, mud splashes, and a glowing orange power core.
- His silhouette must always emphasize the two giant drill hands. Never give him normal hands, medieval clothing, a cape, or life-stone-powered rainbow armor.
- After Jet's attack, the suit is crushed inward and disabled, but Drillov remains visibly alive inside the protected cockpit.

## Alien robot titans

- Exactly three colossal extraterrestrial machines, each so tall that the entire medieval town appears microscopic below them.
- Towering purple-violet armored bodies with black biomechanical joints, magenta energy seams, elongated angular heads, broad shoulders, enormous clawed hands, and legs like mountain-sized pylons.
- Their proportions are alien rather than human military robots. Their surfaces include curved violet plates, luminous geometric channels, and unfamiliar floating components.
- All three share the same civilization's design language but have distinct head crests and chest patterns.
- The hacked titan is identified only after the lightning strike by branching gold-blue electrical veins across its purple armor and clear cyan eyes instead of magenta.
- Never shrink them to building scale, depict more or fewer than three, or turn them into ordinary human mecha, Transformers characters, or stone golems.

## Golden Energy Tether skybridge

- The tether begins in Jet's hand, wraps securely around the pocket mirror's silver frame, and extends upward as a continuous glittering golden rope into a cloud.
- Its mystical bond pulls surrounding clouds together into one enormous dark indigo storm cloud.
- Lightning carries visible blue-gold geometric magical information from the mirror through the tether, across the cloud, and down into one titan.
- The bridge must be spatially clear: pocket mirror → golden tether → storm cloud → lightning → alien titan.

## Medieval fantasy town

- Dense timber-and-stone homes, steep roofs, market square, narrow lanes, defensive wall, green hills, and a central blue-roofed enchantress tower.
- Early scenes use warm daylight, banners, gardens, carts, craftspeople, and practical village life.
- After transformation, ornate magic mirrors stand in homes and public squares, villagers carry pocket mirrors, and friendly gray golems work beside them.
- The life-stone mine lies directly beneath the same town and glows upward through protected shafts.

## Established Storyverse crew continuity

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's Storyverse team. None appears physically in this focused comic. Do not insert them into the town, mine, mirror visions, sky, titan battle, cover, or final page.
