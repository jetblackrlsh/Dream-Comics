# Treatment

## Title

06/19/2026: Num Num Berry Trance

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet enters the Storyverse while carrying waking-world discouragement, accepts emotion-warping Num Num Berries from a false comforter, and must survive a soul-eating hag and a trusted doctor hallucination until Second Brain helps him reclaim his mind.

## Central Conflict

Jet falls asleep dejected, making the lucid dream vulnerable to emotional distortion. The Num Num Berries promise chosen feelings, but they turn desire for comfort into a trance trap. The false young woman tries to consume Jet's soul, and even after Second Brain defeats her true hag form, the berries keep twisting Jet's perceptions through Odin, a doctor Jet trusts until he notices the intelligence gaps, the false memories, and the murderous intent behind the bedside manner.

## Theme

Negativity can root itself into a dream and bend the dreamer's trust against him, but lucidity grows stronger when a loyal part of the mind calls out at the right moment.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins lucid but heavy-hearted, carrying the ache that his waking creations may not matter to other people. The shining berry woman offers relief, and Jet lets himself believe in an easy emotional cure. The trance becomes seductive and numbing until Second Brain breaks through with urgent Lucid Lightning. Jet's shock turns to fear when the woman reveals herself as a soul-eating hag. After that apparent victory, Jet spirals into a quieter, more dangerous loop with Doctor Odin, whose false authority makes him seem safe. Jet's suspicion slowly returns, then snaps into lucidity when he recognizes Odin as another berry hallucination. The ending leaves Jet exhausted but grateful, resolving to enter sleep with a more positive mindset and honoring Second Brain's protection.

## Ending

Jet escapes Odin's collapsing clinic-hallucination as the last Num Num Berry sparks burn away. Second Brain guides him back to the open Storyverse, where Leon glows in the cosmic void and the bubble universes shimmer around them. Jet admits that the dream was a wild ride and recognizes the lesson: going to sleep dejected gave negativity room to distort the dream, but Second Brain was there when Jet needed help.

## Cast

- Jet: the lucid dreamer and protagonist. He is a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow futuristic crystal sword, a golden glittering energy tether, and sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Second Brain: a floating brain made of Lucid Light, shining with sparkling purple night-sky energy. He acts as Jet's loyal warning system and protector. His attacks manifest as Lucid Lightning neuro shocks: bright purple-white lightning with starry sparks.
- The Berry Woman / Succubus Hag: first appears as a beautiful young woman with an hourglass figure, curly silver permed hair, shiny green and blue jumpsuit, and piercing blue eyes. Her true form is an elderly soul-eating hag with the same silver curls turned wild, the same green-blue outfit torn and shadowed, blue eyes glowing cold, long grasping hands, and a smoky soul-hunger aura.
- Doctor Odin: a hallucinated doctor in a surreal clinic loop. He appears calm and trustworthy at first, with silver hair, a neat short beard, pale blue eyes, a white doctor's coat over dark clothes, and a staff-like medical cane. As Jet sees through him, his clinic distorts, his face becomes masklike, and his tools turn threatening.
- Leon: Jet's Ghostly UFO, shown briefly in the ending as a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow, waiting in the Storyverse void.
