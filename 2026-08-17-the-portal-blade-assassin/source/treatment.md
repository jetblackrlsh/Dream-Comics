# Treatment - The Portal-Blade Assassin

## Logline

When a magical breakthrough draws a teleporting assassin into his study, Jet must turn Silonis's life-sensing power against an enemy who can vanish between every strike—then win without killing or imprisoning them.

## Premise

The dream opens quietly inside Jet's magical study rather than with his usual superhero flight. He studies Silonis, the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life, and Savannah's Death Sword, asking why those two weapons stand above other enchanted blades.

Jet realizes that a normal sword could carry an inscription of Viva, Vita, Porta, or another spell. Such a weapon would genuinely channel life or portal magic, but it would still lack the rare crystals and futuristic systems that let a legendary artifact embody, amplify, and automate its power. Silonis is extraordinary because technology, living crystal, and magic operate as one.

## Discovery and worldbuilding

That distinction leads Jet to imagine a Storyverse planet made from portal crystals. Its gems supply massive shipping gates and personal teleporters across advanced civilizations, but the mines are catastrophically dangerous. The crystals constantly open spontaneous passages, allowing creatures and hazards from anywhere to emerge without warning. Miners therefore work under the protection of stationed heroes.

The portal-blade secret appears to connect ideas as readily as places. The moment Jet understands how such swords could exist, an unseen attacker detects the intellectual connection and comes to silence him.

## Central conflict and theme

The portal-blade assassin materializes, slashes Jet, and disappears before he can answer. Every dream strike coincides with a coughing fit in Jet's physical body, creating a rhythm between the Storyverse and waking reality.

Speed alone cannot solve the conflict. Jet uses Silonis to sense the assassin's life energy and perceive where they will reconnect with the room before they appear. He begins parrying every attack.

The theme is that connection can expose a person to danger, but deeper awareness can transform vulnerability into understanding. Jet defeats secrecy without becoming another executioner. His final act offers the assassin enough freedom to choose peace.

## Escalation

As their blades collide across a chain of portals, Jet concludes that a hidden clan hoards portal-sword knowledge by hunting anyone who independently discovers it. This explains why portal gems can be valuable and widespread while portal blades remain almost unknown.

Jet casts Porta and sends the assassin into the Empty Dimension. The assassin's own blade can open a route back, so this is not a conventional prison.

## Ending and emotional arc

Jet remembers that the Empty Dimension is a place without judgment where a person can exist freely. Jet could not remain there because he belongs to both the waking world and the Storyverse, but an assassin who knows only the Storyverse may experience it as the perfect world.

The final image shows the assassin lowering the portal blade instead of reopening a path. Jet does not win by trapping the enemy somewhere unbearable. He wins by giving them somewhere they no longer need to fight.
