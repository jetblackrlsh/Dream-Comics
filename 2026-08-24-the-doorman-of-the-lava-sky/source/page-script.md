# Page Script - The Doorman of the Lava Sky

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, Autumn, Apollo, the god of blades, the black-bone dinosaur, the god of deals, Meltronome, the dimensional elevator, the academy, and all magic follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic full-page composition. Jet stands in the foreground between two glowing Porta rings, surrounded by sparkling purple Lucid Light and gold-white divine energy. Behind him, Autumn stands inside the open dimensional elevator, a black-bone dinosaur rises beside a bone castle, the lightning rhinoceros strains between spatially separated portals, Apollo descends in his radiant chariot, and Meltronome fills the lava-lamp sky.

Visible cover text only: “THE DOORMAN OF THE LAVA SKY” and “AUGUST 24, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - A Wife I Could Not Remember

A clear three-panel establishing page. Show Jet and Autumn stepping from the freestanding elevator; Autumn explaining their relationship while Jet looks bewildered; then reveal the desert, black-bone castles, and molten lava-lamp sky.

Caption text:

- “I stepped from a dimensional elevator beside Autumn, a woman who insisted she was my wife.”
- “She remembered our adventures together. I remembered neither her nor my life as the Doorman.”
- “Outside waited a desert of black-bone castles beneath a sky sealed by living lava.”

## Page 3 - Apollo's Mission

A clear three-panel mission page. Show Autumn using golden magic; Apollo descending in his solar chariot through a small opening in the lava; then Jet left alone as Autumn disappears.

Caption text:

- “Autumn summoned Apollo, who said Meltronome's lava had imprisoned the world without sunlight.”
- “He charged us to defeat the lava god and reopen the sky.”
- “After naming me the dimensional elevator's Doorman, Autumn vanished—and I continued alone.”

## Page 4 - The Bone-Blade Kingdom

A clear three-panel obstacle-and-alliance page. Show the black-bone dinosaur swallowing Jet into its hollow ribs; Jet forming Lucid Light controls and piloting it; then the god of blades granting a black-bone sword after their negotiation.

Caption text:

- “A giant black-bone dinosaur tried to swallow me, but its hollow ribs became my cockpit.”
- “Lucid Light formed controls, letting me pilot the skeleton like a mech.”
- “The god of blades gave me safe passage—and a divine bone blade—when Meltronome's fall promised him greater rank.”

## Page 5 - The Bargain

A clear three-panel divine-deal page. Show the academy's waiting line and Jet bypassing it with the blade; the hulking gray businessman transferring gold-white magic into Jet; then the deal's sacrifice transforming the businessman into a lightning rhinoceros.

Caption text:

- “The blade carried me past an academy's waiting line to a hulking gray god in a business suit.”
- “The god of deals transferred his unlimited but conditional magic into me.”
- “As the bargain's price, he stole another student's power and transformed into a lightning rhinoceros.”

## Page 6 - Porta's Mercy

A clear three-panel reasoning-and-protection page. Show Jet understanding the rhinoceros's strategic escape from godhood; terrified academy students fleeing the transformed creature; then two linked purple portals harmlessly separating his intact upper body from his intact wandering legs.

Caption text:

- “I understood his escape: he traded vulnerable godhood for direct combat power.”
- “Yet the new rhinoceros terrified everyone he had left inside the academy.”
- “I cast Porta, sending his legs into the desert while his harmless upper body remained behind.”

## Page 7 - Breaking the Lava Sky

A clear three-panel climax page. Show gold-white deal magic merging with Jet's purple Lucid Light; Jet extending an immense spell beyond the academy; then Meltronome being cast away as the molten shell surrounding the world ruptures.

Caption text:

- “The deal god's magic had required bargains and sacrifices. My own magic required neither.”
- “Unlimited power flowed through Lucid Light as I reached beyond the academy and seized the lava-filled sky.”
- “With one immense spell, I cast Meltronome away and broke his molten prison around the world.”

## Page 8 - Sunlight and Missing Memories

A clear three-panel resolution page. Show sunlight flooding the black-bone desert; Apollo rising like the sun and declaring himself supreme; then Jet watching the distant dimensional elevator with a faint memory-like silhouette of Autumn.

Caption text:

- “Sunlight flooded the planet, and Apollo rose through the open sky like the sun itself.”
- “He declared himself the world's new supreme god. I could only hope his reign would be kinder.”
- “My mission ended—but the Doorman, Autumn, and the dimensional elevator still waited beyond my missing memories.”
