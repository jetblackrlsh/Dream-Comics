# Character Bible - The Sky-Iron Frog

## Jet

- African-American man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard and mustache, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Long dreadlocks spill from beneath a black hoodie worn over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt.
- Black athletic pants with one clean white stripe down each outer leg, white high-top sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build, observant posture, expressive brows, calm strategic focus, and compassion for the fairies.
- Uses Geomancy/Geokinesis through a grounded outstretched hand and visible mineral-force lines; the power moves stone and metal without resembling a conventional rune spell.

## Giant frog kaiju

- Enormous humanoid frog with dark moss-green wet skin, a pale olive throat, broad muscular limbs, webbed hands, and large gold-brown eyes with horizontal pupils.
- Its extremely long pink tongue lashes through the air to capture fairies. Never show gore or graphic eating.
- In the first attack it is unarmored. From its return onward it wears the same ominous black sky-iron samurai armor: layered shoulder plates, segmented chest and waist plates, forearm guards, shin guards, and a crested kabuto-like helmet.
- The armor has a cold meteorite sheen with tiny silver flecks and suppresses conventional magic.
- A thick glossy mucus membrane lies between the armor and skin, stretching like clear glue when Jet tries to pull the plates away.

## Pyrokinetic fairy

- Adult fairy woman with deep copper-brown skin, bright flame-orange swept-back hair, amber eyes, ember-pattern wings, and fitted red-and-gold leaf armor.
- Creates concentrated orange pyrokinetic fire from focused hands without runes or a wand.
- Brave, decisive, and always positioned as one of the three psychic front-line leaders.

## Cryokinetic fairy

- Adult fairy woman with pale blue skin, icy-blue hair in one thick braid, sapphire eyes, translucent crystalline wings, and fitted white-and-cyan crystal armor.
- Forms sharp blue-white cryokinetic ice from focused hands without runes or a wand.
- Controlled, precise, and visually consistent from page to page.

## Electrokinetic fairy

- Adult androgynous fairy with violet-brown skin, short dark-violet hair, glowing lavender eyes, amethyst wings, and fitted purple-and-silver leaf armor.
- Projects concentrated violet-white electrokinetic lightning from focused hands without runes or a wand.
- Fierce, agile, and completes the psychic trio's coordinated attack.

## Magical fairies and village

- A diverse community of adult fairies in vivid emerald, rose, gold, turquoise, and lavender palettes, each with distinct leaf, petal, or crystal clothing and luminous wings.
- Conventional magical attacks appear as ornate circular runes, sparkling dust, stars, and decorative spell glyphs. These effects fail against sky iron.
- The village consists of warm treehouse homes naturally grown into several different colossal magical trees, linked by branch bridges and surrounded by glowing flowers, floating pollen, and blue-violet night light.

## Continuity rules

- Jet's skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white shoes, and cross-body bag remain consistent on every page.
- The frog is unarmored only during the first attack. Its black samurai armor remains identical after the return and is never successfully removed.
- The pyrokinetic, cryokinetic, and electrokinetic fairies keep their assigned bodies, hair, wings, outfits, colors, and powers.
- No gore, speech bubbles, thought balloons, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, creator signatures, or blank caption boxes.
