Date: 07/29/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void.

My dream began in the same medieval fantasy village where my previous dream had ended. It was the village protected by the Blue Enchantress, a woman with long, messy sky-blue hair, shimmering blue eyes, a pointed witch’s hat, and a flowing blue dress that sparkled with magical light.

I was speaking with the Life Sword, the rainbow crystal sword I carried with me. The sword was alive and capable of flying independently, and I could stand on top of it and ride it through the air like a hoverboard.

After everything we had discovered beneath the village, the Blue Enchantress and I had reached a new understanding of the Life Stones and the true nature of the Life Sword.

Beneath the village was an enormous underground mine filled with Life Crystals. The crystals were made from the same rainbow-colored material as my sword. At first, we had believed that all Life Stones served the same basic purpose: they could be placed inside otherwise lifeless objects and used to animate them. A Life Stone could be placed inside a golem to bring it to life, or inside a magical mirror to transform it into an intelligent assistant.

However, when we traveled deeper into the mines, we discovered another kind of Life Crystal.

Far beneath the surface lived humanoid beings made entirely from rainbow crystal. They did not need to be placed inside anything because they were already alive. They were living Life Stones with minds, bodies, and a society of their own.

This meant that there were at least two different forms of rainbow crystal. The crystals found closer to the surface contained the power to imbue other things with life, while the crystal beings living in the deepest parts of the mine possessed life within themselves.

The Life Sword appeared to combine the qualities of both.

It was independently alive, which explained how it could fly, move on its own, and carry me through the air. At the same time, it could channel life energy into other beings and objects. That was the power I had primarily used throughout my adventures.

The sword also contained some form of advanced technology that we still did not fully understand. It was not merely a sword made from magical crystal. It appeared to be a fusion of living rainbow crystal, life-giving crystal, and mysterious futuristic technology.

This made the Life Sword extraordinarily special.

The Life Sword and the Death Sword were paired artifacts, and together they were considered the most powerful artifacts in the entire Storyverse. Now that word had spread about a bubble universe containing entire mines filled with Life Stones, the medieval village and its surrounding world had become a major target.

We had already defended the mines during the events of my previous dream, but we knew that more dangerous forces would eventually come searching for the crystals.

Before leaving, I used my golden energy tether to form a magical bond between my pocket magic mirror and the Blue Enchantress’s pocket magic mirror. The tether connected the two mirrors so that they could function like magical cellphones. From that point forward, the Blue Enchantress would be able to contact me whenever the village needed help.

I then summoned Leon, my ghostly green UFO.

Leon flew over the village and lowered his tractor beam, pulling me upward and bringing me inside him. Once I was aboard, I asked him to transform into his enormous mecha form.

The Blue Enchantress and I continued communicating through our magically connected pocket mirrors while Leon and I carried out the plan we had created to protect the bubble universe.

I cast my Porta spell, opening an enormous portal into a pocket dimension located outside the normal boundaries of the Storyverse. Because the pocket dimension was separate from ordinary Storyverse space, placing the bubble universe inside it would effectively sever it from conventional travel routes.

With the portal open, I instructed Leon to reverse the direction of his tractor beam. Instead of pulling objects toward himself, he used the beam to push.

Leon aimed the reversed tractor beam at the entire bubble universe and began pushing it toward the portal. The whole universe slowly passed through the enormous gateway and entered the hidden pocket dimension.

Once it was safely inside, I closed the portal behind it.

The medieval village, the Blue Enchantress, the Life Crystal mines, and the living rainbow crystal civilization were now concealed beyond the normal reach of the Storyverse.

We had moved them just in time.

As soon as the portal closed, Leon and I realized that we were surrounded by an enormous armada of Storyverse space pirates. They had all been racing toward the bubble universe, hoping to invade it and loot its rainbow crystals.

Now their treasure had disappeared directly in front of them.

The pirate ships surrounded Leon’s massive mecha form from every direction and opened fire. Laser cannonballs and other powerful projectiles streaked toward us from across Storyverse space.

However, Leon was a ghost.

He transformed his enormous mecha body into an intangible state, allowing every attack to pass harmlessly through him.

Because Leon had been standing in the center of the armada, the pirates’ attacks did not simply vanish after passing through his body. The cannon fire continued traveling forward and struck the other ships positioned on the opposite side.

Within moments, the pirates accidentally blasted one another apart.

The surrounding armada destroyed itself while Leon remained completely unharmed at the center of the chaos.

For a moment, it appeared that the battle was over.

Then an unimaginably large starship cruiser emerged from the darkness of Storyverse space.

Leon was already in a gigantic mecha form large enough to use a tractor beam capable of moving an entire bubble universe. Despite that, this new cruiser was so enormous that it made Leon look small.

Worse, the cruiser was a ghost ship.

It hurtled directly toward us at incredible speed. Leon warned me that because the ship was also intangible, he would not be able to phase through it. If it collided with him, the impact would tear him apart.

I stood inside Leon’s mecha body and looked out at the massive ghost cruiser approaching us.

I still had the Life Sword.

I raised the rainbow crystal blade and channeled its life-giving energy toward the incoming ship. A surge of power fired from the sword and struck the ghost cruiser.

The Life Sword restored the ship to life.

Its spectral form became solid and physical. The ghost ship transformed into a massive living starship made from real matter.

Now that the cruiser was physical, Leon could phase through it.

He turned intangible just before impact, and we passed directly through the center of the enormous ship without being harmed.

We had escaped the collision, but I had also created a new problem. The cruiser was no longer a ghost. It had become a fully physical warship of almost unimaginable size, capable of rampaging across the Storyverse even without access to the Life Stones.

We could not allow it to escape.

I told Leon to phase back into the cruiser and possess it. He entered the ship’s physical structure and spread his ghostly presence throughout its systems, temporarily locking it down and preventing the crew from controlling it.

As Leon entered the cruiser, he allowed me to slip out of his body and into the enemy ship.

I went searching for the ship’s captain.

The captain was an undead space pirate with vampiric qualities. Long, leech-like tendrils extended from his body, and his hands ended in vicious claws.

He attacked me immediately.

His tendrils wrapped themselves around my body and began draining away my life energy. I felt myself becoming weaker as the captain absorbed my strength and added it to his own.

I swung the Life Sword and severed the tendrils, freeing myself from his grip.

However, the damage had already been done.

The captain had drained enough of my life energy to become significantly stronger than he had been before.

He lunged toward me with his sharpened claws.

I cast Aegis, creating a magical shield in front of myself. The captain struck the barrier with tremendous force, but the shield held. His claws shattered against its surface.

I counterattacked with the Life Sword, but the captain transformed into a cloud of mist and teleported away before the blade could hit him.

Every time I attempted a conventional melee attack, he moved too quickly for me to reach him.

I realized that I needed something faster than a sword strike.

I raised my hand and focused my Lucid Light into a concentrated beam. Instead of shaping it into armor or another physical construct, I fired it directly from my hand like a laser.

The Lucid Light moved far too quickly for the captain to evade. The beam struck him and stunned him before he could dissolve into mist.

The attack surprised me because I rarely thought to use Lucid Light as a straightforward energy blast. I had discovered an entirely new way of wielding one of my most familiar powers.

As the stunned vampire captain staggered before me, I realized that light itself was the key to defeating him.

However, Lucid Light was not exactly what I needed.

I needed sunlight.

I thought about the magical vocabulary I had already developed. Porta created portals. Aegis summoned a defensive shield. Chronos unleashed a divine smite. These spell names were based on Greek or Latin words and concepts.

I knew another word that fit what I needed.

“Sol.”

I cast the new spell.

A miniature sun appeared directly above the vampiric space pirate captain. Brilliant sunlight poured down upon him, filling the chamber with overwhelming radiance.

The captain could not escape.

The sunlight completely eviscerated him.

With the captain defeated and Leon possessing the massive cruiser, the threat had finally been neutralized.

By the end of the dream, I had protected the medieval fantasy bubble universe by moving it into a hidden pocket dimension beyond the normal boundaries of the Storyverse. I had established a permanent communication link with the Blue Enchantress through our magically bonded pocket mirrors. Leon and I had defeated an entire armada of Storyverse space pirates and captured the largest pirate battle cruiser I had ever encountered.

I had also gained a deeper understanding of the Life Stones and the Life Sword. The rainbow crystal sword was not merely a weapon capable of giving life to other things. It was itself alive, combining the qualities of both life-imbuing crystals and naturally living rainbow crystal beings, along with mysterious technology from the future.

Finally, I discovered two new abilities that I could carry into future adventures. I learned how to fire Lucid Light as an instantaneous laser-like energy blast, and I created the Sol spell, allowing me to summon a miniature sun and unleash concentrated sunlight against my enemies.
