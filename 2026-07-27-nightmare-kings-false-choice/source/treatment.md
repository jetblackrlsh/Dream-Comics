# Treatment

## Logline

When the King of Nightmares tries to manipulate Jet into destroying an entire nightmare planet, Jet survives a failed attack, saves the Life Sword with his Golden Energy Tether, and defeats the king by transforming the world's nightmares into dreams instead of accepting a false choice between annihilation and inaction.

## Central conflict

Jet reaches a planet of black mirrored cubes whose internal red light matches the eyes of its ruler, the King of Nightmares. The king describes genuinely dangerous nightmares that can make sleeping people wander into traffic, fall down stairs, or enter other lethal situations. He then presents planetary destruction as the only heroic response.

Because Jet is lucid, he can test that command against what he knows. The Storyverse is an infinite multiverse of cosmic bubble universes containing every possible story. This world cannot be the source of all nightmares, and destroying it cannot eliminate nightmares elsewhere. An attempt to eradicate every nightmare world would become an endless crusade. The king's demand is therefore not a solution but a trap: he wants a hero to commit an unjustifiable act and become corrupted by it.

The king intensifies the trap through guilt, demanding whether Jet will simply let people die. Jet rejects that false transfer of responsibility, but his anger pushes him into a tactical mistake. He tries to ignite a being already made from burnt vines. The king pretends to suffer, then wraps Jet in burning thorns, wounds him, and tries to steal the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword.

Jet's less famous Golden Energy Tether becomes the answer. Because the king dismissed it as unimportant, Jet can lasso the sword and create a mystical bond among himself, the tether, and the weapon. Through that connection, Jet activates the Life Sword's force field and healing abilities before the sword physically returns to his hand.

After escaping, Jet does not accept the king's framing. He uses the Life Sword's power over life as a force of purification. The planet becomes a radiant cloud world, its nightmare beings become dreams, and those liberated dreams fight beside Jet. They dismantle the king and scatter his thorns through the cosmic void.

## Theme

Heroism is not obedience to the most frightening demand. A manipulator can disguise corruption as urgency by offering only two choices: commit an atrocity or accept blame for every harm. Jet succeeds when he refuses that frame and creates a third path that protects life without destroying a whole world.

The Life Sword and Golden Energy Tether embody the same idea. The sword supplies tremendous power, but the tether's quieter gift is connection. Jet wins through connection, healing, transformation, and alliance rather than indiscriminate force.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins alert and morally confident. The nightmare planet is terrifying, but lucidity gives him enough perspective to reject the king's impossible mission. The accusation that he is allowing deaths provokes anger, and anger leads to the mistaken fire attack.

Pain and danger replace confidence when the burning vines imprison Jet and pull away the Life Sword. At the low point, Jet recognizes that the ignored tether still gives him agency. His escape restores his physical freedom and clarifies his moral purpose.

When Jet turns toward the planet, the king briefly believes the corruption has succeeded. Jet's purification reveals that he never accepted the forced choice. The transformed dreams gathering around him turn solitary resistance into collective victory. Jet ends sober rather than triumphant: the archetypal king may return, but buying time for dreams and their defenders is a real and worthwhile victory.

## Continuity

This dream belongs to the Storyverse continuity that began on June 11, 2025. The Storyverse is an infinite multiverse where every possible story exists as a cosmic bubble universe floating in a cosmic void. Jet first explored the Elven Village, later discovered Lucid Light, and now travels with Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper. None of those crew members physically appears in this focused encounter.

Jet has dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the open hoodie over a visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.

Jet's Rainbow Crystal Sword is also called the Life Sword. It is a colorful futuristic crystal blade and one of the Storyverse's most powerful artifacts. It can cut as a conventional weapon, manipulate life energy, heal injuries, and generate force fields. Jet's Golden Energy Tether resembles a flexible rope of glittering golden light. Its deeper ability is to create mystical connections and bonds between people and objects.

Lucid Light is sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. Jet uses it to manifest pyrokinetic orange-violet fire around the King of Nightmares. The Life Sword's life energy is visually distinct: clean rainbow-white radiance with an especially strong golden and emerald core.

## Ending

The final page shows the newly transformed dreams dismantling the King of Nightmares while Jet stands above a world of white clouds and golden light. Burnt thorns scatter among distant Storyverse bubbles. Jet understands that an archetypal being may reassemble, but each separated thorn represents time won for dreams, heroes, and sleeping people.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: Jet, the nightmare planet, the King of Nightmares, the Life Sword, the Golden Energy Tether, and a radiant glimpse of the world's transformation.
2. Jet enters the Storyverse nightmare world, meets its king, and hears the command to destroy the planet in order to stop lethal nightmares.
3. Jet explains why one planet cannot be the source of all nightmares; the king answers with guilt and calls Jet a false hero.
4. Jet's fire attack appears effective, but the burnt-vine king reveals his resistance, traps Jet in burning thorns, and begins taking the Life Sword.
5. Jet lassos the sword with the ignored tether, activates its power through their bond, breaks free behind a force field, heals, and reclaims the weapon.
6. The king thinks Jet has finally agreed to destroy the planet, but Jet recognizes that corruption was the true mission and chooses purification instead.
7. Life energy transforms the black mirrored planet into radiant clouds and converts its nightmare beings into free dreams who gather around Jet.
8. Jet and the liberated dreams dismantle the Nightmare King and scatter his thorns, buying precious time even if the archetypal evil eventually reforms.
