# 07/28/2026 - The Magic Mirror Skybridge

Dream Comics entry for July 28, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet enters a medieval Storyverse town to answer a practical question: can magic recreate the modern advances made possible by artificial intelligence? An enchantress with sky-blue hair helps him discover that intelligent magic mirrors can serve as voice, image, and vision assistants, while animated golems can perform the work of robots.

Their breakthrough comes from a mine of life stones beneath the town. The crystals contain the same life-giving substance as Jet's Rainbow Crystal Life Sword, revealing that the legendary artifact may have been forged from naturally occurring material combined with advanced technology. The enchantress and Jet use the crystals to equip homes with intelligent mirrors, give villagers pocket-sized assistants, and animate useful golems.

The transformation attracts Drillov, a technological raider with enormous drills for hands who intends to steal the crystals and sell a recreated Life Sword. Jet disables his suit with a gigantic Lucid Light hand, but three alien robot titans soon crash outside the village. Ordinary golems cannot stop them, and the magical mirrors have no network compatible with extraterrestrial technology.

Jet solves the incompatibility with his Golden Energy Tether. He connects a pocket mirror to a cloud, turning the sky into a magical network. Lightning carries the mirror's information into one titan as an electrical signal, allowing the mirror to hack it. The controlled robot fights the other two until all three destroy one another.

The village survives, but the exposed mine has permanently changed its future. With word of the life stones certain to spread across the Storyverse, the town begins preparing heroes to defend the source of one of reality's most powerful materials.
