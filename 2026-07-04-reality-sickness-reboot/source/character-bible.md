# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. His signature items are a rainbow futuristic crystal sword and an energy tether that looks like a rope made of golden glittery light. When lucid, he is surrounded by sparkling purple Lucid Light like a night sky full of stars. Jet appears clearly on the cover and in the opening, then returns through lucidity during the theater transformation.

## Johnson

Johnson is an orange humanoid goldfish wearing a blue astronaut suit without a helmet. His fish face, orange skin, and blue suit must remain clear. In the opening, he is bitten by vampiric dark elves. Jet uses the Solvo spell to divide him into normal Johnson and vampire Johnson. Vampire Johnson is red, feral, and wears a black astronaut suit. Normal Johnson is frightened but safe.

## Young Janitor Boy

The young janitor boy is Jet's temporary non-lucid dream identity. He is a young boy with dark-brown skin, wide worried eyes, short work gloves, a simple janitor uniform, and a mop bucket. His face should subtly echo Jet without looking like adult Jet. He is immune to the reality sickness and spends the middle of the comic running for his life while trying to understand what went wrong.

## Blue Grape Cook

The old woman is a strange cook with wrinkled skin, gray hair tied back, a stained apron, and a kitchen full of bowls of glowing blue grapes. She is not treated as a major villain. Her mistake is using something unclean while cooking, which makes reality sick. Her kitchen should feel domestic at first, then contaminated by blue-violet cracks.

## Reality Sickness

Reality sickness looks like patchwork corruption. Different places and timelines are sewn together at hard seams: a kitchen connects to a 1920s street, a modern hallway cuts through an old theater, and the sky shows mismatched blocks of time. People distort into monstrous versions of themselves with stretched faces, extra limbs, shadowy eyes, and blue-violet infection cracks. The sickness should always read as reality becoming ill, not random fantasy.

## Superhero Jet

Superhero Jet is Jet's lucid combat form in the theater sequence. He wears red, yellow, and blue power armor, a reflective full face mask, and energy blast gauntlets. He flies by shooting bright energy balls downward at the ground, launching himself upward with each blast. Purple Lucid Light and small hints of Jet's rainbow color should surround the armor so the reader understands that this hero is Jet regaining control.

## The Villain

The villain is a theatrical, dangerous figure who loves the new monstrous world. He wears a dark showman-style coat with sick blue-violet glow lines, has a sharp smile, and moves through the patchwork world with confidence. He wants the reality sickness to continue because it makes the world more exciting and monstrous.

## Reboot Remote

The reboot remote is a small futuristic device with a glowing green reset button, chrome casing, and clear reality-repair energy. When activated, it sends clean white, gold, green, and purple waves through the world, stitching timelines back into one healthy reality.
