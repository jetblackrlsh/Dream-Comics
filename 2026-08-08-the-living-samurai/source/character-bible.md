# Character Bible - The Living Samurai

## Jet

- African-American man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard and mustache, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Long dreadlocks spill from beneath a black hoodie worn over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt.
- Black athletic pants with one clean white stripe down each outer leg, white high-top sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build, compassionate but firm posture, expressive brows, and calm strategic focus.
- Wields Silonis, a colorful futuristic Rainbow Crystal Life Sword. Its blade resembles faceted prismatic crystal and releases brilliant rainbow life energy.
- Can create a translucent faceted rainbow crystal force field around Silonis.

## The pilot before healing

- Adult woman whose life-draining disorder has left her extremely aged, gray-haired, shriveled, and physically fragile.
- Deeply lined face, thin gray hair, exhausted eyes, and a body visibly diminished by decades of leaking life energy.
- Wears an enclosed metallic red-and-yellow pilot bodysuit that matches the mecha.
- Her complexion must remain consistent before and after healing; only age, hair color, vitality, and physical condition change.
- Desperate, guarded, and initially willing to seize Silonis, but motivated by survival and responsibility rather than cruelty.

## The restored woman

- The same woman after healing, now a healthy young adult with long blonde hair and vivid bluish-green eyes.
- Wears the same sleek metallic red-and-yellow bodysuit, now fitting a healthy athletic frame and shining with renewed life energy.
- Her facial structure and complexion remain recognizably continuous with her aged form.
- Moves from astonishment to relief, apology, and the emotional release of receiving a future she can choose.

## The samurai mecha

- A skyscraper-scale humanoid mecha with a consistent red-and-yellow color scheme, black mechanical joints, and polished metallic surfaces.
- Armor resembles a heroic samurai: layered red shoulder plates with gold trim, segmented chest and waist armor, armored forearms and shins, and a horned kabuto-like helmet.
- Before awakening, its eye slit is dim and its movements visibly depend on the pilot.
- After Silonis strikes it, its eye slit glows warm gold, prismatic life-energy veins run through its armor seams, and its posture becomes self-directed and expressive.
- The exact armor silhouette, horned helmet, colors, proportions, and damage state remain consistent on every page.

## Bay kaiju

- An enormous black-blue reptilian kaiju with a heavy muscular body, jagged dorsal spines, ember-orange eyes, and faint molten-orange fissures beneath wet armored skin.
- Rises from Andromeda City's bay amid towering water and attacks buildings without gore.
- Visually distinct from the samurai mecha and large enough to meet it as an equal opponent.

## Andromeda City

- Futuristic coastal metropolis at night under a dense starry sky.
- Buildings resemble huge LED strips, exposed luminous wiring, transparent geometric frames, and glowing circuitry rather than ordinary solid skyscrapers.
- Core palette: electric cyan, magenta, violet, deep blue, red, yellow, and gold reflected across the bay.
- The same skyline, bay, and wireframe architectural language should persist across pages.

## Continuity rules

- Jet's skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white shoes, cross-body bag, and Silonis remain consistent on every page.
- The pilot and restored woman are the same person; preserve her face and complexion across the transformation.
- The mecha's red-and-yellow samurai armor is identical before and after awakening. Only its glowing eyes, life-energy seams, and independent body language change.
- The bay kaiju keeps the same black-blue skin, jagged spines, orange eyes, and molten fissures.
- No gore, speech bubbles, thought balloons, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, creator signatures, or blank caption boxes.
