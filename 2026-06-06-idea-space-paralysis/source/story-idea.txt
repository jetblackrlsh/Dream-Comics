Dream journal date: 06/06/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the ongoing Storyverse lucid dream continuity that began on 06/11/2025. Jet is alone in a white void, thinking about the Idea Space, where ideas may exist independently of our reality and be discovered rather than created.

A staircase of giant playing cards appears as Jet realizes something new: the Queen is the only confirmed female playing card, even though card games often emphasize matching card types without caring about the gender of the cards.

Jet jumps up the staircase and feels several things at once. He can see the sleep paralysis demon on his chest trying to suck imagination out of his body. He also feels complete control of his physical senses.

Jet pushes rainbow imaginary energy into the demon faster than it can absorb it, and the demon pops like a balloon. Jet lets his body experience goosebumps, tingles, hair standing up, and waves of physically relaxing euphoria. The comic ends on the idea that the ability to make yourself feel as good as possible is one of the best perks of lucid dreaming.
