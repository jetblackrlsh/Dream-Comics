# Story Idea - The Perfect Empty Dimension

Jet rescues a woman from a green slime monster by temporarily fusing their life energies with Silonis, then separates them and banishes the creature through Porta into an Empty Dimension. Curious about a place designed for solitude, Jet enters it himself and splits into an observer and a resident self. The dimension offers an ordinary life free from judgment, but when resident-Jet invites observer-Jet to remain forever, Jet chooses his waking commitments over effortless paradise.
