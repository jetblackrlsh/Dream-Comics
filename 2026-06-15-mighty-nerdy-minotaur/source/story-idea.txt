Dream journal entry dated 06/15/2026.

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void.

My name is Jet. I usually appear as a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, warm brown eyes. I have a rainbow sword resembling a colorful futuristic crystal sword and an energy tether resembling a rope made of golden glittery light.

In this dream, I arrive alone on a farm in my werewolf form with black fur, white stripes, and green serpent eyes.

The humanoid cows there see my werewolf form and immediately panic.

The humanoid cows call upon the hero the mighty nerdy minotaur.

The minotaur and I clash, but the minotaur is much stronger than I am.

I use my Lucid Light to try and enhance my strength, but still I struggle against him.

I use my rainbow crystal sword to heal my muscles as they strain to hold back the minotaur, causing my muscles to increase in strength the longer the fight continues.

Eventually I become stronger than the minotaur, but instead of continuing the fight I try to appeal to our common interests.

I bring up multiverse theory and the concept of casually disconnected zones.

The nerdy minotaur is intrigued.

The two of us stop our brawl and begin to enjoy a nice chat about the possible existence of other worlds.
