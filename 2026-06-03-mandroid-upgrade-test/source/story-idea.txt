Dream journal entry dated 06/03/2026.

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025. My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I.

My Ghostly UFO, named Leon, resembles a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025. Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit does not have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025. Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds. Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color with spiky slicked back yellow hair and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025. Lucid Light is a reality warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered the power of Lucid Light in a dream on 07/19/2025. Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah is a pale skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that covered her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She wields a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword. Her father Tecton is a green skinned, humanoid, muscular, cat ogre barbarian who dresses in a brown loin cloth and a silver baldric. My drone Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper all met me in early 2026 while adventuring through the Super Cathedral. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, a full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, a black cross-body belt bag, and warm brown eyes. I have a rainbow sword that resembles a colorful, futuristic, crystal sword and an energy tether that resembles a rope made of golden glittery light.

Superhero Mandroid resembles a human man with white skin, slicked back brown hair, a red superhero unitard, a chest insignia with the initials "SHM" in yellow font, a yellow cape, and large mechanical green fists. He was created by Johnson and I in Leon's robotics lab on 06/02/2026. He fights nightmares across the Storyverse and closes nightmare portals with astral thread.

I spent a lot of the day thinking about Superhero Mandroid and writing comics about him.

When I went to sleep, the first thing I did was return to Leon's robotics lab to tell Johnson about my upgrade ideas.

The two of us quickly got to work and invited Superhero Mandroid over to test out the new gear.

We added a couple of new rocket systems for his rocket fists, as well as a nanite upgrade.

Now Superhero Mandroid can shoot nitro rocket fists for a slight boost in rocket fist speed, or light propulsion rocket fists for a significant boost in rocket fist speed.

We tested his rocket fists out on material grade one through three.

Grade one material wasn't phased at all by his normal rocket fist.

Grade two could be cracked by his nitro rocket fist.

Grade three shatters when hit with his light propelled rocket fist.

The nanite upgrade allows for Superhero Mandroid to use nanites to increase the size of his rocket fists.

This does create weight issues for Superhero Mandroid, as larger fists are harder to lift and aim.

The weight increase leads to a slower shot, but it also leads to increased damage even when using lower level propulsion.

While Superhero Mandroid likely needs more training to get used to these upgrades before using them in the field against actual nightmares, I would say that these upgrades were more or less a success.
