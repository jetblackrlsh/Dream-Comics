Dream journal date: 07/17/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025.

My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I. My Ghostly UFO, named Leon, resembles a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025. Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit doesn't have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025. Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds. Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color with spiky slicked back yellow hair and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025. Lucid Light is a reality warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered the power of Lucid Light in a dream on 07/19/2025. Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah is a pale skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that covered her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She wields a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword. Her father Tecton is a green skinned, humanoid, muscular, cat ogre barbarian who dresses in a brown loin cloth and a silver baldric. Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot. They all met me in early 2026 while adventuring through the Super Cathedral. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, a full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a “Dream Anime” graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, a black cross-body belt bag, and warm brown eyes. I have a rainbow sword that resembles a colorful, futuristic crystal sword and an energy tether that resembles a rope made of golden glittery light.

I arrived alone wearing my augmented reality, blue lens, display sunglasses.

I materialized a portable, handheld touchpad that allowed me to use a mouse on the augmented reality display.

I had to go to the bathroom, but when I entered I lost my lucidity.

The bathroom was very gross and stretched on infinitely, just like how many people describe the bathrooms of Mall World.

Mall World is a dreamscape that many people claim to have visited in their dreams before.

This wasn’t my first time dreaming of Mall World either, but without my lucidity I wasn’t able to piece together where I was very clearly.

An alligator emerged from the floor and grew into a kaiju made of magma.

This fantastical leap in dream logic restored my lucidity.

I stopped feeling powerless and a superhero suit with a yellow and blue color scheme materialized on my body.

I flew into action to try and fight the Magmagator, but it was too strong for me alone to handle.

My evil twin with purple skin, an afro with a white lightning highlight, and a red and black suit crashes into Mall World and helps me fight off Magmagator.

With our combined strength we defeat the menace.

I question why my evil twin would come help me.

I had never met them before, and yet I immediately knew they were my evil twin.

My evil twin explained that we both had powers because we were from an alien planet.

We were sent to Earth to fight off the monsters that had formed from dystopian pollutants of the future.

My evil twin explained that they weren’t truly evil.

They just didn’t care about the Earth, because the Earth wasn’t our home.

We went our separate ways, as I stayed behind on Earth to learn more about the companies polluting the planet and the resistance trying to stop them.

I encountered a woman with long curly black hair and light brown skin, wearing a tight light brown dress.

She was a member of the resistance and she was attracted to my power.

While she was pretty, she wasn’t necessarily my type.

She was very aggressive and tried to come onto me, and it was around that time that my alarm went off and I woke up.
