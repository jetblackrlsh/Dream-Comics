# Character Bible

## Jet

- Identity: man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Outfit: black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Signature gear: rainbow sword shaped like a colorful futuristic crystal sword, and a golden glittering energy tether. Use these as visible accessories when they fit the page, but the story focus is Pop and the Hero Badge.
- Silhouette: grounded lucid dream explorer, observant posture, one hand often raised as if testing dream physics.
- Expression range: focused lucidity, suspicious curiosity, awe, alarmed confusion, cautious explanation.

## Pop

- Identity: pot-bellied purple imp with a skinny diamond-shaped head.
- Outfit: bright neon green shorts, no shirt, no shoes.
- Body language: rubber-hose cartoon movement, elastic limbs, smug grin, impatient pointing, explosive theatrical poses.
- Powers: shapeshifting, reality bending, world reshaping, turning into a Hero Badge.
- Visual rule: Pop should feel like a classic cartoon character rendered in hyper-real anime detail, more solid and bright than the world around him.
- Hero Badge form: a glossy purple-and-neon-green badge with Pop's mischievous face worked into the emblem, pinned or hovering on Jet's chest.

## Hero Station Officials

- Superhero bureaucracy officers in clean colorful uniforms, armor trim, capes, visors, and glowing badge emblems.
- Their mood shifts from stern law enforcement to fascinated recruiters once Jet presents the Pop badge.
- They should look varied and powerful, but not like the recurring Dream Comics crew.

## Worlds

- Storyverse entry: cosmic black void filled with floating bubble universes and sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Purple crater planet: massive rocky purple world, crater fields, violet sky, surreal scale, alien stillness.
- Pop's reality field: the local environment stretches, folds, melts, and snaps like cartoon physics around Pop.
- Superhero planet: bright utopian city, heroic towers, cape-shaped traffic trails, public hero symbols, intense color.
- Hero Station: futuristic recruitment and enforcement hall with glowing counters, badge scanners, superhero officers, and no readable signs except generated caption boxes.
