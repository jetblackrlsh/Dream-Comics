# 07/18/2026 - The Companion Divide

Dream Comics entry for July 18, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet enters the Storyverse alone and searches its cosmic bubble universes for a superhero world that reflects a conflict from waking life. He finds a society divided between government heroes who restrict human relationships with AI companions in the name of protecting birth rates and rebel heroes who defend those relationships as a matter of personal freedom. Jet never projects himself into the world or joins either faction. Instead, he watches from outside the universe, sympathizes with the rebels, reflects on his own choice not to have children, and considers the Cosmic Archive he built as an interactive AI legacy. He wakes frustrated rather than comforted, but with a clearer understanding of why the conflict feels like an unwanted comic-book reality.

The finished package includes the journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, assembled PDF, and preview artifacts.
