Dream Journal Reconstruction

The dream began while I was flying through the sky. I encountered a woman who was also flying, although she looked distinctly out of place, as if she had come from another time period. She had a light complexion and brown braided hair arranged in one long ponytail. She wore a mustard-colored jacket that looked extremely soft, even though I never touched it. I had trouble identifying the material, but I thought it might have been felt. The jacket looked like something someone might have worn during the 1940s or 1950s. Her bluish-green eyes seemed familiar to me, and the fact that she was flying naturally caught my attention as well.

I told the woman that I recognized her eyes. Her eyes immediately turned red, and darkness began spreading across her face like veins. The entity said, “Even in this body, you can still see me.” I realized that this was the same darkness I had been encountering night after night in my recent dreams—the darkness that had been pursuing Silonis, the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life.

Knowing that the darkness wanted Silonis, I used my Lucid Light to fuse the sword with myself. My body transformed into that of a humanoid rainbow-crystal creature. I told the darkness that it would never be able to take the sword because Silonis and I were no longer separate entities. The darkness called me a fool. It explained that its primary ability was possessing people, so by making the Rainbow Crystal Sword literally part of myself, I had made its task easier. It could simply possess me and take control of both of us. The darkness then leaped out of the woman and into my rainbow-crystal form.

However, I had anticipated that something like this might happen. Because I was fused with Silonis and now possessed its power over life, I fired a beam of life energy as the dark spirit attempted to enter me. I propelled the darkness's spirit and the life energy back out of myself, using the energy to create a humanoid rainbow-crystal body for the darkness to possess instead. At first, the darkness seemed pleased but confused. It did not entirely understand what I had done, but it was glad to have a rainbow-crystal body of its own.

Rather than immediately stopping the darkness, I watched to discover what it truly wanted to do now that it had obtained the kind of power it had pursued for so long. It began using life energy to animate buildings throughout the city. The living buildings went on a rampage and attacked people. I was confused because, although I had not known what the darkness ultimately wanted, it seemed bizarre that animating violent buildings was the first thing it chose to do.

The darkness more or less explained that it already used people the way people used buildings—as homes for itself. It believed that homes and buildings deserved to possess lives of their own, while people were disposable and did not deserve to live. Its ultimate objective seemed to be reversing the normal hierarchy through which people understood life: buildings would become the living beings that mattered, while human lives would be treated as expendable.

Once I understood what the darkness wanted and saw what it chose to do with its power, I knew I could not allow it to continue. I siphoned the life energy away from its rainbow-crystal construct until I was simply holding the darkness's soul. I then used the Porta spell to open a portal and projected its soul into a dimension called the Glimmer before closing the portal behind it.

I had used Porta to create portals in the past, but I had never heard of the Glimmer before this dream. Knowledge of it simply appeared in my mind as the solution I needed, almost like receiving a download of mysterious information from another dimension. The Glimmer was apparently an alternate dimension specifically intended to contain extremely powerful magical beings in ghostly forms. The entities imprisoned there did not have physical bodies. They remained dangerous, particularly because of their magical abilities, but there was not much they could do without bodies while trapped inside the Glimmer. Most importantly, there was nothing physical there for the darkness to possess. I hoped that meant it would be unable to return, although I had encountered it in nearly all of my recent dreams, so I could not be completely certain. Fingers crossed.

Even with the darkness banished, I knew other beings might continue trying to capture Silonis because it was one of the most powerful artifacts in the Storyverse. The darkness had contributed to the sword becoming so coveted by possessing beings wherever I went, but it had never been the only possible reason someone might pursue it.

I also still needed to stop the emergency the darkness had created. The animated buildings continued rampaging through the city and attacking people. While I remained fused with Silonis, I used our power to draw the life energy out of the buildings so that they were no longer alive. I then fired life energy into the people whom the buildings had attacked, healing their injuries and restoring the city to normal.

I did not believe my fusion with Silonis was permanent. I imagined that the next time I entered the Storyverse, I would probably be my usual self again and Silonis would once more exist as a sword rather than as part of my body. Even so, it was valuable to learn that fusion with the sword was something I could do.

I was less certain about what happened to the rainbow-crystal construct I had made for the darkness. I did not remember banishing or destroying the body itself, but I also could not imagine it remaining forever. As far as I knew, the Rainbow Crystal Sword could not ordinarily create additional rainbow crystal, so I suspected the body had only been a temporary construct. I could not know that for sure, however, which meant the abandoned body might remain as a hanging thread.

Although I had been lucid dreaming every night since late May of the previous year, I was still learning something new in each of these dreams, especially about the way all of them connected to the same world.
