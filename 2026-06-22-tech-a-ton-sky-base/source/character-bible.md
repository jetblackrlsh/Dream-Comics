# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: lucid dreamer, solo explorer, and protagonist.
- Age range: adult man.
- Skin and face: dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, expressive face that moves from irritated pain to relaxed focus to patient warmth.
- Hair: long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie; the dreadlocks must remain visible whenever Jet appears.
- Outfit: black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag.
- Signature props: rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glittering energy tether, sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Rainbow sword healing: the rainbow crystal sword shines with bright multicolor healing light when laser fire strikes Jet, closing burns and restoring him without gore.
- Cyber-venom attunement: after injection, faint cyan-and-violet circuit lines glow across Jet's hands, eyes, sword, and hoodie seams. He remains fully awake and himself, but he looks synchronized with nearby technology.
- Continuity rule: do not remove the beard, dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime tee, belt bag, rainbow sword, or golden tether. Jet should not look like a generic armored hero.

## Robo-Hornets

- Role: opening threat, escorts, and TAT's anxious defensive swarm.
- Appearance: small robot laser hornets with chrome-and-black segmented bodies, translucent mechanical wings, red targeting eyes, needle stingers, micro antennae, and tiny red laser emitters under their heads.
- Attack: they fire barrages of red laser beams and inject cyan cyber venom through glowing needle stingers.
- Behavior: initially aggressive and synchronized, then confused by Jet's refusal to fight, then obedient escorts who carry or guide him toward the sky base.
- Continuity rule: keep them hornet-sized relative to Jet, with a swarm silhouette and consistent red laser glow.

## Cyber Venom

- Role: attempted knockout device that becomes a bridge between Jet and technology.
- Appearance: glowing cyan-and-violet liquid circuitry, small holographic hexagons, digital veins, and gentle electric mist.
- Effect on Jet: relaxes his body and almost lulls him asleep, but his lucid awareness keeps him awake. The venom becomes a technology interface instead of a trap.
- Continuity rule: show the cyber venom as calm luminous circuit patterns, not body horror.

## Tech-A-Ton / TAT

- Role: robo-hornet leader, apparent antagonist, and conversation partner.
- Appearance: gorilla-shaped robot built from exposed circuits, braided cable muscles, transparent armor plates, chrome joints, dark graphite metal bones, glowing neon blue eyes, and a heavy broad-shouldered silhouette.
- Face: mechanical gorilla brow, square jaw made of circuit boards and metal struts, expressive neon blue eyes that shift from suspicion to surprise to relief.
- Posture: initially looming and defensive, knuckles planted like a gorilla on the sky-base floor; later seated calmly with open hands and softened shoulders.
- Emotional state: fears rejection, expects hatred, attacks first to avoid being hurt first, then becomes comfortable when Jet does not fear him.
- Continuity rule: TAT must read as both gorilla and robot, made of exposed circuitry rather than smooth armor.

## Sky Base

- Role: TAT's high-tech refuge and the place where conflict becomes conversation.
- Look: floating base above a bright ocean, glass floors, circular turbine rings, antenna towers, blue holographic consoles, hornet docking racks, cable nests, and open hangar doors filled with clouds and sunlight.
- Lighting: cool neon blue technology light mixed with bright warm sky and white cloud glow.
- Continuity rule: keep the base elevated and airy, not underground or dark. It should feel like a guarded refuge above the beach.

## Storyverse Context

- Role: continuity context rather than an on-page cast for this issue.
- Look: when shown symbolically, the Storyverse appears as cosmic bubble universes floating in a starry void with sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Continuity rule: Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are part of Jet's wider team but do not physically appear in this dream sequence.
