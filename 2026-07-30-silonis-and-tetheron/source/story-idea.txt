Silonis and Tetheron

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet considers which of his two primary dream artifacts he would choose if he could bring only one into the waking world. The Rainbow Crystal Life Sword appears overwhelmingly powerful, while the Golden Energy Tether offers nearly limitless creative possibilities through magical bonds.

A masked thief steals the tether and pulls Jet into the cosmic Storyverse, turning the question into a direct contest between the two artifacts. When the thief binds Jet with the tether, Jet cuts it with the Life Sword, then uses the sword's restorative energy to heal the severed pieces. The restoration awakens the tether as a living being.

Jet defeats the thief and learns the artifacts' true names: the Life Sword is Silonis, and the Golden Energy Tether is Tetheron. The hypothetical choice loses its meaning because both artifacts are extraordinary living companions rather than interchangeable tools.
