# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears a black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries a rainbow sword shaped like a colorful futuristic crystal blade and an energy tether that looks like a rope of golden glittery light.
- In this comic, Jet begins alone and watchful, becomes awed by Turtalia's confession, then shifts into stealthy determination and urgent survival.
- Visual motifs: rainbow sword glow, golden tether light, purple starry Lucid Light accents, mint-green bond energy shared with Turtalia.

## Turtalia

- Humanoid turtle woman guardian with blue eyes, long black hair worn in a high ponytail, and calm but powerful facial expressions.
- Has subtle turtle features: green-tinted skin, smooth shell-like shoulder and back contours, graceful turtle-pattern markings, strong protective posture.
- Wears glowing labradorite bikini armor: iridescent blue-green-gray stone plates, magical highlights, and a luminous shell-like chest and hip silhouette.
- Former legendary World Turtle who once carried and puppeted worlds, now repentant and committed to protecting freedom.
- Visual motifs: mint-green sparkling bond stream to Jet, labradorite armor glow, emerald tower light, quiet guardian authority.

## Giant Flying Serpent

- A mighty flying serpent large enough for Jet and Turtalia to ride together.
- Long sinuous body with emerald, teal, and midnight-blue scales, luminous eyes, and aurora light reflecting along its body.
- Movement should feel majestic, protective, and fast, with sweeping curves through the Northern Lights.

## Exterminators

- Hostile agents wearing sealed yellow hazmat suits with black gloves, black boots, tinted visors, and utilitarian straps.
- They wield purple crystal acid sprayers: futuristic pressure-gun tanks with jagged violet crystal nozzles and glowing corrosive purple liquid.
- They should feel threatening and organized but not gory. Their acid is an erasure beam used against walls, doors, and silhouettes, with no graphic injuries.

## Black-Clad Warrior

- Strong ally of Turtalia with slicked back dark hair, stern face, athletic build, and an all-black outfit under a long black duster coat.
- Moves with decisive power. Their key action is grabbing Jet and throwing him out of danger toward the open sky.
- Visual motifs: wind-whipped duster, sharp black silhouette against yellow locker-room lights, heroic rescue posture.

## Turtle Towers

- Incredibly tall emerald spirals rising beyond the clouds, with turtle-shell geometry, glowing green seams, and labradorite accents.
- The Multiverse Power is sealed within their walls as a contained cosmic glow of bubble universes, star fields, and green-violet energy.
- The towers must feel sacred, immense, and dangerous to misuse.
