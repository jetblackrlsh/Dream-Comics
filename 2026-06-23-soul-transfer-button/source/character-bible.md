# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: lucid dreamer, Storyverse explorer, and protagonist.
- Age range: adult man.
- Skin and face: dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, expressive face that moves from confident curiosity to shocked displacement to disturbed reflection.
- Hair: long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie; the dreadlocks must remain visible whenever Jet appears.
- Outfit: black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag.
- Signature props: rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glittering energy tether, sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Lab-world variation: after the transfer, Jet wears a white lab coat over the same black hoodie and Dream Anime tee, with a clipboard or tablet. Do not remove his beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, tee, belt bag, rainbow sword, or golden tether unless the panel is a close-up that cannot show all props.
- Continuity rule: Jet should not look like a generic scientist or generic superhero. His Dream Comics identity must remain visible even in the soul-transfer lab.

## Unfamiliar Debate Group

- Role: opening skeptics who deny interaction with other universes.
- Appearance: a mixed group of adults in modern casual clothes, slightly hazy dream faces, gathered in a dim classroom-like or conference-like abstract space.
- Expressions: skeptical, dismissive, certain that interaction with other universes is impossible.
- Continuity rule: they are background dream people, not recurring crew members. Do not depict Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, or Chipper as physically present.

## Green Rotate Button

- Role: lucid interface and inciting portal.
- Appearance: glowing green oval button floating in front of Jet's face, with a bright rotating arrow symbol, glassy edges, and radiant green light spilling across Jet's eyes and hands.
- Motion: when pressed, it should visually twist the page space, as if Jet's physical body rolls one way while his soul is launched another way.
- Continuity rule: it is an interface object, not a machine in the lab.

## Soul-Transfer Researcher Jet

- Role: Jet's transferred identity in the other world.
- Appearance: Jet's same dark-brown skin, full beard, warm brown eyes, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime tee, black pants, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow sword, and golden tether, plus a white lab coat and research badge.
- Emotional state: initially disoriented but functional, then increasingly disturbed by the implications of the experiment.
- Continuity rule: keep the lab coat as an added layer, not a replacement for Jet's core outfit.

## Lead Researcher

- Role: brilliant but frightening soul-transference scientist.
- Appearance: adult man with a long brown beard, round blue glasses, sharp magnified eyes, slightly gaunt face, white lab coat, ink-stained gloves, neat but intense posture, and an unnerving delighted smile.
- Props: clipboard, control console, glowing green transfer controls, rotating scenario models, and observation glass.
- Behavior: enjoys designing strange situations for transferred souls, works with theatrical precision, and seems too happy watching people adapt to lives they do not understand.
- Continuity rule: his long brown beard and round blue glasses are essential in every appearance.

## Transferred Subjects

- Role: evidence that soul transfer is working.
- Appearance: ordinary adults in lab observation rooms, office-like test sets, and domestic scenario mockups. Their bodies move from puppetlike stiffness or confused gestures into normal posture.
- Expressions: after transfer, their faces look alert but unfamiliar, as if a new soul is searching for context.
- Continuity rule: avoid horror gore. The unease should come from expressions, posture, and context.

## Soul Transference Lab

- Role: main setting in the other world.
- Look: polished white-and-steel research facility with teal-green soul projectors, glass observation rooms, body-scan rings, transfer chairs, branching multiverse diagrams, glowing green oval controls, and purple-green soul energy.
- Mood: clean, bright, and scientific, but morally unsettling.
- Continuity rule: the lab should feel like a real research world, not a fantasy dungeon or battlefield.
