# 08/04/2026 - The Dragon Gold Heist

Dream Comics entry for August 4, 2026.

Jet flies through a city toward a fire-breathing dragon, shields himself in Lucid Light armor, and dives through its mouth into a stomach filled with molten gold. Convinced the dragon may eat the treasure it hoards, he turns the discovery into an audacious heist: punch the dragon's uvula, escape in the resulting golden eruption, and let Leon's tractor beam collect both dreamer and treasure.

The dragon pursues Leon through the Storyverse, but Jet refuses to fight it. He uses Tetheron, his golden energy tether, to create a magical bond, tame the dragon, and ride it behind Leon through the bubble universes. By connecting Tetheron directly to the UFO, Jet can finally relax on the dragon's back and enjoy the journey.
