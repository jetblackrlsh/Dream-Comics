# 08/05/2026 - The Elemental Betrayal

Dream Comics entry for August 5, 2026.

At the mouth of a cave, Jet is ambushed by a vine elemental crowned in living fire. The creature wants Silonis, the rainbow crystal Life Sword, so badly that its rage ignites its own body. Jet takes pity on the ashes. With Silonis, he separates the incompatible halves and restores them as independent plant and fire elementals, offering both a peaceful second life.

The two beings betray him immediately. The plant binds Jet while the fire elemental superheats Silonis until he must release it—but the living sword does not need a hand to fight. Silonis summons a tornado, heals Jet, raises a rainbow crystal barrier, and destroys both attackers. Jet leaves the cave reflecting on a troubling truth: the mere chance to possess the power of life can make beings risk everything.
