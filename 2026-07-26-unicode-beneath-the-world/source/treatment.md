# Treatment

## Logline

After losing lucidity inside a king-governed academy, Jet nearly completes a magnetic flying machine from rainbow Morphium, escapes a thankless role through a guarded back door, and discovers that his entire dream world is generated by living Unicode—only for his alarm to wake him moments before he can rewrite it.

## Central conflict

Jet begins trapped inside a role that feels prewritten. The academy's king is also his father, and Jet obeys the king and his aloof wife even when he does not want to help them. The school's disorder provides a second kind of pressure: classmates shout and break rules while Jet tries to complete a serious flying-machine project. Jet resists that chaos through concentration. His spring-loaded magnetic coils and rainbow Morphium nearly produce flight, but the machine does not fully work. By placing it in his bag instead of destroying it, he treats the invention as unfinished rather than failed.

The emotional low point follows another royal errand. Jet gives his father and the king's wife exactly what they requested, but they immediately fall asleep without acknowledging his effort. When school ends, the academy still seems unwilling to release him. Jet must slip through a rear exit, evade security, and choose not to join the same rowdy students outside.

That small act of self-direction breaks the dream's apparent reality. Jet does not regain his usual explicit lucidity or summon Lucid Light. Instead, he perceives characters and symbols bleeding through matter until the academy is replaced by a terminal filled with continuously changing Unicode. He understands that the king, the students, the magnetic coils, and perhaps even Morphium and Amnesium were expressions of this code. His final challenge is comprehension: he has found the source, but he has not yet learned its syntax.

## Theme

An unfinished attempt is not a failed one. Jet saves the almost-working flying machine because its core idea still has value, then reaches a second unfinished invention at the dream's foundation: a reality-editing interface he has not yet learned to use. The academy assigns Jet an obedient role, but independent attention lets him see beneath that role and approach the authorship of the world itself.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins dutiful and absorbed in the academy's assigned reality. The disruptive classroom frustrates but does not distract him, and the nearly successful flying machine gives him determined hope. His father's indifference turns duty into disappointment. Sneaking away creates nervous freedom; refusing the students' company creates calm self-possession. As Unicode replaces the visible world, alienation becomes awe. Jet ends intellectually electrified and ready to experiment, then wakes with frustration—but also with certainty that he reached a real threshold worth returning to.

## Continuity

This dream belongs to the Storyverse continuity that began on June 11, 2025. Jet has dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the open hoodie over a visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. His bag stores the unfinished flying machine.

Lucid Light is sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a night sky full of stars, but Jet does not visibly use it after losing lucidity. Morphium is a separate rainbow-colored malleable substance that can become new creations. Amnesium is a dangerous memory-erasing substance and may appear only as an uncertain, partially forgotten presence among the academy materials.

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper remain Jet's established Storyverse team, but none physically appears in this focused dream. Jet's rainbow sword and golden energy tether are also established equipment, but they remain unused and should not distract from the academy machine or Unicode terminal.

## Ending

The final page visually parallels Jet's two unfinished projects. The saved flying machine rests inside his bag beside the terminal while Jet's hand reaches toward one editable cluster of characters. A single glyph brightens and begins to alter the ghostly outline of the academy—then the alarm fractures the interface into morning light. Jet wakes before confirming the edit. The source remains unmastered, but the discovery is real and the possibility of return is unmistakable.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: Jet reaches toward a radiant Unicode terminal while the academy, royal father, magnetic flying machine, and Morphium peel into code behind him.
2. Jet induces lucidity, loses it, and accepts his obedient role as the king's son and academy student.
3. In the rowdy flying-machines class, Jet combines magnetic coils with Morphium and nearly makes his invention fly.
4. Jet stores the unfinished machine, completes a royal errand, and receives only indifference when his father and the king's wife fall asleep.
5. Jet sneaks through the guarded rear exit, avoids the rowdy students outside, and senses that reality is artificial.
6. Unicode characters bleed through people and matter until the academy completely dissolves into a terminal interface.
7. Jet recognizes the living code as the source that generated every part of the dream, but he cannot yet decipher its mapping.
8. Jet reaches to make his first direct edit; the alarm wakes him at the threshold, leaving both the machine and the deeper interface unfinished rather than disproven.
