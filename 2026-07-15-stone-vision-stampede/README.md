# 07/15/2026 - Stone Vision Stampede

Dream Comics entry for July 15, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet arrives alone in a neon nightclub inhabited by multicolored slime gorgons. They reveal that they devoured the club's former golem and gargoyle patrons, then surround Jet with more stone-vision blasts than his rainbow crystal sword can reflect. Jet answers by summoning a yellow anti-gravity force-field belt with Lucid Light, blocking every beam inside a spherical shield, and rolling through the attackers like a human-powered hamster ball. With the slime gorgons defeated, the club reopens for its intended stone clientele.
