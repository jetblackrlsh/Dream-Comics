# Page Script - The Sanctuary Ladder

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. All characters, artifacts, and settings follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic full-page composition. The black werewolf stands on glowing pink liquid glass inside the living sanctuary, holding the rainbow crystal sword. The enormous black sanctuary rock floats above a distant city. A long ladder descends from it, with the red-and-yellow fire elemental clinging midway down. The academy's black-bar insulation installation and Jet's blue glasses appear as subtle lower-world motifs.

Visible cover text only: “THE SANCTUARY LADDER” and “AUGUST 26, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - The Living Sanctuary

A clear three-panel lucid setup page. Show the black werewolf standing motionless while the pink liquid-glass floor moves in waves; the pale-blue organic ceiling, distant rounded walls, and black rocks; then the werewolf summoning the rainbow crystal sword as unseen music fills the sanctuary.

Caption text:

- “I became lucid inside a black werewolf, standing upon cold pink liquid glass that moved beneath me.”
- “The living sanctuary glowed blue above, while strange black rocks rose from its impossible floor.”
- “A woman's unseen voice sang about sanctuary as I summoned my rainbow crystal sword.”

## Page 3 - Holder Across Time

A clear three-panel revelation-and-descent page. Show the werewolf contemplating the glowing sword as it reveals inherited memories without speech bubbles; the werewolf leaving the immense floating black sanctuary onto a ladder among airborne islands; then the ladder rapidly extending toward the distant city.

Caption text:

- “The sword revealed that this werewolf was one of its holders from across time.”
- “I climbed down from the sanctuary, an enormous black rock floating high above a distant city.”
- “When the ladder touched the ground, my lucidity vanished—and I entered the dream below.”

## Page 4 - Myself at the Academy

A clear three-panel social setup page. Show Jet inside a modern academy where students ignore a video; students curiously examining his wrist-mounted phone and blue augmented-reality glasses; then Jet apologizing to a friend beside a display case of action figures.

Caption text:

- “I became myself again inside an academy where everyone ignored the video we were meant to watch.”
- “People questioned my wrist-mounted phone and blue augmented-reality glasses as I admired the action figures.”
- “A friend felt hurt that I missed their birthday message, so I apologized and comforted them.”

## Page 5 - The Beautiful Hazard

A clear three-panel uncanny-transition page. Show Jet awakening in an academy bed beside the lone watcher lit by blue television light; Jet packing clothes and action figures as the watcher turns off the screen; then Jet emerging into a bright installation whose gemstone-like forms are visibly fibrous insulation held by black metal bars.

Caption text:

- “A false awakening left me in an academy bed beside the video's lone watcher in blue darkness.”
- “I packed my clothes and action figures, but she switched off the screen as I fled.”
- “Beyond the darkness, beautiful suspended gemstones revealed themselves as possibly dangerous insulation.”

## Page 6 - The Pursuit

A clear three-panel chase page. Show female artists beyond a window radiating fiery red auras; the artists chasing Jet with ordinary utensils while ignoring the baseball-cap man; then Jet entering the stairwell as the ponytailed woman hurries upward away from him.

Caption text:

- “The artists radiated red fire, and I sensed they had made their beautiful installation dangerous on purpose.”
- “They screamed, ‘It's a man!’ and chased me with utensils, ignoring another man nearby.”
- “I escaped into a stairwell, where one ponytailed woman hurried away instead of attacking me.”

## Page 7 - Remember the Light

A clear three-panel recovery page. Show Jet reaching with sparkling purple Lucid Light toward the retreating woman without harming her; Jet's face brightening in sudden recognition; then the scene shattering into the sky ladder, where he now inhabits the red-and-yellow fire elemental high above the city.

Caption text:

- “I reached for Lucid Light to stop her long enough to learn the rules of this world.”
- “Remembering my power made me remember the truth: I was dreaming.”
- “Lucidity returned me to the sky ladder as a red-and-yellow fire elemental.”

## Page 8 - The Map of Lucidity

A clear three-panel resolution page. Show the fire elemental viewing the black sanctuary and floating islands above; the distant city far below with the ladder visibly disconnected from it; then a serene symbolic composition linking the werewolf, Jet, and fire elemental through the rainbow sword, ladder, and sparkling purple Lucid Light.

Caption text:

- “The floating sanctuary was a lucid realm, while the city below held my ordinary dream.”
- “The ladder had grounded me into forgetfulness when it touched the city.”
- “Separated from the ground, it carried my awareness between bodies, worlds, and waking.”
