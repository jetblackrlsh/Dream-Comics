# Page Script — The Dragon App Café

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions are deliberately clear, nonredundant, and long enough to explain every major change without making the reader infer the plot. Jet and every recurring design must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 — Cover

One spectacular layered composition inside the completed café. Dragon-form Jet stands centered with purple scales, orange wings spread, beard and dreadlocks visible with his black hoodie and Dream Anime shirt. He holds one glowing app tile in a claw and a rainbow power candy in the other. The café strategist stands beside him with her turquoise tablet. Behind them, customers create apps at AI-agent computers, a luminous communal app library rises on one side, and transparent training rooms show harmless temporary powers striking inanimate targets on the other. Visible cover text only: “THE DRAGON APP CAFÉ” and “JULY 23, 2026”. No issue number.

## Page 2 — An Idea in an Empty Building

Five panels. Jet lies conscious but immobilized during sleep paralysis in a dark bedroom. Sparkling purple Lucid Light releases him into the continuing Storyverse. Jet then stands in the fairly empty commercial building in his established human form while the café strategist waits beside a small table. Jet examines the unused space and recalls the internet cafés he was too young to experience before they largely disappeared from the United States. A visual memory panel contrasts a cheap overnight foreign internet café with an upscale private gaming room, buffet, and comfortable bed. Final panel: Jet enthusiastically presents the empty floor as a place where visitors will make things instead of merely consuming them.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and entered the same Storyverse continuity I had explored since June 11, 2025.”
- “I arrived inside a fairly empty building, where a woman was already waiting to hear my plan.”
- “Internet cafés had fascinated me because I was too young to experience them before they mostly disappeared from the United States.”
- “Elsewhere, I had seen both cheap overnight cafés and luxurious versions with private rooms, meals, beds, and enormous game libraries.”
- “I wanted ours to be meaningfully different: every visitor would arrive ready to create something new.”

## Page 3 — Everyone Builds the Library

Six clear panels showing Jet's proposal as luminous architectural projections around him and the woman. A visitor arrives holding an app sketch. Admission unlocks a sleek computer with a friendly AI coding-agent avatar. The visitor describes an idea and the agent helps turn abstract code blocks into a polished app interface. The finished app becomes a colorful tile on the café's large communal library wall. Other customers browse and use previous visitors' apps. A simple popularity display uses stars and colored bars to show favorites. Final large panel: the formerly empty room appears as a thriving creator café, while human Jet gestures proudly and the strategist studies the model.

Caption text:

- “The admission fee would provide a powerful computer and an AI coding agent, so customers would not need expensive subscriptions of their own.”
- “Each person would enter with an app concept and use the café's tools to turn that idea into a working project.”
- “Every completed app would join a growing public library for future visitors to explore.”
- “A simple popularity system could reveal which creations people used most or found most interesting.”
- “Instead of paying to play one fixed collection of games, customers would expand the experience every time they visited.”
- “Everyone would arrive as both a user and a creator.”

## Page 4 — The Dream Demands a Dragon

Six transformation panels in the same empty building. Human Jet continues explaining while purple color spreads across his hands and face. Orange wing tips tear painlessly into view behind his hoodie. His body grows into the exact dragon-form design: purple scales, dragon face, short horns, orange membrane wings, purple tail, beard and dreadlocks, recognizable outfit, sword, tether, and belt bag. The strategist watches with a small knowing smile. Jet examines his claws and wings with visible annoyance rather than fear. The architectural app-café projection flickers behind him, temporarily overwhelmed by fantastical orange and purple light. Final panel: dragon-form Jet straightens, crosses his arms, and decides to take the hint.

Caption text:

- “While I explained the business, my skin unexpectedly began turning purple.”
- “Orange wings emerged from my back, and I gradually became a humanoid dragon.”
- “This annoyed me. I was sincerely interested in exploring the café as a business thought experiment.”
- “The transformation felt like a message from my subconscious: this was a dream, and my idea was not fantastical enough.”
- “I decided to take the hint without abandoning the part of the concept that mattered to me.”

## Page 5 — A Menu of Temporary Powers

Five panels. Dragon-form Jet notices that the café model lacks food. He imagines a bright counter where jewel-colored chewy candies appear in trays. Close-up shows red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, and pink candies, each paired with one simple power pictogram. A diverse group of ordinary adult visitors chooses candies. Split panels show harmless temporary effects: one visitor levitates, another grows crystal armor, another controls a small spiral of firelight, and another sprouts flowering vines. Jet excitedly expands the café blueprint while the strategist records the idea on her tablet. Final panel reveals a luminous sign motif combining an app-window icon, a wrapped candy, and a dragon wing, without extra readable wording.

Caption text:

- “One important part of an internet café still had no answer: the food.”
- “Because I was dreaming, I imagined small, colorful, chewy candies that each granted a different temporary superpower.”
- “A visitor could choose a flavor and briefly experience flight, speed, fire, crystal armor, plant growth, or another extraordinary ability.”
- “The café would no longer help people create only applications. It would also let ordinary people experiment with new versions of themselves.”
- “We had become an app café and a superpower café at the same time.”

## Page 6 — Power Without Danger

Six panels organized like a clear safety demonstration. Dragon-form Jet points to private training rooms added beside the coding stations. A customer scans a blue candy at a sealed door; a temporary-power timer activates. Inside one room, that customer levitates through hovering rings. In another, a fire-powered customer melts only an inanimate metal target. In another, a speed-powered visitor races around padded obstacles while remaining alone. The strategist monitors every room from her tablet, and automatic cyan shutdown light safely ends the effects. Final wide panel: glass-walled rooms glow beside the calm café while other visitors continue coding undisturbed.

Caption text:

- “Temporary powers needed a place where people could learn without endangering anyone.”
- “I designed private reinforced training rooms filled only with padded obstacles and inanimate targets.”
- “Customers could test one ability at a time while a visible timer measured how long the candy would last.”
- “They would never fight one another. Fire, speed, flight, and every other power would remain inside a controlled environment.”
- “Automatic safety systems would end the effect before the customer returned to the public café.”
- “The fantasy could feel extraordinary because the rules made it safe.”

## Page 7 — Her Marketing Strategy

Five panels. The café strategist finally lowers her tablet and admits the truth to dragon-form Jet. A holographic map shows several competing fantastical internet cafés elsewhere in the dream world: a wizard café, an underwater merfolk café, and a cloud café, all conveyed visually without extra readable names. She points to Jet's purple scales and orange wings, revealing she caused the transformation so their café would be the only one operated by a humanoid dragon. Jet looks briefly incredulous, then studies the unified blueprint. He accepts her reasoning and points out four interlocking icons: AI app creation, public app library, temporary power candy, and safe training rooms. Final panel: Jet and the strategist shake hands in front of the now fully designed café, both smiling.

Caption text:

- “The woman finally admitted that she was the person who had transformed me.”
- “Other fantastical internet cafés already existed throughout this dream world, and she wanted ours to stand out from the competition.”
- “Her marketing strategy was simple: ours would be the only internet café operated by a humanoid dragon.”
- “I accepted her reasoning, but explained that the dragon was now only one part of what made us different.”
- “No competitor combined AI app creation, a communal app library, temporary superpower candy, and private safety training.”

## Page 8 — The Dragon App Café

Six celebratory panels at the completed grand opening. Human visitors arrive carrying sketches and idea cards. At coding stations, AI agents help turn those concepts into working apps. Visitors add luminous finished tiles to the communal library while others browse the growing collection. Customers choose power candies and enter separate training rooms, where levitation, crystal armor, firelight, and speed strike only inanimate targets. The café strategist proudly watches the systems work together. Final large panel: confident dragon-form Jet welcomes a new visitor beneath readable title-and-date signage, orange wings forming a protective arch while the app library and training rooms glow behind him.

Caption text:

- “When the Dragon App Café opened, every customer arrived with an idea instead of merely asking what games were already installed.”
- “AI coding agents helped those ideas become applications, and every finished project made the public library larger.”
- “Colorful candies let visitors borrow extraordinary powers, then private rooms gave them a safe place to practice.”
- “The woman had been right that a dragon proprietor made us memorable.”
- “But the café mattered because technology and fantasy both gave ordinary people permission to create.”
- “Each person entered as a user, became a builder and a temporary hero, then left something new for everyone who came next.”
