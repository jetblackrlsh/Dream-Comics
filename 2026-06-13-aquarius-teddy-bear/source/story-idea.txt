Dream journal entry dated 06/13/2026.

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025. My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I.

Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit does not have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025.

Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds.

Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color with spiky slicked back yellow hair and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025.

Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah is a pale skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that covered her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She is wielding a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword. Her father Tecton is a green skinned, humanoid, muscular, cat ogre, barbarian who dresses in a brown loin cloth and a silver baldric. My drone Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper all met me in early 2026 while adventuring through the Super Cathedral. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I usually look like a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, warm brown eyes. I have a rainbow sword that resembles a colorful, futuristic, crystal sword, and an energy tether that resembles a rope made of golden glittery light.

In this dream, I am alone inside of a castle where I have taken the form of a blonde werewolf dressed like a hippie.

I search for someone to talk to or interact with.

I stumble upon a teddy bear.

The teddy bear is not alive and cannot talk, and yet I feel mystically drawn to it.

A monster with massive claws enters through the window.

The window was pretty high up, so the monster must have used their claws to scale up the side of the castle.

The monster slices off the head of my teddy bear and I feel myself fueled with rage.

But my hippie form prevents me from attacking in the traditional way.

I hold up a peace sign and use those two fingers to poke the monster's eyes.

I give the monster a big hug and use my strength to crush the monster's back.

I tell the monster that I hope they rise like an aquarius as I push them out of the window.

My crew arrives and are surprised that I fought the monster over a teddy bear.

The headless teddy bear leaks a colorful energy, implying that there was more to the bear after all.

Sometimes intuition can reveal invisible truths.

The limitations of circumstance are fuel for creative problem solving.
