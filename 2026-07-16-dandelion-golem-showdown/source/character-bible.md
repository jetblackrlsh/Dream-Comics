# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the black hoodie open over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries the rainbow crystal Sword of Life: a colorful futuristic crystal sword with a translucent faceted blade glowing in vivid red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet bands.
- Uses a golden energy tether that resembles a flexible rope made from glittering golden light. It can attach to buildings and form long luminous arcs without behaving like an ordinary physical rope.
- Uses Lucid Light: sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. On this adventure it holds Jet aloft, pushes him forward, transforms claw fragments into dandelions, and gives life to dandelion golems.
- Continuity rule: Jet's dark-brown skin, full beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped black pants, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow sword, golden tether, and purple starry Lucid Light remain visually consistent on every page.
- Expression range: lucid focus, exhilarated joy, startled pain, determined concentration, playful confidence, amused satisfaction.

## Purple clawed villain

- Tall, powerfully built humanoid antagonist with saturated purple skin or tight purple organic armor.
- Entire silhouette is covered with backward-sweeping spikes along the shoulders, forearms, spine, hips, and shins.
- Has a narrow angular face, glowing pale-yellow eyes, a cruel grin, and no hair.
- Each hand ends in five freakishly long curved claws, several times longer than the fingers. The claws are glossy dark violet with bright magenta edges and can stab into the street, travel underground, and erupt from the ground at a distant target.
- After Jet cuts through the underground claws, the villain's fingertips remain visibly shortened and jagged for the rest of the story.
- Expression range: predatory focus, mocking arrogance, growing irritation, panic, exhausted surrender.
- Continuity rule: keep the villain purple, spike-covered, hairless, pale-eyed, and equipped with the same exaggerated dark-violet claws. Do not add a cape, conventional armor, or extra weapons.

## Dandelions and dandelion golems

- The first dandelions grow directly from the shattered dark-violet claw fragments after purple star-filled Lucid Light wraps around them.
- Each flower has a golden-yellow center, delicate white seed crown, and a luminous green stem threaded with faint purple starlight.
- Dandelion golems are small knee-high humanoids made from braided green stems, leaves, and pale root fibers. Each has a round white dandelion-seed head, simple friendly glowing purple eyes, and soft seed fluff around the shoulders.
- They look whimsical, determined, and nonviolent rather than frightening. They restrain and crowd the villain instead of injuring them.
- When a golem is struck, it separates in a bright puff of white seeds and immediately reforms as two complete golems. Repeated blows create an obvious exponential crowd.

## City

- Bright contemporary dream city with tall glass-and-steel skyscrapers, colorful rooftop signs, broad streets, reflective windows, rooftop gardens, and a clear blue-violet sky.
- Golden tether arcs, purple Lucid Light trails, and drifting dandelion seeds create the recurring color language.
- The claw attack cracks limited sections of pavement but does not cause mass destruction. The streets are largely empty so the action remains visually clear and no bystanders are harmed.

## Storyverse continuity

- The Storyverse is the multiverse of cosmic bubble universes where all possible story ideas exist. It can be suggested briefly in the sleep-paralysis-to-lucidity transition as luminous bubbles floating in a cosmic void.
- Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's wider team, but they do not physically appear because Jet arrives alone.
