# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: lucid dream explorer and narrator, alone for most of this issue.
- Standard waking-lucid design: man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie, black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag.
- Signature props: rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glittering energy tether, sparkling purple Lucid Light aura when he acts with lucidity.
- Paint form: Jet's colors and hoodie silhouette melt into a glossy paint puddle with dark brown, black, white, rainbow crystal color, and golden glitter streaks, still suggesting his identity through color and shape.
- Block form: Jet becomes a humanoid made of large glossy blocks, retaining dark-brown face blocks, blocky dreadlocks under a black block hoodie, blocky "Dream Anime" shirt colors, white block sneakers, rainbow crystal sword fragment, and golden tether lines, but his form looks rigid and imposed.
- Clay form: Jet reshapes into a warm brown clay humanoid with clearly sculpted dreadlocks, beard, hoodie outline, belt bag, sneakers, rainbow sword, and golden tether. The clay texture should look flexible, hand-shaped, and self-directed rather than unfinished.
- Expression range: startled lucidity, focused escape, helpless paint-form panic, discomfort in the block body, calm resolve while becoming clay.

## Humanoid Orange Squid Pilot

- Role: surreal UFO pilot and collector of Jet's paint-puddle form.
- Face and body: humanoid alien with bright orange squid skin, rounded squid head, large glossy black eyes, small beak-like mouth, short tentacle-like tendrils around the jaw and back of the head, two arms with long flexible fingers, and two sturdy legs.
- Outfit: sleek teal and violet pilot harness with silver buckles, transparent utility belt, small glowing control badges.
- Props: silver ladle-like collection scoop, clear containment vessel filled with Jet's paint colors, UFO cockpit controls, reconstitution machine levers.
- Expression range: curious, clinical, not cruel, absorbed in the process of collecting and rebuilding a strange paint-being.

## Ghostly UFO

- Role: abduction craft entering the museum through the skylight.
- Design: flying saucer with a translucent ghostly body, ethereal green glow, soft mist trails, circular green tractor beam, and curved glass cockpit.
- Visual relationship to continuity: resembles Leon's ghostly UFO design, but in this issue it functions as the squid pilot's surreal museum intruder.

## Reconstitution Machine

- Role: identity pressure device.
- Design: tall white, purple, gold, and chrome machine with transparent paint tubes, rotating block molds, glowing purple Lucid Light distortions, geometric readout panels, gold clamps, and a central chamber shaped like an abstract human outline.
- Behavior: turns liquid paint into rigid blocks, then fractures when Jet consciously reshapes himself into clay.

## Key Settings

- Crinkled sketch: a textured paper world with pencil lines, paint splatters, torn wrinkles, and a visible frame that becomes a window.
- Surreal museum: white, purple, and gold interior with polished floors, tall columns, floating frames, skylight, and many twisting coiled hallways arranged so they appear to spin.
- Storyverse sky: cosmic void beyond the skylight with bubble universes and purple Lucid Light sparkles.
