# Page Script - The Molten Barricade

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, long enough to make every development obvious, and nonredundant. Jet, Silonis, the goblins, the giant troll, the cave dragon, the enchanted fire bullets, and the molten barricade must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

One dramatic vertical composition above the futuristic city. Jet flies upward through a spiral of curving orange-red fire bullets with sparkling purple Lucid Light trailing from his body. Silonis hovers protectively beside him and projects a translucent rainbow force-field arc. Below, several green goblins aim compact black guns from rooftops. Behind the skyline, the colossal olive-green troll raises its enormous wooden club. A dark cave opening, cobalt dragon eye, and glowing fused boulders form a subtle lower-background montage. Visible cover text only: "THE MOLTEN BARRICADE" and "AUGUST 3, 2026." No issue number.

## Page 2 - Fire in the Sky

Six panels. Jet and Silonis soar between tall city buildings. Green goblins fire compact black guns from the streets and rooftops. Ordinary bullets ignite in midair, becoming orange-red miniature fireballs that bend sharply around buildings. Jet twists, dives, and rolls through the homing barrage. The final wide panel reveals the enormous troll lumbering between towers with its wooden club raised.

Caption text:

- "I began the dream flying through a city with Silonis, the living Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life."
- "A band of goblins wanted to steal one of the most powerful artifacts in the Storyverse."
- "Their guns looked ordinary, but every bullet had been enchanted with fire magic."
- "Once fired, the ammunition became a flaming projectile that curved through the sky and chased me."
- "I could dodge the miniature fireballs while flying, so the goblins themselves felt like a distraction."
- "Their real threat was the gigantic troll advancing behind them with an enormous wooden club."

## Page 3 - Knocked From the Sky

Six panels. Establish the troll as roughly half a skyscraper tall, not a full kaiju. Jet looks back at the fire bullets while the troll swings from below. The enormous wooden club strikes Jet without gore and sends him tumbling downward between buildings. Silonis immediately flies beneath him and unfolds a curved translucent rainbow force field. Jet lands safely on the glowing barrier above the street. In the final panel he springs forward with urgent purple Lucid Light.

Caption text:

- "The troll was not skyscraper-tall, but at nearly half that height it still felt almost like a kaiju."
- "While I focused on weaving between the goblins' fire bullets, its club swept across my path."
- "The blow knocked me out of the sky and sent me crashing toward the street."
- "Before I could hit the ground, I called upon Silonis for protection."
- "The living sword formed a force field beneath me and softened the impact."
- "I was unharmed, but now I had to escape before the goblins and their troll surrounded me."

## Page 4 - The Living Shield

Six panels. Jet propels himself down a long city road at super speed with a star-filled purple trail. The slow troll falls far behind, but the goblins sprint after Jet with surprising speed. Their curving fire bullets close in from several angles. Silonis leaves Jet's hand and autonomously circles him, knocking each projectile away with rainbow-white flashes and small non-gory bursts of flame. Jet rounds a sharp corner and briefly disappears from the goblins' view. A huge cave entrance waits beyond the buildings.

Caption text:

- "Using Lucid Light, I propelled myself forward at super speed."
- "The enormous troll quickly fell behind, but the goblins were fast enough to keep pace."
- "They continued firing enchanted bullets that curved toward me from every direction."
- "Fortunately, Silonis had a mind and will of its own."
- "The sword flew from my hand, circled me, and independently deflected every flaming projectile."
- "After I rounded a corner and vanished from sight, we discovered the entrance to a massive cave."

## Page 5 - The Dragon's Price

Six panels. Jet and Silonis enter the vast dark cavern while the goblins race past the corner outside. Deep inside, the large cobalt-blue dragon with silver underbelly and folded wings greets them calmly. Jet gestures toward the entrance while explaining the pursuit. The dragon offers shelter, then extends one greedy claw toward Silonis. When the claw closes around the hilt, Silonis surrounds itself with a bright rainbow force field that repels the dragon backward and returns to Jet. The dragon's violet eyes narrow as it turns toward the cave mouth.

Caption text:

- "Hoping to hide from the goblins and the troll, Silonis and I ran into the cave."
- "Deep inside the darkness, we encountered a dragon that initially seemed friendly."
- "We explained that the goblins were pursuing us because they wanted the Rainbow Crystal Sword."
- "The dragon offered us shelter under one condition: I had to surrender Silonis."
- "When it grabbed the sword, Silonis formed a force field, violently repelled the dragon, and returned to me."
- "Angry that it could not claim the artifact, the dragon decided to betray us."

## Page 6 - Trapped in Darkness

Six panels. The cobalt dragon shouts toward the cave entrance using one rectangular caption box containing the only spoken line, then crawls deeper into darkness. Goblins enter with guns raised while the troll's huge legs and club fill and block the bright cave mouth behind them. Jet and Silonis hide silently within a deep violet shadow between rocks. The searching goblins' gun lights pass close by. Jet's concerned expression changes into recognition as an inset memory shows his hands shaping earth with amber-purple energy during an early Storyverse adventure.

Caption text:

- "The dragon shouted toward the entrance: 'They're in here!'"
- "Then it crawled deeper into the cave, abandoning the conflict it had created."
- "The goblins entered with their guns raised while the giant troll blocked the only visible exit."
- "Because the cavern was so dark, Silonis and I were able to hide within the shadows."
- "As the goblins searched, I remembered a power I had learned during one of my earliest Storyverse adventures."
- "I had learned geomancy - and earth magic was perfectly suited to this trap."

## Page 7 - Their Fire Makes It Stronger

Six panels. Jet extends both hands and amber-purple geomancy tears a mass of boulders from the cave ceiling. The rocks crash down between Jet and the goblins, forming a thick wall. On the far side, goblins fire repeatedly; their orange-red enchanted bullets strike the stone. Close panels show the rock faces holding while only the seams begin to glow and fuse. The barricade becomes a solid molten-welded wall, stronger than before. A final upward-looking panel reveals that removing the ceiling stones has opened a large skylight above Jet.

Caption text:

- "I used geomancy to tear a mass of rocks from the ceiling."
- "The boulders crashed down between the goblins and me, forming a thick barricade."
- "From the opposite side, their guns hammered the wall with enchanted fire bullets."
- "The magic did not destroy my defense."
- "Its heat partially melted and welded the stones together, turning the attack into an even stronger molten barrier."
- "Pulling those rocks from the ceiling had also opened a skylight - a new route out of the cave."

## Page 8 - Escape to the Storyverse

Six panels with a triumphant vertical resolution. Jet looks up through the skylight while the fused barricade glows behind him and goblin silhouettes remain trapped on the far side. He launches upward on a column of sparkling purple Lucid Light with Silonis flying beside him. The troll swings too slowly outside the cave and misses beneath them. Jet and Silonis rise high above the landscape, then cross into the cosmic black-violet Storyverse filled with nebulae and floating bubble universes. The final wide panel shows both traveling freely into the luminous multiverse.

Caption text:

- "The molten wall separated us from the goblins, and the opening above us offered freedom."
- "I launched upward through the skylight before the troll could react."
- "Silonis accelerated beside me as we climbed far beyond the cave."
- "Behind us, the goblins remained sealed away by the barrier their own fire had strengthened."
- "The slow troll and the treacherous dragon could no longer stop us."
- "Together, Silonis and I escaped safely into the Storyverse."
