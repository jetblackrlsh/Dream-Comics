# 07/31/2026 - The Metal Rabbit's Rodeo Redemption

Dream Comics entry for July 31, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet immediately encounters a bizarre creature in an empty black void: a grimy humanoid skeletal rabbit made from orange metal, with glowing red eyes and two large buck teeth. The rabbit announces that it wants to frighten him, but Jet is fascinated rather than afraid.

The rabbit fills the void with ominous purple fog and summons decayed clowns that pull chainsaws from their rib cages. Jet calls upon Silonis, his living rainbow crystal Sword of Life, and demonstrates that every injury can be healed immediately. A many-armed black spider steals the sword with an adhesive palm, but Silonis flies independently, slices away the trapped hand, and returns to Jet.

Jet protects himself inside a nearly indestructible rainbow crystal dome. The rabbit notices that the barrier does not extend beneath Jet's feet and sends nearly one hundred puppy-sized mole men through the ground. Their combined weight briefly surprises Jet, but he launches into the air and releases a shock wave of sparkling purple Lucid Light that scatters every summoned creature.

The Metal Rabbit grows into a giant and insists that Jet can never escape. Jet calmly answers that he does not need to escape. Instead of attacking, he creates a gigantic metal carrot. The gift instantly dissolves the rabbit's threatening demeanor and replaces it with astonished gratitude.

Jet suggests that the rabbit abandon its unsuccessful career frightening people and become a rodeo cowboy instead. Beneath its monstrous appearance, Jet senses a strange southern charm. The rabbit becomes hopeful, the two exchange a cheerful “Howdy,” and Jet flies out of the dark void toward the brilliant bubble universes of the Storyverse.
