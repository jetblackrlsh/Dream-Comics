# Page Script - The Rooted Heart

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet and the Rooting Hero follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

The friendly fur-covered Rooting Hero stands in a bright children's hospital atrium, surrounded by joyful children and balloon-tipped hair strands. Jet watches with admiration. Beyond the windows, an intact city floats beneath one enormous luminous balloon while golden roots connect it safely to the ground. A radiant heart-shaped light near a patient foreshadows the hero's healing gift. Visible cover text only: "THE ROOTED HEART" and "AUGUST 13, 2026." No issue number.

## Page 2 - The Hidden Hero

Jet lands on a rooftop beside the ordinary-looking stranger and surprises them by recognizing their secret.

Caption text:

- "While flying through the city, I noticed someone standing alone on a rooftop."
- "They looked ordinary—but a dream download told me they possessed an extraordinary power."
- "I asked whether they were a superhero."
- "They were startled because their ordinary form protected a secret identity."
- "Then they admitted that I was right."

## Page 3 - Hair, Balloons, and Flight

The hero becomes covered in friendly Sasquatch-like hair, forms balloons, swings with a hair whip, and floats above the rooftops.

Caption text:

- "The hero grew hairs from their body and formed colorful balloons at the tips."
- "They covered themselves in fur until they looked like a friendly Sasquatch."
- "Their voice and body seemed to shift from masculine to feminine."
- "A long hair whip let them swing between buildings."
- "A balloon from their back lifted them while the whip steered their flight."

## Page 4 - Uprooted

The hero roots a detached hair into a building, unroots the structure, lifts it by balloon, and reroots it while Jet reacts in alarm.

Caption text:

- "The hero rooted one detached hair into a building."
- "Then they unrooted the entire structure and lifted it beneath an enormous balloon."
- "I feared the plumbing, electricity, and foundations had been torn apart."
- "But rerooting restored every connection perfectly."
- "What looked destructive was completely reversible."

## Page 5 - The City in the Sky

The hero lifts the whole city and restores it as authorities arrive and Jet defends their character.

Caption text:

- "Next, the hero unrooted the entire city."
- "One colossal balloon carried every street and tower into the sky."
- "They rerooted it without leaving any damage."
- "The authorities demanded proof that such frightening power truly helped people."
- "I vouched for the hero because I believed their heart was in the right place."

## Page 6 - The Hospital Hero

The fur-covered hero visits sick children, making balloons and turning their Sasquatch appearance into joy.

Caption text:

- "At the hospital, the hero revealed how they normally used their gift."
- "Covered in soft fur, they visited children who needed hope."
- "Balloon-tipped hairs filled the room with color."
- "The children were delighted that a friendly Sasquatch had come to entertain them."
- "Their strangest form had become a source of comfort."

## Page 7 - A Perfect Rooting

The hero performs a symbolic, non-gory heart transplant through golden rooting magic while doctors and Jet witness the patient's restoration.

Caption text:

- "The hero's most extraordinary work involved organ transplants."
- "They could unroot a failing heart without pain or blood."
- "A viable donor heart could take its place perfectly."
- "The same power that restored buildings restored the patient."
- "A dangerous surgery became a seamless act of healing."

## Page 8 - The Rooted Heart

Jet reflects as the healed patient embraces family, children hold balloons, and the hero stands humbly among them.

Caption text:

- "Someone capable of lifting a city could have chased battles, fame, or fear."
- "If they were cruel, their unrooting power could cause unimaginable destruction."
- "Instead, they chose hospitals, children, and lives in need."
- "Their power seemed bizarre and frightening only until I understood its purpose."
- "The greatest thing they lifted was not a city—it was hope."
