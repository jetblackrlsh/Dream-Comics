# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow sword that looks like a colorful futuristic crystal sword, but this story focuses on his golden energy tether. The tether resembles a rope made from golden glittery light. Jet's expressions move from alert urgency, to thoughtful concern, to startled discomfort, to relaxed relief.

## Super Wizard Man

Super Wizard Man is an adult wizard superhero with a kind face, determined eyes, and a heroic posture. He wears a deep blue wizard robe with bright gold trim, a matching pointed wizard hat, a short red superhero cape, a gold star emblem on his chest, leather boots, and glowing bracers. He carries a polished wooden wand tipped with a tiny blue star. He should look sincere and brave rather than sinister. His magic glows blue, gold, and violet.

## Salem Witch Trial Mob

The Salem crowd wears late-1600s Puritan clothing: dark coats, simple dresses, white collars, bonnets, brimmed hats, and boots. Their faces show fear, anger, and confusion. They should read as a dangerous mob, not as individual villains. They flee when Jet pulls down the storm cloud and lightning-lit rain extinguishes the stake.

## Hidden Underground Lair

Super Wizard Man's lair is hidden beneath Salem behind a stone tunnel and wooden trapdoor. It combines colonial stone walls, candles, shelves of potions, hanging herbs, spell books, a glowing future comic, and superhero training equipment. The future comic should look like a bright, mysterious comic book without needing legible interior text beyond the title if it appears.

## Future Comic

The future comic is the object that inspired Super Wizard Man. It glows with impossible Storyverse light and shows superhero imagery from a time long after Salem. If title text appears, keep it short and clear: "Future Heroes". Avoid extra unreadable text.

## Mystical Salon Chair

The mystical salon chair is summoned by Super Wizard Man inside the lair. It looks like an ornate barber chair mixed with magical crystal machinery, floating gold rings, velvet cushions, and glowing blue-violet runes. It is used for a magical brain cleaning, so it should feel helpful, strange, and relaxing.

## Jet's Brain Cleaning

The brain cleaning should be surreal but non-gory. Jet sits calmly in the mystical salon chair while a transparent glowing image of his brain appears above or around his head. Super Wizard Man uses magical tools such as a glowing brush, soft scrub sponge, sparkling water, tiny star-shaped combs, and blue-gold magic to scratch, brush, scrub, and wash the itch away. The mood is relaxing and gentle, not medical horror.
