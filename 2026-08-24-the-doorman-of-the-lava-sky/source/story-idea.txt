Jet exits a dimensional elevator beside Autumn, a Victorian-dressed woman who claims to be his wife and calls him the Doorman, even though he remembers neither her nor their dimensional adventures. They arrive in a rocky desert filled with black-bone castles beneath a lava-lamp sky that blocks all sunlight. Autumn summons Apollo, who sends Jet to defeat Meltronome, the lava god responsible for the molten sky, then disappears. Jet turns an attacking black-bone dinosaur into a Lucid Light mech and negotiates with the god of blades, who supports Meltronome's removal because it will improve his own divine rank. Carrying the blade god's token, Jet enters a magical academy and meets a gray-skinned god of deals. The deal god transfers his unlimited but conditional magic into Jet, then steals another person's lightning-rhinoceros powers so he can abandon dangerous godhood without becoming defenseless. When the transformed rhinoceros threatens the academy, Jet uses Porta to send its legs into the desert and harmlessly immobilize its upper body. Jet combines the transferred power with his own unrestricted Storyverse magic, casts Meltronome away, and clears the sky. Apollo rises like the restored sun and declares himself the world's new supreme god, leaving Jet to hope the new reign will be kinder and to wonder when Autumn and the dimensional elevator will return.
