# Treatment

## Title

05/20/2026: Blue Skull Lucidity

## Logline

After sleep paralysis opens a lucid door, Jet becomes the Blue Skull, a psychic assassin-superhero whose crystal skull helmet controls machines, then fights through hostile dream zones until his power returns at the exact moment the waking alarm pulls him away.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to hold onto lucidity and the Blue Skull's machine-commanding power, but a forgotten defeat fractures the dream into shifting zones where he must survive with almost no power and only shuriken, instinct, and improvisation.

## Theme

Lucidity can disappear and return like a signal; the real victory is staying present long enough to catch it when it comes back.

## Emotional Arc

Jet starts powerful and mythic, becomes disoriented and outnumbered, fights his way back through practical survival, briefly tastes unlimited reach through drones, then wakes with both frustration and gratitude.

## Ending

The Blue Skull's restored helmet links Jet to drones across the world, offering total freedom for one luminous instant. The 4:30 AM alarm cuts through the vision, leaving Jet awake in bed, carrying the idea into the mundane day.
