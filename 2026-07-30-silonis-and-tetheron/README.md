# 07/30/2026 - Silonis and Tetheron

Dream Comics entry for July 30, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet enters a modern city and considers a hypothetical question: if he could bring only one primary dream artifact into the waking world, would he choose the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword or the Golden Energy Tether?

The sword initially seems like the obvious answer. It can heal injuries, animate objects, purify evil beings, create protective force fields, fly independently, and carry Jet through the air. It is also already alive and capable of communicating, making it a companion rather than a tool that Jet could simply abandon.

The tether has fewer defined abilities, but its power to create persistent magical bonds gives it nearly limitless creative utility. It can connect a laptop to a chair and make that object reprogrammable, leave the bond intact after being removed, extend across any distance, catch Jet's intended target with perfect accuracy, and carry him like a grappling line.

A masked thief steals the tether and shatters the modern city into the cosmic void of the Storyverse. When the thief tries to restrain Jet with the artifact, Jet deliberately cuts it with the Life Sword. The thief celebrates, believing Jet has destroyed one of his own greatest possessions, but Jet gathers the pieces and uses the sword's life energy to restore them.

The restoration does more than repair the tether. It awakens or reveals a living presence within it. Jet defeats the thief with both artifacts working together and learns the names by which they know themselves: the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword is Silonis, and the Golden Energy Tether is Tetheron.

Jet ends the dream understanding that the original comparison was incomplete. Silonis offers extraordinary life-giving power, protection, and flight. Tetheron offers limitless possibilities through magical connection. Both are alive, both matter to Jet, and both will continue exploring the Storyverse beside him.
