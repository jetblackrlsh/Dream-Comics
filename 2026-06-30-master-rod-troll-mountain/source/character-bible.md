# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: protagonist, lucid dreamer, Storyverse explorer, wielder of Lucid Light, rainbow sword, golden energy tether, and Porta spell.
- Stable appearance: man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, warm brown eyes.
- Signature powers and props: rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glitter energy tether, sparkling purple Lucid Light like a night sky full of stars, and the ability to cast Porta as a glowing portal spell.
- Expression range: burdened sadness, cautious hope, determined racing focus, tactical calm, humble honesty, delighted surprise in the later world-shifts.
- Continuity rule: keep Jet recognizably Jet on every page through skin tone, full beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, white stripe pants, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow sword, golden tether, and purple Lucid Light.

## The Trolls

- Role: residents of the grassy green world and witnesses to the quest.
- Appearance: colorful trolls with pointed elf-like ears, red eyes, shaggy fur, bell-shaped heads, broad expressive faces, sturdy hands, and handmade climbing wraps or flower garlands.
- Personality: mostly friendly, emotionally perceptive, communal, and reverent toward The Master.
- Continuity rule: vary fur colors but keep the red eyes, elf-like ears, shaggy fur, and bell-shaped heads consistent.

## Purple-Lightning Speedster

- Role: rival climber, one of the seven trained trolls, tempted by exclusive power.
- Appearance: lean athletic troll with violet shaggy fur, pointed elf-like ears, red eyes, bell-shaped head, silver climbing harness, lightning-shaped purple markings, and crackling purple lightning around ankles and hands.
- Signature power: super speed with purple lightning trails.
- Expression range: smug confidence, anger, shock, confused humility.
- Continuity rule: keep violet fur, silver climbing harness, purple lightning, red eyes, and bell-shaped troll head consistent.

## The Master Rod

- Role: mystical lightning staff left by The Master on the mountain peak.
- Appearance: tall silver-and-storm-blue staff with a forked lightning head, floating runes, rain-glass inlays, and a bright white-blue electrical aura.
- Function: channels divine lightning, launches enemies skyward, and symbolizes miracle hope.
- Continuity rule: never depict it as a sword; it is always a staff or rod of lightning.

## Elemental Core Mountain Bike

- Role: Jet's Lucid Light creation for outpacing the speedster.
- Appearance: motorized mountain bike with rugged black frame, glowing wheel rims, six visible elemental cores mounted along the frame, and colored energy streams for fire, ice, lightning, wind, earth, and water.
- Function: gives Jet enough speed to race directly to the mountain peak, then becomes bait for the energy-vampire ogre.
- Continuity rule: keep the six colored elemental cores visible until the bike bursts.

## Energy-Vampire Ogre

- Role: monster drawn from space by elemental cores.
- Appearance: huge green-gray ogre with tusks, black horns, blackened space-scarred armor, clawed hands, red hungry eyes, and violet cracks glowing across its skin and armor.
- Signature threat: drinks energy through its mouth and hands, but can be overloaded by too much elemental power at once.
- Continuity rule: keep the green-gray skin, tusks, black horns, blackened armor, violet cracks, and hunger aura consistent.

## Female Wrestler Arch-Nemesis

- Role: antagonist in the brief drama television world transition.
- Appearance: powerful athletic woman with tan skin, long crimson hair, intense eyes, black-and-gold wrestling gear, championship belt accents, and a dramatic television-villain posture.
- Relationship to baby dinosaurs: obsessed with defeating Jet while also claiming responsibility for the baby dinosaurs in a negligent, destructive way.
- Continuity rule: keep her visibly wrestler-coded and villainous during the final coda.

## Baby Dinosaurs

- Role: endangered innocents in the downhill energy construct race.
- Appearance: small colorful baby dinosaurs with big frightened eyes, rounded faces, tiny claws, and soft pastel scales, trapped together in a translucent glowing energy-construct plane.
- Continuity rule: make them vulnerable and cute without turning the scene into comedy.

## Grassy Troll World

- Role: primary setting.
- Appearance: beautiful grassy green land covered in pink flowers, rivers flowing in reverse up waterfalls, crystalline spray rising upward, a giant mountain at the edge of the world, and a luminous mythic sky.

## Virtual Anime Battle World

- Role: brief transitional world.
- Appearance: arena-like digital anime battlefield with neon grid horizons, fighters launching black holes, energy absorption bubbles, and dramatic super-move impact effects.
