Dream journal date: 06/20/2026

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and enters the continuing Storyverse lucid-dream continuity. In a jungle, he finds himself among fallen humanoid bees, called Were-Bees. Ahead, a volcano erupts with molten honey. The honey lava has consumed the Were-Bees' homes and is rolling toward Jet as a massive wave.

Jet calls on his team to save the jungle. He tells Leon, his green-glowing Ghostly UFO, to use a tractor beam to lift Were-Bees who are closest to the wave. He tells Skelebot, Johnson's humanoid speedster robot, to use super speed to evacuate the jungle.

As the wave closes in, Jet uses his Aegis spell to summon a golden turtle-shell shield and block the honey lava, but he cannot hold it forever against the scorching force. Jet tells Overdrive to use their matter manipulation powers to pacify the wave. Overdrive increases the atomic motion of the honey, heating it until the dangerous liquid evaporates into honey clouds.

Thanks to quick thinking and teamwork, Jet and the crew save the jungle from certain destruction.
