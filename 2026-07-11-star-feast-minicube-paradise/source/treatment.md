# Treatment

## Logline

Hungry for godlike energy, lucid dreamer Jet devours a star and follows its remains beyond the observable universe, where his attempt to give a lost collective a home turns into a planet-sized pursuit—until a tiny portal and a humble beach-shack meal teach him where real satisfaction lies.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to restore his depleted energy, but the cosmic power he consumes creates consequences when the homeless Minicubes interpret gratitude as assimilation. His compassion saves them, yet he must protect his own identity and escape the living world they become.

## Theme

Compassion does not require surrendering the self, and spectacular power cannot replace simple nourishment and rest.

## Ending

After escaping a planet-sized slime collective, Jet returns to the same beach still hungry. A tortilla-chip-headed chef serves him a rich shake and spicy-honey meat. Jet eats peacefully at sunset, finally restored by hospitality instead of cosmic power.

## Emotional Arc

Jet moves from exhaustion, to reckless exhilaration, to recognition and empathy, to alarm and decisive escape, and finally to grounded contentment.

## Story Continuity

This dream belongs to the continuing Storyverse lucid-dream continuity begun on 06/11/2025. Jet is alone for this episode. His established team—Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper—remains part of the wider continuity but does not appear in the dream's action.
