Above the recoiling vampiric captain floats a blazing miniature golden-white sun. Behind them, the chamber tears open into a cosmic composition: Leon's colossal translucent green mecha phases through the unimaginably larger blue-green ghost cruiser, while wrecked pirate ships burn around them. A small ornate mirror inset shows the Blue Enchantress with her unmistakable long messy sky-blue hair, bangs, pointed hat, and sparkling blue dress. Visible cover text only: “SOL AGAINST THE GHOST ARMADA” and “JULY 29, 2026”. No issue number.

Jet rides through the cavern standing on the independently flying Life Sword like a hoverboard. The final panel shows Jet holding the sword while intricate future circuitry glows inside its hilt.

## Page 4 — The Armada Destroys Itself

Six panels dominated by a surrounding spherical armada. The closing Porta vanishes behind Leon's green mecha. Dozens of pirate ships appear on every side, furious that the bubble universe has disappeared. They unleash crossing streams of red-orange laser cannonballs. Leon becomes translucent and intangible. A precise wide panel shows projectiles passing harmlessly through Leon and striking ships on the exact opposite side. Multiple pirate vessels destroy one another in spectacular but non-gory explosions. In the final tall panel, the darkness behind the wreckage parts and the colossal blue-green ghost cruiser emerges, dwarfing Leon.

Caption text:

- “We had moved the universe just in time. A massive armada of Storyverse pirates arrived seconds after Porta closed.”
- “Their target had vanished in front of them, so every surrounding ship fired its laser cannons at Leon's mecha form.”
- “Leon was a ghost. He became intangible, and every attack passed through his body without causing any harm.”
- “Because he stood at the armada's center, each projectile continued forward into a pirate ship positioned on the opposite side.”
- “Within moments, the entire fleet had blasted itself apart while Leon remained untouched in the crossfire.”
- “Then an unimaginably large ghost cruiser emerged from the darkness—and it made even Leon's universe-moving mecha look small.”

## Page 5 — Give the Ghost Ship Life

Six panels. The ghost cruiser accelerates directly at Leon. A diagram-like action panel makes clear that both Leon and the cruiser are intangible and cannot phase through each other. Jet stands inside Leon's glowing cockpit and aims the Life Sword. A rainbow-white life beam strikes the cruiser, transforming translucent spectral decks into black iron, burgundy armor, golden ribs, and physical mass. Leon turns intangible just before impact and phases safely through the newly solid ship. He then curves back and spreads his green ghostly presence through the physical hull, engines, and controls. Leon opens a green portal from his body and releases Jet into the cruiser's gothic interior.

Caption text:

- “The cruiser charged straight at us, and Leon warned that one ghost could not safely phase through another.”
- “If their intangible bodies collided, the impact would tear Leon apart.”
- “I fired the Life Sword's power into the cruiser and restored the spectral vessel to physical life.”
- “Now that the warship was solid matter, Leon could become intangible and pass through its center without harm.”
- “But I had created a living physical cruiser capable of rampaging across the Storyverse, so we could not let it escape.”
- “Leon phased back inside, possessed its systems, and released me into the ship to stop whoever still commanded it.”

## Page 6 — The Captain Drains Jet

Six panels. Jet enters the gothic command chamber and confronts the vampiric space pirate captain. Four leech-like tendrils wrap around Jet and visibly siphon golden-white life energy into the captain. Jet weakens, then severs the tendrils with the Life Sword. The empowered captain lunges with enormous black claws. Jet casts Aegis; the claws smash against the blue-violet shield and shatter. Jet counterattacks, but the vampire dissolves into black-red mist and reforms behind him, repeatedly escaping every sword strike.

Caption text:

- “The captain was an undead space pirate whose claws and leech-like tendrils made his vampiric hunger visible.”
- “His tendrils wrapped around me and drained my life energy, weakening my body while making him much stronger.”
- “I severed the tendrils with the Life Sword, but the power he had already stolen remained inside him.”
- “When his sharpened claws struck, I cast Aegis. My magical shield held, and his claws shattered against it.”
- “I tried to counterattack with the Life Sword, but he dissolved into mist and teleported away before the blade could touch him.”
- “Every conventional sword strike was too slow. To win, I needed an attack that could reach him before he became mist.”

## Page 7 — A Beam Faster Than Mist

Six panels. Jet lowers the sword slightly and studies his star-filled Lucid Light gathering in his open hand. The captain reforms and lunges. Jet fires a narrow instantaneous purple-white Lucid Light laser; it crosses the chamber faster than the vampire can react. The beam strikes and stuns him in solid form. Jet looks surprised by the direct use of his familiar energy. A clear thought-through-action montage shows Porta as a portal, Aegis as a shield, Chronos as divine lightning, and then the radiant Latin word concept “SOL” represented by a sun symbol without floating prose. The final panel shows Jet raising his hand as the first golden sphere of sunlight forms above the trapped captain.

Caption text:

- “Instead of shaping Lucid Light into armor or another physical construct, I concentrated its star-filled energy in my open hand.”
- “I fired it directly like a laser, and the beam moved too quickly for the captain to evade.”
- “The attack stunned him before he could dissolve into mist, revealing a new use for one of my oldest powers.”
- “Lucid Light created the opening, but a vampire's true weakness was not magical starlight. It was sunlight.”
- “My spells used clear Greek or Latin ideas: Porta opened a portal, Aegis raised a shield, and Chronos unleashed divine punishment.”
- “I knew the word for the exact power I needed. I raised my hand and cast a new spell: ‘Sol.’”

A cosmic panel shows the now-physical colossal cruiser safely locked under Leon's spectral control. A small mirror panel shows the Blue Enchantress and the concealed Life Crystal universe safe inside its pocket dimension. The final wide panel shows Jet on the cruiser's command deck with the Life Sword at his side, a small sun in one palm and a narrow Lucid Light beam in the other.
