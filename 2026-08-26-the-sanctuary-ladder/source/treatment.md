# Treatment - The Sanctuary Ladder

## Logline

After waking lucid inside the body of a black werewolf, Jet discovers that a floating sanctuary, a grounded city, and an impossible ladder form a literal map of his changing dream awareness.

## Central conflict

Jet wants to understand why spirit-hopping dreams place him inside other beings, but every step toward the ordinary city strips away his lucidity. Once grounded, he must survive an academy that shifts from social familiarity to beautiful danger without understanding its rules.

## Theme

Awareness is not a fixed identity or location. It can travel between bodies and worlds, but memory of one's inner power is what makes lucidity possible.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins with wonder and curiosity inside a peaceful living sanctuary. The sword's answer gives him a sense of lineage, but the ladder draws him into forgetfulness. The academy briefly restores ordinary social life before curdling into unease, isolation, and pursuit. In the stairwell, recalling Lucid Light reconnects him to himself. He ends with a calm, symbolic understanding of the sanctuary, city, ladder, and shifting bodies.

## Story structure

1. A black werewolf stands on cold pink liquid glass inside an organic blue-lit sanctuary.
2. The rainbow crystal sword identifies the werewolf as one of its holders across time.
3. An impossible ladder descends from the floating sanctuary to a distant city, grounding Jet into non-lucidity.
4. Jet becomes himself at an academy where people ignore a video, inspect his gadgets, and gather around action figures.
5. A false awakening and a lone blue-lit watcher lead into a beautiful but potentially hazardous insulation installation.
6. Red-aura artists chase Jet with utensils while ignoring another nearby man.
7. Jet reaches for Lucid Light in a stairwell, remembers that he is dreaming, and returns to the ladder as a fire elemental.
8. The sanctuary, city, ladder, werewolf, Jet, and fire elemental resolve into a map of movement between lucid and ordinary dreams.

## Ending

The final page visually separates the floating sanctuary from the city and shows the disconnected ladder carrying Jet's awareness between his three bodies. The rainbow sword and sparkling Lucid Light bind the identities together without collapsing them into one.
