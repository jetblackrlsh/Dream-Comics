# Page Script - The Portal-Blade Assassin

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, Silonis, the portal-blade assassin, and all recurring environments follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic full-page composition inside the magical study. Jet parries the portal-blade assassin with Silonis at the instant the enemy emerges from a shattered violet portal. Rainbow life-energy contours reveal the assassin's arrival before it completes. Through the portal, the dangerous portal-crystal world and a distant peaceful Empty Dimension appear as connected depths of one coherent scene.

Visible cover text only: “THE PORTAL-BLADE ASSASSIN” and “AUGUST 17, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - A Theory of Swords

A clear four-panel page. Jet writes at his magical study desk while studying Silonis. A symbolic vision contrasts Silonis and Savannah's Death Sword with a normal sword carrying a glowing spell inscription. Jet's notes resolve into a model of magic, crystal, and future technology working together.

Caption text:

- “This dream began in my magical study, where I was thinking about the most powerful swords in the Storyverse.”
- “A normal blade could carry Viva, Vita, Porta, or another spell and become genuinely magical.”
- “But Silonis was more than an inscription: living crystal, life magic, and future technology acted as one.”
- “That meant other legendary crystal-tech swords could exist—and portal magic suggested where to look.”

## Page 3 - The World That Opens Everywhere

A clear four-panel worldbuilding page. The first panel reveals the magnificent portal-crystal planet. Miners extract cyan-violet gems on industrial platforms. Heroes defend them as spontaneous portals release alien creatures and violent weather. The final panel shows the crystals powering cargo gates and personal teleporters across distant civilizations.

Caption text:

- “I imagined a planet made from crystals that naturally opened portals.”
- “Civilizations would mine those gems to build shipping gates, teleporters, and automated Porta technology.”
- “But every crystal could connect to anywhere, allowing any creature or catastrophe to appear without warning.”
- “The miners would need heroes beside them—because the most valuable world might also be the most dangerous.”

## Page 4 - The Knowledge Struck Back

A clear four-panel page returning to the study. Jet writes down the portal-sword theory. The assassin suddenly materializes and slashes past him, then vanishes. A narrow mirrored inset shows waking Jet coughing while dream-Jet feels the next strike. The final panel leaves Jet surrounded by empty portal afterimages.

Caption text:

- “As I wrote the theory down, an unseen adversary appeared, struck me, and vanished.”
- “Every slash inside the dream arrived with a coughing fit in my physical body.”
- “I could feel both realities at once, but the attacker moved too quickly for sight alone.”
- “Someone was using the very portal-crystal blade I had just discovered.”

## Page 5 - Life Finds the Arrival

A clear four-panel action page. Jet grips Silonis and closes his eyes as rainbow life-energy contours map the hidden assassin. A glowing silhouette forms before a portal opens. Jet parries the emerging crystal blade. The final panel shows several successful parries across staggered portal openings without duplicating extra characters.

Caption text:

- “I stopped trying to see the assassin and asked Silonis to sense their life energy.”
- “The Life Sword revealed where their living presence would reconnect with the room.”
- “Before each portal opened, I already knew where the next blade would appear.”
- “I did not become faster than the assassin. I became aware sooner.”

## Page 6 - The Clan Behind the Secret

A clear four-panel deduction-and-combat page. Portal blades collide in showers of rainbow and violet crystal light. Jet realizes the weapons may connect minds and ideas as well as places. A shadowy symbolic panel suggests a hidden clan monitoring forbidden discoveries. The final panel shows Jet holding firm while the assassin recoils.

Caption text:

- “Our blades collided again and again, yet the assassin disappeared after every failed strike.”
- “Then I understood: portal swords might connect ideas and minds, not only physical places.”
- “A hidden clan could sense anyone discovering their secret—and silence that person before the knowledge spread.”
- “Portal gems were not rare. Portal blades were rare because their guardians made understanding dangerous.”

## Page 7 - A Door They Could Reopen

A clear four-panel climax. Jet parries once more, casts Porta with his free hand, and opens a vast circular passage. The assassin tumbles safely into the serene Empty Dimension while still holding the portal blade. Jet watches the open doorway, fully aware that the assassin can return whenever they choose.

Caption text:

- “I answered their portal blade with Porta, opening a path to the Empty Dimension.”
- “One final parry redirected the assassin through it without killing them.”
- “Their own sword could reopen the path immediately. This dimension was not a locked prison.”
- “Even so, I believed they would choose not to come back.”

## Page 8 - Somewhere Worth Staying

A clear four-panel resolution. The assassin lands in the peaceful Empty Dimension and sees a world without judgment or pursuit. Their battle posture softens. They kneel beside still water and set the glowing portal blade on the ground instead of opening another doorway. The final wide panel shows Jet peacefully closing Porta as the assassin rests beneath warm stars.

Caption text:

- “The Empty Dimension offered solitude, freedom, and the right to exist without judgment.”
- “I could not remain there forever because both the waking world and the Storyverse still mattered to me.”
- “But the assassin knew only one world—and had spent that life hiding, hunting, and running.”
- “I gave them the power to return. Peace began when they finally found no reason to use it.”
