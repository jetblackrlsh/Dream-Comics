Dandelion Golem Showdown

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in a city. He combines his golden glittering energy tether with sparkling purple Lucid Light so he can swing between skyscrapers without depending on gravity or momentum. He remains afloat between arcs, pushes himself forward through the air, and performs as many flips as he wants without any fear of falling.

A purple spike-covered villain with freakishly long claws attacks Jet because they want his rainbow crystal Sword of Life. The villain stabs their claws into the street, then sends huge claw tips up through the ground beneath Jet's feet to pin him in place. Jet uses the Sword of Life to cut through the claws, frees himself, and collects several shattered purple claw fragments.

The villain boasts that the claw attack can continue all day. Jet answers that the villain should never have let him obtain the fragments. Jet uses Lucid Light to turn the claw shards into dandelions, blows their seeds at the villain, and transforms the seeds into dandelion golems. Every time the villain hits one golem, it divides into more. The multiplying dandelion army eventually overwhelms the villain and forces a surrender.

Jet ends the dream delighted by the freedom of combining swinging with flight and by the dandelion trick, which was inspired by the Monkey King's ability to create clones by blowing pieces of his hair.
