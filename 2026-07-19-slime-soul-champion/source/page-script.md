# Page Script — Slime Soul Champion

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions must remain clearly separated, readable, and nonredundant. Jet and every recurring design must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 — Cover

One dramatic layered composition. Center foreground: Jet in his exact black hoodie, visible Dream Anime shirt, full beard, long dreadlocks, striped pants, white sneakers, belt bag, Life Sword, and golden tether, flying with purple star-filled Lucid Light around one fist. Lower background: the enormous purified cobalt-blue half-orb slime guardian glows gold-rainbow within the ocean and curls its twelve tendrils protectively around tiny boats. Upper right: the thin athletic crimson-haired rival in her emerald wrestling-inspired costume charges with flaming ponytail, fists, and boots. Upper left: four cheerful Sky Snack Society heroes fly with metallic snack pouches and golden triangular chips. Visible cover text only: “SLIME SOUL CHAMPION” and “JULY 19, 2026”. No issue number.

## Page 2 — The Monster in the City

Four panels. Jet lies conscious but immobilized during sleep paralysis in a dark bedroom, then rises through sparkling purple Lucid Light. A wide aerial panel shows him alone, in his exact casual outfit, flying joyfully through the bright coastal superhero city. The giant translucent cobalt-blue half-orb slime monster erupts into the civic plaza with twelve tendrils striking empty cars and buildings while civilians flee safely. Final panel: Jet turns sharply and flies toward the monster, both hands bright with star-filled Lucid Light.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrived alone in a coastal city where flying felt as natural as walking.”
- “For a while, I explored the skyline like a superhero who had finally found the world built for him.”
- “Then a colossal blue slime monster surged into the city, smashing streets with twelve enormous tendrils.”
- “I flew toward it and chose the simplest plan I could imagine: dry the creature out before it could hurt anyone.”

## Page 3 — The Soul in the Vapor

Five panels. Jet fires narrow purple star-filled Lucid Light beams from his eyes into the monster. Only a small turquoise cloud evaporates while the giant body remains intact. The living vapor develops cyan eyes and wispy tendrils, rushes into Jet's open mouth, and makes him clutch his throat while flying. In a comedic but tasteful action panel, Jet braces midair and expels the blue vapor behind him as a forceful spectral puff from the seat of his black pants; no nudity, anatomy, bodily waste, or gross detail. The expelled vapor coughs and sputters as if alive. Final wide panel: Jet stares between the animated gas and the completely motionless slime body below, realizing the truth.

Caption text:

- “My Lucid Light evaporated only a little slime, and the creature sent that vapor straight into my mouth to suffocate me.”
- “If it could enter my body as gas, I reasoned that I could force it to leave my body the same way.”
- “I expelled it as a powerful fart, and the blue cloud emerged coughing as though it were alive.”
- “Below us, the monster's huge body had stopped moving completely.”
- “The vapor was not a piece of the monster. I had accidentally separated the creature's soul from its body.”

## Page 4 — A Guardian Reborn

Five panels. Jet summons the brilliant rainbow crystal Life Sword and gently pierces the turquoise gaseous soul; ugly dark wisps burn away while warm rainbow-gold light fills it. He descends and places the Life Sword into the still half-orb body without gore. The purified soul streams down the blade, and the giant monster springs awake with soft friendly eyes and inner golden-rainbow glow. Jet lifts the enormous creature inside a cradle of sparkling purple Lucid Light and carries it safely over the city to the ocean. Final panoramic panel: the guardian spreads much larger through the sea, uses its twelve tendrils to catch fish, and protectively surrounds small boats while citizens cheer from shore.

Caption text:

- “I summoned my rainbow crystal Life Sword and used it to purify the gaseous soul instead of destroying it.”
- “When I placed the sword into the motionless body, the cleansed soul followed its light home.”
- “The monster sprang back to life, but the evil driving its attacks was gone.”
- “I carried the enormous creature out of the city with Lucid Light and lowered it into the ocean.”
- “Fed by the sea and guided by its purified heart, the former monster grew into a good-hearted guardian of the coast.”

## Page 5 — Challenge to the Number-One Hero

Five panels. A giant public screen displays a simple star-crown symbol above Jet's recognizable silhouette while cheering citizens below indicate that saving the city has made him number one; avoid extra readable screen text. The dark-crimson-haired rival rockets into him with a surprise emerald-boot kick. She follows with precise airborne boxer punches and kicks while short visual flashes show that she protects civilians elsewhere, clarifying she is a rival rather than a villain. Jet crosses his arms under her blows, then coats his recognizable body and clothes in seamless reflective chrome armor using purple starry Lucid Light. Final panel: her wrapped fist strikes his metal jaw with little effect while Jet's heavy chrome fist drives her backward through the open sky.

Caption text:

- “Saving the city established me as the strongest superhero in the world, and the public began calling me number one.”
- “A crimson-haired flying hero attacked without warning, then explained that she was neither evil nor an enemy of innocent people.”
- “She had been the greatest hero before my victory, and she needed a direct fight to decide which of us truly deserved the title.”
- “Remembering the robot boxing I had practiced while awake, I transformed my skin and clothing into protective metal.”
- “Her blows barely moved my armored body, while every counterpunch landed with the weight of a steel machine.”

## Page 6 — The Liquid-Metal Prison

Six dynamic panels. The rival's long crimson ponytail ignites into red-orange fire, then her burning fists and boots batter Jet's chrome armor. His metal skin melts, followed by his entire body and clothing, until he flies as a conscious reflective silver liquid retaining a bearded face and dreadlock-like streams. He surges around the rival's torso and limbs like a smooth full-body shell. Two small rectangular dialogue caption boxes appear over the same panel: “You've become armor for me.” and “Yeah—if I give you the freedom to move.” Jet instantly solidifies as a seamless metal restraint pinning her arms and legs; a smooth band covers her mouth only long enough for her to faint, with no injury, gore, or death. Final panel: Jet releases her safely onto a rooftop, reforms into his exact intact human outfit with Lucid Light, and flies away as she rests unconscious but breathing.

Caption text:

- “She ignited her crimson hair and attacked with enough heat to melt my armor, my clothes, and finally my entire body.”
- “I remained conscious as living liquid metal, and the slime monster's transformation showed me how to use this new form.”
- “I wrapped around her body, hardened into a prison, and restrained her until she briefly lost consciousness.”
- “Then I released her unharmed, rebuilt my human form with Lucid Light, and remained the world's number-one hero.”

## Page 7 — The Strangest Superhero Team

Five panels. Jet flies peacefully above the restored city in his exact normal outfit. Four colorful heroes from the Sky Snack Society glide alongside him without wings, capes, or engines, happily comparing golden triangular chips from metallic snack pouches. They demonstrate rating cards with star symbols and dramatically inspect texture, salt crystals, and seasoning in midair like serious experts. Jet looks baffled while the others explain their purpose through friendly gestures. Final panel: the lavender-curled leader offers Jet a single golden triangular chip sparkling with salt, honey glaze, and pepper flecks.

Caption text:

- “After the duel, I met four more flying superheroes and expected another battle, rescue, or world-saving mission.”
- “Instead, they explained that their team flew around tasting and reviewing different kinds of potato chips.”
- “They had the power to cross the sky, yet devoted their days to comparing crunch, seasoning, and flavor.”
- “I thought this was one of the strangest uses of superpowers I had ever encountered.”
- “Then their leader handed me one golden triangular chip and invited me to judge their mission for myself.”

## Page 8 — The Flavor Worth Flying For

Five panels. Extreme close-up of the golden tortilla-shaped potato chip, showing coarse salt crystals, glossy honey sheen, and tiny pepper flakes. Jet bites it and his warm brown eyes widen as abstract golden honey ribbons, white salt sparkles, and red-black spice stars burst around him. A sequence shows the chip reminding him visually of creamy honey-roasted peanut butter while clearly remaining saltier and spicier. The Sky Snack Society laughs warmly at his astonished delight and shares its metallic pouches with him. Final large sunset panel: Jet and all four heroes fly together above the peaceful coastal city, eating the chips; far below, the purified blue slime guardian protects boats in the glowing ocean.

Caption text:

- “The chip had the shape and crunch of a tortilla chip, with enough salt to make every edge sparkle.”
- “Sweet honey arrived next, followed by a small peppery kick that made the flavor even brighter.”
- “It reminded me of honey-roasted peanut butter, although this was saltier, crunchier, and slightly spicier.”
- “After one bite, I understood exactly why these superheroes had made potato chips their great adventure.”
- “I spent the rest of the dream flying with them, sharing the best chips I had ever tasted above an ocean guarded by the hero I had saved.”
