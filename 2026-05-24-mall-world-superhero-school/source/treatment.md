# Treatment

## Title

05/24/2026: Mall World Superhero School

## Logline

Jet returns to Mall World armed with the remembered power of a plant hero, overcomes the odor trap that defeated him before, exposes the noseless mall-security knights, and crosses into a dreamlike superhero school where new allies and rivals blur into mystery.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to push deeper into Mall World without being turned back by its hidden defenses. The obstacle is not brute force alone: the mall protects itself with an odor its noseless security knights cannot smell, forcing Jet to transform remembered inspiration into an active countermeasure.

## Theme

Lucidity is continuity. A detail saved from one dream can become the tool that opens the next.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins wary but more prepared than before. Remembering the plant hero gives him confidence and a practical answer to the stench. When he sees through the knights' trap, his confidence becomes investigation. The superhero school turns victory into wonder, but the dream's blurred faces leave him with a quieter uncertainty about who he met and what kind of shared space Mall World might be.

## Ending

Jet accepts that the school, the students, and the true nature of Mall World remain unresolved. He wakes with the question intact: were the fellow students other dreamers or dream characters?
