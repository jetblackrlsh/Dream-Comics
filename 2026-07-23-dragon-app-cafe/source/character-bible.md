# Character Bible

## Jet — human form

- Role: lucid dreamer, protagonist, designer, and narrator.
- Appearance: adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath his hood.
- Outfit: black hoodie worn open enough to reveal a “Dream Anime” graphic tee, black athletic pants with one white side stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Signature tools: a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword worn at his side; an energy tether resembling a rope of golden glittering light; sparkling purple Lucid Light that shines like a star-filled night sky.
- Posture and expressions: thoughtful and animated while presenting the business; confused and irritated when the transformation begins.
- Continuity rule: pages 1 and 4 show dragon-form Jet. Pages 2 and 3 show human-form Jet. Human Jet's skin, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, visible shirt, pants stripe, sneakers, and belt bag must remain stable.

## Jet — dragon form

- Role: the same Jet after the woman transforms him; eventual proprietor and living symbol of the café.
- Appearance: tall humanoid dragon with rich purple scales, a dragon-like purple face and snout, warm brown eyes, two short swept-back purple horns, large orange membrane wings, a long purple tail, clawed purple hands, and clawed feet.
- Identity anchors: full dark-brown beard remains around his dragon jaw; long dark-brown dreadlocks remain beneath his black hood; black hoodie, visible “Dream Anime” graphic tee, black pants with one white stripe, black cross-body belt bag, and altered white sneaker-like footwear remain recognizable around the transformed body.
- Signature tools: rainbow crystal sword remains sheathed at his side and golden glittering tether remains coiled at his belt, but neither is used.
- Posture and expressions: initially annoyed and incredulous; then energetic and inventive; finally confident, welcoming, and amused.
- Continuity rule: from the final panel of page 4 through page 8, Jet remains fully transformed. Do not revert him to human form and do not change the purple scales or orange wings.

## The café strategist

- Role: the woman waiting for Jet, his practical collaborator, secret cause of his transformation, and architect of the marketing strategy.
- Appearance: adult woman with medium-brown skin, oval face, intelligent amber eyes, and a voluminous shoulder-length halo of dark auburn curls.
- Outfit: teal blazer with rolled sleeves over a cream shirt, fitted charcoal trousers, teal ankle boots, a slim gold bracelet, and a translucent turquoise tablet.
- Color motif: teal, cream, turquoise, and gold.
- Posture and expressions: attentive and professional during Jet's pitch; quietly pleased during the transformation; confident but not malicious when confessing; proud at the opening.
- Continuity rule: maintain the same auburn curls, teal blazer, cream shirt, charcoal trousers, boots, bracelet, and turquoise tablet on every appearance.

## The Dragon App Café

- Initial state: a fairly empty modern commercial building with pale walls, a polished concrete floor, tall front windows, exposed ceiling beams, and only a small table, two chairs, and the woman's translucent tablet.
- Completed state: a warm futuristic public café with cyan-and-violet neon accents, rows of powerful computers, comfortable private coding booths, a large central luminous app-library wall, a popularity display with simple star and bar icons rather than illegible project names, a candy counter of individually wrapped jewel-colored chews, and transparent reinforced training-room doors.
- Design motif: technology and fantasy deliberately interwoven—clean interfaces, glowing circuitry, dragon-wing arches, prismatic candy light, and safe enchanted chambers.

## AI coding stations and communal app library

- Coding stations: sleek black computers with ultrawide screens showing clear abstract code blocks, app mockups, and friendly luminous agent avatars; never show real company logos or dense unreadable text.
- Communal library: a large cyan-violet wall of colorful app tiles contributed by visitors. Use distinctive pictograms and visible popularity bars or star icons, not gibberish titles.
- Visitor behavior: customers arrive with sketches or idea cards, collaborate with the agent interface, test their finished app, and add a glowing tile to the library.

## Power candies

- Appearance: small square chewy candies resembling jewel-toned fruit chews, individually wrapped or arranged in clean trays. Colors include red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, and pink.
- Powers: each color grants one temporary ability. Visual demonstrations may include harmless firelight, levitation, stretchy limbs, super speed, crystal armor, or plant growth.
- Safety rule: powers are temporary and may be activated only inside a private training room.

## Private power-training rooms

- Appearance: individual reinforced chambers behind transparent cyan force-glass, each containing padded walls, an empty floor, and inanimate practice targets such as foam blocks, hovering rings, crash-test dummies, or metal plates.
- Safety systems: color-coded power timer, automatic shutdown glow, observation window, and sealed door.
- Story rule: customers never fight one another. All powers are directed only toward inanimate targets in a controlled environment.
