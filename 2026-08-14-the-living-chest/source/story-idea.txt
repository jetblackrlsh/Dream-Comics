# Story Idea - The Living Chest

Jet creates a logically grounded dungeon whose monsters and gold came from a buried monster zoo. Sent by the zoo's insurance company to recover its lost earnings, he brings the treasure chest to life, discovers a dragon is holding it, and races an approaching mole monster. Jet retrieves the gold by fusing the living chest into his own body, escapes with Lucid Light, and seals the entrance behind a looping portal.
