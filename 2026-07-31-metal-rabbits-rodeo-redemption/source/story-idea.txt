The Metal Rabbit's Rodeo Redemption

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet encounters a grimy orange-metal skeletal rabbit in an empty black void. The rabbit openly wants to frighten him and reshapes the void with purple fog, zombie clowns carrying chainsaws, a many-armed spider that steals Silonis, and nearly one hundred mole men that exploit the open bottom of Jet's force field. Jet calmly survives every escalation through Silonis's healing and independent flight, a rainbow crystal barrier, and a powerful Lucid Light shock wave.

The rabbit grows into a giant and insists that Jet can never escape. Jet answers that he does not need to escape. Instead of attacking, he creates a gigantic metal carrot. The gift instantly transforms the rabbit's attitude. Jet tells the failed nightmare-maker that its strange southern charm would be better suited to becoming a rodeo cowboy. Hope replaces menace, the two exchange a cheerful “Howdy,” and Jet flies out of the dark void toward the Storyverse's bubble universes.
