# Page Script - The Crystal-Hungry Host

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, long enough to make every development obvious, and nonredundant. Jet, Silonis, the dragon hosts, Leon, Lucid Light, and the Storyverse must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

One dramatic vertical composition in the neon host selection hall. Jet grips radiant Silonis in the foreground. The elegant obsidian-chrome robotic dragon host leans toward the rainbow blade, its red, green, and blue chest stones glowing with hunger. Behind them, the crimson, cobalt, and jade dragon hosts watch from illuminated lecterns while molten-gold cups and neon light frame the conflict. Visible cover text only: "THE CRYSTAL-HUNGRY HOST" and "AUGUST 6, 2026." No issue number.

## Page 2 - The Choice

Six panels. Jet enters the cosmic nightclub selection hall. The three organic dragon hosts stand behind lecterns with open magical tomes. Jet studies their different colors and powers. He discovers the elegant dragon-shaped android and reads that its personality can be customized. Jet points decisively to the robotic host while the other dragons react with immediate frustration.

Caption text:

- "The dream began at a nightclub where every visitor had to choose a humanoid dragon host."
- "Each dragon had a different color, power, personality, and magical tome explaining who they were."
- "Among the organic hosts, I discovered one dragon that was actually an artificial intelligence inside a robotic body."
- "Its tome promised that its personality could be customized for any guest."
- "I immediately thought the adaptable robot was clearly the best choice."
- "The other dragon hosts did not share my excitement."

## Page 3 - The Cost of Competition

Six panels. The crimson, cobalt, and jade hosts confront Jet with weary expressions. Smaller panels show the robot working through day and night while organic hosts sleep, gathering endless club stories and greeting many customers. Jet confidently explains why those qualities impressed him. The dragons then reveal empty gold bowls and the mood changes as Jet understands their hunger.

Caption text:

- "The other hosts complained that they constantly had to compete against a machine everyone considered superior."
- "They had real lives and personal stories, but the customizable robot remained at the nightclub all day and all night."
- "I argued that constant work meant the robot had probably witnessed more club stories than anyone."
- "Its endless experience and adjustable personality made it extremely difficult to beat."
- "Then the dragons explained what losing another customer meant to them."
- "If I chose the robot, the workers already struggling for attention might go hungry."

## Page 4 - Gold for Every Dragon

Six panels. Jet remembers a recent dragon and a reservoir of molten gold in a clear flashback. He raises a hand filled with sparkling purple Lucid Light and calls Leon. The ghostly green saucer appears above the nightclub. Leon lowers heat-resistant cups of glowing molten gold. Jet gives one cup to each organic host, and their worried expressions turn to grateful relief before Jet enters the club beside the robot.

Caption text:

- "I felt bad, because I had recently learned that dragons consumed gold as food."
- "During an earlier adventure, I had taken a large supply of molten gold from another dragon."
- "Using Lucid Light, I called Leon, my ghostly green flying saucer, and asked for that gold."
- "Leon lowered several cups filled with the bright molten meal."
- "I gave one cup to every dragon host I had not selected so that none of them would go hungry."
- "With their immediate need solved, I entered the nightclub beside the robotic host."

## Page 5 - The Robot's Hunger

Six panels. Jet and the robot relax in a curved VIP booth. A close panel reveals the red, green, and blue stones embedded in the robot's chest. The robot announces a low charge as its cyan eyes dim. Its sensors sweep across the lounge and lock onto Silonis. The final large panel shows the robot lunging toward the rainbow crystal sword while Jet recoils.

Caption text:

- "Inside the club, my robotic host brought me to a private VIP lounge."
- "When we sat down, I noticed three magical stones embedded in its chest: red, green, and blue."
- "The robot announced that its power was low and that those magical stones normally kept it charged."
- "Its internal sensors searched the room for another source of energy."
- "The sensors identified Silonis, my rainbow crystal sword, as something the robot could consume."
- "Without warning, the starving machine lunged toward the sword."

## Page 6 - An Illusion Cannot Fool a Sensor

Six panels. Jet pulls Silonis away. Purple star-filled Lucid Light wraps the rainbow blade until it appears to be ordinary gray metal. The robot pauses and scans it with cyan beams. A technical sensor view reveals the rainbow magical crystal beneath the illusion. The robot pursues Jet around the VIP booth, reaching with articulated claws while Jet realizes deception will not work.

Caption text:

- "I pulled Silonis away before the robot could bite into one of the Storyverse's most powerful artifacts."
- "With Lucid Light, I cloaked the rainbow blade and made it appear to be ordinary metal."
- "The disguise would have fooled normal eyes, but the robot did not rely on normal sight."
- "Its internal sensors looked through the illusion and detected the magical crystal underneath."
- "Still desperate to recharge, the machine continued pursuing Silonis across the lounge."
- "Once I knew I could not trick its sensors, I chose a more direct solution."

## Page 7 - Three Stones Removed

Six panels. Jet plants one hand toward the floor and activates geomancy as stone-like energy lines spread. The red chest stone pulls free and floats toward him, followed by green and blue. Each removal dims part of the robot. With the third stone gone, the dragon android freezes mid-lunge and collapses harmlessly into the booth. Jet catches the three glowing stones while keeping Silonis safely behind him.

Caption text:

- "Geomancy was one of the first powers I learned during my early Storyverse adventures."
- "I used that control over stone to pull the red crystal from the robot's chest."
- "The green crystal followed, and more of the machine's lights went dark."
- "When I removed the blue crystal, every remaining circuit faded at once."
- "Taking all three stones was like removing the batteries from a machine."
- "The robotic dragon powered down immediately, and Silonis was safe."

## Page 8 - Hunger Was the Enemy

Six panels resolving into one large final vista. Jet returns to the host selection hall carrying the three stones and explains the shutdown. The crimson, cobalt, and jade hosts accept the stones with relief. Jet looks back through the VIP doorway at the silent robot with genuine regret. Purple Lucid Light surrounds Jet as he rises away. The final vista shows Jet and Silonis flying among Storyverse bubble universes while the neon nightclub recedes behind them.

Caption text:

- "I carried the three power stones back to the organic dragon hosts and explained what had happened."
- "They were relieved that the tireless robot would no longer take nearly every customer."
- "Because I had no better place for the stones, I gave them to the dragons."
- "I still felt sorry for the robot, because it had seemed genuinely cool before its power ran low."
- "It had not attacked from simple malice; unlike dragons that ate gold, the machine consumed magical crystal."
- "With every host's hunger still on my mind, I flew away into the Storyverse and the dream ended."
