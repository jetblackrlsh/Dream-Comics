# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword across his back at the beginning. The sword is a colorful futuristic crystal blade with translucent red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet facets, a dark silver grip, and a radiant rainbow-white aura when activated.
- Carries the Golden Energy Tether at his belt. It is a flexible rope made from golden glittering light with a bright lasso loop when thrown. It has no mechanical cable, grappling hook, chain links, or solid fiber.
- Uses sparkling purple Lucid Light filled with tiny stars to manifest orange-violet pyrokinetic flames. Keep the purple star-field aura visible around Jet's hands so the magic is distinguishable from ordinary fire.
- When injured, thorn cuts appear on his hoodie, forearms, and face without excessive gore. After the Life Sword heals him, the skin closes and only minor tears or singe marks remain in his clothing.
- Expression range: lucid focus, suspicion, patient logical certainty, anger at manipulation, surprise when fire fails, pain, urgent resourcefulness, renewed control, compassionate resolve, sober victory.
- Jet never wears a crown, cape, mask, helmet, conventional superhero costume, school uniform, or unrelated armor.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, face, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, shirt, pants, sneakers, belt bag, sword, and tether on every page.

## King of Nightmares

- A towering twelve-foot humanoid formed entirely from tightly twisted burnt-black vines, thorny branches, and charcoal bark. He has no flesh, skin, clothes, armor, hair, or human facial features.
- His head is a dark knot of vines beneath a crown made from curling black branches. Two narrow glowing red eyes stare from a hollow shadow within the knot.
- Long arms end in branch-like fingers and mobile thorn tendrils. His silhouette is regal, skeletal, broad-shouldered, and predatory.
- The vine body is already burnt and fire-blackened before Jet attacks. Orange flames can cling to it without reducing it to ash.
- His red eyes exactly echo the red light inside the nightmare planet's cubes.
- Expression and posture range: false solemnity, theatrical concern, accusatory authority, feigned agony, sudden predatory triumph, mocking cruelty, shock at purification, fragmentation.
- Never depict him as a horned demon, robed wizard, armored knight, leafy tree person, human king, or generic skeleton. His curling vine crown and burnt thorn construction must remain unmistakable.

## Rainbow Crystal Sword / Life Sword

- One consistent two-handed-length futuristic crystal sword carried by Jet.
- Broad translucent faceted blade with saturated rainbow color running from red through violet, dark silver geometric hilt, and a prismatic edge.
- When used for force fields, it produces a transparent spherical rainbow-white barrier with golden and emerald life-energy veins.
- When used for healing or purification, its light is warm rainbow-white with strong gold and green rather than destructive red fire.
- The sword is an extraordinarily powerful Storyverse artifact, but it never acts independently. Jet accesses it remotely only through the tether's bond.

## Golden Energy Tether

- A supple rope of bright golden glittering energy summoned from Jet's hand or belt.
- When thrown, it forms a clean lasso that wraps securely around the Life Sword's hilt or blade base.
- Its mystical bond is visualized as three linked pulses: Jet's hand, the glowing tether, and the sword all sharing the same golden rhythm.
- It represents connection rather than restraint. Avoid making it look like a weapon that cuts, burns, or electrocutes.

## Nightmare planet

- A dark spherical world floating among the Storyverse's cosmic bubble universes.
- Its surface is constructed from countless individual glossy black mirrored cubes with crisp seams. Every cube glows from deep inside with eerie crimson light.
- The world feels artificial, oppressive, and menacing but is not a machine or a city. No buildings, continents, oceans, or ordinary terrain should appear before purification.
- Its crimson illumination matches the King of Nightmares' eyes.

## Purified dream world

- The same planetary volume after purification, now formed from brilliant white cloud terraces, soft floating islands of vapor, and warm golden illumination.
- The internal red glow is entirely gone. Rainbow-white life energy flows across the former cube seams until the last cubes dissolve.
- Its atmosphere is hopeful, breathable, and radiant without resembling a literal heaven, religious iconography, or a modern city.

## Nightmare beings and transformed dreams

- Before purification, the inhabitants appear as diverse shadowy story-creatures trapped inside or emerging from the mirrored cubes: distorted animals, crawling masks, winged shadows, long-limbed silhouettes, and abstract fear-shapes with red highlights.
- Keep them frightening but not gory. They are a population being manipulated and transformed, not an anonymous pile of corpses.
- Each nightmare becomes a luminous dream counterpart with a recognizable visual echo: shadow wolf to radiant guardian wolf, crawling mask to smiling lantern spirit, winged terror to brilliant dream-bird, abstract fear-shape to playful star creature.
- Transformed dreams use white, gold, cyan, violet, and gentle rainbow accents. They are varied in size and form and move with gratitude, freedom, and determined solidarity.
- On the final page they fight collectively by pulling apart the king's vines, not by attacking Jet or destroying the world.

## Storyverse cosmic void

- Deep indigo-black space filled with colorful cosmic bubble universes at many distances. Each bubble contains a faint suggestion of a different genre-world.
- The nightmare planet and later dream world remain the clear central location.
- Avoid conventional outer-space planets, realistic starscapes, spacecraft, or inserting Jet's absent crew.

## Established Storyverse crew continuity

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's Storyverse team. None appears physically in this comic. Do not insert them into the cosmic void, planet, reflections, force fields, background silhouettes, cover, or final battle.
