# Page Script - The Lucid Chain Alliance

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, the princess, the knight, the quilted dragon, the orcs, and every Lucid Light construct follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic full-page composition in the forest battle. Jet stands in the heroic foreground controlling a massive sparkling-purple Lucid Light chain. The chain loops upward to the silver-armored elven knight, who has long brown hair around a clearly bald crown and swings dynamically with his sword extended. Green orcs recoil from purple shockwaves. Behind them, the sapphire-eyed elven princess conjures needles and thread beside the giant blue-and-white quilted dragon with one repaired fabric wing. A distant castle rises beyond the trees.

Visible cover text only: “THE LUCID CHAIN ALLIANCE” and “AUGUST 20, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - The Quilted Dragon

A clear four-panel establishing page. The dragon flies above an immense forest toward the castle. Close panels establish the sewn quilt texture and blue-and-white fine-china pattern. Jet studies the princess's literal sapphire eyes while the stern knight watches.

Caption text:

- “Above an endless forest, I rode a living dragon sewn from quilted fabric and patterned like blue-and-white china.”
- “An elven princess with literal sapphire eyes guided us toward a distant castle.”
- “Her silver-armored knight watched me with growing suspicion.”

## Page 3 - The Stolen Ring

A clear four-panel confrontation-and-prank page. Jet compliments the princess's jewel eyes and she laughs. The jealous knight advances; Jet steps between them and summons a Lucid Light ring. The knight steals it. In the final panel, the ring moves onto his back, expands into a translucent purple parachute, and launches him safely away from the dragon.

Caption text:

- “Her jeweled eyes were stunning enough that I said so.”
- “She laughed. Her boyfriend—apparently soon to be her ex—did not.”
- “I stepped between them and summoned a ring of Lucid Light. The knight stole it from my hand.”
- “So I moved it to his back, turned it into a parachute, and launched him safely overboard.”

## Page 4 - The Torn Wing

A clear four-panel consequence page. The worried princess turns the dragon toward the forest. An orc's enchanted axe slices through one quilted wing. She crash-lands in a clearing and conjures silver needles with golden-blue thread to begin repairing the fabric. Jet raises a new sister ring and promises to retrieve the knight.

Caption text:

- “The princess did not laugh. The forest below was swarming with hostile orcs.”
- “An enchanted strike tore the quilted dragon's wing and forced us into a clearing.”
- “While she conjured needles and thread to repair it, I promised to retrieve the knight.”
- “His ring had a sister. I expected mine to pull him back to us.”

## Page 5 - Pulled Backward

A clear four-panel motion page. Jet activates the sister ring. Instead of pulling the knight toward the clearing, it yanks Jet backward at tremendous speed through the trees. He narrowly passes trunks and branches before crashing into the back of a green orc. The final panel reveals the knight trapped at the center of a complete circle of armed orcs.

Caption text:

- “The paired rings worked perfectly—in reverse.”
- “Instead of pulling the knight to me, mine yanked me through the trees toward him.”
- “I struck the outer wall of an orc circle and found the knight trapped at its center.”

## Page 6 - The Bond Made Visible

A clear four-panel defense-and-discovery page. Orcs turn to attack Jet. He raises his ring and manifests the bond between the sister rings as a massive cosmic-purple chain. Axes and clubs strike the chain, which blocks them and releases powerful purple shockwaves. Jet grips the chain and notices the knight's sword at the other end.

Caption text:

- “When the orcs turned on me, I made the bond between our rings visible.”
- “A massive chain of sparkling Lucid Light caught every blow.”
- “Each impact sent a shockwave through the attackers.”
- “Then I realized the knight at the other end was carrying a sword.”

## Page 7 - The Human Flail

A clear four-panel cooperative-action page. Jet plants his feet and swings the Lucid Light chain in a broad circle. The attached knight understands and willingly extends his sword, cutting weapons and knocking orcs away with every pass. Their expressions shift from surprise to shared exhilaration. Jet pulls hard on the glowing chain in the final panel.

Caption text:

- “I swung the knight around the circle like a living flail.”
- “He understood immediately and turned every pass into a sword strike.”
- “Our argument vanished inside the rhythm of a ridiculous, spectacular team attack.”
- “For the finish, I pulled until the chain broke.”

## Page 8 - The Alliance

A clear four-panel resolution page ending in a large final panel. The breaking chain releases the knight into a controlled human-Beyblade spin that knocks away the last orcs. He lands safely and regards Jet with new respect. Jet and the knight return side by side to the clearing. The princess waits beside the completely repaired quilted dragon, whose once-torn wing now bears beautiful fresh stitches.

Caption text:

- “The released force sent him spinning through the remaining orcs like a human Beyblade.”
- “When the last enemy fell, the knight looked at me as an ally instead of a rival.”
- “We returned to the clearing together, where the princess had sewn the dragon whole.”
- “Beside the repaired quilted dragon, our strange new alliance completed the dream.”
