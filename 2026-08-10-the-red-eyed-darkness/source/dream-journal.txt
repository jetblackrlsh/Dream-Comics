Dream Journal Reconstruction

I began the dream flying through a city. Flying through cities like a superhero is fairly standard for many of my dreams. I often patrol the streets from above, looking for villains, monsters, or anyone who might need my protection.

As I flew through the city, I noticed a black water tower with what appeared to be a giant black eyeball on it. The sight was strange enough to catch my attention, so I turned around and flew back toward it. As I approached, I had the impression that the water tower was speaking to me telepathically.

The water tower grew dramatically larger until it towered over me. Its enormous eye changed from black to red, and the entire area around me began to darken and turn black. The spreading darkness seemed to close in from every direction as the water tower asked me whether I was scared.

Then the darkness reached for Silonis, my Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life and the most powerful artifact in the Storyverse. I drew Silonis and slashed at the darkness with the Life Sword. The strike wounded it. Wherever the blade's power touched the darkness, the blackness began turning white.

“What have you done to me?” the darkness demanded.

“I'm not scared of you,” I replied.

One of the primary properties of the Life Sword is its ability to purify evil. As Silonis purified the darkness, the corruption vanished from the surrounding environment and from the water tower itself. The darkness and the tower not only turned white; they also began to crack and shatter.

“If you're pure evil, then purification will likely just destroy you,” I told it.

With the entity breaking apart behind me, I flew away.

As I continued flying, rain began to fall. Wanting to get out of the rain, I traveled beyond the city limits and entered a forested area. There, I discovered a wooden trapdoor in the ground. I opened it and descended into an underground home.

The home contained a crafting table, a bed, several chests, and other furnishings. It reminded me of an old house I had once built and lived in during a game of Minecraft. I found it fascinating to experience nostalgia for a home inside a dream—especially a home that had originally existed inside a video game. I had experienced dreams that evoked memories of childhood homes or places where I had lived when I was younger, but I was not sure whether I had ever before felt this kind of dream nostalgia for a virtual home.

I explored the underground house and realized that it was not an exact recreation of my Minecraft home. It included objects such as a refrigerator, and I did not think refrigerators even existed in Minecraft. Even with those differences, the place still carried the familiar feeling of that old game home.

One of the doors in the house led into a mine tunnel. When I opened it, I saw dirt and berry bushes growing within the passage. I remembered the berry bushes from Minecraft and how useful they had been. They required very little care, and I could collect and eat many of the berries for food. The danger was that I could not simply walk through the bushes because their thorns would hurt me.

I picked some berries from the bushes and returned to the main room with the crafting table. Once there, I began wondering how many different dishes I could create from them. I spent some time cooking and experimenting. I made berry juice, berry jam, berry paste, and other variations, trying to imagine all the different ways I could combine and prepare the berries as food.

Eventually, the seeping darkness returned. It behaved like a psychic symbiote, creeping down from above and entering the underground home. Realizing that I needed to escape, I opened the door to the mine shaft and ran inside.

For a moment, I considered traveling deeper through the mines to get away. Then I remembered that, although mine tunnels were often lined with torches, monsters could still form in the shadows between them. Fleeing into the mine suddenly seemed like a terrible idea. I would probably only be escaping from one monster by running directly toward many more.

I decided that I did not need to follow the mine at all. Instead, I flew upward and shaped my Lucid Light into something like a drill. Using it, I bored straight through the ceiling of the mine, continued upward through the earth, and broke through to the surface. From there, I flew high into the sky, passed out into the Storyverse, and escaped.
