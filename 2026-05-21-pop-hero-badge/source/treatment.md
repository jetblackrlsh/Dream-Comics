# Treatment

## Title

05/21/2026: Pop and the Hero Badge

## Logline

After sleep paralysis opens another lucid Storyverse door, Jet meets Pop, a reality-bending purple imp whose cartoon power turns a cratered purple planet into a superhero world where Jet must survive bureaucracy by wearing Pop as a living Hero Badge.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to understand what Pop really is, but Pop wants immediate adventure. Their mismatch pulls Jet into a superhero planet with strict hero laws, where his lack of a Hero Badge turns curiosity into an arrest threat.

## Theme

Lucidity is not always control. Sometimes the dream has its own impossible character, and the only way forward is to accept the joke before it becomes the rule.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alert and investigative after inducing lucidity, becomes unsettled by Pop's impossible hyper-real presence, gets trapped in a superhero system he does not understand, then narrowly adapts when Pop becomes the badge that lets him belong.

## Ending

The heroes begin recruiting Jet and asking about his powers. Jet tries to explain lucid dreaming, but the Hero Station fades as the 4:30 AM alarm pulls him back to waking life, leaving Pop's cosmic mystery unresolved.
