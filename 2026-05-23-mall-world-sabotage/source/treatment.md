# Treatment

## Title

05/23/2026: Mall World Sabotage

## Logline

Jet enters the Storyverse intending to explore the mysterious shared dream space known as Mall World, but a strange armored woman drags him into the mountains and tries to prove that his routines define him.

## Central Conflict

Jet wants to claim agency over his lucid dream by reaching Mall World on his own terms. The unfamiliar knight exists to disrupt that plan, pulling him away from the mall, attacking his focus, and forcing him to defend the memory of what the dream gives him afterward.

## Theme

Lucidity is not only control; it is the stubborn choice to keep hold of meaning when the dream tries to scatter it.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins determined and curious, ready to explore Mall World. The knight's intrusion turns that curiosity into resistance. The stench inside the mall breaks his focus, and the blackout leaves him vulnerable. In the final vision sequence, he stops fighting the whole dream at once and chooses one image to preserve: a plant-and-vine hero who may become an ally for future visits.

## Ending

As Jet is swept back toward waking life, he holds onto the plant hero's image with both hands. The knight remains an unanswered mystery, Mall World remains unexplored, but Jet returns with one new defender in memory and a sharper suspicion that the shared dream space may have rules he has not yet learned.
