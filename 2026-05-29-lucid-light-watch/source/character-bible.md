# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. He can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he is alone, tactical, and improvising under pressure.

## Lucid Light Watch

A transformation watch created from Lucid Light. It is a sleek futuristic wrist device made of purple starry energy, rainbow crystal facets, and a glowing circular dial. The dial projects tiny holographic icons of Jet's crew forms.

Continuity notes: The watch is the central new tool. It should glow with sparkling purple Lucid Light whenever Jet selects a form. It allows Jet to become Lucid Light variants of his crew, abbreviated as LLV forms.

## LLV-Skelebot

Jet transformed into a Lucid Light variant of Skelebot: a humanoid speedster robot body built from white bone-like robotic limbs, chrome joints, and purple starry Lucid Light seams. Jet's dreadlocks and warm brown eyes can appear as ghostly Lucid Light echoes inside or around the form to maintain identity.

Continuity notes: The form is lean, fast, and aerodynamic. Show speed trails, afterimages, and purple starry energy. Avoid making it a horror skeleton; it is a heroic robotic speedster.

## LLV-Tecton

Jet transformed into a Lucid Light variant of Tecton: a massive green-skinned humanoid cat ogre barbarian form with powerful muscles, feline ears and features, a brown loin cloth, a silver baldric, and glowing purple Lucid Light lines. The form can summon and telekinetically control cybernetic weapons.

Continuity notes: The key visual is the contrast between ogre strength and floating cyber axes. Keep the cyber axes metallic, futuristic, and arranged like a prison cage around the beast.

## LLV-Leon

Jet transformed into a Lucid Light variant of Leon: a ghostly flying saucer form with an ethereal green glow, translucent hull, and purple Lucid Light starfield inside the saucer. Jet's presence can be suggested through the rainbow crystal sword and golden tether emerging from the saucer's glow.

Continuity notes: LLV-Leon should feel like a heroic living UFO. The tractor beam is green, luminous, and wide enough to lift the beast completely off the ground.

## Corrupted Lucid Light Beast

A lion-sized dog infected with corrupted Lucid Light. It has the muscular body and ferocity of a huge war hound, with dark fur, jagged purple-black crystal growths, glowing violet eyes, and corrupted purple starry energy leaking from its mouth, claws, and spine. It can warp forward in violent bursts and unleash a mega bark shockwave.

Continuity notes: The animal should look dangerous but still like a corrupted creature that can be saved. Avoid making it demonic beyond rescue. The corruption should be visibly separable from the animal on the final page.

## Purified Dog

The same large dog after the corrupted Lucid Light is cleansed away. Its eyes are calmer, its dark fur is no longer cracked by purple-black crystals, and a soft healthy Lucid Light shimmer remains.

Continuity notes: The purified dog should look exhausted and confused, not hostile.

## Corruption Entity

The expelled corruption manifests as a separate inky purple-black entity made of smoky Lucid Light, jagged crystal shards, and angry violet eyes. It is not the dog; it is the harmful energy pulled out of the dog.

Continuity notes: Keep this entity visually distinct from the dog so the resolution reads as purification, not destruction.

## Storyverse Animal Control

A specialized cosmic animal-control response team in the Storyverse. They wear practical silver-and-teal protective suits with helmets, glowing capture gear, and containment pods designed for magical or cosmic wildlife.

Continuity notes: They arrive only at the end. Their gear should look official and competent, not comedic.

## Storyverse

A cosmic void filled with glowing bubble universes, each containing possible story ideas. The immediate battlefield is a floating dream terrain of luminous paths, nebula sky, and cracked crystal ground lit by purple Lucid Light.

Continuity notes: The Storyverse should remain recognizable as cosmic bubble universes floating together in a dark void. Lucid Light should look like sparkling purple energy shining like a night sky full of stars.
