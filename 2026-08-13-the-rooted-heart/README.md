# 08/13/2026 - The Rooted Heart

Dream Comics entry for August 13, 2026.

While flying over a city, Jet discovers a secret superhero whose body hair can grow into powerful tethers, sprout balloons, and root or unroot almost anything. A playful demonstration escalates from rooftop swings to an entire city floating beneath a balloon, alarming Jet and the authorities despite the hero's ability to reroot every structure perfectly.

At a children's hospital, the hero reveals the compassionate purpose behind those frighteningly vast powers: entertaining sick children and replacing failing organs through seamless unrooting and rerooting. Jet realizes that the hero's greatest act is not lifting a city, but choosing healing over spectacle.

The finished package includes the complete journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, and assembled PDF.
