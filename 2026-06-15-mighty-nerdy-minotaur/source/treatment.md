# Treatment

## Title

06/15/2026: The Mighty Nerdy Minotaur

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet arrives alone on a farm in his black-and-white striped werewolf form, frightens a village of humanoid cows, clashes with their champion the Mighty Nerdy Minotaur, and transforms the fight into a shared conversation about multiverse theory.

## Central Conflict

Jet's frightening werewolf appearance makes the farm residents assume he is a threat. The Mighty Nerdy Minotaur answers their call and overpowers him physically, forcing Jet to combine Lucid Light, healing, and restraint before choosing curiosity over conquest.

## Theme

Strength can end a fight, but shared wonder can turn an enemy into a friend.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins disoriented but lucid, arriving alone in an unfamiliar farm world. Panic turns the dream into a defensive brawl, and the minotaur's greater strength pushes Jet to strain beyond his limit. Lucid Light fails to solve the conflict by force, but the rainbow crystal sword heals and strengthens him through the struggle. Once Jet can win, he chooses not to dominate. He notices the minotaur's intellectual curiosity, brings up multiverse theory and causally disconnected zones, and the mood shifts from combat to excited speculation.

## Ending

The final page resolves with Jet and the Mighty Nerdy Minotaur seated peacefully near a damaged farm fence, surrounded by calmer humanoid cows, discussing distant Storyverse bubble universes and the possibility of other worlds.

## Cast

- Jet in werewolf form: the protagonist, a lucid dreamer in a black-furred werewolf body with white stripes, green serpent eyes, long dark-brown dreadlocks, dream-clothing fragments, rainbow crystal sword, golden energy tether, and purple Lucid Light.
- The Mighty Nerdy Minotaur: a heroic farm defender, huge and physically overwhelming, with bull horns, glasses, a sweater vest or academic harness over heroic armor pieces, notebooks, star charts, and a curious scientific mind.
- Humanoid cow villagers: anxious farm residents whose panic starts the conflict, then softens once the fight becomes a conversation.
- The farm world: a bright Storyverse bubble world of barns, fields, fences, windmills, hay bales, and cosmic hints beyond the sky.
