# Character Bible - The Lucid Chain Alliance

## Jet

- Black adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears a black hoodie over a colorful “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each outer leg, clean white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build; fascinated by the princess's eyes, playfully confident during the ring prank, alarmed after learning about the orcs, focused during the rescue, and exhilarated during the team attack.
- Keep his skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, pants stripes, shoes, and cross-body bag visually consistent on every page.
- His Lucid Light resembles sparkling purple energy filled with tiny stars.

## Sapphire-eyed princess

- Adult elven woman with very pale skin, long flowing silver hair, elegant pointed ears, and two literal faceted sapphire gemstones set into her eye sockets.
- Her eyes must visibly read as solid blue jewels, never ordinary blue human eyes.
- Wears a graceful floor-length white dress covered in the same intricate cobalt-blue fine-china pattern as the dragon, with delicate silver and gold trim.
- Calm, amused, commanding, and capable. She controls the dragon, performs the crash landing, and magically repairs its torn wing.
- Her magic produces luminous silver needles and fine golden-blue thread directly from her fingertips.

## Elven knight

- Adult male elf with a peach complexion, pointed ears, stern angular features, and long brown hair flowing from the sides and back of a clearly bald crown.
- His bald crown must remain plainly visible on every page; do not give him a full head of hair.
- Wears polished silver plate armor with subtle blue cloth accents and carries one bright steel longsword.
- Initially jealous, angry, and rigid. Later surprised, battle-focused, thrilled by the team maneuver, and respectfully allied with Jet.
- The sparkling-purple Lucid Light ring remains attached to him from the parachute scene through the battle.

## Quilted dragon

- A gigantic living dragon made completely from soft sewn quilted blanket fabric, not flesh, scales, glass, ceramic, metal, or porcelain.
- Primarily white fabric covered in intricate cobalt-blue floral and scroll patterns reminiscent of blue-and-white fine china, with silver and gold stitched accents.
- Visible seams, padded quilt squares, soft textile folds, fabric wings, and stitched construction make its material unmistakable.
- Majestic and gentle. One fabric wing is sliced open on page 4, then visibly stitched and restored by the final page.
- The dragon must not have sapphire eyes; the literal sapphire-eye trait belongs only to the princess.

## Hostile orcs

- Large green-skinned fantasy warriors with tusks, rough dark leather, dented iron armor, and heavy axes or clubs.
- Menacing and numerous, but never gory. Defeated orcs are knocked backward or disarmed rather than visibly mutilated.
- Their weapons strike the Lucid Light chain and release non-gory purple shockwaves.

## Lucid Light rings and chain

- The rings are smooth bands of sparkling purple, star-filled energy rather than metal.
- The stolen ring becomes a broad magical parachute made from translucent purple Lucid Light.
- The sister ring on Jet's hand and the knight's ring create a visible connection.
- That connection manifests as a huge flexible chain made from sparkling purple cosmic energy.
- During the human-flail maneuver, Jet controls the chain while the knight willingly extends his sword and turns each swing into a coordinated strike.
- When the chain breaks, it dissolves into purple star sparks and releases the knight into a controlled spinning finish.

## Forest, castle, and clearing

- Immense lush fantasy forest beneath a luminous blue sky. A pale storybook castle rises beyond the treetops during the flight.
- The crash clearing is wide enough for the quilted dragon and surrounded by towering ancient trees.
- The orc battle occurs deeper in the same forest with shafts of blue-green light, roots, moss, and drifting purple Lucid Light particles.

## Continuity rules

- All eight pages use bright, high-saturation modern-anime comic art with crisp linework and cinematic fantasy lighting.
- Story pages use unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes only.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, signatures, gore, extra limbs, duplicate unintended characters, or blank caption boxes.
- Keep all captions and complete caption-box borders inside a generous 6% safe margin from every outer edge.
