# Page Script — Pyrogre's Bachelor Pad

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait anime comic page. Jet and Pyrogre must follow the character bible exactly on every appearance.

## Page 1 — Cover

Dynamic vertical cover. Masked Jet flies upward in his agile final suit, propelled by golden-yellow force rings from the belt, while friendly gigantic Pyrogre leans over the city with an open hand and a warm grin. A surreal cutaway window in Pyrogre's torso hints at neon arcade cabinets and a cozy sofa beneath ivory rib arches. The orange underside of Jet's black cape echoes Pyrogre's flames. Visible cover text only: “PYROGRE'S BACHELOR PAD” and “JULY 14, 2026”. No issue number.

## Page 2 — The Plain Jet Pack

Four panels. A shadowy sleep-paralysis bedroom threshold transforms into a sunrise rooftop. Jet arrives alone in his exact hoodie outfit. A compact silver-gray jet-pack suit assembles around him while leaving his face and dreadlocks visible. He hovers successfully, then lands and studies its overly simple design without excitement.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrived alone on a rooftop above a wide city.”
- “Because I knew I was dreaming, I began trying custom superhero power suits as easily as changing clothes.”
- “The first suit gave me a jet pack, and it lifted me into the air exactly as intended.”
- “It worked, but it felt plain. Flying was not enough if the suit itself did not feel like mine.”

## Page 3 — More Armor, More Weight

Five panels. Jet adds heavy armor to the same prototype and tests its stiff movement. More equipment unfolds into shoulder lasers and forearm emitters. He fires one controlled laser at a harmless rooftop target. He struggles under the oversized backpack and cables. In the final panel he deliberately steps out of the massive suit, relieved.

Caption text:

- “I tried an armored version of the jet-pack suit, hoping protection would give it the missing identity.”
- “The armor made me safer, but it still felt like a generic machine wrapped around me.”
- “Built-in laser weapons brought the design closer to the kind of superhero technology I imagined.”
- “Unfortunately, every new weapon added more metal, more weight, and less freedom.”
- “The most advanced suit was also the one I wanted to escape, so I abandoned powered armor entirely.”

## Page 4 — The Anti-Gravity Belt

Five panels. Jet, back in his hoodie outfit, fastens the yellow belt. He rises lightly with no machinery. He flexes one arm to send a broad shockwave across loose rooftop dust, points the other arm to emit a focused golden force beam at a target, then spreads all four limbs as a transparent spherical shield stops falling masonry. Final panel shows him sustaining calm flight above the roof.

Caption text:

- “I replaced every suit with one light yellow belt that created an anti-gravity force field around me.”
- “The belt let me fly without a jet pack and protected my entire body without heavy armor.”
- “When I flexed a limb in one direction, the field projected a powerful shockwave in that same direction.”
- “A precise motion focused that force into a beam, while a wide stance fortified the field into all-around protection.”
- “Flight, offense, and defense now came from my own movement. The power finally felt free.”

## Page 5 — A Heroic Identity

Four panels. Jet decides the belt still needs a superhero identity. A graceful transformation sequence builds the sleek black bodysuit, green armored chest padding, black gloves and boots, black cape with orange lining, full-head black mask with white eye lenses, and unchanged yellow belt. He poses on the roof with the cape open so the orange lining is obvious. He launches over the bright city using golden force rings.

Caption text:

- “I loved the belt, but I still wanted an actual superhero suit that expressed who I had become.”
- “I chose a full black mask with white eye lenses, green armored chest padding, black gloves and boots, and a black cape lined in orange.”
- “The yellow belt remained the center of the design because its lightness was the reason the whole costume worked.”
- “Fully dressed at last, I launched into the sky to enjoy the city and the simple pleasure of my new powers.”

## Page 6 — The Monster Who Did Not Want a Fight

Four panels. Masked Jet glides happily above the city. Gigantic flaming Pyrogre rises beyond the rooftops, looking intimidating but holding open, peaceful hands. Jet stops in midair behind a fortified golden field and communicates his objection in a rectangular quote-caption. Pyrogre kneels to Jet's level, smiles gently, and answers in a rectangular quote-caption.

Caption text:

- “Then a giant flaming ogre appeared, and the peaceful flight suddenly looked like the beginning of another monster battle.”
- “I told him, ‘Sometimes I just want to enjoy my powers instead of having to fight a big monster.’”
- “The ogre lowered his hands and answered, ‘That is fine. I am a hero too, and I did not come to fight you.’”
- “His name was Pyrogre. The moment we spoke honestly, the expected battle disappeared and a much stranger invitation replaced it.”

## Page 7 — The Bachelor Pad Inside

Five panels. Pyrogre carefully swallows tiny masked Jet in a surreal, harmless transition. Jet lands safely inside the warm non-gory bachelor pad with sofa, snacks, arcade cabinets, game screen, and ivory rib arches. Jet and Pyrogre communicate through a friendly wall-mounted intercom. Jet plays one arcade game. Then he enters the clear training corner and punches one huge ivory rib with a golden force-field fist; exterior inset shows Pyrogre's flames brightening as he becomes stronger.

Caption text:

- “Pyrogre swallowed me safely, and I discovered that the inside of his body contained a comfortable bachelor pad.”
- “Beneath enormous rib arches, he had a sofa, snacks, a game screen, and an entire neon arcade.”
- “Pyrogre explained that damage from inside his body makes him stronger instead of weaker.”
- “I used the belt to punch one of his enormous bones in the training area, carefully keeping the force away from his furniture.”
- “The impact strengthened him immediately. His flames burned brighter, and both of us enjoyed a power-up that required no enemy at all.”

## Page 8 — Relaxation-First Heroes

Four panels. A stronger, brighter Pyrogre gently lets Jet out onto a rooftop. They share an enormous-to-tiny fist bump. Jet sits comfortably on Pyrogre's open palm playing a portable arcade game while Pyrogre lounges against a nearby tower without damaging it. A distant small emergency beacon glows over the city; both heroes notice it calmly and prepare to rise, smiling. Final wide panel shows them flying together over the peaceful skyline, Jet on golden force rings beside Pyrogre's flame trail.

Caption text:

- “Pyrogre was powerful, very nice, and just as interested in enjoying heroism as I was.”
- “Together we became an incredible superhero team focused more on relaxing than searching for a world to save.”
- “That did not mean we would ignore anyone in need. It meant we would help when a real situation demanded it instead of inventing a fight.”
- “With freedom, friendship, and an arcade between adventures, Pyrogre and I had found our own way to be heroes.”
