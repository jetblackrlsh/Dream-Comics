# Character Bible

## Jet — Agentis business-suit form

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- In Agentis he wears a sharply tailored deep-black business suit, white dress shirt, narrow iridescent purple tie, black dress shoes, and a discreet black cross-body belt bag integrated over the suit jacket.
- His long dreadlocks remain fully visible and fall past his shoulders; never shorten them, remove his beard, or lighten his skin.
- His familiar black hoodie, “Dream Anime” shirt, striped pants, white sneakers, rainbow crystal sword, and golden-light tether do not appear in this particular dream scene because the dream explicitly gives him a business suit.
- Thoughtful, curious, lightly amused, then overwhelmed, then calm and decisive. His posture evolves from cautious arrival to uncertain director to grounded creative leader.

## Mara — Agentis guide

- Adult woman with medium-brown skin, dark hazel eyes, and a sleek chin-length silver bob with one longer side lock.
- Wears a fitted midnight-blue futuristic executive uniform with cyan piping, high collar, silver shoulder tabs, and a small circular Agentis insignia showing three connected points.
- Carries a thin translucent cyan tablet. Professional, dryly funny, patient, and never ominous.

## Agentis agents

- A diverse group of adult humanlike AI agents with varied skin tones, genders, faces, hairstyles, and body types.
- Unified by clean futuristic office uniforms in charcoal, navy, white, and cyan, each with the same small three-point Agentis insignia.
- They are neither robots nor secret agents: they look like capable designers, researchers, editors, engineers, and coordinators waiting for meaningful assignments.
- Background agent faces and clothing may vary, but the shared visual language, cyan interfaces, and insignia must remain consistent.

## Agentis command center

- Immense circular futuristic operations room with tiered workstations, curved white architecture, dark graphite floors, cyan holographic interfaces, and a central round director's platform.
- Tall windows reveal a digital version of the Storyverse: cosmic darkness filled with glowing bubble universes, linked by cyan data paths.
- Initially feels crowded and expectant; later becomes organized into a few calm teams around a clear luminous media map.

## Media landscape map

- A central holographic constellation with a bright “COMICS” node connected to abstract, mostly icon-based nodes for a website, community support, audio, video, games, archives, and future ideas.
- Avoid extraneous tiny labels. Its visual meaning should come from recognizable icons and clear connections rather than unreadable text.
