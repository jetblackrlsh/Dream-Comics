# Character Bible

## Jet, Barbarian Dream Form

- Role: protagonist and lucid dreamer.
- Stable identity: dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks, warm brown eyes, heroic posture, rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glitter energy tether, sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Dream form: muscular barbarian version of Jet with long dreadlocks loose around his shoulders, rugged black hooded fur mantle that echoes his usual black hoodie, torn "Dream Anime" emblem cloth across his chest, dark battle trousers with a white side stripe, white wrapped boots, black cross-body belt bag adapted into a leather war satchel.
- Expression range: excited wonder, moral shock, stubborn defiance, lucid focus, compassionate resolve.
- Continuity rule: even when transformed into a barbarian or riding through fantasy terrain, Jet should remain visibly Jet through skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, warm eyes, black hoodie-like mantle, Dream Anime emblem, white stripe pants, white footwear, belt bag motif, rainbow sword, and golden tether.

## Big Barbarian

- Role: antagonist and leader of the cruel raider camp.
- Appearance: gigantic muscular barbarian warlord, towering over Jet, gray-tan skin, bald head with braided black beard, scarred face, heavy horned iron shoulder armor, red fur cape, black iron wrist bracers, massive stone hammer.
- Expression range: mocking confidence, rage, startled fear after Jet's mecha appears.
- Continuity rule: he is much larger than ordinary barbarians and should dominate panels through scale.

## Raider Barbarians

- Role: cruel obstacle force.
- Appearance: rough fantasy raiders in mismatched leather, bone, horn, iron scraps, and red-brown furs, carrying stolen gold sacks and guarding captive villagers.
- Continuity rule: keep them visually separate from Jet by avoiding Jet's black hoodie, Dream Anime emblem, white stripe pants, rainbow sword, and golden tether.

## Captive Villagers

- Role: people Jet rescues.
- Appearance: varied science-fantasy village families, elders, travelers, and artisans wearing colorful cloth robes, tool belts, and village crests; frightened at first, then relieved.
- Continuity rule: show them as captives and returning home without graphic violence.

## Giant Barbarian Mecha Construct

- Role: Jet's Lucid Light counterforce.
- Appearance: enormous translucent mecha made from sparkling purple Lucid Light, barbarian armor shapes, starry energy plates, golden tether cables, rainbow crystal blade edges, and glowing runes.
- Continuity rule: it should look summoned by Jet, not like a separate robot ally. Purple starry energy is the dominant material, with gold and rainbow accents.

## Starlight Horse

- Role: transition from fantasy rescue to superhero base.
- Appearance: majestic horse made of starry white-blue light, transparent mane of constellations, hooves leaving small galaxies, gentle eyes.

## Second Brain

- Role: supporting character in the final superhero-base discussion.
- Appearance: floating brain made of sparkling purple Lucid Light, glowing like a night sky full of stars, with gentle telepathic aura and small orbiting idea glyphs.
- Expression range: curious, analytical, encouraging.

## Futuristic Superhero Base

- Role: epilogue setting.
- Appearance: bright science-fantasy command room with panoramic Storyverse windows, holographic pet translator schematics, animal power armor designs, ethical rescue monitors, purple Lucid Light conduits, silver-white floors, and superhero equipment.
