# Treatment

## Logline

After Jet becomes lucid through sleep paralysis and lands in Salem during the witch trials, he rescues Super Wizard Man from the stake, learns that a comic from the future inspired him to become a magical superhero, and receives a relaxing mystical brain cleaning that relieves an impossible itch.

## Central Conflict

Super Wizard Man wants to help people with heroic magic, but Salem sees any visible magic as a threat. Jet arrives while Super Wizard Man is about to be burned alive, so Jet must stop the execution quickly without hurting the crowd. The conflict changes from physical danger to moral clarity when Super Wizard Man reveals that he is trying to live as a superhero before superheroes exist. Jet understands his good intentions, but he also knows the time period is too afraid to understand them. The final problem is intimate and strange: Jet's own brain starts itching, and only Super Wizard Man's gentle, absurd magic can fix it.

## Theme

Good intentions still need the right context. Super Wizard Man is doing something kind and brave, but he must survive in a time that has no language for superheroes yet.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alone and alert in a dangerous historical nightmare. He acts decisively by using the golden tether to pull a storm cloud over the stake. Once he and Super Wizard Man reach the underground lair, the danger softens into trust and explanation. Jet becomes concerned when the brain itch starts, then relaxes as Super Wizard Man carefully cleans the sensation away. The ending is calm but unresolved: Jet is grateful for the help and worried about whether Super Wizard Man can keep doing good without being destroyed by fear.

## Ending

The final page shows Jet leaving the hidden lair refreshed while Super Wizard Man stands beside the glowing future comic that inspired him. Jet hopes Super Wizard Man can keep helping people from the shadows until the world is ready to understand a wizard superhero.
