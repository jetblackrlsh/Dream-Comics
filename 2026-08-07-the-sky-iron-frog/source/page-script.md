# Page Script - The Sky-Iron Frog

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, the frog, the three psychic fairies, and the village must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

One dramatic vertical composition in the magical treehouse village. Jet commands the center foreground. The giant frog looms behind him in black sky-iron samurai armor with its long tongue arcing across the composition. The orange pyrokinetic fairy, blue cryokinetic fairy, and purple electrokinetic fairy converge from three directions with fire, ice, and lightning. Visible cover text only: "THE SKY-IRON FROG" and "AUGUST 7, 2026." No issue number.

## Page 2 - The Village and the First Attack

Six panels. Jet walks through the luminous village, studies homes grown into many kinds of magical trees, and notices fairies of different colors using different powers. The unarmored frog kaiju suddenly attacks with its long tongue. The fairies combine ornate magical spells and drive it back into the forest. Jet watches, impressed that they have already protected themselves.

Caption text:

- "I entered a world where colorful fairies lived together among many kinds of magical trees."
- "Their homes grew from trunks and branches, and every fairy color seemed connected to a different power."
- "A giant humanoid frog suddenly attacked, snapping its long tongue through the air."
- "The fairies answered with a brilliant combination of magical spells."
- "Their power forced the unarmored creature to retreat into the forest."
- "The danger ended so quickly that I did not need to intervene."

## Page 3 - The Armored Return

Six panels. Jet continues exploring the treehouses. A heavy impact announces the frog's return. It emerges in full black samurai armor. Emerald runes, golden star magic, and pink sparkling spells strike the armor and dissolve harmlessly. The frog's tongue reaches through the helpless defensive line while Jet realizes the rules have changed.

Caption text:

- "After the attack, I explored the treehouses and studied the fairies' different abilities."
- "Then the frog returned wearing black armor shaped like the armor of a samurai."
- "The fairies repeated the magical attacks that had defeated it before."
- "Every glowing spell dissolved harmlessly against the dark plates."
- "Protected from magic, the frog pushed through the village's defenses."
- "If we fought the same way again, the village would be doomed."

## Page 4 - Sky Iron and Mucus

Six panels. Jet remembers a meteor streaking through the Storyverse and dark ore cooling inside it. He identifies the armor as possible anti-magic sky iron. Jet plants one hand and sends mineral-force lines into the plates. The armor visibly pulls several inches away, but clear ropes of mucus stretch between metal and skin and snap it back into place. Jet strains and fails.

Caption text:

- "I remembered legends of meteorite stone whose ore could suppress magic."
- "If the armor was made from that sky iron, removing it should restore the fairies' advantage."
- "Geomancy was one of the earliest powers I learned in the Storyverse."
- "I reached for the mineral ore inside the armor and pulled with all my focus."
- "The plates moved, but the frog's powerful mucus held them like glue."
- "I could affect the armor, yet I could not tear it free."

## Page 5 - The Contradiction

Six panels. Jet kneels beneath a branch bridge while failed spells explode in the distance. He looks from his hand to the armor he just moved. A clear conceptual sequence compares ornate magical fire runes fading against sky iron with clean pyrokinetic flame striking it directly; another panel relabels his rock-moving force from Geomancy to Geokinesis. Jet rises with a sudden realization.

Caption text:

- "For one terrible moment, I had no solution left."
- "Then I noticed a contradiction: anti-magic armor had not stopped my Geomancy from moving it."
- "Perhaps the power I called Geomancy had never been magic at all."
- "Magical control of fire can resemble pyrokinesis, even though pyrokinesis uses psychic force instead of mana."
- "If my earth power was really Geokinesis, sky iron had no reason to block it."
- "That distinction might apply to some of the fairies too."

## Page 6 - A New Battle Line

Six panels. Jet stands on a high branch and signals a disciplined retreat. Fairies using ornate rune magic pull back toward the treehouses. The three psychic fairies advance in a clear triangular formation: orange fire, blue ice, and purple lightning gathering around focused hands without spell circles. The frog charges in black armor while Jet points the new front line forward.

Caption text:

- "I ordered every magical fairy to fall back toward the treehouses."
- "Then I called for every fairy whose power came from the mind to take the front line."
- "The pyrokinetic fairy gathered fire without a wand, rune, or spell."
- "The cryokinetic fairy focused cold into blades of ice."
- "The electrokinetic fairy shaped lightning through pure psychic force."
- "The armored frog charged, believing nothing in the village could hurt it."

## Page 7 - The Psychic Storm

Six panels escalating into one large impact panel. Pyrokinetic flame strikes the frog's chest armor and does not dissolve. Cryokinetic ice locks its legs and tongue in place. Electrokinetic lightning races across the sky-iron plates and blasts the frog backward beyond the village. The three psychic fairies hover together while Jet shields the retreating magical fairies from debris.

Caption text:

- "The first pyrokinetic flame struck the sky iron and kept burning."
- "Psychic power was not magic, so the armor could not erase it."
- "Cryokinetic ice froze the frog's legs and trapped its dangerous tongue."
- "Electrokinetic lightning surged across every black plate."
- "The combined psychic storm blasted the giant frog away from the village."
- "A defense built against magic had failed against the power of the mind."

## Page 8 - Powers of the Mind

Six panels resolving into a large final vista. The village is safe beneath glowing trees. Magical and psychic fairies reunite without hierarchy and celebrate together. Jet examines one floating stone in his palm, then looks upward as the canopy opens onto luminous Storyverse bubble universes. The final image shows Jet framed by the united fairies, thoughtful rather than triumphant.

Caption text:

- "The fairy village survived because we stopped treating every impossible power as the same thing."
- "A failed plan had become evidence, and that evidence revealed the winning strategy."
- "I began to wonder whether any power I used in dreams was truly magic."
- "Dreams exist within the mind, and powers of the mind are normally called psychic."
- "Perhaps every spell I had ever named was another form of thought made real."
- "I did not know the answer, but the Storyverse had given me a new theory to explore."
