# Treatment

## Logline

After Jet becomes lucid through sleep paralysis and debates American Monomyth stories with Doctor Bear, he creates a Storyverse utopia where an orphan troll must save an equal society from a dark elf who tries to use the multiverse itself as an excuse for abandoning one enslaved world.

## Central Conflict

Jet and Doctor Bear imagine a world that rejects special treatment. The utopia accepts an orphan troll with the same kindness it gives everyone else, but a dark elf interprets equality as disrespect and brainwashes every human in the city. Because the elf's magic only works on humans, the troll becomes the only person free enough to respond. The dark elf's Universe Splitter creates a philosophical trap: if another universe already has the happy ending, does the troll still owe his own world a rescue?

## Theme

Other possible worlds do not replace responsibility to the world directly in front of you.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins calm and curious after turning sleep paralysis into lucidity. His conversation with Doctor Bear becomes a creative test of a world without chosen-one privilege. The troll begins as an outsider who is accepted without fanfare, then faces the fear that the best version of events may have split away from his own life. He moves from confusion to commitment when he realizes that equality means refusing to abandon the people beside him. The ending is peaceful because the troll is not elevated above the community; he is simply welcomed back into ordinary life.

## Ending

The troll saves his own world by using the Universe Splitter to choose decisive action. The dark elf is knocked so far away that his brainwashing breaks, the humans recover, and the city returns to normal. When the people wonder about the universe where the troll left in shame, he imagines that version still finding help elsewhere. The troll receives no monument and no special status, because being treated like everyone else is the happy ending.
