# Treatment

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet calms a savage white rat-dog by reshaping it into a cyber-eyed tinkerer, then resists recruitment by a villainous pickle warlord whose endless clones become weaker until he must attack by drone.

## Central Conflict

Jet's first challenge is whether force alone can stop a creature whose aggression keeps returning the instant restraints are removed. The second challenge twists that same idea into comedy and danger: a warlord insists villainy deserves power, but every attempt to multiply his strength drains him further.

## Theme

Control without understanding is temporary. Jet wins by transforming aggression into intelligence, then by refusing the warlord's shallow promise of power until the villain's copies reveal how empty that promise is.

## Ending

After the pickle warlord's weak clones collapse into harmless confusion, Jet is invited into a glowing production company meeting about turning his life into a movie. The attack returns, but the once-menacing warlord is so depleted that he hides behind drones while Jet and the new tinkerer prepare to counter him.

## Emotional Arc

Jet starts alone and reactive, trapping the rat-dog only to discover the calm will not last. He becomes more deliberate when he reshapes the creature instead of simply containing it. By the time the pickle warlord demands loyalty to villainy, Jet is centered enough to refuse, dismantle the clones, and face the final drone interruption with curiosity rather than fear.
