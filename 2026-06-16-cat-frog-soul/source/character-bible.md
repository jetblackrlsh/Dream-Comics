# Character Bible

## Jet

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow crystal sword shaped like a colorful futuristic crystal blade, and a golden glittering energy tether that trails like a rope made of light. His Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy, shining like a night sky full of stars. In this issue Jet is alone, focused, and emotionally shaken by the Shellasaur extinction, then resolute when rebuilding the soul puppet.

## Lucid Light

Lucid Light is sparkling purple reality-warping energy filled with tiny star-like points. It forms Jet's flight aura, space-view glow, and creation energy when he rebuilds the puppet. It should feel cosmic and dreamlike, not electric.

## Rainbow Crystal Sword

The rainbow sword is a colorful futuristic crystal sword carried by Jet. It may appear at his side or across his back, glowing with prismatic color. It is a visual identifier, not the main weapon used against the serpent in this story.

## Shellasaurs

Shellasaurs are gentle dinosaurs with large bodies, long tails, sturdy legs, kind reptile faces, and massive spiral snail-like shells on their backs. The shells are home-like, with small natural openings, glowing interior warmth, moss, painted markings, and travel-worn details. They move like peaceful nomads across a prehistoric dreamland.

## Subterranean Serpent

The serpent is a gigantic underground predator with a dark scaled body, pale underbelly, narrow predatory eyes, jagged ridges, and stretchy jaws that can open impossibly wide. It attacks from below in bursts of dust, cracked earth, and shadow. Its speed and stealth matter more than brute posture; it should look hard to track until Cat Frog Soul forces it into the open.

## Frog Soul Puppet

The soul puppet is a humanoid green frog made of felt, with visible soft fabric texture, round black bead-like eyes, simple stitched mouth, and slightly worn seams. At first it is missing arms and legs, leaving only a head and torso lying helplessly on the dangerous ground. It should read as fragile, symbolic, and personal rather than a normal animal.

## Cat Frog Soul

Cat Frog Soul is the same green felt frog puppet after Jet rebuilds it with long cat arms and long cat legs. The new limbs are feline in shape but still made of green felt, with agile bends, paw-like hands and feet, and long shining claws made of condensed purple Lucid Light. It keeps the frog puppet's round bead eyes and stitched mouth while gaining a low, fast, confident catlike stance. Its silhouette should be unmistakable: frog puppet head and torso, long cat limbs, luminous claws, springing motion.

## Storyverse World

The Shellasaur land is a prehistoric dreamland inside the Storyverse: open plains, giant ferns, rocky mesas, cracked earth, warm sunlight, and a sky where faint cosmic bubble universes shimmer at the edge of perception. From space, the land looks like a small glowing world floating in a vast cosmic void filled with distant bubble universes.

## Visual Continuity Notes

Keep Jet's normal design consistent: dark-brown skin, full beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime tee, black athletic pants with white stripe, white sneakers, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and purple Lucid Light. Keep the soul puppet consistent before and after transformation: green felt material, bead eyes, stitched mouth, handmade texture. After transformation, keep the same puppet body plus long cat arms, long cat legs, and glowing claws.
