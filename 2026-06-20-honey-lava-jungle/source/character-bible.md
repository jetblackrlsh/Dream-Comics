# Character Bible

## Jet

- Role: lucid dreamer, field leader, and protagonist.
- Age range: adult man.
- Skin and face: dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, expressive heroic face that moves from shock to resolve to relief.
- Hair: long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie; the dreadlocks must remain visible whenever Jet appears.
- Outfit: black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag.
- Signature props and powers: rainbow futuristic crystal sword, golden glittering energy tether, sparkling purple Lucid Light, and the Aegis spell.
- Aegis spell: a wide shield of golden light shaped like a turtle shell, with glowing hexagonal shell plates and a curved protective dome.
- Continuity rule: do not remove the beard, dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime tee, belt bag, rainbow sword, or golden tether. The Aegis shield should always look like golden turtle-shell light, not a generic force field.

## Leon

- Role: Jet's Ghostly UFO and aerial rescuer.
- Body: ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow, translucent edges, soft spectral haze, and a friendly protective presence.
- Signature power: green tractor beam that lifts Were-Bees gently out of danger.
- Continuity rule: Leon should look like a ghostly green UFO, not a conventional metal spaceship.

## Skelebot

- Role: humanoid speedster robot and evacuation specialist.
- Body: humanoid skeletal robot frame, mechanical bones, athletic stance, bright speed trails.
- Motion motif: repeated afterimages, streaking jungle leaves, and dust arcs from super speed.
- Continuity rule: Skelebot should read as a robot speedster, not an undead skeleton.

## Overdrive

- Role: matter-manipulating robot and decisive problem solver.
- Body and outfit: metallic chrome robot body, spiky slicked-back yellow hair, glowing green eyes, yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers.
- Power motif: green and yellow atomic diagrams, vibrating molecular rings, and matter-warping light around their hands.
- Continuity rule: use they/them in source notes and keep the chrome body, yellow hair, green eyes, and full yellow outfit consistent.

## Were-Bees

- Role: civilians in danger.
- Body: humanoid bee people with striped yellow-black bodies, translucent wings, antennae, expressive insect eyes, and varied village clothing or light armor.
- Emotional range: fallen, frightened, grateful, and relieved.
- Continuity rule: they should look humanoid and sympathetic, not like ordinary insects or monsters.

## Honey-Lava Wave

- Role: disaster force and central obstacle.
- Appearance: immense molten honey wave pouring from a jungle volcano, thick amber-gold, glowing orange at the hottest edges, with steam, sparks, and sticky curls like lava and syrup combined.
- Transformation: after Overdrive's matter manipulation, the wave becomes luminous golden honey clouds that rise safely above the jungle.
