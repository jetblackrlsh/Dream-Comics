Dream journal date: 06/01/2026.

Jet induces lucidity via sleep paralysis and arrives alone at a nightclub in the Storyverse. Inside, a humanoid stopwatch named Mr. Stopwatch calls him over and warns that only seconds remain before the nightclub explodes and everyone inside dies.

Jet summons his Lucid Light Watch, a purple starry transformation device that lets him become Lucid Light Variants of his crew members. He transforms into LLV-Skelebot and uses super speed to evacuate everyone from the club.

Mr. Stopwatch smirks and reveals that he never said the bomb was stationary. Jet sees that one nightclub guest is actually an android whose pressure reading is about to go critical. Jet tries to clear nearby guests with LLV-Skelebot speed, but the android starts to explode before everyone can be moved.

Jet transforms into LLV-Tecton and uses hardlight holographic blue cyber weapons to build a protective dome around the blast epicenter. The dome holds and saves the crowd.

Authorities arrive to search for the attacker. Jet sees Mr. Stopwatch trying to escape, transforms into LLV-Leon, and uses a green tractor beam to pull Mr. Stopwatch into the authorities' custody. With the day saved, Jet flies off into the Storyverse.
