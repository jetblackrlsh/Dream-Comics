# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries the Life Sword: a colorful futuristic crystal sword with a broad translucent faceted rainbow blade, a dark silver geometric technological hilt, a radiant rainbow-white aura, and subtle signs of independent life.
- Carries the Golden Energy Tether: a flexible rope made entirely from glittering golden light. It is never a solid cable, chain, or grappling hook.
- Uses Lucid Light: sparkling purple reality-warping energy filled with tiny stars like a night sky. The direct beam is a narrow, instantaneous purple-white laser with starry particles, fired from Jet's open palm.
- Casts Aegis as a circular translucent blue-violet magical shield marked by clean geometric runes.
- Casts Sol as a brilliant miniature golden-white sun, several feet wide, floating above the target and radiating unmistakable natural sunlight.
- Expression range: reflective discovery, protective resolve, controlled urgency, shocked improvisation, drained pain, defensive focus, inventive realization, commanding triumph, relieved wonder.
- Jet never wears a crown, cape, mask, helmet, conventional superhero costume, medieval armor, or unrelated clothing.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, shirt, pants, sneakers, belt bag, sword, and tether on every page. On pages 6-7 he looks visibly weakened after the life drain but retains the same outfit.

## The Life Sword

- One consistent independently living futuristic rainbow crystal sword.
- Broad translucent faceted blade containing saturated red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet bands; dark silver geometric hilt; rainbow-white aura; tiny flowing sparks inside the blade.
- Can hover beside Jet, angle itself expressively while communicating, fly independently, and expand a stable plane of light beneath Jet's feet when he rides it.
- Its life-giving attack is a coherent rainbow-white beam filled with tiny prismatic cells and branching organic light.
- It is visually more refined and technologically engineered than raw Life Crystal, but clearly made from the same fundamental material.
- Never depict it as a metal broadsword, katana, ordinary fantasy sword, double-bladed weapon, or Savannah's purple-blue Death Sword.

## The Blue Enchantress

- Adult woman with extremely pale skin, a narrow expressive face, shimmering blue eyes, and very long, messy, voluminous sky-blue hair.
- Thick uneven sky-blue bangs cover her forehead beneath a tall pointed blue witch hat. Her long messy hair and bangs are essential identifiers.
- Wears a flowing sparkling royal-blue dress with layered sleeves, a silver star clasp, and magical blue light like reflected water.
- Carries a palm-sized hinged pocket magic mirror with an ornate silver-blue frame and blue crystal accents.
- Expression range: scholarly certainty, awe at the living crystal civilization, protective concern, focused cooperation through the mirror, relieved gratitude.
- Never depict her as elderly, blonde, brunette, armored, dressed in black, carrying a broom, or missing her hat and bangs.

## Leon — ghostly UFO form

- A friendly ghostly flying saucer with a classic broad disc silhouette, shallow dome, and an ethereal translucent green-white glow.
- Its hull is made from luminous spectral energy rather than metal, with soft trailing wisps and a circular green tractor-beam aperture beneath it.
- Large enough to hold Jet and his crew but still recognizable as a saucer.
- Leon communicates through green light patterns and interior displays rather than a face or mouth.
- Never depict Leon as a solid gray alien craft, organic animal, skull ship, or hostile vessel.

## Leon — enormous mecha form

- Colossal humanoid robot silhouette formed entirely from translucent green ghost energy.
- Leon's flying-saucer disc forms the chest and shoulders; the central dome becomes a glowing cockpit and tractor-beam core.
- Long powerful spectral limbs, broad saucer-like shoulders, luminous green eyes, and wisps trailing from every joint.
- Large enough to push an entire cosmic bubble universe with a reversed tractor beam, yet visibly much smaller than the later pirate cruiser.
- Can become intangible while retaining the same transparent green mecha silhouette.
- Never give him opaque metal armor, mechanical weapon guns, a cape, a mouth, or a different color.

## Rainbow Life Crystals

- Naturally occurring translucent rainbow mineral with internal bands of red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet.
- Near-surface life-imbuing crystals are irregular mineral formations that glow warmly and contain tiny dormant sparks. They are placed inside mirrors, golems, and other objects to animate them.
- The deep living crystal people are humanoid beings made entirely from faceted rainbow crystal. They have clear expressive faces, varied adult silhouettes, glowing eyes, prismatic inner light, distinct crystalline hair shapes, clothing-like crystal facets, and a peaceful underground civilization.
- The living crystal people must look sentient and naturally alive rather than like golems, mannequins, monsters, or crystals inserted into another body.

## Pocket magic mirrors

- Two matching palm-sized hinged mirrors, one belonging to Jet and one to the Blue Enchantress.
- Ornate silver-blue frames, round reflective glass, blue crystal accents, and a calm blue-violet magical glow.
- When bonded, a continuous Golden Energy Tether visibly joins the mirror faces through space, allowing each person to see the other clearly.
- Never depict them as modern smartphones, tablets, flat black screens, or unframed glass.

## Storyverse bubble universe and Porta pocket dimension

- The protected universe is a complete luminous cosmic bubble floating in a star-filled violet-black void. Its transparent boundary contains the medieval village, green hills, deep rainbow mines, and the underground living crystal city as tiny but legible layers.
- Porta is an enormous oval gateway outlined by rotating gold-violet magical geometry. Beyond it lies a quiet dark-blue pocket dimension with sparse silver stars, visually separate from the crowded bubble universes of normal Storyverse space.
- Leon's reversed tractor beam is a wide translucent green cone that pushes the bubble away from his chest and into Porta.

## Pirate armada

- Dozens of varied Storyverse pirate spacecraft surrounding Leon in a complete sphere.
- Design language combines dark iron naval hulls, torn crimson solar sails, skull-like prows, glowing laser cannon ports, chains, and mismatched alien engines.
- Their “laser cannonballs” are bright red-orange energy spheres with long streaking trails.
- The ships should look distinct from Leon and from the final giant cruiser.

## The colossal ghost cruiser

- An unimaginably large space-pirate battle cruiser, many times larger than Leon's already colossal mecha form.
- Long predatory silhouette like a gothic naval dreadnought crossed with a futuristic carrier; layered decks, enormous spectral cannons, jagged prow, cathedral-like command towers, chains, torn energy sails, and thousands of green-white ghost windows.
- Ghost form is translucent blue-green with ragged spectral wakes and stars visible through the hull.
- Restored physical form has blackened iron armor, deep burgundy plating, golden structural ribs, luminous windows, and immense real mass. Subtle rainbow-white living energy remains in its seams after the Life Sword restores it.
- When Leon possesses it, green spectral energy spreads through every corridor, engine, weapon, and control surface.
- Never depict it as another saucer, ordinary sailing ship, modern naval vessel, or something smaller than Leon.

## Vampiric space pirate captain

- Tall undead adult male with gray-pale skin, sunken angular face, glowing crimson eyes, pointed ears, and long swept-back black hair streaked with white.
- Wears a black and burgundy high-collared space-pirate coat with armored shoulders, torn tails, dark trousers, heavy boots, and a crimson gem at the chest.
- Hands end in five long black claws. Four thick leech-like burgundy tendrils extend from his back and torso, each with a circular toothed mouth at the end.
- Can dissolve into smoky black-red mist and reappear across the chamber.
- After draining Jet, his body becomes visibly larger and more muscular, red light courses through his veins, and his eyes burn brighter.
- Sunlight destroys him in a clean, non-gory burst of black-red ash, evaporating mist, and golden-white light.
- Never give him bat wings, a cape covering his identifying coat, normal hands, a human complexion, guns, a helmet, or speech bubbles.

## Captain's chamber

- Vast gothic command chamber inside the cruiser: black iron ribs, burgundy banners, a circular star window, enormous navigation machinery, ribbed floor, suspended chains, and a raised captain's throne.
- Leon's possession gradually changes dead red control lights to friendly spectral green.
- A miniature sun can fit above the central combat floor and fill the room with radiant natural daylight.

## Established Storyverse crew continuity

Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's team but do not physically appear in this focused comic. Savannah's Death Sword is mentioned only as the paired opposite of the Life Sword. Do not insert absent crew members into crowds, reflections, ships, battles, the cover, or the ending.
