# 07/20/2026 - Life Sword: Sea Hag Siege

Dream Comics entry for July 20, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet reunites with a shapeshifting blue slime he previously helped, now the crowned king of Atlantis. Their attempt to stop an ocean-born pollution kaiju is interrupted when a sea hag steals Jet's Life Sword and raises an army of skeletal pirates. Jet reclaims the sword with his golden energy tether, saves the sea and city, then follows the hag's enchanted broom into a hidden treasure cove. There, he must trust Lucid Light claws and the newly bonded broom to defeat a cursed crab familiar and the hag's final undead forces.

The finished package includes the journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, assembled PDF, and preview artifacts.
