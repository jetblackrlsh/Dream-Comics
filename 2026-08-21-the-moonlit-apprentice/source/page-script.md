# Page Script - The Moonlit Apprentice

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet's sheep-dragon form and the wolf knight follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic moonlit full-page composition. Jet's humanoid black sheep-dragon form flies through curling black cloud wisps on enormous ember-veined bat wings. Beside him, the same humanoid wolf flies without wings in his radiant pale-white moonlit form, wearing the navy tunic and dark trousers beneath a silver-blue aura. A huge moon dominates the blue-violet sky above the medieval forest. The wolf's discarded armor and lance are tiny story details far below.

Visible cover text only: “THE MOONLIT APPRENTICE” and “AUGUST 21, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - The Impossible Hybrid

A clear four-panel establishing page. Jet discovers his humanoid black sheep-dragon body, studies his black cloudlike wool and bat wings, then takes flight above the immense wooded medieval landscape. The final panel shows the mounted dark-brown wolf knight approaching below with steel armor and a jousting lance.

Caption text:

- “I began the dream as a humanoid black sheep-dragon—an impossible hybrid even by were-creature standards.”
- “Black wool covered my sheep-like body, while enormous dragon wings unfolded behind me.”
- “Still disoriented, I trusted the sky and flew above a wooded medieval world.”
- “Below, an armored wolf knight lowered his lance and decided what I must be.”

## Page 3 - A Weapon Through Cloud

A clear four-panel attack-and-revelation page. The wolf hurls the lance upward. Jet remains still. The lance passes harmlessly through black wool that opens like a bank of mist and reforms. The wolf stares upward in bafflement while Jet calmly hovers.

Caption text:

- “The knight hurled his jousting lance directly at me.”
- “I did not dodge. My wool opened around the weapon like a cloud around an arrow.”
- “The lance passed through without blood, pain, or even resistance.”
- “His weapon had struck the shape of a sheep-dragon, but not the deeper thing I was.”

## Page 4 - The Skillet Armor

A clear four-panel consequence page. The wolf asks whether Jet is an elemental. Jet exhales controlled orange-gold fire. The steel armor glows dangerously hot while the horse retreats safely. The wolf removes the scalding plates and stands uninjured in his navy padded tunic beside a pile of armor.

Caption text:

- “He demanded to know whether I was some kind of elemental.”
- “I answered with fire, and his protective steel became an oven around him.”
- “The wolf escaped the scalding plates before they could cook him alive.”
- “We agreed that fighting a dragon while dressed like a skillet was a terrible tradition.”

## Page 5 - A Purpose That Failed

A clear four-panel emotional page in the grassy clearing. The unarmored dark-brown wolf gestures helplessly toward Jet, the fallen lance, and his discarded armor. Symbolic inset images show the roles he names: knight against dragon and wolf hunting sheep. Jet lands gently and listens rather than attacking.

Caption text:

- “Without his armor or weapon, the wolf confessed that he no longer understood his purpose.”
- “Knights were supposed to kill dragons. Wolves were supposed to eat sheep.”
- “I was both, yet he could neither kill me nor consume me.”
- “He had not lost a battle. He had lost the story that told him who to be.”

## Page 6 - The Deeper Association

A clear four-panel mentorship page. Jet offers a hand and the skeptical wolf listens. Jet demonstrates black wool becoming cloud mist around his body. A visual chain of symbolic images connects sheep wool to clouds, clouds to the open sky, and the sky to flight. The huge moon begins rising behind the forest.

Caption text:

- “I offered to make him my apprentice and teach him how to fly.”
- “He objected that he had no wings. I explained that wings were only the surface.”
- “My sheep nature connected me to clouds, and clouds belonged to the sky.”
- “Power could come from the deeper meaning inside what we already were.”

## Page 7 - The Moon Answers

A clear four-panel transformation page. Jet asks the wolf what wolves are cosmically associated with. The dark-brown wolf looks toward the enormous moon and remembers wolves howling beneath it. He reaches upward; silver-blue moonlight flows down through him. His fur transforms progressively from dark brown to luminous pale white, and his feet lift from the ground without wings.

Caption text:

- “I asked what waited beyond the surface of wolfhood.”
- “He looked to the moon—the ancient light that wolves had always answered with a howl.”
- “He chose that connection, and silver moonlight poured through his dark-brown fur.”
- “Pale, radiant, and still wingless, my apprentice rose into the air.”

## Page 8 - Beyond the Surface

A clear four-panel resolution page ending in a large final panel. The moonlit pale-white wolf initially wobbles, then learns to glide. Jet flies beside him through black cloud trails and warm ember light. The final panoramic panel shows the contrasting pair soaring above the medieval forest: black sheep-dragon mentor and luminous wingless wolf apprentice, both joyful and free.

Caption text:

- “His first flight began as a startled stumble into the sky.”
- “Then the moonlit wolf found his balance and flew beside me.”
- “Predator and prey, knight and dragon—none of those surface roles could contain us.”
- “We became what we chose by embracing the deeper cosmos within who we already were.”
