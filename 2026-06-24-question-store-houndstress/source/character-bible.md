# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie, black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and black cross-body belt bag. Carries the Life Sword, a rainbow futuristic crystal sword with bright prismatic facets, and a golden glittering rope-like energy tether. He is curious, calm under pressure, compassionate after conflict, and visually framed by purple starry Lucid Light when he uses dream power.

## The Question Store Houndstress

Woman behind the Question Store counter with a round face, freckles, large round green eyes, and an exposed visible brain at the top/front of her head. A living orange golden retriever grows from her hair like a loyal second creature, with soft ears, alert expression, and warm orange fur. Her normal form feels odd, smart, welcoming, and bookish. Her Hell Houndstress form keeps the same face, freckles, green eyes, exposed brain, and golden retriever hair-creature, but adds crimson demonic aura, ember highlights, sharp shadow shapes, and extremely long telekinetic hair tendrils. Her emotional range moves from helpful pride to desperate obsession to quiet gratitude.

## Life Sword

Jet's rainbow futuristic crystal sword. It can create prismatic shields, cut through demonic shadow without gore, and release healing resurrection light. It should look like the same object on every page: angular crystal blade, rainbow spectrum glow, bright white core, and radiant prism flares.

## Question Store

A cozy surreal shop devoted to questions. Shelves hold glowing question marks sealed in jars, floating index cards, blank notebooks, hourglass lamps, and question-shaped neon signs. The counter is polished dark wood with brass trim. The store should feel whimsical and philosophical before the demonic reveal, then gain crimson shadows and moving hair tendrils during the conflict, and finally return to warm gold and green light.

## Dream Camera

A compact silver instant camera created by Jet inside the dream. Its lens glows with purple Lucid Light. Printed photos reveal hidden truths; the photo of the Houndstress shows her Hell Houndstress aura and demonic hair.
