# Treatment

## Logline

After accidentally separating a giant slime monster's soul, lucid dreamer Jet purifies it into an ocean guardian, becomes living liquid metal to defeat a fiery rival, and joins flying superhero chip reviewers for a honey-salt-and-spice feast.

## Central conflict

Jet's powers repeatedly produce transformations he did not expect. His attempt to evaporate a slime monster releases its living soul, while his metallic defense against a rival melts his entire body into liquid metal. Each crisis tests whether Jet can understand the new form quickly enough to turn danger into a constructive solution.

## Theme

True strength is creative adaptation: a frightening transformation can become a cure, a defense, or even a new friendship when someone understands what it has made possible. Heroism also needs room for harmless delight after the battles are over.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins exhilarated by flying alone through a superhero city. He becomes alarmed when a simple attack releases a suffocating soul, then compassionate and triumphant when he purifies the monster rather than destroying it. Public victory attracts a proud rival, and Jet must remain inventive while his own body melts around her. After proving his strength a second time, he is initially baffled by heroes devoted to potato-chip reviews, but one extraordinary taste replaces suspicion with delighted understanding and companionship.

## Continuity

This dream belongs to the Storyverse continuity that began on June 11, 2025. Jet retains his established dark-brown skin, full beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped black pants, white sneakers, black belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden energy tether, and sparkling purple star-filled Lucid Light. In this dream he calls the rainbow crystal sword the Life Sword because he uses it to purify and restore a living soul.

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper remain Jet's established crew, but Jet explicitly arrives alone. They do not appear physically in this comic.

## Ending

Jet flies into a glowing sunset beside the friendly chip-reviewing superhero team. Instead of another enemy, the final image shows everyone happily sharing honey-salt-and-spice tortilla chips high above the peaceful city while the purified blue slime guardian protects the ocean in the distance.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: Jet between the purified ocean slime, the flaming crimson-haired rival, and the airborne chip reviewers.
2. Jet induces lucidity, arrives alone, and confronts a giant blue slime monster attacking the city.
3. Jet's evaporation attempt releases a living gaseous soul that tries to suffocate him before he forces it back out.
4. Jet purifies the soul with the Life Sword, restores the monster, and relocates it to become a good ocean guardian.
5. The victory makes Jet the number-one hero, prompting a crimson-haired rival to attack; metal armor answers her boxing strikes.
6. Her fire melts Jet into living liquid metal, but he surrounds and restrains her before safely reforming himself.
7. Jet meets a flying superhero team devoted entirely to reviewing potato chips and doubts their strange purpose.
8. One perfect honey-salt-and-spice tortilla chip wins Jet over, and he spends the dream's remainder flying and eating with his new friends.
