Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in a Storyverse superhero world where almost every hero has been vaporized by The Liquifier. The survivors believe Liquifier can only become liquid, but Jet and the remaining phasing heroes discover the villain can change anyone they touch into another phase state. When Liquifier sneaks into the heroes' improvised HQ as gas, Ghost Cat, Leon, and Skelebot phase away, but Jet is vaporized. Jet keeps his consciousness as vapor, rises into the clouds where the lost heroes exist as gas, and turns Lucid Light into Lucid Lightning. The storm restores the world's heroic essence with each lightning bolt. The restored heroes Sucker and Void Man combine their powers: Sucker inhales Liquifier toward him with super breath, and Void Man jumps between them to trap Liquifier inside the pocket dimension in his chest.
