# Character Bible

## Jet / The Blue Skull

- Base identity: man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Everyday outfit: black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag.
- Blue Skull superhero form: the same Jet, now wearing a luminous blue crystal skull helmet like a transparent sapphire skull over his head; the helmet glows with psychic circuitry and ancient chip-like facets.
- Weapons and powers: shuriken, psychic machine control, world-spanning drone possession, fading and returning purple Lucid Light.
- Silhouette: athletic heroic stance, dreadlocks spilling behind or beneath the helmet, one hand lifted toward machines as blue psychic energy arcs outward.
- Expression range: focused wonder, stunned defeat, gritty survival, renewed lucid command, disappointed awakening.

## Enemy Assassins

- Rival assassins wearing varied dark tactical ninja-urban armor with red, gray, and black accents.
- They fight in groups and surround Jet from rooftops, party balconies, alley shadows, and zone portals.
- Weapons: shuriken, short blades, garrote cords, smoke bombs. They must never look identical to Jet.

## Captive Friend

- A warm, vulnerable civilian friend in bright party clothes, held hostage during the ambush.
- Must read as a noncombatant caught in danger, not a superhero.

## Squirrel-Dog Hybrid Dancer

- A whimsical squirrel and dog hybrid with expressive eyes, fluffy curled tail, floppy ears, and party-performer energy.
- Appears only on the party page as a strange dream-zone companion who dances with Jet for money.

## Drones

- Small hovering surveillance and utility drones with silver shells, blue optic lenses, and glowing signal trails.
- In the final lucid sequence, multiple drones across varied global zones show Jet's consciousness jumping between bodies.

## World Zones

- Storyverse entrance: cosmic void filled with bubble universes and purple starry Lucid Light.
- Blue Skull origin: neon occult-tech chamber where the blue crystal skull helmet awakens like an ancient computer chip.
- Lost battle aftermath: fractured battlefield with shattered machines and fading blue psychic arcs.
- Assassin guild zone: night rooftops, paper lanterns, angular alleys, shuriken trails, and hostile ambush geometry.
- Party rescue zone: crowded bright party interior with a hostage crisis, music lights, improvised fistfight, and one lucky shuriken throw.
- Retreat zone: log cabins, large swimming pools, giant baked goods, beautiful dreamlike resort guests, and the helmet reactivating.
- Drone mindscape: map-like world montage of cities, deserts, oceans, mountains, and machines linked by blue psychic signal paths.
