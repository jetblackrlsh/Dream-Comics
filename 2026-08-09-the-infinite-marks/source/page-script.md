# Page Script - The Infinite Marks

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, Silonus, the bartender, the Cloud Tavern, the official equipment, and the crown-shaped Marks follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

Jet stands in the Cloud Tavern raising Silonus, its rainbow crystal facets crossed by neon-green circuitry. A rainbow shield repels the bartender's reaching hand while countless crown-shaped Marks spiral from an emerald scanner. Visible cover text only: "THE INFINITE MARKS" and "AUGUST 9, 2026." No issue number.

## Page 2 - Above the Clouds

Jet flies above a nighttime city, ascends through luminous clouds, discovers the floating Cloud Tavern, and sees its mixed heroic culture.

Caption text:

- "I usually descended toward a city to patrol its streets, but this time I flew higher."
- "Beyond the clouds, I discovered a gathering place between realms called the Cloud Tavern."
- "Superheroes shared its tables with armored adventurers, elves, magical warriors, and benevolent demons."
- "A dwarf tested his strength against a gigantic costumed powerhouse."
- "Nearby, a magical superhero studied the terms of a pact beside a demonic guild hero."

## Page 3 - Doors, Missions, and Marks

The three doors, emergency screens, mission traffic, return scanner, green-grid equipment, and crown-shaped currency establish the tavern's system.

Caption text:

- "Screens throughout the tavern announced emergencies in distant realms."
- "The center door was the ordinary entrance I had used after flying through the clouds."
- "The right door carried heroes directly to missions, while the left admitted only those returning from completed work."
- "Returning heroes placed their weapons and equipment beneath a magical scanner."
- "Every registered item carried the same glowing neon-green circuit grid, whether it was a medieval sword or futuristic technology."
- "The system converted recorded accomplishments into crown-shaped golden currency called Marks."

## Page 4 - The Unregistered Sword

The bartender dismisses Silonus, tries to take it, offers an official green-grid sword, and is stopped by Jet's rainbow crystal shield.

Caption text:

- "At the bar, an attendant noticed Silonus and dismissed it as unregistered equipment."
- "They reached for the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword and offered to replace it with a 'real' tavern blade."
- "Without official tracking technology, they claimed Silonus could never earn Marks."
- "I immediately rejected the offer and raised a rainbow crystal force field between their hand and my sword."
- "Silonus was not a worthless blade to surrender for someone else's approval."

## Page 5 - Silonus Adapts

The red hilt gem scans the official sword and integrates its neon-green achievement circuitry into Silonus without changing the crystal sword's identity.

Caption text:

- "The attendant had overlooked that Silonus contained futuristic technology as well as magic."
- "A red gem in its hilt scanned the circuitry running across the tavern sword."
- "Silonus did not surrender its identity. It adapted the new technology into its own crystal structure."
- "Neon-green circuits spread across every rainbow facet."
- "The Life Sword could now speak the Cloud Tavern's language of heroic accomplishment."

## Page 6 - The Backlog

At the return counter, the adapted sword imports Jet's previous accomplishments and overwhelms the scanner.

Caption text:

- "The people at the return counter expected a newly upgraded weapon to contain no history."
- "They did not understand that Silonus had copied more than the ability to count from that moment forward."
- "It had adapted the system so my previous accomplishments could also be recognized."
- "Silonus had generated a backlog."
- "When the scanner read the sword, its display expanded beyond every finite total the tavern could contain."

## Page 7 - Protecting the Storyverse

Jet explains that repeatedly shielding Silonus from evil potentially protects every bubble universe in the Storyverse.

Caption text:

- "No one believed I could have defeated or captured an infinite number of villains."
- "They were measuring my accomplishments incorrectly."
- "Silonus is so powerful that keeping it away from evil protects far more than one person, city, or realm."
- "Each time I defend the sword, I potentially defend the entire Storyverse."
- "I have protected Silonus from those who want to take it almost constantly."
- "Translated into the tavern's system, that multiversal responsibility became an effectively infinite number of Marks."

## Page 8 - The Record to Prove It

The bartender backs down, the heroes recognize Jet's experience, and the three doors open behind Jet and Silonus toward countless realms.

Caption text:

- "My reputation inside the Cloud Tavern changed almost instantly."
- "I had entered as an unknown newcomer who did not understand its doors, equipment, or currency."
- "Now its heroes understood that I was anything but inexperienced."
- "I did not need to exchange Silonus for a weapon the tavern considered real."
- "The Rainbow Crystal Sword had adapted without becoming anything less than itself—and it carried the record to prove what we had protected together."
