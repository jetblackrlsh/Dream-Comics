Dream Journal 05/29/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025.

My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I. My Ghostly UFO, named Leon, resembles a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025. Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit does not have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025. Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds. Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color with spiky slicked back yellow hair and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025. Lucid Light is a reality warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered the power of Lucid Light in a dream on 07/19/2025. Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah, her father Tecton, and my drone Chipper all met me in early 2026 while adventuring through the Super Cathedral. Savannah is a pale skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that covered her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She is wielding a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword. Tecton is a green skinned, humanoid, muscular, cat ogre, barbarian who dresses in a brown loin cloth and a silver baldric. Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, and warm brown eyes. I have a rainbow sword that resembles a colorful futuristic crystal sword, and an energy tether that resembles a rope made of golden glittery light.

I am alone in the Storyverse.

A dog infected with corrupted Lucid Light attacks me.

The beast is the size of a lion and just as ferocious.

It chases after me.

Thinking fast, I create a transformation watch out of Lucid Light designed to allow me to transform into Lucid Light variants of my crew.

Using the watch I transform into a Lucid Light variant of Skelebot.

For brevity I refer to these forms as LLV-Character-Name.

Using the speed of LLV-Skelebot, I am able to outpace the beast.

But the beast uses its Lucid Light to warp forward with a burst of speed, catching me off guard and knocking me to the ground.

Using the watch I quickly transform into LLV-Tecton.

Using LLV-Tecton's ability to summon and telekinetically manipulate cybernetic weapons, I create a floating construct prison made up of cyber axes to trap the beast.

The beast unleashes a mega bark shockwave that blows the construct apart and knocks me back once again.

I realized that I needed a power that could not be easily resisted physically.

Using the watch I quickly transform into LLV-Leon.

Using LLV-Leon's tractor beam ability, I project a tractor beam to lift the beast off the ground and hold it in place.

The beast without traction simply flops around in mid-air, giving me the perfect opportunity.

Using my energy tether, I connect the beast to my rainbow crystal sword.

The purifying energies of the sword connect to the beast via the tether and cleanse the beast of its corrupted Lucid Light.

The beast's corruption manifests as a separate entity, but luckily Storyverse Animal Control arrives in time to detain it.

I really like the Lucid Light Watch, and I will probably use it in future adventures.

It lets me travel solo while still embracing the power of my crew.
