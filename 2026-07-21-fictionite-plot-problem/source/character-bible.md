# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword secured across his back and a rope made of golden glittering light clipped at his belt. They remain present for continuity but are never drawn, raised, or used as weapons.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy shining like a night sky full of stars. It surrounds his hands when he imagines Fictionite and expands through the museum when he summons the birthday party.
- Jet never wears a cape, mask, conventional superhero costume, armor, or helmet.
- Expression range: lucid concentration, aerial freedom, intellectual curiosity, unbothered calm, patient explanation, dry amusement, playful confidence, relaxed satisfaction.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, visible shirt, striped pants, white sneakers, belt bag, secured sword, and tether whenever visible.

## Lead robotic biker

- A highly advanced masculine humanoid robot with a brushed gunmetal face and body, segmented jaw, expressive cyan optical eyes, and narrow antenna-like fins swept back from his temples.
- Wears a black futuristic leather motorcycle jacket with electric-cyan piping and chrome studs, dark armored riding pants, heavy boots, and fingerless black gloves over mechanical hands.
- His broad black hoverbike has thick glowing cyan wheel rims, chrome turbine details, and angular handlebars. It may rest amid wall debris after the crash.
- Begins with the swaggering, wide-legged posture of a gang leader, but his shoulders and hands progressively soften as he becomes confused, thoughtful, and finally friendly.
- Holds the Fictionite crystal carefully rather than absorbing it or gaining a new transformation. The crystal never changes his design.
- Continuity rule: keep the same gunmetal face, cyan eyes, temple fins, black-and-cyan jacket, riding clothes, and cyan hoverbike throughout pages 1 and 3-8.

## Robotic biker gang

- Four to six supporting advanced humanoid robots with varied chrome, bronze, and black metal heads, each wearing a distinct black leather motorcycle jacket and futuristic riding gear.
- Their hoverbikes use neon cyan or magenta glowing wheel rims. They appear as a threatening gang during the crash, then remain increasingly awkward in the background as the philosophical heist unravels.
- They do not speak, transform, fire weapons, or become party guests in the final close-up unless visible casually at the edge of the larger celebration.

## Purple-haired woman

- Tall adult woman with a narrow expressive face, light lavender skin, dramatic violet eye makeup, and extremely tall feathery purple hair rising in a huge soft plume at least as tall as her head and upper torso combined.
- Wears an elegant sleeveless dress constructed from overlapping pearlescent blue fish scales, with aqua, cobalt, and pink highlights, along with dark blue high heels and long iridescent gloves.
- Moves like a theatrical storybook villain: arched back, raised chin, clawed hands, broad evil grin, and exaggerated cackle. Her confidence becomes visible nervousness, sweating panic, and finally a desperate sprint.
- She briefly holds Fictionite aloft, but the crystal never grants her a costume change, aura, weapon, or transformation.
- Continuity rule: her towering feathery purple hair and pearlescent fish-scale dress are indispensable identifiers on pages 1 and 6-7.

## Fictionite crystal

- A rare fist-sized asymmetrical faceted crystal resting on a small black museum pedestal inside a clear glass case.
- Its translucent core shifts through luminous violet, cyan, hot pink, and gold, while tiny impossible images—stars, comic panels, miniature castles, and spiraling galaxies—seem suspended inside it.
- Emits a subtle prismatic glow but causes no beams, transformations, portals, attacks, or powers at any time.
- A museum placard may be visible as an abstract design, but it must contain no extra readable text.
- Continuity rule: the same fist-sized multicolor asymmetrical crystal passes from the case to the biker, to the woman, and finally lies untouched on the museum floor.

## Museum and city

- The unfamiliar city is a bright vertical metropolis combining recognizable glass towers, elevated transit, rooftop gardens, flying vehicles, and impossible floating architectural sections.
- The grand science-and-imagination museum has polished dark-blue floors, cream stone walls, huge arched windows, display cases, suspended cosmic models, dinosaur-like fictional skeletons, and gold architectural trim.
- The bikers create one jagged wall opening edged with rubble and daylight. It remains visible through the party scenes.
- The birthday transformation fills the central gallery with brightly colored balloons, streamers, party tables, an enormous chocolate ice cream cake covered in fluffy white frosting, a separate smaller matching cake for Jet, and a joyful diverse crowd.

## Birthday crowd

- A diverse group of ordinary and fantastical Storyverse city residents in colorful contemporary and imaginative clothing, all appearing suddenly but safely around party tables.
- Their expressions are delighted and celebratory. They hold plates, party hats, or balloons, but do not obscure Jet, the biker, the fleeing woman, the fallen crystal, or the cakes.

## Established crew continuity

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's Storyverse team. None appear physically in this comic because Jet arrives alone. Do not insert them into the city, museum exhibits, biker gang, crowd, reflections, or background cameos.
