Dream Journal 06/24/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025. My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I. My Ghostly UFO, named Leon, resembles a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025. Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit doesn't have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025. Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds. Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color with spiky slicked back yellow hair and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025. Lucid Light is a reality warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered the power of Lucid Light in a dream on 07/19/2025. Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah, her father Tecton, and my drone Chipper all met me in early 2026 while adventuring through the Super Cathedral. Savannah is a pale skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that covered her forehead, glowing red eyes, a futuristic silver bodysuit, and a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword. Tecton is a green skinned, humanoid, muscular, cat ogre, barbarian who dresses in a brown loin cloth and a silver baldric. Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, and warm brown eyes. I have a rainbow sword, which resembles a colorful, futuristic crystal sword, and an energy tether, which resembles a rope made of golden glittery light.

I arrive alone at the Question Store.

The woman working at the Question Store is a Houndstress.

Houndstresses are women who have a dog growing out of their hair.

The Houndstress working behind the counter specifically has an orange golden retriever growing out of her hair.

I am amazed by her appearance as she has a round face, large round green eyes, freckles, and an exposed brain.

She explains to me that she must be smart in order to help customers find new questions and answer new questions.

I ask her about the cost and she says that the store offers its services for free.

She helps me think through questions about how to spice up my dreams, which leads me to create a camera and start taking pictures.

Taking photos within a dream is actually quite fun, and I am surprised that I haven't done that before.

A photo of the Houndstress reveals that she actually is a Hell Houndstress with demonic power.

She controls her hair, making it grow longer and move telekinetically.

She tries to steal my rainbow crystal sword, the Life Sword, so that she can figure out the answer to the ultimate question: What is the meaning of life?

I can't allow for the Life Sword to fall into the wrong hands.

I use its shielding abilities to defend against her hair strikes, and then I use the sword to both slay and resurrect the Houndstress.

As she returns to life thanks to the Life Sword's healing power, I explain to her that life is fleeting and that anyone can die at any time.

I also explain that there are powers greater than death like the Life Sword that can bring people back to life.

Death is not permanent and yet neither is life.

We focus a lot on how fleeting life is and on how death is eternal, but in reality what matters about your life is what you do with it.

She recognizes that, despite being a demon, she doesn't have to spend her life being a monster.

She truly enjoys running the Question Store and she promises not to use her demon powers to harm people.

With her demon side tamed, she takes a moment to simply enjoy her second chance at life.

This dream was very philosophically satisfying and visually interesting.
