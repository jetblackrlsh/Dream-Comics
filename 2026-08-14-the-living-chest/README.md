# 08/14/2026 - The Living Chest

Dream Comics entry for August 14, 2026.

Jet's frustration with illogical fantasy dungeons inspires a Storyverse world where the dungeon has a real history: it was once a monster zoo whose failed magical wards forced its owners to bury the entire attraction underground. The zoo's insured earnings remain trapped inside with its escaped monsters, and a bald insurance executive recruits Jet to recover the gold.

Rather than charge into the darkness, Jet drives Silonis into the earth and sends life energy toward the treasure. The pulse brings the chest to life—but alerts both the dragon clutching it and a mole monster tunneling toward Jet. Jet solves the impossible race by fusing with the living chest, transforming into a man of wood and gold before sealing the cave with a portal that endlessly returns intruders to the side they entered.

The finished package includes the complete journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, and assembled PDF.
