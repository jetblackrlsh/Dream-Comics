After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet arrives alone in Mall World with augmented-reality sunglasses and a handheld touchpad. He loses lucidity inside an infinite filthy bathroom, but regains it when an alligator erupts from the floor and grows into the magma kaiju Magmagator. A yellow-and-blue superhero suit materializes around Jet, yet he cannot defeat the monster alone. His purple-skinned “evil twin” crashes into the mall and helps him win. The twin explains that both of them are aliens sent to Earth to fight monsters born from future industrial pollution, and that the twin is not evil so much as indifferent to a planet that is not their home. Jet chooses to remain and investigate the polluting companies and the resistance opposing them. A resistance woman aggressively comes on to him, and Jet’s alarm wakes him before he can learn more.
