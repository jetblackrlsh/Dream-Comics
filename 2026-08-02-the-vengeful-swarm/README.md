# 08/02/2026 - The Vengeful Swarm

Dream Comics entry for August 2, 2026.

After killing a roach before bed, Jet enters a lucid dream where the ghosts of countless insects flood toward him seeking vengeance. He uses Lucid Light to compress the entire swarm into one six-to-seven-foot humanoid opponent, but the creature possesses the combined strength, speed, durability, instincts, and hive intelligence of countless species.

The ghost-bug humanoid rapidly constructs an inhibitor chip, implants it in the back of Jet's neck, and disables his Lucid Light. Repeated awakenings return Jet to the same struggle until he realizes that the chip has not taken away his independent tools.

Jet summons Psilones, his living rainbow crystal Sword of Life. Acting on its own, Psilones pierces the inhibitor and brings the chip itself to life. The device grows tiny mechanical legs, crawls free, and restores Jet's power.

Instead of retaliating, Jet recognizes that the insects believe he wronged them by killing them. Drawing upon his history as an ordained minister and mystical practitioner, he blesses the entire collective. Its anger dissolves, the humanoid separates into thousands of peaceful spirits, and the ghost bugs pass into the afterlife.
