# 08/18/2026 - The Device Beyond Time

Dream Comics entry for August 18, 2026.

Inside his magical study, Jet investigates Adapt Tech: a handheld future device that scans ordinary technology, copies its functions, and assimilates with magical crystals. When a standalone unit attempts to scan Silonis, the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life, both the device and Jet erupt into a blue-brown-green crystalline glitch.

Jet shuts the experiment down and discovers the rule that makes crystal swords special: Adapt Tech can copy technology, but magic must be joined through assimilation. The revelation triggers involuntary spirit-hops through women using Adapt Tech in different eras, leading Jet to suspect that the technology itself connects users, thoughts, bodies, and identities across time.

The finished package includes the complete journal source, treatment, character bible, page script, eight generated comic pages, and assembled PDF.
