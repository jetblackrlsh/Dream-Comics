# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether, though this issue centers on his transformed hands. Jet can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, black pants with white stripe, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. For this dream, both hands have been replaced with smooth green metal stumps at the wrist. From those stumps he manifests prosthetic hands made of transparent neon green holo light. The prosthetics can become shears, a machete blade, a soundwave blaster, hooked claws, and rocket thrusters. Keep the green metal stumps visible at the base of each prosthetic form.

## Green Holo-Light Prosthetics

Transparent neon green constructs generated from Jet's green metal wrist stumps. They look like luminous holographic machinery, with crisp angular edges, glowing circuit lines, and faint particles. The forms are summoned by focus and can instantly reshape.

Continuity notes: Every prosthetic form should remain green holo light connected to the same green metal stump base. The shears form has oversized glowing scissor blades for cutting vines. The machete form is a wide single blade for cutting through attacks. The soundwave blaster form is a compact cannon that emits green concentric sonic rings. The hook form has two large curved claw hooks used for prying. The rocket form becomes twin green thrusters with white-green exhaust.

## Giant Spiders

Large jungle spiders with dark emerald-black armored bodies, many glowing green eyes, long angular legs, and stylized crystal-like fangs. They should feel dangerous and alien, but the comic should avoid graphic gore.

Continuity notes: When spiders are defeated, show stylized bursts of green energy, cracked chitin fragments, smoke, and motion effects rather than realistic gore.

## Queen Spider

Massive queen spider, far larger than Jet, with a crown-like carapace, thick armored legs, glowing green and violet eyes, enormous mandibles, and a cavernous mouth. It drops from the jungle canopy like a falling boulder and can counter Jet's soundwave blaster with a powerful screech.

Continuity notes: Keep the queen spider enormous across pages 6 and 7. Its screech should be visible as jagged violet sound rings overpowering Jet's green sonic rings. Its defeat should be symbolic and stylized: the mouth stretches with green hook light, then the queen splits into dark armored halves with bright energy fracture lines, not realistic gore.

## Storyverse Jungle

Thick overgrown tropical jungle inside the Storyverse, full of huge leaves, tangled vines, towering trees, luminous flowers, mist, insects, and shafts of gold sunlight. The canopy is dense enough to feel like a trap at ground level, but from above it becomes a peaceful world full of life.

Continuity notes: Start with claustrophobic greens, heavy vines, and close framing. Gradually open the environment through the clearing, sky, and final aerial view. Lucid Light may appear as faint sparkling purple star-energy during the sleep-paralysis entry, but the main issue color motif is neon green holo light against lush jungle.
