# Page Script - The Device Beyond Time

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, Adapt Tech, Silonis, the temporal users, and all recurring environments follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic full-page composition in the magical study. Jet holds the red-lit T-shaped Adapt Tech device as blocky blue-brown-green crystalline corruption spreads across one side of his body. Silonis stands beside him with its faceted rainbow blade. Behind Jet, four distinct women from different eras hold matching devices while violet-white temporal threads bind their moments into one impossible timeline.

Visible cover text only: “THE DEVICE BEYOND TIME” and “AUGUST 18, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - The Hilt Was a Device

A clear four-panel page. Jet studies Silonis inside the magical study. Close views isolate its technological hilt, T-shaped profile, and red scanner. Holographic diagrams separate the Adapt Tech unit from the Rainbow Crystal blade. Jet realizes the object may not have been created only as a sword hilt.

Caption text:

- “This dream began in my magical study, where I questioned why crystal-sword hilts were always called future technology.”
- “The T-shaped handle had a red scanner and could function as a complete device on its own.”
- “It was called Adapt Tech: technology designed to learn from whatever surrounded it.”
- “If it could become almost any machine, perhaps this ‘sword hilt’ had never been made only for swords.”

## Page 3 - An Everything Device

A clear four-panel demonstration page. Adapt Tech scans a laptop and projects its functions, scans a Wi-Fi router and becomes a glowing network hub, then assimilates seamlessly with a magical crystal housing. The final panel returns to Jet comparing the standalone unit with Silonis.

Caption text:

- “Adapt Tech could scan an ordinary machine, explain it, and copy its functionality.”
- “One device could learn from a laptop, a router, or nearly any technology nearby.”
- “It could also assimilate into existing objects—even magical crystals—and adapt both systems to work together.”
- “That raised one dangerous question: if it could copy any function, why did Silonis still need a physical Rainbow Crystal blade?”

## Page 4 - The Scan That Failed

A clear four-panel experimental page. Jet forms a second unattached Adapt Tech unit from sparkling purple Lucid Light. He aims its red scanner at Silonis's rainbow blade. The scan beam touches the crystal and fractures into blocky blue-brown-green artifacts. The last panel shows the corruption leaping from the device onto Jet's arm.

Caption text:

- “Using Lucid Light, I created a second Adapt Tech device identical to Silonis’s hilt, but without a crystal.”
- “I aimed its red scanner at the Rainbow Crystal blade and ordered it to copy the magic.”
- “The device did not learn the crystal’s power.”
- “Instead, the failed scan broke the boundary between the machine and me.”

## Page 5 - Magic Cannot Be Copied

A clear four-panel crisis-and-discovery page. Crystalline cubes and fractured facets spread over Jet while his body produces glitch afterimages. He fights through the distortion and shuts down the device. The corruption collapses and Jet returns to normal. The final panel shows a clean diagram of Adapt Tech assimilated with Silonis rather than scanning it.

Caption text:

- “Blue, brown, and green crystal-like blocks consumed the device and spread across my body.”
- “I forced Adapt Tech to shut down, and the corruption released me.”
- “Then its limit became clear: Adapt Tech could copy technology, but it could not scan magic.”
- “Magic had to be joined through assimilation. That partnership was what made crystal swords unique.”

## Page 6 - I Became Someone Else

A clear four-panel identity-shift page. Jet's awareness crosses a violet temporal thread into the ancient desert scholar. She becomes the point-of-view protagonist while studying Adapt Tech. The next panels transition into the Renaissance engineer and Edo-era researcher, each holding the same device and carrying Jet's subtle connected-eye glow.

Caption text:

- “Once I returned to normal, my awareness jumped involuntarily into a woman in another era.”
- “I became her so completely that I almost forgot the body and identity I had left behind.”
- “The next hop carried me into another woman, and then another.”
- “Every life was investigating, using, or being hunted for the same impossible future technology.”

## Page 7 - The Thread Across Eras

A clear four-panel trans-temporal page. The Edo-era researcher focuses on her device and dissolves into violet-white threads that return Jet to his body in the study. He is immediately pulled into the far-future technician helping people in a luminous city. The final panel reveals all devices connected through a vast star-filled timeline.

Caption text:

- “Whenever I focused on Adapt Tech, its connection pulled me back to my own body in the study.”
- “Then it hurled me into another user somewhere else in history.”
- “I was not choosing these journeys. The technology seemed to connect everyone who touched—or even contemplated—it.”
- “Maybe Adapt Tech was scattered through history because adapting meant escaping the boundaries of time.”

## Page 8 - The Device Beyond Time

A clear four-panel resolution page. Jet returns to the study and compares violet portal geometry with the web of Adapt Tech connections. The temporal women appear peacefully in their own eras, each linked but autonomous. Jet sets the standalone device beside Silonis and records his final realization beneath a vast holographic timeline that has no visible beginning.

Caption text:

- “The answer echoed what I had learned about Portal Crystals: connection could bridge not only places, but thoughts.”
- “Adapt Tech might never have been sent into the past. Every instance could belong to one system spread loosely across the timeline.”
- “It could copy machines, join with magic, and link identities across eras—but each power still obeyed a hidden rule.”
- “I ended the dream with more understanding, and a deeper mystery: who created a device that existed beyond when?”
