# Treatment - The Device Beyond Time

## Logline

When Jet's experiment forces a universal future device to scan magic, the resulting glitch tears at his body and identity—then reveals that Adapt Tech may connect every user across the entire Storyverse timeline.

## Premise

The dream opens inside Jet's magical study rather than with his usual superhero flight. He studies the T-shaped technological hilt of Silonis, the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life, and asks why the Storyverse consistently calls it future technology rather than merely advanced technology.

Jet identifies the device as Adapt Tech. Its red scanner reads ordinary machines, explains them, and copies their functionality. A single unit can become the equivalent of a laptop, router, or nearly any other device it encounters. It can also assimilate into an existing object, adapting its systems to work seamlessly with magical crystal.

## Central conflict

If Adapt Tech can copy any function, why does Silonis require a physical Rainbow Crystal blade? Jet uses Lucid Light to create a second, unattached Adapt Tech unit and commands it to scan the blade.

The device cannot interpret magic as ordinary information. Instead, it produces blocky blue, brown, and green crystal-like corruption that spreads across Jet's body while both he and the device glitch violently. Jet must shut the system down before the failed adaptation consumes his form.

## Discovery

Once normal again, Jet understands the rule. Adapt Tech can scan and reproduce technology, but it cannot copy magic. Magic must be joined through assimilation. The Rainbow Crystal blade is therefore essential, and the partnership among technology, living crystal, and magic is what makes a crystal sword extraordinary.

## Escalation across time

The experiment opens another mystery. Jet involuntarily spirit-hops into several women in different eras, each investigating, using, or being hunted for Adapt Tech. While inside each body, he identifies so fully with that person that he nearly forgets himself.

Concentrating on Adapt Tech repeatedly pulls Jet back to his own body in the study, only for another involuntary hop to carry him away again. The technology itself appears to be the connecting thread.

## Theme and ending

The dream develops the Storyverse principle that sufficiently powerful connection can treat places, thoughts, time, and identity as versions of distance. Portal Crystals connect locations and possibly minds; Adapt Tech may connect users and experiences across history.

Jet concludes that Adapt Tech may never have been conventionally sent into the past. Its adaptive nature may extend beyond ordinary dimensional limits, allowing every instance to participate in one trans-temporal system. He ends with a clearer understanding of the device's rules but a deeper question about its origin: who created technology that does not belong to a single time?
