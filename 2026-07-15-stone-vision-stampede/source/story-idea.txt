Stone Vision Stampede

Dream date: 07/15/2026

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrives alone in a nightclub occupied by multicolored gorgons whose bodies are made of slime. The gorgons boast that they ate the club's former golem and gargoyle patrons by dissolving their stone bodies. They explain that they have not fired one stone-vision blast at Jet because his rainbow crystal sword of life might restore him or reflect the beam. Instead, they surround him and prepare to fire from every direction at once. Jet realizes that they only understand the powers they can see. He summons a yellow anti-gravity force-field belt with sparkling purple Lucid Light, stretches his limbs to create a spherical shield, blocks the simultaneous stone beams, and rolls the shield through the club like a hamster ball to crush and defeat the slime gorgons. The nightclub then reopens for golems and gargoyles.
