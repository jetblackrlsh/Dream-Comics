# 08/21/2026 - The Moonlit Apprentice

Dream Comics entry for August 21, 2026.

Jet begins the dream transformed into a humanoid black sheep-dragon: a black-woolly were-creature with enormous batlike wings, fire breath, and a body that behaves like cloud mist. Above a wooded medieval-fantasy landscape, a humanoid wolf knight hurls his jousting lance at Jet. The weapon passes harmlessly through Jet's cloudlike wool, and Jet's answering flame turns the knight's metal armor into a scalding oven.

Unable to fulfill the roles he thought defined him—knights kill dragons and wolves eat sheep—the wolf loses his sense of purpose. Jet offers him an apprenticeship and teaches him to look beyond literal anatomy toward the deeper cosmic associations within his identity. Jet connects sheep to clouds and clouds to the sky. The wolf discovers his own connection to the moon, channels silver lunar energy through his dark-brown fur, transforms into a radiant pale-white moonlit form, and learns to fly without wings.
