# Treatment

## Title

06/22/2026: Tech-A-Ton Sky Base

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet arrives alone on a beach, survives a robo-hornet ambush without choosing violence, and earns the trust of Tech-A-Ton, a lonely circuit-gorilla robot who expected the world to reject him.

## Central Conflict

Jet is dropped into a hostile beach scenario where robot laser hornets attack before he can understand the world. He can fight, but he is not in the mood for battle. The hornets' lasers hurt him, and their cyber venom is meant to knock him unconscious, but Jet uses his rainbow sword to heal and his lucid awareness to interface with the venom instead of surrendering to it. The real challenge is not defeating the hornets. It is reaching their leader without fear, meeting Tech-A-Ton honestly, and proving that conversation can disarm a threat built from rejection and preemptive anger.

## Theme

Lucid strength is not only the power to win battles. Sometimes it is the power to stay awake, stay kind, and make room for someone who assumed they would be hated.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins calm but under attack, annoyed that the dream opens with violence when he wants a different kind of experience. The robo-hornets' cyber venom shifts the dream from pain to strange relaxation, and Jet turns that danger into technological attunement. When he reaches the sky base, Tech-A-Ton looks intimidating: a gorilla-shaped robot of exposed circuits and neon blue eyes. Jet's intuitive recognition of his name surprises TAT and breaks the expected pattern of fear. The conversation moves from suspicion to curiosity, then to trust. TAT admits that he attacked first because he could not imagine being accepted. Jet helps him see that people differ, opinions differ, and rejection should not be assumed before anyone has a chance to choose. The dream ends as a satisfying conversation instead of an epic battle.

## Ending

The robo-hornets stand down around the sky base while Jet and Tech-A-Ton watch the sky together through a wall of glass and open machinery. TAT is no longer hiding behind aggression, and Jet leaves with the satisfaction of having solved the dream by listening rather than fighting.

## Cast

- Jet: the lucid dreamer and protagonist. He is a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow futuristic crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether.
- Robo-Hornets: small robot laser hornets with chrome-and-black bodies, sharp mechanical wings, glowing red targeting eyes, needle-like stingers, and tiny laser emitters. They attack in swarms, fire red laser bolts, and inject glowing cyan cyber venom.
- Cyber Venom: a luminous cyan-and-violet circuitry fluid that tries to make Jet sleepy and relaxed. When Jet resists sleep, it becomes a visible network of holographic circuit lines across his hands, eyes, and sword glow, helping him interface with technology.
- Tech-A-Ton, or TAT: the robo-hornet leader. He is a gorilla-shaped robot made of exposed circuits, braided cable muscles, transparent armor plates, and glowing neon blue eyes. He is physically imposing but emotionally guarded, curious, and relieved when Jet does not fear him.
- Sky Base: a floating technological base above the clouds with glass floors, turbine rings, antenna spires, blue holographic consoles, hornet docking racks, and open walls looking down at the beach and ocean far below.
