The Vengeful Swarm

After killing a roach before bed, Jet enters a lucid dream where the ghosts of countless insects flood toward him seeking vengeance. He compresses the swarm into one powerful humanoid target, but its combined hive intelligence creates a chip that inhibits his Lucid Light and traps him in a repeating struggle. Jet finally calls upon Psilones, his living rainbow crystal Sword of Life, which brings the inhibitor chip itself to life so it crawls away. With his power restored, Jet recognizes that combat was the wrong answer: the insects are angry because they believe he wronged them. Drawing upon his history as an ordained minister and mystical practitioner, Jet blesses the entire swarm, releases its anger, and helps every ghost bug pass peacefully into the afterlife.
