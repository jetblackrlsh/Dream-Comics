# Page Script - The Snowfire Avatar

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, his robotic dream-avatar layer, the sanctuary, bonded flame, and fire demons follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic full-page composition in the snowy sanctuary. Jet stands in front of the purple couch and magical fire pit, holding bonded orange-gold flame in one bare hand. The left side of his recognizable face reveals sleek silver-black robotic plating without blood or gore. His mechanical eye fires a blue-violet laser into a towering frozen fire demon, splitting into rainbow rays that cascade through many ice-encased demons and ignite spectacular fireworks of ice, flame, and color across the night sky.

Visible cover text only: “THE SNOWFIRE AVATAR” and “AUGUST 19, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - A Living Room Without Walls

A clear four-panel page. Jet arrives in the snowy wilderness and settles onto the purple couch. The fire pit's amber aura holds snow and cold away from him. Close panels show his peaceful face and the warm sanctuary floating within the frozen forest. The final panel shows him thoughtfully considering role-playing games.

Caption text:

- “I wanted a new place to think, so my dream built a warm living room without walls in the middle of a frozen wilderness.”
- “A purple couch and a magical fire pit sheltered me while snow fell beyond an invisible boundary.”
- “As I rested there, I wondered why finding human role-play felt harder than playing whenever I wanted with AI.”

## Page 3 - Letters Into Silence

A clear four-panel introspective page. Dreamlike inset imagery around Jet shows an open online role-playing forum, highly specific wanted notices represented without readable UI text, and his character sending glowing paper letters into darkness. The final panel turns the floating unanswered letters into an inviting solo role-playing journal around him.

Caption text:

- “I imagined online role-play as a world where anyone could enter a scene and strangers could continue it together.”
- “Instead, I found wanted ads, specific requirements, and characters sending letters that might never receive answers.”
- “Maybe the letters did not need replies to matter.”
- “Writing them could become its own solo role-playing game.”

## Page 4 - Hell, Then Winter

A clear four-panel transformation page using the same camera geography. Jet questions the peaceful sanctuary, snaps his fingers, and the snow becomes a red-black hellscape while the couch and fire pit remain. Flaming demons charge. Jet snaps again; winter returns and instantly traps every demon in thick cobalt ice.

Caption text:

- “Then I questioned whether my sanctuary was truly different from the magical study—or merely cozy in another shape.”
- “With one snap, I replaced the snow with Hell, and flaming demons charged the purple couch.”
- “With another, I restored the winter.”
- “The cold flash-froze every demon, proving this wilderness could extinguish even the fires of Hell.”

## Page 5 - The Flame That Knew Me

A clear four-panel discovery-and-attack page. Jet studies the frozen demons and the fire pit. He scoops orange-gold fire into his bare hand without injury, then walks among the ice statues inside an amber aura. In the final panel his flame accidentally thaws one demon's face, which lunges and bites away the left human exterior of Jet's face; show no blood or gore, only clean robotic plating beneath.

Caption text:

- “Only the fire pit could keep me warm, which meant its flame was as extraordinary as the cold.”
- “It was bonded to me. I could hold it in my hand and carry its protection into the snow.”
- “The frozen demons became statues—until my flame passed too close to one face.”
- “Its head thawed. Before I could retreat, it bit away the surface of my face.”

## Page 6 - The Avatar Beneath

A clear four-panel revelation page. Jet recoils, then sees the sleek robotic layer beneath the left side of his face. A dreamlike inset contrasts his sleeping physical body far away with his conscious perspective piloting the standing avatar. He touches the metal face, considers its possibilities, and charges blue-violet diodes in the exposed eye.

Caption text:

- “There was no blood beneath my skin—only the face of a machine.”
- “The discovery felt strangely logical. My waking body was elsewhere; this dream body was an avatar I piloted.”
- “If the avatar was robotic, perhaps it had abilities I had never tested.”
- “I charged the diodes in my eyes and fired.”

## Page 7 - Every Demon Became a Prism

A clear four-panel action page. Jet's first eye laser strikes the thawed demon and refracts through its ice before detonating it in a non-gory burst. Jet deliberately aims at a central frozen demon. The beam splits repeatedly through the cluster, turning each ice shell into another prism while Jet stands protected by the flame.

Caption text:

- “The beam refracted through the demon's ice and detonated the frozen hellfire within.”
- “I aimed again—not at the nearest enemy, but at the center of the entire cluster.”
- “One ray split into many, and every frozen body became another prism.”
- “The wilderness transformed into a chain reaction of laser light, ice, color, and flame.”

## Page 8 - A Beautiful Explosion

A clear four-panel resolution page that expands into a large final splash panel. Rainbow laser paths connect frozen demons across the forest; they burst into fireworks of cobalt crystal, orange fire, magenta, cyan, and gold. Jet stands unharmed beside the purple couch and fire pit, bonded flame in one hand and the clean robotic layer still visible on his face, watching the snowy sky become a celebration.

Caption text:

- “The frozen forest erupted around me in prismatic explosions of ice, color, and fire.”
- “What began as a quiet place to think had become a celebration.”
- “My dream avatar stood unharmed inside the storm of light.”
- “That beautiful, explosive spectacle was the end of my dream.”
