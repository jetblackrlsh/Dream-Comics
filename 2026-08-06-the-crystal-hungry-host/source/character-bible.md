# Character Bible - The Crystal-Hungry Host

## Jet

- Dark-brown-skinned Black man with warm brown eyes.
- Full dark-brown beard and mustache.
- Long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Black hoodie over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt.
- Black athletic pants with a clean white side stripe, white high-top sneakers, black crossbody belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build; curious, compassionate, confident, and decisive when threatened.
- May fly with a subtle aura of star-filled purple Lucid Light when entering the Storyverse.

## Silonis, the rainbow crystal sword

- A large colorful futuristic crystal sword with a brilliant faceted rainbow blade.
- The blade shifts through red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, violet, and magenta crystal light.
- Angular silver-and-black crystalline guard with a warm gold core; dark practical grip.
- Always radiant and unmistakably made from magical crystal unless Jet temporarily cloaks it to look like plain gray metal.
- Never depict Silonis as a generic steel sword.

## Robotic dragon host

- Tall humanoid dragon-shaped android, elegant rather than bulky.
- Polished obsidian-chrome segmented mechanical scales and an angular draconic face.
- Long swept-back metal horns, cyan circuit-line eyes, articulated clawed hands, and a thin segmented tail.
- Tailored midnight-blue nightclub host jacket with sharp silver lapels over the mechanical torso.
- Three large magical stones embedded vertically across the upper chest: one glowing red, one glowing green, and one glowing blue.
- Initially poised, welcoming, and customizable; becomes single-minded and predatory only when its sensors identify edible magical crystal.
- When all three stones are removed, every light goes dark and the body becomes completely motionless.

## Organic dragon hosts

- A small ensemble of distinct humanoid dragons stationed behind illuminated lecterns with open magical tomes.
- Crimson fire dragon woman: ruby scales, swept gold horns, ember-orange eyes, elegant red-and-gold host attire.
- Cobalt ice dragon man: deep blue scales, silver horns, pale-blue eyes, tailored white-and-blue host attire.
- Jade storm dragon person: emerald scales, branching bronze horns, electric-violet eyes, dark green-and-purple host attire.
- Their powers and colors remain visually distinct, but all show the exhaustion and worry of workers losing customers.
- Dragons eat gold; they react to cups of molten gold as nourishing food rather than treasure.

## Leon

- Jet's ghostly flying saucer, named Leon.
- Smooth classic saucer silhouette with an ethereal translucent green glow.
- Appears above the nightclub and lowers several safe, heat-resistant cups filled with bright molten gold.

## Nightclub, VIP lounge, and Storyverse

- Host selection hall: lavish cosmic nightclub entrance, neon magenta and cyan lighting, polished black floor, floating signs, illuminated lecterns, open magical tomes.
- Main club: moving beams of violet light, crowded dance floor glimpsed in the background, luxurious futuristic fantasy decor.
- VIP lounge: private curved booth, dark velvet, gold trim, glowing crystal table, quieter cyan and purple light.
- Storyverse: cosmic void filled with many luminous bubble universes, purple-blue starlight, nebulae, and crystalline sparkles.

## Continuity rules

- Jet's face, skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, pants, shoes, and crossbody bag remain consistent on every page.
- The robotic dragon keeps the same obsidian-chrome body, midnight-blue jacket, cyan eyes, horns, tail, and red-green-blue chest stones until the stones are removed.
- Silonis keeps the same rainbow crystal blade and angular hilt except during the brief gray-metal illusion.
- The organic hosts keep their assigned colors, horns, eyes, and outfits.
- No speech bubbles, thought balloons, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, or creator signatures.
