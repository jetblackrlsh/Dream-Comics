# Treatment

## Logline

When a sea hag steals the Life Sword during Jet's alliance with the slime king of Atlantis against a pollution kaiju, Jet must reclaim his artifact, expose the hag's pirate scheme, and turn her own enchanted broom into the ally that defeats her remaining undead army.

## Central conflict

Jet faces two connected-looking disasters that are actually separate threats: a pollution kaiju born from environmental damage and a treasure-hungry sea hag who opportunistically steals the Life Sword. After stopping the public crisis, he refuses to accept the hag as an unexplained coincidence and follows the evidence into her treasure cove. There, cursed claws prevent him from relying on his most familiar weapons, forcing him to use Lucid Light in a new form and trust a magical bond with the hag's broom.

## Theme

Resourcefulness turns stolen power and enemy tools back toward protection. Clear thinking also matters as much as strength: understanding that the kaiju and hag have different motives is what allows Jet to solve the entire crisis rather than merely win one battle.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins exhilarated by solitary superhero flight and reassured by reuniting with a former ally. The hag's theft turns his hopeful purification plan into apparent catastrophe, but his tether lets him reclaim control and save the city. Curiosity then replaces triumph; Jet investigates instead of leaving the hag's motives unresolved. Inside the cove, he becomes isolated, injured, and badly outnumbered. His bond with the broom creates an unexpected partnership, giving him room to adapt, defeat the cursed crab without endangering his artifacts, and finish the danger completely.

## Continuity

This dream belongs to the Storyverse continuity that began on June 11, 2025. Jet retains his established dark-brown skin, full beard, long dreadlocks, black hoodie, visible Dream Anime shirt, striped black pants, white sneakers, black belt bag, rainbow crystal Life Sword, golden energy tether, and purple star-filled Lucid Light. The blue slime is the same cobalt creature Jet purified and moved to the ocean in the July 19, 2026 dream; as a shapeshifter and king of the sea, he now takes the form of a gigantic faceless blue humanoid with a crown-shaped head.

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper remain Jet's established crew, but Jet arrives alone and none of them appear physically in this comic.

## Ending

The enchanted broom finishes scattering the final skeletal pirates while Jet defeats the crab familiar's vulnerable molted form with Lucid Light claws. Jet then flies out of the opened treasure cove on the broom, carrying the protected Life Sword and tether. Below, the slime king stands watch over a clean ocean and the safe city, making the result of Jet's choices visible.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: Jet rides the crowned slime king toward the pollution kaiju while the sea hag, pirate skeletons, crab familiar, broom, Life Sword, tether, and Lucid Light claws frame the siege.
2. Jet induces lucidity, flies alone through the city, reunites with the slime king, and learns the pollution kaiju is beyond the king's strength.
3. Jet rides the enlarged slime king toward the monster, but a sea hag steals the raised Life Sword and resurrects skeletal pirates across the city.
4. Jet lassos the sword, shocks the hag loose with Lucid Light, drains the pirate army's stolen life, and destroys the kaiju with the supercharged Life Sword.
5. Jet investigates the hag's apparently unrelated arrival, bonds with her surviving broom, and follows it into a treasure cove that reveals her plan to expand an undead raiding army.
6. The hag's red crab familiar attacks with cursed claws; Jet protects his artifacts, summons Lucid Light claws, and is surrounded by the remaining skeletal pirates.
7. Jet calls the bonded broom, which raises a dust screen and batters the pirates while Jet heals and repeatedly heats the crab's shell with Lucid Light strikes.
8. The overheated crab molts and falls to Jet's final attack; the broom defeats the last pirates, and Jet returns above the safe city and sea with every threat resolved.
