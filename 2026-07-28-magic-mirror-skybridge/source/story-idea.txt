After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet visits a medieval fantasy town to discover whether magic can recreate modern artificial intelligence and robotics. He and a sky-blue-haired enchantress use life stones from a mine beneath the village to create intelligent magic mirrors, portable mirror companions, and working golems. Their success attracts Drillov, a futuristic thief who wants to manufacture and sell a life-based weapon, followed by three mountain-sized alien robot titans.

Because the enchanted mirrors and the alien machines use incompatible systems, Jet cannot hack the titans directly. He connects his pocket mirror to a cloud with the mystical Golden Energy Tether. The cloud becomes a storm, and lightning carries the mirror's magical information into one titan as an electrical signal. The hacked titan fights the other two until all three are destroyed. The village survives, but the discovery of its life-stone mine means it will need heroes to defend it from future invaders.
