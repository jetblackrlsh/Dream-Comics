# Character Bible - The Clockwork Wanderer

## Jet

- Adult Black man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears a black hoodie over a “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white side stripe, clean white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether only as continuity accessories; neither is used in this story.
- Calm, observant, concerned, inventive, and respectful of other people's autonomy.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy filled with tiny points of light like a starry night sky.
- Keep his skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, black outfit, white stripe, white shoes, and cross-body bag consistent whenever visible.

## The astronaut woman

- Adult woman with an athletic feminine build. Her face, hair, eyes, age, and identity remain completely unknown.
- Wears a slim, fitted, solid white hard-polymer astronaut suit. It resembles sleek armor but must never look like bulky fabric, puffed insulation, or heavy metal plate.
- Her helmet has a smooth, fully opaque black mirrored visor. No facial features may ever be visible through it.
- Glowing green slime-like spherical orbs are mounted in orderly rows along both arms and both legs. Their function remains unexplained.
- Cobalt-blue ringed gauntlets encircle both wrists. They generate circular blue energy fields that reflect incoming laser blasts back toward attackers.
- Initially has no visible gun, sword, or offensive weapon.
- Carries herself with quiet competence and complete emotional control even after losing her ship.
- She rejects rescue but accepts a tool that preserves her independence.
- Beginning on page 7, she wears a compact white-and-cobalt jetpack with gold mechanical details. Two swept mechanical wings unfold from its sides, and purple-white exhaust provides flight.
- Keep the suit, mirrored helmet, green orbs, cobalt gauntlets, and later winged jetpack identical across pages.

## Clockwork barbarians

- A group of adult superhuman women with vivid pink skin, glowing red eyes, extremely long wild white hair, muscular physiques, and fierce expressions.
- Wear practical black and dark-brown space-barbarian armor made from leather-like straps and salvaged metal pieces.
- Carry large black-and-bronze futuristic laser blasters that fire red energy bolts.
- Their raw strength is visually demonstrated by cracked gear plates beneath their feet and enormous leaps across the landscape.
- They are dangerous treasure hunters and ship thieves, but the story contains no gore.
- Keep the group visually consistent; three recurring barbarians are enough to communicate the threat clearly.

## Clockwork planet

- A planet-sized accumulation of obsolete analog spacecraft, ancient pirate vessels, broken hulls, and machinery drawn together by gravity.
- The surface is formed from interlocking bronze gears, gold mechanisms, black iron structures, riveted decks, enormous clock faces, and corridors made from fused ship interiors.
- Open gaps reveal deeper moving mechanisms and occasional views of the surrounding stars.
- Treasure chests contain old metallic credits and analog-era antiques.
- The environment should feel ancient, mechanical, cosmic, and coherent rather than like an ordinary steampunk city.

## Winged jetpack

- Manifested directly from Jet's sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Compact hard-polymer body that visually complements the astronaut's white suit, cobalt gauntlets, green orbs, and the planet's gold mechanisms.
- Two swept mechanical wings unfold during activation.
- Purple-white thrusters and small starry Lucid Light particles show that its power source is extraordinary.
- It grants mobility and escape options without automatically retrieving the astronaut's stolen ship or completing her mission.

## Continuity rules

- All eight pages use bright, high-saturation modern-anime comic art with crisp linework, intricate machinery, and cinematic cosmic lighting.
- Story pages use unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes only.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, signatures, gore, blank caption boxes, or decorative gibberish.
- Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from every outer edge.
- Jet never forces the astronaut to accept help and never joins her mission without invitation.
- The astronaut's face is never revealed.
- The green orbs' purpose is never confirmed.
- The barbarians flee on page 4 and do not return afterward.
- The astronaut has no jetpack before page 7 and keeps it through page 8.
