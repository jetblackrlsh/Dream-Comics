# Page Script - The Glimmer Prison

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, Silonis, the flying woman, the darkness, the living buildings, and the Glimmer follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

Crystal-fused Jet floats above a city while holding the darkness's bodiless soul in one radiant hand. Behind him, the possessed rainbow-crystal villain commands enormous living buildings, and a violet Porta portal opens onto the ghostly Glimmer. Visible cover text only: "THE GLIMMER PRISON" and "AUGUST 12, 2026." No issue number.

## Page 2 - Familiar Eyes

Jet meets the flying woman above the city. Her familiar eyes reveal the darkness.

Caption text:

- "While flying through the sky, I met a woman who seemed to come from another time."
- "Her braided hair and mustard jacket belonged to the 1940s or 1950s—but she was flying."
- "Her bluish-green eyes felt familiar."
- "When I said so, those eyes turned red and darkness spread across her face."
- "Even in another body, I could still recognize the enemy pursuing Silonis."

## Page 3 - The Fusion Gambit

Jet merges with Silonis and becomes a humanoid rainbow-crystal being. The darkness believes it has won.

Caption text:

- "I fused Silonis with myself until the Sword of Life and I became one rainbow-crystal being."
- "I told the darkness it could never take a sword that was no longer separate from me."
- "It called me a fool."
- "Possession was its greatest power, and I had placed the prize inside the body it planned to steal."
- "The darkness abandoned the woman and leaped into me."

## Page 4 - A Body for the Darkness

Jet's anticipated counterattack ejects the spirit and builds it a temporary crystal body.

Caption text:

- "But I had anticipated the invasion."
- "From inside the fusion, I fired Silonis's life energy through the possessing spirit."
- "The blast forced the darkness back out of me."
- "I wrapped the expelled energy around it and formed a separate humanoid rainbow-crystal body."
- "The darkness was confused—but delighted to possess a body like mine."

## Page 5 - The Buildings Awaken

Jet watches the darkness reveal its purpose by animating city architecture.

Caption text:

- "I waited to learn what the darkness truly wanted."
- "It poured life energy into the city."
- "Towers bent like giants. Windows opened as eyes. Streets shook beneath living walls."
- "The animated buildings rampaged through the city and attacked the people below."
- "Its first choice with the power of life was mass destruction."

## Page 6 - A Reversed World

The darkness explains its ideology while living buildings terrorize people.

Caption text:

- "The darkness already used people as homes for itself."
- "It believed buildings deserved lives of their own—and that human beings were disposable."
- "Homes would become the lives that mattered. People would become expendable objects."
- "I had given it freedom to reveal its purpose."
- "Now I knew I could not let that purpose continue."

## Page 7 - Into the Glimmer

Jet strips the life energy from the construct and banishes the bodiless spirit through Porta.

Caption text:

- "I siphoned the stolen life energy from its crystal construct."
- "Soon, only the darkness's bodiless soul remained in my hand."
- "The knowledge of a prison dimension called the Glimmer appeared in my mind."
- "I opened a Porta portal and cast the spirit into that ghostly realm."
- "With no physical bodies inside the Glimmer, there would be nothing for it to possess."

## Page 8 - Life Restored

Jet ends the building rampage, heals the injured people, and reflects on the fusion's value and remaining uncertainty.

Caption text:

- "The living buildings were still attacking the city."
- "While fused with Silonis, I drew the life energy out of them and ended their rampage."
- "Then I returned that energy to the injured people, healing everyone the buildings had harmed."
- "The abandoned crystal body might remain as a dangerous loose thread."
- "But I had learned that fusion with Silonis could turn possession itself against the possessor."
