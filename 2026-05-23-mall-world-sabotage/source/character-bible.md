# Character Bible

## Jet

- Identity: lucid dream explorer and Storyverse traveler; man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Outfit: black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Signature gear: rainbow sword shaped like a colorful futuristic crystal sword, and a golden glittering energy tether.
- Powers: uses Lucid Light, a sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a night sky full of stars.
- Body language: focused explorer posture, refusing to be pulled off course, hands raised to anchor reality when the dream destabilizes.
- Expression range: determined curiosity, irritation, strained focus, disorientation, fierce concentration.

## The Disruption Knight

- Identity: strange unfamiliar woman who claims she exists to disrupt Jet's routines.
- Appearance: adult woman with brown skin, freckles, green eyes, no nose, and spiky silver hair.
- Outfit: knight armor with a red chestplate and blue greaves; metallic shoulder and arm guards; no helmet so her spiky hair and noseless face remain visible.
- Body language: invasive, confident, pulling Jet by invisible force or grabbing his arm, always trying to turn him away from Mall World.
- Expression range: cool certainty, taunting calm, predatory satisfaction, frustration when Jet refuses to break.
- Visual motif: jagged red-and-blue armor geometry, silver hair spikes, green eyes glowing against rocky mountain wind.

## The Plant Hero

- Identity: original hero born from Jet's imagination, the one vision he is allowed to remember.
- Appearance: superhero with a humanoid body made of green plants, twisting vines, leaves, bark-like muscle fibers, glowing yellow-green eyes, and a living cape of broad leaves.
- Silhouette: protective and athletic, rooted stance, arms wrapped in vine shields, chest glowing with soft green life energy.
- Role: a remembered future ally, possibly able to defend Jet from disruptors like the knight.
- Visual motif: bright green vines, leaf armor, flowers and roots forming superhero shapes, warm life-energy glow.

## Mall World

- Exterior: a massive impossible mall at the base of rocky mountains, glowing entrances, endless glass walls, neon signs, parking lots fading into dream fog.
- Interior: endless atriums, escalators, bright storefronts, polished tile floors, unnatural depth, and a terrible invisible stench that warps the air and breaks focus.
- Mood: shared dream space, both familiar and cosmic, possibly governed by hidden rules.

## Storyverse

- Appearance: cosmic black void filled with floating bubble universes and sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Role: the larger continuity that carries Jet from sleep paralysis into Mall World, the mountain sabotage, and the hero-vision chamber.
