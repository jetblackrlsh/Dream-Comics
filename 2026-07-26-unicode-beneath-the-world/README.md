# 07/26/2026 - The Unicode Beneath the World

Dream Comics entry for July 26, 2026.

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis but loses it almost immediately, becoming the obedient son of a king inside a chaotic academy. While classmates riot around him, Jet builds a magnetic flying machine from spring-loaded coils and Morphium, the rainbow substance from his earlier lucid journeys. The invention nearly works, so he stores it in his bag instead of treating the attempt as a failure.

After Jet completes another thankless errand for his royal father and the king's aloof wife, they fall asleep without acknowledging him. Jet slips out through a guarded rear exit and separates himself from the rowdy students waiting outside. That act of independence changes his awareness. Unicode characters bleed through the scenery until the academy and everyone inside it vanish.

Jet finds himself seated at a terminal displaying the source beneath the dream. He understands that its continuously changing Unicode describes the academy, the king, the students, the flying machine, and perhaps even Morphium and Amnesium. He has reached a deeper threshold of lucidity: instead of reshaping the dream with Lucid Light, he may be able to rewrite reality at its source. His alarm wakes him an instant before his first edit.
