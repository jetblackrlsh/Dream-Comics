Dream Journal 06/09/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void.

I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025. My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I.

My Ghostly UFO, named Leon, resembles a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025.

Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit does not have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025.

Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds.

Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color with spiky slicked back yellow hair and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025.

Lucid Light is a reality warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered the power of Lucid Light in a dream on 07/19/2025.

Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah is a pale skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that covered her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She wields a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword.

Tecton is Savannah's father: a green skinned, humanoid, muscular, cat ogre barbarian who dresses in a brown loin cloth and a silver baldric.

Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot.

Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper all met me in early 2026 while adventuring through the Super Cathedral. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, and warm brown eyes. I have a rainbow sword, which resembles a colorful futuristic crystal sword, and an energy tether, which resembles a rope made of golden glittery light.

Alone, I created a new world in the Storyverse.

This new world is specifically designed to give me plenty to do.

It is divided into a few key sections: the portal station, the power armory, the leviathan lands, the robot printer, and the meditation alcove.

The portal station is where you spawn into the world and where you can travel to other worlds.

The power armory is where you can get different power armor suits that grant you unique abilities.

The leviathan lands are thick jungles full of monsters that you are allowed to go out and hunt using power armor.

The robot printer is where you can have a custom AI robot printed for you to accompany you on your adventures.

The meditation alcove is a private area where you can more easily build up and manipulate life energy.

I spent all of my time at the robot printer and in the meditation alcove.

I printed a bot named Goth Bot to accompany me.

Goth Bot is a gothic android with orange skin, curly shoulder length orange hair with black highlights, green eyes with purple eyeshadow, black lipstick, red freckles, an hourglass figure, and a black leather short dress with neon purple trim.

Goth Bot and I meditated in the peaceful alcove.

Thanks to the alcove's unique properties, even robots could channel and produce life energy.

While in the alcove she became more and more alive.

We cycled life energy through each other like a circuit, helping us form a powerful bond.

It was incredibly relaxing and allowed us to feel as if we were one being.

My lucidity drifted away from me, but when it returned I found myself in charge of a group of powerful heroes who needed to defend a home from a team of their greatest villains.

Despite the power of the heroes I was matched with, a lack of communication between team members led to a few of them being taken out during a test run before the villains even arrived.

A giant green humanoid squid was the only villain to get close enough to the house to be a real threat.

They burst through the wall of the house, but I was able to use my powers to rebuild and fortify the wall while pushing the giant back outside.

A big lesson from the dream is that regardless of how powerful your team is, without proper communication you cannot actually work together.

A true team is one that can work as one, like how Goth Bot and I were in sync back in the alcove.
