# Page Script — Life Sword: Sea Hag Siege

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation anime comic page. Captions must remain clearly separated, readable, long enough to make the action obvious, and nonredundant. Jet and every recurring design must follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 — Cover

One dramatic layered composition. Center foreground: Jet in his exact black hoodie, visible Dream Anime shirt, full beard, long dreadlocks, striped pants, white sneakers, and belt bag crouches on the right shoulder of the gigantic faceless cobalt-blue slime king. Jet raises the glowing rainbow crystal Life Sword while his golden tether spirals around him and one hand bears a star-filled purple Lucid Light claw. The slime king's head itself forms five crown points. Behind them, the black-green pollution kaiju towers from the harbor. Upper right: the blue-green sea hag dives on her crooked broom. Lower corners: skeletal pirates and the rune-clawed red crab familiar emerge from treasure and surf. Visible cover text only: “LIFE SWORD: SEA HAG SIEGE” and “JULY 20, 2026”. No issue number.

## Page 2 — The King Who Needed Help

Four panels. Jet lies conscious but immobilized during sleep paralysis in a dark bedroom, then rises through sparkling purple Lucid Light. A wide aerial panel shows him alone, in his exact outfit, flying joyfully through the bright coastal city. The gigantic faceless humanoid blue slime king with crown-shaped head rises from the harbor and greets Jet with a welcoming open palm. Final wide panel: both heroes face the much larger black-green pollution kaiju churning the ocean beside the city.

Caption text:

- “I induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and arrived alone in a coastal Storyverse city, where I began flying like a superhero.”
- “While exploring, I reunited with the blue slime creature I had once helped. He could shapeshift, and since our last meeting he had become the crowned king of Atlantis.”
- “His new form was a gigantic faceless blue humanoid, but even the king of the sea could not defeat the monster poisoning his waters.”
- “Pollution had mutated into a colossal kaiju, so my old ally and I prepared to confront it together.”

## Page 3 — The Life Sword Stolen

Five dynamic panels. The slime king grows even larger while Jet climbs onto his broad right shoulder. They stride through the harbor toward the kaiju. Jet raises the rainbow crystal Life Sword, which sends gentle rainbow-green purification light toward the polluted beast. The sea hag suddenly streaks past on her broom and snatches the sword from Jet's hand. She channels sickly green energy through the rainbow blade. Final wide panel: dozens of skeletal pirates claw up through seawater and cracked city streets while the kaiju continues advancing in the background.

Caption text:

- “The slime king grew even larger, and I climbed onto his shoulder so we could approach the pollution kaiju together.”
- “I raised the rainbow crystal Life Sword, believing its life-giving power could cleanse the creature instead of merely destroying it.”
- “Before I could strike, a sea hag flew past on a witch's broom and snatched the Life Sword directly from my hand.”
- “She forced its energy into the dead and resurrected an army of skeletal pirate warriors across the city.”
- “The kaiju still threatened the sea, and now one of the Storyverse's greatest artifacts was creating a second disaster.”

## Page 4 — The Tether Turns the Battle

Six action panels. Jet throws the golden glittering tether and loops it tightly around the Life Sword while the hag holds it aloft. Sparkling purple star-filled Lucid Light races from Jet through the gold line into the sword and shocks the hag with purple-white energy. She releases the blade and falls from the broom at a distant, non-gory angle. Jet yanks the sword back and catches it. He sweeps the blade over the outdoor skeletal army; sickly green life streams out of every rib cage and returns to the rainbow crystal, collapsing the pirates. Final large panel: Jet aims the supercharged Life Sword from the slime king's shoulder and fires a massive rainbow-gold blast through the kaiju, transforming it into clean water, sea plants, light, and harmless debris as the ocean clears.

Caption text:

- “I still had my golden energy tether, a lasso that could also carry power from one place to another.”
- “I looped it around the stolen Life Sword and poured my sparkling Lucid Light through the golden line.”
- “The surge shocked the sea hag through the sword, knocked her from her broom, and let me pull the Life Sword safely back into my hands.”
- “I drained the borrowed life energy from every resurrected pirate, collapsing the army and supercharging the blade.”
- “Then I released all that recovered power into the pollution kaiju.”
- “The blast completely eliminated the monster and purified the sea, saving both the city and the slime king's kingdom.”

## Page 5 — The Broom Knows the Truth

Five panels. Jet and the slime king stand amid the now-clean harbor, looking puzzled toward the fallen hag and intact hovering broom; the kaiju is gone. Jet checks the hag from a respectful distance and sees she died in the fall, without gore. He wraps the golden tether around the broom's crooked handle; gold light forms a magical knot and mixes with its green sparks. Jet rides the bonded broom across the sea into a cliff opening. Final two panels reveal a vast hidden treasure cove, preexisting skeletal pirate guards, overflowing riches, and visual planning maps of pirates raiding the city with no extra readable text.

Caption text:

- “The pollution explained the kaiju, but neither the slime king nor I understood why the sea hag had appeared during the battle.”
- “She had died in the fall, yet her broom remained intact, so I bonded my tether to it and sensed that the broom knew its way home.”
- “I let it carry me into a hidden treasure cove already guarded by skeletal pirates.”
- “The treasure and raid plans revealed the truth: the hag had been hoarding riches and wanted a much larger undead army to pillage the city.”
- “Her arrival during the kaiju battle had been a coincidence. She had simply recognized a chance to steal the Life Sword.”

## Page 6 — Cursed Claws in the Cove

Five panels. The towering humanoid red crab familiar bursts from behind treasure chests and swipes its violet-rune-covered black-red cursed claws toward Jet's golden tether and rainbow sword. Jet protects both artifacts by securing the Life Sword across his back and recoiling the tether around his belt bag. He summons two huge translucent star-filled purple Lucid Light claws around his hands. The crab squares up like a warrior. Final wide panel: the remaining skeletal pirate garrison surrounds Jet and the crab in a tightening ring while Jet's hoodie is scuffed and he realizes he is alone.

Caption text:

- “Inside the cove, the hag's humanoid red crab familiar attacked with cursed claws that might damage either my tether or the Life Sword.”
- “I refused to risk those artifacts in direct combat, so I protected them and shaped my Lucid Light into a pair of glowing claws.”
- “The crab fought like an armored warrior, and every swipe of his cursed pincers forced me backward.”
- “Then the skeletal pirates stationed in the cove surrounded us, leaving me injured, outnumbered, and alone.”
- “For a moment, it seemed that surviving the first battle had only carried me into a trap.”

## Page 7 — An Enemy's Broom Becomes an Ally

Six panels. Jet raises one Lucid Light claw and calls. The bonded broom rockets through the pirates to his side, its gold knot blazing. It sweeps across the cave floor, raising a thick but luminous dust screen. Using its handle like a staff, it batters and disarms skeletons while weaving rapidly between them. The crab familiar lunges half-blind through the dust. Jet briefly draws the Life Sword only to bathe his scuffed body in healing rainbow-green light, then returns it safely to his back. Final panels: Jet dodges cursed claw swipes and hammers the red shell repeatedly with purple star-filled claws; visible waves of violet-white heat make the shell glow orange from inside.

Caption text:

- “Then I remembered the magical bond I had formed with the hag's broom and commanded it to fight beside me.”
- “The broom swept dust into the air, creating a smokescreen that blinded the pirates and their crab commander.”
- “It wielded its wooden handle like a staff and almost single-handedly kept the entire skeletal garrison away from me.”
- “That opening let me heal my injuries with the Life Sword without exposing the blade to the crab's cursed claws.”
- “I attacked with my Lucid Light claws, and every impact sent another wave of intense heat through the familiar's shell.”
- “The armor that protected him was becoming an oven he could no longer endure.”

## Page 8 — The Shell Breaks, the City Endures

Five panels. The red crab familiar's incandescent shell splits cleanly and he molts out as a pale pink soft-bodied humanoid crab, leaving the empty red armor behind without gore. Jet strikes the exposed familiar once with crossed Lucid Light claws and defeats him in a burst of purple stars. The enchanted broom finishes the remaining skeletal pirates with sweeping staff blows, leaving collapsed bones and rusted cutlasses across the treasure. Jet stands victorious with intact Life Sword, tether, and broom while the cove's entrance opens to daylight. Final large sunset panel: Jet rides the friendly broom above the safe coastal city with the rainbow sword secured and tether glowing; below, the faceless crown-headed slime king raises a victorious fist from a sparkling clean ocean.

Caption text:

- “The repeated heat finally forced the crab familiar to molt and abandon the armored shell that had protected him.”
- “Without that shell, one final strike from my Lucid Light claws defeated him.”
- “At the same time, the enchanted broom overwhelmed the last skeletal pirates and ended the sea hag's remaining army.”
- “My alliance with the slime king, the Life Sword, the golden tether, Lucid Light claws, and even the hag's own broom had each solved a different part of the crisis.”
- “With every threat eliminated and both artifacts safe, I left the treasure cove above a city and sea that were finally secure.”
