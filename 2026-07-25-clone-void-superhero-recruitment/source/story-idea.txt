On July 25, 2026, Jet induced lucidity through sleep paralysis and entered the continuing Storyverse. He appeared alone in an endless black void, where a comic-book enthusiast seated at a table asked him to pitch a comic idea. Jet considered promoting a friend's idea, but learned that the stranger did not work for a publisher and could only recommend creators to one.

A swollen, pulsing sensation from Jet's waking stomach translated into the dream as an impossible pregnancy. Still lucid, Jet used star-filled purple Lucid Light to remove the mass safely. It became a large black bag containing a glowing humanoid silhouette. The stranger abruptly transformed into a massive rat monster, pummeled the bag, and fled. Jet restored the victim with the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life. The torn bag revealed an exact clone of Jet.

Jet then lost lucidity as the dream shifted into a superhero recruitment montage. Ordinary people were confused by heroes asking them to become superheroes, but some gradually embraced the idea. When Jet regained awareness, he was inhabiting the body of a cookware-themed female superhero while remaining mentally himself. A potential recruit turned out to be a telekinetic shapeshifter who copied the heroine's exact appearance.

Jet escaped the villain's ranged attacks, remembered that his Lucid Light did not depend on his physical form, and manifested pots and pans. He hurled the cookware, fought with it at close range, and finally summoned a massive storm of pots and pans that buried the shapeshifter. Jet woke when his alarm sounded.
