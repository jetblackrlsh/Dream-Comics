# Character Bible

## Jet

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie.
- Wears the open black hoodie over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a flexible energy tether made from golden glittering light, but neither is used during this dream. Keep them subtle or out of frame so they do not compete with the flying machine.
- Lucid Light would appear as sparkling purple reality-warping energy filled with stars, but Jet does not summon it in the academy or terminal sequence.
- Expression range: sleep-paralysis concentration, unquestioning obedience, intense workshop focus, guarded hope, disappointment, stealthy alertness, social detachment, dawning unreality, astonishment, analytic fascination, urgent readiness, waking frustration.
- Jet never wears a crown, school uniform, cape, mask, helmet, conventional superhero costume, or unrelated armor.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, face, beard, dreadlocks, hoodie, shirt, pants, sneakers, and bag on every page.

## The king, Jet's dream father

- Older dark-brown-skinned man in his late fifties with a broad dignified face, close-cropped salt-and-pepper hair, a trimmed square beard, and stern amber-brown eyes.
- Wears an elegant midnight-blue academic robe with gold edging over a dark royal tunic and a restrained geometric gold crown.
- Governs the academy from an office that combines a headmaster's study with a throne room.
- Carries himself with calm institutional authority. He assumes Jet will obey and does not need to threaten him.
- Expression range: formal expectation, distracted approval, emotional indifference, sleep.
- He is a dream role rather than a depiction of Jet's waking father.

## The king's wife

- Elegant East Asian woman in her late forties with light skin, almond-shaped dark eyes, high cheekbones, and glossy black hair arranged in a precise low sculpted bun.
- Wears a refined ivory-and-crimson royal academy gown with gold geometric trim and a narrow gold diadem.
- Her posture is immaculate and aloof, with a cool, demanding expression. Avoid exaggerated ethnic features or comedic caricature.
- She behaves like a queen-consort who is accustomed to being served. After Jet completes the errand, she falls asleep beside the king without acknowledging him.

## Mr. Carter-inspired lead instructor

- Original fictional academy teacher rather than an exact portrait of a real person.
- Dapper middle-aged man with warm brown skin, striking blue eyes, a carefully curled dark mustache, a small pointed goatee, and swept-back dark hair graying at the temples.
- Wears a fitted teal waistcoat, cream rolled-sleeve shirt, purple bow tie, tailored charcoal trousers, and polished shoes.
- Relaxed, encouraging posture and a naturally “groovy” creative energy even while the classroom is chaotic.
- Two additional instructors may appear only as generic original adults with contemporary tech-presenter styling; they must not resemble identifiable public figures.

## Rowdy academy students

- Diverse older teenagers and young adults in eclectic contemporary clothes with small royal-academy gold patches.
- They shout, lean over desks, toss paper mechanisms, socialize, and ignore rules. Their behavior is chaotic but not violent.
- Outside the academy, several gather in a pickup lane to talk while waiting for rides. They are ordinary peers Jet does not personally connect with, not villains or monsters.
- Avoid duplicated faces, uniforms that resemble real schools, recognizable celebrities, or cruel stereotypes.

## Magnetic flying machine

- A consistent backpack-sized experimental craft built by Jet.
- Oval copper-alloy frame with two opposing spring-loaded magnetic coils, visible blue-white magnetic arcs, four small folding fins, and a clear central chamber holding rainbow Morphium.
- It should look handmade but intelligently engineered from academy workshop parts.
- Its near-success is visible when it rises several inches and wobbles before settling back onto the workbench.
- Jet folds the fins and stores the full device inside his black cross-body bag. It is unfinished and intact, never smashed or discarded.

## Morphium

- A glossy, viscous rainbow material with saturated bands of red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet moving through it like living stained glass.
- It can stretch and hold engineered shapes. In Jet's machine it forms a luminous adaptive core around the coils.
- Morphium is not Lucid Light: it has a material goo-like body and rainbow coloration, never purple star-field energy.

## Amnesium

- A dangerous memory-erasing dream substance whose presence is uncertain.
- If glimpsed, it appears inside a sealed smoky glass vial as pale silver-violet fluid surrounded by soft gaps or missing fragments in the background.
- It remains peripheral and partially obscured; no character deliberately handles or consumes it.
- Do not let it replace Morphium as the machine's visible core.

## Academy

- A grand but strange royal academy combining Gothic stone arches, warm wood workshops, copper scientific apparatus, banners with abstract gold coil symbols, and modern classroom worktables.
- The flying-machines classroom is crowded and brightly lit, with component bins, coil rigs, chalk diagrams made from abstract shapes, and disorderly students.
- The king's office combines bookshelves and a headmaster's desk with a modest raised throne platform and curtained sleeping couches.
- The guarded rear exit leads to a golden-hour pickup lane behind the academy.

## Unicode source terminal

- A dark futuristic terminal on a simple desk, surrounded by deep black space after the academy dissolves.
- The display and the space around it contain streams of authentic-looking Unicode glyphs from many writing systems, mathematical operators, geometric marks, arrows, box-drawing symbols, and combining marks.
- These glyph streams are visual source data, not prose captions and not meaningless watermark text. They flow in stable luminous rows and continuously reorganize.
- Ghostly wireframe outlines of the king, students, academy arches, coils, Morphium, and flying machine emerge directly from matching regions of code, making the mapping visually obvious.
- Use cyan, emerald, violet, and white light for the code, with small rainbow traces where Morphium is represented.

## Established Storyverse crew continuity

Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper are established members of Jet's Storyverse team. None appears physically in this comic. Do not insert them into the academy, pickup lane, terminal, code, reflections, silhouettes, or cover.
