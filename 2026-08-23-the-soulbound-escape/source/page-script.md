# Page Script - The Soulbound Escape

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. The lucid prisoner, warden, Life Sword, exoskeleton, prison, turret, desert, and pine forest follow the character bible exactly. Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from all four outer edges.

## Page 1 - Cover

A dramatic full-page composition. The lucid prisoner launches away from the desert prison in orange pants and his gunmetal exoskeleton. A shimmering prismatic crystal shield deflects bullets from the automated turret while the spectral Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life glows through his chest. The horrified warden stands behind him, and the distant evergreen forest opens ahead.

Visible cover text only: “THE SOULBOUND ESCAPE” and “AUGUST 23, 2026.” No issue number.

## Page 2 - A Stranger in Chains

A clear three-panel establishing page. Show the isolated prison surrounded by rocky desert with pine forest on the far horizon; introduce the lucid prisoner looking down at the unfamiliar body; then show him smashing ore-bearing rock with the exoskeleton.

Caption text:

- “I became lucid inside the body of a prisoner I did not recognize.”
- “Beyond the prison's rocky desert, an evergreen forest promised freedom.”
- “An exoskeleton let me shatter ore-bearing stone with my bare fists.”

## Page 3 - Power Redirected

A clear three-panel decision page. Show orange prison pants and the powerful harness; guards trusting him to work among mining carts; then the prisoner turning away from the rocks and sprinting toward the perimeter.

Caption text:

- “The orange pants named me a prisoner, but the harness made me dangerously strong.”
- “I reasoned this man must have earned extraordinary trust through good behavior.”
- “I refused to inherit his confinement. I turned from the rocks and ran.”

## Page 4 - Not Bulletproof

A clear three-panel reversal page. Show the exoskeleton driving the prisoner's sprint; the warden raising and firing his compact sidearm; then the prisoner collapsing without gore as the forest remains distant.

Caption text:

- “The harness drove me faster than an ordinary man—but not faster than a bullet.”
- “The warden fired once, and the strength-enhancing machine could not make me bulletproof.”
- “As my borrowed body fell, I knew I was dying inside a dream.”

## Page 5 - The Soul Split

A clear three-panel supernatural page. Show the fallen prisoner remaining lucid; the spectral Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life appearing through his chest; then a translucent soul silhouette rising and being pulled back into the body by rainbow light.

Caption text:

- “Lucidity gave me one power the prison could never predict.”
- “I summoned the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life through my chest.”
- “It split my departing soul from the dying body—then pulled me back inside.”

## Page 6 - The Impossible Prisoner

A clear three-panel escalation page. Show the prisoner rising alive in rainbow light; the warden recoiling in terror because the harness cannot explain it; then the warden activating the huge automated turret.

Caption text:

- “I rose again, resurrected by the sword's command over life.”
- “The warden panicked. No exoskeleton could explain what he had witnessed.”
- “He activated the prison turret and ordered it to erase the impossible.”

## Page 7 - Shield and Leap

A clear three-panel climax page. Show the turret opening fire; the prisoner summoning a faceted prismatic crystal shield that deflects every bullet; then the exoskeleton launching him in an enormous leap over the prison wall.

Caption text:

- “I called the Life Sword's power into a shimmering crystal shield.”
- “Every turret round struck rainbow light and deflected harmlessly away.”
- “With the harness and the sword together, I launched myself beyond the prison walls.”

## Page 8 - Only Freedom

A clear three-panel resolution page ending with a wide forest panel. Show the prisoner soaring beyond bullet range; crossing the final rocky ground; then disappearing among sunlit evergreen trees while the prison shrinks behind him.

Caption text:

- “The bullets faded behind me as the rocky desert gave way to evergreen trees.”
- “I had escaped the sentence, the gunfire, and even death itself.”
- “I disappeared into the forest carrying no borrowed history—only my freedom.”

## Caption checklist

- Page 1: 2 visible text elements.
- Pages 2-8: exactly 3 caption boxes per page.
- Every caption is required verbatim, unnumbered, and enclosed in a complete rectangular cream box.
