Jet returns lucidly to the medieval Storyverse village protected by the Blue Enchantress and learns that the Life Sword is a unique fusion of naturally living rainbow crystal, life-imbuing rainbow crystal, and mysterious future technology. To protect the village, its Life Crystal mines, and the civilization of living crystal people beneath it, Jet and Leon move the entire bubble universe into a hidden pocket dimension. A pirate armada arrives too late and destroys itself when Leon phases through its crossfire, but an impossibly large ghost cruiser can still collide with him. Jet makes the cruiser physical with the Life Sword, Leon possesses it, and Jet battles its vampiric captain. A direct Lucid Light laser creates the opening Jet needs to invent “Sol,” a miniature-sun spell that destroys the vampire and completes the capture of the cruiser.
