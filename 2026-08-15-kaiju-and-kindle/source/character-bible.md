# Character Bible - Kaiju & Kindle

## Kaiju - organic human form

- Exceptionally tall, hulking, monstrously muscular adult man with vivid orange skin and striking red eyes.
- Short messy brown hair, heavy and layered, swept toward one side rather than a neat bowl cut.
- Wears simple purple knee-length shorts and no shirt or shoes.
- Square heroic face, powerful jaw, huge hands, and a visually intimidating silhouette at every size.
- His expressions are gentle, intelligent, cautious, and compassionate unless a giant monster threatens civilians.
- Can shrink very small or grow to skyscraper scale while retaining the same extreme musculature and identifying colors.
- Can transform into muscular humanoid animals and mythical creatures, always preserving orange coloration and red eyes.
- Can roboticize any current form: integrated silver mechanical plating replaces parts of his organic body, making him a true robot rather than a person wearing armor.

## Kaiju - giant robotic dragon form

- Skyscraper-sized humanoid orange dragon with extreme musculature, red eyes, short swept messy brown hair, huge orange wings, claws, horns, and a long tail.
- Integrated silver mechanical plating, joints, chest structures, and reinforced limbs clearly make his body partly robotic.
- Fires a concentrated white-orange nuclear fire-breath beam upward.
- Must remain recognizably the same Kaiju through orange anatomy, red eyes, brown hair, purple accents, musculature, and silver roboticization.

## Kindle

- Sleek feminine humanoid robot with a predominantly white armored body, black flexible joints and torso accents, and luminous orange trim.
- Black LCD screen face with exactly two huge, perfectly round glowing orange eyes resembling zeroes; no human mouth, nose, skin, or hair.
- Exactly two long black rabbit ears rise from her head, hang backward, and are tied together near the base like a ponytail.
- Athletic, elegant proportions and expressive body language; cute, confident, warm, and highly intelligent rather than childlike.
- Subtle orange-white propulsion from hover-boot feet allows flight; feet must not be shaped like jet engines.
- Can project delicate orange digital connection lines toward cameras, networks, nanites, and other technology.
- Kaiju's equal superhero partner and romantic partner; their poses should communicate mutual care and trust.

## Anti-Tekistan paramilitary soldiers

- Numerous adult soldiers in matte black tactical uniforms, helmets, and face coverings with narrow red-orange visor slits.
- Carry distinctive futuristic black rifles with glowing orange nanite chambers and compact muzzle assemblies.
- Their equipment has no readable logos, flags, or real-world national insignia.
- They are threatening but remain human opponents whom Kaiju is determined not to kill.

## San Francisco and analogized technology

- Recognizable San Francisco skyline, steep streets, bay, Golden Gate Bridge, and Transamerica Pyramid.
- Advanced contemporary city technology contrasts with transformed analog objects.
- Nanite effects appear as swarms of orange digital particles converting smartphones into black rotary phones and sleek electric cars into antique black Model T-style automobiles.

## Storyverse framing

- Jet is the lucid creator-observer but is not physically present in the action.
- Sparkling purple Lucid Light may frame the opening page as the energy shaping this bubble universe.
- Do not depict Jet as a participating hero in any page.

## Continuity rules

- Kaiju's orange skin, red eyes, swept brown hair, purple shorts, immense musculature, and gentle intelligence remain consistent.
- Kindle's white-black-orange robot body, black LCD face, two round orange eyes, two tied-back black rabbit ears, and hover-boot flight remain consistent.
- Roboticized Kaiju is a true orange-and-silver machine-organic transformation, never ordinary armor.
- Anti-Tekistan weapons consistently use glowing orange nanite chambers.
- No gore, speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, creator signatures, or blank caption boxes.
