# Character Bible - The Soulbound Escape

## The lucid prisoner

- Adult muscular man who appears half white and half East Asian.
- Light-to-medium warm skin tone, angular face, dark eyes, and short-to-medium straight black hair parted clearly down the middle.
- Clean-shaven; no beard, mustache, dreadlocks, hoodie, shirt, shoes, belt bag, or Jet's normal physical appearance.
- Shirtless throughout the story, wearing only loose orange prison-jumpsuit pants and a gunmetal metallic exoskeleton harness.
- The harness wraps over both shoulders, across the upper torso, around the waist, and along the arms and legs with compact pistons and articulated braces. It amplifies strength, speed, and durability but is not full armor and does not make him bulletproof.
- Intelligent, observant, rebellious, determined, and increasingly awed by the Life Sword's power.
- Keep his face, hair, skin tone, muscular build, orange pants, bare feet, and exact exoskeleton design consistent on every page.
- The visual identity represents Jet's lucid consciousness inhabiting a stranger's body. Do not transform him into Jet's ordinary appearance.

## The warden

- Older adult man with a stern lined face, pale skin, close-cropped gray hair, and a square gray mustache.
- Wears a dark charcoal futuristic prison-command uniform with silver shoulder bars, a high collar, black gloves, and a compact black sidearm.
- Initially controlled and authoritative; visibly shocked after the prisoner's resurrection; frantic when activating the turret.
- Keep the same face, mustache, uniform, and sidearm whenever visible.

## Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life

- A colorful futuristic crystal sword with a long translucent blade containing distinct red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet light.
- Ornate crystalline crossguard and luminous silver-white core.
- Normally associated with healing and life. Here it appears spectrally through the prisoner's chest without gore.
- The soul-splitting technique appears as a translucent luminous duplicate of the prisoner rising from his body, severed and immediately pulled back by ribbons of rainbow light.
- Its power also creates a spherical or domed prismatic crystal shield composed of shimmering facets.
- Never depict the sword as a plain steel weapon or as the purple-blue Death Sword.

## Prison and desert

- Futuristic but brutalist prison built from pale concrete, dark steel fences, guard towers, floodlights, and automated turrets.
- The labor yard contains jagged tan and rust-colored rock, broken stone piles, mining carts, and exposed veins of luminous rare crystals.
- The surrounding landscape is a barren rocky desert under a vivid blue sky.
- A distant dense evergreen pine forest is always visible beyond the desert as the visual destination and symbol of freedom.

## Prison turret

- Large automated gun emplacement mounted near the prison wall.
- Gunmetal housing, paired rotating barrels, amber targeting lights, and a heavy industrial base.
- Fires ordinary high-velocity bullets rather than lasers.
- Must remain clearly separate from the warden's compact sidearm.

## Continuity rules

- All eight pages use bright, high-saturation modern-anime comic art with crisp linework, luminous prismatic effects, expressive faces, and cinematic desert lighting.
- Story pages use unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes only.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, signatures, gore, blank caption boxes, or decorative gibberish.
- Keep every caption, letter, title, date, and complete caption-box border inside a generous 6% safe margin from every outer edge.
- The prisoner has no visible Life Sword before page 5.
- The prisoner is wounded on page 4 without gore; the wound is represented through posture, torn fabric at the waist, and fading light rather than blood.
- The Life Sword appears spectrally through his chest on page 5 and remains available as rainbow energy through pages 6-8.
- The crystal shield first appears on page 7 and remains around him during the escape leap.
- The pine forest is distant on pages 1-7 and becomes the dominant environment only on page 8.
