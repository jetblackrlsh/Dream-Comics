# Character Bible - The Molten Barricade

## Jet

- Black man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hood.
- Wears an open black hoodie over a clearly visible colorful "Dream Anime" graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic but natural build. No crown, cape, mask, helmet, conventional superhero costume, armor, or wings.
- Expression range: confident focus, startled impact, urgent concentration, cautious hope, betrayed anger, quiet calculation, decisive effort, and relieved triumph.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy filled with tiny stars, like a living night sky. It propels Jet in flight and at super speed.
- Geomancy appears as powerful amber-purple earth magic flowing from Jet's hands into rock, with no wand or spellbook.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hooded outfit, shirt, pants, sneakers, and belt bag on every page.

## Silonis - Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life

- One consistent living futuristic rainbow crystal sword and one of the most powerful artifacts in the Storyverse.
- Broad translucent faceted blade containing saturated red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet bands; dark silver geometric technological hilt; radiant rainbow-white aura; tiny flowing sparks inside the blade.
- Possesses its own mind, life, will, and autonomous movement. It can hover, create translucent rainbow force fields, intercept projectiles, and return to Jet without being wielded.
- Never depict Silonis as a metal broadsword, katana, ordinary fantasy sword, double-bladed weapon, or another character's weapon.
- Continuity rule: maintain the same faceted rainbow blade, geometric hilt, color order, size, and glow on every page.

## Goblins

- A pursuit group of short, wiry green-skinned goblins with pointed ears, sharp expressive faces, yellow eyes, and practical dark charcoal street armor with ember-orange trim.
- Each carries an ordinary-looking compact black firearm. When fired, the ammunition transforms in the air into bright orange-red enchanted fire bullets with comet tails that curve after Jet like miniature homing fireballs.
- They are fast, coordinated, greedy, and determined to steal Silonis. They remain threatening without blood, gore, or realistic injury.
- Continuity rule: keep their green skin, charcoal-and-orange armor, and compact black guns consistent.

## The giant troll

- A massive olive-green troll roughly half the height of a skyscraper: far larger than a normal giant, but not full kaiju scale.
- Extremely broad and muscular with a heavy brow, small amber eyes, blunt tusks, dark shaggy hair, a rough brown hide-and-metal loincloth, wrist wraps, and bare feet.
- Carries one enormous battered wooden club sized to match its body.
- Moves slowly and heavily but can reach airborne targets with the club and physically block the cave entrance.
- Never depict the troll as skyscraper-tall, armored like a knight, winged, or carrying anything other than its wooden club.

## The cave dragon

- A large serpentine dragon with deep cobalt-blue scales, a pale silver underbelly, two swept-back ivory horns, luminous violet eyes, and broad folded wings.
- Initially wears a calm, almost welcoming expression, then becomes openly greedy and treacherous when Silonis rejects it.
- Reaches for Silonis with one claw, is repelled nonviolently by a rainbow force field, calls the goblins to the cave, and retreats deeper rather than fighting.
- Continuity rule: maintain the same cobalt scales, silver underbelly, ivory horns, violet eyes, and folded-wing silhouette.

## The molten barricade

- A thick wall of irregular dark-gray cave boulders pulled from the ceiling through geomancy.
- Enchanted fire bullets strike from the unseen goblin side, heating the seams until they glow orange-gold and partially fuse together.
- The finished wall remains solid and impassable rather than becoming liquid lava. Its welded seams radiate heat while the rock faces stay visibly intact.

## Settings

- City: a bright futuristic-modern metropolis of glass towers, concrete streets, rooftop signs without readable branding, blue daylight, and strong vertical flight corridors.
- Cave: a vast shadowy cavern of charcoal rock, violet-blue reflected light, jagged walls, and enough scale to contain the dragon and the troll-framed entrance.
- Storyverse: a cosmic black-violet void filled with sparkling purple nebulae and floating bubble universes, reached only in the final escape.

## Continuity exclusions

- Jet's established crew - Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper - does not physically appear in this focused dream.
- Tetheron does not appear.
- Do not add police, soldiers, civilians, other heroes, additional dragons, extra trolls, or named characters.
- No blood, gore, wounds, dismemberment, logos, watermarks, issue numbers, or numbered caption boxes.
