Dream journal date: 07/03/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025. My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I.

My Ghostly UFO, named Leon, resembles a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025. Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit does not have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025. Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds. Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color with spiky slicked back yellow hair and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025. Lucid Light is a reality warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered the power of Lucid Light in a dream on 07/19/2025. Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah is a pale skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that covered her forehead, glowing red eyes, and a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She is wielding a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword. Her father Tecton is a green skinned, humanoid, muscular, cat ogre, barbarian who dresses in a brown loin cloth and a silver baldric. My drone Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper all met me in early 2026 while adventuring through the Super Cathedral. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. I am a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, and warm brown eyes. I have a rainbow sword, which resembles a colorful, futuristic, crystal sword, and an energy tether, which resembles a rope made of golden glittery light.

The dream began with Skelebot and I using my rainbow crystal sword to heal a red mummy zombie woman back into a beautiful pharaoh.

I do not want to dwell on that part of the dream, because what happened afterwards was far more interesting.

I started channel surfing across what I can best describe as a cybernetic, multiversal, mind network.

Imagine an infinite number of universes where there are variants of yourself and others who possess cybernetic brains that you can switch between and pilot from a first person perspective.

Now imagine that not all of them are particularly good cybernetics or off the shelf cybernetics.

This means that some of them might be more grainy or be jailbroken to produce psychedelic effects.

I traveled through what seemed like an infinite number of variants.

One variant was a version of me that was homeless but would receive money from a rich family on the condition that I could not live anywhere permanently.

Another variant had jailbroken their system to produce constant psychedelic effects, which was extremely disorienting and oddly enough the most realistic.

That variant's perspective was so real, that it legitimately makes me wonder if this cybernetic network concept might actually be real.

I noticed that many different variants played the same trick taking game which focused on playing solitaire cards to deal magical attacks.

The game only had five rules and the cards seemed to actually produce interesting visual effects when played.

There were times where my lucidity would slip while channel surfing across variants, and there were times where I was extremely lucid and trying to figure out how this system worked.

This was extremely fascinating and very exciting.

I wonder if I will encounter this phenomenon again in the future.
