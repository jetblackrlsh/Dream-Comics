# Treatment - The Metal Rabbit's Rodeo Redemption

## Logline

In a lucid void, Jet defeats an orange-metal rabbit's escalating nightmare tricks without surrendering to fear, then transforms the would-be monster with an unexpected metal carrot and a new calling as a rodeo cowboy.

## Central conflict

The Metal Rabbit defines itself by its ability to frighten Jet. Each failed scare makes it escalate from ominous atmosphere to physical danger and overwhelming numbers. Jet's challenge is not merely to survive, but to refuse the rabbit's premise that their encounter must end through fear, escape, or destruction.

## Theme

Creative compassion can redirect a hostile identity more completely than domination. Jet wins because he remains secure in his own power and uses that security to imagine a better role for his adversary.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins fascinated and unafraid. The rabbit becomes frustrated as fog, clowns, chainsaws, and theft all fail. The mole swarm finally surprises Jet, but he regains control through Lucid Light. At the apparent climax, the rabbit becomes enormous and declares that escape is impossible. Jet answers with playful generosity: a gigantic metal carrot. Menace collapses into gratitude, and Jet recognizes a hopeful southern charm beneath the monster's grimy exterior.

## Ending

Jet tells the rabbit to stop frightening people and become a rodeo cowboy. A radiant imagined rodeo future appears behind the newly hopeful creature without claiming that the dream literally showed a later career. Jet and the rabbit exchange a cheerful “Howdy.” The final panel shows Jet flying into the bright Storyverse while the giant rabbit waves beside its metal carrot, no longer trying to hold him in the dark.
