# Character Bible

## Jet

Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears the black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a colorful futuristic rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. He can wield Lucid Light, sparkling purple reality-warping energy like a night sky full of stars.

Continuity notes: Keep Jet's dreadlocks, beard, hoodie, "Dream Anime" shirt, belt bag, rainbow crystal sword, golden tether, and white sneakers visible whenever possible. In this issue he is contemplative, unsettled by soul travel, and increasingly lucid.

## Professor Weird

Powerful wizard with a full beard, expressive eyes, and a distinctive cowlick of black-and-white hair. He wears layered colorful wizard robes with bright cosmic patterns, mystical jewelry, and an authoritative but compassionate posture. His magic appears as ornate circular sigils, rainbow sparks, and careful diagnostic light.

Continuity notes: Professor Weird is a referee and healer, not an antagonist. Keep his black-and-white cowlick, full beard, colorful robes, and calm referee presence consistent.

## Infiniter

Reality warper with short messy black hair, clear blue eyes, and an all-white jumpsuit. He looks confident, competitive, and slightly impatient. His reality-warping aura appears as clean white light edged with faint blue infinity loops and geometric distortions.

Continuity notes: Infiniter wants a football contest and does not think of himself as creative. Keep the white jumpsuit, messy black hair, blue eyes, and white-blue reality-warping aura consistent.

## Jet's Award-Winning Double

A woman who heavily resembles Jet: dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, long dark-brown dreadlocks, and a proud focused expression. She wears a black hoodie-inspired fashion jacket over a comic-arts academy uniform with tasteful gold award accents. She has a heroic resemblance to Jet without being identical.

Continuity notes: She has just won both Comic Writer Cup awards. She should feel like a mirror of Jet's creative pride and possibility.

## The School Killer

A shadowy serial killer presence haunting the comic-book arts academy. Keep the figure mostly concealed: long dark coat, blank pale mask or obscured face, gloved hands, and a dangerous silhouette glimpsed in corridors, trophy reflections, or behind doors.

Continuity notes: Avoid gore. The killer represents sudden death and threat inside the created school reality.

## Storyverse

A cosmic void filled with glowing bubble universes, each containing possible story ideas. Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple energy like a night sky full of stars. Jet's woven reality appears as glowing blue threads braided into a new bubble universe.

Continuity notes: The Storyverse should feel vast and spiritual here, distinct from the later school dream. Bubble universes, cosmic void, blue reality threads, and purple Lucid Light are key visual anchors.

## Comic Arts Academy

A massive school of the arts focused on crafting comic books. It has grand studio classrooms, drafting desks, wall-sized comic pages, trophy halls, dramatic stairways, supernatural shadows, mission markers like a videogame interface, and crime-drama tension.

Continuity notes: The academy is inside Jet's woven creation. It should feel busy, strange, and story-saturated, with supernatural forces hiding under normal school life.
