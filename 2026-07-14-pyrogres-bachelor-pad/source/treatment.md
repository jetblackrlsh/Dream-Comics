# Treatment

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet abandons a series of cumbersome power suits for a light anti-gravity force-field belt, then discovers that the giant flaming ogre who interrupts his first flight wants friendship, relaxation, and an unconventional power-up instead of a fight.

## Central conflict

Jet wants the pleasure and freedom of superhero powers without automatically being trapped inside the usual cycle of armor, weapons, and monster battles. Each prototype suit gives him more equipment but less freedom. When Pyrogre appears, Jet must reject the assumption that every enormous monster is an enemy before he can discover a hero whose values match his own.

## Theme

Power does not need to be heavy, violent, or constantly justified through combat. Heroes can enjoy what they are capable of, communicate before fighting, and still choose to help when help is genuinely needed.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins lucid, curious, and playfully experimental. The increasingly elaborate suits leave him dissatisfied because every upgrade adds weight without giving him the feeling he wants. The anti-gravity belt restores freedom, and his completed costume gives that freedom a heroic identity. Pyrogre's arrival briefly threatens to turn enjoyment into an obligatory fight. Jet honestly voices that frustration, learns that Pyrogre feels no hostility, and enters the ogre's surreal internal home. Their mutually beneficial training turns apprehension into delight. Jet ends with both a power system and a new teammate perfectly suited to his desire for low-pressure heroism.

## Ending

Jet and a newly strengthened Pyrogre relax together on a rooftop above the city, playing a handheld arcade game while keeping watch. Their heroic emblem is not a battle pose but an easy fist bump between friends. A distant emergency signal reminds the reader that they will act when needed, but neither hero rushes to invent a crisis. They have formed a superhero team built around freedom, friendship, and helping by choice.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: Jet and Pyrogre above the city, with a glimpse of the impossible arcade inside Pyrogre.
2. Jet becomes lucid on a rooftop and finds the plain jet-pack suit unsatisfying.
3. Armored and laser-equipped prototypes become increasingly bulky.
4. The yellow belt reveals flight, shockwaves, force beams, and all-around protection.
5. Jet completes his green, black, orange, and yellow superhero look and flies over the city.
6. Pyrogre appears; Jet refuses an assumed fight and learns the ogre is a fellow hero.
7. Pyrogre's internal bachelor pad and arcade are revealed; Jet safely strengthens him from within.
8. The two heroes form a relaxation-first team that remains ready to help.
