Jet begins the dream transformed into a humanoid black sheep-dragon with enormous batlike wings, fire breath, and black wool that behaves like intangible cloud mist. A humanoid wolf knight in metal armor attacks him above a wooded medieval-fantasy landscape, but the knight's lance passes harmlessly through Jet's cloudlike body. Jet's fire then heats the armor until the wolf tears it off. When the wolf admits that he has lost his purpose because knights are supposed to kill dragons and wolves are supposed to eat sheep, Jet offers to make him an apprentice. Jet explains that his own flight comes from a cosmic chain of associations connecting sheep, clouds, and the sky. The wolf discovers his corresponding association with the moon, channels moonlight through his fur, transforms into a radiant pale-white lunar form, and flies beside Jet without wings. The dream ends with both of them embracing deeper identities beyond the roles imposed by their surface appearances.
