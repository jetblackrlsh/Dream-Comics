# Page Script - The Red-Eyed Darkness

All story text appears inside unnumbered rectangular cream caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, numbered captions, or issue numbers. Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait high-saturation modern-anime comic page. Captions must be readable, clearly separated, informative, nonredundant, and short enough to fit naturally. Jet, Silonis, Lucid Light, the red-eyed tower, the darkness, and the underground home follow the character bible exactly.

## Page 1 - Cover

Jet flies between the rain-darkened city and the exposed underground refuge, swinging Silonis through black tendrils as the gigantic red-eyed water tower looms behind him. The sword's rainbow life energy turns the struck darkness white and shatters it, while sparkling purple Lucid Light spirals upward from the home below. Visible cover text only: "THE RED-EYED DARKNESS" and "AUGUST 10, 2026." No issue number.

## Page 2 - The Tower Watches

Jet patrols above a rainy city, notices the black-eyed water tower, turns back, and faces its impossible transformation.

Caption text:

- "Flying through cities like a superhero is standard in many of my dreams."
- "I patrol from above for villains, monsters, or anyone who needs protection."
- "That night, a black water tower caught my attention because a gigantic black eye stared from its tank."
- "When I flew back to investigate, the tower grew until it dominated the city."
- "Its eye burned red, the world darkened, and a voice entered my mind: Was I scared?"

## Page 3 - What Life Does to Evil

The darkness reaches for Silonis. Jet draws the sword, slashes through the entity, and watches purification spread.

Caption text:

- "The darkness did not merely surround me. It reached for Silonis."
- "The Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life is the most powerful artifact in the Storyverse."
- "I answered the reaching shadows with one decisive strike."
- "Wherever Silonis touched the corruption, blackness became brilliant white."
- "The darkness demanded to know what I had done to it."
- "I told it the truth: I was not afraid."

## Page 4 - Pure Evil Breaks

Purification races across the tendrils and tower. Jet explains the consequence, flies away from the shattering entity, and enters the rain beyond the city.

Caption text:

- "One of the Life Sword's primary powers is the purification of evil."
- "The corruption vanished as the darkness and its tower cracked apart."
- "If the entity was pure evil, purification would likely destroy it completely."
- "I left the breaking tower behind and continued across the rain-soaked sky."
- "Beyond the city, a forest trapdoor promised shelter from the storm."

## Page 5 - A Home From Another Life

Jet enters the cozy underground home, recognizes its Minecraft-like details, explores the berry-lined mine entrance, and experiments with food.

Caption text:

- "Below the trapdoor waited a crafting table, bed, chests, and a home I somehow remembered."
- "It felt like an old house I once built inside Minecraft, even though this version included a refrigerator."
- "I had felt nostalgia for childhood homes before, but never so strongly for a place that began inside a game."
- "A doorway opened into a mine where thorny berry bushes grew between the torches."
- "I gathered their fruit and turned it into juice, jam, paste, and every recipe I could imagine."

## Page 6 - The False Escape

The psychic darkness seeps down into the warm home. Jet runs through the mine doorway, then sees possible monsters taking shape in the shadows between torches.

Caption text:

- "Eventually, the darkness returned like a psychic symbiote seeping down from above."
- "It invaded the underground home and drove me toward the mine shaft."
- "For one moment, going deeper seemed like the obvious escape."
- "Then I remembered what could form in the shadows between widely spaced torches."
- "Running forward might only trade one monster behind me for many monsters ahead."

## Page 7 - A Third Direction

Jet stops between the pursuing darkness and the monsters ahead, rises from the ground, and forms sparkling purple Lucid Light into an enormous upward drill.

Caption text:

- "I did not have to accept either danger as my only choice."
- "The mine was a path, but it was not my path."
- "I flew upward and shaped sparkling purple Lucid Light around myself."
- "Reality-warping energy became a drill aimed straight through the ceiling."
- "Instead of following the tunnel, I began creating an exit of my own."

## Page 8 - Beyond the Trap

The Lucid Light drill breaks through earth and forest. Jet bursts into open sky with Silonis, leaves the darkness underground, and ascends into the cosmic bubble universes of the Storyverse.

Caption text:

- "Lucid Light carried me through stone, soil, roots, and rain."
- "I broke through to the surface and left the darkness beneath me."
- "Courage had helped me face the tower, but judgment showed me when to leave its trap."
- "No enemy could limit me to the routes it expected me to take."
- "I flew beyond the sky, entered the Storyverse, and escaped by choosing a direction that had not existed until I made it."
