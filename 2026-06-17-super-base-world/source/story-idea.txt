Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis and surfs across the Storyverse on Leon, his ghostly green UFO and longtime mobile headquarters. Wondering what it would be like to have a traditional superhero base, Jet and Leon tour empty caves, castles, rainforests, Bermuda Triangle bunkers, space stations, temples, and moon bases before reaching Mall World. Inside the infinite mall, Jet discovers Super Base World: a secret superhero headquarters that combines all those base types into a vast liminal patchwork built for potentially infinite protectors. After finding his way out, Jet encounters playful but unsettling aliens who possess people, turning them green, super strong, and yellow-antennaed. Jet accidentally ragdolls one possessed person with a touch and wakes before he can judge whether the aliens are enemies.
