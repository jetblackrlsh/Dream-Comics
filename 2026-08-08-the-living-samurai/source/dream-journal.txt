Dream Journal Reconstruction

I found myself inside a futuristic city called Andromeda City. It was nighttime beneath a starry sky. Many of the buildings looked as though they were made from enormous LED lights and wires, giving the entire city an exposed, wireframe-like appearance.

Towering over the city was a giant mecha with a red-and-yellow color scheme. It resembled a massive samurai robot. I flew up to the mecha and discovered that it was being piloted by a shriveled, gray-haired person. She explained that she was the protector of Andromeda City. Giant monsters and kaiju regularly attacked the city, and it was her responsibility to defend it.

I was confused by how old she appeared, as though she might have spent her entire life doing this. When I asked her about it, she explained that she had a disorder that caused her life energy to seep out of her body. The mecha functioned as an enormous life-support system that prevented her remaining life energy from escaping.

I began to tell her that I possessed Silonis, the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword. Before I could finish explaining, however, she used the mecha to reach out and grab the blade. I immediately summoned a rainbow crystal force field around Silonis, repelling the mecha’s hand.

She insisted that I did not understand and that she needed the sword in order to survive. I told her that she did not need to take it from me. Silonis was the most powerful artifact in the Storyverse, and I could simply use it to heal the damage that had been done to her, stop the loss of her life energy, and restore her youth.

She explained that healing her body would not solve the entire problem. The mecha was bound to her, and she was the only person capable of piloting it. Even if I restored her health so that she no longer needed the mecha as life support, she feared that she would not necessarily be allowed to simply leave it behind. She needed a way to continue protecting Andromeda City once she was no longer forced to pilot the machine. That was why she believed she needed Silonis for herself.

Once I understood, I realized that there was a simple solution. I swung Silonis and released a powerful slash of life energy that struck both the pilot and the mecha. The energy sealed the leak that had been draining the woman’s life force, healed the damage to her body, and restored her youth. She transformed into a blonde woman with bluish-green eyes. She wore a metallic, shining bodysuit in the same red-and-yellow color scheme as the mecha.

At the same time, the life energy from Silonis infused the mecha and brought it to life. It was no longer merely a machine that required a pilot, but an independent living entity. For the first time in her life, the woman was able to exit the mecha.

At that moment, a giant kaiju rose from the bay and attacked Andromeda City, destroying buildings as it advanced. The newly awakened mecha immediately moved on its own to confront the monster and defend the city.

The woman apologized for trying to steal Silonis. She had not known that the sword was capable of bringing an inanimate object to life. Awakening the mecha had completely changed everything for her. She was no longer burdened with having to serve as the hero of Andromeda City herself. I had not merely restored her youth. I had given her life back.
