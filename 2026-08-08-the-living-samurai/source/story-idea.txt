In futuristic Andromeda City, Jet meets a woman whose life-draining disorder has trapped her inside the red-and-yellow samurai mecha she alone can pilot. She tries to take Silonis because she believes the Rainbow Crystal Life Sword is her only path to survival, freedom, and continued heroism. Jet finds a better answer: one life-energy slash heals the woman and brings the mecha itself to life. When a kaiju attacks from the bay, the awakened guardian chooses to defend the city while its former pilot finally receives her own life back.
