# 08/23/2026 - The Soulbound Escape

Dream Comics entry for August 23, 2026.

Lucid inside the unfamiliar body of a trusted prisoner, Jet performs forced mining labor in an isolated desert prison. The muscular prisoner wears orange jumpsuit pants and a metallic exoskeleton harness that lets him smash ore-bearing rocks with his bare fists. Seeing that the same machine also gives him the strength and speed to flee, Jet rejects the unknown history of the body he inhabits and makes a break for the distant evergreen forest.

The warden shoots him before he can escape. The harness makes him stronger and tougher, but not bulletproof. As the borrowed body dies, Jet summons the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life through his chest. The sword separates his departing soul from the body and pulls it back inside, resurrecting him.

Terrified by the impossible revival, the warden activates a prison turret. Jet surrounds himself with a shimmering prismatic crystal shield, deflects the barrage, and combines the Life Sword's power with the exoskeleton's strength to leap beyond the prison's reach. He crosses the rocky desert, enters the pine forest, and claims freedom from the prison, the gunfire, death, and the stranger's inherited narrative.
