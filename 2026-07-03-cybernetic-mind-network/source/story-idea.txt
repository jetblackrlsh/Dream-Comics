Adapt the 07/03/2026 dream journal entry into an eight-page Dream Comics package.

Jet induces lucidity through sleep paralysis inside the ongoing Storyverse continuity. The dream begins with Jet and Skelebot using Jet's rainbow crystal sword to heal a red mummy zombie woman into a beautiful pharaoh, but the important part begins when Jet starts channel surfing across a cybernetic, multiversal mind network. In that network, infinite variants of Jet and other people have cybernetic brains that can be switched into and piloted from a first-person perspective. Some systems are clean and ordinary, some are grainy and cheap, and some are jailbroken to create psychedelic effects.

Jet pilots through many variants. One is a homeless version of Jet who receives money from a rich family only if he never lives anywhere permanently. Another variant has jailbroken their cybernetics to produce constant psychedelic effects, and this disorienting feed feels more real than the others. Across many lives, Jet notices people playing the same five-rule trick-taking game, using solitaire cards to deal magical attacks that make visible effects. Jet's lucidity rises and falls as he tries to understand whether the network is only a dream or evidence of a real phenomenon he may encounter again.
