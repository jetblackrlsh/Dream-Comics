# 08/25/2026 - The Dynamancer Interruption

Dream Comics entry for August 25, 2026.

Jet becomes lucid inside a God dream and creates a Storyverse world where data centers cool themselves with swamp water containing frog DNA. Pollution transforms people into humanoid frog Dynamancers whose powers are limited only by a single governing concept. The extraordinary change sharpens the world's debate: some welcome a new age of idea-powered superheroes, while others fear that the same event will create supervillains.

Jet abruptly loses lucidity and finds himself living at a modern academy filled with technology and toys from the 1990s. Armored paramilitary guards escort a former school bully into his dorm to repair a power-and-monitor problem. The man has accidentally killed someone and needs a signed release form proving that his good deeds deserve freedom. Jet tearfully refuses to sign while acknowledging that somebody else may eventually choose to forgive him.

After Jet compares the bully to a demon-fighting television character whose life also depends on good deeds, the comparison becomes literal. Red-and-blue symbiote tendrils transform the man into a demon, and he attacks Jet through the retro dormitory. Jet improvises with a full-sized motorized zip-line toy, rides it into the hallway, and kicks the creature through a window.

The victory restores Jet's lucidity. He returns to observing the unchanged frog-Dynamancer world, marvels at the complete story that overtook him while his control was gone, senses morning approaching, and chooses to wake.
