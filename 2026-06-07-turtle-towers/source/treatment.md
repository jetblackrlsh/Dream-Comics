# Treatment

## Logline

Jet reaches the emerald Turtle Towers and joins their penitent guardian, Turtalia, to keep the dangerous Multiverse Power sealed away from Exterminators who would erase anyone in their path.

## Central Conflict

The Multiverse Power can carry and control worlds, but Turtalia has renounced the old tyranny of the World Turtle. Jet must decide whether to treat that power as a prize, a weapon, or a burden that must remain protected, while the Exterminators try to seize it through infiltration and acid-erasure.

## Theme

True guardianship is restraint. The strongest cosmic power becomes safer when someone chooses not to dominate others with it.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins alone beneath impossible towers, awed by a mythic power he does not yet understand. Turtalia's confession turns that awe into trust and shared responsibility. Their mint-green bond lets them patrol the sky together, but the Exterminator base forces Jet into stealth, danger, and improvisation. He ends not by claiming the Multiverse Power, but by escaping with renewed loyalty to the guardian who chose freedom over control.

## Ending

After Jet is hurled out of the Exterminator locker rooms by Turtalia's black-clad ally, Turtalia and the giant flying serpent catch him in the aurora-lit sky. The Turtle Towers remain sealed behind them, glowing with restrained power as Jet understands that protection sometimes means leaving ultimate power untouched.
