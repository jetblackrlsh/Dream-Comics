# Treatment

## Logline

When a spike-covered villain pins lucid dreamer Jet with subterranean claws in order to steal the rainbow crystal Sword of Life, Jet turns the villain's own shattered claw fragments into an endlessly multiplying army of dandelion golems.

## Central conflict

Jet's joyful experiment with gravity-free swinging is interrupted by a villain who wants his Sword of Life and can attack through the city streets with freakishly long claws. Once the claws rise beneath Jet and hold both feet in place, Jet must escape without surrendering the sword and find a counterattack that makes the villain's apparent endless stamina work against them.

## Theme

Lucid creativity can turn an enemy's weapon into a peaceful-looking but unstoppable answer. Jet wins not by overpowering the villain with a stronger blow, but by changing destructive fragments into living possibilities that multiply under aggression.

## Emotional arc

Jet begins exhilarated and carefree as he discovers that Lucid Light can remove the danger from swinging while preserving all its joy. The villain's ambush brings sudden pain and restraint, but Jet remains observant enough to save the broken claw fragments. When the villain mocks him, Jet shifts from defense to playful confidence. His dandelion trick converts the villain's violence into an ever-growing disadvantage, and the surrender leaves Jet delighted by both his aerial freedom and his inventive solution.

## Continuity

This dream belongs to the Storyverse continuity that began on June 11, 2025. Jet has the same established appearance, Sword of Life, golden energy tether, and Lucid Light. Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper remain part of Jet's wider team, but Jet explicitly arrives alone and none of them physically appear in this dream sequence.

## Ending

The defeated villain raises both hands and surrenders beneath a soft blizzard of dandelion seeds while a vast crowd of small dandelion golems surrounds them without striking. Jet rises safely above the city on Lucid Light, catches his golden tether, and reflects that the Monkey King inspired the trick of blowing a small piece of oneself into many helpers.

## Eight-page structure

1. Cover: Jet swings above the city as dandelion golems overwhelm the purple clawed villain below.
2. Jet induces lucidity, arrives alone, and learns to combine tether swinging with flight.
3. Jet enjoys consequence-free flips until the villain attacks for the Sword of Life.
4. Underground claws pin Jet's feet; he cuts himself free and keeps the fragments.
5. The villain mocks Jet, who transforms the captured claw shards into dandelions.
6. Jet blows the seeds forward and creates the first dandelion golems.
7. Every strike divides the golems into more until the villain is completely overrun.
8. The villain surrenders, and Jet celebrates the aerial freedom and Monkey King-inspired solution.
