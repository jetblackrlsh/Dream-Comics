# Character Bible - The Sword Remembers

## Jet

- African-American man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard and mustache, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Long dreadlocks spill from beneath a black hoodie worn over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt.
- Black athletic pants with one clean white stripe down each outer leg, white high-top sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic heroic build, compassionate expression, decisive posture, and the ability to fly under his own power.

## Silonis

- Jet's Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life and the most powerful artifact in the Storyverse.
- Futuristic sword made from large, faceted, translucent crystals spanning the full rainbow spectrum.
- Radiates prismatic life energy that can create life, build rainbow-crystal force fields, heal, and purify corruption without causing physical injury.
- Possesses enough autonomy to reject the librarian and fly back to Jet.

## The librarian

- Elegant pale-skinned woman with greenish-blue eyes and very long pale-blue sparkling hair.
- Wears flowing sky-blue robes decorated with small colorful gemstones and delicate gold chains.
- Appears serene and exhausted before the corruption is revealed; becomes desperate and possessive around Silonis.
- When the darkness reasserts control, glossy black corruption crawls across her clothing and skin while her eyes glow crimson-red.

## The knight

- Ornate empty suit of silver-blue plate armor with subtle gold trim and a deep-blue cloth tabard.
- Before true life, it moves through the librarian's blue magic. After Silonis awakens it, intense blue light burns within its visor and joints.
- Its awakened posture is angry, expressive, and desperate for freedom.
- Remains contained inside a transparent rainbow-crystal force-field bubble after its charge at the librarian.

## The darkness

- Glossy black corruption resembling liquid tar, smoke, roots, tendrils, and reaching hands, threaded with faint crimson veins.
- Moves between hosts while preserving the same predatory red glow and obsession with Silonis.
- Never becomes a separate conventional humanoid villain. It manifests through the librarian and ghostlike silhouettes of prior hosts.

## The library of life books

- Seemingly infinite archive with impossibly tall dark-wood bookshelves, scattered books, blue crystal lights, and cathedral-sized glass windows.
- The windows reveal a brilliant star-filled night sky and eventually the glowing bubble universes of the Storyverse.
- Life books are ornate volumes whose past pages appear fixed while future writing visibly shifts and rewrites itself.

## Continuity rules

- Jet's skin tone, beard, long dreadlocks, hoodie, Dream Anime shirt, striped pants, white shoes, cross-body bag, and Silonis remain consistent on every page.
- Silonis keeps the same faceted rainbow-crystal form and never becomes a conventional metal sword.
- The librarian retains pale-blue sparkling hair, greenish-blue eyes before possession, sky-blue gemstone robes, and the same facial design.
- The knight remains silver-blue with gold trim, blue inner light, and no exposed human face.
- No gore, speech bubbles, thought balloons, floating dialogue, subtitles, numbered captions, issue numbers, watermarks, logos, creator signatures, or blank caption boxes.
