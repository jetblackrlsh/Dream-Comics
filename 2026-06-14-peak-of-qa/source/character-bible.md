# Character Bible

## Jet, Usual Dream Identity

Jet is a man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks. His usual dream outfit is a black hoodie over a "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow crystal sword and a golden glittering energy tether. These details can appear in the sleep paralysis transition and as faint echoes under the transformation.

## Jet, Qa Werewolf Form

For most of this comic, Jet is a tall athletic werewolf with black fur, clean white tiger-like stripes across his body and muzzle, green snake-like eyes with slit pupils, a strong wolf snout, pointed ears, sharp but controlled claws, and long dark-brown dreadlocks flowing like a mane from the transformed head. His usual black hoodie, "Dream Anime" tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and cross-body belt bag are stretched and partially transformed but still recognizable as dream-clothing fragments. His rainbow crystal sword and golden energy tether appear as symbolic gear at his side, not as weapons he uses. His expression moves from startled isolation to heightened awareness, then calm conviction and serene power.

## The Voice

The voice is not a person. Show it as dark translucent cosmic lettering shapes, shadow ripples, or a looming abstract silhouette made of smoky black and cold blue light. It should feel accusatory and interpretive rather than evil. It has no face, no body, and no speech bubble. Its words appear only through caption boxes generated in the comic art.

## Mountain Of Qa

Qa is an impossibly tall crystalline mountain peak above creation, with luminous stone, violet starlight, dawn-gold edges, and a small flat summit where Jet can sit in meditation. Below the peak, the Storyverse appears as countless glowing bubble universes floating in a cosmic void. The summit should feel peaceful, sacred, and high enough to see all possible story worlds.

## Lucid Light And Energy

Lucid Light is sparkling purple reality-warping energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. In this comic it begins as sleep paralysis shimmer, floods through Jet's werewolf transformation, becomes the force of desire that moves him to Qa, and finally radiates outward as peaceful purple waves.

## Visual Continuity Notes

Keep Jet's werewolf form consistent after the transformation: black fur, white stripes, green snake-like eyes, long dark-brown dreadlock mane, athletic build, fragments of the black hoodie and dream outfit, rainbow sword at his side, and golden tether coiled or floating nearby. Change only lighting, pose, and emotional expression.
