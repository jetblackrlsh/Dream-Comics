# Character Bible - The Patron's Stew

## Jet

- Adult Black man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hood.
- Black hoodie over a vivid Dream Anime graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with a white side stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Curious and confident while choosing his warlock identity; alert and decisive in danger; muddy after being thrown into the swamp; thoughtful and morally uneasy at the end.
- Always holds the Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life once the quest begins.

## Second Brain

- Large floating anatomical brain formed from translucent sparkling purple Lucid Light, with readable folds and a soft star-filled inner glow.
- Wears a small ornate silver necklace set with a violet gem that manifests two dramatic dark-purple vampire-bat wings.
- No face, arms, or legs; emotion is conveyed through tilt, wing posture, glow intensity, and position near Jet.
- Small enough to float beside Jet's shoulder, but large enough to read clearly as his unique familiar.
- During the finishing attack, merges into the Rainbow Crystal Sword as purple lucid lightning rather than appearing separately.

## Apollo

- Majestic adult Greek sun god with warm golden skin, athletic build, shoulder-length wavy golden hair, and luminous amber eyes.
- White-and-gold classical robes, radiant laurel crown, and a casual, faintly bemused expression.
- Appears in the sky among parted storm clouds, usually standing in or leaning from a brilliant golden sun chariot.
- His sunlight is a concentrated gold-white beam with radiant shafts, never fire or lightning.

## Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life

- Large futuristic crystal blade made of clean faceted rainbow segments from red through violet.
- Dark ornate hilt with subtle metallic gold accents.
- Emits bright multicolored glow, thin prismatic trails, and green-gold healing radiance.
- When infused with Second Brain, vivid purple starry Lucid Light and branching violet lightning surge across the blade.
- Must remain unmistakable and visually consistent throughout the issue.

## Giant bat

- Enormous non-humanoid bat with charcoal-black fur, leathery black wings, long pointed ears, red-brown eyes, hooked claws, and a wingspan several times Jet's height.
- Animal anatomy only: no humanoid torso, clothing, armor, tools, or speech.
- Frightening and powerful but not evil; it tries to take the Sword and later retreats wounded into the mine.
- Wing severing is non-gory: cuts flare with prismatic crystal light, and no blood or exposed tissue is shown.

## Dark swamp

- Nighttime marsh filled with shallow black-green water, cypress trunks, hanging moss, tangled roots, reeds, low violet mist, and broken moonlight.
- A weathered timber-supported mineshaft opens from a mossy stone rise.
- The swamp becomes dramatically gold-lit when Apollo parts the clouds.

## Flooded mineshaft

- Narrow abandoned tunnel supported by decaying timbers and jagged wet stone.
- Deep opaque green-black still water fills a collapsed chamber.
- Nearly lightless except for the Rainbow Crystal Sword and Second Brain's purple glow.
