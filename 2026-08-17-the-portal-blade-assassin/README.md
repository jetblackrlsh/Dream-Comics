# 08/17/2026 - The Portal-Blade Assassin

Dream Comics entry for August 17, 2026.

Inside a magical study, Jet develops a theory about the hierarchy of enchanted swords. A normal weapon can carry a spell inscription, but legendary blades such as Silonis combine magic, rare living crystal, and futuristic technology. That realization leads him to imagine a dangerous planet of portal crystals whose mines supply automated teleportation technology across the Storyverse.

The knowledge attracts an unseen portal-blade assassin who materializes, strikes, and vanishes in rhythm with Jet's coughing physical body. Jet uses Silonis to sense the attacker's life energy and parry each arrival before casting Porta to send the assassin into the Empty Dimension. Although the assassin's sword could bring them back, Jet believes the dimension's complete freedom will give them a reason to stop running.
