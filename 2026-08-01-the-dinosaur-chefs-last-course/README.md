# 08/01/2026 - The Dinosaur Chef's Last Course

Dream Comics entry for August 1, 2026.

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet enters a prehistoric jungle and encounters a harmless, dopey green-and-orange dinosaur. A mysterious woman in a wicker dress and a wide sun hat covered with bright red berries approaches. Her face is entirely hidden in shadow except for glowing red eyes. She offers Jet dinosaur bacon, then asks him to kill the innocent creature so she can cook it.

The woman reveals an impossible outdoor kitchen and proudly calls herself a Dinosaur Chef. Her argument quickly unravels: dinosaur meat tastes like spoiled poultry unless mixed with the berries hanging from her hat. Jet suggests eating or baking with the berries instead. When he refuses to kill the dinosaur, the Chef draws a knife and stabs him.

Jet summons Silonis, his living rainbow crystal Sword of Life, and heals the wound. The Chef grows into a giant humanoid dinosaur and tries to crush him, declaring that eating dinosaurs has made her big and strong because “you are what you eat.” Silonis catches her stomp with a rainbow crystal barrier, and Jet escapes the impact with a Lucid Light back-dash.

Jet uses Tetheron, his golden energy tether, to link the Chef's mind with the harmless dinosaur. She experiences the fear, grief, and horror of the creatures she has cooked as if those feelings were her own. Although the revelation briefly horrifies her, she rejects Jet's humane alternatives, declares that meat is everything, and decides to eat him instead.

Jet surrounds himself with sparkling Lucid Light armor and rockets into the sky. The Dinosaur Chef has reached the maximum size of her transformation and cannot follow. Jet rises beyond her reach, leaves the prehistoric world, and flies into the brilliant bubble universes of the Storyverse with his freedom and principles intact.
