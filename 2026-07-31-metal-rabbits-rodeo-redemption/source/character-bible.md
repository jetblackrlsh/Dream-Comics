# Character Bible - The Metal Rabbit's Rodeo Redemption

## Jet

- Black man in his late twenties with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, a full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hood.
- Wears an open black hoodie over a clearly visible colorful “Dream Anime” graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants with one white stripe down each side, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic but natural build. No crown, cape, mask, helmet, armor, cowboy outfit, or conventional superhero costume.
- Expression range: fascinated curiosity, calm confidence, dry amusement, brief surprise, determined focus, playful generosity, and friendly warmth.
- Lucid Light appears as sparkling purple reality-warping energy filled with tiny stars, like a living night sky.
- Continuity rule: maintain his exact skin tone, beard, dreadlocks, hooded outfit, shirt, pants, sneakers, and belt bag on every page.

## Silonis - Rainbow Crystal Sword of Life

- One consistent independently living futuristic rainbow crystal sword.
- Broad translucent faceted blade containing saturated red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet bands; dark silver geometric technological hilt; radiant rainbow-white aura; tiny flowing sparks inside the blade.
- Can hover, twist, and fly independently with expressive motion.
- Healing appears as warm rainbow-white life energy with gold and green currents that instantly repairs Jet's injuries.
- Force fields are transparent domes of faceted rainbow crystal threaded by gold and emerald light. The dome surrounds Jet from above and the sides but leaves the ground beneath his feet open.
- Never depict Silonis as a metal broadsword, katana, ordinary fantasy sword, double-bladed weapon, or Savannah's purple-blue Death Sword.

## The Metal Rabbit

- A tall humanoid skeletal rabbit built from dirty, weathered orange metal bones and narrow mechanical joints.
- Long upright orange-metal ears, a skull-like rabbit face, two oversized buck teeth, and bright glowing red eyes.
- Its entire body is visibly coated with grime, soot, scratches, rust stains, and packed dirt. It is eerie but also strangely expressive.
- Initially poses theatrically and aggressively while trying to frighten Jet. Frustration becomes manic delight when a trick appears successful.
- It can reshape the void, summon creatures, and grow into a gigantic towering version of the same exact design.
- After receiving the gigantic metal carrot, the threatening posture vanishes. The same red eyes and monstrous face become wide with gratitude and hopeful southern charm.
- Do not add fur, skin, clothing, armor, a flesh face, antlers, extra limbs, or a conventional cute rabbit body. Do not place a cowboy hat on it as a literal event; its rodeo future appears only as a luminous imagined possibility.

## Zombie chainsaw clowns

- A small group of decayed skeletal clowns clawing out of black ground like zombies.
- Torn faded carnival suits, cracked face paint, frayed colored hair, skeletal limbs, and eerie theatrical poses.
- Each pulls one chainsaw from inside its rib cage. The moment should be macabre fantasy rather than graphic gore.
- They threaten and strike Jet, but Silonis's life energy repairs every injury immediately. Avoid lingering wounds or excessive blood.

## Adhesive spider

- A sleek black humanoid spider with six long arms, two legs, an angular spider-like head, and glowing red eyes.
- Swings rapidly on dark webbing and steals Silonis with one adhesive palm.
- When Silonis escapes, the stuck hand is severed cleanly in one fast stylized action, with no blood or gore; it remains visibly adhered to the sword for that beat only.

## Mole men

- Nearly one hundred small wild subterranean humanoids, each only the size of a puppy or large rat.
- Brown-gray fur, tiny clawed hands, blunt mole snouts, squinting eyes, scraps of dirt-colored cloth, and frantic expressions.
- Individually weak and almost comical; collectively dangerous because they erupt beneath the open-bottomed force field and pile over Jet with crushing weight.

## Void and Storyverse

- The encounter begins in empty black darkness with a subtle reflective ground plane.
- The rabbit summons thick ominous purple-black fog that hides the limits of the environment.
- Lucid Light shock waves make the void blaze with sparkling purple starlight.
- Beyond the dark void lies the Storyverse: deep indigo space filled with purple stars and many colorful translucent cosmic bubble universes, each containing the faint suggestion of a different world.

## Gigantic metal carrot

- A towering polished carrot sculpted from orange-gold metal with faceted green-metal leaves, created by Jet's sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- Large enough to delight the giant Metal Rabbit. It is solid metal, not food, not an ordinary carrot, and not a weapon.

## Continuity exclusions

Jet's established crew—Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper—does not physically appear in this focused dream. Tetheron also does not appear. Do not insert any of them into the void, summoned creatures, imagined rodeo, cover, or Storyverse ending.
