# Character Bible

## Jet — unmasked rooftop appearance

- Adult man with dark-brown skin, warm brown eyes, full dark-brown beard, and long dark-brown dreadlocks.
- Long dreadlocks spill from beneath a black hoodie.
- Black hoodie worn over a clearly visible “Dream Anime” graphic tee.
- Black athletic pants with one white stripe down each outer leg, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag.
- Athletic, grounded posture; curious and expressive rather than aggressive.
- His rainbow sword and golden energy tether belong to his established continuity but are not used during this dream and should not appear in the prototype testing panels.

## Prototype power suits

- Plain prototype: streamlined silver-gray flight suit with a simple compact twin-thruster jet pack; intentionally visually plain and lightly equipped.
- Armored prototype: the same silver-gray base with thick dark metallic shoulder, chest, forearm, and shin plates plus a larger jet pack; protective but still generic.
- Laser prototype: the armored version expanded with bulky shoulder laser cannons, forearm emitters, thick power cables, and an oversized backpack; powerful but obviously heavy and restrictive.
- Jet's face and long dreadlocks remain visible while he evaluates all prototypes so his identity stays clear.

## Jet — final superhero appearance

- Full-body black superhero suit.
- Smooth black mask covers his entire head and face, with large clean white eye lenses and no visible mouth opening.
- Green armored chest padding is the strongest color block on his torso.
- Black gloves and black boots.
- Flowing black cape with a vivid orange inner layer.
- Bright yellow anti-gravity force-field belt around his waist, with a round glowing central buckle.
- Belt effects appear as transparent golden-yellow pressure rings, curved force planes, lens-like distortions, and luminous geometric fields—not fire or electricity.
- The final silhouette is athletic and agile, never bulky.

## Pyrogre

- A gigantic, broad, muscular humanoid ogre who towers above rooftops.
- Charcoal-black volcanic skin with glowing orange magma cracks, especially across the shoulders, arms, and chest.
- A crown-like mane of bright orange and yellow flame rises from the head and runs down the upper back.
- Heavy brow, broad nose, two small lower tusks, and large expressive amber eyes.
- Initially imposing silhouette, but relaxed shoulders, open hands, and an easy friendly smile reveal a gentle personality.
- Wears rugged dark brown shorts and a broad fireproof utility belt; no armor and no weapons.
- When strengthened from within, the magma cracks and flame mane shine brighter and his muscles become more defined, but his core design does not change.

## Pyrogre's internal bachelor pad

- A dreamlike, non-gory living space inside Pyrogre's enormous torso.
- Warm red-orange organic walls are smooth and architectural rather than anatomical or bloody.
- Huge clean ivory ribs curve overhead like stylish support arches.
- Cozy brown sectional sofa, snack table, mini refrigerator, neon arcade cabinets, pinball machine, large game screen, warm lamps, and soft rugs.
- A safe open training corner exposes one immense ivory rib that Jet can punch without damaging the furnishings.
- The environment is humorous, comfortable, and inviting, never horrific.

## Visual continuity rules

- Before the final costume, always show Jet's exact dark-brown skin, full beard, long dreadlocks, hoodie, graphic tee, striped pants, white sneakers, and cross-body bag whenever the prototype does not cover them.
- During the flight and all Pyrogre scenes, keep Jet's full-head black mask, white eye lenses, green chest armor, black gloves and boots, black cape with orange lining, and yellow belt unchanged.
- Never add a helmet, visible hair, exposed beard, or exposed skin to Jet's final superhero costume.
- Never make the final costume bulky.
- Keep Pyrogre volcanic, enormous, friendly, and consistent; his internal home must remain a cozy arcade bachelor pad.
