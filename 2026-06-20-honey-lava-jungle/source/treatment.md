# Treatment

## Title

06/20/2026: Honey Lava Jungle

## Logline

After inducing lucidity through sleep paralysis, Jet awakens in a ruined jungle of fallen Were-Bees and leads Leon, Skelebot, and Overdrive in a desperate rescue before an erupting volcano's molten honey wave destroys everything.

## Central Conflict

The jungle is moments from catastrophe: homes have already been swallowed by honey lava, Were-Bees are trapped in the path of the wave, and Jet's Aegis spell can slow the disaster but not stop it. The conflict is not a battle against a villain, but a test of lucid command under pressure. Jet has to assign the right task to each teammate before the shield fails.

## Theme

Lucidity becomes strongest when imagination turns into coordinated action. One heroic spell is not enough; the jungle survives because Jet trusts each crew member's unique power.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins in the uncanny transition of sleep paralysis, then snaps into lucidity inside a jungle disaster. His first emotion is shock at the fallen Were-Bees and the honey-lava wave. Shock becomes focused leadership as he commands Leon and Skelebot. When the Aegis shield starts to crack, Jet feels the limit of his own power, but he refuses to panic. He makes the decisive call to Overdrive, whose matter manipulation changes the entire disaster. The ending is relief, awe, and gratitude as golden honey clouds rise over a rescued jungle.

## Ending

Overdrive increases the atomic motion of the molten honey until the wave flashes into harmless luminous honey clouds. Leon's tractor beam lowers rescued Were-Bees to safety, Skelebot returns with evacuees, and Jet drops the fading golden turtle-shell shield. The final image shows the Were-Bees, Jet, and the crew looking over a saved jungle under sweet golden clouds.

## Cast

- Jet: lucid dreamer and protagonist. He is a man with dark-brown skin, a full dark-brown beard, warm brown eyes, and long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie. He wears a black hoodie over a visible "Dream Anime" graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, and a black cross-body belt bag. He carries a rainbow futuristic crystal sword and uses a golden glittering energy tether. His Aegis spell appears as a shield of golden light shaped like a turtle shell.
- Leon: Jet's Ghostly UFO, a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon uses a green tractor beam to lift Were-Bees away from danger.
- Skelebot: a humanoid speedster robot created by Johnson. He has a skeletal robot frame, dynamic speedster posture, and bright motion trails as he evacuates the jungle.
- Overdrive: chrome robot with spiky slicked-back yellow hair, glowing green eyes, yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They manipulate matter by increasing the atomic motion of the honey lava.
- Were-Bees: humanoid bees with striped yellow-black bodies, translucent wings, expressive insect eyes, and village clothing or armor. In this story they are injured, exhausted, and frightened, but not monstrous.
- Savannah, Tecton, Chipper, Johnson, and Second Brain: part of Jet's wider crew. They may appear as distant support silhouettes or cameo background responders, but the main visual action stays focused on Jet, Leon, Skelebot, Overdrive, and the Were-Bees.
