Date: 06/26/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse, a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void.

I appear alone inside of a crinkled, paint splattered sketch.

I find the frame of the sketch and use it as a window to escape into a surreal museum, but my body immediately collapses into a puddle of paint.

The museum interior is white, purple, and gold.

The museum has a lot of twisting hallways that are coiled in such a way that they look like they are spinning.

A UFO flies in through the museum skylight and abducts me.

The pilot is a humanoid orange squid who collects my paint puddle form and pours it into a machine.

The machine reconstitutes me as a being made of large blocks, but I do not like my blocky appearance.

I reshape myself into a being of clay, so that I have control over who I am and what I want to become.

The dream was very psychedelic with a theme focused on defining my own identity, rather than allowing where I am or who I am with to define my identity for me.
