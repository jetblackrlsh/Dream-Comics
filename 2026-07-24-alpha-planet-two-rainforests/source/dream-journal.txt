Dream journal entry dated 07/24/2026

I induced lucidity via sleep paralysis.

This dream takes place in the same continuity of lucid dreams that started on 06/11/2025 and has continued to the present. These dreams take place in the Storyverse. The Storyverse is a multiverse where all possible story ideas exist as cosmic bubble universes that float together in a cosmic void. I initially spent most of my time in one particular world in the Storyverse called the Elven Village, which I discovered on 06/11/2025. My team includes Leon, Johnson, Second Brain, Skelebot, Overdrive, Savannah, Tecton, Chipper and I.

My Ghostly UFO, named Leon, resembles a ghostly flying saucer with an ethereal green glow. Leon first appeared in my dreams on 06/11/2025.

Johnson resembles an orange, humanoid goldfish. He wears a blue astronaut suit but his suit doesn't have a helmet. I befriended him in a dream on 08/03/2025.

Second Brain is a floating brain made of Lucid Light. I created him in a dream on 09/06/2025. On the same day I created Second Brain, he helped me discover the much wider Storyverse. This was the start of my adventures to explore other Storyverse worlds.

Overdrive is a robot with a metallic chrome color with spiky slicked back yellow hair and glowing green eyes. It wears a yellow shirt, yellow jacket, yellow pants, and yellow sneakers. They have the power to manipulate matter, and they were created in a dream on 10/20/2025.

Lucid Light is a reality warping energy wielded by Lucid Lords. It resembles sparkling purple energy that shines like a night sky full of stars. I discovered the power of Lucid Light in a dream on 07/19/2025.

Skelebot is a robot created by Johnson to act as the team's humanoid speedster. He was created in a dream on 09/21/2025.

Savannah is a pale skinned cyborg woman with long black hair, bangs that covered her forehead, with glowing red eyes, and wearing a futuristic silver bodysuit. Her long black hair and bangs are key identifying features. She is wielding a futuristic purple and blue crystal sword known as the Death Sword.

Her father Tecton is a green skinned, humanoid, muscular, cat ogre, barbarian who dresses in a brown loin cloth and a silver baldric.

My drone Chipper resembles a small levitating silver crab robot.

Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper all met me in early 2026 while adventuring through the Super Cathedral. Savannah, Tecton, and Chipper became official crew members aboard Leon on 02/18/2026.

My name is Jet. Here is a general description of what I look like: Man with dark-brown skin, full dark-brown beard, long dark-brown dreadlocks spilling from beneath a black hoodie over a “Dream Anime” graphic tee, black athletic pants with a white stripe, white sneakers, black cross-body belt bag, warm brown eyes. I have a rainbow sword. My rainbow sword resembles a colorful, futuristic, crystal sword. I have an energy tether. My energy tether resembles a rope made of golden glittery light.

I found myself moving through a vast corridor, although the corridor may only have seemed enormous because I had been reduced to the size of a mouse. As I traveled through it, a humanoid purple tiger woman with long hair began chasing me. She was much larger than I was, but I knew that I was dreaming, so I did not feel intimidated when she caught me in her claws.

I summoned my Lucid Light and used it to construct a gigantic humanoid tiger mech around myself. The mech grew until I towered over the purple tiger woman. She immediately stopped attacking and stared up at me in surprise.

“Oh, Alpha,” she said. “I didn’t recognize you.”

She had mistaken me for the leader of her pack. I used my Lucid Light to shrink the mech until I was the same height as her, then dismissed the armor entirely. Once she saw that I was not really the Alpha, her confusion turned into anger.

“Imposter!” she shouted.

She lunged at me and attacked with her claws, but I was carrying my Rainbow Crystal Sword. The sword healed every wound she inflicted, making her attacks useless. When she realized what the sword could do, she became determined to steal it from me. I used the sword to create a radiant rainbow barrier between us, preventing her from reaching me.

I told her that I was leaving to go on an adventure. She was welcome to travel with me, but I had no reason to remain there while she continued attacking me.

“You’ll pay for impersonating the Alpha,” she snarled. She insisted that the Alpha would want my sword and that she intended to take it for him.

I decided not to waste any more time arguing with her. Protected by my rainbow shield, I flew upward and crashed through the ceiling without being harmed. I soared away from the planet and into space.

Behind me, the entire planet began to unfurl. Its spherical shape opened and transformed into the enormous body of the Alpha, a gigantic purple tiger king whose true form had apparently been the planet itself. He tried to pursue me, but I accelerated beyond his reach.

While flying through space, I created a doorway into another reality. It was not a glowing portal, but an ordinary wooden door with a metal knob standing alone in the emptiness of space. I opened it and stepped through.

On the other side, I entered a strange rainforest filled with trees whose canopies were round. The entire landscape seemed tinted blue, and snow was falling through the tropical vegetation. It resembled the dense wilderness of the Amazon, yet it existed in the middle of a winter climate.

As I explored the snowy rainforest, I discovered a cave containing a river of lava. I followed the glowing current deeper underground. The heat radiating from the lava kept me warm, while only a faint trace of the cold wind from outside reached me. I rested near the lava and enjoyed its warmth.

My sleep was briefly disturbed, pulling me away from the dream. The interruption was minor, and I returned to sleep quickly, but I was frustrated that the experience had been broken. Once I reentered the dream, I tried to reconstruct it and begin the journey again.

The second version did not unfold exactly as the first one had. The planet no longer transformed into the Alpha, although I still found the wooden door in space and passed through it.

This time, the rainforest was experiencing autumn.

The tropical landscape was covered in orange, red, and yellow leaves. Fallen leaves blanketed the ground beneath the rainforest canopy. I had never imagined a tropical rainforest in the fall before, and the sight fascinated me. Instead of the frozen blue atmosphere of the previous version, the temperature was mild and comfortable.

I found the cave again and followed the river of lava into its depths. This time, instead of merely warming myself beside it, I stepped directly into the molten current and allowed myself to become submerged.

The lava did not burn me. It spread across my skin and transformed my entire body. I emerged in a powerful lava form, as though I had transcended into a god of fire and molten stone.

I flew out of the cave and rose high above the landscape. From the sky, I looked down upon the autumn rainforest and remembered how the same world had appeared before, covered in snow beneath a blue atmosphere. The two versions of the world began to overlap and merge inside my mind.

I moved between those visions like a photographer without a camera, carefully taking in every detail and imagining how each landscape would look as a photograph. I contemplated the contrast between the snowy rainforest with its rounded treetops and the warm, red-and-gold rainforest of autumn, preserving both impossible worlds within my memory as the dream came to an end.
